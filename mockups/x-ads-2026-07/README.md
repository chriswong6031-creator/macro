# MastermindX X Category campaign — mobile-first

Five complete 4:5 image-ad concepts for the X mobile feed. Every concept tells
the full MastermindX story, makes stock signals the focal point, shows the
market intelligence behind the call, and carries the Founding Pro offer.

## Deliverables

- `index.html` — editable 1440 × 1800 artboards and gallery.
- `manifest.json` — post copy, headlines, hypotheses, destinations and offer
  guardrails.
- `site/assets/landing/x-category-ads-2026-07/*.png` — launch-ready PNGs.

Render one exact artboard with:

```text
index.html?ad=mx-cat-01
```

## Concepts

| ID | Persuasion idea | Product story |
|---|---|---|
| `mx-cat-01` | Whole-market signal | Every force moving a stock resolves into a ranked signal and trade plan. |
| `mx-cat-02` | Noise to trade plan | A visible decision pipeline turns context into entry and invalidation. |
| `mx-cat-03` | Terminal difference | A real browser Terminal with the market-intelligence layer built in. |
| `mx-cat-04` | Market-aware AI | The evidence Mastermind AI reads before it builds the stock plan. |
| `mx-cat-05` | Signals with receipts | Every stock call exposes its supporting evidence and track record. |

## Mobile creative rules

- Master canvas: 1440 × 1800 (4:5).
- 72 px minimum perimeter safe area.
- Headlines are 87–94 px; primary support copy is 34 px.
- Product UI is simplified for ad-scale legibility, not pasted from a desktop
  homepage.
- Every concept includes the complete suite strip and the same offer treatment.

## Offer language

Founding Pro is `$75/month billed annually` (`$900/year`). Monthly Pro is
`$149/month`, so the offer is `$888` less than 12 monthly payments, or about
50% less. It is not described as 50% off the standard annual price.

Paid plans include a seven-day trial. The Founding Pro offer remains limited to
2,000 memberships and the locked rate lasts while the membership stays active.

## Experiment discipline

1. Run all five creatives against the same audience, placement, bid strategy,
   destination and schedule.
2. Use the same 4:5 placement for every concept.
3. Judge on `trial_started / landing_view`, not click-through rate alone.
4. Wait for at least 100 conversion opportunities per creative before deciding.
5. Pause the set immediately if Founding Pro terms change.
