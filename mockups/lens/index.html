Illustration disc插图色盘 — a category-tinted jewel with a hand-drawn spot glyph; the idea, anchored before the words.类型着色的小圆盘配手绘图标；文字之前先点题。
Kicker眉题 — names the kind of answer: definition, reading, track record, source, or caveat.先亮明回答类型：定义、读法、战绩、来源或提示。
Title + body标题与正文 — the term and one to three short plain sentences. Glance-tier vocabulary only — never machine text.术语加一到三句大白话。只用一级词汇——绝无机器文本。
The receipt凭据小票 — n, window, source, cadence in small mono under a dashed perforation. Optional; torn off, never leading.样本数、区间、来源、频率以小号等宽字排在虚线下。可选；只垫底，不领跑。
Aurora hairline极光描边 — a 1px gradient ring with a category-tinted arc at the crown, and a single light sweep on open. Then it holds still.1px 渐变描边，顶部带类型色弧光，开启时扫过一道光，随后归于安静。
