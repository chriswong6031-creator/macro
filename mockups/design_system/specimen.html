1 · Identity — calm institutional intelligence

1 · 品牌气质 — 沉稳的机构级智能

Dark is the command center; light is the research workspace. One typeface at extreme weight range; figures always tabular; hue is a reserved word (direction · health · wayfinding · provisional · lock) — everything else is achromatic. The page compresses; it never performs.

深色是指挥中心，浅色是研究工作台。一款字体、极大字重跨度；数字永远等宽对齐；色彩是保留字（方向 · 健康 · 导航 · 暂定 · 锁定）——其余一律无彩。页面做压缩，不做炫技。

2 · Color — surfaces, semantics, inks, series

2 · 色彩 — 表面、语义、文字级墨色、图表序列

Fill-grade tokens tint chips, bars and borders; any state printed as text uses its --ink-* rung. Direction flips under 中文 (红涨绿跌); health never flips; violet means locked and nothing else. Toggle the language above and watch the direction pair swap live.

填充级色用于底色、色条与描边；作为文字出现的状态一律用 --ink-* 档位。方向色在中文下翻转（红涨绿跌）；健康色永不翻转；紫色只表示锁定。用上方按钮切换语言，可见方向色实时互换。

6 · Density law — briefing, not museum

6 · 密度法则 — 是简报，不是陈列馆

One primary question; the answer first; ≤5 first-level sections (hard ceiling 7); a number earns Tier 1 only if it changes what you do today, carries its meaning, and isn’t derivable from a visible sibling; one canonical count per population, printed once.

一页只答一个主问题；答案先行；一级区块 ≤5（硬上限 7）；数字要上一级界面，须同时满足：影响今日决策、自带含义解释、且不能由页面上其它数字推得；同一总体只有一个官方计数，只印一次。

「93 个形态」·「81 个形态」·「74 只标的」——同一看板，一页三个总数。

连自己都说不拢的台面，卖不出「机构级」的承诺。

The one-integer law: quote or derive, never recount.

唯一计数法则：只引用或推导，绝不重复统计。
