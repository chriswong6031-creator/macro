Composition record构成记录

Ordering is a stated, non-scored precedence rule (DNR:KILL-FUSED-COMPOSITE) — the dividers ARE the ordering, which is why no rank number or score appears anywhere. Most-recent-first inside each class; no weights, no blending, no hidden inputs. Each row's LENS receipt prints source class, time, and which slot it filled (rendered open in the spec appendix). Rows carry no per-row date: the window is uniform overnight, and a constant belongs in the header once (doctrine Law 4).

排序是明示的、非打分的优先级规则（DNR:KILL-FUSED-COMPOSITE）——分隔线本身就是排序，所以全页不出现任何名次或分数。同档内按最近优先；无权重、无混合、无隐藏输入。每行的 LENS 凭据写明来源类别、时间与所属档位（在下方规范附录中展开示意）。行内不放日期：窗口统一为隔夜，恒定信息只在页头出现一次（信条第四法则）。

No dial, no gauge, no chart: the regime instrument is archetype D's identity device and A may not borrow it. Regime WORD takes health ink (a judgment); the overnight move keeps direction ink and flips honestly under 红涨绿跌. Anonymous visitors have no saved state — the primary row defaults to US, and to CN when the locale is zh.

没有仪表盘、没有量表、没有图表：状态仪表是 D 型页面的身份签名，A 型不得借用。状态词用健康墨色（属判断）；隔夜涨跌保留方向墨色，在红涨绿跌下如实翻转。匿名访客没有保存状态——主市场行默认美国，中文语境下默认中国。

Five L1 sections exactly (market state · needs attention · Prophet today · your watchlist · risk & calendar) — the command_center budget, not extended. A flat equal-weight stack would fail the archetype. 390w carries exactly two declared reductions: market state collapses to the primary market row plus a scrolling chip strip, and Prophet today collapses to ladder + one card + “See 2 more”. First swipe at 390×844 = session token, primary market line, and the first three change rows. Wide content scrolls inside its own container; the page never scrolls horizontally.

一级区块恰好五个（市场状态 · 需要留意 · 先知今日 · 你的关注列表 · 风险与日程）——指挥中心的预算，未加码。桌面端采用双栏指挥布局，即 A 型的身份签名：§2 为三分之二的主答案栏，§4 与 §5 位于三分之一侧栏；§1 与 §3 为通栏。若摊成等权重的单列堆叠即不合原型。390 宽度下只做两处既定收敛：市场状态收为主市场行加一条可横向滚动的芯片带，先知今日收为阶梯 + 一张卡片 + 「再看 2 个」。390×844 首屏 = 时段标记、主市场行、前三条变化行。宽内容在自身容器内滚动；页面永不横向滚动。
