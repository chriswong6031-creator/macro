# TOP ANATOMY Phase-0 — extended-move anatomy: topped vs continued

**Verdict.** 5 of 36 preregistered features separate topped from continued extended moves on the registration track — one of them, `D3_updown_dvol_ratio21` (up-day vs down-day dollar-volume ratio, trailing 21 sessions), in its preregistered direction: **REGISTERED**, MID lead-time class (first threshold-cross median 20 sessions before the peak, IQR −1 to +54 — a quarter of crossings come after the top; 1,519 of 2,154 TOPPED episodes cross at all). It is sign-stable in point estimate across every era and dollar-volume tercile, but the primary block CI excludes zero in only 2 of 6 stability cells — **not** in the current 2025-26 era (−0.077 [−0.179, +0.044]) and not in the high-dollar-volume tercile (−0.034 [−0.147, +0.076]); the one era cell that does exclude zero rests on 5 peak-month blocks. Four more separate as exploratory discoveries (`A4_r252`, `B2_rsi14`, `D1_dvol_z` EARLY; `C6_tr5_over_tr63` LATE) — discovery-only grades that can never reach DETECTION. This is anatomy, not detection: the pooled 37-feature model's increment over extension+volatility is sign-consistent **at day level only** (+0.016 grouped / +0.004 walk-forward, the latter spanning zero); at episode level the two schemes disagree (grouped −0.054 [−0.067, −0.040], walk-forward +0.029 [+0.014, +0.044]). Every surviving leg fires with a median 10–13% of the move still ahead, the median name places at most 17% of its fires within ±10 sessions of the peak (0% for four of five legs), and the fires' forward-63 return is better than the all-extended-day base (excess +4.2% to +5.1% for four legs; `B2_rsi14` alone is no better than the base, −0.1%) though still slightly negative in absolute terms (median −0.9% to −1.8%; `B2_rsi14` −6.1%). A warning that leaves you better off than the average extended day is not a top call. No surface built on this may show a probability, a rank, or a call.

**Who this describes (read before any number below — G0.5).** The registration cohort is fast small/mid-caps by construction: +50% trailing-126-session return, a $2M/day liquidity floor, and extended days only from **2022-07-18** onward (**2022H2–2026**) — the store opens 2021-07-06 and a name needs 260 prior in-segment sessions before it can qualify, so no extended day can exist before that date. The 2021–22H1 speculative-top cohort is in the tape but structurally unmeasurable; 20.7% of the tape (260 of 1,254 sessions) carries zero extended days. As of the store's last bar (**2026-07-02** — **40 days** before the run date; the appendix is a vintage readout, not today's market), 224 names are extended. **The registered leg fires on 15 of those 224 (6.7%) — below its own 10% control-tail base rate: CCCC, CRWL, DDOG, DINO, EWTX, FLTW, HTLD, IMOS, MATX, PALU, PDEX, PLXS, QTTB, VLO, VSAT** (cohort `D3` min 0.519 / median 1.939 / max 12.128). The cohort is biotech- and small-cap-heavy with a thin large-cap contingent (led by refiners, managed care, and security software), **zero gold/PGM miners**, and **zero extended AI leaders** — NVDA, AVGO, AMD, MU, SMCI, VRT, ANET, MRVL, PLTR, ORCL, ARM, CRDO, ALAB and DELL are all absent. The moderate-velocity leaders that motivated this program never clear the +50% bar. Every finding below describes the fast cohort it was measured on. W2's tier-widening arms (`r63≥+0.35` and `(c−MA200)/ATR63≥6`, already computed as sensitivity counts) are chartered BEFORE any claim generalizes, and the W1 display surface counts wear only on the cohort the library covers and says so.

**What the anatomy says.** The registered leg reads as effort quietly failing while price still runs: topped cases show a lower **up-day-to-down-day dollar-volume ratio** (an unbounded ratio, level ~1.9 in the current cohort — the −0.066 median gap is ~3% of that level) than their **quarter-, extension-, volatility- and dollar-volume-matched** continued controls (**duration is NOT matched** — see F1): −0.066 [−0.150, −0.003], q=0.088. It is visible in the 6–21 and 1–5 session windows before the peak and **not** in the 22–63 session window (EARLY −0.030 [−0.113, +0.036], p=0.31). Around it, the failed-registration exhibits tell one coherent, uncomfortable story — relative to matched controls, topped episodes were YOUNGER (F1, −9.7 sessions, q=0.001), CLOSER to their 63-day highs (F3, −2.25, q=0.001), with RSI still RISING into the top (B3, +1.42, q=0.006). The folk anatomy of an aging, churning, fading top is backwards at these horizons on this tape. Each exhibit has a mechanical counter-explanation this design cannot exclude: **F3 is partly definitional** (the case day sits 5/10/21 sessions before the episode argmax — 1,792 of 4,233 cases at offset 5 — while controls carry no local-max anchor), **B3 is measured over a rising leg by construction**, and **F1 is what length-biased, day-weighted control sampling produces** when nothing matches on age or episode length. §5a is the discriminating diagnostic and it does not settle them: none of the three strengthens monotonically toward the peak the way a pure anchor artefact would — F1 (−17.4 → −14.8 → −11.6) and B3 (+5.0 → +3.3 → +2.3) *weaken* as the peak approaches and F3 is flat pre-peak (−3.0 / −2.5 / −2.9) with its only sharp move *after* the top (−5.4), which is exactly where a definitional anchor bites hardest. So the anchor does not account for the pre-peak gaps, it cannot be ruled out for F3, and no design without anchor-matched controls can separate anatomy from anchor. Those wrong-sign results register nothing — their preregistered directions said the opposite, and the sign discipline exists precisely so a tight CI cannot promote a backwards hypothesis — but they are the ore body that phase-1 re-registration exists to mine. Stability fences: `A4_r252`'s inversion strengthens with recency and concentrates in the **HIGH** dollar-volume tercile (−0.194 vs −0.057 low), so on the study's own dollar-volume-as-cap proxy it is a **2022H2–2026, larger-and-more-liquid-name** phenomenon; `B2_rsi14`'s magnitude fades era over era. Track D's walk-forward E1b AUCs are below 0.50 for every model (M0 0.440, M1 0.453, M2 0.492), so its +0.039 M2−M1 increment is movement inside a sub-coin-flip regime, not evidence of structure; D is survivorship-tilted context and registers nothing either way.
