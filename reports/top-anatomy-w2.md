Findings, stated at matched strength:

1. **Heat confirms on both arms.** `B2_rsi14` is W2-CONFIRMED twice: in moderate-velocity cohorts, topped episodes run hotter than matched continued controls. The four nested cells are heterogeneous — +0.76 (R63 FULL, *below* phase-0's +1.29) / +3.87 (R63 DISJOINT) / +2.02 (ATRZ FULL) / +3.92 (ATRZ DISJOINT) — and the heterogeneity itself is the finding: the effect is largest exactly where phase-0's episodes are absent, which is the opposite of overlap-inflation but also means a single "the effect is X" number does not exist. No cross-cohort ratio is quoted because matching bins are re-cut within each arm and a ratio of medians across different matched designs carries no CI.
2. **Matched does not mean matched-on-everything.** The design matches quarter × r126-quintile × rv63-tercile × dvol-tercile (coarse bins). Episode duration is NOT matched — phase-0 says so, and on these panels topped episodes are 2.2 (R63 DISJOINT) to 17.0 (ATRZ) sessions younger than controls (F1, §4) — and RSI-14 is a bounded 0–100 transform whose gaps at levels ~63–68 partially reflect where names sit in that saturation range. R63 DISJOINT also dropped 27 of 498 cases (5.4%) for want of an admissible control (controls-per-case 3.66 vs 4.00 on FULL) — non-random thinning in sparse bins. A heat gap surviving anchor-matched controls with duration handled is what phase-1 must show before B2's confirmation graduates from anatomy to anything more.
3. **D3, C6 and D1 fail to confirm at the disjoint panels' power — which is not evidence of absence.** Every disjoint CI for these three CONTAINS phase-0's point estimate (the R63 disjoint panel tests n≈306 episodes over 39 blocks and cannot resolve effects of phase-0's size). What the disjoint panels affirmatively rule out concerns `A4_r252` on BOTH arms: its R63 disjoint CI [−0.078,+0.024] excludes phase-0's −0.128 (attenuation), and its ATRZ disjoint CI excludes both zero (wrong side) and −0.128 (reversal) — the "less long-run gain" leg is inconsistent with phase-0's effect wherever the widened cohorts can speak. D3's R63 sign flip is reported at face value (+0.152, q=0.85) with its CI printed: wide, containing both zero and phase-0's effect.
4. **Era-stability receipt: none.** The prereg's E-series for W2 deliberately omitted phase-0's E3/E4 stratifications, so this wave carries no era read, and phase-0's own fence on this exact leg — "`B2_rsi14`'s magnitude fades era over era" — has no W2 counterpart. Peak-month composition of the disjoint panels (R63: 39 of 47 months) is not printed in the artifacts, so an era-composition rival for the disjoint-vs-full heterogeneity in (1) is not examinable here. Phase-1 must carry era stratification for any B2 claim it inherits.

## §7 Adjudication

1. **W2b (surface widening) is chartered with a stratified-library MANDATE — grounded on A4 across both disjoint panels, not D3.** The affirmative evidence that anatomy differs across tiers is `A4_r252`: attenuated on R63 disjoint (CI excludes phase-0's −0.128) and reversed on ATRZ disjoint (CI excludes both zero and −0.128), with B2's four-cell heterogeneity as supporting pattern. The ATRZ tier additionally needs a cohort-honesty banner (§1: its bar admits a defensive low-velocity contingent).
2. **Phase-1 (anchor-matched re-registration) inherits a sharper target than the draft gave it:** the F1/F3/B3 ore body replicating on all four panels (§4) with its mechanical counter-explanations still standing, B2's confirmations with duration unmatched (§3.2), and a mandatory era-stratification requirement (§3.4). Its prereg should declare B2's disjoint baselines (+3.87/+3.92) and the F1/F3/B3 table as the anchor findings whose artifact-vs-anatomy status it exists to decide.
3. **The ATRZ walk-forward cells go to the gauntlet pipeline as observations sitting on a chance-level baseline** (§5) — phase-0's own standard for not crediting such increments applies until a promotion prereg beats it.
4. **Out-of-time replication remains the binding open question** for every W2 result. The store refresh (#5319 workstream) is the unblocking event; the first post-refresh wave should re-read the vintage rosters and exemplar census before anything else.
