## 2. Direction of the move — stated before any quality claim

- `win_pct`: **63.6 -> 59.4** overall — of which 63.6 -> 61.5 is the unfreeze and 61.5 -> 59.4 is the era.
- `expectancy_pct`: **1.19 -> 0.75** overall — of which 1.19 -> 0.92 is the unfreeze and 0.92 -> 0.75 is the era.
- `profit_factor`: **1.7 -> 1.38** overall — of which 1.7 -> 1.49 is the unfreeze and 1.49 -> 1.38 is the era.
- `capture`: **0.71 -> 0.38** overall — of which 0.71 -> 0.43 is the unfreeze and 0.43 -> 0.38 is the era.

The proposal's §4 pre-registration measured the era arm going **up** (`expectancy_pct` 0.94 -> 1.29) on a smaller cohort at an older panel vintage. Measured on the real panel and the real published cohort, **it goes down** — on both legs. The pre-registered direction did not survive the re-measurement, which is exactly the outcome a pre-registration exists to make visible rather than negotiable.

## 3. Era effect at row level (LEGACY vs NEW)

- episodes matured in both arms: **374**
- P&L moved: **122 (32.6%)** · median |Δ| 2.0 pp · max |Δ| 19.1 pp
- exit bar moved: 123 · exit reason moved: 58

Every one of those moves is a trade that had already closed. Under the retired grid they would keep moving as leading history rolled off; under the absolute anchor they are fixed. That is the reason for the break — the record is now well-defined, not better.

## 4. What did NOT change

Entry (next session's close), stop (90-session trough x0.97), horizon (10 sessions), and the overbought threshold are untouched. `data/us_board_ledger/retro_grades.parquet` and `site/factordata/us_board_track.json` do not read `_ob_mask` and are outside this boundary.
