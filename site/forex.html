💱 Forex Vector

A dollar-first currency board. The broad-dollar regime sits on top; each pair is scored on its move AFTER stripping the shared dollar beta, so the board is not one repeated dollar bet. Verdicts are RISK-CONTEXT, not trade calls.以美元为先的货币面板。广义美元格局置于顶部；每个货币对在剥离共同的美元贝塔后再评分，因此整个面板并非重复押注美元。结论为风险背景，而非交易建议。 · As of截至 Jun 19, 2026

Correlation to the broad dollar与广义美元的相关性 · 63d

CONTEMPORANEOUS co-movement, not causation or a forecast. Dollar↔asset betas are regime-dependent and unstable — sign flips happen (the dollar move is often the same risk/tightening impulse). "If USD ↑" reads off today's sign and can reverse. The EM channel has a slow (~1–1.5y) financial-conditions lead in the literature; everything in this grid is daily and coincident.同步共动，非因果或预测。美元与各资产的贝塔随格局变化且不稳定 — 符号会翻转（美元波动往往就是同一个风险/紧缩脉冲）。"若美元↑"读取的是当前符号，可能反转。文献中新兴市场渠道存在约1–1.5年的金融条件领先；本表均为日频同步关系。

How to read this · and what it is not如何解读 · 以及它不是什么

Each pair is scored by a multi-factor conviction engine — the same machinery as the Commodity Vector — over its DOLLAR-ORTHOGONALIZED residual: trend & structure (idiosyncratic, ex-dollar), carry (foreign minus US short rate, vol-penalized), risk appetite (the global risk-off composite times the currency's archetype beta — havens rally, risk currencies fall in stress), the per-pair risk index, residual shocks, and COT positioning when available. The score maps to a LONG / SHORT / FLAT bias on the BASE currency vs USD.每个货币对由一个多因子信心引擎评分 — 与大宗商品向量同一套机制 — 基于其剥离美元后的残差：趋势与结构（特异、去美元）、套息（外币减美国短端利率，按波动折减）、风险偏好（全球避险综合乘以货币原型贝塔 — 避险货币上涨、风险货币在压力下下跌）、单货币对风险指数、残差冲击，以及（可用时）COT持仓。评分映射为基础货币对美元的做多/做空/观望倾向。

Honesty bar.诚信底线。 FX violates uncovered interest parity (the carry "puzzle"), and carry has fat-tailed crash risk that clusters with equity crises (1998 / 2008 / 2015 / 2020 / 2024). So these are RISK-CONTEXT reads, not trade signals or measured alpha. Weights are MEASURED by split-half forward-return calibration (Spearman IC, split 2015, peg windows excised) — the glyph on each factor marks its verdict: ✓ CONFIRMED (held sign in both halves), ⇄ INVERTED (robustly predicts the opposite — e.g. the carry forward-premium puzzle), ~ DIRECTIONAL (full sample only), · CONTEXT (weak). Most FX factors land DIRECTIONAL or CONTEXT, so confidence stays dampened until a pair has ≥2 robust factors. Pegs & intervention zones (USD/JPY MoF, SNB, managed USD/CNH) cap or flatten the verdict.外汇违反无抛补利率平价（套息“谜题”），且套息具有与股市危机同步聚集的肥尾崩盘风险（1998/2008/2015/2020/2024）。因此这些是风险背景解读，而非交易信号或已测得的超额收益。权重由前后半段远期收益校准测得（Spearman IC，2015分割，剔除挂钩窗口）— 每个因子的字形标注其判定：✓ 已确认（两半段同号）、⇄ 反向（稳健预测相反方向，如套息远期溢价之谜）、~ 有方向（仅全样本）、· 背景（弱）。多数外汇因子落入有方向或背景，故在某货币对具备≥2个稳健因子前信心保持折减。挂钩与干预区间（美元/日元财务省、瑞士央行、受管理的美元/离岸人民币）会限制或抹平结论。

The Dollar Desk (§01) is display-only.美元桌（§01）仅供展示。 The dollar is the master variable — it conditions commodities, equities, bonds and crypto — so we read it deeply: real-rate regime, the Fed policy path (US leg of the rate differential), COT positioning, REER valuation, a multi-horizon trend stack, USD liquidity, and a smile-confirmation tally. But NONE of it is scored. The dollar move is often the tightening itself (coincident), FX direction is famously unforecastable (Meese-Rogoff), and every dollar leg is labeled coincident or context. The "lean" is a balance of context flags — a summary, not a trade call. There is deliberately no aggregate "USD → BUY/SELL".美元是主控变量 — 它影响大宗商品、股票、债券与加密货币 — 因此我们深入解读：实际利率格局、美联储政策路径（利差的美国端）、COT持仓、REER估值、多周期趋势栈、美元流动性，以及微笑确认计数。但这些都不参与评分。美元波动往往就是紧缩本身（同步），外汇方向出了名地不可预测（Meese-Rogoff），每个美元项都标注为同步或背景。"倾向"是背景信号的平衡 — 仅为汇总，非交易建议。我们刻意不给出汇总的"美元→买/卖"。

Transmission (§02) is contemporaneous.传导（§02）为同步关系。 Dollar↔asset correlations are computed live and are regime-dependent and unstable — sign flips happen, and the stability dot flags them. Gold is shown with its real-rate leg; the 10y-Treasury sign is unstable because the rate channel and haven channel oppose each other. This is a map of how today's dollar move is rippling, not a forecast — and it complements the cross-asset PCA on the Cross-Asset page (which asks "are the markets one bet?") rather than duplicating it. Scorecards (§06) are honest PIT backtests of risk premia with fat tails; the Max-DD and skew are the truth the Sharpe hides.美元与各资产的相关性为实时计算，随格局变化且不稳定 — 符号会翻转，稳定性圆点会标记之。黄金附带其实际利率项；10年期国债符号不稳定，因为利率渠道与避险渠道相互抵消。这是当前美元波动如何扩散的地图，而非预测 — 它与跨资产页面的PCA（"各市场是否同一笔交易？"）互补而非重复。记分卡（§06）是对带肥尾风险溢价的诚实时点回测；最大回撤与偏度才是夏普比率掩盖的真相。
