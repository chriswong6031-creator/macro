How to read this · and what it is not如何解读 · 以及它不是什么

Each pair is scored by a multi-factor conviction engine — the same machinery as the Commodity Vector — over its DOLLAR-ORTHOGONALIZED residual: trend & structure (idiosyncratic, ex-dollar), carry (foreign minus US short rate, vol-penalized), risk appetite (the global risk-off composite times the currency's archetype beta — havens rally, risk currencies fall in stress), the per-pair risk index, residual shocks, and COT positioning when available. The score maps to a LONG / SHORT / FLAT bias on the BASE currency vs USD.每个货币对由一个多因子信心引擎评分 — 与大宗商品向量同一套机制 — 基于其剥离美元后的残差：趋势与结构（特异、去美元）、套息（外币减美国短端利率，按波动折减）、风险偏好（全球避险综合乘以货币原型贝塔 — 避险货币上涨、风险货币在压力下下跌）、单货币对风险指数、残差冲击，以及（可用时）COT持仓。评分映射为基础货币对美元的做多/做空/观望倾向。

Honesty bar.诚信底线。 FX violates uncovered interest parity (the carry "puzzle"), and carry has fat-tailed crash risk that clusters with equity crises (1998 / 2008 / 2015 / 2020 / 2024). So these are RISK-CONTEXT reads, not trade signals or measured alpha. Weights are MEASURED by split-half forward-return calibration (Spearman IC, split 2015, peg windows excised) — the glyph on each factor marks its verdict: ✓ CONFIRMED (held sign in both halves), ⇄ INVERTED (robustly predicts the opposite — e.g. the carry forward-premium puzzle), ~ DIRECTIONAL (full sample only), · CONTEXT (weak). Most FX factors land DIRECTIONAL or CONTEXT, so confidence stays dampened until a pair has ≥2 robust factors. Pegs & intervention zones (USD/JPY MoF, SNB, managed USD/CNH) cap or flatten the verdict.外汇违反无抛补利率平价（套息“谜题”），且套息具有与股市危机同步聚集的肥尾崩盘风险（1998/2008/2015/2020/2024）。因此这些是风险背景解读，而非交易信号或已测得的超额收益。权重由前后半段远期收益校准测得（Spearman IC，2015分割，剔除挂钩窗口）— 每个因子的字形标注其判定：✓ 已确认（两半段同号）、⇄ 反向（稳健预测相反方向，如套息远期溢价之谜）、~ 有方向（仅全样本）、· 背景（弱）。多数外汇因子落入有方向或背景，故在某货币对具备≥2个稳健因子前信心保持折减。挂钩与干预区间（美元/日元财务省、瑞士央行、受管理的美元/离岸人民币）会限制或抹平结论。

The Dollar Desk (§01) is display-only.美元桌（§01）仅供展示。 The dollar is the master variable — it conditions commodities, equities, bonds and crypto — so we read it deeply: real-rate regime, the Fed policy path (US leg of the rate differential), COT positioning, REER valuation, a multi-horizon trend stack, USD liquidity, and a smile-confirmation tally. But NONE of it is scored. The dollar move is often the tightening itself (coincident), FX direction is famously unforecastable (Meese-Rogoff), and every dollar leg is labeled coincident or context. The Stress & Regime Radar (§1.5) is an empirical base rate, not a forecast.压力与格局雷达（§1.5）是经验基率，而非预测。 Each scenario builds a strictly-causal stress intensity from the joint configuration of currency moves — VELOCITY (recent speed in σ-units and its acceleration) and LITERAL level (drawdowns, percentiles, distance to peg/intervention zones, the CNH basis). The probability is a PAST-TENSE conditional frequency: of all the days in history at least this stressed, the share that saw the defining adverse move within N days — shown beside the unconditional base rate, the sample n, an overlap-deflated n_eff = n/N (forward windows overlap, so the effective sample is smaller and the interval wider), and a Wilson 95% interval on both. We did NOT search a threshold to maximize it — today’s reading is the only condition — so there is no multiple-testing bias to correct. It is computed once over the full causal record (no peg-window excision, because this is a realized frequency, not a fitted weight), and it is DISPLAY-ONLY: it never feeds a score, conviction or BUY/SELL. FX direction is famously unforecastable (Meese-Rogoff); a wide interval means thin history, not low risk; and the record cannot capture tail events outside the sample (1998 / 2008 / 2015 / 2020 / 2024).
