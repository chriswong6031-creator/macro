The value of one pip is your pip size times your position size, expressed in the quote currency, then converted into your account currency. On a standard 100,000-unit lot of a pair quoted in your own currency, one pip is worth about 10 of that currency — and that number scales straight up and down with the size you trade.一个点的价值等于一个点大小乘以仓位规模，先以报价货币表示，再换算成你的账户货币。对于以你自身货币报价的一手 100,000 单位标准仓位，一个点约值该货币的 10 个单位——而这个数字会随着交易规模等比例增减。

01The formula公式

Pip value (quote) = Pip size × Position size每点价值（报价） = 一个点大小 × 仓位规模
Pip value (account) = Pip value (quote) × Quote→account rate每点价值（账户） = 每点价值（报价） × 报价→账户 汇率
Per 0.1 lot (10,000 units) = Pip size × 10,000 × Quote→account rate每 0.1 手（10,000 单位） = 一个点大小 × 10,000 × 报价→账户 汇率

A pip is the standard smallest quoted step of an exchange rate — 0.0001 for most pairs, and 0.01 for pairs quoted in Japanese yen, where prices carry fewer decimals. The pip value falls out of the currency the pair is quoted in (the second one, e.g. the USD in EUR/USD); the rate then carries that into the currency your account is denominated in. When the quote currency already is your account currency, the rate is 1 and the two figures match.一个点是汇率报价的标准最小变动单位——多数货币对为 0.0001，而以日元报价的货币对为 0.01，因为其价格小数位更少。每点价值由货币对的报价货币决定（即第二种货币，例如 EUR/USD 中的 USD）；汇率再把它换算成你账户所计价的货币。当报价货币本身就是你的账户货币时，汇率为 1，两个数值相等。

02A worked example一个算例

Worked example算例

Suppose a trader holds one standard lot — 100,000 units — of a USD-quoted pair, and their account is also in USD (so the rate is 1).假设一位交易者持有一手标准仓位——100,000 单位——的以美元报价的货币对，且账户同样以美元计价（因此汇率为 1）。

  • Pip value (quote) = 0.0001 × 100,000 = $10.00每点价值（报价） = 0.0001 × 100,000 = $10.00
  • Pip value (account) = 10.00 × 1 = $10.00每点价值（账户） = 10.00 × 1 = $10.00
  • Trade a mini lot (10,000 units) instead and one pip is worth $1.00; a micro lot (1,000) makes it $0.10.若改为交易一手迷你仓位（10,000 单位），一个点值 $1.00；一手微型仓位（1,000 单位）则为 $0.10。

For a yen pair — pip size 0.01, one standard lot — a pip is worth 1,000 JPY. If the account is in USD and one USD buys ~150 JPY, the rate is 1 ÷ 150 ≈ 0.006667, so the pip is 1,000 × 0.006667 ≈ $6.67.对于日元货币对——一个点大小 0.01，一手标准仓位——一个点值 1,000 日元。若账户以美元计价，且 1 美元约兑 150 日元，则汇率为 1 ÷ 150 ≈ 0.006667，因此一个点约为 1,000 × 0.006667 ≈ $6.67。

03The common trap常见陷阱

Using the wrong rate — or forgetting it entirely用错汇率——或干脆忘了它

The rate here converts the quote currency into your account currency, not the base currency. On EUR/USD with a USD account, that rate is simply 1 and often gets dropped without harm. But on a cross where neither currency is your account currency — say GBP/JPY held in a USD account — the pip is first worth a number of yen, and you must convert from the yen into dollars. Plug in the base-currency rate by mistake and the pip value can be off by a wide margin.这里的汇率是把报价货币换算成你的账户货币，而不是基准货币。对于以美元计价账户的 EUR/USD，该汇率就是 1，省略也无妨。但对于两种货币都不是你账户货币的交叉盘——比如以美元账户持有的 GBP/JPY——一个点先以若干日元计价，你必须从日元换算成美元。若误填基准货币的汇率，每点价值可能相差甚远。

04Where this breaks它的局限

This math gives the pip value cleanly for a single position at a single moment. A few honest limits:这套算法能干净地算出单一仓位在某一时刻的每点价值。几点诚实的局限：

  • The rate moves. Your quote→account rate is itself an exchange rate that drifts through the session, so the pip value shifts slightly as it does. For sizing, the value at entry is close enough; for a precise mark, use the current rate.汇率是变动的。报价→账户汇率本身也是一个会在盘中漂移的汇率，因此每点价值也会随之小幅变化。用于定仓时，入场时的数值已足够接近；若需精确计价，请使用当前汇率。
  • Fractional pips and spreads. Many brokers quote a fifth decimal (a "pipette", one-tenth of a pip). This tool values a whole pip; the spread you pay is a separate cost, not part of the pip value.零点几个点与点差。许多经纪商报第五位小数（即“分点”，等于一个点的十分之一）。本工具计算的是一个整点的价值；你支付的点差是一项独立成本，不计入每点价值。
  • Contract-size quirks. A "lot" is a convention, not a law — some instruments and CFDs use non-standard contract sizes. Enter the actual number of units you hold rather than assuming 100,000.合约规模的特殊情形。“手”是一种约定俗成，而非硬性规则——某些品种和差价合约采用非标准合约规模。请填入你实际持有的单位数，而不要默认为 100,000。
The one line to remember记住这一句

Pip value scales one-for-one with position size, and it lands in the quote currency before it lands in yours.每点价值与仓位规模一一对应地缩放，且它先落在报价货币上，再落到你的账户货币上。
