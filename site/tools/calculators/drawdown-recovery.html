Losses are asymmetric: a 50% drawdown requires a 100% gain to recover. The math is straightforward — if you lose half your money, you need to double what remains. This asymmetry is one of the most important and most overlooked facts in risk management.亏损是不对称的：50%的回撤需要100%的涨幅才能回本。数学很直接——如果你损失了一半的钱，你需要将剩余的资金翻倍。这种不对称性是风险管理中最重要、也最常被忽视的事实之一。

01The formula公式

Required gain% = d ÷ (100 − d) × 100所需涨幅% = d ÷ (100 − d) × 100
where d = drawdown %其中 d = 回撤百分比
Years to recover = ln(100 ÷ (100 − d)) ÷ ln(1 + g ÷ 100) (requires annual return g)恢复年数 = ln(100 ÷ (100 − d)) ÷ ln(1 + g ÷ 100) （需填年化收益率g）

When you supply peak and current values, the drawdown is calculated as (peak − current) ÷ peak × 100 and that derived figure is used for all subsequent calculations.当你填入峰值和当前值时，回撤计算为 (峰值 − 当前) ÷ 峰值 × 100，并用该数值进行后续计算。

02A worked example一个算例

Worked example算例

A portfolio falls 50% from its peak. The assumed recovery rate is 10% per year.一个投资组合从峰值回撤 50%。假设年化收益率为 10%。

  • Required gain = 50 ÷ (100 − 50) × 100 = 100%所需涨幅 = 50 ÷ (100 − 50) × 100 = 100%
  • Years to recover = ln(100 ÷ 50) ÷ ln(1.10) ≈ 7.27 years恢复年数 = ln(100 ÷ 50) ÷ ln(1.10) ≈ 7.27年
  • A 20% drawdown requires only 25% to recover — far less damaging, and the contrast illustrates why avoiding large drawdowns matters more than chasing extra returns.20%的回撤只需 25% 才能回本——损害小得多。这一对比说明了为什么避免大回撤比追逐额外回报更重要。

04The common trap常见陷阱

Treating drawdown and return as symmetric将回撤与涨幅视为对称

"I'm down 30%, I just need to make back 30%." No — a 30% loss requires a 42.9% gain. A 50% loss requires 100%. The further you fall, the steeper the climb out. This is why position sizing and stop-losses exist: preventing a large drawdown is worth far more than chasing an equivalent gain."我跌了30%，只需要再涨30%即可。"不对——30%的亏损需要42.9%的涨幅。50%的亏损需要100%。跌得越深，爬出来越陡峭。这就是为什么仓位管理和止损存在：防止大回撤远比追逐等量涨幅有价值。

05Where this breaks它的局限

  • Recovery rate is assumed constant. Real returns are volatile. A 10% annual return assumption is just that — an assumption. Sequence of returns matters: a bad year in the early recovery extends the timeline.假设恢复速率固定。实际回报率是波动的。10%的年化回报率假设仅仅是假设。回报顺序很重要：恢复初期的一个坏年份会延长恢复时间线。
  • The formula uses the peak-to-trough definition. "Drawdown" is sometimes quoted as the loss from the high-water mark. Make sure the percentage you enter matches this definition.公式使用峰谷定义。"回撤"有时被引用为从高水位线开始的亏损。请确保你输入的百分比符合这一定义。
  • Taxes and fees reduce the effective recovery rate. If capital gains are taxed and trading incurs fees, the net annual return available for recovery is lower than the gross figure.税收和费用降低有效恢复速率。若资本利得需要纳税，且交易产生费用，则可用于恢复的净年化收益率低于毛值。
The one line to remember记住这一句

Cutting a loss in half doesn't cut the recovery time in half — it eliminates most of it. Protecting capital is asymmetrically more valuable than finding returns.将亏损减半不能将恢复时间减半——而是几乎消除了大部分。保护本金在不对称上远比寻找回报更有价值。
