The risk-to-reward ratio is the distance from your entry to your target, divided by the distance from your entry to your stop. A 3:1 ratio means you stand to make three dollars for every dollar you risk — and that single number tells you how often you need to be right just to break even.盈亏比是入场价到目标价的距离，除以入场价到止损价的距离。3:1意味着你每冒1美元风险，预期获得3美元——这个数字直接告诉你需要多高的胜率才能保本。

01The formula公式

R:R = | Target − Entry | ÷ | Entry − Stop |R:R = | 目标 − 入场 | ÷ | 入场 − 止损 |
Breakeven win rate = 100 ÷ (1 + R:R)保本胜率 = 100 ÷ (1 + R:R)
Expectancy (R) = win rate × R:R − (1 − win rate) (requires win rate)期望值（R） = 胜率 × R:R − (1 − 胜率) （需填胜率）

The breakeven win rate is the floor: if you win less often than that, this trade shape loses money on average even when R:R looks attractive. Expectancy in R combines both sides — a positive number means the setup has a mathematical edge per trade.保本胜率是下限：若胜率低于此值，即便盈亏比看起来不错，这类交易长期也会亏损。R期望值将两者合并——正值意味着该设置每笔交易具有数学优势。

02A worked example一个算例

Worked example算例

You buy at $100, place your stop at $95, and set a target at $115. Your win rate from similar setups is 40%.你在 $100 买入，止损设在 $95，目标设在 $115。历史同类设置的胜率为 40%。

  • Risk per share = | 100 − 95 | = $5每股风险 = | 100 − 95 | = $5
  • Reward per share = | 115 − 100 | = $15每股收益 = | 115 − 100 | = $15
  • R:R = 15 ÷ 5 = 3.0R:R = 15 ÷ 5 = 3.0
  • Breakeven win rate = 100 ÷ (1 + 3) = 25%保本胜率 = 100 ÷ (1 + 3) = 25%
  • Expectancy = 0.40 × 3 − 0.60 = +0.60 R per trade期望值 = 0.40 × 3 − 0.60 = +0.60 R 每笔

You're risking $1 to make $3. You'd need to win more than 25% of the time for this trade shape to break even — and at 40%, you clear that bar with room to spare.你以1美元的风险换3美元的回报。只需胜率超过25%即可保本——而40%的胜率轻松超过这条线。

03The common trap常见陷阱

Chasing ratio without an edge追求比率却没有优势

A 5:1 ratio looks spectacular. But if you only win 10% of the time, expectancy is still negative: 0.10 × 5 − 0.90 = −0.40 R. The ratio and the win rate are a pair — you can't optimise one while ignoring the other. Moving a stop tighter to improve the ratio also reduces the win rate; there's no free lunch.5:1的比率看起来非常诱人。但若胜率只有10%，期望值仍为负：0.10 × 5 − 0.90 = −0.40 R。比率与胜率是一对——不能只优化其中一个。将止损收紧以改善比率，同时也会降低胜率；没有免费的午餐。

04Where this breaks它的局限

  • Win rate is estimated, not known. Unless you have a meaningful sample of identical setups, any expectancy calculation is a hypothesis, not a projection.胜率是估计值，不是已知量。除非你拥有大量相同设置的样本，否则任何期望值计算都只是假设，而非预测。
  • Targets and stops are not always hit cleanly. Slippage, gaps, and partial fills mean the realized R:R often differs from the planned ratio. Plan around a range, not a single number.目标价和止损价并非总能干净成交。滑点、跳空和部分成交意味着实际R:R往往与计划值有差距。以区间而非单一数字来规划。
  • R:R varies by time frame. A 3:1 ratio on a five-minute chart and a daily chart are very different problems — holding period, news risk, and mean reversion all change the picture.R:R因时间周期而异。5分钟图和日线图上的3:1代表截然不同的交易——持仓时间、消息风险和均值回归均会改变情况。
The one line to remember记住这一句

R:R tells you the shape of one trade. Win rate tells you how often that shape pays off. You need both numbers to know if a setup has an edge.R:R描述的是单笔交易的形态。胜率告诉你这种形态多频繁能带来回报。你需要两个数字才能判断一个设置是否具有优势。
