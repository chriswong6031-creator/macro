A covered call earns you the premium up front in exchange for capping your upside at the strike. Two returns matter: the static return — the income you keep if the stock stays flat and the option expires worthless — and the return if called — the total gain if the stock finishes above the strike and your shares are sold at it. Both are measured against your cost basis.备兑看涨期权（covered call）让你预先赚取权利金，代价是把上涨空间封顶在行权价。有两个回报值得关注：静态回报——若股价持平、期权作废，你留下的收入；以及被行权回报——若股价收在行权价之上、股票以行权价被卖出时的总收益。两者都以你的成本价为基准衡量。

01The formula公式

Static return % = 100 × premium ÷ cost basis静态回报 % = 100 × 权利金 ÷ 成本价
Return if called % = 100 × (strike − basis + premium) ÷ basis被行权回报 % = 100 × (行权价 − 成本价 + 权利金) ÷ 成本价
Break-even = cost basis − premium保本价 = 成本价 − 权利金
Annualized if called % = 100 × ( (1 + return if called)^(365 ÷ days) − 1 )被行权年化 % = 100 × ( (1 + 被行权回报)^(365 ÷ 天数) − 1 )

The premium does double duty: it is your income and your first line of downside protection — the stock can fall by the premium before you’re underwater, which is exactly the break-even. The annualized figure simply scales the if-called return to a full year; it assumes the same trade could be repeated at the same pace, which is a big assumption.权利金一物两用：既是你的收入，也是你的第一道下行保护——股价可以下跌至多一个权利金的幅度你才开始亏损，这个点正是保本价。年化数字只是把被行权回报按比例换算成整年；它假设同样的交易能以同样的节奏反复进行——这是一个很大的假设。

02A worked example一个算例

Worked example算例

Suppose a trader owns shares at a $100.00 cost basis and sells a one-month call at the $105 strike, collecting $3.00 per share. Days to expiration: 30.假设某交易者以 $100.00 的成本持股，卖出一个月后到期、行权价 $105 的看涨期权，每股收取 $3.00 权利金。到期天数：30。

  • Static return = 100 × 3 ÷ 100 = 3.00% — the income if the stock is flat.静态回报 = 100 × 3 ÷ 100 = 3.00%——股价持平时的收入。
  • Return if called = 100 × (105 − 100 + 3) ÷ 100 = 8.00%.被行权回报 = 100 × (105 − 100 + 3) ÷ 100 = 8.00%。
  • Break-even = 100 − 3 = $97.00.保本价 = 100 − 3 = $97.00。
  • Annualized if called = 100 × (1.08^(365÷30) − 1) ≈ 155% — hypothetical, if repeated every month for a year.被行权年化 = 100 × (1.08^(365÷30) − 1) ≈ 155%——假设性数字，前提是每月重复一整年。

That ~155% headline is the number covered-call marketing loves to quote — and it is illustrative only. It presumes you can re-sell a call at the same premium, twelve times, with the stock landing just right each time. The honest figures here are the 3% you actually collect this month and the 8% ceiling if the shares are called.这个约 155% 的醒目数字，正是备兑看涨营销最爱引用的——但它仅供说明。它假设你能以同样的权利金反复卖出看涨期权十二次，且每次股价都恰到好处。真正诚实的数字，是你这个月实际收到的 3%，以及股票被行权时 8% 的上限。

03The common trap常见陷阱

Treating the annualized number as a forecast把年化数字当成预测

The annualized-if-called figure is a scaling, not a promise. It quietly assumes the trade repeats perfectly all year — same premium, same favorable close — and it ignores the months the stock falls (where the premium only softens the loss) or gaps far above the strike (where you leave the extra upside on the table). A single good month annualized to triple digits is a hypothetical, not an expected return.被行权年化数字是一种换算，而非承诺。它悄悄假设交易全年完美重复——同样的权利金、同样有利的收盘价——却忽略了股价下跌的月份（那时权利金只是减轻亏损），以及远高于行权价的跳空（那时你放弃了额外的上涨）。把一个好月份年化成三位数，只是假设，而非预期回报。

04Where this breaks它的局限

This math gives a clean picture of one covered call at expiration. A few honest limits:这套算法清晰地描绘了单个备兑看涨期权在到期时的情形。几点诚实的局限：

  • Capped upside. If the stock rockets past the strike, your gain stops at the strike plus premium — the return if called is your ceiling, no matter how high it runs.上涨封顶。若股价一飞冲天越过行权价，你的收益止步于行权价加权利金——无论涨多高，被行权回报就是你的天花板。
  • Real downside remains. The premium cushions a fall only down to the break-even; below that you lose money on the shares, and a large drop dwarfs the income collected.真实下行仍在。权利金只能缓冲下跌到保本价为止；再往下你就在股票上亏钱，一次大跌足以让收到的收入相形见绌。
  • Taxes, fees, dividends, and early assignment. This calculator ignores commissions, the tax treatment of premium and any called-away gain, dividends you may lose to early assignment, and the chance of being assigned before expiration. All shift the real outcome.税费、股息与提前行权。本计算器不计佣金、权利金与被行权收益的税务处理、可能因提前行权而失去的股息，以及到期前被指派行权的可能。这些都会改变真实结果。
The one line to remember记住这一句

A covered call trades your upside above the strike for income today. Judge it by the premium you keep and the ceiling you accept — not by an annualized number that assumes a perfect year.备兑看涨期权，是用行权价以上的上涨空间，换取今天的收入。用你留下的权利金和你接受的上限来评判它——而不是用一个假设完美一整年的年化数字。
