🔮 Anticipation预判

as of截至 ·

A probability game, not a price oracle. For each asset we condition the FORWARD DISTRIBUTION (return quantiles + drawdown) on a confluence of validated signals, over three horizons. We lead with the forecastable quantity — the risk cone — and label short-horizon DIRECTION as the coin-flip it measurably is.这是概率游戏，而非价格预言。对每个标的，我们用一组经验证的信号合流，对其前瞻分布（收益分位 + 回撤）在三个周期上进行条件化。我们以可预测的量 — 风险锥 — 为主，并将短期方向标注为其本质：抛硬币。

⚖️ Validated by Phase-0 on 1.17M observations: the strongest forward-drawdown predictors are credit spreads, volatility regime and the VELOCITY of deteriorating technicals. Direction is a measured coin-flip; geopolitical risk is coincident, not leading. The cone is the product — not a buy/sell signal.基于117万样本的 Phase-0 验证：最强的前瞻回撤预测因子是信用利差、波动率状态与技术面恶化的"速度"。方向为实测的抛硬币；地缘政治风险是同步而非领先。锥体是产品 — 并非买卖信号。
