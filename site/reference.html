# Market Regime市场状态 Indicator指标 A plain-word read of the market's current posture — risk-on, mixed, or risk-off — drawn from the dashboard's composite indicators, shown alongside the day's briefing. 对市场当前态势的简明判断——风险偏好、混合或避险——来自看板综合指标，与当日简报一并展示。

Also called也称 regime call · market posture read市场态势 · 市场状态判断

What it is定义

A plain-word read of the market's current posture — risk-on, mixed, or risk-off — drawn from the dashboard's composite indicators, shown alongside the day's briefing. 对市场当前态势的简明判断——风险偏好、混合或避险——来自看板综合指标，与当日简报一并展示。

Why it matters为什么重要

Gives a fast, one-word orientation before reading any detail, so you know whether the backdrop currently favours or discourages taking risk. 在查看细节前先给出一个词的方向感，帮助判断当前环境是偏向承担风险还是偏向谨慎。

Basis计量Categorical label (risk-on / mixed / risk-off)分类标签（风险偏好／混合／避险）

How to read it怎么读

▲Rises上行
Risk-on — supportive backdrop; trend, breadth, and cross-asset signals broadly line up. 风险偏好——环境偏支持性；趋势、广度与跨资产信号总体一致。
▼Falls下行
Risk-off — stressed backdrop; tape under pressure and trend pointing lower. 避险——环境承压；盘面承压，趋势向下。
■Steady持平
Mixed — signals disagree or the regime is in transition; treat with extra caution. 混合——信号不一致或市场状态处于转换期，应格外谨慎。

Worth knowing需要注意

  • A confirmation-style read of current conditions, not a forecast of what happens next.这是对当前状态的确认性判断，不是对未来走势的预测。
  • Can change quickly when underlying signals flip; check the update time before acting.底层信号变化时该判断可能迅速更新，行动前请核对更新时间。

# Regime Quadrant宏观象限 Indicator指标 A four-way classification of the macro backdrop by the direction of growth and inflation: e.g. growth rising with inflation falling, or growth falling with inflation rising. 按增长与通胀的方向变化，将宏观环境划分为四种象限：例如增长上行、通胀下行，或增长下行、通胀上行等组合。

Also called也称 growth-inflation quadrant · macro quadrant增长通胀象限 · 经济周期象限

What it is定义

A four-way classification of the macro backdrop by the direction of growth and inflation: e.g. growth rising with inflation falling, or growth falling with inflation rising. 按增长与通胀的方向变化，将宏观环境划分为四种象限：例如增长上行、通胀下行，或增长下行、通胀上行等组合。

Why it matters为什么重要

Different quadrants historically favour different sectors and factors, so it helps frame which parts of the market the backdrop tends to support. 不同象限历史上通常利好不同板块与风格因子，有助于判断当前环境更支持哪类资产。

Basis计量Categorical quadrant label with a stability qualifier象限分类标签，附带稳定性说明

How to read it怎么读

▲Rises上行
Growth up / inflation down — historically the most supportive quadrant for equities. 增长上行／通胀下行——历史上对股票最为有利的象限。
▼Falls下行
Growth down / inflation up — historically the most defensive quadrant. 增长下行／通胀上行——历史上最需防御的象限。
■Steady持平
Growth and inflation both rising or both falling — a mixed backdrop for sector tilts. 增长与通胀同向上行或同向下行——对板块配置而言属于混合环境。

  • This is a coincident, market-derived read of the cycle, not an independent forecast of the real economy — it tends to move with price rather than ahead of it.该判断主要由与市场同步的指标推导，属于同期指标而非独立的经济预测，不具备领先性。
  • The label can lag the sign-rule read of today's data during a confirmation window.在确认期内，该标签可能滞后于当日数据本身的方向。

Also called也称 pullback risk · drawdown radar回撤风险 · 风险雷达

What it is定义

A leading advisory score for pullback risk across seven scare types — credit stress, rate shocks, bubble unwind, growth scares, volatility events, and breadth breakdowns. 针对七类「惊吓」情景（信用压力、利率冲击、泡沫回落、增长担忧、波动率事件、市场广度恶化等）给出的前瞻性回撤风险提示评分。

Why it matters为什么重要

Flags rising pullback risk earlier than a lagging drawdown measure, so you can size or hedge before stress becomes visible in price. 相比滞后性的回撤指标能更早提示风险上升，帮助在压力体现到价格之前调整仓位或对冲。

Basis计量Categorical band (calm / watch / caution / elevated / risk-off)分类区间（平静／观察／谨慎／偏高／避险）

How to read it怎么读

▲Rises上行
Elevated or risk-off — historically higher-than-normal odds of a near-term pullback; a de-risking sizing consideration, not an exit signal. 偏高或避险——历史上意味着近期回撤概率高于平常，属于降低仓位风险的参考，而非清仓信号。
▼Falls下行
Calm or watch — no unusual pullback pressure detected among the tracked scare types. 平静或观察——所跟踪的各类「惊吓」情景中未检测到异常回撤压力。
■Steady持平
Caution — some scare types are stirring; a reason to tighten risk controls, not to override a supportive market read. 谨慎——部分「惊吓」情景出现苗头，提示应收紧风险控制，但不足以推翻整体偏支持性的市场判断。

Worth knowing需要注意

  • This is a probability-of-pullback advisory, not a certainty or a timing call.这是回撤概率的提示性评分，不是确定性判断或择时信号。
  • Elevated/caution bands describe sizing context; they do not by themselves override the broader market read.高位/谨慎区间提供的是仓位管理参考，不会单独凌驾于整体市场判断之上。

# Correlation Regime相关性环境 Term术语 How closely different assets or stocks are moving together, and whether that relationship is currently tighter (higher correlation) or looser (lower correlation) than usual. 不同资产或个股之间同向变动的紧密程度，以及当前该相关性相较平常是偏高还是偏低。

Also called也称 asset correlation · cross-asset correlation资产相关性 · 跨资产相关性

What it is定义

How closely different assets or stocks are moving together, and whether that relationship is currently tighter (higher correlation) or looser (lower correlation) than usual. 不同资产或个股之间同向变动的紧密程度，以及当前该相关性相较平常是偏高还是偏低。

Why it matters为什么重要

Rising correlations reduce the benefit of diversification, since more assets tend to move together during periods of stress. 相关性上升会削弱分散配置的效果，因为压力时期更多资产往往同向变动。

Basis计量Correlation coefficient or relative descriptor (high / normal / low)相关系数或相对描述（高／正常／低）

How to read it怎么读

▲Rises上行
Rising correlation — assets moving more together; diversification benefit reduced. 相关性上升——资产同向变动增多，分散配置效果减弱。
▼Falls下行
Falling correlation — assets moving more independently; diversification benefit improved. 相关性下降——资产变动更趋独立，分散配置效果提升。
■Steady持平
Correlation roughly typical for the period observed.观察期内相关性大致处于常态水平。

Worth knowing需要注意

  • Correlation is measured over a historical window and can shift quickly around major market events.相关性基于历史窗口计算，在重大市场事件前后可能迅速变化。
  • High correlation does not itself indicate direction — only that assets are moving together.相关性偏高本身并不能说明方向，只说明资产之间同向变动的程度。
