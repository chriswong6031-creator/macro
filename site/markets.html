Where every market stands各市场所处位置

Nine national equity markets on one clock — the US, UK, Europe, Japan, China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, India and Canada, each plotted on real, web-sourced data. The y-axis is how far each index has retraced from its all-time high: high = at the highs (extended), low = washed-out. Observed history (solid) and a regime-conditioned projection (dashed, with an uncertainty cone), plus valuations — so you see not just when in the cycle each market sits, but how cheap or rich it is there.九个国家股市同框 —— 美国、英国、欧洲、日本、中国、香港、台湾、印度与加拿大，全部基于真实的网络数据。纵轴表示每个指数距其历史高点回撤了多少：越高=越接近高点（拉伸），越低=超卖。实线为已发生的历史，虚线（含不确定性锥形）为情景预测，并附估值 —— 让你不仅看到每个市场处于周期的哪个阶段，还能看到它在该位置是便宜还是昂贵。
