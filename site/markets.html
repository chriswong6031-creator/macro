Where every market stands各市场所处位置

Nine national equity markets on one clock — the US, UK, Europe, Japan, China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, India and Canada. The y-axis is a cycle oscillator (0–100): high = extended near record highs, low = washed-out deep below them. Seven markets are engine-backed (country_cycles pos_v2); US and Europe show analyst estimates until their proxies are added to the engine. Observed history (solid), projection (dashed + uncertainty cone), and curated valuations with as-of dates — so you see not just when in the cycle each market sits, but how cheap or rich it is there.九个国家股市同框 —— 美国、英国、欧洲、日本、中国、香港、台湾、印度与加拿大。纵轴是周期振荡器（0–100）：高=接近历史高点（拉伸），低=深跌超卖。七个市场使用引擎数据（country_cycles pos_v2）；美国与欧洲的代理 ETF 尚未入引擎，显示分析师估算。实线为历史，虚线（含锥形区间）为预测，并附带标注截止日期的精选估值 —— 让你不仅看到每个市场处于周期的哪个阶段，还能看到它在该位置是便宜还是昂贵。
