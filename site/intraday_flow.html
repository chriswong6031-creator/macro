Playbook — Washout to Continuation操盘手册 — 洗盘至延续 field guide实战指南

1 · Universe & leadership screen1 · 覆盖范围与领涨筛选 ▸

This board covers the Mag7, AI infrastructure, AI software, AI semiconductor, semicap equipment, reshoring, defense, and power-grid baskets — names with demonstrated institutional sponsorship and options liquidity. The goal is not to find cheap stocks; it is to find tape-active leaders that have absorbed selling and are showing durable re-accumulation. Only the leader quality universe is relevant — trap-prone names (failed-breakout-trap chip) are deprioritized.

本看板覆盖Mag7、AI基础设施、AI软件、AI半导体、半导体设备、回流制造、国防与电网篮子——这些标的具有机构持续性参与和期权流动性。目标不是寻找廉价股，而是寻找经历卖压洗盘后表现出持续吸筹的活跃领涨股。只关注领涨质量标的——回踩陷阱型标的（trap-prone标签）优先级降低。

2 · Washout context — what makes a setup valid2 · 洗盘背景 — 何为有效布局 ▸

A valid washout context requires at least one of: a Bollinger-lower-band reclaim within the past 10 sessions (L1a), or a 21-day max drawdown ≤ −12% with recovery already begun (L1b). Without prior compression or selling, a high RVOL reading is just noise. The washout gives the continuation move its structural floor — the stop goes below the washout structure, not below the current bar.

有效的洗盘背景要求满足至少一项：过去10个交易日内收复布林下轨（L1a），或21日最大回撤≤−12%且已开始修复（L1b）。没有前期压缩或卖压，高RVOL读数只是噪声。洗盘为延续走势提供了结构性底部——止损应设在洗盘结构下方，而非当前K线下方。

3 · Volume & RVOL — durable accumulation vs noise3 · 成交量与RVOL — 持续吸筹vs噪声 ▸

RVOL_tod compares today's cumulative volume to ADV20 × the expected time-of-day volume share from trailing 20 sessions. A reading ≥ 1.3× (L3) is elevated. Volume durability (L4) requires ≥ 60% of today's hourly bars to close in their upper half with above-baseline volume — this distinguishes a sustained bid from a single spike. Higher-lows count confirms price is not giving back each bar's gain. RVOL alone is insufficient; it must arrive with durability.

RVOL_tod将今日累计成交量与ADV20×过去20个交易日该时段平均成交占比的乘积对比。读数≥1.3×（L3）为放量。成交量持续性（L4）要求今日≥60%的小时K收盘在上半段且成交量高于时段基准——这区分了持续买盘与单次放量。Higher-lows计数确认价格未在每根K线后回吐涨幅。RVOL单独不够，必须配合持续性。

4 · Options tape — tape activity & flow persistence (~soft)4 · 期权带 — 活跃度与流动持续性（~软性） ▸

The options flow block shows tape activity and options-flow persistence. Direction reads are SOFT — ~net call premium (NCP) is signed by minute tick-rule, not NBBO, so the call/put direction is approximate. Flow durability = share of 5-min windows with NCP > 0 + the longest positive streak. This is a confluence input, not a standalone signal, and is not used as an entry trigger in isolation. The bias badge counts (WHALE, FRESH, Z_OUTLIER, etc.) are deterministic; 0DTE is bucket-labeled only, never highlighted.

期权资金块展示期权活跃度与流动持续性。方向读数为软性近似——~净看涨权利金（NCP）通过逐分钟打单规则定向，非NBBO，因此看涨/看跌方向为近似值。流动持续性=NCP>0的5分钟窗口占比+最长正向连续条数。这是汇聚输入，非独立信号，不单独用于入场触发。偏好标签计数（WHALE、FRESH、Z_OUTLIER等）为确定性计算；0DTE仅做桶分类标注，不特别强调。

5 · Confluence K/7 & entry timing5 · 汇聚K/7与入场时机 ▸

K is a simple count of 7 independent boolean legs — no weighting, no score. Higher K means more conditions align simultaneously; it does not imply outcome probability. Useful timing: K rises intraday as more legs activate. The ideal sequence is L1 (washout context) + L6 (mtf_upturn) already set from nightly, then L3+L4 (volume) and L2 (reclaim above VWAP & prevClose) confirm intraday, optionally with L5 (~flow bid). Let the runners run — do not tighten stops into natural retracements on a durable leader.

K是7个独立布尔腿的简单计数——无加权、无评分。K值越高表示更多条件同时满足；不代表结果概率。实用时机：K值在盘中随更多腿激活而上升。理想顺序：L1（洗盘背景）+L6（多周期上行器官）已由夜间确认，然后L3+L4（成交量）和L2（收复VWAP及前收）盘中确认，可选配L5（~资金偏多）。让盈利跑——不要在持续领涨股的正常回踩中收紧止损。

6 · Reference levels & trap-prone names6 · 参考价位与回踩陷阱型标的 ▸

Reference levels (stop, VWAP, session low + ATR%) are computed from nightly entry_signal and intraday fastpath data — they are reference only, not advice. Stop comes from nightly structure; revisit it if the setup changes materially intraday. Trap-prone names (failed_breakout_trap = true) show a red trap-prone 回踩陷阱 chip and are deprioritized — these names have a track record of faking breakouts and retesting lows repeatedly. Use them only if the full K/7 is elevated AND washout is recent and deep.
