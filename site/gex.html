dealer-gamma levels, the walls, the vol surface & expected move — look up any name做市商Gamma价位、墙、波动率曲面与预期波动 — 可查询任意标的

Daily, delayed Cboe chains — NOT live intraday flow. A VOL-REGIME + LEVELS MAP (where dealer hedging concentrates), not a buy list and not directional: long gamma → dealers fade moves (price pins / mean-reverts, lower realized vol); short gamma → dealers chase (moves trend / amplify, higher vol). The dealer long-call/short-put SIGN is an unobservable assumption — robust for indices, FRAGILE for single names (a covered-call ETF or heavy retail call-buying can flip it). Walls / flip / max-pain are levels to watch, never targets.每日、延迟的 Cboe 期权链 — 并非实时盘中流动。这是波动率体制 + 价位地图（做市商对冲集中之处），并非买入清单、也无方向性：多头Gamma → 做市商抑制走势（价格磁吸/均值回归、已实现波动更低）；空头Gamma → 做市商追逐（走势趋势化/放大、波动更高）。做市商“多头看涨/空头看跌”的符号是不可观测的假设 — 对指数稳健，对个股脆弱（卖备兑ETF或散户大量买看涨可翻转）。墙/翻转/最大痛点仅供观察，绝非目标价。
