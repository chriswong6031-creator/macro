🧲 Dealer-gamma magnets做市商Gamma磁吸

where dealer option-hedging concentrates — the walls, the flip, the pins做市商期权对冲集中之处 — 墙、翻转、磁吸点

Daily, delayed Cboe levels — NOT live intraday flow. This is a VOL-REGIME + LEVELS MAP (where hedging concentrates), NOT a buy list and NOT directional: gamma tells you the CHARACTER of moves (long-gamma → dealers dampen, price pins/mean-reverts, lower realized vol; short-gamma → dealers amplify, moves trend, higher vol), not which way. The dealer long-call/short-put SIGN is an unobservable assumption — robust enough for indices, FRAGILE for single names (a covered-call ETF or heavy retail call-buying can flip the true sign). Magnet/flip strikes are levels to watch, never targets.每日、延迟的 Cboe 价位 — 并非实时盘中流动。这是波动率体制 + 价位地图（对冲集中之处），并非买入清单、也无方向性：Gamma 揭示走势的“性质”（多头Gamma → 做市商抑制、价格磁吸/均值回归、已实现波动更低；空头Gamma → 做市商放大、走势趋势化、波动更高），而非方向。做市商“多头看涨/空头看跌”的符号是不可观测的假设 — 对指数稳健，对个股脆弱（卖备兑ETF或散户大量买看涨可翻转真实符号）。磁吸/翻转价位仅供观察，绝非目标价。
