Quality + Value (QV / QuantSoft)质量＋价值

Fintel Ranked backwards排序方向相反

Durable cash generators that have fallen out of favour.

A six-factor model that ranks companies on their cash-generating ability and growth, with a significant value factor. Developed by Wilton Risenhoover from research at UCLA Anderson. Scored 0-100 as a percentile of a screened universe. Vendor description厂商描述 · Fintel, syndicated on Nasdaq, 2023-01-04 · Fintel, syndicated on Nasdaq, 2023-08-21 · Fintel, accessed 2026-08-05
Their performance claim, and what we make of it厂商的业绩宣称，以及我们的看法
The Quality+Value Score was analysed by an independent firm and they found that an investing strategy based on the model outperformed both the Russell 2000 and the S&P over time. In one test over the period of 1992 to 2013, the theoretical CAGR of the Quality+Value score was 20.73% vs. the Russell 2000 CAGR of 10.33%. In that analysis, the Sharpe Ratios were 0.91 (Q+V) vs. 0.46 (R2000) and the Sortino Ratios were 1.18 (Q+V) vs. 0.48 (R2000).

Reproduced as the vendor's claim. It is not evidence about our universe: the window (1992-2013) predates the model's publication, the benchmark is the Russell 2000 (small caps), the testing firm is unnamed, and no turnover, capacity, or transaction-cost assumption is stated. Treat as marketing provenance, not a prior.

How the parts are combined各项如何合成

none_categorical — There is deliberately no composite, and its absence is the design. A fused escalating score is a FORBIDDEN shape before the gate (RO-2 / Signal Commons R3), so the multi-primitive families ship as categorical reads over named, separately-visible primitives and only genuinely single-primitive measures carry a number. Each primitive is scored on its own against its own pre-registered sign; nothing is blended into something rankable.

Methods that do not pick names不做选股的方法 ?The models above rank stocks, so we can score them by whether their ranking sorted the right way. Some published methods make a different kind of claim — about when a strategy tends to work, not about which stock to hold. There is no ranking to check, so each one is tested on its own terms and the test is written out here.上方模型对个股排序，因此可用排序方向是否正确来评分。有些公开方法提出的是另一类主张－关于某类策略在什么时候有效，而非该持有哪只股票。没有排序可供检验，因此每项方法按其自身逻辑测试，并在此写明检验方式。

What these results cannot tell you这些结果无法说明什么

  • Three years is one market mood. Quality and value lagged badly over this stretch; a different stretch could easily read the other way.三年只覆盖一种市场情绪。质量与价值在这段时间明显落后；换一段时间很可能得出相反读数。
  • Companies that were delisted are missing from our prices, so every number here flatters the result slightly.已退市公司不在我们的行情数据中，因此此处所有数字都略微偏乐观。
  • Twelve quarterly readings is thin. Treat a borderline result as undecided rather than settled.十二个季度的读数偏少。临界结果应视为未定论，而非已定论。
  • We are testing our rebuild, not their model. Where a part is a stand-in, a poor result may be our substitute failing rather than their idea.我们检验的是自己的复刻件，而非厂商的模型。凡使用替代口径之处，结果不佳可能是替代口径失效，而非其思路有误。
  • The options family is a different panel: six weeks of a single market regime, which at a five-day horizon is about six independent windows. Every significance number on it is empty by construction — read those rows as directions to watch, not as measured effects.期权族系所用面板完全不同：仅六周、单一市场状态，按五日前瞻计算约为六个独立窗口。其上任何显著性数字在构造上都不成立－该部分应视为待观察的方向，而非已测得的效应。
  • Options rows carry the date our collector ran, not the trading session it read: 9 of 41 stamps simply repeated the session before them. We collapse those before scoring, but the change-based rows still rest on about 13 usable dates.期权数据行标注的是采集程序的运行日期，而非其读取的交易时段：41个标记中有9个只是重复了前一时段。计分前已合并重复项，但基于变化量的行仍仅依赖约13个可用日期。
