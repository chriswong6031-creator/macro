For each fund we diff its daily holdings and strip out mechanical creation/redemption flow AND the price move, leaving the manager's actual conviction per name — measured in percentage POINTS of fund weight committed, not raw share %. We rank accumulation by that conviction, so a real add to a meaningful position beats a tiny position doubling.
