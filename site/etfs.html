🐳 Real fund moves基金真实动作

where thematic & active managers are putting real conviction主题与主动经理人真正下注之处

For each fund we diff its daily holdings and strip out mechanical creation/redemption flow AND the price move, leaving the manager's actual conviction per name — measured in percentage POINTS of fund weight committed, not raw share %. We rank accumulation by that conviction, so a real add to a meaningful position beats a tiny position doubling. Cash, FX and money-market lines are excluded.对每只基金，我们比对其每日持仓，剔除机械性的申购／赎回资金流以及价格变动，留下经理人对每只个股的真实信念——以投入的基金权重百分点衡量，而非原始份额百分比。我们按该信念对增持排序，因此对有意义仓位的真实增持会胜过微小仓位的翻倍。现金、外汇与货币市场科目已排除。 ?Universe = curated THEMATIC / industry funds (SPDR sector, Global X) plus ACTIVELY managed funds (ARK). Broad-index trackers (SPY/QQQ) are deliberately excluded — they just mirror the whole market, so their 'moves' are reconstitution noise, not a curated read. The 'type' tag tells you whether a move is a discretionary manager's conviction or a curated index basket. Where a holding has price history, its cycle state shows (✅ = accumulation that also has a forming buy setup). Needs ≥2 daily snapshots to appear.范围 = 精选的主题／行业基金（SPDR 板块、Global X）加上主动管理基金（ARK）。宽基指数基金（SPY/QQQ）被刻意排除——它们只是镜像整个市场，其“动作”是再平衡噪音而非精选解读。“类型”标签会告诉你某动作是主动经理人的信念还是精选指数篮子。若持仓有价格历史，会显示其周期状态（✅=增持同时出现正在形成的买入形态）。需要≥2个每日快照才会显示。

The board now spans roughly 50 curated / actively-managed funds across SPDR, Global X, VanEck, First Trust, Sprott, Amplify, Defiance, Bitwise, ARK, Invesco and Roundhill — plus a labelled third-party feed that reaches otherwise-walled funds (e.g. WGMI).本看板现覆盖约 50 只精选／主动管理基金，横跨 SPDR、Global X、VanEck、First Trust、Sprott、Amplify、Defiance、Bitwise、ARK、Invesco 与 Roundhill——外加一路带标注的第三方数据源，触达那些原本被封锁的基金（例如 WGMI）。

⭐ Favored stocks — cross-fund consensus⭐ 被青睐个股 — 跨基金共识?Cross-fund consensus rolls every tracked fund's per-name conviction together, so you see which stocks the managers collectively favor — not one fund's isolated bet. 'Funds in' counts how many are accumulating (▲) vs trimming (▼) the name; a stock accumulated by many funds at once is a far stronger read than a single large add. 'Contested' means both camps are meaningful at the same time.跨基金共识把每只被追踪基金对个股的信念汇总起来，让你看到经理人集体青睐哪些股票——而非某一只基金的孤立下注。“参与基金”统计有多少只在增持（▲）对多少只在减持（▼）；被多只基金同时增持的个股，其信号远强于单只基金的大额买入。“分歧”表示增减两派同时具备分量。

stock个股 sector板块 funds in参与基金 net conviction净信念?Net conviction = the sum of every fund's conviction (in weight percentage POINTS) on this name — accumulation adds, trimming subtracts. Positive & broad = collectively accumulated; negative = collectively distributed. It nets opposing bets against each other, so a hotly contested name lands near zero even when gross activity is high.净信念 = 所有基金对该股信念（以权重百分点计）之和——增持为正、减持为负。为正且分布广=被集体增持；为负=被集体派发。它让对立的下注相互抵消，因此争议激烈的个股即便总活动量很大，其净值也会接近于零。 breadth detail广度明细?The top funds behind this name, each with its own conviction in weight points (e.g. BUG +5.3 · CIBR +2.1).
