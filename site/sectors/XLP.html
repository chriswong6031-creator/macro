Read the table before trusting any state. What it shows: stocks drift up over a month from EVERY state (that's the market's long upward bias), the spread between states is small, and the "scary" states measured slightly BETTER forward returns than the buy states — markets mean-revert, and waiting for full confirmation costs part of the bounce. So use the ladder for structure: where you are in the cycle, what would confirm a turn, and exactly where you're wrong (the invalidation price) — and use position size, not certainty, to express conviction. It is not a return predictor, and the table proves we checked.

⚡ Do the early (pre-emptive) signals actually help? We measured it the same way. When a "nearing a low" reading ALSO had an early bullish signal (RSI divergence / histogram turning / StochRSI pop), the next month was up 57.8% of the time (avg +1.16%, n=384) — versus 58.8% (avg +1.58%, n=10007) without one. So on average the early signals did not beat simply waiting — they fire sooner and catch the occasional sharp V-bottom, but at a higher false-alarm rate. Use them to know when to pay attention, then still require confirmation before committing. That's the honest tradeoff, measured rather than assumed.
