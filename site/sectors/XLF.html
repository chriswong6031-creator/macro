⚡ Do the early (pre-emptive) signals actually help? We measured it the same way. When a "nearing a low" reading ALSO had an early bullish signal (RSI divergence / histogram turning / StochRSI pop), the next month was up 57.8% of the time (avg +1.16%, n=384) — versus 58.8% (avg +1.58%, n=10006) without one. So on average the early signals did not beat simply waiting — they fire sooner and catch the occasional sharp V-bottom, but at a higher false-alarm rate.
