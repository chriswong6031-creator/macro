⚡ Do the early (pre-emptive) signals actually help? We measured it the same way. When a "nearing a low" reading ALSO had an early bullish signal (RSI divergence / histogram turning / StochRSI pop), the next month was up 57.8% of the time (avg +1.16%, n=384) — versus 58.8% (avg +1.58%, n=10007) without one.
