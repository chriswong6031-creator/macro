⚡ Do the early (pre-emptive) signals actually help? We measured it the same way. When a "nearing a low" reading ALSO had an early bullish signal (momentum diverging, turning up, or popping off the low end), the next month was up 56.0% of the time (avg +1.05%, n=257) — versus 58.2% (avg +1.67%, n=7274) without one. So on average the early signals did not beat simply waiting — they fire sooner and catch the occasional sharp V-bottom, but at a higher false-alarm rate. Use them to know when to pay attention, then still require confirmation before committing.
