How much to trust these states — measured, not promised ?Every trading day since 2000, across sector ETFs and large stocks, we computed the same ladder state from data available at the time and measured what price did over the next month. These are those base rates. They are tendencies with real spread around them — position sizing beats certainty.

Read the table before trusting any state. What it shows: stocks drift up over a month from EVERY state (that's the market's long upward bias), the spread between states is small, and the "scary" states measured slightly BETTER forward returns than the buy states — markets mean-revert, and waiting for full confirmation costs part of the bounce. So use the ladder for structure: where you are in the cycle, what would confirm a turn, and exactly where you're wrong (the invalidation price) — and use position size, not certainty, to express conviction. It is not a return predictor, and the table proves we checked.

Cycle methodology after graddhy.com / thefinancialtap.com: equity daily cycles typically run 36-42 trading days trough-to-trough; timing bands catch roughly 70% of lows, so ~3 in 10 arrive early or late — that's why confirmation (swing low, 10-day average) gates every buy state.
