⚡ Do the early (pre-emptive) signals actually help? We measured it the same way. When a "nearing a low" reading ALSO had an early bullish signal (RSI divergence / histogram turning / StochRSI pop), the next month was up 57.8% of the time (avg +1.16%, n=384) — versus 58.8% (avg +1.58%, n=10007) without one. So on average the early signals did not beat simply waiting — they fire sooner and catch the occasional sharp V-bottom, but at a higher false-alarm rate. Use them to know when to pay attention, then still require confirmation before committing. That's the honest tradeoff, measured rather than assumed.这些提前（预判）信号真的有用吗？ 我们用同样的方法做了测量。当一次“接近低点”的读数同时伴有提前的看多信号 （RSI 背离／柱状图转向／StochRSI 跳升）时，此后一个月上涨的概率为 57.8%（均值 +1.16%，n=384）— 而没有此信号时为 58.8%（均值 +1.58%，n=10007）。 所以平均而言，提前信号并未胜过单纯等待 — 它们触发更早，能抓住偶尔出现的尖锐 V 型底， 但误报率也更高。用它们来判断何时该留意，然后在投入之前仍需要确认。 这就是诚实的取舍 — 实测得出，而非臆断。
