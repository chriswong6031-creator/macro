The U.S. macro map still shows Goldilocks, but growth has cooled to the edge while inflation ticked up, putting the regime in transition. The copper/gold ratio is the closest indicator to flipping the growth axis negative—if it breaks lower, the regime would shift into Stagflation, which has historically been tough for equities. Today's dominant driver is a hawkish repricing of the Federal Reserve: the market is pricing out rate cuts and driving front-end yields higher, which strengthens the dollar and creates headwinds for most risk assets. The bond desk confirms a healthy but late-cycle picture, with long rates rising fastest and credit beginning to widen, while stocks and bonds have decoupled so bonds aren't hedging. 美国宏观图景仍显示金发姑娘经济，但增长已降温至边缘，而通胀小幅上升，使经济体制进入转型期。铜/金比率是使增长轴转为负值的最接近指标——若其跌破低点，体制将转为滞胀，这在历史上对股市不利。今日主导因素是美联储的鹰派重新定价：市场正在剔除降息预期并推高短期收益率，这加强了美元，并对大多数风险资产形成阻力。债券部门确认了健康但处于周期后期的状况，长期利率上升最快，信用利差开始扩大，而股票和债券已脱钩，因此债券无法提供对冲。
