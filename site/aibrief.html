The US economy is still mapped as Goldilocks, but growth has cooled to -0.07 from +0.53 just 20 days ago, while inflation has warmed from -0.68 to +0.40. The regime is transitioning, and the margin to flip is thin — a further drop in growth would push us into Reflation or Stagflation. Today's market is driven by a sharp unwind in AI and semiconductor stocks: chips are lagging the market by the widest margin in about a year, dragging growth stocks lower as rising yields favor value. The selloff is clear and concentrated. 美国经济仍被描绘为金发姑娘，但增长已从20天前的+0.53冷却至-0.07，而通胀从-0.68升温至+0.40。体制正在转变，翻转的余地很薄——增长进一步下降将使我们陷入再通胀或滞胀。 今日市场由人工智能和半导体股票的急剧平仓驱动：芯片落后市场的幅度约为一年来最大，拖累成长股走低，而收益率上升有利于价值股。抛售清晰且集中。
