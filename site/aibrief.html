The full read完整解读
The US macro is in a Q1 Goldilocks regime (low recession risk, loose financial conditions) but the dominant market driver is a tech-led de-rating: semis relative strength at -2.5σ, growth vs value at -2.1σ, cap-weight vs equal-weight at -1.9σ. This unwind is being driven by rising real yields (real 10y 2.35%) and a bear-steepener that favors value over growth. The cross-asset bond read, from a leading `rates_credit` tape, signals late-cycle credit stress not yet reflected in equities: the curve is late-cycle, credit is tight but widening, and the stock-bond correlation has broken down (0.56, bonds not hedging). The `cross_asset_confirm` divergence flags a medium-severity credit caution as a leading vote, while FX remains risk-on with a strengthening USD on a US growth premium. Entry-quality breadth adds a calibration check: trend breadth is broad-up (75% above 200-day) but only 38% of fresh entries clear the buy-filter, indicating narrow leadership, not broad conviction. In the policy realm, the Fed is hawkish on hold, the FOMC tone is neutral, and the market is aligned; the policy-intel thesis frames this as a credibility-banking setup, but that desk has no scored track record. Overall, a rotation-driven sell-off in the most-loved names under a still-calm macro surface. 美国宏观环境处于第一季度的“金发姑娘”状态（低衰退风险、宽松金融条件），但主导市场驱动力是科技股引领的估值下调：半导体相对强弱 -2.5σ，成长股 vs 价值股 -2.1σ，市值加权 vs 等权重 -1.9σ。这一平仓是由实际收益率上升（实际10年期收益率2.35%）和利空陡化（bear-steepener）推动的，后者有利于价值股而非成长股。跨资产债券解读（来自领先的`rates_credit`指标）预示着后期周期信用压力尚未在股票中反映：曲线处于后期周期，信用紧缩但利差扩大，股债相关性已破裂（0.56，债券不再对冲）。`cross_asset_confirm`的偏离发出中等严重程度的信用谨慎信号作为领先投票，而外汇仍保持风险偏好，美元因美国增长溢价而走强。入场质量广度增加了校准检查：趋势广度广泛上涨（75%高于200日均线），但只有38%的新入场通过买入过滤器，表明领导力狭窄，而非广泛信心。政策领域，美联储鹰派按兵不动，FOMC语气中性，市场与之对齐；政策情报论将其框定为信誉积累设置，但该交易台没有评分记录。总体而言，在仍平静的宏观表面下，最受青睐的个股出现由轮动驱动的抛售。
