The full read完整解读
The US macro regime is still Goldilocks — growth above trend and inflation cooling — but it sits at the edge. Growth has weakened over the past month, while inflation has firmed, pushing the regime toward a transition. The copper/gold ratio, a key growth signal, is barely above its tipping point. The dominant driver is a hawkish Fed repricing: short-term yields and the dollar have surged as the market prices out rate cuts, punishing long-duration assets like tech and Bitcoin. This shift creates friction: the Goldilocks backdrop normally supports risk-taking, but the strong dollar acts as a brake on commodities, emerging markets, and crypto. Bond markets remain healthy with no credit stress, yet the steepening yield curve favors value over growth. The result is a market with narrow leadership — trend breadth is broad but entry-quality is thin, so conviction should be limited. 美国宏观体制仍处于金发姑娘状态——增长高于趋势且通胀降温——但已处于边缘。过去一个月增长有所减弱，而通胀有所企稳，推动体制走向转变。作为关键增长信号的铜金比，略高于其临界点。主导驱动因素是鹰派美联储重新定价：随着市场定价排除降息，短期收益率和美元飙升，惩罚科技和比特币等长久期资产。这一转变造成摩擦：金发姑娘背景通常支持冒险，但强势美元对大宗商品、新兴市场和加密货币构成制约。债券市场保持健康，无信用压力，但收益率曲线趋陡有利于价值股而非成长股。结果是市场领导力狭窄——趋势广度广阔但入场质量薄弱，因此应保持有限信心。
