The full read完整解读
The US economy is still in a Goldilocks zone — growth is positive and inflation is not hot — but the ground is shifting. Growth has cooled sharply in the last few weeks, while inflation has warmed, bringing the regime to the edge of Reflation. The primary force driving markets right now is the unwind in AI and semiconductor stocks, which is pulling the whole tech sector lower. That unwind is not happening in isolation. The bond market's credit spreads are widening slightly, and the dollar is strengthening, both of which normally weigh on risk assets. The stock market hasn't fully absorbed that caution yet — it's priced for calm while the plumbing underneath is getting tighter. 美国经济仍处于金发女孩区间——增长为正、通胀不热——但根基正在动摇。过去几周增长急剧降温，而通胀升温，使该状态濒临再通胀边缘。目前驱动市场的主要力量是人工智能和半导体股票的平仓，这拖累整个科技板块走低。 这种平仓并非孤立发生。债券市场的信用利差小幅扩大，美元走强，这两者通常都会打压风险资产。股市尚未完全消化这种谨慎情绪——它定价的是平静，而底层流动性却在收紧。
