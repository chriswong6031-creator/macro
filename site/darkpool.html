Methodology & Limitations方法论与局限性

T1e — Off-exchange (FINRA-facility) volume: T1e — 场外（FINRA设施）成交量： FINRA publishes daily consolidated short-volume (CNMSshvol) files reporting each member firm's short-sale and total volume routed through FINRA-regulated facilities (e.g. FINRA's Alternative Display Facility, broker internalization). These are NOT equivalent to NYSE/Nasdaq consolidated tape volumes. FINRA每日发布综合融券成交量（CNMSshvol）文件，记录各成员机构通过FINRA监管设施（如FINRA另类显示设施、券商内部化）路由的卖空及总成交量。此数据与NYSE/纳斯达克综合报价带成交量并不等同。

Short ratio: 融券比率： Short-sale volume ÷ FINRA-facility total volume for the session. A higher ratio means more of the off-exchange routed volume was flagged short. This is a noisy, daily signal; standalone predictive edge is modest and contested in academic literature. Used here as a positioning context confirmer, not a scored alpha factor. 当日卖空成交量÷FINRA设施总成交量。比率越高说明场外路由成交量中被标记为卖空的比例越大。该信号噪声较大，独立预测能力有限，学术界亦存争议。本台仅作为仓位情境印证，非评分型阿尔法因子。

Off-exchange share denominator: 场外占比分母： Consolidated day volume sourced from Yahoo Finance EOD data (Yahoo consolidated day volume). This is the best available free denominator for the display universe; note that Yahoo volumes can lag intraday rounding differences vs official tape totals. 综合日成交量来源于雅虎财经EOD数据（雅虎综合日成交量）。这是展示范围内最优质的免费分母数据；需注意雅虎成交量与官方报价带汇总数据可能存在日内舍入差异。

T2e — FINRA OTC Transparency (weekly ATS): T2e — FINRA场外透明度（每周ATS）： FINRA's OTC Transparency programme mandates weekly reporting from all registered ATS. Data is published with a 2–4 week lag via the FINRA API (api.finra.org/data/group/OTCMarket/name/weeklysummary, keyless). The venue table shows aggregate shares and trade counts per ATS for the latest published reporting week. FINRA场外透明度计划要求所有注册ATS每周上报数据，经由FINRA API（api.finra.org，无需密钥）公布，滞后2–4周。场所表展示最新公布报告周各ATS的汇总成交量与交易笔数。
