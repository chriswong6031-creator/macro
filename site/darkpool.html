What "dark" means here.这里的「暗池」指什么。 The share of a stock's daily volume reported to FINRA's off-exchange facilities (dark pools + broker internalisers) rather than the lit exchanges. This is settled end-of-day tape — NOT live prints, and NOT direct dark-pool trade records. "Off-exchange share" = FINRA-facility volume ÷ consolidated day volume (exact date match).个股当日成交量中上报至FINRA场外设施（暗池+券商内部化）而非公开交易所的比例。此为结算后的日终成交数据——非实时成交，亦非直接的暗池成交记录。「场外占比」=FINRA设施成交量÷综合日成交量（精确日期匹配）。

Why the lean is honest but soft.为何这个倾向诚实但偏软。 Off-exchange volume hides who is trading, so it can't call direction on its own. Roughly half of off-exchange volume is short-marked by default, so we read the change in short-marking (recent vs 40-day baseline) and each name's own norm — never the raw level. A name is a "standout" only when its off-exchange share is both well above its own norm (≥1.5σ) and material (≥40% of volume). Heavy dark volume with short-marking fading leans accumulation; with short-marking building fast, distribution; mixed, unusual. A broad-market off-exchange spike can light up many names at once — this is context, a heads-up, never a trade signal.场外成交隐藏了交易方身份，因此本身无法判断方向。约一半的场外成交默认被标记为卖空，故我们观察卖空标记的变化（近期对比40日基准）与各股自身常态——绝不看绝对水平。个股仅在其场外占比既明显高于自身常态（≥1.5σ）又足够显著（≥成交量40%）时才列为「焦点」。暗池成交沉重而卖空标记回落偏向吸筹；卖空标记快速上升偏向派发；混合则为异常。全市场场外占比整体跳升可能同时点亮多只个股——这是背景提示，绝非交易信号。
