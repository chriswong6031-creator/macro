Hong Kong SFC reprimands Bright Smart Securities, fines HK$2.8 million香港证监会：对耀才证券作出谴责并处以罚款280万港元

The Hong Kong Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has reprimanded and fined Bright Smart Securities International (Hong Kong) Limited (Bright Smart) HK$2.8 million for failing to implement adequate and effective internal control procedures to monitor and detect wash trades conducted by its clients. The SFC's investigation found that from November 1, 2023, to September 13, 2025, Bright Smart's insufficient internal controls allowed 615 client accounts to execute 1,021 wash trade pairs involving 736 stocks and warrants. The SFC also found that because Bright Smart relied primarily on post-trade monitoring and manual review to identify suspicious trading activities before March 2024, clients were able to conduct wash trades before detection. (SFC website)香港证监会对耀才证券国际（香港）有限公司（耀才）作出谴责并处以罚款280万港元，原因是该公司没有实施充足而有效的内部监控程序，以监察和侦测由其客户进行的清洗交易。香港证监会的调查发现，于2023年11月1日至2025年9月13日期间，由于耀才的内部监控措施不足，导致615个客户帐户执行了1,021对涉及736只股票及权证的清洗交易。香港证监会亦发现，由于耀才在2024年3月前主要依赖交易后监察及人手审查来识别可疑交易活动，客户因而得以在被侦测前进行清洗交易。（香港证监会网站）
