Xie Guangqi, Director of the PBOC's Monetary Policy Department, said at a State Council Information Office press conference that the central bank is continuously optimizing and improving its monetary policy framework, pushing for a transition from quantity-based to price-based regulation to create a suitable monetary and financial environment. PBOC: Do not judge policy stance based on single open market operation amount央行：不宜根据单次公开市场操作量多少判断央行政策取向

On July 15, PBOC Deputy Governor Zou Lan said at a State Council Information Office press conference that the central bank will consider multiple factors, reasonably arrange the variety and scale of open market operations, sometimes draining and sometimes injecting, ultimately keeping total liquidity at an appropriate level to promote short-term rates running steadily near the policy rate. The scale of a single operation serves the need for aggregate liquidity control and should not be used to judge the central bank's policy stance. Short-term market rates are a more suitable observation indicator than the central bank's operation scale. (Securities Times)7月15日，人民银行副行长邹澜在国新办新闻发布会上表示，人民银行会统筹考虑多种因素变化，合理安排公开市场操作的品种和规模，有时回笼，有时投放，最终是保持流动性总量处于合适水平，从而促进短端利率在政策利率附近平稳运行。某一次的操作量多少是服务于流动性总量调控的需要，不宜根据单次操作量的多少来判断央行的政策取向。相对于央行的操作量，短端市场利率水平是一个更适宜的观测指标。（证券时报）
