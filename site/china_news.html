Citi: Japan need not sell over $1.1 trillion in US Treasury holdings to defend yen花旗：日本捍卫日元不必抛售逾1.1万亿美元的美债持仓

Citigroup said Japan does not need to sell its over $1.1 trillion holdings of U.S. Treasuries to defend the yen, and even though its available short-term bond holdings are running thin, other tools remain available. Strategists led by Jason Williams said Japanese authorities can use the Federal Reserve's Foreign and International Monetary Authorities Repo Facility (FIMA Repo Facility), which allows foreign central banks to pledge U.S. Treasury holdings as collateral to obtain dollar liquidity from the Fed without selling the bonds. Citigroup noted that Japan could also draw on $160 billion in deposits held at foreign central banks, most of which may be parked at Fed-related institutions, or use euro assets to buy yen.花旗集团表示，日本在捍卫日元时不必抛售逾1.1万亿美元的美国公债持仓，即使其可动用的短期债券持有部位已所剩不多，仍有其他工具可供运用。以Jason Williams为首的策略师表示，日本当局可以动用美联储的“海外和国际货币管理当局回购交易安排”(FIMA Repo Facility)。该机制允许海外央行以持有的美国公债作为抵押，向美联储取得美元流动性，无须出售公债筹措现金。花旗指出，日本还可以动用存放在外国央行的1,600亿美元存款，其中大部分可能存放在美联储相关机构中，或使用欧元资产购买日元。
