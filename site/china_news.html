"Fed whisperer" Nick Timiraos: Forward guidance is a statement from a central bank about its planned future actions or conditions for taking action. This differs greatly from a reaction function, which is a mapping like the Taylor rule. It requires no forecasts. A reaction function doesn't indicate where the economy or policy is headed; instead, it answers how policy should respond given specific economic conditions, trade-offs, or other factors.“美联储传声筒”Nick Timiraos：前瞻指引是央行对未来计划采取的行动，或者在何种情况下将采取某项行动所作出的声明。这与反应函数有很大不同。反应函数是一种映射关系，例如可以表现为泰勒规则。它并不需要任何预测。反应函数并没有说明经济或政策未来将走向何方；相反，它回答的是在面对特定经济状况、政策权衡或其他因素时，政策应当如何作出回应。
