PBOC reverse repos show zero operations again; analysts say bond market adjustment mostly driven by profit-taking央行逆回购再现“零操作”，分析称债市调整多出于止盈需求

The central bank's reverse repos again saw "zero operations." On August 11, the PBOC carried out no 7-day reverse repo operations, with 46.5 billion yuan in reverse repos maturing that day, resulting in a net withdrawal of 46.5 billion yuan from the open market. On August 11, funding conditions were generally stable, while the bond market pulled back in choppy trading after a period of consecutive gains. Those surveyed believe that this "zero operation" of reverse repos was mainly due to ample liquidity at the beginning of the month and reduced financing demand from financial institutions. The bond market adjustment came more from profit-taking demand after rapid yield declines. With the tax period approaching, government bond issuance accelerating, and ultra-long bond trading becoming crowded, the bond market will still fluctuate in the short term.
