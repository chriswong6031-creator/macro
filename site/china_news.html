Ministry of Commerce: China never deliberately pursues a trade surplus; the current account surplus as a share of GDP is generally within the internationally recognized reasonable range.商务部：中国从不刻意追求贸易顺差 总体上经常账户顺差占GDP比重处于国际公认合理区间

The Ministry of Commerce released the 'China Position on the So-Called 'Overcapacity' Issue' on July 28. The document states that China never deliberately pursues a trade surplus. China's export growth stems from both economies of scale and innovation capabilities, as well as demand from global green transformation and industrial development. For example, China's export growth to Europe is mainly concentrated in photovoltaics, new energy vehicles, lithium batteries, and chemical products, reflecting demand for energy products driven by green transformation and the energy crisis raising production costs for European industries like chemicals. China also never deliberately pursues export shares of labor-intensive products, whose share fell from 20.7% in 2012 to 15.1% in 2025. Regarding trade benefit distribution, 'surplus in China, benefits to all parties.' In 2025, foreign-funded enterprises accounted for 27% of China's exports and 16% of the trade surplus.金十数据7月28日讯，商务部7月28日发布《关于所谓“产能过剩”问题的中方立场》，文件指出，中国从不刻意追求贸易顺差。中国出口增长既源于规模经济与创新能力提升，也来自各国绿色转型和工业化发展的需求。比如，中国对欧出口增长主要集中于光伏、新能源汽车、锂电池和化工产品等，更多反映了绿色转型带动对能源产品的需求，以及能源危机抬升了欧洲化工等行业的生产成本。中国也从不刻意追求劳动密集型产品出口份额，相关产品出口比重由2012年的20.7%下降为2025年的15.1%。从贸易利益分配看，“顺差在中国，利益在各方”，2025年外资企业占中国出口的27%、顺差的16%
