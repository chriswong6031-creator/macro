China's CSRC and Hong Kong's SFC jointly announced a series of new measures to further deepen practical cooperation and close coordinated development between the two markets. The measures span multiple areas including listing and financing, index collaboration, futures products, exchange-traded funds (ETFs), financial institution internationalization, green finance, and professional qualification facilitation. Specific measures include: continuing to support eligible mainland enterprises to list and raise funds in Hong Kong; supporting closer cooperation between index companies of the two places to launch more indices based on Chinese assets and enhance the international influence of Chinese indices and assets; deepening futures market cooperation between the two places and supporting Hong Kong in launching more RMB-denominated and settled futures products; supporting institutions from both places in launching more ETF products based on the two markets and aligned with China's modern industrial system, and implementing a fast-track registration mechanism for conventional stock ETFs.中国证监会与香港证监会联合公布一系列进一步深化两地市场务实合作、密切协同发展的新举措。涵盖上市融资、指数合作、期货产品、交易所买卖基金（ETF）、金融机构国际化、绿色金融及专业资格便利化等多个范畴，具体举措包括：继续支持符合条件的境内企业赴港上市融资；支持两地指数公司加强合作，推出更多基于中国资产的指数，提升中国指数和中国资产国际影响力；深化两地期货市场合作，支持香港推出更多人民币计价结算的期货品种；支持两地机构推出更多基于两地市场、布局中国现代化产业体系的ETF产品，对常规股票ETF产品实施快速注册机制等等。
