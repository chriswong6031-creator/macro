South Korea to suspend new single-stock leveraged ETF listings to curb market volatility韩国将暂停新的单一股票杠杆ETF上市 以抑制市场波动

South Korea will suspend the listing of new single-stock leveraged exchange-traded products to curb market volatility, following surging demand for leveraged funds linked to Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix. The Financial Services Commission said in a statement on Thursday that the ban will remain in effect until market conditions stabilize. Additionally, authorities will raise the minimum margin for leveraged ETFs from 10 million won to 30 million won ($20,300) starting August 5. The decision was made after a meeting of regulators, the finance ministry, and central bank officials, amid growing calls for policymakers to curb volatility in the $4.1 trillion stock market. Many market participants believe that over a dozen leveraged ETFs launched two months ago have exacerbated fluctuations, as these products require daily large-scale rebalancing to maintain target leverage levels.韩国将暂停新的单一股票杠杆交易所交易产品上市，以抑制市场波动。此前与三星电子和SK海力士挂钩的杠杆基金需求激增。韩国金融服务委员会周四发表声明称，该禁令将持续有效，直至市场状况稳定。此外，当局还将自8月5日起把杠杆ETF的最低保证金从1000万韩元提高至3000万韩元（20300美元）。该决定是在监管机构、财政部和央行官员举行会议之后做出的，要求决策者抑制规模4.1万亿美元股票市场波动的呼声增强。许多市场参与者认为，两个月前推出的十多只杠杆ETF加剧了市场波动。由于需要保持目标杠杆水平，这类产品需要每日进行大量资金再平衡。

UK economy grows as services sector defies energy and political turmoil英国经济实现增长 服务业无视能源和政治动荡

The UK economy unexpectedly grew in May, showing that consumers and businesses were more resilient than expected amid political turmoil and energy price rises. The Office for National Statistics said on Thursday that GDP rose 0.1% in May, after a 0.1% decline in April. Economists had expected the data to be flat. The services sector grew 0.3% month-on-month in May, driven by strong performance in medical science R&D and retail, with warm weather prompting consumers to visit shops. Meanwhile, construction fell 0.8%, and industrial production fell 0.5%, as a small increase in manufacturing was offset by a decline in energy and utility output. The overall economy showed a moderate improvement amid political shocks and an energy crisis.英国经济5月意外实现了增长，这表明面对政治动荡和能源价格上涨，消费者和企业的韧性比预期的更强。 英国国家统计局周四表示，5月份国内生产总值上升了0.1%，4月份曾下降0.1%。经济学家此前预计该数据将持平。受医疗科学研发领域以及零售业表现强劲的推动，5月服务业环比增长0.3%，温暖的天气促使消费者前往商店购物。与此同时，建筑业下降0.8%，工业生产下降0.5%，原因是制造业的小幅增长被能源和公用事业产出的下降所抵消。在经济夹在政治风波和能源危机之间的背景下，整体经济出现了温和改善。

Ministry of Commerce spokesperson He Yadong, at a regular press conference on July 16, responded to questions about China's temporary ban on helium exports, saying China is a major helium importer. To ensure domestic supply, the Ministry of Commerce and the General Administration of Customs jointly issued an announcement deciding to temporarily prohibit helium exports.
