With support from the National Natural Science Foundation of China and other projects, a team led by Associate Professor Zhang Wei, Professor Xiong Shiyun, and Professor Huang Shaoming from the School of Materials and Energy at Guangdong University of Technology has made significant progress in anode materials for sodium-ion batteries. The findings were recently published in Advanced Materials.
