Italy's preliminary data released on Thursday showed the economy grew 0.2% in the second quarter from the previous three months, slightly above market expectations, providing support for the economic outlook this year. Italy's National Institute of Statistics said GDP rose 1.0% year-on-year from April to June, also significantly above market expectations. Economists had forecast GDP growth of 0.1% quarter-on-quarter and 0.7% year-on-year. The data indicated that Italy's economy performed better than expected despite a sharp rise in energy costs due to the Iran conflict. ISTAT said the quarterly growth was mainly driven by a positive contribution from domestic demand, which was enough to offset the drag from trade on economic growth. The institute did not release details of sub-items in the preliminary data but said services grew while industry and agriculture contracted.意大利周四公布的初步数据显示，第二季度经济较前三个月增长0.2%，略高于市场预期，为今年经济前景提供了支撑。意大利国家统计局表示，意大利4月至6月国内生产总值（GDP）同比增长1.0%，也明显高于市场预期。经济学家此前预计GDP环比增长0.1%，同比增长0.7%。数据表明，尽管伊朗冲突导致能源成本大幅上升，意大利经济表现仍好于市场预期。意大利国家统计局表示，第二季度环比增长主要得益于国内需求的正向贡献，其增量足以抵消贸易对经济增长造成的拖累。统计局在初步数据中未公布各项分项的具体数据，但表示服务业实现增长，而工业和农业均出现萎缩。
