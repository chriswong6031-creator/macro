[Market Liquidity Indicator] Market rates fall below policy rate【市场流动性指标】市场利率回落政策利率下方

On August 3, the DR007 closed down for a third consecutive session to 1.4204%, and further fell below the key rate level of 1.4% to 1.3836% in early trading today. After several days of liquidity injections, the central bank achieved a net drain of 559 billion yuan today.
--------
Note: Short-term money market rates such as DR007 reflect the abundance of market funds. Besides lending rates for the real economy, many transactions in financial markets like stocks and bonds rely on 'borrowing' to leverage; loan costs depend on short-term funding rates. DR007 is typically regarded as China's short-end rate anchor. The gap between DR007 and the central bank's 7-day reverse repo rate (policy rate) shows whether the market is short on cash. If DR007 is much higher than the policy rate, it indicates market tightness, and the central bank may inject liquidity; conversely, it signals8月3日，DR007收盘三连阴，降至1.4204%，今日盘初进一步跌破关键利率水平1.4%至1.3836%，央行连续数日投放流动性后，今日实现净回笼5590亿元。
--------
