Everbright Securities: Market may continue to rebound in short term; focus on three earnings主线光大证券：短期内市场或继续反弹 关注三条业绩主线

Everbright Securities research report believes the market may continue to rebound in the short term, focusing on three earnings-driven investment lines. Recent stabilization and recovery in overseas stock markets, especially the semiconductor sector, has had a positive effect on the stabilization of A-share technology stocks. The intensive disclosure period for half-year reports in August is expected to become the turning point for the market to shift from valuation digestion to earnings-driven growth, and the earnings reporting season may be the best allocation window for the second half of the year. Allocation recommendations are centered on three earnings-driven lines. First is tech hardware (semiconductors/AI computing power/storage), which is the strongest direction of earnings improvement in this cycle; second is the price-hike chain (nonferrous metals/chemicals/coal), which directly benefits from PPI recovery with high certainty of earnings delivery; the third line is export manufacturing (energy storage/power equipment/automobiles), benefiting from global manufacturing restocking and supply chain advantages.
