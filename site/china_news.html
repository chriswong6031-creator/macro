SAC: When securities firms issue bond investment advice, in principle the position size of a single bond investment advisory account should not exceed 25%中证协：券商出具债券投资建议时 原则上单一债券投顾账户持仓规模不超过25%

The Securities Association of China has issued the 'Management Rules for Securities Firms' Bond Investment Advisory Business' to strengthen self-regulation of broker bond advisory operations and standardize industry practices and service models. Key rules clarify risk control requirements for position concentration in bond advisory business and set a hard regulatory red line: when brokers issue bond investment advice, they generally may not cause a single bond advisory account's cost of a single bond position to exceed 25% of the account's total asset scale, strictly controlling concentration risk in single bond holdings. The new rules also specify three categories of exemptions: investment targets including treasury bonds, central bank bills, policy financial bonds, local government bonds, and negotiable certificates of deposit of systemically important domestic banks; within three months of the advisory service's first engagement with the corresponding account; and passive limit breaches caused by objective factors such as bond resale or adjustments to the systemically important bank list.中证协制定发布《证券公司债券投资顾问业务管理规则》，旨在强化券商债券投顾业务自律管理，规范机构展业及服务模式。新规重点明确债券投顾业务持仓集中度风控要求，划定硬性监管红线：券商出具债券投资建议时，原则上不得导致单一债券投顾账户单券持仓成本，超过该账户总资产规模的25%，严控单一债券持仓集中风险。同时新规明确三类豁免情形，具体包括投资标的为国债、央行票据、政策性金融债、地方政府债、境内系统重要性银行同业存单；投顾首次服务对应账户的三个月内；因债券回售、系统重要性银行名单调整等客观因素导致的被动超限情况。
