On August 20, six Shanghai departments, including the Municipal Housing and Urban-Rural Development Commission, the Municipal Housing Administration Bureau, the Municipal Finance Bureau, the PBOC Shanghai Branch, the Shanghai Financial Regulatory Bureau, and the Municipal Housing Provident Fund Management Center, jointly issued the "Notice on Optimizing the City's Real Estate Policy Measures," effective from August 21, 2026. Shanghai: Implementing 'trade-in' housing purchase loan subsidies上海：实施“以旧换新”购房贷款补贴

On August 20, six Shanghai departments, including the Municipal Housing and Urban-Rural Development Commission, the Municipal Housing Administration Bureau, the Municipal Finance Bureau, the PBOC Shanghai Branch, the Shanghai Financial Regulatory Bureau, and the Municipal Housing Provident Fund Management Center, jointly issued the "Notice on Optimizing the City's Real Estate Policy Measures," effective from August 21, 2026. It proposes implementing a "trade-in" home purchase loan subsidy. From the effective date of this Notice to March 31, 2027, households that purchase newly built commercial housing outside the Outer Ring Road and complete online contract filing, and sell their second-hand home in Shanghai within 1 year before or after the filing date, may apply for a subsidy from the real estate transaction center of the district where the purchased new home is located. The subsidy amount is calculated at 1% of the total loan amount for the newly purchased commercial housing, with a maximum subsidy of RMB 50,000 per unit; the total subsidy fund is RMB 200 million, available on a first-come, first-served basis until exhausted.8月20日，上海市住房城乡建设管理委、市房屋管理局、市财政局、人民银行上海市分行、上海金融监管局、市公积金管理中心等六部门联合印发《关于优化本市房地产政策措施的通知》，自2026年8月21日起施行。其中提出，实施“以旧换新”购房贷款补贴。在本《通知》实施之日起至2027年3月31日，居民家庭在本市外环外购买新建商品住房并完成合同网签备案，且在网签备案日前后1年内出售本市二手住房的，可向购入新建商品住房所在区房地产交易中心申请补贴。补贴金额按购买新建商品住房贷款总额的1%计算，单套住房最高补贴5万元；补贴总金额2亿元，用完即止。
