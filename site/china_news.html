Policy政策 华尔街见闻 76 high高

Hong Kong SFC optimizes regulatory framework for single-day leveraged and inverse products to ensure orderly market trading香港证监会优化单日杠杆及反向产品的监管框架以确保市场有序交易

Today, the Hong Kong Securities and Futures Commission issued a revised circular requiring leveraged and inverse products, whose capacity is highly susceptible to market condition changes, to adopt a flexible leverage structure. Under this structure, the leverage ratio can be adjusted daily within the existing caps (i.e., 2x for leveraged products and -2x for inverse products). Therefore, product providers can lower the target leverage ratio of these products when necessary, allowing greater room to manage the product during high-volume trading periods. The leverage ratio for the next trading day will be disclosed after each day's close. The potential daily adjustment of the leverage ratio also helps deepen investor understanding that leveraged and inverse products are single-day products, reminding investors that these products are not suitable for holding beyond one day. (SFC website)香港证监会今天发出经修订的通函，将规定其容量高度受市况变化影响的杠杆及反向产品，须采用灵活杠杆结构。在此结构下，杠杆倍数可在现有上限（即杠杆产品为2倍，反向产品为-2倍）内每日调整。因此，产品提供者在必要时可下调该等产品的目标杠杆倍数，从而于高交易量期间有更大空间管理产品。该等产品于下一交易日的杠杆倍数将于每日收市后披露。杠杆倍数每日的潜在调整，亦有助加深投资者对杠杆及反向产品属单日产品的认识，提醒投资者该等产品并不适合持有超过一日。（香港证监会网站）

Recently, the Ministry of Finance and the State Administration of Taxation issued an 'Announcement on Matters Concerning the Collection of Individual Income Tax on Offshore Trusts.' Officials from the Ministry of Finance's Tax Policy Department and the State Administration of Taxation's Income Tax Department answered reporters' questions on this matter. It mentions that for income generated during the existence of an offshore trust funded by a resident individual's property, the resident individual is the taxpayer. The income is classified annually as 'income from property transfer' or 'interest, dividends, and bonus income,' subject to a 20% individual income tax rate. 'Income from property transfer' is calculated based on the proceeds from transferring property such as equity, stocks, and real estate, minus the original cost of the property and reasonable expenses. Gains and losses within a tax year can be offset against each other, but losses cannot be carried forward to subsequent years. 'Interest, dividends, and bonus income' is calculated based on the total of various other incomes such as interest, dividends, and bonuses.近日，财政部、税务总局发布了《关于离岸信托征收个人所得税有关事项的公告》。财政部税政司、税务总局所得税司有关负责人就此回答了记者提问。 其中提到，对于居民个人装入财产的离岸信托存续期间产生的收益，以居民个人为纳税人，根据收益性质分别按照“财产转让所得”和“利息、股息、红利所得”，按年计算缴纳个人所得税，适用20%税率。其中，“财产转让所得”按照转让股权、股票、房产等财产收入额扣除财产原值和合理费用后的余额计算，在一个年度内的盈利和损失额可以互抵，但损失额不得结转以后年度；“利息、股息、红利所得”按照利息股息红利等其他各项所得总和计算。
