Russia, typically a net fuel exporter, has turned to imports after months of Ukrainian attacks on its energy facilities reduced refining output and triggered nationwide shortages. About one-third of the August imports from Asia come from India, with the rest loaded off South Korea and Malaysia, according to data and sources. The cargoes, arranged by traders, are transferred between tankers, likely to conceal origin and route as trade with Russia could trigger Western sanctions.
