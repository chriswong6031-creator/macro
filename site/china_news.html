Nine ministries including MOFCOM: Strengthen fiscal, tax, and financial policy support for aviation bonded maintenance商务部等9部门：加强航空保税维修财税、金融政策支持

Opinion of 9 ministries including the Ministry of Commerce on promoting the high-quality development of aviation bonded maintenance: products after aviation bonded maintenance that are not re-exported but sold domestically may, after obtaining relevant airworthiness certificates, go through domestic sales procedures in accordance with national regulations. For products sold domestically after maintenance within a comprehensive bonded zone, import tariffs, import value-added tax and consumption tax shall be levied according to the actual declared status of the repaired goods; for products sold domestically after bonded maintenance outside the comprehensive bonded zone, import tariffs, import value-added tax and consumption tax shall be paid retroactively based on the first-line import status of bonded goods, and domestic value-added tax and consumption tax shall be levied as required. Maintenance enterprises providing bonded maintenance and other external repair services may apply for export tax rebate (exemption) policies as stipulated. Strengthen coordination between fiscal and financial policies such as insurance, enhance enthusiasm and service quality of export credit insurance underwriting, and support the high-quality development of aviation bonded maintenance.商务部等9部门关于促进航空保税维修高质量发展的意见，其中提到，航空保税维修后的产品，不复运出境转为内销的，在获得相关适航证明后，可以按照国家有关规定办理内销手续。综合保税区内维修后产品内销，以修理后货物的实际报验状态照章征收进口关税、进口环节增值税和消费税；综合保税区外保税维修后产品内销，按保税货物一线进口状态补缴进口关税、进口环节增值税和消费税，照章征收国内环节增值税、消费税。维修企业提供保税维修等对外修理修配服务，可按规定适用出口退（免）税政策。加强财政与保险等金融政策联动，提升出口信保承保积极性和服务质效，支持航空保税维修高质量发展。
