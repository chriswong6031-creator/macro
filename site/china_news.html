Hong Kong SFC reprimands Bright Smart Securities, fines HK$2.8 million香港证监会：对耀才证券作出谴责并处以罚款280万港元

SFC reprimands and fines Bright Smart Securities International (HK) Ltd. HK$2.8 million for failing to implement adequate internal controls to monitor and detect wash trades by its clients. An SFC investigation found that from 1 Nov 2023 to 13 Sep 2025, due to insufficient internal controls, 615 client accounts executed 1,021 pairs of wash trades involving 736 stocks and warrants. The SFC also found that as Bright Smart relied mainly on post-trade monitoring and manual reviews to identify suspicious trades before Mar 2024, clients were able to conduct wash trades before detection. (SFC website)香港证监会对耀才证券国际（香港）有限公司（耀才）作出谴责并处以罚款280万港元，原因是该公司没有实施充足而有效的内部监控程序，以监察和侦测由其客户进行的清洗交易。香港证监会的调查发现，于2023年11月1日至2025年9月13日期间，由于耀才的内部监控措施不足，导致615个客户帐户执行了1,021对涉及736只股票及权证的清洗交易。香港证监会亦发现，由于耀才在2024年3月前主要依赖交易后监察及人手审查来识别可疑交易活动，客户因而得以在被侦测前进行清洗交易。（香港证监会网站）
