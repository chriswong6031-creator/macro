Indonesia announced the terms of its first panda bond issuance on Tuesday. As Southeast Asia's largest economy, the country is seeking to diversify funding sources amid exchange rate volatility and fiscal challenges. According to the bond issuance documents, Indonesia plans to issue three-year and five-year panda bonds with an initial combined size of up to 7 billion yuan. The documents show Indonesia has been approved to issue up to 30 billion yuan in panda bonds on China's interbank bond market over the next two years. 'Investors have been very positive about our Chinese bond issuance,' Indonesian Finance Minister Purbaya Yudhi Sadewa said at a briefing on Tuesday.
