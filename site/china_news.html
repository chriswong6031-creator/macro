Japan to tighten scrutiny of bank lending to overseas non-bank financial institutions日本将加强对银行向海外非银行贷款机构贷款的审查

According to the Nikkei, Japan's Financial Services Agency will review whether Japanese financial institutions are adequately assessing credit risks related to lending to overseas non-bank lenders and real estate companies. This idea will be incorporated into the draft of the agency's annual strategic priority plan, which is set to be finalized in June next year. Overseas non-bank lending operations of major Japanese banks are expanding, with so-called non-bank financial intermediaries growing globally and increasingly obtaining financing from Japanese banks.
