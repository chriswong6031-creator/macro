🔮 Thematic Foresight Desk主题前瞻台

Catch the theme before the revisions explode在盈利预期爆发前抓住主题

Anticipate themes at the precipice of induction and convert them to calls before estimate revisions broaden. Physical-supply tightness (T1) leads the revision; customer capex (T2) and revision-breadth (T4) confirm; the buy is deferred to a dislocation. Worked case: the June-2024 13D HBM/DRAM call — sold out, concentrated, underpriced, right and ~9 months early.在主题处于诱发临界点时提前布局，在盈利预期普遍上修之前转化为决策。物理供给紧张（T1）领先预期修正；客户资本开支（T2）与预期上修广度（T4）确认；买点交由错位回调触发。范例：2024年6月 13D 的 HBM/DRAM 判断——售罄、集中、被低估，正确且早约9个月。

Methodology & honesty方法与诚实声明

Display-only. Nothing here is a scored signal or a sized position. Bottleneck (T1) reads free FRED capacity / inventory / orders / industry-PPI + SEC EDGAR "sold out / on allocation" language; it is the leading thesis and shows AWAITING until the FRED series collect. Demand (T2) projects the hyperscaler-capex pool onto AI-beneficiary themes (capex leads beneficiary revenue ~2–4 quarters). Guidance (T3) reads free SEC EDGAR 8-K "raising / lowering guidance" language — management pre-signaling that leads the consensus revision by days-to-weeks (≥2 filers to call a tilt; a coarse directional band, not a numeric beat/miss — the numeric gap vs whisper is the paid upgrade). Revisions (T4) roll per-name analyst EPS-revision breadth up to the theme — the confirmation gauge, never the entry. The desk is a watchlist-builder / thesis-confirmer, not an entry-timer: 13D was right and ~9 months early, so the BUY is deferred to the dislocation overlay (buy the flush, not the pop). Exit-risk (glut) inverts the bottleneck — capacity rolling over, inventories restocking, pricing fading, and capacity expanding to chase the shortage — and takes precedence over a late read: a forming glut while estimates are still high is the trim/exit clock. No point-dates; turn-detection is measured coincident, so hazard reads are banded.仅供展示。此处任何内容都不是评分信号或仓位。瓶颈（T1）读取免费的 FRED 产能/库存/订单/行业 PPI ＋ SEC EDGAR “售罄/配额”用语；它是领先论点，在 FRED 序列采集前显示待采集。需求（T2）将云厂商资本开支池投射到 AI 受益主题（资本开支领先受益方营收约2–4个季度）。指引（T3）读取免费的 SEC EDGAR 8-K “上调／下调业绩指引”用语——管理层预先释放信号，领先市场一致预期修正数日至数周（需≥2家公司方可判定倾向；为粗略方向区间，而非精确超预期幅度——与whisper对比的数值缺口为付费升级）。预期修正（T4）将个股分析师 EPS 上修广度汇总到主题层——确认指标，绝非入场点。本台是观察名单构建器／论点确认器，而非择时器：13D 正确但早约9个月，故买点交由错位叠加（买回调，不买冲高）。退出风险（过剩）与瓶颈相反——产能见顶回落、库存回补、定价走弱、产能为追赶短缺而扩张——并优先于偏晚读数：预期仍高而过剩正在形成即为减仓／退出倒计时。不给精确日期；拐点检测为同步指标，故风险读数以区间呈现。
