    Financial Literacy AZ

    • **Financial Literacy Gap as Systemic Risk**: Global high literacy at 17.5% (US: 13%, France: 11%), with critical gender (men 23.9% vs women 11.2%) and generational (Gen Z 12.3% vs Boomers 22.2%) disparities driving behavioral risks and poor investment decisions in volatile markets.
    • **Inflation Misjudgment Distorts Behavior**: 48.7% globally overestimate inflation (Italy 70.6%, France 70.0%), directly suppressing asset allocation; Gen Z overestimates 60% vs 19% accurate, increasing demand for inflation-protected assets.
    • **Wealth Impact of Literacy**: Shifting 50% savings to bonds/equities lifts per capita wealth 14–21% over 20 years (Germany: EUR11,700 surplus); low-literacy households (53%) cannot identify investment products vs 21% of high-literacy groups.
    • **AI as Augmentation, Not Replacement**: 48% use AI weekly but literacy gaps persist (18% highly literate users vs 17% overall); overconfidence risks costly errors—portfolio managers must integrate AI with human judgment.
