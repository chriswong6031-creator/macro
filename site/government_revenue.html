These are lower-bound residuals from a bounded official award-detail sample—not company-reported or GAAP backlog. Coverage is shown per company.这些是有限官方合同明细样本中的已观测余量下界，并非公司披露或 GAAP 积压；每家公司均显示覆盖度。

Latency & identity延迟与身份

USAspending is official and keyless, but DoD records may arrive with substantial delay. Parent mapping starts from curated recipient-name queries and is shown with explicit match confidence until UEI/CAGE lineage is fully audited. Shared joint ventures may appear in more than one parent exposure series, so the headline obligation total is exposure—not unique federal spend.USAspending 官方且无需密钥，但国防部记录可能显著延迟。母公司映射从人工整理的收款方名称查询开始，在 UEI/CAGE 关系完全审计前会明确显示匹配置信度。共享合资企业可能出现在多个母公司暴露序列中，因此头部义务额是暴露总和，并非唯一联邦支出。
