How to read this如何解读

What the two boards mean两个榜单的含义

Where money keeps landing资金反复流入 — stocks that keep drawing big options money, day after day. It marks where the money is concentrating — not a promise the price goes up.反复吸引大额期权资金的股票。它标记资金集中的位置 — 并非价格上涨的承诺。

Turning up from a beating洗盘后转强 — beaten-down names where options money just flipped from selling to buying, with the price already washed out and starting to recover.超跌股中，期权资金刚由卖转买，且价格已洗盘并开始回升。

The ladder and the state word梯格与状态词

The ladder is a row of dots — one per confirming sign (heavy premium, new positions opened, near a high, volume, and so on). Filled dots are confirming; dim dots aren’t yet; dotted dots mean that data isn’t in. Hover the ladder for the full read.梯格是一排圆点 — 每个对应一项确认信号（权利金放大、建立新仓、接近高点、成交量等）。实心为确认；暗色为尚未；虚线为数据未到。悬停梯格查看完整解读。

Crowding in / Just turned means the signs are stacking up; On the radar / Turning up means it’s early; Lining up means every required sign confirmed — worth a close look, still not an automatic buy.资金涌入 / 刚刚转向表示信号在累积；雷达关注 / 转强中表示尚早；信号齐备表示所有必需信号已确认 — 值得密切关注，但仍非自动买入。

The caution tags警示标签

Tags like Earnings soon, 0DTE-heavy, Both sides, Looks hedged and Fragile tape lower your confidence in a name — they never rank, score or veto it. Hover any tag for what it means.诸如临近财报、0DTE为主、双向押注、疑似对冲、盘面脆弱等标签会降低对该标的的信心 — 但从不参与排名、评分或否决。悬停任一标签查看含义。

Display only — a watchlist, not buy or sell advice, and it doesn’t feed any rating. Direction is approximate; the size of the money flow is the reliable signal. Thresholds are fixed in advance, not tuned to look good.仅供展示 — 这是观察名单，非买卖建议，也不输入任何评级。方向为近似；可靠信号是资金流的规模。阈值为预先设定，非事后调优。
