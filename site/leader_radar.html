What does the market regime mean?"市场制度"是什么意思？

The market's internal structure is checked across breadth conditions: what share of stocks are above their 200-day average, how correlated they are, whether leadership is concentrated in a few mega-caps, and whether a rare breadth thrust event fired.市场内部结构通过多项广度条件检验：多少股票站在200日均线以上、相关性高低、领涨是否集中于少数超大盘股，以及是否触发罕见的广度冲击事件。
