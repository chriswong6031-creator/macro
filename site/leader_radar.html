RS underperforming SPY for ≥3 months, drawdown ≥25% from 52-week high, valuation in cheap territory. Often years below the 200dma. Key question: is the compression cyclical or structural?RS相对SPY下跌≥3个月，自52周高点回撤≥25%，估值处于低廉区间。通常在200日均线下方多年。关键问题：估值压缩是周期性的还是结构性的？

Stage 2 — Quiet Accumulation阶段2 — 静默积累▶
Base forming: 60-day range compression. At least 2 of: revision cycle turning positive, RS slope turning up while long-term RS still negative (RS-turn), accumulation-day count building, OBV making new highs while price has not. This is the highest-ROI watch stage — incomplete setups here are the Pick Lab's precipice ledger.筑底形成：60日区间压缩。至少2项：预测周期开始转正，RS斜率上升但长期RS仍为负（RS转向），积累日计数增加，OBV创新高而价格未创新高。这是回报率最高的观察阶段 — 此处未完成的布局是Pick Lab的临界台账。
Lead:领先： rs_turn, revision_positive, obv_divergenceRS转向，预测上调，OBV背离
Lag:滞后： price confirming (bw_emerging)价格确认（突破新兴）
Stage 3 — Catalyst Window阶段3 — 催化窗口▶
Ignition trigger within 10 sessions: gap ≥+5% on ≥2× 20-day volume, breakaway_watch emerging, or RS-line making 126-day new high while price has not (RS leads price). The Stage-2 foundation is the necessary precondition — ignition without a base almost always reverts.10个交易日内出现点火触发：跳空≥+5%且成交量≥20日均量的2倍，突破观察新兴，或RS线创126日新高而价格未创（RS领先价格）。阶段2基础是必要前提条件 — 没有筑底基础的点火几乎总是反转。

Stage 4 — Breakaway / Leadership阶段4 — 突破/领涨▶
Detector-D onset (liquid ∧ rel-breakaway ∧ 63d-new-high ∧ vol-confirm). Continuation = RS top-decile persistence ≥4 weeks. Concentration fingerprint: leader RS-accel > 0 while same-basket peer-median RS < 0 (unique leader vs peers diverging).突破探测器D启动（流动性 ∧ 相对突破 ∧ 63日新高 ∧ 成交量确认）。延续 = RS前十分位持续≥4周。集中度指纹：领头者RS加速>0，而同篮子同伴中位RS<0（唯一领头者与同伴背离）。

Stage 5 — Crowded阶段5 — 过度拥挤▶
≥3 of: extension ≥25% vs 50dma (or ≥90th pctile own history), monthly RSI ≥80, parabolic (4w return > 2× annualized 13w), valuation ≥80th pctile 5y, analyst buy-share ≥85%, call-skew richness, basket-correlation ≥0.75 rising from below 0.5. Late-stage; handoff watch most relevant here.≥3项：延伸≥25%（相对50日均线）或≥90分位（自身历史），月RSI≥80，抛物线（4周回报>2倍年化13周），估值≥5年80分位，分析师买入比例≥85%，看涨偏斜过高，篮子相关性≥0.75从低于0.5上升。后期阶段；交接观察在此最为重要。
Stage 6 — Failed阶段6 — 失败▶
Breakaway attempt that reversed — Detector-D "failed" state. Evidence dissipated. Not a permanent label; a name can re-accumulate and re-enter Stage 2 (21-session + full de-escalation lockout before the ledger refires).突破尝试后反转 — 突破探测器D"失败"状态。证据消散。不是永久标签；一个标的可以重新积累并重新进入阶段2（台账重新触发前需21个交易日 + 完全去升级锁定）。
