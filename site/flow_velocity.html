🌊 Capital Flow Velocity资金流速
Where China’s big money is rotating — A-share sectors ranked by 主力 (large-order) flow velocity, drilled into the individual names driving each, with the question at every name: does the institution agree? Cross-border Stock-Connect flows sit below, as context.中国大资金在向哪里轮动——A股板块按主力（大单）资金流速排序，下钻至驱动它们的个股，并在每只个股追问：机构是否认同？ 跨境沪深港通资金作为背景置于下方。
Display-only positioning lens — flow is never scored into an allocation signal. Velocity = standardized net-flow rate (cumulative-flow drift ÷ its own vol); acceleration = its 2nd derivative. Confluence is a binary agree/diverge read where 主力 flow and Dragon-Tiger institutional seats land on the same name — context, not a manufactured score.仅供定位参考——资金流向从不计入配置信号。流速＝标准化净流入率（累计流向漂移 ÷ 自身波动）；加速度＝其二阶导。共振＝当主力资金与龙虎榜机构席位落在同一只个股上的二元一致/背离判断——仅为背景，非人造分值。
