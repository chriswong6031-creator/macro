🧺 Thematic baskets主题篮子

as of截至 —

Curated thematic stock baskets — every membership decision dated and documented; equal-weighted, monthly-rebalanced, buy-and-hold between rebalances. Where a listed sector ETF approximates the theme the basket is cross-checked against it daily. A monitoring/structure frame, not a buy list.精选主题股票篮子 — 每次成分调整均注明日期与理由；等权重、按月再平衡、调整间买入持有。若有相近行业 ETF，则按日交叉验证。仅作监测/结构参考，并非买入清单。

★✅ What to act on now现在该做什么 buy / add · reduce / avoid买入加仓 · 减仓回避

The executional shortlist — themes with a clean entry to buy or add right now, and themes to reduce or avoid. Click any theme to see the specific stocks to act on. If nothing has a clean entry, the right move is patience.可执行清单 — 当前具备干净入场点、值得买入或加仓的主题，以及应减仓或回避的主题。点击任一主题可查看应操作的具体个股。若无干净入场点，最佳操作是耐心等待。

★🧭 Theme Rotation Desk主题轮动台 scored · labeled · actionable评分 · 标注 · 可执行

Every theme scored 0–100 from transparent legs — trend, breadth, ±3% impulse, macro tailwind and a crowding penalty — then labeled (dominant / emerging / fading / deteriorating) with an actionable read. The verb accounts for dominant themes topping out vs less-dominant themes just emerging.每个主题以透明分项打分 0–100 — 趋势、广度、±3% 脉冲、宏观顺风与拥挤惩罚 — 并标注（主导／新兴／退潮／走弱）及可执行结论。结论会区分见顶的主导主题与刚刚新兴的次主导主题。

★🛑 Market concentration & theme leadership市场集中度与主题领导力 who owns the advance vs the decline谁主导上涨 vs 谁在下跌

The tape can narrow — a few names carry the index while breadth rots underneath. This reads the broad-market advance-decline (daily → monthly), which THEMES own the advancers vs sit in the decline, and the clean entries vs roll-over watch — so you accumulate the right theme and step out of the topping one.行情会变窄 — 少数个股拉动指数，而广度在底下恶化。此处读取大盘涨跌家数（日→月）、哪些主题主导上涨、哪些处于下跌，以及干净入场 vs 回落观察 — 帮助你加仓正确的主题、退出见顶的主题。

★📆 5-Day Theme Rotation5日主题轮动 weekly climbers & fallers本周上升与下降

Who moved this week — ranked by 5-day return relative to the S&P 500, with the change in 20-day relative-strength rank over the same 5 sessions. A fast read on which themes to lean into and which are losing the tape.本周谁在动 — 以相对标普 500 的 5 日回报排名，并标注同期 20 日相对强度排名的变化。快速判断该倾向哪些主题、哪些正在失去走势。

★⚡ Impulse & Extremes脉冲与极值 across the thematic universe覆盖全部主题成分

Breadth of the daily move across every name in the baskets: how many jumped ≥3% vs fell ≥3% (impulse / volatility), and how many printed a fresh 52-week high vs low. Net counts read the tape's pressure at a glance.篮子内所有个股当日波动的广度：多少只上涨≥3%、多少只下跌≥3%（脉冲／波动），以及多少只创 52 周新高对新低。净数一眼读出走势压力。

What this is &amp; how it was validated这是什么 · 如何验证

A detection + description layer, NOT a forecast. It ranks where cross-sectional flow is concentrating across the unbiased point-in-time GICS sectors and the thematic baskets, and maps the cross-group cluster. Rigorously validated to have NO forward edge (rank-IC ~0 on clean sectors; the basket "edge" was survivorship) — the read-quality bar measures data/coherence, not directional confidence. The cohesion gate is the one measured-but-modest, stress-conditional signal (a dispersion-recovery proxy, not inflows). Built on a ~3y cache; reconfirm as data accrues. A wide detector that focuses a separate inference layer — never scored, never an axis.这是探测+描述层，并非预测。它在无偏的时点 GICS 行业与主题篮子中排出资金横截面集中之处，并刻画跨组集群结构。经严格验证无前瞻优势（干净行业 rank-IC≈0；篮子的"优势"实为幸存者偏差）— 读数质量条衡量数据/一致性，而非方向置信度。共动门是唯一实测但有限、压力条件下的信号（压力离散修复代理，并非资金流入）。基于约3年缓存，随数据积累需复核。这是聚焦独立推断层的"宽探测器" — 从不评分、绝不入轴。 validation meta验证元数据
