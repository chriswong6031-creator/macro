What "time-to-peak" and "capture rate" mean"峰值时间"与"捕获率"含义

The scoreboard detail row shows how a typical trade played out over 25 sessions after entry. Time-to-peak (median session of the best gain) tells you how long the move usually takes to reach its high point — so a peak on day 8 with a 21-day exit means you are typically selling after the move has already rolled over. Capture rate is what fraction of that best gain you actually keep at a given exit horizon — a 10-day exit with 70% capture means a typical hold of 10 days keeps about 70 cents of every peak dollar. These are descriptive numbers, not targets; they help you decide which exit horizon fits the thesis. 记分板详情行显示了入场后25节内一笔典型交易的表现。峰值时间（最佳收益的中位节次）告诉你走势通常需要多久才能到达高点——例如第8日见顶而21日退出，意味着你通常是在走势已经回落后才卖出。捕获率是在某一退出地平线实际保留的最佳收益比例——10日退出70%捕获率意味着持有10日通常能保留约每个峰值美元的70分。这些是描述性数字，不是目标；它们帮助你判断哪个退出地平线符合论点。
