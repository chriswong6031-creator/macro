Market State Score市场状态分 Macro dashboard宏观仪表盘

A 0–100 read of how the US market is behaving right now — risk-on, mixed, or risk-off. It confirms the tape; it does not forecast it.0–100 的读数，说明美股当下的状态：风险偏好、混合或避险。它确认当前走势，而不是预测未来。

Why it matters为何重要Green means trend-follow. Yellow means size smaller. Red means defend capital first.绿色顺势加仓，黄色减小仓位，红色优先保住本金。

Why it matters为何重要Use it for sector and style tilts, not for timing the market itself.用于板块与风格配置，而非判断大盘拐点。

Why it matters为何重要A just-changed reading means wait for confirmation before making big bets.刚转变时，应等待确认后再下重注。

Why it matters为何重要A higher reading means trade smaller — not necessarily sell.读数越高，仓位应越小——不一定要清仓。
