⚡ Standout individual stocks⚡ 表现突出的个股 · cycle-aligned bottoming · weekly + 3-day + daily turning up周期共振筑底 · 周线＋3日＋日线同向上行 ?The actionable per-stock shortlist — the China parallel of the US 'Standout individual stocks' card strip. This is a MULTI-TIMEFRAME BOTTOMING-ALIGNMENT screen, not a deep-dip catch: a name is shown only when its weekly momentum is NOT still falling (basing, turning up, or already rising — never mid weekly bear leg), its 3-day is at or nearing a bullish MACD cross, and its daily has just crossed up or is about to (1-2 bars) — or is already rising but not over-extended. A still-falling weekly name (a falling knife) is excluded by design even if its trailing relative strength is strong. Ranked by alignment quality (weekly-heaviest), with the A-share reversal / relative-strength leg only as a tiebreaker. A 🟡 near-aligned tag marks a backfill name (weekly OK + one lower timeframe turning, but not all three confirmed) shown only when too few names are fully aligned — treat it as a watch, not a confirmed entry. Context to size and confirm — not a buy list.可操作的个股精选名单 —— 美国“表现突出的个股”卡片的中国版。这是一套多周期共振筑底筛选，而非深跌抄底：仅当个股的周线动量不再下行（筑底、转向上行、或已上行——绝不处于周线空头段），3日线处于或临近看多 MACD 交叉，且日线刚刚上穿或即将上穿（1-2 根 K 线）——或已上行但未过度延伸时，才会显示。仍在下行的周线个股（接飞刀）即便趋势强度很高也按规则剔除。按共振质量排序（周线权重最高），A 股反转/相对强度仅作并列时的次级排序。🟡 临近共振标记表示补位个股（周线合格＋某一较低周期转向，但三周期尚未全部确认），仅在充分共振的名称过少时显示——视为观察项而非确认入场。用于定仓与确认的背景 —— 而非买入清单。
