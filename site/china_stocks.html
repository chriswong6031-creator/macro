This is a MULTI-TIMEFRAME BOTTOMING-ALIGNMENT screen, not a deep-dip catch: a name is shown only when its weekly momentum is NOT still falling (basing, turning up, or already rising — never mid weekly bear leg), its 3-day is at or nearing a bullish MACD cross, and its daily has just crossed up or is about to (1-2 bars) — or is already rising but not over-extended. A still-falling weekly name (a falling knife) is excluded by design even if its trailing relative strength is strong. Ranked by alignment quality (weekly-heaviest), with the A-share reversal / relative-strength leg only as a tiebreaker. A 🟡 near-aligned tag marks a backfill name (weekly OK + one lower timeframe turning, but not all three confirmed) shown only when too few names are fully aligned — treat it as a watch, not a confirmed entry.
