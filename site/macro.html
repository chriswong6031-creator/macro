🚦 Dislocation Gate-1 · buyable washout vs falling knife错位一道闸 · 可买入错杀 对 接飞刀 ?A risk FILTER that sits in FRONT of the capitulation gauge: when a stress dislocation hits (VIX panic, a >10% drawdown, a variance-premium extreme or VIX backwardation), it asks ONE question — is the Fed put present? PUT-ABSENT = a recession is underway (real-time Sahm >= 0.50) OR inflation locks the Fed (sustained 10y breakeven >= 2.5%). The whole edge is in REFUSING the falling knife (2000/2008/2022), not in catching the bounce — measured on SPY 1997-2026, see research/DISLOCATION_VALIDATION.md.位于恐慌触底指标之前的风险过滤器：当压力错位出现（VIX 恐慌、>10% 回撤、方差溢价极端或 VIX 倒挂）时，它只问一个问题 — 美联储托底是否在位？托底缺失＝衰退进行中（实时 Sahm ≥ 0.50）或通胀锁住美联储（持续十年期盈亏平衡 ≥ 2.5%）。全部优势在于拒绝接飞刀（2000／2008／2022），而非抄到反弹 — 基于标普 1997-2026 实测。
