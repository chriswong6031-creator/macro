Two separate questions, deliberately kept apart. CONVICTION (the rank) is how good the name looks: mostly how far it is outrunning its own sector, confirmed by value, quality, profitability, low volatility and insider buying. BUY (its own column) is whether you can act today — a name only reads 'Buy zone' once the entry signal has actually fired, or is about to. A high-conviction leader that is rolling over is NOT a buy; it shows a muted dash. The 'trend' chip tells you how much risk to take, never whether to buy, and it never touches the rank — folding timing into the rank measurably hurt when we tested it.
