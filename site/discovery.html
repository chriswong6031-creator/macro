🏆 Alpha leaders阿尔法领先股

sector-neutral residual momentum across the S&P 1500 · winners within their sector板普 1500 的板块中性残差动量 · 各板块内的领先股

The durable form of the operator's idea: strip each stock's market AND sector beta, then rank the residual ('alpha') information ratio WITHIN its GICS sector over a 12-1-month window. The entry tag flags a leader that ran hot (extended → reversal risk) vs one on a recent pullback (constructive). Validated on a deep 1962-2026 panel (research/RESIDUAL_ALPHA_MOMENTUM.md): residual is the modern-durable variant of momentum, but it's a modest, decayed, crowded edge — a research lens and context, NOT a buy list. Short-term reversal is an entry overlay, not the score; 'acceleration' was tested and dropped (anti-predictive).操作者想法的持久形式：剔除每只股票的市场与板块贝塔，再在其 GICS 板块内按 12-1 个月窗口对残差（“阿尔法”）信息比率排名。入场标签标示领先股是否过热（extended → 反转风险）或处于近期回调（constructive）。已在 1962-2026 深度样本验证（research/RESIDUAL_ALPHA_MOMENTUM.md）：残差是动量的现代持久变体，但属温和、已衰减、拥挤的边际信号——仅作研究视角与背景，并非买入清单。短期反转为入场叠加项而非评分；“加速度”经测试已剔除（反向预测）。
