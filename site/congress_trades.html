Congressional Trades国会交易

Every member of the U.S. House and Senate must disclose their stock trades (the STOCK Act). We pull those filings from Quiver Quantitative and turn them into a copy-signal watchlist: a ticker joins the moment a member discloses a trade in it and stays ~6 months from its latest transaction before falling off. Names disclosed by more — and bipartisan — members rank as more important. Each name is then scored on the politician cluster, the engine technical read, and a sector/theme buyable-zone gate. The cluster generates the idea; the engine confirms it — a heavily-bought name that is extended or rolling over is high-interest but not "act now."
