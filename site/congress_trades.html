🏛️ Politician-Disclosed Trades Desk政界披露交易台

Congressional Trades国会交易

Every member of the U.S. House and Senate must disclose their stock trades (the STOCK Act). We pull those filings from Quiver Quantitative and turn them into a copy-signal watchlist: a ticker joins the moment a member discloses a trade in it and stays ~6 months from its latest transaction before falling off. Names disclosed by more — and bipartisan — members rank as more important. Each name is then scored on the politician cluster, the engine technical read, and a sector/theme buyable-zone gate. The cluster generates the idea; the engine confirms it — a heavily-bought name that is extended or rolling over is high-interest but not "act now." Display / research only.美国国会参众两院议员必须披露其股票交易（STOCK 法案）。我们从 Quiver Quantitative 获取这些申报，转化为可跟单的观察清单：一旦有议员披露某标的交易，它即进入清单，并从最近一次交易日起保留约 6 个月后移出。被更多议员（尤其两党共同）披露的名字更重要。随后按政界集群、引擎技术面与板块/主题可买区间评分。集群产生想法，引擎负责确认——被大量买入但已过度拉升或走弱的名字关注度高，但并非"现在就买"。仅供展示/研究。
