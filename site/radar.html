🛰️ Divergence Radar背离雷达

Where independent REAL ACTIVITY — federal contracts, Quiver gov-contracts / congress / lobbying / patents, and modeled news flow — runs AHEAD of or BEHIND price. The radar stays silent when activity and price agree; it speaks only on the divergence. Plus a Claude reading of each theme's durability, graded against the tape over time. Display-only context, never a trade trigger.独立的真实活动 —— 联邦合同、Quiver 政府合同／国会／游说／专利，以及建模新闻流 —— 领先或落后于价格之处。活动与价格一致时雷达保持沉默，只在背离时发声。另含 Claude 对各主题持续力的解读，并随时间对照行情评分。仅供参考，非交易触发。
