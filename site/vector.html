Rule book规则手册

  • Hold only while price is above the 200-day trend.仅在价格高于 200 日趋势时持有。
  • Reduce exposure as price stretches above its long-term mean.价格过度偏离长期均值时降低仓位。
  • Move to cash when the trend breaks.趋势走坏时转为现金。

Honest limits真实局限

  • Trend following gives back part of every high and re-enters late after lows.趋势策略会回吐部分顶部收益，也会在低点后较晚重新入场。
