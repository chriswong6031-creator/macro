How does the Pro 7-day trial work?Pro 的 7 天试用如何运作？
The trial is on Pro. You add a card and get full access for seven days — cancel any time before day 7 and you’re never charged. If you don’t cancel, Pro renews at the price you picked. Essential has no trial: it bills today, and you can cancel any time.试用适用于 Pro。绑定银行卡后即获得七天完整访问——第 7 天前随时取消，不会产生任何扣费；若未取消，Pro 将按所选价格续订。Essential 无试用期：今日即扣款，且随时可取消。
Can I switch between monthly and annual?可以在按月与按年之间切换吗？
Yes. Change your billing interval any time from your account — annual simply costs less per month.可以。随时在账户中更改结算周期——按年只是每月更便宜。
What’s the difference between Essential and Pro?Essential 和 Pro 有什么区别？
Essential unlocks the entire site and the fast chat lane. Pro adds the deep Opus chat lane — the heavyweight model for real research — plus far more chat every month.Essential 解锁整个网站与快速对话通道。Pro 另加深度 Opus 对话通道——用于真正研究的重量级模型——并大幅提升每月对话额度。
Is the charts terminal really free?图表终端真的免费吗？
Yes — the charts terminal is free for everyone. Live options data is the paid part, and it’s included with Essential and Pro (the Pro trial included).是的——图表终端对所有人免费。实时期权数据为付费部分，Essential 与 Pro 均已包含（Pro 试用期也含）。
Can I cancel any time?可以随时取消吗？
Any time, from your account. You keep access through the end of the period you already paid for.随时都可以，在账户中操作。已付费周期结束前你仍保有访问权限。
