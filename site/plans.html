How does the 7-day trial work?7 天试用如何运作？
You add a card and get full access for seven days. Cancel any time before day 7 and you’re never charged. If you don’t cancel, the plan renews at the price you picked.绑定银行卡后即获得七天完整访问。第 7 天前随时取消，不会产生任何扣费；若未取消，则按所选价格续订。
Can I switch between monthly and annual?可以在按月与按年之间切换吗？
Yes. Change your billing interval any time from your account — annual simply costs less per month.可以。随时在账户中更改结算周期——按年只是每月更便宜。
