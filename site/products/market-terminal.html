Research workspace, not execution venue

The Terminal does not place trades. Market data, research panels and engine outputs can update on different schedules, and some live options or intraday features require an eligible plan or trial. The pricing page owns the current access boundary.

Always check the timestamp attached to the surface you are reading. A chart label, engine state or AI annotation is not a recommendation and cannot guarantee an outcome.
