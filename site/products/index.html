One investigation flow一套研究流程

01 · ORIENT

Read the market state读取市场状态

Start with nightly macro, cycle, breadth, sector and cross-asset context. Keep each reading attached to its own timestamp.先看每晚更新的宏观、周期、宽度、行业与跨资产背景，并保留每项读数自己的时间戳。

02 · INVESTIGATE

Open the underlying evidence打开底层证据

Move from a broad observation into a chart, stock dossier or indexed research page instead of treating a dashboard label as the final word.从宏观观察深入图表、股票档案或已索引研报，而不是把仪表盘标签当作最终结论。

03 · EXPLAIN

Ask in context基于上下文提问

Use Mastermind AI to explain the surface you are viewing, then inspect the linked source and its freshness before relying on the summary.让 Mastermind AI 解释当前页面，再检查链接来源及其新鲜度，之后再决定如何使用摘要。
