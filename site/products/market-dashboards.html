Access & limits
Free to read, honestly labeled

The dashboards are free to read. Some desks and live features require an eligible plan — the pricing page owns the current access boundary. Every surface carries its own timestamp, and the readings are research context, not individualized investment advice.

Surfaces update on different schedules: most desks rebuild nightly, while a few intraday panels refresh during the session and say so on the panel. Where data is missing, it reads as unavailable rather than being padded with a stale number.

Product UI drawings on this page are hand-drawn examples that show layout and wording; the figures in them are not live market data.
