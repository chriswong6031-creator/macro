The full read完整解读
China’s market map sits squarely in stagflation — growth has cooled and inflation is warming, while the central bank’s liquidity support is still contracting. Defensive sectors like banks, coal, and staples have jumped to the front, while the previously hot semiconductors and tech stocks have been hammered. The broad picture is one of a cautious market rotating into safety as liquidity dries up, but with rate cuts and reserve-requirement reductions already announced, a pivot to easier money is telegraphed — it just hasn’t reached the financial pipes yet. Hong Kong shares the same stagflation mark, but it gets an extra push from global forces: the US dollar is strong, and money is leaking out of the Hong Kong dollar peg — both are headwinds for stocks. The market just staged a washout recovery, but with that kind of external pressure, any bounce may be short-lived. 中国市场地图完全处于滞胀之中——增长已降温，通胀在升温，而央行的流动性支持仍在收缩。防御性板块如银行、煤炭和必需消费品已跃居前列，而此前热门的半导体和科技股则遭受重创。总体而言，随着流动性枯竭，市场谨慎地转向安全资产，但降准降息已宣布，宽松转向的讯号已经发出——只是尚未到达金融管道。 香港同样面临滞胀，但全球力量带来了额外压力：美元走强，资金正在流出港币挂钩——两者都是股票面临的逆风。市场刚刚经历了一场洗盘后的反弹，但面对这种外部压力，任何反弹都可能短暂。
