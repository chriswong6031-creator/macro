Mainland China is still in stagflation on the map, but growth has stopped falling and inflation is cooling — the drift is toward a better spot. However, the central bank's cash taps are still not reaching companies and stocks, so the improvement is more promise than delivery. Hong Kong shares the same stagflation label, but it dances to the dollar's tune. A strong US dollar and a currency peg under pressure mean global money is seeping out, making HK much more fragile than the mainland. 中国内地仍处于滞胀状态，但增长已停止下滑，通胀正在降温——正朝着更好的方向移动。然而，央行的资金龙头仍未触及企业和股票，因此改善更多是承诺而非兑现。 香港同样被贴上滞胀标签，但受美元牵制。美元走强、联系汇率承压，意味着全球资金外流，使香港比内地更加脆弱。
