Macro Regime Dashboard

Goldilocks ?The market 'weather'. Four regimes based on whether growth and inflation are rising or falling: GOLDILOCKS (green) = growth up, inflation down — historically the friendliest for stocks. REFLATION (yellow) = both heating up — favors energy, banks, industrials. STAGFLATION (red) = inflation up while growth rolls over — historically the most dangerous. GROWTH SCARE (blue) = both falling — favors defensives and bonds. The label changes only after 7 straight days of evidence (or one extreme shock day), so it doesn't flip-flop. STABLE ?The transition radar — is the current regime ending? STABLE: no warning signs, trust the label. WEAKENING: 2+ warning signals (e.g. fewer stocks participating, credit getting nervous) — stop adding regime-dependent bets. TRANSITIONING: 3+ warnings — the regime is probably ending before the label flips; reduce risk now. NEW REGIME: the label just changed this week. signal agreement 51% ?How many of the underlying indicators point the same way. Above ~45%: well-supported, trust it. 30-45%: moderate. Below 30%: the indicators disagree — expect choppy markets and trust the transition radar more than the regime label. Fed liquidity neutral ?Money the Fed system is adding to or draining from markets (balance sheet minus reverse-repo minus Treasury account, 4-week trend). EXPANDING has historically been the single most reliable tailwind for stocks in our backtest (~73% positive months). CONTRACTING is a persistent headwind. NEUTRAL: flat, no signal. cycle mid ?Rough position in the multi-year business cycle, estimated from the yield curve, credit spreads and market breadth. EARLY: recovering after stress — usually the best risk/reward. MID: expansion cruising. LATE: curve inverted/flattening, credit calm but breadth thinning — enjoy it but keep one eye on the exit.
