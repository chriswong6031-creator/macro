Goldilocks ?The market 'weather' right now — NOT a calendar quarter. Four regimes based on whether growth and inflation are rising or falling: GOLDILOCKS (green) = growth up, inflation down — historically the friendliest for stocks. REFLATION (yellow) = both heating up — favors energy, banks, industrials. STAGFLATION (red) = inflation up, growth rolling over — historically the most dangerous. GROWTH SCARE (blue) = both falling — favors defensives and bonds. The label changes only after 7 straight days of evidence (or one extreme shock day). TRANSITIONING: 3+ warnings — the regime is probably ending before the label flips; reduce risk now.
