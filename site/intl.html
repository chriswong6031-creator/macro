Method & honest limits方法与诚实声明

  • All keyless: equity indices & FX via Yahoo; macro via FRED OECD/ECB CSV; the stock universe via iShares UCITS holdings files.全部免密钥：股指与汇率来自 Yahoo；宏观来自 FRED OECD/ECB CSV；个股范围来自 iShares UCITS 持仓文件。
  • USD performance rebuilds each price index in dollars (index × FX, or ÷ FX for USD-quoted pairs like USD/JPY); the equity and currency legs are both measured on the same calendar and sum exactly to the USD return. These are PRICE-return indices (no dividends) — fine for cross-market comparison, not a total-return claim.美元表现将每个价格指数以美元重建（指数 × 汇率，USD 报价对如美元／日元则 ÷ 汇率）；股票腿与货币腿在同一日历上计量，且精确相加等于美元收益。这些是价格指数（不含股息）—— 适合跨市场对比，非全收益主张。
  • US-vs-world rotation & correlation use the S&P 500 PRICE index for an apples-to-apples comparison with the other price indices. Relative strength is a focus lens, not a scored signal.美国对全球轮动与相关性采用标普500价格指数，以与其他价格指数同口径对比。相对强弱是聚焦视角，非评分信号。
  • Regime / recession / drawdown / bubble reads are descriptive and history-anchored — no forward edge is claimed and none drives a scored buy/sell.周期／衰退／回撤／泡沫读数均为描述性且以历史锚定 — 不主张前瞻优势，且不驱动评分买卖。
  • CPI: FRED OECD series are discontinued for Japan (2021) and lag for the UK/Korea — shown with as-of dates and dropped from scoring when stale. Eurozone uses current Eurostat HICP.CPI：日本（2021 年）FRED OECD 序列已停更，英国／韩国滞后 — 标注截至日期并在过时时退出评分。欧元区采用当前 Eurostat HICP。
  • Taiwan has the thinnest keyless macro coverage (no yields/CPI on FRED) — flagged data-limited; its read leans on the equity tape. The liquidity impulse is ECB-only (the one clean keyless weekly CB series).台湾的免密钥宏观覆盖最薄（FRED 无收益率/CPI）— 标注数据有限；其读数依赖股市行情。流动性脉冲仅含欧央行（唯一干净的免密钥周度央行序列）。
  • No keyless implied-vol (VSTOXX / Nikkei-VI / VKOSPI) is available, so equity risk uses realised vol + own-history extension.无免密钥隐含波动率（VSTOXX / 日经VI / VKOSPI），故股市风险采用已实现波动率 + 自身历史扩张度。
