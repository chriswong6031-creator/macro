Read it correctly如何正确阅读

A record of what was said — not a market call.这是对已说内容的记录，而非市场判断。

This is a transcript-only, historically incomplete publication. Releases, filings, slides, consensus and price reaction are visibly marked as not joined; lower-evidence packets remain held instead of being inferred.这是仅基于电话会文本且历史覆盖尚不完整的发布。新闻稿、披露文件、演示材料、一致预期和股价反应均明确标注为未接入；证据不足的数据包会被暂缓，而非由推断补齐。
