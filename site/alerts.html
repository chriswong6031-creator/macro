How this board ranks — and why it refuses to fake a hit-rate本看板如何排序 — 以及为何拒绝编造命中率
Triage priority (0-100)分诊优先级 (0-100)
A transparent sum of four parts: conviction (act 40 / watch 22 / context 8), severity (critical 30 / major 18 / minor 6), recency (fresh 20 → old 2), and cross-asset agreement (+10 confirm, −6 diverge). It tells you what to LOOK at first. It is explicitly NOT a probability the call is correct.四部分的透明加总：信念（执行40／观察22／背景8）、严重度（关键30／重要18／次要6）、新鲜度（新20→旧2）、跨资产一致性（佐证+10、背离−6）。它告诉你先看什么，明确不是判断正确的概率。
Severity you can trust — corroboration, not volume可信的严重度 — 靠佐证，而非音量
Each engine has its own 'high/medium' scale, so a single-name flag can read as loud as a credit blow-out. We re-tier every alert by its TRUE cross-asset importance, then cap it back down unless something corroborates it — a measured edge, agreement from the cross-asset tape, or true act-tier weight. A severity band is only ever LOWERED, never inflated, so nothing shouts louder than it has earned.每个引擎有自己的“高／中”刻度，使单一个股信号看起来与信用崩溃一样响。我们按真正的跨资产重要性重新分级，再予以回调 — 除非有佐证：可测边际、跨资产盘面的一致性，或真正的执行级权重。严重度只会被下调，绝不虚高，因此没有信号会喊得超过它应得的音量。
Re-fired is a count, not a streak“重复触发”是次数，不是连续天数
A flag that keeps coming back is worth seeing, so we keep the fire count and the span between the first and last firing instead of de-duplicating them away. But an event log can only prove WHEN something fired — never that the condition held continuously in between. Three firings scattered across 29 days are not a 29-day condition, so the card says 're-fired 3× across 29d' and the word 'persisting' is reserved for a source that actually verifies current-state continuity. Recurrence is also no longer allowed to corroborate a severity band: it is shown, it does not rank.反复出现的信号值得关注，因此我们保留触发次数以及首末触发之间的跨度，而非直接去重丢弃。但事件日志只能证明某事在何时触发 — 无法证明该状态在此期间持续存在。分散在 29 天里的三次触发并不构成一个持续 29 天的状态，因此卡片显示“重复触发 3 次，历时 29 天”，而“持续中”一词仅保留给真正验证了当前状态连续性的数据源。重复触发也不再被用作严重度的佐证：它只作展示，不参与排序权重。
Partial evidence is not a calm tape证据不完整不等于盘面平静
Every source is read with an explicit outcome: it read fine, it read fine and had nothing to say, it has no store yet, or it could not be read at all. Those are not the same thing. If a consequential family — macro, bonds, commodity, the BTC risk regime — cannot be read, its alerts do not simply vanish into a quieter-looking board: the page says which evidence is missing and withholds the overall stance and the pressure score entirely. A missing risk feed must never be able to make the product look safer, and we do not substitute an invented cautious number either.每个数据源都带有明确的读取结果：读取正常、读取正常但无内容、尚无存储文件，或完全无法读取。这些并不等同。若关键证据类别 — 宏观、债券、大宗商品、比特币风险机制 — 无法读取，其警报不会悄然消失、使看板显得更平静：页面会指明缺失的证据类别，并完全不给出总体立场与压力分数。缺失的风险数据源绝不能让产品看起来更安全，我们也不会用臆造的保守数字来代替。
Measured edge, or honest silence可测边际，或坦诚沉默
A hit-rate / IC / Deflated-Sharpe appears ONLY where the validation layer actually measured that signal family (the recession composite, the regime quad, the BTC risk regime, the commodity risk index). For condition-based alerts never backtested as timing signals, we say 'documented, not backtested' rather than invent a number.只有当验证层确实度量过该信号族（衰退综合、机制四象限、比特币风险机制、商品风险指数）时，才显示命中率／IC／去偏夏普。对于从未作为择时信号回测的条件型警报，我们标注“有据可查，但未回测”，而非编造数字。
