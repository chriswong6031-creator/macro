Triage priority (0-100)分诊优先级 (0-100)
A transparent sum of four parts: conviction (act 40 / watch 22 / context 8), severity (critical 30 / major 18 / minor 6), recency (fresh 20 → old 2), and cross-asset agreement (+10 confirm, −6 diverge). It tells you what to LOOK at first. It is explicitly NOT a probability the call is correct.四部分的透明加总：信念（执行40／观察22／背景8）、严重度（关键30／重要18／次要6）、新鲜度（新20→旧2）、跨资产一致性（佐证+10、背离−6）。它告诉你先看什么，明确不是判断正确的概率。
Severity you can trust — corroboration, not volume可信的严重度 — 靠佐证，而非音量
Each engine has its own 'high/medium' scale, so a single-name flag can read as loud as a credit blow-out. We re-tier every alert by its TRUE cross-asset importance, then cap it back down unless something corroborates it — a measured edge, agreement from the cross-asset tape, or genuine persistence (it kept firing for days). A severity band is only ever LOWERED, never inflated, so nothing shouts louder than it has earned.每个引擎有自己的“高／中”刻度，使单一个股信号看起来与信用崩溃一样响。我们按真正的跨资产重要性重新分级，再予以回调 — 除非有佐证：可测边际、跨资产盘面的一致性，或真实的持续性（连日反复触发）。严重度只会被下调，绝不虚高，因此没有信号会喊得超过它应得的音量。
Persistence is signal持续性即信号
A flag that re-fires every day for a week is stickier than a one-off — a plain de-duplicated list throws that away. We keep the fire count and streak so a persisting alert earns its place, and show it on the card ('fired 9× over 22d').连续一周每天触发的信号比一次性的更具黏性 — 普通去重列表会将其丢弃。我们保留触发次数与连续天数，使持续型警报凭此立足，并在卡片上显示（“22天内触发9次”）。
Measured edge, or honest silence可测边际，或坦诚沉默
A hit-rate / IC / Deflated-Sharpe appears ONLY where the validation layer actually measured that signal family (the recession composite, the regime quad, the BTC risk regime, the commodity risk index).
