How to read this · and what it is not如何解读 · 以及它不是什么

This board reads the bond market HEALTH-FIRST: what the Treasury curve, corporate credit, real yields, rates volatility and funding plumbing say about economic health, regime and cycle position — not a "trade the bond" vector. The health score is a transparent composite of the already-MEASURED recession-risk and drawdown-risk gauges (engine.conditions) plus the credit, rates-vol and funding stress reads.本面板以“健康”为先解读债券市场：美国国债曲线、公司信用、实际利率、利率波动与资金管道对经济健康、周期状态与所处阶段的判读——而非“交易债券”的向量。健康度评分是已测得的衰退风险与回撤风险（engine.conditions），加上信用、利率波动与资金压力读数的透明综合。

Honesty bar.诚信底线。 Thresholds (curve, OAS distress, MOVE bands) are documented practitioner levels, NOT tuned to a backtest. scripts/calibrate_bonds.py MEASURES each leg vs strictly-forward S&P drawdowns + NBER recession, split-half (with a forward-window embargo around the split, and CONFIRMED requiring BOTH halves to be meaningful, not just sign-stable): drawdown-risk and rates-vol are CONFIRMED, recession-risk and credit are DIRECTIONAL (strong pre-2013/2008, faded through QE), funding-plumbing is CONTEXT (it rarely fires, so it rarely discriminates). The composite is CONFIRMED — but, honestly, it does NOT beat the drawdown-risk leg alone (≈ it), so treat it as an explainable synthesis, not a better single predictor. The health-score weighting now USES these measured (verdict-scaled) weights, auto-refreshed each weekly calibration (config prior as fallback). All alerts are CONTEXT.阈值（曲线、OAS困境、MOVE分带）为有据的从业者水平，未对回测调参。scripts/calibrate_bonds.py 对每个分项相对严格向前的标普回撤与NBER衰退做前后半段测量（在分割点周围设前向窗口隔离带，且“已确认”要求两半段均有意义、而非仅同号）：回撤风险与利率波动为已确认，衰退风险与信用为有方向（2013年前/2008强、QE期间减弱），资金管道为仅背景（极少触发，故极少有区分力）。综合指标已确认 — 但诚实地说，它并不优于单独的回撤风险分项（≈相当），故应视为可解释的综合，而非更优的单一预测。健康度权重现采用这些已测得（按判定缩放）的权重，每周校准自动刷新（以配置先验为后备）。所有警报皆为背景。
