Fresh Stage 2 · early in an advance新进上升期 · 上升初段 ? What this board is
Stocks that recently crossed into Stage 2 (an advance) and are 10 weeks or less in — the freshest breakouts. Ordered by a ranking aid (not a trade signal) that blends how fresh the move is, how strong the 30-week line is turning, volume confirmation, and extra confirmation from the independent T1–T3 confluence cascade. Relative strength and earnings tone are shown for context; neither is a screen condition — relative strength added no measured edge in testing. This board ranks nothing you trade on and feeds no sizing. 本看板的含义
近期刚进入阶段2（上升期）、进入不超过10周的股票 — 最新鲜的突破。按排序参考（非交易信号）排列，综合突破新鲜度、30周线转强力度、成交量确认，以及来自独立 T1–T3 汇聚体系的额外确认。相对强度与财报语气仅作背景展示；两者都不是筛选条件 — 相对强度在测试中未带来可测边际。本看板不排序任何可交易信号，也不参与仓位计算。

The product — freshest breakouts, ranked. Not buy signals; a place to start your own work.核心产品 — 最新突破，已排序。非买入信号，仅为自研起点。

Earnings call desk财报电话会台 ? Context, not a gate
A language model reads each fresh Stage 2 name's latest earnings call and summarises the tone plus one thing that stood out on each side. It is shown alongside the stage — it does not move the ranking and is kept out of any signal until it earns its place on a shadow ledger over time. A model's read is not a fact about the company. 背景，而非门槛
语言模型判读每只新进上升期股票的最新财报电话会，总结语气及正反两面各一处要点。它与阶段并列展示 — 不影响排序，在长期通过影子台账验证前不计入任何信号。模型的判读并非关于公司的事实。

Sector stage weather行业阶段天气 ? Context, not a gate
The share of each sector's stocks that are in Stage 2 (an advance), and whether that share is rising or falling. Useful for seeing where the advance is broad. It is deliberately not a screen condition: industry strength inside the breakout screen flattened its edge in testing, so it stays as context only. 背景，而非门槛
各行业中处于阶段2（上升期）股票的占比，以及该占比在上升还是下降。有助于看清上升在哪里更广泛。它刻意不作为筛选条件：将行业强度纳入突破筛选会在测试中削平其边际，故仅作背景保留。
