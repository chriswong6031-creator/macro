📚Why this is context, not a prediction为何这是背景而非预测methodology方法▶

The honest thesis诚实的论点

The day-1 IPO “pop” is genuinely predictable out-of-sample (Huang–Jiang–Li–Wu; Çolak–Fu–Hasan) — its strongest input is the bookbuilding price revision, finalised the night before trading. But it is measured from the rationed offer price and the opening trade already prices it in (Ritter–Welch), so without an offer-price allocation (which retail and small funds structurally cannot get) it is NOT capturable. Held from the first close, IPOs underperform (Ritter: median 3-year return ≈ −26%). So this page never predicts pops — it reads whether the window is open and shows what buying new issues in the secondary market has actually returned.新股首日“爆涨”在样本外确实可预测（Huang–Jiang–Li–Wu；Çolak–Fu–Hasan）—— 其最强输入是簿记建档中的定价修正，在交易前夜才敲定。但它是相对被配售的发行价计量的，开盘价已将其计入（Ritter–Welch），因此没有发行价配售（散户与小型基金结构上拿不到）便无法捕获。若从首日收盘持有，新股长期跑输（Ritter：3 年中位回报约 −26%）。故本页从不预测爆涨 —— 它判断窗口是否开启，并展示在二级市场买入新股的真实回报。

US vs Hong Kong美国 vs 香港

Hong Kong is structurally more retail-tradeable — a mandatory public-offer tranche with a clawback, a published over-subscription multiple, and a grey market that trades the night before listing. But the keyless data for those deal-level demand signals is currently broken/blocked, so the HK shelf shows the liquidity backdrop (southbound flow, HIBOR, the HKD peg, HK risk appetite) rather than a deal signal.香港在结构上更适合散户参与 —— 强制公开发售部分与回拨机制、公开的超额认购倍数，以及上市前夜交易的暗盘。但这些交易级需求信号的免密钥数据目前失效/受阻，故香港栏目展示流动性背景（南向资金、HIBOR、港元联系汇率、香港风险偏好），而非交易信号。
