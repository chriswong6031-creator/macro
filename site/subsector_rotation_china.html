🌀 China Subsector Rotation中国子行业轮动

An A-share rotation map, read like the US desk. Toggle between Subsectors — the 同花顺 (Tonghuashun) concept boards, filtered to the ~233 reliable ones — and Themes — our curated A-share thematic baskets. It plots relative strength (vs the median basket) against its velocity, screens for baskets heating up early, and shows what leaders are rotating into and out of. Relative strength is cross-sectional (not vs CSI 300 directly); it measures the velocity of relative strength, not nominal return. A breadth/rotation lens, not a buy list.A股轮动图，读法与美国台相同。可在「子行业」——同花顺概念板块（筛选出约 233 个可靠概念）——与「主题」——我们精选的 A 股主题篮子——之间切换。它以相对强度（相对中位数篮子）对其变化速度作图，筛选早期升温的篮子，并展示龙头资金正在轮入/轮出的方向。相对强度为横截面口径（非直接对沪深300）；衡量的是相对强度的变化速度，而非名义涨跌幅。这是广度/轮动视角，并非买入清单。

⟲ China Rotation Events中国轮动事件

First-class leadership handoffs across curated A-share baskets: blowoff-crash on one basket leg + fresh-low turn on a peer leg + pair ratio confirming. Port of the US Rotation Command detector (RC-R14). Display-tier context with receipts — ledgered, expected-NULL, never a buy list. Binding kills: no cycle-position confluence, no A-share reversal gating by rotation state, no Connect flow as a detector input.精选A股篮子间的领导权交接事件：某篮子冲顶崩落 + 同类篮子自新低回升 + 两者比值确认。美国 Rotation Command 检测器的 A 股移植（RC-R14）。展示层上下文（含依据）——已入账、预期无效应声明，绝非买入清单。约束条款：不做周期位置叠加，不以轮动状态门控A股反转信号，沪深港通资金不作为检测器输入。
