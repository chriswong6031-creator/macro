⚖️ Risk budgeting across the six markets六大市场的风险预算 ?If you expressed all six market views as one book, how should they be weighted? Equal-weight (1/6 each), inverse-vol (more in the calm markets), and equal-risk-contribution (ERC — each market contributes the SAME share of portfolio risk). The 'risk' column is each market's share of total portfolio variance: equal-weight quietly concentrates risk in the most volatile market; ERC equalizes it. This is the honest 'size as uncorrelated bets' weighting — a reference, not a trade list.若把全部六个市场观点合成一个组合，该如何配权？等权（各 1/6）、逆波动率（在平静市场配得更多），以及等风险贡献（ERC — 每个市场对组合风险贡献相同份额）。'风险'列是各市场对组合总方差的占比：等权会悄悄把风险集中在波动最大的市场；ERC 将其均衡。这是诚实的'按不相关押注定量'配权 — 仅作参考，非交易清单。

Equal-weighting all 6 market views puts 38% of portfolio RISK in one market (vs 17% if truly balanced); ERC rebalances to ~equal risk (heaviest weight: Dollar 0.43). Diversification ratio 1.69.

🧮 Factor IC scorecard — leak-free (point-in-time)因子 IC 记分卡 — 无前视（时点） ?Each equity factor's Information Coefficient: the cross-sectional rank correlation of its score vs forward returns, per quarter, built point-in-time (EDGAR as-of fundamentals + prices truncated to the date) so no factor ever sees a not-yet-filed report. IC-IR is mean/vol (the signal-to-noise); t_HAC is a Newey-West t-stat (overlapping windows autocorrelate); q_FDR is the Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery rate ACROSS the factor panel. Judge survivors vs ~0 — post point-in-time fix the spreads are honestly weak.每个股票因子的信息系数：其分数对未来回报的横截面秩相关，按季度、以时点方式构建（EDGAR 截至日基本面 + 截断至当日的价格），因此任何因子都不会看到尚未提交的报表。IC-IR 是均值／波动（信噪比）；t_HAC 是 Newey-West t 统计量（重叠窗口存在自相关）；q_FDR 是跨因子面板的 Benjamini-Hochberg 错误发现率。以约 0 来评判幸存者 — 时点修正后价差确实偏弱。
