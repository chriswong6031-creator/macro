⚖️ Risk budgeting across the six markets六大市场的风险预算 ?If you expressed all six market views as one book, how should they be weighted? Equal-weight (1/6 each), inverse-vol (more in the calm markets), and equal-risk-contribution (ERC — each market contributes the SAME share of portfolio risk). The 'risk' column is each market's share of total portfolio variance: equal-weight quietly concentrates risk in the most volatile market; ERC equalizes it. This is the honest 'size as uncorrelated bets' weighting — a reference, not a trade list.若把全部六个市场观点合成一个组合，该如何配权？等权（各 1/6）、逆波动率（在平静市场配得更多），以及等风险贡献（ERC — 每个市场对组合风险贡献相同份额）。'风险'列是各市场对组合总方差的占比：等权会悄悄把风险集中在波动最大的市场；ERC 将其均衡。这是诚实的'按不相关押注定量'配权 — 仅作参考，非交易清单。

🧮 Factor IC scorecard — leak-free (point-in-time)因子 IC 记分卡 — 无前视（时点） ?Each equity factor's Information Coefficient: the cross-sectional rank correlation of its score vs forward returns, per quarter, built point-in-time (EDGAR as-of fundamentals + prices truncated to the date) so no factor ever sees a not-yet-filed report.
