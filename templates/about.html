ABOUT

MastermindX

MastermindX is an AI-powered market intelligence platform for active investors — graded stock signals, options flow, macro regime dashboards, deep research and an AI analyst, rebuilt nightly.

WHAT IT IS
A market intelligence platform. Dashboards, graded stock signals, options flow and full-length research — rebuilt every night and published in English and Chinese.
WHO IT'S FOR
Active investors and traders who want the reasoning behind a number, not just the number.
MARKETS
US, China, Hong Kong, Canada and international equities, plus rates, currencies, commodities and crypto for context.
UPDATED
Every night. The engines re-run, check each other's output, and republish before the next session opens.
WHAT IT IS NOT
Market research and data — not personalized investment advice. We do not know your circumstances and we never tell you what to buy.

How the research is built

Every night a chain of engines rebuilds the whole picture — prices, breadth, flows, filings and macro releases — and each stage checks the stage before it. A result that does not survive the check is marked on the page rather than quietly dropped.

AI writes the explanations and answers your questions. It does not create signals or scores — those come from the engines, and the AI may only describe what the engines produced.

Read the methodology →

What MastermindX is not

MastermindX publishes market research, data and tools. It is not a broker, it does not manage money, and nothing on the site is personalized investment advice: we do not know your circumstances, your time horizon or your tolerance for loss. What you do with the research is your decision.
