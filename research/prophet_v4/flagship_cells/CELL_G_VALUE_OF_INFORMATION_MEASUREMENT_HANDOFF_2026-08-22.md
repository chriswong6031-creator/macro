# 0. Observable mission

Design the scientific acceptance system that can answer, for **every new Prophet intelligence family or head**:

> **Did this information make Prophet discover useful opportunities earlier, prioritize them better, avoid traps, reduce late/chased surfaces, or improve the Chairman's research workflow—over a lawful forward tape and beyond simpler baselines?**

The result must let Sol say one of four things with evidence:

1. **Promote** — the family has earned a bounded form of ranking/priority authority.
2. **Keep context-only** — useful explanation/research information, predictive lift not proven.
3. **Accrue longer** — construct is plausible but sample/coverage is insufficient.
4. **Reject/demote** — construction adds no incremental value, leaks, delays candidates, or worsens path quality.

This cell does not create another grader or scoreboard. It designs how the existing Eval/Fusion/Prophet measurement systems should test the flagship expansion.

---

# 1. Why this is strategically load-bearing

A company can accumulate dozens of impressive-looking intelligence features and still become worse at finding opportunity.

The most dangerous failure mode for Mastermind is **confirmation intelligence**: a feature looks accurate because it fires after the market has already moved. It raises precision while destroying opportunity.

The Chairman's actual objective is not “highest future return classifier accuracy.” It is closer to:

> **Find a large share of eventual high-quality opportunities while they are still early and actionable, rank the best ones near the top, avoid fragile/false-bounce traps, and explain the decision well enough to improve real research.**

Therefore Cell G must make **earliness, actionability, path quality and incremental value** first-class evaluation dimensions.

The moat is the accumulating empirical map of which information families help which candidate species, at which stage, with which other evidence, and at what latency.

# 3. North-star construct: Early Actionable Winner Capture

The cell must formalize a flagship north star, provisionally named **Early Actionable Winner Capture (EAWC)**.

This is not necessarily one scalar. The research should determine whether a dashboard/vector is more honest than a composite.

Core idea:

> A future strong outcome is most valuable when Mastermind surfaces it **before most of the move**, while deterministic entry is still open, with acceptable path risk and enough evidence to justify human attention.

A model that surfaces the same winner 10 days later after +25% price appreciation is not equally successful.

# 4. Required metric architecture

The research must define each metric mathematically, specify denominator, clocks, exclusions and failure cases.

## 4.1 Discovery / earliness

Investigate metrics such as:

- **winner recall before move fraction X** — percentage of eventual winners surfaced before 20/30/50% of their evaluation-window favorable move occurred;
- **lead time vs baseline** — sessions/hours earlier than V3/technical-confirmation/reference expert;
- **first-surface anchor distance** — return/ATR distance from structural anchor at first valid surface;
- **first-surface availability** — proportion first observed while `ENTRY_OPEN` vs wait/chased/unavailable;
- **opportunity-loss rate** — strong outcomes whose first high-priority surface occurred only after deterministic opportunity closed;
- **time from first knowable evidence to first Prophet surface** — separates source latency from inference latency.

Do not optimize these without controlling false positives/review burden.

## 4.2 Ranking / prioritization

Potential metrics:

- NDCG@K;
- precision@K;
- strong-outcome recall@K;
- average/median rank of future winners;
- rank IC where meaningful;
- top-K turnover/stability;
- concentration by theme/species/market cap;
- probability that a new family merely reorders ties without incremental outcome lift.

Use listwise metrics for a listwise product. AUC over all rows may be misleading when the Chairman only reviews the top 10–30.

## 4.3 Path quality

Required outcome dimensions:

- MFE;
- MAE;
- realized/frozen R;
- time to payoff;
- stop/invalidation incidence;
- false-bounce rate;
- drawdown before payoff;
- failure tail severity;
- availability duration before payoff;
- gap/event risk when relevant.

The research should test whether a family improves return but worsens MAE/path survival, and keep that trade visible.

## 4.4 Actionability / chase prevention

Measure:

- percent of top-ranked names that are actually `ENTRY_OPEN`;
- percent correctly withheld because thesis is strong but price is chased;
- subsequent pullback re-entry rate;
- avoided late-entry drawdown;
- amount of alpha lost by enforcing availability vs risk/chase avoided.

**Availability is not a label for the intelligence model to predict.** It is an independent deterministic state used to evaluate useful surface timing.

## 4.5 Product / research value

The flagship product is a research OS, not only a backtest.

Potential human-value metrics:

- number of candidates requiring review per session;
- percentage of high-priority cards with a complete “why now” explanation;
- dossier-open / watch / promote / pass actions;
- review time;
- repeat revisits;
- manual override/contradiction rate;
- alert usefulness;
- candidate abandonment because information is insufficient;
- whether receipts/counterexamples change the final research decision.

Treat behavior as product evidence, not automatically as ground-truth alpha labels.

# 5. Define “winner” and avoid label theater

The cell must not assume a single winner threshold.

Research several target families:

### Outcome strength

Examples: thresholded R/MFE over H10/H21/H42/H63 or continuous graded outcomes.

### Path-adjusted outcome

Strong MFE with extreme MAE may be a poor research opportunity.

### Opportunity relevance

A stock that gains 40% after being `CHASED` at first surface is evidence about thesis quality, but not necessarily successful actionable discovery.

### Time-to-payoff

A slow 6-month winner and an early 10-day winner may serve different Prophet lanes.

The result should likely preserve multiple labels/heads rather than collapse them into one “correct” bit.

# 6. Per-lobe Value of Information protocol

For each intelligence family/head, design a preregistered **incremental value** study.

## 6.1 Required baseline hierarchy

Possible baselines:

1. current production ranking/priority law;
2. same candidate pool with the family removed (LOFO);
3. boring deterministic baseline using only pre-existing stable features;
4. shuffled/placebo version of the family;
5. context-matched baseline where needed.

The research must state which comparison answers which question.

## 6.2 Same-tape principle

Where possible, compare alternatives on the **same candidate episodes and same availability state**. If adding a family changes candidate intake, separately evaluate discovery effect and ranking effect.

Do not let a different denominator make one challenger look better.

## 6.3 Coverage stratification

Report:

- full candidate population;
- covered-only cohort;
- missing/not-covered cohort;
- species/theme/liquidity strata;
- time/regime strata only when estimable.

A family that looks powerful on 4% of names must not be presented as universal.

## 6.4 LOFO / ablation

For each family:

- remove family while leaving all else constant;
- measure top-K changes;
- measure winner recall/earliness/path changes;
- identify candidates whose rank changed materially;
- inspect whether another correlated family substitutes.

LOFO is not proof of causal independence but is useful for marginal system value.

## 6.5 Complementarity

Investigate whether pairs/groups create value beyond individual families. Guard against combinatorial fishing.

Require preregistered interaction hypotheses or hierarchical shrinkage; do not test thousands of combinations and cherry-pick winners.

# 7. Lead-time preservation gate

This is a binding flagship requirement.

A new family must not be promoted merely because it increases precision if it systematically fires later.

The research must define a **lead-time preservation frontier**. Example conceptual acceptance:

- precision/top-K improves with no material degradation in median first-surface lead time; OR
- a modest lead-time cost buys a large, preregistered reduction in false-bounce/MAE and belongs to a named conservative lane; OR
- family fails promotion for the primary early-discovery lane.

Report the full tradeoff curve instead of hiding it inside one metric.

Potential measurements:

- median sessions of lead lost/gained;
- percent of winners first surfaced after >X% of move;
- percent of high-priority surfaces no longer `ENTRY_OPEN`;
- earliness-adjusted precision.

# 8. Estimability and sample-size law

The cell must design a reusable decision tree:

1. Is the construct defined before outcomes?
2. Is PIT replay possible?
3. Is coverage sufficient?
4. Is N sufficient for the proposed statistic/model?
5. Are outcomes independent enough, or clustered by date/theme/event?
6. Is there a valid baseline/control?
7. Has the comparison family/horizon matured?
8. Does multiple testing materially inflate false discovery?

Required practices to evaluate:

- date/session clustered bootstrap;
- block bootstrap where serial dependence exists;
- Wilson/Beta-binomial intervals for proportions where appropriate;
- hierarchical shrinkage for sparse species/families;
- minimum effective sample size;
- confidence intervals rather than threshold theater;
- predeclared “UNESTIMABLE” output.

The E3-A Earnings refusal to invent a numeric usefulness bar from N=7 is the correct cultural precedent: **small N must be allowed to remain small N.**

# 9. Multiple testing / researcher-degrees-of-freedom law

The flagship expansion creates many possible families, heads, horizons, species and interactions. Without discipline Mastermind will mine noise.

The research must propose:

- preregistered primary metrics and secondary diagnostics;
- frozen horizons;
- frozen family versions;
- test-era registration;
- correction for multiple comparisons where many constructs are tested;
- separation of exploratory discovery from confirmatory promotion;
- immutable forward test cohorts;
- no choosing the favorable control/estimator after results;
- explicit negative/placebo features.

If a feature is redesigned after seeing outcomes, it starts a **new version/evidence clock** rather than inheriting old proof.

# 10. Promotion ladder

Design a ladder that reconciles with current Eval/Fusion vocabulary. Candidate conceptual stages:

1. `OBSERVED` — source/construct exists.
2. `CONTEXT_ONLY` — useful product context, no predictive authority.
3. `ACCRUING` — frozen construct collecting forward data.
4. `REPLAY_SUPPORTED` — PIT/walk-forward retrospective evidence, still no live authority.
5. `FORWARD_SHADOW` — prospective challenger.
6. `PROMOTED_FOR_PRIORITY/RANK` — bounded earned influence.
7. **Trade/size/entry authority remains separate and much harder.**

Do not mint these literal enums if existing canonical vocabulary differs. The research must map to current Eval/Fusion states.

Promotion requirements should include:

- definition/version frozen;
- coverage threshold;
- minimum N/effective N;
- non-degrading earliness gate;
- metric lift with uncertainty;
- path-risk check;
- species/regime concentration check;
- negative-control sanity;
- forward shadow period;
- Sol/CEO adjudication where architecture requires it.

Demotion must also be possible if forward performance decays or a source changes.

# 11. Counterfactual / control hierarchy

Do not impose one control on every construct.

The research should classify candidate evaluations:

### Ranking challenger

Same-tape incumbent/challenger paired comparison.

### Security outcome

Benchmark residual, sector residual, theme residual or matched peer depending construct and owner law.

### Theme/transmission claim

Common-theme/sector baseline + non-edge controls.

### Catalyst claim

Matched event/history or issuer-specific expectation baseline.

### Product behavior

A/B or within-user workflow comparisons where ethical/practical, otherwise descriptive telemetry.

### Descriptive context family

May never need an alpha counterfactual; measure coverage/usefulness instead.

Control feasibility itself is evidence and must be reported.

# 13. Failure states / adversarial cases

The research must explicitly test or design for:

- feature version changed mid-race;
- outcome label revised after correction;
- candidate missing because publication failed;
- only winners are priceable (selection bias);
- same market day creates highly correlated observations;
- theme/event cluster drives apparent N;
- family covers only liquid/large-cap names;
- backfill sneaks into prospective cohort;
- availability state computed with future price;
- metrics calculated only on candidates the new model itself selected;
- missing rows excluded from denominator in a way that improves coverage/performance;
- family improves future return but delays first surface;
- family improves top-K by concentrating one theme;
- control universe changed after results;
- many hypotheses tested and only best published;
- UI telemetry interpreted as alpha proof.

# 14. Required reference studies

Design at least these hypothetical/real-data study packets:

1. **Family adds true early lift:** winner surfaced earlier, top-K improves, MAE unchanged.
2. **Late confirmer:** precision improves but lead time collapses; reject for early lane.
3. **Sparse specialist:** strong covered-only effect but 8% universe coverage; context/specialist lane only.
4. **Redundant family:** looks good alone, LOFO near zero because another family carries same information.
5. **Fragility protector:** future return unchanged but MAE/false-bounce materially improves; possible risk-head value.
6. **False positive from leakage:** backtest great, PIT reconstruction fails; reject.
7. **Insufficient N:** construct coherent, confidence interval huge; accrue.
8. **Product-only value:** no alpha lift, but dramatically improves research explanation/decision speed; keep context-only.
