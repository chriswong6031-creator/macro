# 0. Research law

We study external systems for the **job they solve and the methodological shape**, not to reproduce proprietary code, hidden taxonomies, non-public data or brand identity.

For every benchmark below distinguish:

- **Observed capability** — what the public/primary source says the system does.
- **Generalizable lesson** — a product/research idea Mastermind can independently implement.
- **Do not copy** — proprietary taxonomy, model, code, licensed corpus or hidden thresholds.
- **Mastermind differentiation** — where our flagship architecture aims to go beyond the external method.
- **Owner route** — which canonical Mastermind owner/cell should absorb the lesson.

External benchmark evidence is not a build authorization.

# 1. FactSet RBICS with Revenue — quantified multi-business economic exposure

**Primary/public source:**  
https://www.factset.com/marketplace/catalog/product/factset-rbics-with-revenue

## Observed capability

FactSet describes RBICS with Revenue as normalizing company-reported business segments and mapping segment revenues into a granular multi-sector classification. It exposes primary and ancillary revenue participation rather than forcing a company into one sector, enabling screening, peer analysis, market-share trends and quantified multi-industry participation.

Public product details describe a hierarchy with multiple levels of industry depth and a global public-company universe.

## Generalizable lesson

A diversified company should not be represented by a single sector or a flat binary theme tag.

Mastermind should build one lawful segment/product economic truth substrate and derive **multi-axis exposures** from it.

Useful principles:

- segment revenue is a stronger economic denominator than mention count;
- primary and ancillary businesses both matter;
- historical exposure must respect segment revisions/restatements;
- peer sets can be derived from economic overlap rather than GICS alone;
- diversified firms need multiple exposures at once.

## Do not copy

- RBICS taxonomy;
- FactSet normalization rules;
- proprietary company mappings;
- vendor datasets.

## Mastermind differentiation

RBICS-style revenue decomposition is mostly structural exposure. Prophet needs to combine structural exposure with:

- dynamic ThemeState;
- typed transmission;
- event/catalyst changes;
- price incorporation;
- deterministic entry availability;
- prospective opportunity measurement.

## Owner route

- Cell A / GMI Theme Graph;
- Data OS / FIF / segment financial owner for canonical segment truth;
- later D5 adapter via Cell F.

# 2. MSCI Strategy Explorer / Strategy Exposure — natural-language themes mapped to segment revenue

**Primary/public source:**  
https://www.msci.com/data-and-analytics/index-data/strategy-explorer

## Observed capability

MSCI publicly describes Strategy Explorer as mapping company business segments to investment themes and calculating thematic exposure. Its Strategy Exposure methodology is described as revenue-based economic linkage between a company and an investment trend. Public FAQ text says an LLM assesses each business-segment description against in-scope/out-of-scope subtheme criteria, with multiple iterations, and exposure is calculated from revenue of matched segments.

## Generalizable lesson

Models can be useful **semantic mappers** between messy segment descriptions and a theme definition, but the denominator should come from governed economic data and the theme definition must be explicit.

Useful shape:

```text
human/governed theme definition
→ in/out criteria
→ model segment-membership proposal
→ deterministic evidence/identity checks
→ economic denominator
→ exposure + evidence/confidence
```

## Do not copy

- MSCI theme libraries;
- proprietary segment enrichment;
- proprietary prompts/iterations;
- proprietary exposure data.

## Mastermind differentiation

Mastermind should not stop at revenue exposure. It needs distinct axes such as:

- revenue;
- profit;
- capex;
- customer/supplier dependency;
- strategic optionality;
- trading sensitivity;
- attention;
- network role.

It should also maintain known-at/correction lineage and connect exposure to dynamic theme propagation and entry timing.

## Owner route

Cell A / GMI, with model-use law from the flagship masterplan.

# 4. FactSet 2026 diversified-firm industry momentum research — direct precedent for exposure-weighted response pressure

**Current research source (Aug 2026):**  
https://insight.factset.com/unlocking-industry-momentum-in-diversified-firms

**Earlier methodological context:**  
https://insight.factset.com/the-challenge-of-calculating-industry-momentum-for-diversified-firms

## Observed capability / study shape

FactSet's current 2026 public research revisits an industry-momentum signal for diversified firms using RBICS with Revenue.

The public methodology describes:

1. identifying pure-play vs diversified firms using revenue concentration;
2. constructing granular industry return benchmarks from pure plays;
3. creating a diversified company's **synthetic return** as the revenue-exposure-weighted sum of those industry benchmark returns;
4. ranking diversified firms on the synthetic signal;
5. measuring forward returns over several horizons;
6. testing enhancements such as market-excess industry returns, market-share weighting and volatility normalization.

## Why this is strategically important

This is unusually close to a bounded version of the Mastermind Theme→Dislocation thesis:

> **economic exposure × external/peer impulse → expected security response**

It shows a practical institutional baseline for translating segment economics into a synthetic market signal without claiming intrinsic value.

## Generalizable Mastermind baseline

A first Theme Transmission research baseline could be:

```text
pure_play_theme_impulse(k,t)
  = robust residual return / evidence impulse of sufficiently pure theme members

expected_theme_response(i,t)
  = Σ_k economic_exposure(i,k,t_known) × pure_play_theme_impulse(k,t)
```

Then compare:

```text
observed_security_residual(i,t)
vs
expected_theme_response(i,t)
```

This is a **research baseline**, not a production score.

## Required Mastermind improvements / falsifiers

- point-in-time exposure membership;
- no current-snapshot historical backtest;
- theme breadth / common-factor controls;
- same-sector placebo;
- minimum pure-play member N;
- robustness to equal-weight vs cap/market-share weighting;
- volatility normalization;
- lag sensitivity;
- transaction / liquidity sensitivity;
- issuer concentration;
- theme overlap / multicollinearity;
- no beneficial baseline selection after outcome read.

## Mastermind differentiation

FactSet's study is industry-momentum research. Mastermind can extend the **general job** with:

- events/expectations/materiality;
- typed transmission mechanisms;
- evidence novelty/persistence;
- price incorporation state;
- crowding/attention;
- entry availability;
- prospective value-of-information testing.

## Owner route

Cell A + Cell B + Cell G.

This should become a required benchmark baseline in their research, not optional reading.

# 5. FactSet Revere supply-chain / relationship research — point-in-time network propagation

**Public research source:**  
https://insight.factset.com/hubfs/White%20Papers/Signal_Processing_Logistics_of_Supply_Chain_Alpha_WP.pdf

## Observed capability / methodological lesson

FactSet research describes point-in-time customer/supplier and other business relationships built from filings plus broader public company information such as calls, presentations, releases and websites.

The paper emphasizes a real coverage problem: mandatory disclosure thresholds make direct quantified customer relationships sparse; broader public evidence improves graph coverage.

It also distinguishes multiple relationship classes beyond customer/supplier, including competitors.

## Generalizable lesson

A useful economic graph must preserve:

- typed relationship;
- evidence source;
- relationship validity interval;
- known-at / update clock;
- quantified share where disclosed;
- unknown share where not disclosed;
- confidence / source tier;
- correction / expiry.

The graph should not hallucinate a sales percentage because a relationship exists.

## Do not copy

- Revere data;
- proprietary relationship extraction;
- vendor relationship corpus.

## Mastermind differentiation

Mastermind should connect relationship truth to:

- current shock/ThemeState;
- mechanism-specific sign/lag hypotheses;
- expected response pressure;
- observed price incorporation;
- Prophet opportunity workflow.

## Owner route

Cell A / GMI Transmission.

# 7. RavenPack News Analytics — event taxonomy, relevance, novelty, temporal context

**Primary/public product:**  
https://www.ravenpack.com/products/edge/data/news-analytics

**Public factor description:**  
https://marketing-prod.ravenpack.com/products/edge/factors/company-news

**Public methodology discussion:**  
https://www.ravenpack.com/blog/new-ravenpack-analytics-event-detection

## Observed capability

RavenPack publicly describes large-scale news/event entity detection with:

- event/topic taxonomy;
- entity relevance;
- novelty tracking;
- temporal scoring;
- sentiment / event analytics;
- document-level and event-level aggregations.

Public research emphasizes that higher relevance and novelty filters can materially change signal quality, while reducing event volume.

## Generalizable lesson

For Mastermind evidence trajectories, distinguish:

- **event identity/type**;
- **source root**;
- **entity role**;
- **relevance**;
- **novelty**;
- **first-seen / repetition**;
- **temporal/effective date**;
- **sentiment only where the method is validated**.

Novelty is particularly useful for:

- duplicate suppression;
- “what changed?” detection;
- emergence;
- evidence-root counting.

## Important warning

A relevance/novelty/sentiment score is a text-processing construct, not market comprehension.

Even SEC disclosures about products using RavenPack algorithms explicitly discuss misclassification and scoring risk. Mastermind should never treat model-generated relevance/sentiment as automatic rank authority.

## Do not copy

- taxonomy;
- proprietary source corpus;
- scoring formulas;
- factor construction.

## Mastermind differentiation

Our focus is evidence → economics → incorporation → availability, not news sentiment by itself.

## Owner route

Cell F evidence grammar; Cell A emergent evidence; Cell G VOI; source-specific owner for actual data.

## Observed methodological role

MSCI describes equity factor models as decomposing risk/return across market, industry, style and other common factors. Public material describes stock-specific return as the part not attributable to common factor components.

## Generalizable lesson

An incorporation-gap study should not confuse:

- market rally;
- sector rally;
- factor rotation;
- theme/common basket rally;

with issuer-specific response to the evidence under study.

## Do not copy

- Barra model specifications;
- proprietary factor exposures/covariance estimates.

## Mastermind differentiation

Mastermind can build lawful public/first-party factor-neutral research baselines or use licensed data if available, but its product output is not “Barra residual.” The point is to avoid attributing generic movement to a specific information event.

# 9. Cohen & Frazzini (2008) — economic links and delayed cross-firm incorporation

**Journal source:**  
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2008.01379.x

## Research finding / relevance

The paper reports return predictability across publicly known customer–supplier links and interprets results through limited investor attention. It also examines operational co-movement and informed investor attention.

## Generalizable hypothesis for Mastermind

If a value-relevant shock hits an economically linked firm, a connected issuer may adjust with a delay, especially when the economic link is not salient to the market.

This directly motivates testing:

```text
source-company shock
× destination economic exposure
× investor-attention / crowding context
→ destination residual response lag
```

## Critical caution

A historical academic anomaly is not assumed to persist unchanged in 2026 markets.

Mastermind must re-test:

- modern era;
- broader relationship classes;
- current information speed;
- analyst/institutional attention;
- liquidity;
- theme crowding;
- realistic data known-at clocks.

## Owner route

Cell A Transmission + Cell B Incorporation + Cell G validation.

# 10. Menzly & Ozbas (2010) — market segmentation and cross-predictability

**Journal source:**  
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2010.01578.x

## Research finding / relevance

The public abstract reports cross-predictability among economically related supplier/customer industries and weaker predictability where analyst coverage / institutional ownership is higher, consistent with gradual information diffusion under market segmentation.

## Generalizable lesson

**Attention is not only a signal; it can change the rate of incorporation.**

This supports explicitly testing whether:

- high evidence + low attention → slower response;
- high evidence + saturated attention → faster incorporation;
- institutional overlap changes propagation lag.

# 15. Rights / sourcing implications

Researching a public methodology does not create rights to its data.

For any proposed implementation:

- identify first-party/public/licensed source;
- record terms/rights tier;
- do not scrape sources whose terms prohibit automation;
- do not reverse-engineer proprietary taxonomy/weights;
- do not paste vendor text/corpora into training sets without rights;
- derive original code/contracts;
- keep vendor-specific identifiers behind adapters where licensed.

If a capability fundamentally requires unavailable licensed data, return `BLOCKED_RIGHTS_OR_SOURCE` rather than fabricating a substitute.

# 16. Questions future cells should **not** repeat from scratch

The broad benchmarking answers these sufficiently for architecture purposes:

- Is multi-axis / segment-level exposure institutionally useful? **Yes.** Research the Mastermind implementation and incremental value, not the existence of the job.
- Is NLP useful for early thematic/entity/event mapping? **Yes.** The open question is governance, evidence quality and incremental value.
- Are point-in-time economic links potentially useful for propagation? **Yes.** The open question is modern Mastermind estimability and mechanism-specific edge.
- Are detailed expectations/KPIs superior to top-line event labels? **Yes.** The open question is lawful data availability and domain implementation.
- Should price response be factor-adjusted before claiming issuer-specific incorporation? **Yes.** The open question is the correct baseline hierarchy per family.
- Is novelty/relevance distinct from sentiment? **Yes.** The open question is whether those features add independent value here.
- Can information diffuse gradually across related firms/events? **Historically, yes in multiple settings.** The open question is whether the effect survives modern PIT-safe Mastermind tests.

Future sessions should spend their scarce context on those **open questions**, not re-proving the generic industry premise.

# 17. Benchmark falsification discipline

Do not use this document as “institutional firms do it, therefore it works.”

For each imported methodological concept ask:

1. Does Mastermind have lawful PIT data?
2. Can we reproduce the job with original methods?
3. Is the feature estimable in our universe?
4. Is it independent of what Prophet already knows?
5. Does it improve early/actionable outcomes prospectively?
6. Does it preserve lead time?
7. Does it generalize across eras/species or require a bounded scope?
8. Does it remain useful after realistic missingness?
9. Is its benefit product/explanation value rather than alpha?
10. What kills it?

That is the difference between benchmarking and cargo-cult architecture.

## Closing conclusion

The external landscape validates the **pieces** of the flagship idea: quantified economic exposure, AI-assisted thematic discovery, relationship networks, expectation/revision intelligence, structured event novelty, and factor-adjusted return attribution all exist as serious institutional jobs.

The Mastermind opportunity is to integrate those pieces around a stricter and more useful investor question:

> **What changed, where should it propagate, what has the market already incorporated, and is there still a lawful entry?**

That combined question — plus a prospective evidence clock proving whether each component actually improves decisions — remains the intended differentiation.
