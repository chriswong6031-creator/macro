# 1. Why this matters to the Chairman / product thesis

Prophet cannot become the strongest early candidate creator merely by adding more technical detectors. The Chairman's intended edge is that Mastermind should recognize **changes in the economic/information graph before price fully catches up**.

Theme intelligence therefore has three distinct jobs:

1. **Exposure:** identify *who is genuinely affected* by a theme and in what way.
2. **State:** identify *whether the theme itself is strengthening, weakening, broadening, concentrating or diffusing now*.
3. **Transmission:** infer *where the impulse should travel next* through a typed economic relationship.

These must remain separate. A company can have high exposure to a dormant theme. A theme can be accelerating while a highly exposed company is already fully repriced. A company can have low direct revenue exposure but high capex/supplier dependency. A trading-beta relationship can be real without proving economic dependence.

The moat is the accumulated PIT history of **exposure × theme state × transmission mechanism × lag × eventual security response**, not a static thematic taxonomy.

# 4. Complete user / machine journey

The research should design toward this journey:

## 4.1 Theme impulse begins

A canonical evidence owner observes one or more changes: capex language, order growth, policy/procurement, earnings revisions, commodity input, product adoption, sector breadth, or validated price/attention behavior.

The system records the exact knowable time and source receipts. A model may extract semantic evidence, but source-backed facts remain distinguishable from model interpretation.

## 4.2 ThemeState evaluates the theme itself

The theme may show:

- level;
- velocity;
- acceleration/deceleration;
- breadth/diffusion;
- leadership concentration;
- cross-region confirmation;
- persistence/decay;
- evidence coverage/rights/freshness.

The state does NOT yet say which stock to buy.

## 4.3 Exposure answers “who is affected?”

For each company-theme pair, expose independent axes such as:

- economic/revenue share;
- profit contribution where supportable;
- capex intensity/dependency;
- customer/end-market dependency;
- supplier/input dependency;
- management/filing attention;
- strategic/product optionality;
- observed trading sensitivity;
- network role/centrality.

Each axis has its own denominator, source, confidence and null reason. There is no forced composite.

## 4.4 Transmission answers “why should the impulse reach this company?”

The system names the mechanism and edge path, e.g.:

`hyperscaler capex acceleration → data-center build intensity → power/cooling demand → VRT`

or:

`uranium spot/term contracting + utility contracting cycle → miner realized-price/backlog economics → producer cohort`

The path must be evidence-backed and time-aware. If only co-movement is known, say co-movement—not transmission.

## 4.5 Prophet later consumes a bounded adapter

Prophet may eventually receive structured theme/transmission evidence as one family in its D5 intelligence vector. It still independently evaluates price incorporation, fragility, candidate emergence and deterministic availability.

**Strong theme + strong exposure + closed entry = strong thesis / unavailable opportunity.**

That state is mandatory.

# 5. Research program A1 — Exposure Axis V2

The session must create an **Exposure Axis Decision Table**. For every proposed axis answer:

- What real economic question does this axis answer?
- What is the subject and denominator?
- Is the source directly observed, derived, statistical or model-generated?
- What is `known_at`?
- How do restatements/corrections work?
- What makes the axis `not_applicable`, `not_covered`, `unestimable`, or `neutral`?
- Is it descriptive only or a future promotable feature?
- What historical depth exists?
- Can it be computed for the broad Prophet universe or only selected sectors?

Minimum axes to investigate:

### Economic / revenue exposure

Potential denominator: consolidated external revenue. But do not pretend segment revenue equals theme revenue. Research the mapping chain:

`reported segment/product → business activity → theme/subtheme → revenue allocation rule`.

A segment may map to multiple themes; a theme may capture only part of a segment. If subsegment allocation cannot be defended, retain an interval/qualitative classification rather than inventing a point estimate.

### Profit exposure

Revenue share can mislead when margin profiles differ. Determine whether segment operating income/gross profit can lawfully and consistently improve the exposure view. Expect sparse coverage; sparse truth is acceptable.

### Capex exposure

Useful for companies whose theme sensitivity is driven by investment rather than revenue. Examples: hyperscaler AI infrastructure spend, utility grid capex, mining growth projects. Define whether the company is **spending into** the theme or **benefiting from customer capex**—opposite economic roles.

### Customer/end-market dependency

Could be more predictive than revenue taxonomy. Requires relationship/source proof. Separate named customer concentration from inferred end-market composition.

### Supplier/input dependency

Useful for margin/capacity shocks. Must encode sign/mechanism: lower input price may benefit a downstream user while hurting the producer.

### Management attention / strategic exposure

Filing/call language is not revenue. Treat as a separate axis. Research novelty/change in attention and source-span support.

### Trading sensitivity

Residual beta/correlation can be useful but is **market behavior, not economic exposure**. Keep separate, PIT, with sample length and confidence. Do not use it to fabricate causality.

### Network role

Producer, bottleneck, enabler, distributor, buyer, substitute, etc. This may be categorical rather than numeric.

# 8. Research program A4 — Transmission Measurement

A typed edge is descriptive until evidence shows it adds useful forward information.

The cell must design experiments separating:

1. broad market return;
2. sector/common theme return;
3. same-time co-movement;
4. relationship-specific response;
5. genuinely lagged propagation.

Candidate methods to compare, in increasing complexity:

- event-window residual response;
- matched peer controls;
- panel regressions with entity/time effects;
- distributed-lag models;
- shrinkage/hierarchical estimates by mechanism;
- synthetic controls for major discrete impulses;
- survival/time-to-response analyses;
- later graph challengers only after deterministic/statistical baselines.

Mandatory falsifiers:

- randomly permuted edges;
- same-sector non-edge controls;
- dead/expired relationships;
- future edge membership leakage test;
- same-day common-shock placebo;
- current-snapshot membership backtest trap;
- leave-one-theme/company-out robustness;
- sign reversal across regimes/species when enough N exists.

If relationship-specific lift does not beat common theme/sector baselines, the edge remains explanatory context, not predictive propagation authority.

# 10. Data / time / null / correction rules specific to Cell A

## Identity grain

Company exposure belongs at canonical company/company-epoch grain; trading response belongs at security grain. A company with multiple share classes must not duplicate its economic exposure facts while security-specific trading beta remains distinct.

## Membership history

A theme/company membership must be reconstructible as known at a past date before it is used for historical evaluation. Current snapshot taxonomy cannot be retroactively treated as historical membership.

## Exposure history

An exposure estimate is tied to its source reporting period and knowable date. A 10-K filed in February cannot be treated as known in December merely because it describes the prior fiscal year.

## Corrections

Restated segment revenue or corrected relationship evidence produces a new belief/correction record; historical replay preserves what was knowable before the correction.

## Missingness

Use distinct states such as:

- `NOT_APPLICABLE` — axis concept does not apply;
- `NOT_COVERED` — source family does not cover issuer;
- `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`;
- `RIGHTS_BLOCKED`;
- `IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED`;
- `MAPPING_UNRESOLVED` — segment/activity→theme cannot be defended;
- `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`;
- `UNESTIMABLE`;
- `MEASURED_ZERO` — only when a real measured denominator supports zero.

Never coerce them to 0 exposure.

# 11. Required product reference compositions

The research artifact must show at least these five states.

## Composition 1 — direct economic beneficiary, early propagation

Theme accelerating; verified meaningful economic/customer-capex exposure; second-order peer/supplier path; target security lagging theme/peers; evidence receipts; confidence and missing axes shown.

## Composition 2 — theme member but economically weak exposure

High attention/trading beta but low/unknown direct economics. User can see why the stock trades with the theme without being told it has large revenue exposure.

## Composition 3 — strong exposure, already repriced

Exposure and theme state strong, but Prophet availability later says chased/unavailable. Theme intelligence remains bullish context; it does not force an actionable call.

## Composition 4 — contradictory axes

Revenue exposure high but theme is a cost/capex burden, or trading beta negative/unstable. System explains axes instead of averaging them.

## Composition 5 — insufficient/rights-blocked

System explicitly shows no defensible economic share because segment mapping or source rights are unavailable. It may still show structural membership/trading relationship with correct authority.
