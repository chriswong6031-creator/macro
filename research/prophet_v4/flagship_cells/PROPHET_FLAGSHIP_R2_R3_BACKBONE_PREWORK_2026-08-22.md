- current award;
- potential/option ceiling;

One-time vs multi-period vs structurally recurring, with source-backed horizon/uncertainty.

How concentrated the issuer's economics/asset/program are in the relevant event/asset/customer, only from lawful owner truth.

### `balance_sheet_dependency`

Only from Capital Structure/FIF owner evidence; no local cash-runway computation inside Cell C.

### `estimability`

`MEASURED`, `INTERVAL`, `QUALITATIVE_ONLY`, `UNESTIMABLE`, etc., plus reasons.

# 9. External/primary-source method anchors for R2/R3

These sources are method anchors, not source-right grants or predictive evidence.

## ClinicalTrials.gov

Official definitions/documentation establish that Primary Completion is final data collection for the primary outcome; estimated completion is a submitted estimate. This supports C's strict schedule-vs-outcome distinction.

## SEC materiality guidance

SAB No. 99 warns against exclusive numerical thresholds and emphasizes quantitative + qualitative context. Mastermind's issuer-materiality construct is not a legal-materiality determination, but it should inherit the anti-shortcut lesson: denominator-relative scale alone cannot capture every economically important event.

## USAspending

Official award data distinguishes obligated/current/potential amounts. C/Defense must retain those distinctions rather than map a potential ceiling to expected revenue.

## FINRA short interest

Official short-interest reporting is a periodic position snapshot with settlement/publication timing and revisions. E must not call it live flow.

## SEC 13F

Quarter-end holdings are filed with substantial publication lag. Use as slow ownership context, not current positioning.

## SEC Form 4

Transaction disclosures can be relatively prompt, but transaction code/role/holdings context determine meaning. A sale is not automatically bearish.

## Options/OCC/OIC semantics

Open interest is outstanding contract count and does not by itself identify bullish/bearish investor intent. E must distinguish observation from interpretation.

## Analogue / network / routing literature

Nearest-neighbor, economic-peer and soft mixture-of-experts methods demonstrate feasible research shapes. They do not validate Mastermind species, distances or routing. D must compare them against simple interpretable baselines and owner-specific OOS evidence.
