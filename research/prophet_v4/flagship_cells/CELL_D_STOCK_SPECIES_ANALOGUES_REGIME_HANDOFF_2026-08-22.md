# 0. Observable mission

Determine whether Prophet can become materially better by understanding **what kind of security/setup it is looking at** and by retrieving **historically comparable, point-in-time episodes**—without creating arbitrary species that merely fit past returns and without building another memory system.

The intended 10/10 experience is:

> “This is not just a software stock with a technical reversal. Its behavior and economics resemble a high-duration, capex-sensitive, event-light growth cohort; the current episode resembles 37 historical setup episodes under comparable liquidity/availability state. The analogue distribution shows high upside but unusually large early MAE. The current market regime is only shown because this specific interaction is estimable; otherwise regime remains context.”

The cell must answer three separate questions:

1. **Representation:** should issuer behavior be represented by discrete species, continuous fingerprints, hierarchical labels, or a mixture?
2. **Analogue retrieval:** how should Market Memory retrieve genuinely comparable episodes and counterexamples?
3. **Regime conditioning:** which specific context interactions are scientifically estimable enough to matter, and which broad regime ideas should stay rejected?

# 2. Chairman intent — what “stock species” should mean

The intended insight is not simply “biotech acts differently from banks.”

A useful species/fingerprint should change at least one decision-relevant conditional distribution:

- which expert family is informative;
- how quickly reversals tend to develop;
- expected MAE/MFE path;
- catalyst sensitivity;
- theme sensitivity;
- liquidity/reflexivity behavior;
- fragility/capital-dependence;
- appropriate analogue cohort.

A label that sounds descriptive but does not change any useful conditional relationship should remain metadata, not a router.

The moat is the accumulated map of **behavioral/economic identity → setup episode → expert relevance → path/outcome**, with epochs when a company changes character.

# 4. Representation research — discrete species vs fingerprints

The cell must compare at least five architectures.

## 4.1 Discrete economic/behavioral species

Examples worth testing, NOT pre-approved categories:

- binary-event biotech;
- defense/government-contract/backlog conversion;
- commodity producer;
- capital-equipment / cyclical supplier;
- high-duration growth;
- recurring-revenue mature software;
- mature compounder;
- financial balance-sheet business;
- REIT / rate-sensitive asset vehicle;
- China/HK policy-sensitive security;
- high-short/high-float-reflexive security;
- financing-dependent pre-profit company;
- asset-heavy bottleneck/enabler.

A valid category needs explicit membership criteria and a reason it changes inference.

## 4.2 Continuous fingerprint

Potential components:

- beta / residual vol;
- gap frequency;
- drawdown/reversal shape;
- trend persistence;
- earnings/event concentration;
- options/short reflexivity where covered;
- liquidity;
- sector/theme sensitivity;
- balance-sheet/capital-dependence;
- revenue cyclicality;
- commodity sensitivity;
- policy/procurement dependence.

Each component must be PIT and methodologically lawful.

## 4.3 Hierarchical representation

Example:

```text
company economic archetype
→ security behavioral fingerprint
→ current episode/setup state
```

This may be more truthful than forcing one species label.

## 4.4 Mixture / soft membership

A company can be both AI supplier and cyclical capex equipment, or biotech and financing-dependent. Investigate soft memberships only if they remain interpretable and estimable.

## 4.5 No explicit species — metric/nearest-neighbor representation

The system may get better analogues from continuous distance than from named classes. The research must allow this to win.

# 5. Species membership / epoch law

A company's behavior can change materially.

Required cases:

- pre-profit → profitable;
- diversified conglomerate spins off business;
- commodity exposure shifts;
- large new government program changes revenue mix;
- meme/reflexive episode temporarily changes trading behavior;
- acquisition changes company economics;
- ticker reuse / entity change;
- business model pivots;
- leverage/refinancing state changes.

The research must distinguish:

- stable structural archetype;
- time-varying behavioral fingerprint;
- temporary episode state;
- canonical identity epoch.

Do not back-project today's species into historical periods without evidence.

# 6. Analogue retrieval — user job

The Chairman should eventually be able to ask:

> “What previous Mastermind episodes actually looked like this one at the time, including the failures—not what later became famous winners?”

A useful analogue response should include:

- number of valid comparable episodes;
- selection criteria/distance explanation;
- distribution of MFE/MAE/R/time-to-payoff;
- nearest positive examples;
- nearest failure/counterexamples;
- coverage/clock caveats;
- which dimensions are poorly matched;
- whether current episode is outside historical support.

The first product should be descriptive. Do not convert analogues into a probability automatically.

# 8. Distance / similarity research

Compare:

### Hand-weighted interpretable distance

Good first baseline; easy to audit.

### Mahalanobis / standardized distance

Requires stable covariance and enough data.

### Learned metric

May learn what pre-outcome characteristics define similar path behavior, but risks leakage/overfit. Only after frozen training/test separation.

### Embedding distance

Could help with qualitative event/context, but must not replace structured identity/clocks and must be source/version pinned.

### Hybrid retrieval

Hard filters on identity/clock/liquidity + soft distance on fingerprint/setup.

The research must define missing-feature distance behavior. A candidate should not look “similar” merely because both lack data.

---

# 9. Analogue eligibility / PIT law

An episode can be used only if the features in its retrieval vector were knowable at the analogue's own comparison time.

Required protections:

- no post-outcome labels in retrieval;
- no future theme membership;
- no current security identity applied backward incorrectly;
- no later restatement replacing historical known state;
- no survivor-only universe;
- retain delisted/failed episodes when source data supports them;
- no backfilled synthetic candidate admission presented as prospective memory;
- separate historical reconstructed episodes from prospectively captured episodes.

The output must reveal reconstruction quality.

---

# 10. Counterexample-first analogue UX

A flagship system must fight confirmation bias.

Every analogue set should intentionally surface:

- nearest successful episodes;
- nearest failed/false-bounce episodes;
- cases with same setup but different catalyst/theme;
- cases with same catalyst but different price geometry;
- cases where entry was unavailable/late;
- cases that look similar but are excluded and why.

Do not sort analogues only by future success or “most inspiring.”

---

# 11. Empirical prior architecture

A descriptive prior may include:

- median and quantile MFE/MAE;
- probability of crossing a predeclared threshold with confidence interval;
- median time-to-payoff;
- false-bounce share;
- entry-open duration;
- tail-risk distribution.

Every statistic must state:

- N/effective N;
- sampling/cluster structure;
- date range;
- selection rule/version;
- coverage/refusal count;
- prospective vs reconstructed composition.

If N is too small, say `UNESTIMABLE` and show the raw analogue cases.

# 12. Selective regime conditioning

Regime is allowed only as a **specific tested interaction**, not ambient multiplier.

Examples of plausible hypotheses to test if estimable:

- high-duration growth reversal paths differ materially under high vs low real-rate regimes;
- commodity-producer signals differ during commodity uptrend vs downtrend;
- small-cap financing-dependent names behave differently under tight vs loose financial conditions;
- dealer-gamma/volatility context changes false-bounce risk for highly reflexive names.

Every regime hypothesis must freeze:

```text
mechanism
regime variable/source
threshold/continuous formulation
PIT clock
species/episode population
minimum cell/effective N
primary outcome
expected direction if theory supports it
control/base model
forward validation plan
```

If power is insufficient, regime remains descriptive context.

Do not create dozens of named market regimes and then search for the best one.

# 15. Required reference compositions

At least:

1. **Strong analogue support:** 40+ comparable episodes, balanced success/failure, current setup inside support.
2. **Sparse species:** only 6 comparable episodes; raw cases shown, prior `UNESTIMABLE`.
3. **Continuous fingerprint beats label:** same industry label but very different behavior; retrieval explains why.
4. **Species transition:** company changed archetype; historical pre-transition episodes excluded.
5. **Counterexample:** nearest failure differs by one critical fragility dimension.
6. **No-match/OOD:** current candidate outside historical feature range.
7. **Regime interaction estimable:** specific mechanism with sufficient N and preregistered result path.
8. **Regime interaction not estimable:** system refuses modifier despite intuitive narrative.

# 16. Evaluation / falsification

Representation/routing research eventually must test:

- does species/fingerprint improve expert-family fit OOS vs no routing?
- does analogue retrieval produce better calibrated empirical priors than broad unconditional cohorts?
- do nearest episodes remain stable under small feature perturbations?
- are results driven by one sector/era/ticker?
- does discrete species add value beyond continuous features?
- do regime interactions survive multiple-testing/cluster controls?
- does routing improve earliness/path quality, not just future return?

Negative controls:

- shuffled species labels;
- random neighbors matched only on date/liquidity;
- current industry labels alone;
- future-informed epoch labels;
- outcome-selected per-name expert;
- deliberately irrelevant regime variable.
