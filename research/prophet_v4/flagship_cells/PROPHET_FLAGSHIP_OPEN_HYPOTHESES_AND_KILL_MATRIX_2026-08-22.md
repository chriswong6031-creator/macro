# 0. Why this exists

A masterplan can accidentally turn a desired product capability into a presumed empirical truth.

This file prevents that.

Every major flagship “alpha” concept is written as a hypothesis with:

- owner;
- current evidence state;
- minimum prerequisite;
- frozen comparison baseline;
- primary question;
- falsifiers;
- concentration/leakage dangers;
- PASS path;
- MIXED path;
- FAIL path;
- maximum authority before forward promotion.

**A failed hypothesis does not fail the flagship product thesis.** It means Mastermind learned not to spend authority/complexity on that construction.

Architecture should survive hypothesis failure.

---

# 1. Status vocabulary

- `OPEN` — question can be researched now.
- `ESTIMABILITY_FIRST` — first output must be a census/power/coverage read, not a performance claim.
- `BLOCKED_DATA` — concept cannot yet be honestly measured.
- `PREREGISTERED_ACTIVE` — a current experiment already owns the test; do not contaminate it.
- `FORWARD_ACCRUING` — design is frozen; await prospective N.
- `REJECTED_BROAD_FORM` — generic version already disfavored; only narrow hypotheses may reopen.
- `CONTEXT_ONLY_UNTIL_PROMOTED` — can be useful in product before predictive authority.

---

# 2. Universal adjudication law

For every row:

### PASS

Means the frozen construction clears the preregistered effect/robustness/coverage criteria. PASS can justify an owner-routed shadow implementation or next research rung, **not automatic live rank authority**.

### MIXED

Examples:

- works in one species but not broadly;
- improves risk but not direction;
- improves product explanation but not rank;
- works only at a longer horizon that destroys the early-entry mission;
- good effect but inadequate coverage;
- baseline-dependent.

Mixed evidence should **narrow** scope, not be averaged into a universal score.

### FAIL

The construction fails its key falsifier. Record and stop. Preserve useful descriptive context only if it has independent product value.

No same-outcome retuning rescue unless a genuinely new hypothesis is separately preregistered.

# 15. Leakage threat registry

Every cell should explicitly inspect:

- current theme membership used historically;
- restated financial segment data used before it was known;
- analyst estimate history without point-in-time snapshots;
- relationship edges backfilled from current websites;
- corporate identity splices / reused tickers;
- event taxonomies changed after seeing returns;
- source publication time vs capture/generated time;
- delayed vendors / first-availability confusion;
- options OI revisions;
- delisted/survivor-only universes;
- model features derived from future episode labels;
- analogue retrieval using future outcome descriptors;
- corrections applied retroactively to training without contemporaneous replay.

A leakage-positive study is descriptive archaeology, not alpha evidence.

# 18. What may survive a predictive FAIL

A feature can fail rank utility and still have legitimate value:

- exposure → explanation / peer grouping;
- relationships → graph navigation / dossier context;
- catalyst taxonomy → source organization;
- analogues → counterexample retrieval;
- fragility → risk disclosure;
- crowding → path warning;
- ThemeState → market context;
- evidence trajectory → chronology;
- user-facing evidence independence → trust.

Record the surviving job explicitly so a later session does not accidentally revive the failed alpha claim.

# 19. Portfolio-level stop conditions

Pause the flagship expansion and return to Sol if:

- three or more cells independently require the same new owner/store — likely an unrecognized canonical-architecture gap;
- the D5 envelope cannot represent cell outputs without becoming a source warehouse;
- availability/intelligence separation proves impossible under actual product needs — requires Chairman/Sol architecture ruling, not local workaround;
- source rights make the core economic-exposure/expectation plan nonviable;
- identity/correction lineage cannot support PIT replay;
- evaluation lacks enough prospective throughput to ever promote the proposed feature density;
- product complexity increases review burden without evidence of decision value.

These are architecture-level findings, not reasons for a local session to invent a workaround.

## Closing law

The flagship masterplan is ambitious about **capabilities** and conservative about **claims**.

We want the system to be able to understand themes, catalysts, economic relationships, expected responses, incorporation, fragility and historical analogues.

We do not assume any particular version of those ideas predicts returns until it survives a frozen test.

Future Sol sessions are authorized to make the masterplan **smaller and stronger** by killing weak hypotheses. They are not authorized to make it incoherent by preserving every idea as an unvalidated score.
