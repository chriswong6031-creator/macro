# 0. Observable mission

Design the evidence architecture that lets Prophet say **both** of the following at the same time:

> **“This looks like a strong opportunity.”**

and

> **“This setup carries meaningful structural/path/crowding risk.”**

without secretly subtracting one from the other inside an opaque score.

The 10/10 system helps the Chairman distinguish:

- thesis quality;
- fragility of the company/path;
- market positioning/crowding;
- deterministic entry availability;
- evidence confidence/coverage.

Those are separate epistemic objects.

A future candidate may therefore be:

- high opportunity / low fragility / low crowding;
- high opportunity / high fragility;
- high opportunity / extreme crowding;
- low opportunity / high fragility;
- unknown crowding because source coverage is absent.

The cell must make those states explicit and buildable.

---

# 1. Why this matters to Prophet's flagship thesis

Early discovery naturally exposes Mastermind to more false starts and incomplete situations. If Prophet becomes earlier without becoming more **risk-aware**, it can simply surface fragile names sooner.

At the same time, over-penalizing risk destroys the core product: some of the best asymmetric opportunities are precisely in names with financing, volatility, concentration or event risk.

Therefore this cell is **not** a veto engine. It is an evidence system that answers:

- What could break the thesis or path?
- Who is already positioned?
- How expensive/crowded is the current expression?
- What risks are measured vs merely unknown?
- Does risk evidence add predictive/path value for this species/setup?

The moat is the accumulation of **risk state at first surface → subsequent MAE/false-bounce/path/outcome** by issuer/species/context.

# 3. Chairman intent — separate fragility from crowding

These are not the same problem.

## Structural / thesis fragility

Risk that issuer economics, financing, corporate structure or event path invalidate/damage the thesis.

## Market / path fragility

Risk that even a correct thesis produces a poor tradable path because of volatility, liquidity, gaps, positioning or reflexivity.

## Crowding / positioning

Evidence about how much market participants may already be in or paying for the trade.

## Availability

Deterministic current entry geometry/chase/invalidation state. Owned elsewhere.

The cell must not collapse these into “risk score 67.”

# 4. Structural fragility family research

Investigate owner-backed evidence such as:

## Financing / dilution

- active shelf/ATM/ELOC capacity;
- convertible/warrant overhang;
- recent issuance;
- cash runway/funding need where authoritative financial facts support it;
- maturity/refinancing walls;
- share-basis changes.

The source fact belongs to Capital Structure/FIF. Cell E defines evidence semantics and future evaluation, not the fact engine.

## Balance-sheet fragility

- leverage;
- liquidity;
- interest burden;
- covenant/refinancing dependence;
- working-capital stress;
- cash burn.

Only from canonical financial truth. No LLM-estimated ratios.

## Business concentration

- named customer concentration;
- geographic dependence;
- commodity/input concentration;
- single-program/single-asset dependence;
- supplier bottleneck.

Must distinguish measured concentration from generic narrative concern.

## Legal/regulatory/event binary risk

Use specialist owner facts; do not duplicate trial/regulatory/procurement/legal stores.

## Accounting/disclosure fragility

Potential future FIF/Forensics findings with exact evidence. A forensic finding is not automatically predictive or bearish.

# 6. Positioning / crowding family research

The cell must distinguish raw observations from directional inference.

Potential source families:

## Options

- IV percentile / term structure;
- skew;
- implied move vs historical realized event move;
- OI concentration;
- OI persistence/change under valid PIT law;
- call/put concentration;
- dealer positioning / GEX where assumptions are explicit;
- unusual activity with source/coverage constraints.

Options data is **not automatically investor intent**. Open interest has two sides. Call volume is not synonymous with bullish smart money.

## Short / borrow

- short interest;
- days-to-cover;
- borrow cost/utilization if lawful source exists;
- change/persistence;
- squeeze/reflexivity context.

High short interest can be bearish consensus, squeeze fuel, or both.

## Ownership / institutional

- 13F concentration/change;
- fund-following evidence;
- insider ownership/trading;
- passive/ETF ownership.

Ownership data has delay and disclosure clocks; do not treat stale quarterly holdings as current positioning.

## Flow / attention

- ETF flow exposure;
- news/search/social attention where rights and calibration exist;
- retail attention;
- volume/turnover anomalies.

Attention is not position and position is not conviction.

# 7. Typed evidence architecture

The cell must propose separate evidence families/heads rather than one score.

Candidate high-level outputs:

### `structural_fragility`

Why the issuer/thesis can break.

### `path_fragility`

Why the price path can be adverse even if thesis is right.

### `crowding_positioning`

How much participation/expensive expression may already exist.

### `risk_coverage_confidence`

What sources were actually measurable.

Each should retain component receipts so user can distinguish, e.g.:

> “Structural fragility HIGH because 9-month funding runway + active ATM; crowding UNKNOWN because ThetaData not current for this root; path fragility MEDIUM due to historical gap risk.”

Do not average UNKNOWN into MEDIUM.

# 8. Applicability / species law

Not every risk matters equally for every company.

Examples:

- dilution capacity may matter enormously for pre-profit biotech and little for cash-rich megacap;
- option crowding signals may be meaningful only with sufficient liquid chain coverage;
- customer concentration matters differently for an early-stage defense supplier and diversified conglomerate;
- commodity exposure is core for miners but not SaaS;
- high short interest on a low-float name creates different path dynamics than on a mega-cap.

Cell D/Stock Identity may later provide species routing. Cell E should design an **applicability matrix** and default to `NOT_APPLICABLE`/`UNESTIMABLE` rather than generic weights.

# 9. Null / coverage law

Mandatory states include:

- `NOT_APPLICABLE`;
- `NOT_COVERED`;
- `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`;
- `STALE`;
- `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`;
- `UNESTIMABLE`;
- `RIGHTS_BLOCKED`;
- `MEASURED_LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH` or native measure where defensible.

Examples:

- no ThetaData current chain → `NOT_COVERED`, never “uncrowded”;
- no active shelf found after a complete canonical search may be a measured absence, but only if owner contract supports completeness;
- no forensic finding because FF source plane is incomplete ≠ clean accounting;
- no short-interest history ≠ low short interest.

# 10. Time / correction law

Risk evidence can change rapidly and historical replay must know what was knowable.

Examples:

- ATM filed after candidate surface;
- short interest published with settlement/reporting delay;
- 13F delayed by filing deadline;
- option OI date/availability semantics;
- financial restatement;
- contract modification/deobligation;
- capital structure registration expires/becomes effective;
- borrow conditions change intraday.

Each component must expose source/event/known/captured clocks appropriate to owner law. Never stamp observation time as source availability to make data look fresh.

# 11. Crowding vs thesis semantics

The product must support these apparently contradictory but coherent states:

### Strong thesis + low crowding

Potential attractive early setup.

### Strong thesis + high crowding

Thesis may remain correct but payoff asymmetry/path can worsen; do not automatically reject.

### Weak thesis + low crowding

Lack of crowding is not positive evidence.

### Negative thesis + extreme short crowding

May still carry squeeze/path risk.

### Strong thesis + unknown crowding

Do not make confident claim from missing source.

# 13. Measurement / falsification

A component can be product-useful before predictive authority, but promotion must test specific hypotheses.

Examples:

### Structural fragility

Does high financing fragility predict worse MAE/false-bounce/path survival conditional on candidate strength/species?

### Options crowding

Does extreme implied move/OI/skew state predict lower incremental upside or higher MAE after otherwise similar candidate surfaces?

### Short/reflexivity

Does high short/borrow alter tail distribution rather than mean return?

### Forensics

Do source-supported findings add path/outcome value beyond conventional fundamentals?

Negative controls:

- shuffled risk labels;
- same source completeness but no finding;
- high IV unrelated to candidate event;
- no-options-coverage cases;
- post-event filings that were not knowable at entry;
- generic leverage without species/materiality.

Report effects on MFE/MAE/false-bounce/lead-time separately. A risk feature that reduces MAE but also removes all winners requires explicit product tradeoff, not hidden weighting.
