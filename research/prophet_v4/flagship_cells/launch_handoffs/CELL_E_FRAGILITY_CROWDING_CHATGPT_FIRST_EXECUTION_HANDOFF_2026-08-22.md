# 2. Why this matters

Earlier discovery increases both opportunity and uncertainty. If Prophet surfaces situations earlier but lacks explicit fragility/crowding context, it may simply surface traps earlier. If it automatically filters all risky situations, it destroys asymmetric opportunities.

Therefore Cell E is not a veto engine.

The user should be able to distinguish:

- great thesis / fragile capital structure;
- great thesis / expensive or crowded expression;
- great thesis / unknown positioning because the source is missing;
- weak thesis / uncrowded — which is not positive evidence;
- negative thesis / high short interest — with squeeze/path risk;
- strong thesis / chased entry — still not actionable because Availability owns that answer.

# 5. Four-part architecture

## 5.1 `structural_fragility`

Question:

> What can break or weaken the issuer/economic thesis?

Candidate component families:

- financing capacity / shareholder supply;
- funding need / cash dependency;
- maturity/refinancing dependence;
- leverage/liquidity/interest burden;
- customer/program/product/geographic concentration;
- single-asset or binary-event dependency;
- accounting/disclosure warning;
- legal/regulatory dependency;
- input/supplier concentration where owner truth exists.

## 5.2 `path_fragility`

Question:

> Even if the thesis is right, what can make the tradable path unusually adverse/discontinuous?

Candidate components:

- residual/realized volatility;
- gap frequency;
- historical MAE after comparable setups;
- liquidity/spread/ADV;
- halt/binary-event exposure;
- event proximity;
- price-impact sensitivity;
- borrow/short reflexivity;
- options-implied path richness where covered.

**Do not duplicate deterministic chase/entry/invalidation Availability.**

## 5.3 `crowding_positioning`

Question:

> What observable evidence exists about participation, expensive expression, consensus ownership or reflexivity?

Potential components:

- options IV/skew/term/OI/expected move;
- official short interest;
- borrow cost/utilization if an owner/source exists;
- 13F ownership/concentration/change;
- insider transactions;
- passive/ETF ownership;
- attention/turnover/flow proxies.

## 5.4 `coverage_quality`

Question:

> Which of the above was actually measurable now?

Coverage is not cosmetic metadata. It is required to interpret all other heads honestly.

# 11. Short / borrow / ownership research law

## Short interest

High SI can simultaneously mean:

- bearish positioning;
- skepticism/negative fundamental view;
- potential squeeze reflexivity;
- high borrow/path risk.

Separate these semantic hypotheses.

## Borrow

Only if a lawful, stable owner/source exists. Borrow cost/utilization can change faster than official SI; do not fuse clocks.

## 13F

Useful for:

- slow institutional breadth/concentration;
- manager overlap;
- historical ownership context;
- delayed accumulation/distribution evidence at filing-known time.

Not live flow.

## Insider

Classify transaction codes/context; open-market purchase differs from award/exercise/tax/gift. Raw sale count is not enough.

## ETF/passive

Require issuer/fund identity, holdings effective/publication time, historical snapshots, derivative/cash treatment and rights. Do not assume one universal free source.

# 13. Research hypotheses

## E-H1 — typed structural fragility improves path-risk assessment

Primary outcomes:

- MAE;
- tail loss;
- invalidation;
- false bounce;
- time underwater;
- path survival.

Controls:

- opportunity/evidence strength;
- size/liquidity;
- simple leverage/funding baseline;
- species where lawful.

A feature can PASS path-risk and FAIL direction.

## E-H2 — crowding predicts path/asymmetry more reliably than direction

Study separate hypotheses for:

- options expected move / richness;
- OI concentration;
- official short state;
- ownership concentration;
- attention saturation.

Do not fuse first.

## E-H3 — options-implied expected move helps event/incorporation interpretation

Only covered names and proper event windows. Compare to realized-vol baseline. Coordinate with B; E does not compute incorporation.

## E-H4 — slow institutional context moderates information diffusion

13F/analyst/ownership may be a moderator, but size/liquidity and publication lag are major confounds. Estimability-first.

### Negative controls

- coverage indicator alone;
- shuffled values within covered cohort;
- stale prior snapshot;
- post-event filings unavailable at surface;
- unrelated high IV event;
- generic leverage without applicability;
- no-finding rows from incomplete source plane.
