# 4. Product thesis — five separate questions

## Q1 — Can I act?

Owned by deterministic Availability.

User needs:

- action verb;
- current price/zone/distance;
- blocker if not open;
- invalidation/risk geometry.

## Q2 — Why now?

What changed recently enough to create this candidate/priority?

Not static quality.

## Q3 — Why might the thesis be real?

Independent owner evidence:

- theme/exposure/transmission;
- event/surprise/materiality;
- fundamentals;
- empirical prior;
- other accepted intelligence.

## Q4 — How much looks reflected?

Only when Cell B/accepted incorporation method can support the statement. Otherwise explicit `UNESTIMABLE / ACCRUING / INPUT_PENDING`.

## Q5 — What could make this wrong or painful?

- contradictory roots;
- structural/path fragility;
- crowding;
- missing/stale/rights-blocked evidence.

These five questions must not become one overall conviction score.

# 6. Five-second hierarchy — preliminary freeze

A candidate card should be understandable in this order:

## 1. `ACTION`

Most visually dominant.

Examples:

- `ENTRY OPEN`
- `APPROACHING ENTRY`
- `WAIT FOR PULLBACK`
- `RAN — DON'T CHASE`
- `INVALIDATED`

## 2. `WHY NOW`

One short evidence-backed change sentence.

Example shape:

> Customer capex evidence strengthened this week; supplier peers repriced first; early turn fired today.

## 3. `EVIDENCE`

1–3 strongest **independent root groups**, not five derivatives of one call.

Compact example:

> 2 independent roots · 6 observations

## 4. `PRICEDNESS / RESPONSE`

Examples only when method authority permits:

- response small vs comparable history;
- response typical;
- response already large;
- not enough history;
- method accruing.

Never pseudo-fair-value.

## 5. `KEY RISK / MISSING`

One or two decision-relevant warnings:

- financing fragility;
- high crowding;
- conflicting earnings root;
- options not covered;
- analogue support weak.

Do not show ten equal warning chips.

# 7. `Why now` explanation architecture

This sentence is strategically important and high-risk.

## Preferred starting method

Deterministic/controlled composition from approved explanation primitives.

Each clause should have:

```text
claim_type
plain-language template/value
source/evidence ref
known_at
owner
authority
```

A composer chooses top relevant clauses under word budget.

### Bad

> Great AI stock with strong fundamentals and improving momentum.

### Better

> Two large customers raised data-center spending this week; verified supplier exposure is high; peers moved first; an early entry signal fired today.

Only emit facts whose owner contract exists.

## Future model-generated prose

If later used, the model may rewrite supplied facts for fluency but cannot add new causal/materiality/predictive claims. The factual clause set remains the auditable authority.

# 8. Evidence independence UX

The product must visually distinguish roots from derivatives.

Glance:

> `2 independent roots · 6 observations`

Inspection:

```text
Earnings root
  guidance raise
  Q&A demand topic
  accepted semantic change
Customer capex root
  customer A increase
  customer B increase
```

Depending on Cell F/A10 economics, two documents can still be economically dependent. H should not present “independent” merely because URLs differ.

If independence is unresolved, use safer copy such as `2 evidence roots` rather than `2 independent confirmations`.

# 11. Analogue UX

When D/Market Memory can support it:

Show:

- raw/effective comparable N;
- match quality/support state;
- MFE/MAE/time distributions only if estimable;
- nearest success;
- nearest failure;
- critical mismatch;
- reconstructed vs prospective history.

Small N:

> `6 comparable episodes — too few for a stable rate. See cases.`

OOD:

> `No credible historical analogue under the current feature contract.`

Never default to a win probability.
