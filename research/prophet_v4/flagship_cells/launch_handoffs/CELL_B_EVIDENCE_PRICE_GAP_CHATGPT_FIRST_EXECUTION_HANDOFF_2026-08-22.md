# 1. Observable mission

Determine whether Mastermind can lawfully and usefully infer a **price-incorporation state** from validated evidence without pretending to know intrinsic fair value.

The target question is:

> Given a specific evidence family whose economic magnitude/materiality is knowable at time `t`, how much response would comparable economics/history normally imply, how much target-specific response has actually occurred after defensible common-factor/peer adjustment, and is the difference informative enough to classify the price as unusually under-, normally-, or over-incorporated?

The result must explicitly distinguish:

- market underreaction;
- normal/correct incorporation;
- overreaction;
- evidence that the market correctly ignored because it was weak/expected/immaterial;
- unestimable cases;
- disagreement among defensible baselines.

---

# 2. Why this matters to Prophet

This is potentially one of the flagship system’s highest-value ideas because it transforms “good news” or “strong theme” into a more decision-relevant question:

> **Has the market already paid for it?**

But it is also one of the easiest ideas to fool ourselves with. A bad implementation can generate fake alpha through:

- target self-inclusion in the benchmark;
- post-event beta/exposure estimation;
- future-fold leakage;
- current/final-corrected historical data;
- choosing the baseline/horizon after outcomes;
- defining “materiality” from the very price move being explained;
- survivor-only event families;
- labeling every low response as underreaction;
- retuning the current blinded Dislocation experiment after seeing its outcomes.

Cell B’s value is therefore as much **scientific refusal** as signal creation.

The moat, if the thesis survives, is a point-in-time history of:

- ex-ante evidence magnitude/materiality;
- the market’s contemporaneous response under multiple frozen baselines;
- the incorporation state Mastermind assigned at each decision session;
- later path/outcome distributions;
- which evidence families, species and horizons exhibited persistent delayed incorporation.

# 3. 10/10 end-state behavior

A mature future product might say:

> Customer capex guidance materially improved the supplier’s demand outlook. Economic exposure is verified, the evidence root was knowable before today’s open, and the target issuer is excluded from the theme-response basket. Across the registered market-, sector-, independent-theme- and matched-peer baselines, the target’s residual response is in the bottom historical decile for comparable evidence magnitude. State: **experimental LOW_RELATIVE_RESPONSE / candidate UNDER_INCORPORATED**. History support is adequate but not yet promotion-grade. Entry remains a separate deterministic decision and is currently `ENTRY_OPEN`.

It must also be able to say:

> Headline was large, but issuer materiality is low and consensus already expected most of it. The stock’s low response is consistent with history. State: `IN_RANGE / CORRECT_IGNORE`.

or:

> Market-excess says under-response, matched peers say normal, and synthetic control says over-response. State: `CONFLICTED_BASELINES`; no incorporation claim.

or:

> Evidence magnitude cannot be calibrated point-in-time. State: `UNESTIMABLE`.

No exact upside percentage is implied unless a separately validated future model earns that authority.

Start with **one evidence family whose magnitude/materiality is genuinely estimable**.

Candidate first families, subject to current owner maturity:

## B-P3 — first calibration should be an empirical state/distribution, not a price target

Within a walk-forward/fold-frozen training population:

1. compute the ex-ante evidence magnitude/materiality;
2. compute the registered response baseline(s);
3. estimate the historical conditional response distribution;
4. compare the current response against the frozen prior;
5. emit a state such as:

```text
LOW_RELATIVE_RESPONSE
IN_RANGE
HIGH_RELATIVE_RESPONSE
CONFLICTED_BASELINES
INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY
UNESTIMABLE
```

Only after calibration and forward evidence may product language graduate to experimental `UNDER_INCORPORATED` / `OVER_INCORPORATED`.

## B-P4 — the baseline ladder is a robustness hierarchy, not a result-shopping menu

Candidate ordered stack:

1. raw return;
2. market excess;
3. sector/industry excess;
4. **independent** theme excess;
5. owned/public factor residual;
6. matched peer basket;
7. synthetic control where preregistered and estimable.

Freeze:

- one primary baseline;
- secondary robustness baselines;
- disagreement semantics;
- horizon;
- minimum support;
- any species conditioning.

If defensible baselines reverse the conclusion, emit `CONFLICTED_BASELINES`.

## B-P5 — `CORRECT_IGNORE` is mandatory

Low response is not automatically underreaction. Negative classes include:

- economically immaterial evidence;
- already expected evidence;
- weak or disputed exposure;
- low-quality evidence;
- positive and negative evidence roots offsetting;
- liquidity/microstructure delay;
- wrong benchmark;
- evidence whose eventual causal story was false.

Design the taxonomy before results.

## B-P6 — original-time and evolving-episode beliefs are separate

At `t0`, classify only with `t0`-knowable information. Later evidence may update the episode prospectively but may not rewrite history.

Candidate append-only observation grain:

```text
incorporation_observation
  episode_id
  decision_session
  evidence_snapshot_version
  baseline_version
  state
  known_at
```
