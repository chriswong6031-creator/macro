# 1. Observable mission

Design, validate and, only after current owner authorization, implement the **one canonical evidence translation contract** that lets Prophet consume heterogeneous specialist intelligence without:

- copying specialist truth into another warehouse;
- turning null into neutral;
- losing known-at/correction lineage;
- counting correlated derivatives as independent confirmation;
- blending all families into one arbitrary score;
- becoming a second cross-family ranker;
- changing deterministic `ENTRY_OPEN` availability.

The machine question is:

> At this candidate episode and decision session, what intelligence families are applicable, what exactly do they say, how fresh/covered/trustworthy are they, how are they related/dependent, what changed versus prior observations, what is missing or conflicted, and what authority has each family actually earned?

# 2. Why this is the keystone R1 contract

Theme, catalysts, incorporation, analogues, fragility and positioning can each be excellent separately yet still fail as a product if every owner emits incompatible shapes and every consumer rebuilds translation logic.

Cell F should make the following future behavior possible:

```text
owner-native truth
→ typed family adapter / references
→ episode-scoped D5 evidence envelope
→ explainable semantic heads
→ explicitly registered Fusion member(s) only where earned
→ product explanation + Eval/VOI
```

The D5 layer is therefore a **semantic grammar and evidence join**, not a predictive authority by itself.

Its moat comes from durable, correction-safe, point-in-time evidence history and cross-family dependence lineage—not from a giant feature matrix.

## F-P3 — family, semantic head and Fusion member are different concepts

**Family** = owner/source lineage and native method.

**Semantic head** = user/research meaning, e.g.:

```text
emergence
evidence_trajectory
theme_state
economic_exposure
transmission
expectation_surprise
issuer_materiality
price_incorporation
empirical_prior

crowding_attention

```

**Fusion member** = one explicitly registered rank input with version/authority/clock.

One family may populate multiple heads. Several families may contribute to one head. Neither automatically implies a Fusion member.

## F-P4 — reference, do not warehouse

D5 should store enough normalized evidence to support:

- availability of the family;
- decision-time semantics;
- explanation;
- lineage;
- downstream registered features;

but source-heavy payloads remain owner-native.

Example:

```text
family_id: earnings_intelligence
source_refs:
  - earnings_event_id
  - expectation_packet_id
features:
  surprise_state: POSITIVE
  issuer_materiality_state: HIGH
```

Not the transcript, entire filing, raw broker table and duplicated event store.

## F-P5 — independence requires both provenance and economic-dependence lineage

Minimum candidate lineage:

```text
evidence_root_id
root_event_id
source_object_id
feature_derivation_id
economic_dependence_group
```

Reason:

- six features from one transcript are one root;
- news coverage and a filing can be different roots but may encode the same underlying economic event;
- a customer capex announcement can appear in Theme, Earnings read-through and supplier intelligence; it must not count three times automatically.

Cell F must design the join/identity semantics for these groups without creating a new universal evidence warehouse.

# 7. Core semantics Cell F must settle

## 7.1 Identity grain

For every family decide whether the subject is:

- security;
- economic issuer;
- theme;
- event;
- relationship edge;
- episode;
- mixed/reference.

D5 must not join on naked ticker strings.

## 7.2 Clocks

At minimum distinguish:

- `source_effective_at`
- `known_at`
- `captured_at`
- `generated_at`
- `corrected_at`
- `decision_session`

Not every family has every clock. Never collapse a source event date into a market-knowable date if they differ.

## 7.3 Null / neutral / unavailable

A family must explicitly distinguish:

- measured neutral;
- not applicable;
- not covered;
- unavailable source;
- stale;
- rights blocked;
- identity unresolved;
- insufficient history;
- unestimable;
- conflicted.

## 7.4 Corrections

Define whether a correction:

- appends a new evidence version;
- retracts/annuls a prior observation;
- changes the latest current view;
- leaves prior decision-time snapshots immutable;
- creates `CORRECTION_PENDING` until owner resolution.

Historical decisions must use contemporaneous belief, not silently corrected final truth.

## 7.5 Authority

A family needs a machine-readable authority state such as:

- descriptive/display only;
- research experimental;
- shadow ranking eligible;
- promoted rank feature;
- prohibited from rank/gate use.

Cell F should reuse existing authority vocabulary where possible rather than mint another taxonomy.
