# 0. Docket law

Every future flagship session should classify a question as one of:

- `SETTLED_ARCHITECTURE` — do not re-litigate absent new contradictory evidence or Chairman instruction.
- `OWNER_DECISION` — canonical owner may decide within accepted architecture.
- `SOL_ADJUDICATION_AFTER_RESEARCH` — research should return options/evidence; Sol decides.
- `CHAIRMAN_DECISION` — changes product thesis, authority, commercial posture or a protected strategic choice.
- `NOT_YET_A_DECISION` — insufficient evidence; do not escalate theatrically.

The fact that a question matters does not make it a Chairman decision.

# 6. Decision request quality bar

A session may request Sol/Chairman adjudication only after it presents:

1. exact decision sentence;
2. why current architecture/owner cannot decide locally;
3. alternatives;
4. measured current state;
5. user/machine capability impact;
6. scientific implications;
7. rights/cost implications if any;
8. no-rebuild implications;
9. reversible vs irreversible consequences;
10. recommended ruling;
11. what work is blocked pending decision;
12. what work can continue regardless.

“Do you want X or Y?” with no evidence is not an executive decision packet.

---

# 7. How Linear should represent executive decisions

Do not create speculative Chairman-decision tickets for every open research question.

A Linear/Agent OS decision projection should exist only when:

- the research return has made the decision concrete;
- alternatives are bounded;
- a canonical owner cannot proceed without it;
- the decision meets the quality bar above.

This prevents Linear from becoming a backlog of hypothetical executive questions.

---

# 8. Decision-change law

A settled decision may be reopened only with one of:

- new Chairman instruction;
- new production evidence directly contradicting the premise;
- new rights/legal constraint;
- failed prospective evidence showing the design is practically harmful;
- canonical-owner impossibility discovered during implementation;
- explicit Sol architecture re-review.

A different model/agent preferring another architecture is **not** new evidence.

---

## Closing ruling

The flagship program should give future Sol sessions **freedom to research and kill hypotheses**, but very little freedom to accidentally rewrite the company architecture.

Settled system boundaries stay settled.

Open empirical questions stay open.

Executive attention is reserved for the few forks that genuinely change product thesis, authority, control planes, source strategy or commercial posture.
