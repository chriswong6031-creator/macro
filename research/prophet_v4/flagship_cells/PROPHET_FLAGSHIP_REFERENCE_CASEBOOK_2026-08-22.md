# 0. Why this exists

Architecture prose leaves room for divergent interpretation.

These cases make the intended machine reasoning and user experience concrete across:

- strong positive states;
- bad-entry states;
- weak-context states;
- high-risk states;
- missing-data states;
- conflicting-evidence states;
- justified-impairment counterexamples;
- identity/correction states;
- emergent-theme probation;
- analogue insufficiency;
- rights-blocked evidence;
- empty/no-action days.

Every case is **synthetic**. Symbols such as `SYN-A` are invented placeholders. Any numerical values exist solely to illustrate contracts, not to imply a calibrated threshold.

Each case distinguishes:

- deterministic facts;
- statistical/empirical observations;
- model-generated proposals;
- experimental/unpromoted inferences;
- binding availability;
- user-facing output;
- forbidden interpretation.

A future product implementation should be able to represent every case without semantic contradiction.

# 1. Case A — Strong theme evidence + under-incorporation hypothesis + ENTRY_OPEN

## Situation

Synthetic company `SYN-A` manufactures specialized power-management equipment.

At decision clock T:

- canonical identity is resolved;
- source-backed segment truth shows substantial current economic participation in data-center power infrastructure;
- two large customers independently raise forward data-center capex expectations;
- a related procurement/demand evidence family accelerates;
- the theme's breadth and residual impulse strengthen;
- SYN-A's own factor-adjusted response remains modest relative to a frozen exposure-weighted theme baseline;
- early technical experts begin turning;
- current price lies inside the deterministic entry zone;
- structural fragility is low-to-moderate;
- crowding is rising but not extreme.

## Method classification

**Deterministic/source truth**

- identity;
- segment revenue facts;
- customer capex disclosures;
- current price;
- entry zone/invalidation;
- evidence clocks.

**Statistical/empirical**

- residual theme impulse;
- exposure-weighted expected-response baseline;
- historical response band;
- crowding percentile if promoted/covered.

**Model-generated proposal**

- model-assisted theme-membership proposal, only if validated against source spans and ratified by GMI.

**Experimental inference**

- `price_incorporation = UNDER_INCORPORATED`, authority false unless separately promoted.

## Machine reasoning

```text
Emergence: strong and accelerating
Economic exposure: high / verified
Transmission: customer-capex → supplier demand, evidence supported
Expectation/materiality: positive, issuer-relevant
Observed residual response: modest
Incorporation: under-incorporation hypothesis, experimental
Fragility: low/moderate
Crowding: moderate
Early technical state: turning
Availability: ENTRY_OPEN
```

## Five-second user copy

> **ENTRY OPEN — evidence is strengthening before the stock has fully responded.** Customer capex, theme breadth and company-specific demand evidence are rising across independent sources. The company has verified economic exposure and price remains inside the entry zone. Price-incorporation analysis is experimental.

## Dossier detail

- top exposure axes with sources and known-at;
- customer→supplier transmission path;
- evidence-root timeline;
- expected-response baseline variants;
- observed residual return;
- why baselines agree/disagree;
- fragility/crowding;
- exact entry/invalidation;
- missing items;
- analogue successes **and failures**.

## What Prophet must NOT say

- “Fair value is 37% higher.”
- “Three AI signals guarantee upside.”
- “Theme evidence opened the entry zone.”
- “Customer capex equals future SYN-A revenue.”

## Evaluation labels later

- first_surface_session;
- availability_at_first_surface;
- move_consumed_at_surface;
- H10/H21/H42/H63 path metrics;
- lead time vs current champion;
- family LOFO contribution.

## Situation

`SYN-B` has stronger verified theme exposure than SYN-A and more positive evidence roots. However:

- price is +28% from first qualified evidence;
- current residual response is high relative to comparable theme episodes;
- price is well outside the deterministic entry zone;
- chase/extension rules mark the setup unavailable.

## Machine reasoning

```text
Thesis strength: high
Evidence quality: high
Economic exposure: very high
Price incorporation: high / possibly over-incorporated
Availability: RAN_DONT_CHASE
```

## Five-second copy

> **RAN — DON'T CHASE.** The evidence and economic exposure remain strong, but the stock has already moved far beyond its entry geometry. A good company/theme thesis is not the same thing as a good current entry.

## Product requirement

SYN-B may rank highly **inside the `RAN_DONT_CHASE` lane** for research importance, but it cannot outrank SYN-A into an `ENTRY_OPEN` action slot by having stronger fundamentals.

## Forbidden interpretation

- “Highest score = buy now.”
- green theme styling that implies actionability.

# 9. Case I — Negative counterexample: the drop is justified impairment, not dislocation

## Situation

`SYN-I` falls sharply after an adverse operational event.

At t0:

- event is not clearly temporary;
- asset integrity is impaired;
- restart timing is unknown;
- funding need increases;
- covenant/refinancing risk rises;
- negative evidence persists in subsequent first-party disclosures;
- peers do not show a comparable move;
- early technical rebound appears briefly.

## Machine reasoning

```text
Adverse evidence: strong and persistent
Recoverability: unproven
Structural fragility: rising
Price drop: large but plausibly justified
Technical bounce: insufficient to override evidence
Dislocation state: NOT_ESTABLISHED / likely impairment
```

## Five-second copy

> **Drop may reflect real impairment, not temporary dislocation.** The price is lower, but recoverability and financing risk remain unresolved. A technical bounce alone does not make the selloff irrational.

## Why this case is mandatory

The Dislocation system must learn to **refuse attractive-looking dips**.

## Forbidden interpretation

- “Down 30% = cheap.”
- “Positive RSI/MACD turn = evidence the adverse event is temporary.”

## Closing product law

The flagship product is not successful if it can explain only the obvious winner.

It must be equally coherent when:

- the thesis is strong but the entry is bad;
- the evidence is contradictory;
- the drop is justified;
- coverage is missing;
- history is insufficient;
- a correction changes the thesis;
- a theme is too new;
- no trade is worth taking.

That ability to **refuse, qualify and separate states** is part of the intelligence moat, not a weakness of the product.
