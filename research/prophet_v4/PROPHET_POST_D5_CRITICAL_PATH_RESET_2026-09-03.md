## 4. Why Prophet still recommends candidates late

### 4.1 Slow confirmation still carries too much authority

The current estate still contains hardcoded slow-cascade admission behavior. Early
systems were added beside it, not given a canonical route to the main decision surface.
A candidate may therefore exist in TURN WATCH, Radar or another early source while the
flagship waits for 2D/3D maturity.

### 4.2 Entry availability is not a single server-authoritative fact

Several systems describe zones, chase, stops, buy-soon or live states, but B4 has not
reconciled them. Until B4:

- a mature signal may be extended;
- a strong intelligence thesis may be unbuyable;
- the browser may infer a state the server did not authorize;
- current-price truth can be confused with signal quality;
- strategy and horizon cannot lawfully change entry/add interpretation.

### 4.3 Lifecycle axes remain collapsed

B3 is still missing. The product must separately represent:

```text
candidate episode state
technical emergence/maturity
current deterministic Availability
intelligence/evidence state
strategy eligibility
```

Without those axes, the board appears bipolar because a change in one clock is rendered
as though the whole thesis reversed.

### 4.4 Theme, sector and market-cycle intelligence is incomplete at the consumer

The graph and identity estate is meaningful, but canonical dynamic ThemeState and
transmission evidence do not yet reach Prophet as a complete point-in-time owner
contract. D5 currently contains only the bounded Earnings family. It cannot answer:

- where capital is rotating;
- whether leadership is broadening or narrowing;
- whether a theme is early, mature, crowded or decaying;
- whether policy/liquidity transmission supports the candidate;
- whether the company is a leader, supplier, beneficiary, laggard or false analogue.

### 4.5 The product lacks strategy-specific interpretation

A tactical swing, a quality-earnings position and Cycle Capture can disagree without
one being wrong. The current flagship still tends to render one ticker-level stance.
This makes short-horizon expiration look like a long-horizon thesis break and makes a
valid core hold look inconsistent with `RAN_DONT_CHASE` for a new tactical entry.

### 7.3 B4 — strategy-aware deterministic Availability

**User capability unlocked:** green means a lawful entry is actually available now.

- `engine/entry_signal.py` zone/chase/stop/null semantics;
- `engine/prophet_live/live_states.py` five-minute predecessor and hysteresis;
- current bridge/board availability behavior;
- early-entry geometry and chase decay.

Its conceptual function is:

```text

  canonical_episode,

  horizon,
  entry_policy,
```

Shared non-waivable facts include freshness, identity, corporate-action basis,
liquidity, fillability, price, volatility, gaps, events, invalidation and data
completeness. Strategy-governed parameters may differ only through a versioned,
preregistered policy. Intelligence, excitement, LLM output and theme strength may not

## 11. Completion law

Prophet is not complete when D5 passes. D5 proves one evidence family can travel safely
from owner truth into one episode detail. The flagship completion standard remains:

### Truth

Current, correction-safe, rights-safe identity, price, event, theme and market context.

### Intelligence

Structured evidence, relationships, expectation/materiality, contradiction,
incorporation and missingness without premature authority.

### Product

A broad early-candidate workflow whose green state means a real entry is available now,
with strategy/horizon truth and a complete user journey.

### Learning

Cohort-honest prospective measurement of discovery lead time, availability, operator
selection, outcomes, controls and strategy-specific value.

The next phase must therefore be judged by one question:

> Did Prophet become earlier, more actionable, more contextually intelligent and more
> honest about uncertainty on the real production path?

A new schema, another score, green CI or one authenticated endpoint response alone does
not answer that question.
