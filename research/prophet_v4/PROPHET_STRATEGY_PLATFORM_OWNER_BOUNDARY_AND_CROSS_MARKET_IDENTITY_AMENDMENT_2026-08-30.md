## 0. Why this amendment is required

Adversarial review of the first architecture freeze found two hidden duplicate-system risks.

First, the proposed Cycle Capture sleeve named `CORE_HOLD`, a long-horizon casebook, thesis invalidation, and long-horizon outcome science without explicitly binding them to the existing **Long-Hold Thesis Layer**, **Thesis Funnel**, and **Winner Autopsy** owners. Those capabilities already define the horizon firewall, hold-thesis discipline, deterministic falsifier boundary, survival shadow, winner/failure case science, long-horizon labels, survivorship law, effective-N law and display-only authority ceiling. Creating a new Cycle Capture hold lifecycle, case corpus or outcome plane would violate the one-canonical-system law.

Second, the freeze could be read as making the currently proven U.S. B1 candidate episode a global candidate identity for Hong Kong, China A-shares, commodity futures, miners, ETFs and crypto. B1 is the canonical **current U.S. Prophet** candidate-episode plane. It is not evidence that every market already has a compatible candidate identity, completed-bar clock, instrument basis or episode contract.

This amendment closes both gaps before any implementation or CW0 research begins.

## 1. Governing ruling

### 1.1 Cycle Capture is a strategy view, not a new hold or identity system

Cycle Capture may define a strategy contract, strategy-specific eligibility, entry/add interpretation, and operator-facing composition. It may not create a parallel:

- candidate or episode identity;
- thesis-funnel or survival gate;
- hold-thesis lifecycle;
- falsifier engine;
- winner/failure case library;
- long-horizon label store;
- outcome grader or promotion authority;
- plan, position, portfolio or risk ledger.

Its lawful architecture is:

```text
market-native security/instrument identity
  -> market-native canonical candidate/event identity
  -> owner-native technical/economic/context evidence
  -> strategy eligibility read model
  -> B4 deterministic entry/add Availability
  -> existing Prophet plan identity and operational state
  -> Long-Hold-owned thesis/falsifier interpretation
  -> Portfolio/Risk-owned actual position and capital state
  -> Evaluation OS / owner-native outcome and promotion evidence
```

For current supported U.S. Prophet episodes, the candidate identity is B1. Outside that scope, the candidate/event owner must be proven separately.

### 1.2 `CORE_HOLD` is projection semantics only

`CORE_HOLD` is useful operator language, but it is not permission to mint a new lifecycle authority.

A future Cycle Capture hold projection may exist only when it:

- is keyed to an accepted strategy version plus canonical candidate/plan identity;
- reads Long-Hold-owned thesis evidence and falsifier semantics through an owner-approved contract;
- preserves evidence revision, source, correction, missingness and staleness;
- carries `horizon_role=hold_thesis`;
- creates no append-only lifecycle or position ledger of its own;
- cannot originate size, leverage, trim, hedge or exit orders;
- terminates only through the accepted thesis/invalidation and downstream risk authorities.

A tactical state such as `WAIT_PULLBACK`, `RAN_DONT_CHASE` or `ADD_CLOSED` may coexist with a valid core-hold projection. Tactical expiry or a daily oscillator reversal cannot silently terminate the long-horizon thesis. Conversely, a structural invalidation or terminal owner event may terminate the core even when short-term price action looks attractive.

## 3. Horizon firewall law

The existing Long-Hold horizon firewall remains controlling.

### 3.1 Current `hold_thesis` outputs cannot become entry gates

Current Long-Hold, Thesis Funnel, Winner Autopsy and long-horizon label artifacts are not authorized inputs to board ordering, alert triage, push floors, entry rank or B4 Availability merely because Cycle Capture is a long-horizon strategy.

Cycle Capture may consume:

1. the underlying source-native facts through their canonical owners; or
2. a future owner-published `context` or explicitly approved `dual` contract that passes the existing synapse and CI laws.

It may not relabel a `hold_thesis` artifact inside the consumer, copy the logic into a new module, or treat `thesis_candidate_shadow` as `ENTRY_OPEN`.

### 3.2 Tactical outputs cannot become hold authority by convenience

TOI, Radar, B4 or another tactical/entry artifact may inform a hold surface only through its declared horizon role. A price trigger, daily cross, tactical expiry or new-entry block is not itself a thesis break.

Any genuinely dual contract requires:

- exact producer ownership;
- explicit horizon-role declaration;
- separate entry and hold semantics;
- CI enforcement;
- no fused score or shared verdict;
- an owner-approved version change.

### 3.3 Separate projections, one strategy identity

A complete Cycle Capture experience may eventually show four separate projections:

```text
strategy eligibility / context
new-entry or add Availability
hold-thesis / falsifier state
portfolio action and risk state
```

They may share `strategy_id` and canonical identities. They may not be collapsed into one all-purpose status or opportunity score.

## 4. Existing Long-Hold, Thesis Funnel and Winner Autopsy boundaries

### 4.1 Long-Hold Thesis Layer

The Long-Hold Thesis Layer already owns the discipline that entry and hold are different species with different clocks. Its durable laws include:

- the mechanical `horizon_role` firewall;
- deterministic falsifier-based thesis transitions;
- LLM commentary/de-escalation only;
- long-horizon FDR isolation;
- survivorship and effective-N requirements;
- separate long-horizon outcome research rather than widening the live tactical grader.

Cycle Capture must reuse or lawfully extend those contracts. It does not supersede them.

### 4.2 Thesis Funnel

Current `thesis_candidate_shadow` and `watch_for_thesis` states are transparent display-only survival conjunctions. They are neither active theses nor current entry/add permission.

Cycle Capture may:

- display their relationship to a candidate;
- compare its own strategy eligibility against them;
- request an owner-compatible context contract if needed.

It may not:

- rename them as Cycle Capture states;
- use them as a universal long-horizon admission gate;
- let them waive B4;
- create a second survival/falsifier funnel.

### 4.3 Winner Autopsy

Winner Autopsy already owns the long-horizon case and fingerprint science substrate, including `winner_case.v1`, mechanical winner/failure episodes, Breakaway Watch, same-date controls, era and survivorship disclosures, and display-only promotion boundaries.

The CW0 `REFERENCE_AND_FAILURE_CASEBOOK` is therefore a **development evidence packet**, not a new canonical case library. Any durable structured Cycle Capture cases must:

- reuse `winner_case.v1` when semantically sufficient; or
- extend it through the Winner Autopsy owner with an explicit schema/version ruling.

Existing Winner Autopsy cases and Chairman-cited examples are development-visible and contaminated. They cannot be represented as untouched OOS evidence.

Winner Autopsy's breakaway episodes are also not automatically Cycle Capture entry episodes. They are later-stage price/volume events useful as controls and outcome references. Cycle Capture must still prove its own lawful early candidate semantics through the canonical candidate owner.

### 4.4 Existing long-horizon labels

Existing long-hold labels, thesis-funnel states and winner-autopsy outcomes are valuable controls and diagnostics. Their historical gaps, survivorship limits, construction-specific populations and horizon laws remain attached.

A new Cycle Capture result may not:

- rename an existing label as strategy validation;
- pool several outcome populations;
- cite raw fire counts as independent N;
- open a new long-horizon grader when the accepted owner can be extended.

## 5. U.S. B1 and cross-market candidate identity

### 5.1 Current U.S. scope

B1 is `PROVEN_LIVE` for the accepted U.S. Prophet generation and remains the canonical current U.S. opportunity identity. U.S. Cycle Capture research should attach to B1 when the instrument and episode are within B1's accepted scope.

### 5.2 No global-B1 fiction

B1 is not presently a proven universal identity for:

- Hong Kong equities or baskets;
- China A-share equities or baskets;
- commodity futures or rolling contracts;
- commodity ETFs, producers or miners as interchangeable exposures;
- crypto;
- any market with different calendars, price limits, suspensions, contract rolls, corporate-action basis or correction law.

CW0 must record, for every candidate proving domain:

```text
identity owner
candidate/event owner
schema and version
market and instrument coverage
completed-bar and source clocks
corporate-action / roll / basis law
correction and replay law
rights and historical availability
B1 compatibility or explicit non-compatibility
```

If no accepted candidate owner exists, CW0 must return a typed HOLD or REROUTE under its existing verdict vocabulary with `reason=IDENTITY_OR_CANDIDATE_OWNER_GAP`. It may commission no generic global candidate database by implication.

### 5.3 Instruments sharing one economic thesis remain distinct instruments

A future, ETF, producer, miner and royalty company may express the same commodity cycle while carrying different identity, carry, roll, dilution, balance-sheet, jurisdiction and operating-leverage risks. They may be related under an accepted opportunity or graph owner. They are not one interchangeable candidate identity.

K5 OpportunityCase remains dependency-held. CW0 may map the future need, but it may not create a substitute OpportunityCase store.
