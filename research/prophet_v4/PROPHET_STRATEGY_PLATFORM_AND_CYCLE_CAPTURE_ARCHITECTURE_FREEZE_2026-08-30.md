## 0. Executive ruling

Prophet will remain **one canonical opportunity platform** with **multiple governed strategy sleeves**. It will not become one universal all-weather score, and it will not fragment into disconnected Prophet products with duplicate identity, evidence, lifecycle, availability, evaluation, or publication truth.

The Chairman's founding washout thesis is preserved and elevated:

> In genuinely cyclical markets, the highest-value trade may be to identify a deep washout and pivot early, build a durable position near the cycle bottom, and hold the core as the market matures into a sustained bull run.

The difficulty of the current U.S. sideways/rotation regime must not redefine the whole product. It is one environment that exposes the need for tactical and range-aware strategies. ```text
one truth and evidence platform

## 1. Recovered founding intent

### 1.1 The original user job

The Chairman built Prophet partly to solve a rare but enormous opportunity:

1. recognize when an asset, sector, market, or cyclical equity has been deeply washed out;
2. distinguish a genuine bottoming process from an unfinished structural decline;
3. enter close enough to the pivot that the downside is bounded and the upside remains convex;
4. build conviction and position size only as the thesis, market, and technical structure mature;
5. avoid trading away the core position because of normal daily volatility;
6. hold through the portion of the bull cycle that creates disproportionate wealth;
7. reduce or exit when cycle maturity, distribution, structural invalidation, or macro change makes the original asymmetry disappear.

This is different from the current tactical problem of finding two-to-fifteen-session opportunities in a rotating market. Both jobs are valid. They require different horizons, triggers, evidence, holding rules, and failure models.

### 1.2 The machine job

For cycle capture, the machine must do more than detect an oscillator turn. It must:

- determine whether the instrument belongs to a legitimately cyclical opportunity class;
- identify the relevant cycle grain and asset-specific clock;
- measure depth, duration, breadth, dispersion, capitulation, liquidity, and structural damage;
- distinguish a tradable washout from permanent impairment, dilution, fraud, delisting, or secular decline;
- locate the earliest lawful pivot evidence without manufacturing intraday history;
- maintain one canonical episode across many technical and intelligence observations;
- separate accumulation eligibility from trend confirmation and from current entry availability;
- understand whether the broader asset, sector, commodity complex, policy state, or liquidity regime supports a new cycle;
- preserve the core-hold thesis through maturation while allowing separately identified tactical adds, trims, or hedges;
- record every invalidation, correction, adverse path, missed turn, false pivot, and eventual cycle outcome;
- evaluate the strategy by asset class and era before granting any ranking, sizing, or leverage authority.

### 1.3 The moat

The moat is not a MACD crossover. It is the governed longitudinal relationship among:

- asset identity and instrument type;
- cycle and washout state;
- candidate episode identity;
- market, sector, theme, commodity, policy, and liquidity context;
- company quality, balance-sheet survivability, dilution and catalyst evidence;
- point-in-time entry availability;
- strategy and horizon identity;
- operator accumulation and hold decisions;
- path-aware outcomes, corrections, and counterfactuals.

That system can learn when early washout accumulation is exceptional, when it is merely noisy, and when another strategy should be selected instead.

## 3. Correct product model: one platform, multiple sleeves

### 3.1 Shared canonical platform

All strategy sleeves reuse these owners:

- **Data OS / market-data owners:** security identity, corporate actions, prices, calendars, corrections, source and rights truth;
- **B1 candidate episodes:** one opportunity identity to which many observations and strategies may attach;
- **technical catalog and Setup Species:** deterministic primitives, setup identity, trials, lifecycle, and scientific status;
- **Live Entry Radar:** tactical intraday expert events;
- **Technical Opportunity Intelligence:** slower Weekly/Daily/4H occurrence semantics and two product queues;
- **GMI / Market Ontology:** themes, relationships, cohort state, policy, and economic transmission;
- **Earnings and specialist intelligence owners:** source-native facts, expectations, revisions, materiality, and corrections;
- **D5 intelligence vector:** episode-scoped point-in-time evidence read model;
- **B4 availability:** deterministic current trade geometry;
- **existing Prophet plan/lifecycle system:** plan identity and operational state;
- **Evaluation OS / QLedger / owner-native ledgers:** outcome labels, controls, and promotion evidence;
- **Portfolio/Risk:** capital allocation, sizing, concentration, leverage, and portfolio risk.

No sleeve may recreate any of those systems.

### 3.2 Strategy sleeve contract

Every governed sleeve must define independently:

```text
strategy_id and version
supported markets and instrument classes
primary and secondary horizons
economic thesis
setup species and candidate-admission law
required and optional evidence families
market/regime eligibility and explicit abstention
expected economic response
expected market response
price-incorporation test
residual-opportunity test
entry trigger and availability requirements
position lifecycle and hold law
invalidation, expiry, distribution, and exit-review law
risk facts required by the downstream owner
benchmark, control, costs, and evaluation targets
scientific status and authority tier
```

A candidate can qualify for several sleeves simultaneously. The strategies may disagree. Prophet must display the disagreement rather than average it into one score.

Example:

```text
CYCLE_CAPTURE:      PIVOT_ELIGIBLE / core thesis intact
TACTICAL_ROTATION:  ENTRY_OPEN for 5-10 sessions
MOMENTUM_CONTINUE:  RAN_DONT_CHASE
QUALITY_EARNINGS:   ACCRUING evidence
RANGE_REVERSION:    NOT_APPLICABLE
```

### 3.3 Regime is a strategy selector, not a universal factor

Macro/regime state is largely constant across securities on a given date and cannot by itself rank stocks cross-sectionally. Its correct initial role is:

1. describe the current environment;
2. determine which strategy families are plausible, held, or abstaining;
3. set research cohorts and interaction tests;
4. never manufacture a stock score or waive deterministic availability;
5. receive production authority only for specific powered mechanism-by-regime relationships.

The platform must preserve the `DNR:KILL-BROAD-REGIME-ROUTER` intent reflected in existing research law: no broad fitted router receives authority merely because regime labels exist. Specific, preregistered, estimable interactions may later be promoted.

## 4. Strategy family map

### 4.1 Flagship long-horizon sleeve: Cyclical Washout Accumulation / Cycle Capture

**Primary job:** acquire an eligible cyclical asset near a durable pivot and retain the core through the high-value portion of the ensuing cycle.

**Typical horizon:** weeks to quarters; potentially longer where the underlying cycle supports it.

**Distinctive edge sought:** unusually favorable asymmetry from deep prior liquidation, improving cycle state, survivable fundamentals or asset economics, and early technical repair before broad consensus reprices the opportunity.

### 4.2 Early Leadership / Sector Rotation

**Primary job:** identify new capital leadership before or near the start of a multi-session or multi-week rotation.

This is the first tactical proving sleeve for difficult sideways markets and should remain distinct from cycle capture.

### 4.3 Quality Earnings / Expectation Revision

**Primary job:** own durable company-information-state changes not yet fully incorporated into price.

### 4.4 Policy and Event Swing

**Primary job:** exploit bounded scheduled or policy-driven transmission where actor incentives, expected market response, positioning, and invalidators are explicit.

### 4.5 Catalyst and Dislocation

**Primary job:** identify company information state versus market price state disagreements with a defined catalyst and residual opportunity.

### 4.6 Liquidity / Debasement / Real Assets

**Primary job:** express sustained monetary, fiscal, currency, and real-asset regimes through the appropriate instruments without making a permanent narrative a permanent position.

### 4.7 Range / Mean Reversion

**Primary job:** trade bounded overshoot in non-trending environments with tight invalidation, instead of forcing medium-term continuation trades.

### 4.8 Defensive / Avoidance / Hedge Research

**Primary job:** identify exhaustion, distribution, adverse policy exposure, weak trajectory, or liquidity fragility. Directional short authority remains separately governed; at birth this sleeve supplies avoidance, reduced-risk, hedge-research, and relative-value context only.

## 5. Cyclical Washout Accumulation architecture

### 5.1 Core epistemic separation

The system must never treat these as synonyms:

```text
washout observed
bottoming process forming
pivot evidence observed
accumulation eligible
entry available now
trend confirmed
core-hold thesis active
cycle mature
distribution risk rising
exit required
```

A `WASHOUT_TURN` observation is technical evidence. It is not automatically an accumulation recommendation, a bottom claim, or leverage permission.

### 5.2 Asset and market eligibility

The sleeve is not universally applied.

#### U.S. equities

The highest-quality cases may require a broad-market, sector, industry, or company-specific washout with sufficient liquidity, survivability, and evidence that the decline is cyclical rather than permanent impairment. Because broad U.S. washouts are less frequent, the strategy should be selective and willing to remain inactive.

Candidate classes may include:

- liquid cyclicals after industry liquidation;
- commodity producers after an underlying commodity cycle washout;
- quality leaders after broad deleveraging or valuation compression;
- sectors with synchronized breadth capitulation and improving fundamental/earnings state;
- special situations where forced selling creates a temporary dislocation.

#### Hong Kong and China-linked markets

These require separate market microstructure, policy, capital-flow, listing, currency, and technical calibration. The thesis that their cycles can be more technically legible is a research hypothesis, not a transferred U.S. constant. Existing HK/China Prophet, basket, flow, and washout owners must be used.

#### Commodities and miners

Commodity cycles require additional owner-native facts:

- futures curve and carry where available;
- inventories and supply response;
- producer discipline and cost curves;
- currency and real-rate context;
- demand and policy transmission;
- instrument roll, liquidity, and basis;
- equity operating leverage and dilution for miners/producers.

A commodity future, an ETF, and a leveraged producer are different instruments and may not share one entry, risk, or holding contract.

#### Crypto and other high-volatility assets

No automatic admission. They require their own market structure, liquidity, custody, gap, and leverage law before entering this sleeve.

### 5.3 Proposed occurrence lifecycle

This is a strategy projection over canonical episode and technical-owner facts, not a new lifecycle database.

```text
OBSERVING
  -> WASHOUT_DEEPENING
  -> BASE_FORMING
  -> PIVOT_ELIGIBLE
  -> ACCUMULATION_OPEN
  -> CORE_HOLD
  -> MATURE_TREND
  -> DISTRIBUTION_REVIEW
  -> TERMINAL
```

Adverse branches:

```text
FAILED_PIVOT
STRUCTURAL_INVALIDATION
FUNDAMENTAL_IMPAIRMENT
LIQUIDITY_UNAVAILABLE
IDENTITY_OR_BASIS_UNRESOLVED
DATA_UNAVAILABLE
THESIS_EXPIRED
```

The existing TOI occurrence vocabulary remains authoritative for technical formation/trigger/confirmation. The cycle sleeve maps accepted owner states into strategy meaning; it does not rename or duplicate the technical lifecycle.

### 5.4 Required evidence domains

A first serious cycle-capture case should expose, not necessarily score, these domains:

1. **Washout depth and path** - drawdown, duration, volatility, breadth, dispersion, failed rallies, capitulation and reset quality.
2. **Technical repair** - pivot defense, reclaim, compression/release, higher-low structure, multi-timeframe maturation and invalidation.
3. **Cycle context** - market, sector, theme, commodity, liquidity, rates, currency, policy and historical phase.
4. **Economic survivability** - balance sheet, financing need, dilution risk, cost position, earnings/cash-flow trajectory and structural impairment.
5. **Positioning and fragility** - crowding, forced sellers, short/ownership facts, derivatives where lawful, liquidity and gap risk.
6. **Catalyst and expectation** - what can change consensus, when, and through which economic mechanism.
7. **Price incorporation** - how much of the expected recovery is already reflected.
8. **Residual opportunity** - what upside remains relative to invalidation and expected cycle path.
9. **Evidence quality** - freshness, coverage, source rights, corrections, disagreements and missingness.

Missing evidence abstains. It never becomes a favorable zero.

### 5.5 Entry law

Accumulation eligibility and current entry availability are separate.

A candidate may be `PIVOT_ELIGIBLE` while B4 says:

- `APPROACHING_ENTRY`;
- `ENTRY_OPEN`;
- `WAIT_PULLBACK`;
- `RAN_DONT_CHASE`;
- `INVALIDATED`;
- `UNAVAILABLE_DATA`.

Only the deterministic Availability owner may publish the current trade geometry. Intelligence strength cannot waive stale quotes, excessive extension, structural invalidation, poor liquidity, ambiguous corporate-action basis, or excessive risk to the invalidation point.

### 5.6 Position-building and conviction ladder

Prophet may describe a proposed staged process, but sizing remains downstream.

Conceptually:

```text
research watch
-> exploratory/starter eligibility
-> pivot-confirmed accumulation eligibility
-> core-hold eligibility
-> add-on-pullback eligibility
-> mature-trend hold
-> distribution/exit review
```

Each transition must cite the new evidence that changed the state. The system may not increase conviction merely because price rose.

### 5.7 Core-hold law

A cycle-capture strategy fails if it buys early and then exits the core because of routine daily noise. The hold architecture must therefore distinguish:

- **structural thesis state**;
- **cycle maturity**;
- **current tactical availability**;
- **short-term trading state**;
- **distribution or structural invalidation**.

The same canonical episode may support:

- a long-horizon core position;
- separately identified tactical adds on pullbacks;
- tactical trims or hedges;
- no change to the core thesis.

A tactical `RAN_DONT_CHASE` state may block a new add while the existing `CORE_HOLD` remains valid. Conversely, a mature trend cannot preserve a core position after the structural thesis has failed.

### 5.8 Leverage boundary

Early entry can create favorable geometry, but leverage magnifies model error, gap risk, liquidity risk, correlation, and false-pivot loss. Therefore:

- Prophet has zero leverage or sizing authority at sleeve birth;
- no technical state may be described as "impenetrable" in the machine contract;
- leverage is a downstream Portfolio/Risk decision over a promoted strategy;
- any later leveraged implementation must use portfolio-level exposure, correlation, stress, liquidity, stop/gap, volatility, concentration, financing, and drawdown constraints;
- risk must be evaluated across concurrent positions and common cycle exposures, not name by name;
- unlevered and risk-scaled controls are mandatory;
- leverage promotion requires prospective evidence, not only attractive historical examples.

The research question is not "could leverage have made the best historical trade enormous?" It is "under a frozen, point-in-time rule, did the strategy produce enough repeatable edge and controlled failure behavior to justify a bounded risk budget?"

### 5.9 Exit and maturity law

Exit review may be triggered by independently observed evidence such as:

- structural invalidation;
- failed pivot or failed retest;
- cycle or commodity economics turning;
- adverse earnings or balance-sheet change;
- distribution and deteriorating breadth;
- crowding/blowoff state;
- policy or liquidity regime change;
- valuation/price incorporation exhausting residual opportunity;
- thesis expiry;
- owner-defined risk limit.

A short-term oscillator rollover alone is insufficient to liquidate a valid long-horizon core unless the preregistered strategy explicitly says otherwise.

## 7. Evaluation architecture

### 7.1 Unit of evidence

The evaluation unit is the canonical candidate episode plus strategy/version/horizon identity. Detector events remain attached evidence. A single episode may contribute to several strategy cohorts, but their claims and outcomes are separately partitioned.

### 7.2 Required targets

Cycle capture requires more than forward return:

- lead time from first lawful observation to pivot and to broad confirmation;
- maximum adverse excursion before thesis validation or failure;
- maximum favorable excursion;
- hit-before-invalidation;
- false-pivot rate;
- time to recovery, confirmation, target, failure, and cycle maturity;
- cycle-capture ratio: realized/available move under the frozen hold law;
- holding efficiency and turnover;
- add-on quality versus core-only control;
- drawdown, tail loss, gap loss and liquidity cost;
- benchmark and sector/asset excess;
- performance by asset class, market, cycle type, era and evidence coverage;
- concentration and common-factor exposure;
- missed generational-move rate;
- abstention quality and correct-ignore cases;
- performance with no leverage, frozen risk scaling, and any later separately authorized sizing rule.

### 7.3 Mandatory controls

At minimum:

- current Prophet V3 same-tape output;
- existing washout observation alone;
- simple time-series trend or moving-average baseline;
- simple breakout/reclaim baseline;
- asset/sector benchmark buy-and-hold;
- anchor-, drawdown-, and volatility-matched random controls;
- late-confirmation entry control;
- core-only versus tactical-add variants;
- unlevered versus frozen risk-scaled control;
- honest transaction cost, spread, roll, financing and delisting treatment where applicable.

### 7.4 Scientific law

- membership, thresholds, horizons, targets, controls, nulls and multiple-testing law freeze before outcomes are opened;
- no per-name own-outcome audition;
- no after-the-fact choice of successful markets, bottoms, or bull cycles;
- no backfilled intraday evidence from EOD bars;
- identity epochs, delistings, survivorship, corporate actions and corrections remain point in time;
- asset classes receive separate calibration and may abstain;
- small-N generational cases remain case studies, not unstable probabilities;
- learned challengers stay shadow-only until the existing promotion gauntlet accepts them;
- LLM narratives and summaries have zero rank, gate, size, leverage, or trade authority at birth.

Academic and institutional method literature is a prior for experiment design, not evidence that Mastermind's specific implementation has alpha. Relevant method anchors include time-series momentum, long-history trend following, momentum-crash behavior in sharp rebounds, commodity-futures fundamentals, volatility-managed risk, long-term reversal, and risk-constrained Kelly-style sizing. None substitutes for point-in-time Mastermind evaluation.

- **Forming / Armed** - high-value setups approaching eligibility;

```text
instrument and market
cycle-capture sleeve + horizon

core thesis and expected response
what appears priced / remaining opportunity
invalidation and key risk
evidence coverage and scientific authority
```

Strategy disagreement is displayed as useful information. It is not averaged away.
