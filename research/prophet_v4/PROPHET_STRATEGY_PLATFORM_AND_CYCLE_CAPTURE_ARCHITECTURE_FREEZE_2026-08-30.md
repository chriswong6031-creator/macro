## 0. Executive order

Prophet will remain **one canonical opportunity platform** with **multiple governed strategy sleeves**.

It will not become:

- one universal all-weather score;
- one current-regime tactical system presented as the complete product;
- several disconnected Prophet applications with duplicate identity, evidence, lifecycle, availability, evaluation, plan, or publication truth.

The Chairman's founding long-horizon thesis is preserved:

> Some of the largest opportunities arise when an eligible cyclical asset has been deeply liquidated, a durable pivot begins forming, and an early position can be held through the high-value portion of the following bull cycle.

The current U.S. sideways/rotation market is important evidence about one environment. It is not the definition of all markets. Other markets, sectors, commodities, miners, and asset classes may reward early cycle capture rather than short tactical rotation. Whether Hong Kong, China-linked markets, commodities, miners, or another domain offer more technically legible cycles is a research hypothesis to test separately, not a reason to transfer U.S. constants.


## 1. What changed in the strategic understanding

### 1.1 The current hard market is a routing problem, not a universal product definition

The present environment can punish a slow confirmation strategy:

1. leadership rotates quickly;
2. daily confirmation arrives after a short move;
3. weekly follow-through fails to develop;
4. the plan inventory retains a thesis while capital rotates elsewhere;
5. Prophet appears to chase local tops and reverse stance repeatedly.

That failure mode justifies tactical rotation, policy/event, range-reversion, and early-entry sleeves. It does not invalidate the long-horizon strategy that motivated Prophet.

### 1.2 Cyclical markets pose a different user job

In a true cycle opportunity, the objective is not to trade every daily oscillation. It is to:

1. recognize a deep liquidation in an eligible market or instrument;
2. distinguish a cyclical washout from secular or permanent impairment;
3. identify a lawful base and pivot early enough for favorable geometry;
4. build conviction only as independent evidence arrives;
5. preserve a core position through normal volatility and trend maturation;
6. use tactical adds, trims, or hedges without confusing them with the core thesis;
7. reduce or exit when the cycle, economics, structure, distribution, or risk invalidates the original asymmetry.

### 1.3 The exact killed seed remains negative evidence

`DNR:KILL-WASHOUT-TURN` records that the old washout-times-turn two-week operator seed died in test. This architecture does not reopen it.

The founding strategy is broader and more demanding than that seed. CW0 must explicitly prove that any proposed Cycle Capture species is not:

- the same old washout/turn conjunction with new thresholds;
- a relabelled oscillator crossover;
- a rotation-times-cycle confluence prohibited by `DNR:KILL-ROTATION-CYCLE-CONFLUENCE`;
- a broad regime scorecard prohibited by `DNR:KILL-REGIME-SCORECARD`;
- a fused opportunity score prohibited by `DNR:KILL-FUSED-COMPOSITE`;
- a trigger-lane population merge prohibited by `DNR:KILL-PROPHET-POP-MERGE`.

The old construction should remain a baseline or negative control where scientifically useful.

## 2. Recovered founding intent

### 2.1 The Chairman's user job

The Chairman needs Prophet to support two distinct classes of decision:

#### Tactical opportunity

- What can move over the next sessions or weeks?
- Which leaders, events, policy dates, dislocations, or ranges are actionable now?
- When must the trade be closed quickly?

#### Cycle capture

- Which eligible markets or assets are undergoing major liquidation and base formation?
- Is a durable cycle turn becoming plausible before consensus recognizes it?
- Is the present price suitable for staged accumulation?
- What evidence supports holding a core position through trend maturation?
- What specifically invalidates the core thesis?

Neither job should erase the other.

### 2.2 The machine job for Cycle Capture

The machine must do more than detect an oversold state. It must:

- identify exact market, security, vehicle, and identity epoch;
- classify whether the instrument belongs to a legitimate cyclical opportunity class;
- determine the relevant cycle grain and completed-bar clock;
- measure depth, duration, breadth, dispersion, capitulation, failed rallies, liquidity, and structural damage;
- distinguish forced liquidation from permanent impairment, fraud, delisting, dilution, insolvency, or a broken commodity/economic thesis;
- locate the earliest lawful pivot evidence without reconstructing intraday history from later closes;
- attach all observations to one canonical B1 candidate episode;
- separate washout observation, base formation, pivot eligibility, trend confirmation, current Availability, and core-hold state;
- incorporate market, sector, theme, commodity, policy, liquidity, earnings, balance-sheet, supply/demand, and positioning evidence through their canonical owners;
- estimate what the market has already incorporated and what residual opportunity remains;
- preserve corrections, missingness, rights, stale inputs, contradictory evidence, and invalidations;
- distinguish a core-hold thesis from tactical add/trim opportunities;
- grade false pivots, missed turns, adverse paths, cycle capture, concentration, costs, and tail risk;
- remain inactive when the market, data, or instrument does not qualify.

### 2.3 The moat

The moat is the governed longitudinal relationship among:

- identity and instrument class;
- candidate episode;
- liquidation, base, pivot, and cycle state;
- economic survivability and catalyst path;
- market, sector, theme, commodity, policy, and liquidity context;
- price incorporation and residual opportunity;
- current deterministic Availability;
- strategy and horizon identity;
- operator accumulation/hold decisions;
- corrections, failures, outcomes, and controls.

A crossover is copyable. A correction-safe, point-in-time, cross-market opportunity and learning system is much harder to reproduce.

## 6. Cyclical Washout Accumulation / Cycle Capture

### 6.1 Core epistemic separation

The system must never treat these as synonyms:

```text
washout observed
base forming
pivot evidence observed
accumulation thesis eligible
entry available now
trend confirmed
core-hold thesis active
cycle mature
distribution risk rising
structural invalidation
exit required
```

A descriptive `WASHOUT_TURN` row is evidence, not a bottom claim.

### 6.2 What makes this strategy genuinely different from the killed seed

A lawful Cycle Capture species must include independent and explicit answers to:

- Is the instrument an eligible cyclical exposure?
- Is the decline a liquidation/cycle event rather than permanent impairment?
- Is the exact identity, listing, contract, roll, and corporate-action basis valid?
- Is a base/pivot process present through accepted technical-owner evidence?
- What economic, policy, supply/demand, earnings, balance-sheet, or liquidity mechanism can sustain recovery?
- What does the market already price?
- What residual opportunity remains?
- Is a current entry or add available under B4?
- What keeps a core hold valid through normal volatility?
- What terminates the thesis?
- What point-in-time evidence and controls will measure it?

If the proposed species can be reduced to "deep washout + turn over two weeks," it is the killed construction and must be rejected.

### 6.3 Market and instrument eligibility

The sleeve is not universally applied.

#### U.S. equities

Potential cases may include:

- liquid cyclicals after industry liquidation;
- commodity producers after an underlying commodity cycle washout;
- high-quality leaders after broad deleveraging or valuation compression;
- sectors with synchronized damage but improving economic/earnings state;
- forced-selling dislocations with survivable balance sheets and exact catalysts.

Broad U.S. washouts may be rare. Inactivity is acceptable.

#### Hong Kong and China-linked markets

These require separate listing, policy, capital-flow, currency, price-limit, suspension, liquidity, identity, and technical calibration. The Chairman's hypothesis that their cycles can be more technically legible is preserved for testing, not assumed.

#### Commodities, ETFs, producers, and miners

These are different instruments.

Commodity analysis may require:

- futures curve/carry and roll;
- inventory and supply response;
- cost curves and producer discipline;
- currency and real rates;
- demand and policy transmission;
- contract liquidity and basis.

Producer/miner equity analysis additionally requires:

- operating leverage;
- jurisdiction;
- balance sheet and financing;
- dilution;
- hedging;
- mine/asset quality and execution.

A future, an ETF, and a leveraged producer may not share one entry or risk contract.

#### Crypto and other high-volatility assets

Held outside the first program unless current owner/data law proves a distinct market-structure, liquidity, custody, gap, basis, and leverage contract.

### 6.4 Descriptive occurrence and strategy state

TOI remains the technical occurrence owner. Cycle Capture is a strategy projection over accepted owner states and B1 episodes, not a new lifecycle database.

Conceptual strategy state:

```text
OBSERVING
  -> LIQUIDATION_DEEPENING
  -> BASE_FORMING
  -> PIVOT_ELIGIBLE
  -> ACCUMULATION_OPEN
  -> CORE_HOLD
  -> MATURE_TREND
  -> DISTRIBUTION_REVIEW
  -> TERMINAL
```

Typed adverse or unavailable states:

```text
FAILED_PIVOT
STRUCTURAL_INVALIDATION
FUNDAMENTAL_IMPAIRMENT
CYCLE_THESIS_BROKEN
LIQUIDITY_UNAVAILABLE
IDENTITY_OR_BASIS_UNRESOLVED
DATA_UNAVAILABLE
RIGHTS_BLOCKED
THESIS_EXPIRED
```

The strategy state cannot overwrite the underlying TOI, Radar, B1, B4, earnings, theme, or market-state owner value.

### 6.5 Required evidence domains

A serious case should expose, without necessarily scoring:

1. **Liquidation depth and path** - drawdown, duration, volatility, breadth, dispersion, capitulation, failed rallies and reset quality;
2. **Technical repair** - pivot defense, reclaim, compression/release, higher-low structure, multi-timeframe maturation and invalidation;
3. **Cycle context** - market, sector, theme, commodity, liquidity, rates, currency, policy and historical phase;
4. **Economic survivability** - balance sheet, financing need, dilution, cost position, earnings/cash-flow trajectory and structural impairment;
5. **Positioning and fragility** - forced sellers, ownership/short facts, crowding, derivatives where lawful, liquidity and gap risk;
6. **Catalyst and expectation** - what can change consensus and through which mechanism;
7. **Price incorporation** - how much of the expected recovery appears reflected;
8. **Residual opportunity** - what remains relative to invalidation and the expected cycle path;
9. **Evidence quality** - clocks, freshness, coverage, rights, corrections, disagreements and missingness.

Missing evidence abstains. It never becomes a favorable zero.

### 6.6 Entry law

Accumulation thesis eligibility and present actionability are different.

A `PIVOT_ELIGIBLE` episode may currently be:

- `APPROACHING_ENTRY`;
- `ENTRY_OPEN`;
- `WAIT_PULLBACK`;
- `RAN_DONT_CHASE`;
- `INVALIDATED`;
- `UNAVAILABLE_DATA`.

Only B4 may publish current entry/add geometry. No intelligence, cycle conviction, ranker, model, or LLM may waive stale data, excessive extension, invalidation, poor liquidity, ambiguous basis, excessive risk, or an unavailable input.

### 6.7 Position-building ladder

Prophet may describe eligibility for a staged process, but capital allocation remains downstream:

```text
research watch
-> exploratory/starter eligibility
-> pivot-confirmed accumulation eligibility
-> core-hold eligibility
-> add-on-pullback eligibility
-> mature-trend hold
-> distribution/exit review
```

Every transition must cite new evidence. Price appreciation alone is not new independent evidence and cannot mechanically increase conviction.

### 6.8 Core-hold and tactical state

Cycle Capture fails if an early position is liquidated on routine daily noise. It also fails if a long-horizon story excuses structural impairment.

The system must keep separate:

- structural thesis state;
- cycle maturity;
- present tactical Availability;
- existing core position state;
- tactical add/trim/hedge state;
- distribution and structural invalidation.

Examples:

- `CORE_HOLD` may remain valid while a new add is `RAN_DONT_CHASE`;
- a pullback may open a tactical add without creating a second candidate episode;
- a tactical trim does not silently terminate the core thesis;
- structural invalidation terminates the core even when a short-term oscillator turns upward.

### 6.9 Leverage boundary

Early pivot geometry can be favorable, but leverage magnifies false-pivot, gap, liquidity, correlation, financing, concentration, and model risk.

Therefore:

- Prophet has zero sizing or leverage authority at sleeve birth;
- the machine contract never describes a position as "impenetrable";
- any later leverage decision belongs to downstream Portfolio/Risk authority;
- a promoted strategy is a prerequisite, not sufficient permission;
- portfolio-level correlation, common cycle exposure, liquidity, stop/gap risk, volatility, financing, concentration, stress and drawdown constraints are mandatory;
- unlevered and frozen risk-scaled controls are mandatory;
- no best historical winner can justify leverage by itself.

The scientific question is whether a frozen point-in-time strategy has repeatable edge and controlled failure behavior, not whether leverage would have amplified famous winners.

### 6.10 Exit and maturity law

Exit review may arise from:

- structural invalidation or failed retest;
- cycle/commodity economics turning;
- adverse earnings, balance-sheet, dilution, or financing change;
- distribution and deteriorating breadth;
- crowding/blowoff;
- policy, liquidity, rates, currency, or demand regime change;
- price incorporation exhausting residual opportunity;
- thesis expiry;
- downstream risk limit.

A short-term oscillator rollover alone is not a core exit unless the preregistered sleeve says it is.

## 9. Evaluation architecture

### 9.1 Unit of evidence

The unit is:

```text
canonical candidate episode
+ strategy_id/version
+ horizon
+ first lawful surface/eligibility time
+ Availability observations
+ operator actions
+ path-aware outcomes and controls
```

Detector events remain attached evidence. Several strategies may attach to one episode, but their claims and denominators remain separate.

### 9.2 Cycle Capture targets

Required measures include:

- lead time from first lawful observation to pivot and broad confirmation;
- MFE and MAE;
- hit-before-invalidation;
- false-pivot rate;
- time to recovery, confirmation, failure, target and maturity;
- cycle-capture ratio under the frozen hold law;
- holding efficiency and turnover;
- tactical-add quality versus core-only control;
- benchmark and sector/asset excess;
- transaction, spread, roll, financing and slippage costs where applicable;
- max drawdown, tail loss, gap loss and liquidity failure;
- concentration and common-factor exposure;
- performance by market, instrument class, cycle type, era, regime and evidence coverage;
- missed-major-cycle rate;
- abstention quality and correct-ignore cases;
- unlevered and frozen risk-scaled variants;
- effective N and cluster concentration.

### 9.3 Mandatory controls

At minimum:

- current Prophet V3 same-tape output where applicable;
- the exact killed `DNR:KILL-WASHOUT-TURN` seed as negative/control evidence where reconstructible;
- existing descriptive washout observation alone;
- simple moving-average/time-series trend;
- simple breakout/reclaim;
- asset/sector benchmark buy-and-hold;
- anchor-, drawdown-, and volatility-matched random controls;
- late-confirmation entry;
- core-only versus tactical-add variants;
- unlevered versus frozen risk-scaled variants;
- explicit abstention/no-trade control.

### 9.4 Scientific law

- thresholds, markets, horizons, targets, controls, nulls and multiple-testing law freeze before final outcomes are opened;
- no per-name own-outcome audition;
- no selection of only famous winners, successful markets, or completed bull cycles;
- known Chairman examples and prior inspected periods are development-visible, not OOS;
- no backfilled intraday evidence from EOD data;
- identity epochs, delistings, survivorship, suspensions, corporate actions and corrections remain point in time;
- asset classes receive separate calibration and may abstain;
- small-N generational cases remain cases, not unstable probabilities;
- learned challengers remain shadow-only until existing promotion accepts them;
- LLM narratives have zero rank, gate, size, leverage or trade authority under `DNR:KILL-LLM-ORIGINATION`;
- no fused composite or broad regime scorecard.

Academic and institutional evidence on time-series momentum, long-history trend following, momentum crashes, commodity-futures fundamentals, long-term reversal, volatility-managed portfolios, and risk-constrained Kelly methods is a prior for experiment design only. It does not validate Mastermind's implementation.
