## 0. Executive ruling

Prophet should evolve from a technical candidate board into Mastermind's **Opportunity Inference Engine**:

> **What is beginning to change → why is it changing → where should that change propagate → which securities have not fully reflected it → which still have valid entry geometry → which are most asymmetric now?**

This document does **not** authorize a new Prophet architecture. The V4 architecture already made the core decomposition correctly:

- Discovery is high recall.
- Maturity describes confirmation.
- Availability is deterministic and binding.
- Intelligence explains priority/asymmetry.
- Uncertainty discloses coverage/freshness/quality.
- Outcome/Eval OS decides whether a research family earns more authority.

The correct next move is **not** twelve new subsystems. It is a federated research program that strengthens existing canonical owners and fills only genuine ownership gaps.

### Binding fan-out law

1. **Linear is dispatch/current-queue state, not canonical architecture.** Detailed research lives under `research/`; cross-session authority and handoffs live in Agent OS.
2. **Research may fan out immediately.** Fresh Sol sessions may do read-only archaeology, external/primary-source research, experimental design and architecture work in parallel.
3. **No research cell gains mutation authority by existing.** Before any implementation, the session must reconcile current `main`, open PRs, Agent OS and the canonical owner.
4. **When an owner exists, extend it. Do not rebuild it.** GMI owns the graph; Market Memory owns horizontal episode/analogue memory; Conditional Fusion owns cross-family fusion machinery; Eval OS owns promotion evidence; specialist lobes own domain truth.
5. **Prophet owns composition, not source truth.** It consumes versioned evidence adapters and keeps entry availability separate from intelligence.
6. **A compelling backtest is not promotion.** Every statistical/model-derived family must pass PIT replay and forward evidence under the canonical ruler.
7. **No monolithic Prophet Score.** Preserve an intelligence vector / named heads. A learned ranker may consume the vector later, but it must not erase provenance, missingness or authority distinctions.
8. **No active lane interference.** If a cell finds an overlapping active PR/session, it records the delta and stops before mutation unless the canonical owner explicitly adopts the work.

# 3. Research Cell A — Theme Intelligence: Exposure, Transmission & Emergent Taxonomy

**Covers:** ideas 2, 3, 11  
**Canonical owner:** `WS:GMI-THEME-GRAPH`; V4 D2→D3→D4  
**Mutation authority now:** NONE. Research/architecture only until D2B3 natural proof and later owner gates.

## Observable mission

Produce a flagship-grade architecture and experimental program that upgrades GMI from static company↔theme membership into a **typed, multi-axis, point-in-time economic exposure and transmission graph** capable of answering:

1. *How is this company exposed to this microtheme?*
2. *How strong/central is that exposure, under which measurement axis, and at what confidence?*
3. *Is the theme actually accelerating now?*
4. *By which economic relationship should the impulse transmit to this company?*
5. *How quickly has that type of relationship historically transmitted?*
6. *Has the company already moved enough to plausibly incorporate the impulse?*
7. *Can new candidate microthemes be proposed from converging evidence without corrupting canonical taxonomy?*

## Required archaeology

- W2 exposure-axis prereg/probe and every current consumer.
- V4 D1/D2 records, ThemeState feasibility and D4 transmission freeze.
- Neural Web `thematic_state`, Theme Tape, Foresight/Forming Narratives, subsector rotation.
- Existing relationship types and evidence/correction clocks in GMI.
- Data OS/FIF/Earnings segment/product/financial facts that could support economic exposure.
- External primary methodology from FactSet RBICS, MSCI thematic/strategy exposure, Kensho-style NLP classification and other lawful public methodology sources. Learn the job-to-be-done, not proprietary taxonomy/code.

## Hard research questions

### A1. Exposure axes v2
Do **not** create a single “theme percentage.” Determine which axes are actually estimable:

- direct revenue/economic share;
- gross-profit/operating-profit exposure where supportable;
- capex exposure;
- customer/supplier dependency;
- trading beta / residual sensitivity;
- management/filing attention share;
- product/strategic optionality;
- network centrality / role;
- geographic/policy exposure.

For each axis specify: source, PIT law, denominator, null semantics, revision behavior, confidence and whether it is descriptive or promotable.

### A2. Segment revenue/product ingestion
Design the smallest truthful vertical that can make `economic_share` non-null for a real diversified issuer without fabricating splits. Compare SEC segment disclosures, company-reported segment revenue, XBRL dimensional facts and lawful vendor/public sources. Explicitly handle:

- segments that do not map 1:1 to themes;
- one segment participating in several themes;
- eliminated/intersegment revenue;
- segment-definition changes;
- restatements;
- missing product revenue;
- revenue vs profit vs capex exposure disagreement.

### A3. Transmission ontology
Define typed mechanisms, not generic graph adjacency:

- supplier → customer;
- customer capex → supplier demand;
- competitor/substitute;
- shared commodity input;
- financing/rate sensitivity;
- distribution/platform dependence;
- regulatory/policy dependence;
- peer read-through;
- geographic demand;
- shared end-market;
- capacity constraint/bottleneck;
- funding/appropriation chain.

Every mechanism must state expected sign *if any*, expected lag hypothesis, evidence owner and falsifier. “Related” is not a causal mechanism.

### A4. Transmission experiments
Design PIT-safe tests that distinguish:

- theme-wide common factor;
- relationship-specific incremental propagation;
- contemporaneous co-movement;
- genuine lagged read-through;
- post-event leakage.

Include negative controls, dead links, unrelated same-sector controls, stable membership vintages and correction replay.

### A5. Emergent microtheme proposal system
Research a **proposal**, not auto-authority, pipeline:

`candidate cluster → machine evidence packet → ontology novelty test → duplicate/parent/theme collision check → rights/identity review → human/curator ratification → probation → canonical theme`.

Inputs may include filings/calls, capex language, procurement, product launches, co-movement and graph-neighbor concentration. The model may propose names/definitions but cannot silently mint a canonical theme.

## Deliverable

A new research artifact under `research/theme_graph/` containing:

- capability ledger;
- exposure-axis v2 contract candidate;
- segment-ingestion architecture and first vertical recommendation;
- transmission ontology + experiment matrix;
- emergent-theme proposal/ratification architecture;
- current-vs-new map to avoid rebuilding existing GMI/Neural Web functions;
- exact prerequisites for D3/D4 and what can be deferred;
- 3–5 bounded vertical implementation waves, **not authorized by the research itself**.

## Stop condition

Return to Sol with the architecture, falsifiers and owner-routed waves. No graph/runtime/schema mutation.

# 4. Research Cell B — Evidence–Price Gap / Dislocation → Future Prophet Adapter

**Covers:** idea 1 and part of idea 4  
**Canonical owner:** `WS:ALPHA-INTELLIGENCE-INTEGRATION` / Cross-Issuer Dislocation  
**Mutation authority now:** NONE outside the already-authorized Dislocation program. Do not contaminate P0.

## Observable mission

Determine whether Mastermind can lawfully and statistically estimate an **information-incorporation gap**:

> Given the magnitude/type of newly knowable evidence and historically comparable episodes, did this security's residual price respond less, roughly as much, or more than expected?

This is intentionally narrower than intrinsic valuation and must not claim a precise fair price.

## Binding blindness law

The current Dislocation source extraction remains technically unable to see price/outcomes until its frozen manifest exists. This research cell must **not** introduce candidate labels, tuning, feature selection or taxonomy changes using P0 outcomes.

## Research questions

1. What exactly is the target: residual return gap, standardized surprise-response gap, expected impulse vs realized impulse, or a set of separate observations?
2. What is the correct baseline return to subtract: market, sector, theme factor, matched peers, synthetic control or a preregistered hierarchy?
3. How do we avoid circularity when theme returns themselves contain the candidate security?
4. How should event/evidence magnitude be normalized across earnings revisions, procurement, clinical milestones, fundamental changes and relationship read-through?
5. Can we estimate response distributions by event family/species without enough observations? Define abstention/estimability law.
6. How do we separate **underreaction** from “the market correctly judged this evidence immaterial/low quality”?
7. How do we separate underreaction from liquidity/market microstructure delay?
8. What happens when several evidence packets arrive in one attribution window?
9. What is the correct time horizon: minutes, day, 5D, 20D, family-specific?
10. What output should Prophet eventually consume: `incorporation_state`, interval/percentile, residual response z, or purely descriptive decomposition?

## Candidate deterministic formulation to attack, not assume

`expected_response_pressure = issuer_specific_surprise + Σ(theme_impulse × exposure_axis) + Σ(relationship_impulse × transmission_weight)`

compared with a PIT-safe residualized realized response.

The research must attempt to **kill** this formula. Simpler baselines must win if they explain the same information.

## Deliverable

Under `research/dislocation_intelligence/`:

- current Dislocation capability map and non-interference proof;
- target estimand alternatives;
- leakage and causal-identification threat model;
- baseline hierarchy and controls;
- estimability census plan;
- output contract candidate that remains context-only;
- exact promotion sequence from blinded research → replay → forward shadow → optional Prophet D5 adapter.

## Stop condition

No Prophet integration; no P0 retuning; return architecture to Sol/Dislocation owner.

# 6. Research Cell D — Stock Species, Empirical Priors & Selective Regime Conditioning

**Covers:** ideas 5, 8, narrowed 10  
**Owners:** Stock Identity, Market Memory, Market OS/regime owners, future Prophet E4  
**Mutation authority now:** NONE.

## Observable mission

Determine whether Prophet can improve precision by conditioning interpretation on **behavioral/economic species and empirically comparable historical episodes**, while refusing regime conditioning where the data cannot support interaction estimates.

## Ruling on regime idea

The broad “every family gets a current-regime reliability multiplier” concept is **rejected as a default architecture**. Prior Mastermind research found redundancy, weak interaction effects and large unestimable cells. The research cell may only reopen narrower hypotheses after an estimability census.

## Research questions

### D1. What is a useful species?
A species must change a decision-relevant conditional distribution or expert relevance. Possible axes to evaluate:

- binary-event biotech;
- government-contract / backlog conversion businesses;
- commodity producers;
- cyclicals;
- high-duration growth;
- mature compounders;
- REITs;
- semis / capital equipment;
- financials;
- China/HK policy-sensitive names;
- high-short/high-volatility reflexive names;
- asset-heavy vs asset-light;
- profitability/financing dependence.

Do not encode industry labels as species unless they actually improve routing or estimability.

### D2. Species vs continuous fingerprint
Compare:

- discrete archetype routing;
- continuous behavioral fingerprints;
- hierarchical routing;
- mixture-of-experts challenger;
- nearest-neighbor/analogue retrieval without hard classes.

### D3. Analogue retrieval
Design the Market Memory query object for a candidate episode:

- identity/species constraints;
- setup/emergence geometry;
- catalyst/evidence family;
- theme state;
- market cap/liquidity;
- regime only where estimable;
- availability state;
- source-clock and membership vintage.

Define distance, counterexamples, minimum N, no-match and “too heterogeneous” abstention.

### D4. Outcome display before authority
The first useful output may be descriptive:

- comparable episode count;
- median/interval MFE/MAE;
- failure/false-bounce rate;
- median time to payoff;
- nearest positive and negative counterexamples;
- coverage/selection caveats.

Do not immediately turn the analogue distribution into a trade probability.

### D5. Regime reopening gate
Any conditional regime hypothesis must state:

- exact interaction;
- prior reason to expect effect;
- minimum cell N/power;
- source and clock;
- control/alternative;
- preregistered direction if applicable;
- forward validation plan.

## Deliverable

- species/archetype capability census;
- estimability matrix;
- recommended routing representation;
- Market Memory analogue query/response contract candidate;
- regime hypotheses accepted/rejected/deferred;
- E4 prerequisite list.

## Stop condition

No router/model implementation and no new memory store.

# 10. Research Cell H — Product Experience: From Evidence to a 5-Second Decision

**Status:** DESIGN-RESEARCH FOLLOWER. Start only after Cells A/F establish stable candidate contracts; may be explored read-only beforehand.  
**Owner:** Prophet product shell / existing MP-1 design authority.

## Mission

Design what the Chairman should actually see when a flagship candidate appears. The product must distinguish:

- **Why now?**
- **What changed first?**
- **Which independent evidence families agree?**
- **What is still unconfirmed?**
- **What has already been priced?**
- **What could invalidate the thesis?**
- **Why is this stock connected to the active theme/catalyst?**
- **Is the entry actually open now?**
- **What historical analogues/counterexamples exist?**

Reference composition target:

> “Data-center power/cooling demand is accelerating. This issuer has economically verified exposure; two customers raised capex; its peer group has repriced while the issuer has moved only modestly; an early technical turn fired; current geometry remains ENTRY_OPEN. Fragility is low/medium; crowding is not yet elevated. Evidence confidence is X because Y is missing.”

Counterexample:

> “Thesis strong; opportunity unavailable. The security already moved 28%, options are crowded and price is beyond the deterministic chase firewall. WAIT_PULLBACK.”

This design work must never imply precision the underlying contracts do not support.

# 16. End-state

The flagship Prophet should not merely say “bullish candidate.” It should eventually be able to distinguish:

- a good company;
- a company whose evidence state just improved;
- an accelerating theme;
- a company economically exposed to that theme;
- a relationship through which an impulse should propagate;
- an underreacted security;
- a scheduled/material catalyst;
- a crowded or fragile setup;
- a technically emerging candidate;
- and an **actionable entry now**.

Those are separate epistemic objects. The system becomes powerful when it learns how they interact **without collapsing them into one unverifiable number**.

The durable moat is therefore the accumulating empirical map of:

> **which kinds of evidence matter → for which kinds of companies → in which contexts → in what sequence → through which relationships → with what lag → and at what point the market has already incorporated enough of the information that the opportunity is gone.**

This research fan-out exists to build that map systematically while preserving Mastermind's one-system/no-rebuild and prospective-promotion laws.
