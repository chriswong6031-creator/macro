# 1. Chairman outcome — the thing Prophet must ultimately become

Prophet should become Mastermind's **Opportunity Inference Engine**.

Its primary continuous question is:

> **What is beginning to change → why is it changing → where should that change propagate → which securities have not fully reflected it → which still have valid entry geometry → which are most asymmetric now?**

This is meaningfully different from:

- a technical screener;
- a news feed;
- a sentiment score;
- a thematic basket builder;
- a fundamental stock ranker;
- a generic AI “buy/sell” opinion;
- an intrinsic-value calculator;
- an options-flow scanner;
- a black-box return predictor.

The end-state Prophet recognizes that several distinct questions must be answered separately:

1. **Discovery:** did something begin changing?
2. **Identity / relevance:** which economic issuer/security/theme/relationship does that evidence actually belong to?
3. **Exposure:** how economically exposed is the company, under which axis?
4. **Transmission:** through what mechanism should the evidence propagate?
5. **Expectation:** what did the market/analysts/company appear to expect beforehand?
6. **Surprise:** what changed relative to that expectation?
7. **Materiality:** how important is the change to this issuer rather than merely headline-large?
8. **Incorporation:** how much of the expected response appears already reflected in price?
9. **Fragility:** what structural or path risks could break the opportunity?
10. **Crowding / attention:** who may already be in the trade, and how intensely is it being watched?
11. **Timing:** is an early technical/behavioral transition occurring?
12. **Availability:** is the trade still buyable under deterministic geometry/freshness/invalidation rules?
13. **Priority:** among opportunities in the same lawful availability lane, which deserve human attention first?
14. **Learning:** did each evidence family actually improve early actionable winner capture prospectively?

No one number is allowed to erase these distinctions.

# 2. The flagship product thesis

## 2.1 The user job

The primary investor/research user needs to discover important changes **before the opportunity has already been consumed by price**, understand why the candidate exists, know whether the evidence is independent or redundant, see what is still unknown, and decide whether the setup is actionable now.

The user should be able to answer in roughly five seconds:

- **Can I act now?**
- **Why is this here now?**
- **What actually changed?**
- **Which independent evidence supports the thesis?**
- **What is the biggest risk / missing fact?**
- **Has the price already run?**

A deeper investigation should then expose the entire evidence chain, relationship graph, expectation baseline, corrections, analogous episodes, counterexamples and receipts.

## 2.2 The machine job

The machine must continuously:

- ingest correction-safe owner evidence;
- preserve known-at / captured-at / effective-at / corrected-at distinctions;
- identify candidate episodes at high recall;
- join evidence to canonical issuer/security identity;
- calculate only estimable exposure/transmission/expectation/materiality features;
- keep missing/uncovered/inapplicable distinct from neutral;
- compare observed price response against lawful baselines;
- apply deterministic availability independently;
- prioritize within availability lanes using explainable evidence;
- record forward outcomes on one canonical evaluation plane;
- promote, demote or retire features based on prospective evidence.

## 2.3 The moat

The moat is **not any single model**.

It is the accumulated, correction-safe integration of:

- exact identity and provenance;
- point-in-time evidence;
- dense multi-axis company/theme exposure;
- typed economic relationships and transmission history;
- specialist domain intelligence;
- expectation and revision baselines;
- empirical analogue memory;
- price-state / entry timing;
- deterministic actionability;
- prospective outcome evidence;
- user-feedback learning;
- an increasing history of what Mastermind knew, when it knew it, and what happened next.

As this grows, a competitor must reproduce both the present system and the historical evidence clock. That historical clock cannot be vibe-coded retroactively without leakage.

# 6. The central intelligence decomposition

The final Prophet reasoning spine is:

```text
SOURCE TRUTH
   ↓
EVIDENCE CHANGE
   ↓
CANONICAL IDENTITY
   ↓
ECONOMIC EXPOSURE + RELATIONSHIP MECHANISM
   ↓
EXPECTED INFORMATION / RESPONSE PRESSURE
   ↓
OBSERVED PRICE RESPONSE, RESIDUALIZED
   ↓
INCORPORATION STATE
   ↓
FRAGILITY + CROWDING + EMPIRICAL PRIORS
   ↓
EARLY TECHNICAL / BEHAVIORAL STATE
   ↓
DETERMINISTIC AVAILABILITY FIREWALL
   ↓
EXPLAINABLE PRIORITY WITHIN AVAILABILITY LANES
   ↓
PROSPECTIVE OUTCOME + VALUE-OF-INFORMATION LEARNING
```

Three concepts are especially important and must never be collapsed:

### A. Economic exposure

**How much and by what mechanism is the issuer/security economically connected to the evidence source?**

### B. Expected response pressure

**Given evidence magnitude, expectation surprise, issuer materiality and economic exposure, how much directional pressure would comparable history suggest should exist?**

This is *not* intrinsic fair value.

### C. Observed price incorporation

**How much relevant price response has already occurred after removing ordinary market/sector/theme/style movement and respecting the actual tradable clock?**

The possible inference is then not “fair price = $X,” but:

- `UNDER_INCORPORATED`
- `PLAUSIBLY_INCORPORATED`
- `OVER_INCORPORATED / EXTENDED`
- `CONFLICTED`
- `UNESTIMABLE`

Those states require empirical calibration. They are not born as authority.

- valid interval;
- first-known clock;
- current confidence;

# 13. Catalyst federation — fact, expectation, surprise, materiality, incorporation

All catalyst domains should answer the same *questions* without using the same *method*.

## 13.1 Event fact

What occurred / is scheduled / changed?

Domain owner examples:

- Earnings: report, guidance, Q&A, KPI disclosure.
- Bio: trial milestone, readout, regulatory action when lawfully sourced.
- Defense: award, modification, appropriation, program milestone.
- Capital structure: filing, ATM, registration, financing, maturity.

## 13.2 Expectation baseline

What was expected *before* the event?

Possible domain sources:

- analyst consensus / detailed KPIs;
- prior company guidance;
- sell-side range/dispersion where rights permit;
- historical cadence;
- market-implied expected move for options where coverage is lawful;
- explicit program/budget baselines;
- specialist domain prior.

No expectation source = `EXPECTATION_UNAVAILABLE`, not surprise zero.

## 13.3 Surprise

`actual - expectation`, under a domain-defined basis.

A surprise is meaningful only when actual and expectation are basis-matched.

## 13.4 Issuer materiality

How much does the event matter to this issuer's economic state?

A large headline amount can be low materiality; a small event can be high materiality to a small issuer.

Materiality can depend on:

- revenue/profit/cash-flow scale;
- asset/program concentration;
- funding/runway;
- probability-adjusted future economics **only if the domain owns a validated method**;
- strategic bottleneck / option value;
- revision of prior expectation;
- persistence.

## 13.5 Incorporation

Cell B / downstream price analysis asks whether the material information has already moved the security enough relative to an appropriate baseline.

The domain owner does not silently originate that price-gap authority.

# 14. Expected response pressure — formal research object

Do **not** start with a universal scalar formula in production.

For research, define an inspectable conceptual object:

```text
ResponsePressure(i,t)
  = f(
      evidence_magnitude,
      surprise,
      issuer_materiality,
      economic_exposure,
      transmission_mechanism,
      historical_response_prior,
      evidence_novelty,
      persistence,
      confidence
    )
```

The function may initially be a set of named components rather than a scalar.

For a theme impulse, one testable baseline resembles:

```text
ThemePressure(i,t)
  = Σ_k exposure(i,k,t_known) × residual_theme_impulse(k,t)
```

For a relationship graph:

```text
TransmissionPressure(i,t)
  = Σ_j,m shock(j,t) × exposure(i←j,m,t_known) × mechanism_weight(m)
```

`mechanism_weight` is empirical, not hand-authored alpha authority.

If insufficient evidence exists to estimate a component, preserve the component as null rather than using a default that impersonates knowledge.

# 15. Observed price incorporation

Observed response must be measured on the correct economic/tradable clock.

Candidate baselines, increasingly demanding:

1. raw return;
2. market excess;
3. sector/industry excess;
4. theme-factor excess;
5. multi-factor specific/residual return;
6. matched peer basket;
7. synthetic control;
8. episode-specific counterfactual where preregistered.

No single baseline is universal.

The research question is whether the conclusion is robust across defensible baselines, not which baseline makes the backtest look best.

### Incorporation object

```text
ObservedResponse(i,t0→t)
ExpectedPressure(i,t0→t)
GapState = calibrate(ExpectedPressure, ObservedResponse | family, species, horizon)
```

Possible output states:

- `UNDER_INCORPORATED`

- `OVER_INCORPORATED`
- `REVERSING`
- `CONFLICTED_BASELINES`
- `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`
- `UNESTIMABLE`

No precise upside percentage is implied by this state unless a separately validated model earns that authority.

# 19. Fragility — keep opportunity and danger simultaneously visible

Three separate families:

## 19.1 Structural fragility

Examples:

- funding/runway;
- refinancing/maturity wall;
- dilution/ATM/ELOC/registration capacity;
- leverage / covenant / liquidity;
- customer/supplier concentration;
- accounting/forensic concern;
- litigation/regulatory exposure;
- commodity/input vulnerability;
- asset concentration.

## 19.2 Path fragility

Examples:

- gap risk;
- extreme extension;
- low liquidity;
- event binary risk;
- volatility/expected-move mismatch;
- unstable technical state;
- known near-term catalyst that can invalidate the entry path.

## 19.3 Crowding / positioning

Examples:

- options OI/skew/term structure where coverage is complete enough;
- short interest / borrow;
- institutional concentration;
- ETF/passive concentration;
- attention/media acceleration;
- ownership overlap;
- prior move / factor crowding.

Fragility does not automatically mean bearish.

Crowding does not automatically mean avoid.

The end-state can lawfully say:

> **Opportunity strong · structural fragility high · entry open**

and force the human to understand the tension.

Intelligence may explain why a candidate deserves research attention.

Intelligence may not turn a chased candidate green.

A perfect thesis at a terrible entry is `WAIT_PULLBACK` / `RAN_DONT_CHASE`, not `ENTRY_OPEN`.

---

# 23. Priority — rank inside lawful lanes

The first flagship priority mechanism should be deterministic and explainable.

It may use named evidence heads / rules / monotonic transformations, but must not collapse everything into an unexplained public score.

Priority should answer:

> “Among candidates with the same actionability state, which deserve attention first?”

A ranker should not compare an `ENTRY_OPEN` candidate against a `RAN_DONT_CHASE` candidate as if the latter could win by having stronger fundamentals.

Later learned models remain challengers until promotion.

# 24. Modeling ladder — complexity must earn its existence

## Stage 0 — deterministic truth

Identity, clocks, evidence extraction, availability, lifecycle, typed nulls.

## Stage 1 — deterministic explainable retrieval / ranking

Named rules and evidence-head composition.

## Stage 2 — regularized/tabular/listwise challenger

Examples: monotonic / regularized models, gradient boosted listwise ranking. Optimize ranking metrics and path metrics, not raw next-period return alone.

## Stage 3 — conditional router / multi-head

Only if Stock Identity / estimability proves heterogeneous local behavior.

## Stage 4 — temporal heterogeneous graph challenger

Only after:

- graph identity is clean;
- edge types are meaningful;
- histories are PIT;
- deterministic transmission baseline exists;
- Stage 2/3 establish a benchmark worth beating.

A GNN is not a milestone by itself.

## Stage 5 — promotion

Only after prospective evidence under Eval OS / Fusion gauntlets.

# 25. Why the model target is not “future return”

Optimizing only for future return creates predictable failure modes:

- surfaces winners after most of the move;
- rewards high-beta continuation;
- ignores MAE / path risk;
- learns availability indirectly and unreliably;
- overweights crowded themes;
- makes a later candidate appear “more accurate.”

Flagship targets should include:

- rank relevance / top-K discovery;
- lead time;
- first-surface entry availability;
- opportunity-loss / chase rate;
- MFE;
- MAE;
- R;
- time to payoff;
- false-bounce rate;
- invalidation rate;
- tail loss;
- abstention quality;
- calibration;
- coverage;
- human review burden.

---

# 26. North-star metric — Early Actionable Winner Capture (EAWC)

EAWC is a family of metrics, not one vanity scalar.

At minimum report:

- **Early Winner Recall:** fraction of later-defined winners surfaced before a frozen move-consumption threshold.
- **Actionable-at-First-Surface:** fraction first surfaced while availability was lawful.
- **Lead Time:** trading sessions between first eligible surface and a benchmark confirmation / major move.
- **Opportunity Loss:** candidates first surfaced only after move consumption.
- **Top-K Precision / Recall / NDCG:** whether ranking moves useful early candidates upward.
- **Path Quality:** MFE, MAE, R, time-to-positive, time-underwater.
- **Trap Rejection:** false bounce / invalidation / tail loss.
- **Incremental Family Value:** same-tape LOFO / ablation against the champion.
- **Coverage-Adjusted Value:** no family earns authority by working on a tiny easy subset unless explicitly scoped.
- **User Value:** review-to-action conversion, watch/pass/reject behavior, explanation usefulness.

No improvement is accepted if precision rises primarily because the system waits longer, unless that is a separately named conservative lane.

---

# 27. Value-of-information law

For evidence family `f`, ask:

> **What decision or ranking improvement exists because `f` was available at that time, versus the same system without `f` on the same tape?**

Required comparisons:

- champion vs champion-minus-f;
- champion vs shuffled/placebo f;
- complete-coverage cohort;
- sparse-coverage cohort;
- species/sector strata only when estimable;
- early vs late surface;
- path outcomes;
- error/failure modes.

A family may be useful as:

- display context;
- research-priority ordering;
- risk disclosure;
- calibrated forecast input;
- rank feature.

It does not have to become rank authority to be valuable.

---

# 28. Promotion ladder

Suggested authority progression:

1. `SOURCE_TRUTH` — exact fact only.
2. `DISPLAY_CONTEXT` — visible, no rank/gate.
3. `RESEARCH_PRIORITY_SHADOW` — may order an operator research queue; no trade/availability authority.
4. `RANK_CHALLENGER` — model/rule evaluated prospectively against champion.
5. `RANK_AUTHORITY` — earned promotion for a frozen version/cohort.
6. `CALIBRATED_FORECAST` — only if probability/expected outcome calibration is independently earned.

No family is allowed to jump directly from “interesting research” to trade origination.

Promotion is version-specific and can be revoked.

# 29. External-method lessons incorporated into the architecture

## 29.1 Business exposure

Institutional thematic products quantify multi-sector participation from business segments/revenue rather than relying solely on one sector label. This supports the multi-axis exposure design, but Mastermind should preserve missingness and overlapping themes rather than copy a proprietary taxonomy.

## 29.2 Early theme detection

NLP/ML can identify early commercial involvement from filings/public sources. Mastermind should use models to **propose** membership/evidence, with deterministic evidence validation and human/ontology ratification before canonical theme minting.

## 29.3 Diversified-company transmission

Revenue-weighted synthetic industry returns provide a concrete baseline for asking whether a diversified issuer should respond to movement in the industries it economically participates in. Mastermind should generalize this carefully to theme/relationship response pressure and test it prospectively.

## 29.4 Supply-chain propagation

Point-in-time customer/supplier/competitor relationships are valuable but sparse if restricted to mandatory disclosures. Broader evidence can improve coverage, but every relationship needs evidence confidence and known-at lineage.

## 29.5 Expectations

Detailed consensus/KPI estimates and revisions are a superior expectation baseline to generic “beat EPS” labels. Domain owners should preserve basis and dispersion.

## 29.6 News/event structure

Relevance, novelty, temporal scoring and event taxonomy are useful dimensions for emergence and duplicate suppression. They must not be assumed predictive without incremental tests.

## 29.7 Residualization

Systematic factor decomposition is important for distinguishing security-specific incorporation from ordinary market/industry/style movement.

## 29.8 Academic economic-link evidence

Research on customer/supplier and horizontally related industries supports the hypothesis that public information can diffuse gradually across economically linked firms, especially under attention constraints. This motivates — but does not prove — Mastermind transmission features.

# 39. Time law

# 41. Correction law

Corrections must preserve history.

A correction should be able to answer:

- what Mastermind believed before;
- which source/evidence supported it;
- when the correction became knowable;
- what changed;
- which derived features are invalidated/recomputed;
- whether a historical evaluation row uses contemporaneous or corrected truth.

Never silently rewrite old features and then claim a clean historical backtest.

# 42. Rights law

A source can be useful internally without being publishable externally.

Every family needs at least:

- internal research right;
- model-training right if relevant;
- derived-data right;
- public/product display right;
- export/API right.

`RIGHTS_BLOCKED` is a first-class state.

Do not replace a rights problem with an unlicensed scraper.

# 43. LLM/model law

LLMs are valuable for:

- candidate extraction;
- semantic mapping proposals;
- document segmentation;
- relationship proposals;
- theme proposals;
- source-supported summarization;
- explanation generation.

LLMs do **not** directly originate:

- canonical identity;
- arbitrary economic exposure percentages;
- surprise when no expectation exists;
- materiality probability without a validated domain method;
- rank authority;
- entry availability;
- trade size;
- historical labels;
- a backfilled known-at clock.

Model proposals must carry source spans/receipts and pass deterministic validation where possible.

# 44. Emergent theme discovery

# 45. Attention as both signal and confound

Attention can play several roles:

- emergence detection;
- crowding;
- explanation for underreaction disappearing;
- source-volume confound;
- liquidity/price-discovery accelerator.

Therefore do not use “more mentions” as a monotonic bullish feature.

Research should explicitly test interactions such as:

> strong economic evidence + low prior attention vs strong evidence + already saturated attention.

# 46. Options / positioning law

Options and positioning should initially answer questions such as:

- what move is priced?
- where is positioning concentrated?
- is skew/term structure unusual?
- is OI persistent or event-driven?
- is the name crowded?
- are there path/expiry risks?

They do not become direct trade originators merely because the tape looks unusual.

Coverage must be sufficient and point-in-time. Missing chain data is not neutral positioning.

# 47. Regime law

A universal “current regime multiplier” is rejected by default.

- exact mechanism is stated;
- state is knowable at decision time;

- interaction is preregistered;
- baseline without regime is included;
- forward validation is possible.

Otherwise regime remains descriptive context.

# 52. 10/10 reference reasoning — target qualitative output

The final system should be able to produce a chain like:

> **ENTRY OPEN — early evidence is strengthening before full price incorporation.** Data-center power infrastructure has accelerated across several independent evidence roots. The company has high verified economic exposure through its power-management segment and sits on a customer-capex transmission path from two hyperscalers. Relevant segment/KPI expectations have revised upward, while the security's factor-adjusted move remains modest relative to the exposure-weighted theme impulse. The 1D/4H entry experts have begun turning and price remains inside the deterministic entry zone. Structural fragility is low-to-moderate; crowding is rising but not extreme. Theme incorporation analysis is experimental and does not control entry status.

And, for a different security:

> **RAN — DON'T CHASE.** The company has even stronger economic exposure and evidence quality, but the security has already moved far beyond its entry geometry and its residual response is high versus comparable theme episodes. The thesis may still be strong; the current opportunity is not.

And:

> **EARLY RADAR — evidence incomplete.** A technical turn has appeared, but only one independent contextual evidence root is available and theme exposure is not yet verified. Watch for new evidence; no current intelligence rank authority.

Those are three different valid outcomes. The system must never average them into one score.

## Closing ruling

The ambition is deliberately large: Prophet should eventually combine the kinds of economic exposure, event expectations, relationship intelligence, point-in-time evidence and empirical validation that institutional research systems use, **but integrate them around one thing those systems often treat separately — the live question of whether the information has actually entered the price while a lawful entry still exists**.

The architecture is frozen enough to prevent drift. The empirical claims remain open enough to be killed honestly.
