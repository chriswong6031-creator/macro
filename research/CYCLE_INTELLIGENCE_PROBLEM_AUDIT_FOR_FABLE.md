# Cycle Intelligence Platform — Problem Audit for Fable

**As of 2026-07-02.** An adversarial, code-grounded problem-audit of the entire cycle-intelligence surface — `cycle.html`, `markets.html`, `country_cycles.html`, `sector_cycles(.html/_china)`, `sector_central(.html/_china)` — and the engines beneath them. This document is the *problem* half of the house workflow: it enumerates what is wrong, mislabeled, unmeasured, or architecturally incoherent, with file:line evidence, so **Fable** can attack the solutions. It does not prescribe final designs; it points at every defect precisely enough to act on.

> **Provenance.** Audited against canonical `main` @ `a51665054e` (this session's branch `redesign/risk-radar` is 338 commits behind and stale — the audit used a clean `main` worktree, so every `file:line` reference below is against `main`, not the working tree). Method: an 11-subsystem fan-out where each subsystem was deep-read by one auditor and then **independently re-verified against the code by a second adversarial agent** (refuted claims dropped), followed by dual-lens synthesis. 24 agents, 426 tool calls. The keystone flagship finding (cycle_app.js `build()`) and the empty-grader finding were additionally hand-verified by the author. **89 findings survived verification: 13 critical, 34 high, 31 medium, 11 low.**

## TL;DR — the verdict

**This is not one cycle-intelligence platform. It is two products wearing one skin, plus a measurement apparatus that has graded neither of them yet.**

- **Product A — hand-authored opinion drawn as a curve** (`cycle.html`, `markets.html`). Every position, phase, turn date, cone width, confidence tag and falsifier is a human-typed string in a JS literal; the "build" script only copies the file. No live data, no pipeline, no backtest, no grader, no freshness guard. It silently rots the moment nobody re-edits the JS.
- **Product B — a data-driven engine family** (`sector_cycles`, `china_sector_cycles`, `country_cycles`, and the `sector_central` fusions) sharing one causal kernel (`engine/sector_cycles._record_core`) with real forward logs and self-graders.

The two families share the same nav column, the same 0–100 y-axis label, the same phase vocabulary and the same oscillator styling — so **a hand-typed opinion is laundered into the visual authority of a measured engine.** That schism is the root disease; most other findings are symptoms of it. On top sit five compounding failures:

1. **No single cycle ontology.** "Position 0–100" means three incompatible things across sibling pages; "phase" is fragmented across ~5 vocabularies that co-fire and contradict on a single card; "turn" is detected 4 different ways. "Every cycle on one clock" actually ships four clocks that disagree.
2. **Evergreen rot.** `TODAY` is a frozen literal (`2026.48`); no `Date.now()` anywhere on the flagships. Even the "data-driven" pages let a frozen hand-authored `narratives.json` string override the live engine.
3. **A distorted, mislabeled data substrate.** Every engine cycle is built on dividend-adjusted *total-return* Yahoo closes but marketed as "true price" — corrupting ZigZag turns, detrended position, the failed-cycle "strongest bearish tell", RS, and every excess-vs-benchmark number. `country_cycles` additionally folds the USD/FX cycle into every "equity cycle".
4. **Calibration theater.** Fusion weights, tier cut-points, cone amplitudes, ladder scores and cycle periodicities are hand-tuned constants presented as validated models. The one populated calibration (`ladder_calibration.json`) is loaded for *display only*, never binds the score — and on the endpoint-return lens it is **inverted** (DECLINE/"AVOID" out-returns FRESH BUY/"BUY").
5. **The honesty apparatus is empty.** All three forward logs began 2026-06-28 and hold a *single* stamp date; the shortest horizon is 21 trading days, so **zero calls have matured.** Every scorecard reads "accruing"; the flagships have no grader at all. "MEASURED, not asserted" is, today, unmeasured.

**The single biggest unverified question:** because nothing has matured, there is *no evidence today* that any oscillator, phase read, or projection on this platform has forward predictive power. The recommended retroactive PIT backfill (below) would produce the first real evidence — and must be run on a corrected (unadjusted-price, leak-free) basis or the grades are worthless.

## Part I — Systemic & architectural problems

*Lens: the schism, the missing ontology, evergreen rot, the data substrate, and how to re-architect into one self-updating coherent system.*

### Executive read

This is not one cycle intelligence platform — it is two products wearing one skin, plus a measurement apparatus that grades neither of them yet. Product A (cycle.html, markets.html) is a **hand-authored opinion drawn as a curve**: every position, phase, turn date, cone width, confidence tag, and falsifier is typed by a human into a JS literal, then rendered by a build script that does nothing but copy the file (`build_cycle.py:45` renders the shell with no context; `build_markets.py` copies `markets_data.js` verbatim). Product B (sector_cycles, china_sector_cycles, country_cycles, sector_central) is a **data-driven engine family** sharing one causal kernel (`sector_cycles._record_core`, delegated to by `china_sector_cycles.py:152/196` and `country_cycles.py:130`) with real forward logs and graders. The two families share the same nav column, the same 0-100 y-axis label, the same phase vocabulary, and the same cosine/oscillator visual language — so a hand-typed opinion is laundered into the visual authority of a measured engine. This schism is the root systemic disease; almost every other finding is a symptom.

The second systemic failure is that **there is no single cycle ontology**. The "0-100 position" that anchors the entire product means three incompatible things: a hand-set euphoria level force-fit through a cosine at TODAY (`cycle_app.js:73`), a deterministic drawdown-from-ATH transform `92·exp(-dd/30)` (`markets_app.js:71`), and a trailing 252-day detrended-stochastic percentile (`sector_cycles.py:92-103`). An "82" simultaneously means near-record-high, top-of-detrended-range, and hand-set euphoria on three sibling pages under one axis label. Phase is fragmented across at least five vocabularies (5-phase wheel, 8-state ladder, DC-phase, IC-phase, free-text `phaseLabel`) that co-render on a single card with no crosswalk — so one card shows `phase=Peak`, `timing_state=BOTTOM WATCH`, `action=GET READY`, `signal=SELL` at once (`country_cycles_data.js` EWN; `sector_cycles.py:329-337`). Turn detection is implemented four times (hand-dated cosine turns; ZigZag `_detect_swings`; windowed-minima `find_troughs`; k-bar `_pivots`) with unrelated magic constants (14%/18% reversal vs 10/18-day windows), so the same instrument has no single answer to "when did it last bottom." The product promises "every cycle on one clock" and ships four clocks that contradict each other on the same surface.

The third systemic failure is **evergreen rot with no self-updating spine and no matured measurement**. TODAY is a frozen literal (`cycle_data.js:29 = 2026.48`; `markets_data.js:10 asOf 2026-06-25`) with no `Date.now()` anywhere (`cycle_app.js` grep = 0), so the "Now" dot, the history/projection split, and "% to next turn" silently drift the moment wall-clock time passes the hard-coded date — and worse, the `Math.max(central, today+0.15)` guards would push already-elapsed turns forward as "fresh future" if TODAY were ever wired live. Even the data-driven pages inherit hand-authored rot: `sector_cycles.py:342` hard-codes `read: None` so the frozen `narratives.json` "now" string always wins over the live engine, meaning the most-read sentence on a "data-driven" card can go months stale and contradict the live gauge beside it (`country_cycles narratives.json` says "91.4/100" while the engine computes 90.6). And the honesty apparatus is empty: all three forward logs began 2026-06-26/28 and span 4 stamp dates; with a 21-day minimum horizon **zero calls have matured**, so every scorecard reads "accruing." The cycle/markets flagships have no grader at all (6 graders exist; none references cycle/markets/country).

The fourth systemic failure is **a distorted, unlabeled data substrate under everything**. Every data-driven cycle is built on dividend-adjusted total-return Yahoo closes (`collectors/yahoo.py:100 auto_adjust=True`) yet marketed as "true price" (`sector_cycles.py:6`) and "real USD price" (`country_cycles.html.j2:30`). This inflates the ZigZag magnitudes (XLK 2018 leg reports 281% on a back-adjusted linear series), biases detrended position upward for high-yield sectors, weakens the failed-cycle break (the platform's self-described strongest bearish tell, `cycles.py:14-15`) by understating the anchor low, and makes the failed-cycle test compare a drifting TR close to a months-old TR low. For country_cycles it compounds with an undisclosed FX conflation — the engine's own `cycle_dna.json` admits "EWJ fell 33% in dollars while local equities were roughly flat," so a "Japan trough" can be a yen crash. And every grader's excess-vs-benchmark is structurally biased: China grades a price-return sector against a total-return benchmark (`china_sector_central_grader` benchmark is `auto_adjust=True`), baking a ~dividend-yield negative drift into every "excess" number.

The fifth failure is **calibration theater**: fusion weights, tier cut-points, cone amplitudes, ladder scores, and periodicity bands are hand-tuned constants presented as validated models, and where a grader exists it is decorative rather than binding. `LADDER_SCORE` is a static dict whose own walk-forward calibration is loaded only for display (`build_site.py:2172/2312`) and never feeds back — and on the endpoint-return lens it is *inverted* (DECLINE, score −80, has the highest 21d hit-rate and forward return; FRESH BUY, +80, among the lowest). The trend gate is counted as a directional confluence vote despite its cited validation saying "no mean-return edge" (`thematic_rotation_phase0.json:224`). The China regime gate is labeled "validated" while borrowing a US drawdown result and the actual China book loses to equal-weight (+3%/yr vs +6.59%). Graders pool 80% survivorship-biased hindsight-curated baskets with clean ETFs into one rank-IC, grade basket calls against a live-recomputed current-membership series (a look-ahead leak), and print rank-IC to 4 decimals on n=3 with no confidence intervals — while a *sibling grader in the same repo* (`china_sector_cycles_grader`) already implements MIN_EARN_N=40, Wilson CIs, and date-blocked bootstraps. The rigor exists; it just isn't propagated.

### The systemic problems

#### 1. The narrative/engine schism: hand-typed opinions are laundered into the visual authority of measured engines under one nav group

**Severity: CRITICAL**

The platform is two philosophies bolted together and presented as one. **Product A** (cycle.html, markets.html) is a static JS opinion: `build_cycle.py:45` renders the HTML shell with *no context* and `build_markets.py` `shutil.copy2`s `markets_data.js` verbatim — zero ingestion, zero recompute, zero forward log, zero grader. Every position (`cycle_data.js:88 pos:91`), phase, turn date, cone, `confidence:'medium'`, and falsifier is a hand-typed string. **Product B** (sector/china/country/central engines) shares the causal `_record_core` kernel (`china_sector_cycles.py:152`, `country_cycles.py:130`), auto-detects turns, forward-logs, and self-grades (6 graders in `engine/`).

The schism is dangerous because the two families are **visually and semantically indistinguishable**: same nav column, same 0-100 axis, same 5-phase vocabulary (`sector_cycles.py:68` comment: 'identical hues/labels to site/cycle_data.js'), same cosine/oscillator styling, shared `cycle.css`/`mm_charts.js`. A user cannot tell that cycle.html's 'Peak, pos 91' is a drawing of an opinion while sector_cycles' 'Peak, pos 80' is a measurement. The falsifiers (`cycle_data.js:98-101`) create an *illusion of scientific rigor* — precise, testable prose ('new SOX high above 14,655 on broadening breadth') that no code ever evaluates (grep `confidence`/`falsifier` in cycle_app.js = 0 reads). The user trades real capital on 23 unfalsified hand-typed theses framed as a 'prediction machine.'

**Direction of solution (novel):** Collapse to ONE architecture where narrative is a *layer*, never a *source*. Every plotted number (position, phase, turn, cone) must be computed by the shared engine kernel; the hand-authored JSON is demoted to an optional, dated, staleness-badged prose overlay that can *annotate* but never *set* a plotted value. cycle.html/markets.html have clean ETF tapes already in `yahoo_closes` (SPX, EWJ, EWU, FXI, EWT...) — back them with `_record_core` so they refresh and grade automatically. Add a build-time assertion that fails when any hand-authored `pos`/turn disagrees with the engine beyond tolerance. Cite: `scripts/build_cycle.py:28,45-51`; `scripts/build_markets.py:28-51`; `site/cycle_data.js:20-22,88,96-101`; `engine/sector_cycles.py:342` (`read:None`), `:68`.

#### 2. No single cycle ontology: 'position', 'phase', and 'turn' are each defined 3-5 incompatible ways under one shared axis and vocabulary

**Severity: CRITICAL**

The product promise is 'every cycle on one clock.' It ships **at least four clocks** that contradict each other on the same card.

**Position (three definitions, one axis label):** hand-set euphoria force-fit through a cosine at TODAY (`cycle_app.js:73-79`; 82='Euphoric·topping'); deterministic drawdown transform `clamp(round(92*exp(-dd/30)),3,97)` where 92='At ATH', 14='Crash low' (`markets_app.js:71,167`); trailing-252d detrended-stochastic percentile where 100='stretched above trend' (`sector_cycles.py:92-103`). An '82' means near-record-high, top-of-range, and hand-set euphoria simultaneously — cross-page reads are actively misleading. Verified verbatim in this session.

**Phase (five vocabularies on one card):** 5-phase wheel; 8-state ladder (DECLINE...FRESH BUY, `cycles.py:403`); DC-phase (`new/mid/approaching_band/in_band/stretched`); IC-phase (`early/mid/late/overdue`); a fifth BUY/SELL `signal` off yet another osc threshold (`sector_cycles.py:329-330`); plus free-text `phaseLabel` in markets. Live EWN card shows `phase=Peak`, `timing_state=BOTTOM WATCH`, `action=GET READY` at once — a topping market tagged 'get ready to buy.' No crosswalk exists in code.

**Turn (four detectors, no contract):** hand-dated cosine turns; ZigZag `_detect_swings` (14% US / 18% CN / vol-scaled country); windowed-minima `find_troughs` (10/18-day); k-bar `_pivots`. `_record_core` runs the ZigZag AND `cycles.analyze` (find_troughs) on the *same* series, so the chart's last trough and the card's DC-clock trough can disagree.

**Direction of solution (novel):** Author a single ONTOLOGY MODULE both Python and JS import — one canonical position semantic (recommend the causal detrended-stochastic osc, the only cross-asset-comparable, data-driven one, already shared by 3 of 5 pages), one master phase taxonomy (the 5-phase wheel) with the ladder/DC/IC states declared explicitly as *sub-reads* of a master phase (e.g. 'Peak' ⊇ {TOP WATCH, ROLLING OVER, late-IC}), and ONE turn primitive with an explicit contract (basis, confirmation rule, provisional-last handling). Enforce with a test asserting no card can emit mutually-contradictory phase/action labels. Cite: `cycle_app.js:73,44-47`; `markets_app.js:71,167`; `sector_cycles.py:92-103,318,329-337`; `cycles.py:403,461-473`; `china_sector_cycles.py:50`; `country_cycles.py:130`.

#### 3. Evergreen rot: frozen-TODAY constants + hand-authored 'live reads' + zero matured graders mean the 'prediction machine' silently decays and has never been measured

**Severity: CRITICAL**

Three compounding rot mechanisms defeat the 'evergreen' goal.

**(1) Frozen TODAY.** `cycle_data.js:29 today=2026.48` and `markets_data.js:10 asOf='2026-06-25'` are hard literals; grep for `Date`/`Date.now()` in cycle_app.js = 0. So the 'Now' dot, history/projection boundary, and '% to next turn' never advance — already-past dates render as future. Worse, `tc=Math.max(yf(central),today+0.15)` (`cycle_app.js:63`) means if TODAY were ever wired live, an elapsed turn would be *pushed forward and drawn as fresh*, hiding staleness rather than exposing it. markets_app.js even labels the frozen snapshot 'Live read · <date>' (`:504`).

**(2) Hand-authored reads override the live engine even on data-driven pages.** `sector_cycles.py:342` hard-codes `read:None` and `sector_cycles.js:1001` prefers `NARR[s.id].now`, so the frozen `narratives.json` string ALWAYS wins. `country_cycles narratives.json` hard-codes '91.4/100, +6.8% RS, ranking third, trough ~Oct 2026' while the live engine reports pos=90.6, rs=4.6 beside it. Keeping it true requires hand-editing 24+ country blocks every rebuild — the exact hand-curation failure smuggled into a page sold as data-driven.

**(3) The honesty apparatus is empty.** All three forward logs (`sector_central`, `china_sector_central`, `china_sector_cycles`) began 2026-06-26/28 and span 4 stamp dates; the shortest horizon is 21 trading days, so **0 calls have matured** — every scorecard is 100% 'accruing' and will be for ~2 months (21d cells) to a year+ (`MIN_EARN_N=40`). The flagships have no grader at all.

**Direction of solution (novel):** (a) Drive TODAY from wall-clock `Date` for boundaries + a staleness banner; better, regenerate `now.pos`/`proj` from the engine every build so the page *cannot* rot. Handle elapsed hand-typed turns explicitly (don't silently push forward). (b) Make the engine own all point-in-time numbers; narrative supplies only durable causal 'why', with a staleness badge when `narratives.version < asOf`. (c) **Retroactively backfill the forward logs**: the phase/conviction/signature stamps are deterministic PIT functions of price, so recompute them at every historical month-end (each stamp using only tape ≤ its date — the engine already respects PIT trimming) to synthesize hundreds of matured windows on day one, turning an empty prospective ledger into a leak-free walk-forward backtest. Cite: `cycle_data.js:29`; `cycle_app.js:63,504`; `sector_cycles.py:342`; `sector_cycles.js:1001`; `country_cycles/narratives.json`; forward_log parquets (4 dates each); `china_sector_cycles_grader.py:64`.

#### 4. Distorted, mislabeled data substrate: total-return closes (and undisclosed FX) marketed as 'price', corrupting turns, positions, the strongest bearish signal, and every excess-vs-benchmark

**Severity: HIGH**

Every data-driven cycle is built on `collectors/yahoo.py:100 auto_adjust=True` — dividend/split-adjusted TOTAL RETURN — while marketed as 'true price (not a stylised oscillator)' (`sector_cycles.py:6`) and 'real USD price' (`country_cycles.html.j2:30`). The distortion propagates everywhere: the fixed-% ZigZag on a back-adjusted linear series reports absurd early magnitudes (XLK 2018 leg mag=281.7%); high-yield sectors (XLU/XLRE/XLE) drift up faster than price, biasing detrended position and shifting turn dates on exactly the defensive sleeves that matter most late-cycle; RS-vs-SPY is a TR/TR ratio that flatters high-yield leadership as a pure dividend artefact (`sector_cycles.py:268`). Most damaging: the failed-cycle break — the platform's self-described strongest bearish tell (~80%, `cycles.py:14-15`) — compares today's drifting TR close to a months-old understated TR low (`cycles.py:253`), biasing it *against* triggering, and quotes a TR invalidation level that won't match a broker's price screen.

For country_cycles it compounds with **undisclosed FX conflation**: the USD ETF tape is the product of the local equity cycle AND the USD/FX cycle, and the engine's own `cycle_dna.json` admits 'EWJ fell 33% in dollars while local equities were roughly flat' — so a 'Brazil trough' can be a BRL crash, and the cross-market overlay is apples-to-oranges across strong/weak-currency markets. And every excess-vs-benchmark is structurally biased: `china_sector_central_grader` subtracts a `auto_adjust=True` total-return benchmark from a price-return Shenwan sector, baking a chronic ~dividend-yield negative drift into 'excess' that will discredit good calls regardless of skill.

**Direction of solution:** Establish an explicit price-basis contract. Feed *unadjusted/close-only* price into all price-structure math (ZigZag turns, detrended osc, DCL invalidation, failed-cycle) and reserve TR only for return/momentum stats, labeled. For country_cycles compute the cycle on LOCAL-currency TR (USD ETF ÷ FX) and carry the FX/USD overlay as a separate labeled leg; decompose each turn into equity- vs FX-driven. Match numerator/denominator basis in every grader (price sector vs price benchmark). Cite: `collectors/yahoo.py:100`; `sector_cycles.py:6,268`; `cycles.py:253,14-15`; `country_cycles.html.j2:30`; `data/country_cycles/cycle_dna.json` ewj; `china_sector_central_grader.py:128-129,173`; `collectors/china_prices.py:82`.

#### 5. Two independent clocks per card contradict with no reconciliation — the slow phase wheel says 'Peak/SELL' while the daily ladder says 'BOTTOM WATCH/GET READY' on the same instrument

**Severity: HIGH**

`_record_core` emits a slow multi-month phase (from the 0-100 osc + MACD, `sector_cycles.py:321-322`) AND a daily timing-ladder state/action (from `cycles.analyze`, `:334-340`) as raw, orthogonal outputs that are never reconciled. Live examples: XLK `phase=Peak/SELL, osc_slope=-18.4` while `timing_state=BOTTOM WATCH/GET READY`; EWN `phase=Peak/pos 90.6` while `timing=BOTTOM WATCH`; EWT `phase=Peak/88.7` while `timing=TURN SIGNALED/BUY SETUP`. A stretched, topping market carries a buy-the-bottom ladder action on the same card. Because this lives in the shared kernel, all three engine pages inherit it. The user acts on these labels and can be led to buy into a top.

This is the ontology problem (#2) in its most acute, action-generating form: it is not merely a vocabulary mismatch but two engines emitting *opposite trade directions* with equal visual weight and no 'clocks disagree' state.

**Direction of solution (novel):** Fuse the two into one coherent state machine in `_record_core`: the daily ladder may only fire buy-setups within a phase that permits it (e.g. suppress/relabel ladder buys as 'countertrend only' when pos>68 and phase∈{Peak,Downturn}), and emit an explicit 'clocks disagree' state instead of two raw contradictory badges. Add a regression test asserting phase and ladder action are never mutually contradictory. Fix once in the kernel so all three pages benefit; grade the reconciled call. Cite: `sector_cycles.py:314-322,334-340,329-337`; `cycles.py:403,431`; `country_cycles_data.js` EWN/EWT/EWY.

#### 6. Calibration theater: hand-tuned constants and self-graders that don't bind — the ladder score is inverted vs its own walk-forward, 'validated' gates aren't, and rigor already in the repo isn't propagated

**Severity: HIGH**

The engines *look* calibrated but the numbers are asserted, not earned, and where a grader exists it decorates rather than governs.

**Score doesn't bind to calibration, and is inverted.** `LADDER_SCORE` is a static dict (`cycles.py:406`); `calibrate_ladder`'s output is loaded only for template display (`build_site.py:2172,2312`) and never feeds back (grep confirms LADDER_SCORE read only at `:1038`). On endpoint return the ordering is *inverted*: DECLINE (score −80, 'AVOID') has the highest 21d hit (60.8%) and forward return (+2.37%); FRESH BUY (+80, 'BUY') is among the lowest — and all states cluster 56.8-60.8%, near the bull-tape base rate, so none shows edge over its own base rate.

**'Validated' means something else than it says.** The US trend gate is counted as a directional confluence vote (`sector_central.py:342`) and de-rates conviction (`:332`) despite its cited evidence saying 'no mean-return edge... lower volatility & drawdown' (`thematic_rotation_phase0.json:224`) — a risk lever mislabeled as alpha. The China regime gate is labeled 'validated' (`china_sector_central.py:6-15`) while borrowing a 27-year US drawdown result, and the China book actually loses to equal-weight (+3%/yr vs +6.59%, `ALLOCATION_CHINA_AUDIT.md`).

**Graders are contaminated and under-powered — while the fix exists next door.** Both central graders pool 80% survivorship-biased hindsight-curated baskets with clean ETFs into one rank-IC (`sector_central_grader.py:165-168`), grade basket calls against a *live-recomputed current-membership* series (a look-ahead/survivorship leak; basket levels are null in the store so they re-run `compute_china_baskets()` at grade time — `china_sector_central_grader.py:33-42,114-124`), count 'flat' tier calls as misses in the denominator (37-78% of calls), and print rank-IC to 4 decimals at n=3 with NO confidence intervals. Yet `china_sector_cycles_grader.py` in the *same repo* already uses `MIN_EARN_N=40`, Wilson CIs, and 800-draw date-blocked bootstraps. The overlapping-window autocorrelation (daily stamps → 62/63 shared forward days; monthly shift(-6) → 5/6 shared months) inflates apparent significance ~2.4-6x with no correction.

**Direction of solution (novel):** (a) Make calibration BIND: fit LADDER_SCORE and fusion weights/tier cuts from the walk-forward on a risk-adjusted metric (the drawdown lens is the only one showing monotonic separation), reporting edge OVER each instrument's base rate. (b) Split roles: trend/regime gates become SIZE/risk caps, removed from directional confluence and relabeled 'risk-control, no return edge'; run a China-specific backtest before the word 'validated' appears. (c) Propagate the `china_sector_cycles_grader` machinery into ALL graders — Wilson/block-bootstrap CIs, MIN_PROVEN_N floor, non-overlapping cohorts, FDR correction across the horizon×tier grid, refuse to emit a point estimate without its interval. (d) Grade sectors and baskets in SEPARATE scorecards; freeze a daily basket-level parquet + membership-hash per stamp so grades are stable PIT and never re-marked on a refreshed (or truncation-fabricated) membership. Cite: `cycles.py:406,1038`; `build_site.py:2172,2312`; `ladder_calibration.json`; `sector_central.py:328-342`; `thematic_rotation_phase0.json:224`; `china_sector_central.py:6-15,248`; graders `sector_central_grader.py:159-183`, `china_sector_central_grader.py:33-42,150-177`, `china_sector_cycles_grader.py:64-77`.

*Lens: leakage/repainting/survivorship, undersized samples and too-tight intervals, unbound calibration, and the near-total absence of out-of-sample proof of predictive power.*

### Executive read

## The core verdict: this is an unfalsified assertion machine wearing the costume of a measured one

Across all eleven subsystems, the single most important fact from a statistical-rigor lens is this: **not one output of this platform has ever been scored against a realized outcome.** I verified the three self-grading logs directly — `data/sector_central/calls.parquet`, `data/china_sector_central/calls.parquet`, and `data/china_sector_cycles/forward_log.parquet` each contain a *single* stamp date (2026-06-28), not the four the findings estimated. With the shortest horizon at 21 trading days, `n_matured` is exactly **zero** for every cell, every horizon, every page. The dashboards render a rigorous-looking apparatus (rank-IC to 4 decimals, Wilson-flavored conditional rates, tier hit-rates) that has graded nothing and, absent retroactive backfill, will grade nothing meaningful for months (21d cells) to a year-plus (the `MIN_EARN_N=40` cycles grader). The "MEASURED, not asserted" promise is, today, false at the level of the data on disk (predictive-power-2, china-sector-cycles-4, sector-central-us-6, sector-central-china-4).

**Where measurement does exist, it is either inverted, in-sample, or grading the wrong object.** The one genuinely populated calibration — `ladder_calibration.json`, 8,556–15,757 obs per state — is not wired back into anything: I confirmed `LADDER_SCORE` (engine/cycles.py:406) is read exactly once (1038) and `calibrate_ladder` buckets purely by `st["state"]` (cycles.py:2175), never by `st["score"]`. So the calibration is decorative. The score is roughly defensible only on the *drawdown* lens, which the code itself privileges but does not bind to. Meanwhile the business-cycle recession signal is grid-searched and reported on the *same* 1985+ window (validate_business_cycle.py:191/198), so "3/3 caught, 5.7m lead" is in-sample on N≈3 recessions with revision-leaked leading legs — an upper bound the module ships as a headline (macro-regime-2, predictive-power-6). The live firing threshold (−1.25) is itself the grid-search winner, silently merged over config (macro-regime-miss).

**Look-ahead, repainting, and survivorship contaminate the few numbers that do get computed.** Turn detection is non-causal by construction: `find_troughs` requires `window` bars on *both* sides, so a fresh daily low is invisible for ~10 days and the anchor repaints (cycles-core-4); on weekly bars the investor-cycle low repaints for ~6 weeks — and that weekly anchor is what *re-labels* daily buys as COUNTERTREND (cycles-core-miss). Basket forward returns in both the US and China graders are computed off a *live-recomputed* equal-weight series built from *today's* membership, so an old stamp is silently re-scored on a survivorship-refreshed, mutable history (sector-central-us-miss, china-sector-central-3, predictive-power-miss). And 80% of the US sector_central rows graded for rank-IC and hit-rate are hindsight-curated baskets pooled into one cross-section with 44 clean ETFs (sector-central-us-4).

**Every confidence interval that is shown is too tight, and most quantities have none at all.** The China pathway prints a Wilson CI on 6-month *overlapping* windows as if independent — effective n is ~n/6, so the band is ~2.4× too narrow, and for banks the true effective n is ~6 (china-sector-cycles-2). The two central graders report rank-IC to 4 decimals with *no* CI, no Wilson band, no bootstrap, no multiple-testing correction across the tier×horizon grid — while the *cycles* grader in the same repo already has all of it (`_wilson`, 800-draw `_boot_gap_ci`, `MIN_EARN_N=40`). The rigor exists in one file and is simply not ported to the others (predictive-power-3).

**Finally, the platform grades only one thing (conviction→forward-return) and never grades what it actually promises: turns, timing windows, and phase transitions.** Cone widths are literal magic ramps (`lerp(1.5,13)`, tilt weights 1.35/0.7) with zero realized-coverage feedback (cycle-flagship-4, markets-global-4); no code checks whether a projected turn landed inside its band; no reliability curve exists for any probabilistic output (predictive-power-4). The measurement surface is not just empty — it is *pointed at the wrong target*.

### The systemic problems

#### 1. The self-grading apparatus is empty by construction — every scorecard has zero matured calls and cannot accrue meaningful n for months to a year

**Severity: CRITICAL**

I verified on disk: `data/sector_central/calls.parquet`, `data/china_sector_central/calls.parquet`, and `data/china_sector_cycles/forward_log.parquet` each hold a **single** stamp date (2026-06-28), 53–56 rows. Shortest horizon is 21 trading days (`sector_central_grader.py:29 _HORIZONS_D=(21,63,126)`; `china_sector_cycles_grader.py:61 HORIZONS=(21,63)`), so `_fwd_return` returns None for every row and every cell is 'accruing' (`sector_central_grader.py:159-160`; `china_sector_central_grader.py:150-151`; `china_sector_cycles_grader.py:165-166 MIN_EARN_N=40`). Grading starts only from log-creation day with **no historical backfill**, so the 21d central cells need ~2 months to reach n≥3 and the cycles grader needs ~a year to reach 40.

This is the keystone statistical failure: a machine whose entire honesty claim is 'MEASURED, not asserted' has measured nothing, while the dashboard renders rank-IC/hit-rate/Wilson scaffolding that implies it has. **Direction of solution:** the stamp signals (phase, signature, pos, proj, conviction) are *deterministic functions of point-in-time price* — so backfill them. Re-run every classifier over deep history at each month-end (or weekly), writing a historical PIT stamp using only tape ≤ that date (the engines already trim to `last_ts`, and `scripts/_cycle_fix_backtest.py` already does expanding-window `analyze()`). This converts an empty prospective ledger into a walk-forward backtest with hundreds of matured windows on day one. Guard look-ahead exactly as `china_sector_cycles_grader` does (grade at bar i+1). This single move unblocks nearly every other rigor fix below, because they all need matured outcomes to calibrate against. (predictive-power-2, china-sector-cycles-4, sector-central-us-6, sector-central-china-4)

#### 2. Nothing grades the platform's actual promises — turn hit-rate, cone coverage, and phase-transition accuracy are never measured; only conviction→return is

**Severity: CRITICAL**

A cycle prediction machine sells three things — *turns, timing windows, phase transitions* — and grades none of them. The only graded quantity anywhere is conviction/score vs forward RETURN (both central graders, `sector_central_grader.py:163-181`) plus a CN drawdown channel and a degenerate next-turn direction call whose 'Brier' collapses to the miss rate because it is emitted as a hard p=1 (`china_sector_cycles_grader.py:383-413`). Turn-detection precision/recall is nowhere — `zigzag_turns` exists (`china_sector_index.py:182`) but is never matched to any projected turn. Cone coverage is nowhere — `cycle_app.js:105-113` draws a band whose amplitude is a hardcoded `lerp(1.5,13)` with tilt weights 1.35/0.7 and **no** realized-containment feedback (cycle-flagship-4; markets `posFromDrawdown` and cone ramp equally uncalibrated, markets-global-4).

This matters because it means even *after* backfill, the machine would still only know whether high-conviction names drift up — not whether it can time a turn or whether its 68% cone actually contains 68% of realized turns. **Direction of solution:** build three new measurement primitives, all reusing the bar-i+1 forward machinery already in `china_sector_cycles_grader`: (1) **turn precision/recall** — match auto-detected/projected turns against realized swing extrema within a tolerance band, report precision, recall, median timing error; (2) **cone-coverage tracking** — log each projected [low,high] turn window, score empirical containment vs nominal, and *recalibrate the cone ramp from realized error* instead of the 1.5→13 magic constants; (3) **probabilistic reliability curves** — force every directional/odds output to carry a calibrated probability, bucket predicted-prob vs realized frequency (reliability diagram + Brier vs base rate). Precision/recall/Brier infra already exists in `hysteresis.py`/`market_state_tune.py`/`risk_radar_backtest.py` for the risk-radar subsystem — it is portable, just never wired to the cycle projections. (predictive-power-4, cycle-flagship-4, markets-global-4)

#### 3. The one populated calibration is decorative AND inverted; the shipped scores are governed by unfalsifiable magic constants across every fusion engine

**Severity: CRITICAL**

`ladder_calibration.json` is the platform's only richly-populated measurement (8,556–15,757 obs/state). I confirmed it is **not bound**: `LADDER_SCORE` (engine/cycles.py:406) is read exactly once (1038); `build_site.py` loads the calibration (2144-2145) and passes it to the template (2285) but never re-derives the score; `calibrate_ladder` buckets by `st["state"]` (2175), never `st["score"]`. #### 4. Systematic look-ahead / repainting / survivorship leaks the few computed numbers, and it hides inside 'causal-looking' code

**Severity: HIGH**

Multiple independent leakage paths, each subtle enough to survive a casual read: (1) **Non-causal turn anchors that repaint.** `find_troughs` needs `window` bars on both sides (cycles.py:188-191), so a fresh daily low is invisible ~10 days and dc_day/translation/failed-cycle repaint as bars arrive; on weekly bars this is ~6 weeks, and that weekly anchor is what re-labels daily buys as COUNTERTREND (cycles-core-4, cycles-core-miss). The `calibrate_ladder` backtest is itself causal (trailing window, cycles.py:2184) but therefore *bakes in* the same lag rather than revealing its cost. (2) **Full-sample rank in the China HARD gate.** `dist.rank(pct=True)`/`dd.rank(pct=True)` place each historical turn's percentile using the *whole* series including future bars (china_sector_pathway_backtest.py:46-47), over just 3–4 turns/sector — non-causal *and* underpowered, and inconsistent with the live trailing-1260d percentile (china-sector-cycles-5). (3) **Mutable-history survivorship in basket grading.** Both central graders and the cycles grader recompute basket EW levels from *current* membership at grade time (`china_sector_central_grader.py:117-122` calls `compute_china_baskets()`; `sector_central_grader.py` `_fwd_return` has no creation-date guard, `baskets.py:277`), so an old stamp is scored on a refreshed, survivorship-curated, rebased-to-1000 series — a documented fabricated-removal event would silently rewrite prior grades (china-sector-central-3, sector-central-us-miss, predictive-power-miss). Even the rebase anchor `iloc[0]` moves when membership changes (china-sector-central-miss). (4) **In-sample recession calibration with revision leak** (macro-regime-2/6, predictive-power-6, macro-regime-miss).

**Direction of solution:** (a) split CONFIRMED vs PROVISIONAL turns — detect a candidate low causally with an explicit 'may repaint' flag surfaced to UI and grader, and quantify the missed-recent-trough cost the current causal backtest hides; (b) replace full-sample ranks with expanding/trailing-window ranks matching the live `_position`, and pool turns across sectors to lift n; (c) **freeze a daily basket-level parquet and stamp a membership-hash per call** so forward returns are drawn from an immutable PIT series, never a live recompute — a truncation event should *invalidate* affected grades, not silently rewrite them; (d) move business_cycle to leave-one-recession-out / walk-forward and wire ALFRED vintages to kill revision leak.

#### 5. Every confidence interval shown is too tight; most quantities have none; overlapping windows and pooled heterogeneous cross-sections corrupt the headline statistics

**Severity: HIGH**

The statistics that *are* displayed overstate their own certainty in three compounding ways. (1) **Overlapping-window CIs.** The China pathway feeds raw overlapping counts to `_wilson` on 6-month forward windows that share up to 5/6 months (china_sector_pathway.py:83,100-102); effective n ≈ n/6, so a printed CI is ~2.4× too narrow and banks' true effective n is ~6 — a headline conditional that looks like it excludes the base rate can be pure noise (china-sector-cycles-2). Daily-stamped central graders will have the identical pathology (63d calls sharing 62/63 days) once populated (sector-central-us-6). (2) **No CI at all where it matters most.** Both central graders print rank-IC to 4 decimals and tier hit-rates with a floor of n≥3 and **zero** CI/SE/Wilson/bootstrap/multiple-testing correction (grep confirms none), while the *cycles* grader in the same repo has `_wilson`, 800-draw date-blocked `_boot_gap_ci`, and `MIN_EARN_N=40` — the fix is a port, not an invention (predictive-power-3, china-sector-cycles-4). (3) **Pooled heterogeneous cross-sections.** US rank-IC ranks 44 clean SPDR sectors against 180 survivorship-inflated baskets in one per-date correlation (sector-central-us-miss, sector-central-us-4); the China excess-vs-bench subtracts a *total-return* benchmark (`000001.SS` auto_adjust=True) from *price-return* Shenwan sectors, baking a ~dividend-yield negative drift into every excess number (china-sector-central-2). Add the flat-call denominator inflation — hit-rate divides directional hits by `len(g)` including 37.5% (US) / 78% (CN) flat calls, structurally capping skill and grading tier-membership as if it were a long/short signal (sector-central-us-3, china-sector-cycles-4).

**Direction of solution:** adopt one shared measurement library across ALL graders — (a) de-overlap or block-bootstrap every forward window and report effective n, never raw n; (b) attach Wilson CIs to hit-rates and HAC/bootstrap CIs to IC/excess, and *refuse to emit a point estimate without its interval* ('IC +0.08 [−0.31,+0.44], n=6'); (c) apply an FDR/Bonferroni note across the tier×horizon grid; (d) never pool clean sectors with hindsight baskets — separate scorecards keyed by `kind`, with baskets graded only on creation-date-forward live tracking; (e) match numerator/denominator return bases (price vs total-return); (f) compute hit-rate over directional calls only and report flat coverage separately.

#### 6. The headline 'prediction machine' (cycle.html / markets.html) is an ungradable hand-typed drawing, and its position axis is neither computed nor consistent with the engines

**Severity: HIGH**

From a validation lens the flagship narrative pages are the worst offenders because they *claim* prediction while being uninstrumentable. `cycle_app.js:57-126` builds a half-cosine cosmetically pinned so `value(today)===now.pos` where `now.pos` is a hand-typed integer (cycle_data.js:88 pos:91); the falsifiers are inert prose no code evaluates; `confidence` is read nowhere; `META.today=2026.48` is frozen so the page silently rots and mis-draws 'now', and once wired to a real clock the `Math.max(tc, today+0.15)` guard would *push elapsed turn dates forward* and draw them as fresh (cycle-flagship-1/2/3, cycle-flagship-miss). markets.html is worse still: it *discards* the curator's hand-typed `pos` and derives the plotted position from `posFromDrawdown = 92*exp(-dd/30)` (markets_app.js:71,83) — an unvalidated exponential steep near zero (−3%→83 vs −8%→70) that contradicts the analyst's own phase call by up to 21 points and produces a self-contradictory tooltip (India plots 'Late·extended' while the chip says 'Downturn', markets-global-2/4/miss). #### 7. Total-return price basis silently distorts every turn, oscillator, drawdown, invalidation level, and excess-return statistic across the whole stack

**Severity: MEDIUM**

This is a measurement-validity defect that touches literally every cycle: `collectors/yahoo.py:100` downloads `auto_adjust=True`, so `data/yahoo/*.parquet` `close` is dividend-adjusted **total return**, not price (corroborated `manager_trades.py:25`, `_stock_ohlc.py:92`). The fixed-percent ZigZag, the detrended-stochastic percentile, `find_troughs`, the `failed-cycle` test (`c.iloc[-1] < dcl_price`, cycles.py:253), and the printed invalidation *price level* all treat a TR series as raw price (sector-cycles-us-1, cycles-core-5, country-cycles-7, cross-cutting-ontology-5, predictive-power-5, macro-regime-3). **Direction of solution:** feed the price-structure/turn/oscillator/invalidation math an *unadjusted* (or split-only) close, and reserve the TR series for return/momentum stats — store both. This is 'medium' only because it biases rather than fabricates — but it silently corrupts turn dates, cone amplitudes, and hit-rates for the entire ex-clean-ETF universe, so it must be fixed before any backfilled grade is trustworthy.

### H · `engine/cycles.py` — shared MTF/turn/ladder/entry core

*8 findings — 4 high, 3 medium, 1 low.*

> cycles.py is the platform's shared cycle brain: it detects troughs/pivots (find_troughs, _pivots), classifies an instrument's position in a hand-fixed daily/investor-cycle clock (cycle_state, using the asset-class presets DC_BAND=(36,42), IC_BAND_W=(16,26)), builds a daily/3-day/weekly/monthly MACD-RSI-StochRSI snapshot (mtf_snapshot), and folds all of that into an 8-state signal ladder (ladder_state) with an entry-timing narrator (entry_timing) and a walk-forward grader (calibrate_ladder). The architecture LOOKS rigorous and self-honest — it computes forward hit-rate and drawdown per state and ships ladder_calibration.json to the UI. But three deep problems hollow it out. First, the conviction score that actually drives ranking is a hardcoded LADDER_SCORE dict whose ordering is INVERTED relative to the module's own measured stats: DECLINE (score -80, 'AVOID') has the best forward return (+2.37%, 60.8% hit) while FRESH BUY (score +80, 'BUY') has the worst (+1.13%, 57.4%), and all eight states cluster at the ~58% up-day base rate — the calibration is loaded for display only and never binds the score. Second, the cycle bands are cycle-theory numerology never fit to or validated against the instrument's own trough spacing (which find_troughs computes and then discards). Third, turn detection is fragmented — cycles.find_troughs powers the ladder while sector_cycles._detect_swings (a % ZigZag) powers the visible cycle wheels that country_cycles and china_sector_cycles delegate to — so the same 'cycle low' concept has two contradictory answers. Compounding all of it, the detectors are non-causal (the last window/k bars can't confirm, so the anchor repaints), the price series is dividend-adjusted total-return (biasing the failed-cycle test and quoting untradeable invalidation levels), and a stack of hand-tuned overlay nudges (+/-8-12 pts for liquidity/early/macro/vol) shifts the score by more than the entire between-state spread while being graded by nothing.
