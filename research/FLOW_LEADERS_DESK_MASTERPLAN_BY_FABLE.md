# Flow Leaders Desk — masterplan (by Fable)

**Status:** ADJUDICATED + DISPATCHED 2026-07-11 (post red-team round 1). **Owner:** Fable main loop (rulings/merges); Sonnet builds; Opus reviews.
**Provenance:** operator directive 2026-07-11 ("integrate options flow / top net impact into top stock picks; find momentum leaders that become durable crowded winners; rethink how Terminal uses options flow to surface picks") → 7-lane codebase census + supplemental charting-app census → external methods dossier (academic + practitioner, formula-level) → 2 independent Opus red-teams (epistemics: REWORK→resolved; architecture: SHIP-WITH-FIXES→applied) → this ruling of record.

> **In plain English:** today the pipeline is *picks-first*: stocks get chosen (standouts, conviction funnel) and options are consulted afterwards as context. Meanwhile the "Top Net Impact" board already displays the day's flow leaders — but it is display-only, soft-signed, 0DTE-contaminated, has no persistence memory, no OI confirmation, and feeds nothing. This program adds the missing *flow-first* lens: a nightly desk that tracks, per name, **recurring net-impact leadership with price confirmation** (Board A) and **washouts whose flow flips positive** (Board B), each as K-of-N boolean confluence chips over deterministic legs, each accruing a Pick Lab forward ledger. Everything is display-tier with honest per-source and coverage labels; nothing ranks, gates, or sizes any authority surface; promotion is a separate pre-registered question that opens only when the ledgers mature (~2026-10-15+). The desk *describes* flow leadership; it does not predict winners.

## §1 Verdict on current usage (census findings this ruling stands on)

1. **"We just take stocks from Standout Stocks and assess their options" — partially true, and weaker than assumed.** In Mastermind, standouts are one of four candidate sources; options context is read per candidate (`portfolio/lenses.py::_flow_row`) with direction taken only from ΔOI; as of 2026-07-09 `gate_go=false`, so zero standout names even enter from that source. In the charting-app Terminal there is **no** standout surface and **no** leaders board; the Options Hub Tide tab shows `top_net_impact[]` (R2 `live_flow/tide_current.json`) as pure display. **Nowhere does flow generate candidates.**
2. **Top Net Impact today** = top-15 by |net_premium_mn| from the SOFT minute-tick path (net-sign recovery 0.41), 0DTE included, mega-cap dollar bias, no persistence, no OI confirmation.
3. **The reliable signed path exists but is embryonic:** `data/tape_flow/daily/` (Lee-Ready on ThetaData NBBO; agreement 0.8848 / net-sign recovery 0.80, both calibration bars PASSED; per-source direction authority per RO-9) holds **only SPY (1 row)** on origin/main despite `--mode forward` being designed for the full `gex_symbols()` universe — the forward accrual is broken or never ran. W5a audits this FIRST.
4. **OI confirmation is possible since 2026-06-16:** `data/polygon_gex/chains/` (per-strike OI/volume/IV, ~180k rows ≈ 5 MB/day, ~370–403 underlyings) is the only per-strike OI time series; it cannot be backfilled.
5. **Confluence machinery exists and is consumed, never rebuilt:** `mtf_upturn.v1` (D/3D/W/2W MACD legs via epoch-anchored `htf_durability._biweekly_close`), `macd_bars_to_cross` ETA (D/3D/W/M; missing 2W), per-stock StochRSI math pinned at `engine/signal_quality.py::_stoch_rsi_kd` (weekly sector variant in `engine/oracle/oscillators.py`), IFT washout/RVOL/durability functions, personality labels (`stair_step_leader`, `failed_breakout_trap`), momentum block (`mom_12_1`, `high52w_prox`, `rs_1m/3m/6m`), `rel_volume`, `obv_slope_up`, Pick Lab ledger/grader/scoreboard.
6. **External dossier** (formula-level) supports exactly what is computable EOD at $0: flow z vs own history (z≥2), market-cap normalization for cross-sectional ranking, OI-confirmed flow, term-structure breadth, 0DTE exclusion (≥7 DTE), earnings-window exclusion (Cremers et al. 2023: option purchases do NOT predict returns ahead of scheduled events), vol-trade both-sides flags. Multi-day leaderboard recurrence ("crowding-in") is practitioner-grade and academically unvalidated — hence display + forward ledger, zero claims.

## §3 Rulings

- **FL-R1 — Flow-first discovery lane ADOPTED (display + shadow-ledger only).** A nightly Flow Leaders engine turns the flow store into a *described* candidate stream with two archetype boards. The existing picks-first options assessment is retained unchanged. **No authority-path consumer exists in this program** (see FL-R10/FL-R13; the Mastermind intake is PARKED, §5).
- **FL-R2 — Rank-quantity law (argued, not asserted).** Each board sorts by ONE measured quantity: Board A by **leadership recurrence count** (sessions in the top-20 of the ex-0DTE-aware, mktcap-normalized net-impact table within the trailing 10; ties broken by normalized |net premium|); Board B by **days since flow inflection** (ascending). Why this is not the R3-forbidden composite: a count of an observable event over a fixed window is a single measured statistic — the same legal shape as IFT's `RVOL_tod` (a constructed ratio) and flow_desk's |net premium| (a constructed signed sum), both shipped precedents; it fuses no positioning keys, carries no weights, and (with W4 parked) orders no authority surface — the only downstream consumer is the Pick Lab **shadow** ledger, which is precisely CONST-ART2's sanctioned earning path. Window parameters (top-20, trailing-10, ≥3-of-5) are **pre-registered-arbitrary**: frozen now, disclosed as dossier-unsupported choices, changeable only via engine-version bump + ledger reset, and not tuned on any observed case (the META 2026-07-09/10 example motivated the *question*, not the thresholds; z≥2 and 0DTE exclusion come from the external dossier §7.5/§6.4).
- **FL-R3 — Per-source signing honesty.** Every flow field carries `signing_source ∈ {tape, minute_tick}` + per-field `asof`. Direction renders `~`-soft everywhere (RUL-F3.12) regardless of source until the multi-session tape calibration extension flips; magnitude leads all copy. No options root-direction flip (NWF3-U6, nondelegable).
- **FL-R4 — 0DTE hygiene.** Leader quantity computed ex-0DTE where the source allows (tape DTE buckets); on the soft path, names with `zerodte_share > 0.60` are excluded from recurrence counting and carry a "0DTE-dominated" chip (frozen threshold per dossier §6.4's ≥7-DTE principle). 0DTE is bucket-labeled context, never highlighted.
- **FL-R5 — OI-confirmation flag ADOPTED (display), PIT-pinned.** Definitions are self-contained on the chains store: *dominant strikes of flow-day d* = top-3 strikes by that day's per-strike `volume` in `chains/<d>.parquet`; `oi_confirmed(d)` = ΔOI(d→d+1) > 0 at those strikes AND flow-day volume ≥ 3× prior OI there. The flag therefore becomes computable only on the **next** nightly; it stamps the board `asof` on which it is known, never retroactively qualifies a prior fire (see FL-R8 PIT law). **Adjacency registered (RUL-2):** DOI *persistence* is DEAD at sector level (W-E1) and S-DOI single-name accrues under skeptical prior — this flag is a different construction (freshness confirmation of an observed flow event), is never a standalone signal, and inherits the skeptical prior in FLW-2. `net_doi` stays display context only, never a leg.
- **FL-R6 — Confluence = K-of-N booleans; tri-state legs; frozen leg sets.** Legs are True/False/**null**; null legs are excluded from both numerator and denominator — boards display **K/N_available** per row, never bare K.
  **Board A — Flow Leadership (K of ≤8):** A1 `flow_recur` (≥3 of trailing 5 sessions in top-20 normalized table) · A2 `flow_z_hot` (gross-premium z ≥ 2, min 20 obs) · A3 `oi_confirmed` (FL-R5) · A4 `ts_breadth` (≥2 non-0DTE expiry buckets net-positive; tape source only) · A5 `price_leader` (`ribbon_up` AND `rs_1m > 0`) · A6 `near_high` (`high52w_prox ≥ 0.90`) · A7 `vol_confirm` (`rel_volume ≥ 1.30` OR `obv_slope_up`) · A8 `not_trap` (NOT `failed_breakout_trap`).
  **Board B — Washout Turn (K of ≤8):** B1 `washout_recent` (IFT `washout_context`) · B2 `oversold_osc` (weekly StochRSI K<20 within 3 weekly bars OR `rsi_stack_oversold` within 10 sessions) · B3 `turn_organ` (`mtf_upturn ≥ UPTURN_WATCH`) · B4 `htf_cross_near` (W/2W MACD crossed or ETA ≤ 2 bars — **display chip only, never fire-qualifying**, per A2 "catastrophically late" case law) · B5 `flow_inflect` (soft path: net premium flips positive after ≥3 negative sessions, `~`-labeled; tape path: ask-side call premium resumes) · B6 `oi_confirmed` · B7 `vol_confirm` · B8 `not_trap`.
  `dt_contra` excluded (DT-R11b). DOI-persistence and skew-deceleration are NOT legs. IFT/`mtf_upturn`/pick-lab functions consumed by import; re-implementation is a build error.
- **FL-R7 — 2W oscillator surface BUILT (display).** New pure helpers in `engine/htf_oscillators.py`: per-stock **2W StochRSI K/D** (via `htf_durability._biweekly_close` + `engine/signal_quality.py::_stoch_rsi_kd`; `resample('2W-FRI')` FORBIDDEN) and **2W MACD `bars_to_cross` ETA** (new 2W frame following the `cycles._tf_state` projection pattern, cap 6 bars; `mtf_upturn` untouched). Empty/short series (`_biweekly_close` gates: <20 daily bars or <4 weekly bars) → null with `htf_coverage=false`, never fake-False. **A2 case law cited:** the 2W StochRSI turn tested NULL at basket grain ("catastrophically late") — these fields are operator-requested display context and B-board chips only; never standalone, never a sort key, never fire-qualifying.
- **FL-R8 — Forward ledgers via Pick Lab, single-writer preserved.** Two new frozen books registered **in `engine/pick_lab/registry.py::REGISTRY`** and fired **inside `build_pick_lab.py`'s existing loop** (no second writer to `fires.jsonl`; both `== 23` asserts and count tests bump to 25): `plab_flow_leader` (Board A, entry rule = A1 AND A8 AND ≥1 of {A2,A3}, top-12 by recurrence, ruler `21d_spy_excess`) and `plab_flow_washout` (Board B, entry rule = B1 AND B5 AND B8 — **washout × flow-inflection, a construction distinct from the KILLED washout×2W-turn (#1747, ESXA3-FV-C: scope_fence null, adjacency registered with skeptical prior); the late 2W legs do not qualify fires** — ruler `21d_abs_reversion_capture_mfe_mae`).
