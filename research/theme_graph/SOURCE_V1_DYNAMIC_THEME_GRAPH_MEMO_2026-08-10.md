# Mastermind Dynamic Theme Graph

## Brainstorming & Strategic Ideation Document

## Executive Thesis

Traditional sector systems such as GICS were designed for
classification, reporting, and indexing. They are not optimized for
understanding how modern markets actually move.

Modern markets organize around overlapping economic narratives:

-   Artificial Intelligence
-   Data Center Infrastructure
-   Defense Modernization
-   Space Economy
-   Nuclear Renaissance
-   Semiconductor CapEx
-   Energy Security
-   Critical Minerals

The strategic opportunity is to create a living Theme Intelligence Layer
inside Mastermind.

The model:

Company → Theme Graph → Market Behavior → Capital Flow → Investment
Decision

Theia Insights validates this direction through its dynamic
classification, thematic factor models, and theme indices. Their public
materials describe dynamic company exposure mapping, 200+ thematic
factors, and daily theme indices. The opportunity is to create a
trading-focused version optimized for Mastermind.

# Three-Layer Theme Model

## 1. Business Exposure

Question:

"What does this company actually do?"

Sources:

-   SEC filings
-   Earnings calls
-   Investor presentations
-   Revenue segments
-   R&D
-   CapEx
-   Contracts
-   Partnerships
-   Acquisitions

Output:

Company → Theme exposure percentage

Example:

Uber:

-   Ride Sharing
-   Freight Logistics
-   Delivery Platforms
-   Mobile Applications
-   AI
-   EV

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Narrative Exposure

Question:

"What does the market believe this company represents?"

Sources:

-   News
-   Analyst research
-   Social discussion
-   Earnings language
-   Investor presentations

Important because markets often trade narratives before fundamentals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Market Exposure

Question:

"Does this stock actually trade with the theme?"

Inputs:

-   Returns
-   Correlation
-   Factor regression
-   Options activity
-   ETF flows
-   Institutional ownership

This creates the empirical market theme.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Theme Scoring

Avoid binary labels.

Use weighted exposures.

Concept:

Theme Score = Business Exposure × Narrative Exposure × Market Exposure ×
Confidence

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Ontology Design

Do not start with thousands of themes.

Start:

Level 1: 10-15 macro categories

Level 2: 50-100 industries

Level 3: 200-300 tradable themes

Level 4: Micro themes that grow organically

Example:

Defense

→ Defense Technology

→

-   Missile Defense
-   C4ISR
-   Military AI
-   Electronic Warfare

-   Space Defense
