# Mastermind × 同花顺

## Deep Research Brainstorming & Intelligence Architecture Expansion Memo

## Executive Thesis

同花顺 is not valuable because of its charts. Its real strength is that
it represents the market through many simultaneous observation layers:

-   market direction
-   breadth
-   liquidity
-   capital migration
-   sector rotation
-   theme popularity
-   participant behavior
-   news catalysts
-   macro environment

The opportunity for Mastermind is not to copy the dashboard. It is to
transform these observations into a persistent reasoning system.

同花顺 answers:

> What is happening?

Mastermind should answer:

> Why is it happening, what state are we in, what historical situations
> resemble this, and what happens next?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Market Digital Twin

The ultimate goal should be a living representation of market state.

Instead of isolated indicators:

    Market:
      Risk appetite: 72/100
      Liquidity: improving
      Theme expansion: accelerating
      Speculation: recovering
      Institutional participation: rising
      Crowding risk: moderate

The system continuously updates the market's current "state".

# 3. Dynamic Theme Intelligence Engine

Traditional sectors are insufficient.

Markets move through narratives:

Examples:

-   AI
-   commercial space
-   defense
-   silver shortage
-   robotics
-   semiconductor equipment

A theme should be represented as a graph.

    Theme
     |
     +-- companies
     |
     +-- supply chain
     |
     +-- catalysts
     |
     +-- related themes
     |
     +-- historical cycles

Each company can have:

-   economic exposure
-   trading exposure
-   narrative exposure

These are different.

A company may have low current revenue exposure but become the dominant
trading vehicle for a theme.

# 9. Dislocation Engine

The biggest institutional opportunities often come from mismatches.

Examples:

## Price vs Fundamentals

Stock falls 15%. Business unchanged.

## Price vs Theme

Theme breaks out. Stock ignored.

## Flow vs Price

Accumulation occurs without price movement.

## Attention vs Price

Narrative rises before valuation reprices.

Mastermind should hunt these automatically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
