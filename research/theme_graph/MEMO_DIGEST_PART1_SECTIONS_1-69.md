### PART I — Why static taxonomies fail (§5–12)

- **§5 Layer above GICS, not a replacement.** Cross-industry "economic transmission system" grouping (AI capex supercycle: compute/networking/power/cooling/construction as one system spanning Nvidia/Arista/Vertiv/Eaton/Constellation/Quanta/Micron). Universal pattern, listed against ~14 other candidate cross-industry systems (nuclear renaissance, GLP-1, missile defense, grid modernization, copper electrification, memory cycles, critical minerals, humanoid robotics, sovereign AI, data-center power, stablecoins, drone warfare).
- **§6 Institutional precedent (context only, not a Mastermind claim).** Theia Insights (245 major themes, 3,200+ microthemes, 5 taxonomy tiers, revenue+R&D+CapEx+partnerships-based inference), S&P/Theia thematic dashboard (200+ themes), S&P Kensho (NLP/ML thematic indices from filings), MSCI (LLM-driven segment-to-theme mapping, ~40,000 securities), Morningstar (analyst judgment + fund-holdings consensus). Establishes that multi-label, text-driven thematic classification is now normal institutional practice — used to justify feasibility, not to be copied.
- **§7 Positioning: Theia = universal economic ontology; Mastermind = trading intelligence ontology.** Theia answers "who does what, by how much"; Mastermind should answer "what does the market currently believe matters, how is capital reacting, how reliable is that relationship, what opportunity does it imply." Worked example: a company with 12% economic exposure but 91% trading beta to a theme is "functioning as a high-beta proxy despite weak economic purity" — treated as signal, not noise.
- **§8 Three Reality dimensions**, formalized per company×theme×time: `E_business` (revenue segments, contracts, CapEx, R&D, guidance), `E_narrative` (news, analyst language, earnings-call emphasis, social association, search behavior, fund holdings, co-mentions), `E_market` (return correlation, residual correlation, theme beta, synchronized volume, options co-movement, cross-sectional leadership, ETF flow sensitivity). Attributed to "the prior Dynamic Theme Graph memo" (see provenance note above).
- **§9 Temporal Reality / point-in-time correctness.** `ThemeEdge{valid_from, valid_to, observed_at, confidence_at_time, evidence_available_at_time}` — required to avoid "today's ontology leaking into yesterday" and producing counterfeit backtest predictive power. Worked example: Amazon's theme identity shifted 1999→2024 (e-commerce → +logistics → +AWS/cloud → +AI infra/ads).
- **§10 StructuralExposure vs CatalystActivation.** Structural = who *can* benefit; Catalyst Activation = why *now* (recency, economic materiality, specificity, surprise, probability of monetization, time-to-revenue, procurement/CapEx confirmation). Worked example: permanent missile-defense exposure vs. a new "Golden Dome" procurement announcement activating it.
- **§11 Supply-chain role tagging** per theme↔company edge: Enabler / Input Supplier / Critical Bottleneck / Manufacturer / Platform / Distributor / Customer / Beneficiary / Substitute / Competitor / Hedge-Loser. Enables causal propagation chains (worked example: hyperscaler CapEx↑ → GPU demand↑ → HBM↑ → packaging↑ → power density↑ → cooling↑ → grid bottlenecks↑). Explicitly: "without supply-chain role this is a basket; with it, it's a causal network."
- **§12 Market-perceived trading-role taxonomy**: Fundamental Anchor / Narrative Flagship / Momentum Leader / Liquidity Leader / First Mover / Confirmation Stock / Upstream Bottleneck / Secondary Beneficiary / Sympathy Proxy / Late Follower / Laggard / False Association. Purpose: breadth must not be counted naively — "flagship +10%, 40 members flat" (fragile) reads differently than "flagship +6%, bottlenecks +5%, median +3%, ignored secondaries breaking out" (diffusion).
