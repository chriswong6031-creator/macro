## 0. Executive ruling

The Codex memo's diagnosis is right and its inventory is wrong. The house does **not** lack thematic machinery — the census found a ~4,900-line, 18-theme foresight desk with a live stage machine (PRECIPICE/BROADENING/RE-RATING/GLUT-RISK/WATCH), a deterministic thesis monitor (9 open / 3 BROKEN), a forward-graded ledger (45 rows accruing), a demand desk with 40 open falsifiable theses, a divergence radar with an IC harness (113 theses, 3,860 snapshots), 46 scored baskets with lifecycle labels, an 8-leg real-activity fuser, and 268-subsector RRG rotation. What the house lacks is **civilization between the organs**:

1. Three uncoordinated theme vocabularies (foresight's 18, baskets' 46, Finviz's 40/268) with no crosswalk — the root cause of every silo.
2. No theme-level thesis objects (mechanism / winners / losers / falsifiers as queryable records).
3. No persisted phase-state history (stages recomputed nightly, transitions mostly unlogged).
4. No generalized beneficiary/loser graph (one hardcoded AI-capex chain; 3 macro pathways).
5. No per-leg asymmetry read, no loser maps, no cross-theme terminal, no NW citizenship (themes are invisible to world_state, ask_brain, cortex, mastermind_context).
6. Accountability theater in three places: `earliness_log.jsonl` is written and read by nothing; qledger `falsifier`/`check_by` fields are stored and never evaluated; foresight's ledger logs only 2 of 5 stages.

TIL is therefore **connective tissue plus honesty machinery, not new signals**. Everything ships display/shadow tier freely (gauntlet = promotion gate, not build gate); the grading waves are what let any of it ever earn more.

> Product target (unchanged from the Codex memo, now enforceable): when asked "what is the next asymmetric theme?", the system returns a ranked *investigation packet* — theme, phase, mechanism, evidence, contradictions, crowding, entry cleanliness, falsifiers, maturity, and what data would change the view. Never a stock pick.

## 2. Rulings (R-TIL-1 … R-TIL-9)

**R-TIL-1 — Not a lobe; theme ≠ stock.** Thematic intelligence decomposes into an organ cluster + waves (Claim-Reliability precedent: misfiling waves as lobes is how sprawl happens). The DO_NOT_REBUILD "thesis lobe" KILL (#1666/#1669/#1671) closed the *per-stock Long-Term-Thesis/Expectations-Drift* construction; theme-level thesis objects are a different construction and are legal. **Fence:** the theme thesis ledger holds zero per-stock thesis records; anything thesis-shaped about an individual name routes to the long-hold program's amendment process.

**R-TIL-2 — Beneficiary/loser graph is a curated taxonomy, never a rotation call.** The graph ships as PR-reviewed config (TI-R2 shape: versioned membership + one-line rationale per edge) rendered display-only, mirroring `mechanism_pathways.v1` conventions. The TI-R5 kill stands in full force: no runtime "shock/phase active → beneficiary now" mapping may exist; graph context may temper, never direct; no board ordering, no brain-prompt escalation, no beneficiary field on any scored path. Loser legs are AVOID-shaped evidence (short-side lobe ruling: L1 is AVOID-not-SHORT).

**R-TIL-3 — No fused asymmetry number.** WA-R1 precedent: the asymmetry surface is a per-leg component panel; no master score, no weighted composite, no rank derived from it. Any pairwise leg interaction requires its own prereg naming both parents (factor-kill-interaction law).

**R-TIL-4 — Ownership/attention legs are hazard context only.** NEXTL-U13: ownership-style data may never be a positive signal; it survives as crowding-hazard context. The attention-vacuum construct is already shipped (divergence radar hidden-opportunity quadrant) — TIL surfaces it, does not rebuild it.

**R-TIL-5 — LLM = extraction with receipts, nothing else.** LLM use in TIL is limited to structuring human-originated text (Citrini articles, filings language) into records carrying exact-substring citations validated deterministically (char-span check). No LLM-originated signals, scores, escalations, or phase calls. De-escalation of calibrated keys only, per house law.

**R-TIL-6 — Grading law.** All TIL artifacts are display/shadow. Promotion of anything (phase-transition value, asymmetry legs, Citrini-follow) requires: pre-registered trial on the accrued PIT tapes; placebo control (random-theme + time-shifted, extending the qledger placebo sampler); hits defined as **alpha over a free factor panel, not raw excess** (the selection-effect test — Citrini's own claim, now our ruler); era law and time-preserving nulls apply. First eligible reads ~2026-10+.

**R-TIL-7 — Citrini is a graded evidence source, not an oracle.** DannyTrades precedent: external analysts get graded, not trusted. Signal Commons W6 (paid-data SKIP-ALL) does not bind data the operator already purchased (ThetaData precedent). Positioning-fusion law binds hard: Citrini weights/changes are display/context + PIT tape; any "follow their adds" construct is a future pre-registered trial on the accrued tape. **Ingestion mechanics are OPEN-OPERATOR** (§7.4) — no credential handling, no scraping, no docsend/substack/citrindex fetcher ships until the operator rules on the four pending access questions.

**R-TIL-8 — Scenario lab deferred, curated-only.** Citrini-style scenario maps ("who is synthetically short theme X") are legal only as PR-reviewed curated configs with `not_forecast: true` + probability bands, display-only, class-level. DEFERRED to a post-core wave; no runtime scenario→position mapping ever.

**R-TIL-9 — New-collector law.** New public-trace legs (DOL PERM/LCA, USITC/Census trade, ClinicalTrials theme rollup, phrase-velocity) must be: keyless/free, PIT-clean, off the render path (collect lane or R2), entity→ticker joins via the existing WARN/sponsor fuzzy-map pattern, and each lands as *evidence into existing organs* (theme_activity SOURCES, discovery feeds) — never as a new parallel scorer.
