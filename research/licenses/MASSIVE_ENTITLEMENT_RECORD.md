## The record

- **2026-08-09 (operator):** Mastermind holds **full licensing and distribution
  rights** for Massive stock-market data. The governing instrument is an
  **Enterprise Market Data License and Redistribution Addendum** between
  Massive.com, Inc. and Mastermind, effective **2026-08-09**.
- **The executed document is held privately by the operator and is deliberately
  NOT committed here.** This repository is PUBLIC; the agreement carries mutual
  confidentiality over each party's non-public contractual and commercial terms.
  This file is the in-repo record; the instrument itself is not a repo artifact.
  Do not commit it, quote its commercial terms, or paste its text into an issue,
  PR, or public surface.

## Rights confirmed (what engineering may rely on)

Across the enterprise data scope — trades and last sale, quotes/NBBO, aggregates
and bars (second/minute/hourly/daily), snapshots, historical archives, reference
data, corporate actions, financials, technical/calculated fields, flat-file and
bulk datasets, and WebSocket/streaming:

- **Display** — real-time and historical, in web, desktop, mobile, terminal, and
  embedded interfaces.
- **External redistribution** — raw and derived, to subscribers, customers,
  partners, and enterprise clients, through APIs, files, feeds, alerts, reports,
  dashboards, and applications.
- **Non-display use** — screening, backtesting, execution analytics, factor
  research, risk modeling, portfolio analytics, surveillance, anomaly detection,
  automated calculation.
- **Derived Materials** — indicators, features, signals, forecasts, scores,
  rankings, research, charts, market-intelligence products. **Mastermind owns
  them**, and they survive the term.
- **AI/ML** — training, fine-tuning, evaluation, retrieval, grounding, feature
  engineering, embeddings, and inference. No vendor ownership attaches to model
  weights, embeddings, features, or outputs.
- **White-label and embedded** — Mastermind branding, co-branding, OEM, reseller,
  affiliate and partner distribution. **Source attribution is not required.**
- **End-user export** — CSV, JSON, Parquet, API responses, chart data, reports,
  and other machine- or human-readable formats.
- **Retention** — archival copies for audit, reproducibility, model validation,
  backtesting, compliance and continuity; previously delivered history and all
  Derived Materials remain usable after the term.
- **Commercial freedom** — Mastermind sets its own pricing, packaging, tiers,
  bundling, trials, and end-user terms.

## Operating notes

- Some datasets carry provider-side conditions (for example, raw NBBO
  redistribution can require an exchange entitlement). Those are identified by the
  vendor in writing per dataset; treat a written vendor designation as the
  authority for a specific feed, not this file.
- The **debrand law still applies**: the vendor is never named on public product
  surfaces, regardless of the fact that attribution is not contractually required.
- Nothing here changes the epistemic gauntlet. Licensing governs whether data may
  be shown and redistributed; it grants no signal authority. Promotion to
  rank/size/gate still runs the normal pre-registered gates.
