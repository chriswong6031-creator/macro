# Three-Lobes Adjudication — Zero New Charters from an Attractive Memo

**Source:** PR #1673. Primary doc: `research/NW_CODEX_THREE_LOBES_ADJUDICATION_BY_FABLE.md`. **Status:** canonical (RUL-SUCC-8).

## What was asked

Codex proposed three new Neural Web lobes: (6) Claim Reliability / Narrative Truth, (7) Macro & Policy Transmission Fingerprints, (8) Portfolio & Thesis-Independence. The question was whether to charter any of the three.

## What was decided (the holding)

- **RUL-C1 (no charters):** zero new lobes chartered. The two-lobe concurrency cap (L1 short-side + L3 dispersion) was fully consumed. All work authorized below is a rail wave, an existing-artifact wave, or a registered study.
- **Codex-6 (Claim Reliability):** decomposed into: (a) an already-built substrate (`site/qledger/track_record.json`), (b) a coverage-accountability audit (W-A → PR-B), (c) an additive bridge lobe key `claim_reliability` in `mastermind_context.py` (W-B → PR-C), and (d) two come-back experiments (NARR-2/NARR-3, calendar-gated — 21d/63d grades not yet matured). Not a lobe.
- **Codex-7 (Macro Transmission):** identified as docket entry L6, already gated on a Phase-0 study. W-C authorized a gate-clearing study (L6-P0 — see case 09). No charter.
- **Codex-8 (Portfolio Independence):** identified as docket L8 (mostly out-of-scope held-book = Mastermind repo). In-scope slice (~70% already built via `reflexivity.py`). W-D authorized a reflexivity wave: five-candidates-one-thesis detector + N_eff history.
- **RUL-C4 (L6-P0 legal shape):** per-axis, never fused (Signal Commons R3 applies); sector/basket grain only; PIT flags at fire date; governor-registered; kernel untouched; drawdown-covariate control mandatory; pass re-opens charter question without auto-chartering.
- **RUL-C11 (L6-P0 FDR family):** `fdr_family='macro_tx'` flat pooled (not `'replay'`); declared budget 12 (4 axes × 3 horizons, all budget-counted even if labeled descriptive); sub-scoping prohibited.
- **RUL-C10 (LLM law):** no LLM originates, escalates, or scores anything in this program; bridge carries only measured qledger statistics.
- **RUL-C3 (QI ownership boundary):** qledger grading semantics belong to QI program; this program is read-only over grade artifacts; no modification of `scripts/grade_qledger.py` or claim schemas.
- **RUL-C7 (reflexivity rules):** R-A (held-agnostic), R-E (US board only), R-F/R7 (display-only, `is_context_only`, no behavioral consumer) all stand.

## Lenses that did the work

- **Case law:** cap rule (two-lobe max, L1+L3 consumed) blocked all three charters before evidence was weighed. Two of three were already in the docket (L6, L8) with standing gates Codex had not cited.
- **Authority:** RUL-C4 enforced the per-axis/never-fused fusion prohibition (Signal Commons R3) on the conditioning study; RUL-C10 enforced LLM de-escalation-only law on the bridge key.
- **Statistics:** RUL-C11 resolved the FDR family question — a conditioning contrast study requires its own `macro_tx` family, not the `replay` family; all 12 cells budget-counted to prevent forking-paths laundering.
- **Ownership:** RUL-C3 fenced QI's grading semantics; `claim_reliability` is read-only over QI's committed artifacts.
- **Build feasibility:** Red-team (Opus ×2) falsified six draft claims before specs were finalized (§2.5), including that PR-B's markdown append would survive nightly regeneration (it does not — doc gets its own writer).

## Citable holding

When a lobe proposal decomposes entirely into existing-artifact waves, rail waves, and gated studies, the correct taxonomy verdict is zero charters; misfiling waves as lobes is how program sprawl happens, and the two-lobe concurrency cap is case law that blocks charter approval at Tier 2 while it is consumed.
