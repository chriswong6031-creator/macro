# R-ORTH Covariance Rail — Rail Not Lobe, Taxonomy Discipline

**Source:** PRs #1739 (PR-1 research record + diagnostics), #1748 (PR-2 pca_health engine lane), #1768 (PR-3 covariance spine MVP). Primary doc: `research/pca_factor_orthogonality/R_ORTH_MASTERPLAN_BY_FABLE.md`. **Status:** canonical (RUL-SUCC-8).

## What was asked

Codex watched external quant lectures and proposed building a Neural Web lobe that measures PCA orthogonality to detect when five agreeing lobes are independent witnesses versus one crowded bet wearing five badges. The question was whether to charter a new lobe and what machinery to build.

## What was decided (the holding)

- **RUL-ORTH-1 (rail, not lobe):** R-ORTH is a rail — `tier: infrastructure` (JSON state) / `tier: display` (rendered surfaces), `horizon_role: context`, `owner_program: nw-rails`, `scored_path_surfaces: []`. It never originates a trade, never gates, never ranks, never moves sizing.
- **RUL-ORTH-8 (null-calibration law):** the OOS-orthogonality-decay headline is a noise artifact, not evidence. Opus review reproduced the diagnostic and constructed the null: at n=21 the max-|off-diagonal| PC correlation statistic has a finite-sample floor of ~0.40–0.71 regardless of OOS status; a within-window contiguous-block slice already yields a median of 0.477 vs the observed 0.5425. Consequence: ALL orthogonality/stability/decay metrics shipped by this program must carry their within-window null (contiguous-block resample, ≥200 draws) and display as a percentile vs that null. Raw-threshold health bands on small-sample correlation statistics are illegal in this program.
- **RUL-ORTH-2 (registration):** `data/neuralweb/covariance_spine.json` registered in `config/synapse.yml` as context-only; storage in git (data/neuralweb/ is git-tracked by design).
- **RUL-ORTH-3 (pca_health placement):** yield-curve PCA health lives inline in `engine/yield_curve.py` as additive optional sub-block `shape["pca"]["pca_health"]` — the heavy 45-year rolling harness stays in `scripts/research/treasury_pca_diagnostics.py`.
- **RUL-ORTH-5 (visibility before replay):** effective-witness fields are visible on committee/admin surfaces immediately as display-only descriptive context with an explicit "descriptive — not gauntleted" label; no trust conditioning until an R1 replay shows lift.
- **RUL-ORTH-6 (DISP-EIGEN-1 deferred):** gate activation deferred until DISP-GATE-1's basis non-stationarity blocker (34.8% flip rate) is resolved. Descriptive eigen fields ship now on a fixed trailing-252d basis only — no expanding-window percentiles.
- **RUL-ORTH-9 (no recomputation):** board-candidate breadth stays in `reflexivity.py`; theme ENB stays in `foresight_enb.py`; factor decorrelation stays in `factor_orthogonal.py`. R-ORTH reads their outputs, never recomputes.
- **RUL-ORTH-7 (residual RV lobe bar):** charter drafted only if ALL FOUR `RORTH-RV-*` experiments show net-of-cost lift surviving flat `replay` FDR family, incremental to existing Oracle/Entry/Factor evidence, and persistent outside crisis windows.
- **RUL-ORTH-11 (deterministic annotations):** all committee annotations (`same_bet_warning`, `dominant_overlap_cluster`) are generated by deterministic rules in the engine; LLM surfaces may quote and explain them but never originate or escalate them.
- **RUL-ORTH-12 (factor residual layer deferred):** per-name residual factor coordinates deferred — substrate is weak (~3y factor history, annual fundamentals, zero FDR survivors).

## Lenses that did the work

- **Case law:** taxonomy precedent from the docket (LOBE vs RAIL vs WAVE classification) resolved the core question immediately. Five existing orthogonality systems were already built (`reflexivity.py`, `foresight_enb.py`, `factor_orthogonal.py`, `factor_exposure.py`, `cross_asset.py`) — the case law lens confirmed the gap is specifically live lobe-to-lobe covariance accounting, not a new lobe.
- **Statistics:** RUL-ORTH-8 is the most important statistical ruling in this case. Opus review showed the OOS decay signal was finite-sample noise (null median 0.477 vs observed 0.5425); without a within-window null, raw decay statistics are uninterpretable. This ruling propagates to every future orthogonality metric in this system.
- **Build feasibility:** spine density is thin (most engines < 15 active days since 2024; only `track_record` is dense at 638 active days). The covariance spine therefore prints coverage (`n_lobes_measurable`, per-pair floors) and emits nulls where density is insufficient. The program is honest about its own sparsity rather than claiming completeness.
- **Ops budget:** pca_health inline in `yield_curve.py` is bounded to milliseconds (small block-resample null on 9×9 matrices); covariance spine is a non-fatal step in the nightly after `build_kernel_estimates`.

## Citable holding

When an external analysis proposes a new lobe for functionality that decomposes into (a) reading existing system outputs and (b) one genuinely new cross-system accounting artifact, the correct taxonomy is rail at infrastructure tier — and any stability metric published by the rail must carry its within-window null distribution or it is uninterpretable on small samples.
