## 0. Routing law

These commissions reduce Fable token burn without delegating architectural judgment. Grok receives one narrow observable mission at a time. It does not reconstruct the whole vision, choose feature signs, modify thresholds after seeing outcomes, decide rights, merge PRs, or grant authority.

No commission is automatically active. Fable or Sol must name the exact commission id and prove its status gate is open.

## 1. Common operating contract

- Preserve exact raw source strings, clocks, and receipts.
- Use official/primary sources where the commission involves rules or public events.
- Never infer a missing actor, event, or clock.
- Never backfill first-seen from occurrence date.
- Never access outcomes before a preregistration/fixture commission says so.
- Keep fixtures independent of production helpers when the purpose is to test those helpers.
- A null or blocked result is a valid return.
- Do not touch `engine/china_board_rank.py`, `engine/china_prophet_shadow.py`, exact-plane authority constants, or AgentOS decisions unless a Fable commission explicitly names the path.
- No public product copy or UI design.
