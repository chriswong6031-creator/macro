1. **Anatomy:** deterministic public evidence and observation-state masks.

**User value:** earlier, clearer, less chase-prone recognition of plausible China reratings.  
**Machine value:** lawful structured context that distinguishes carrier, transition, mechanism, stage, and access.

**Commercial value:** better capture of accessible winners and better allocation of scarce featured shelf slots—not a higher count of unfillable boards.  
**Data moat:** immutable chronology linking public professional/corporate evidence, exact market state, transition anatomy, sector topology, Prophet state, predictions, corrections, and actual access/outcomes.

### 2.4 The moat is chronology, not indicator count

A competitor can compute drawdowns, wicks, volume ratios, or moving averages. The defensible system is the correction-safe sequence:


## 4. Research synthesis frozen from R1–R5

### 4.1 The strongest working model

The evidence supports the following hierarchy:

1. **Carrier:** washout, basing, under-long-reference, low supply elasticity.
2. **Transition:** downside price impact falls; bad external days do less damage; constructive volume becomes more effective; reclaims hold.
3. **Mechanism authorization:** professional discovery, public catalyst shadow, ownership/supply change, or sector relay becomes lawful and visible.
4. **Release:** Prophet’s fresh entry state identifies timing while extension and access remain acceptable.
5. **Recognition/reflexivity:** retail attention, LHB, broad turnover, and boards become visible—often later and increasingly crowded.

Static washout is not the missing selector. More visible buying is not a stable bullish primitive. The likely incremental information lies in **negative supply becoming less effective**, independent professional/corporate evidence, and ordered sequence.

### 4.2 Six mechanism families remain separate

1. **Fundamental/professional discovery**
2. **Public corporate catalyst and information resolution**
3. **Policy/theme relay and sector diffusion**
4. **Ownership/control/capital action and supply change**
5. **Reflexive attention and momentum ecology**
6. **Supply squeeze and seal microstructure**

A seventh outcome annotation—earnings/fundamental reinterpretation—belongs inside professional/corporate discovery, not a separate scoring plane.

### 4.3 Actor-state law

Professional discovery, public dissemination, retail recognition, and speculative crowding are different actor states with potentially opposite signs. `UNKNOWN` never becomes institution, retail, informed, insider, or smart money.

### 4.4 Clock law

Every evidence observation is defined by:

```text
event/occurrence time
publication time
Mastermind first-seen time
system-recorded time
decision cutoff
correction time
```

The lawful known-at time is the latest binding clock required by the source contract. A backfilled occurrence date never creates historical knowledge.

### 4.5 Sequence law

The flagship sequence hypothesis is:

```text
professional discovery or public process becomes known
→ ownership/supply or belief begins changing
→ downside damage weakens
→ reclaim/controlled release appears
→ leader/sector relay authorizes the move
→ Prophet becomes actionable
→ broad retail/board attention appears
```

The reversed sequence and unordered family count are mandatory controls.

### 4.6 Event/access law

The program optimizes accessible repricing, not board count:

```text
P(first exact board)
P(continuous rerating without board)
P(adverse breakdown)
P(pre-onset access)
P(next-open access)
P(continuation | actual proxy fill)
capacity / queue risk
```

### 9.1 Predictions are immutable promises

### 9.2 Correction cases

| Case | Frozen behavior |
|---|---|
| Source corrected before cutoff | Use the corrected value and receipt in the first prediction. |
| Source corrected after cutoff | Preserve original prediction; append correction branch. |
| Candidate row changes | Candidate digest mismatch; quarantine comparison. |
| Calendar moves entry/session | Same prediction id; append calendar correction. |
| Outcome changes | Append superseding grade; preserve prior grade. |

**Rationale:** Different corporate, policy, ownership, reflexive, and supply mechanisms can produce the same limit-up endpoint but have different lawful clocks, precursors, confounders, access, and continuation.

### DEC:CNLI-CARRIER-CONTEXT-NOT-SELECTOR

**Decision:** Structural washout is a carrier/baseline, not the new selector.

**Rationale:** Prophet already concentrates in washed-out/coiled names and P-B2/P-B3 did not certify static-state timing/occupancy.

### DEC:CNLI-SEQUENCE-OVER-COUNT

**Decision:** Ordered mechanism sequence is the primary conjunction object; unordered confirmation count is a negative control.

**Rationale:** Correlated technicals manufacture confidence. Professional discovery, transition, sector authorization, Prophet actionability, and retail recognition have directional time order.

### DEC:CNLI-ACTOR-NEUTRAL-TAPE-LANGUAGE

**Decision:** Tape features describe downside ineffectiveness, resilience, reclaim quality, and constructive-volume asymmetry; they never label hidden accumulation, insider activity, manipulation, or orchestration.

**Rationale:** Intent is latent and cannot be inferred from OHLCV. Actor claims require independent public evidence.

### DEC:CNLI-OUTCOME-VECTOR

**Decision:** First exact board, continuous rerating, adverse breakdown, access, continuation, and capacity are separate outcomes.

**Rationale:** A model can predict locked boards yet be commercially useless; board probability and achievable expectancy are not interchangeable.

### DEC:CNLI-EXACT-CENT-PRIMARY

**Decision:** Authority-grade first-board outcomes require unadjusted nominal prices, vendor exact legal limits, positive-volume eligibility, and integer-cent equality.

**Rationale:** Adjusted prices, reconstructed ratios, tolerant primary labels, and ties-to-even rounding caused the binding stop-ship.

### DEC:CNLI-ONE-CANONICAL-PROPHET-CHAIN

**Decision:** Use the existing candidate plane, board/path grader, identity, event, and nightly planes; CN-Limit adds only derived anatomy plus a referential immutable research sidecar.

**Rationale:** A duplicate candidate plane or grader would create diverging truth and destroy attribution.

### DEC:CNLI-MEASUREMENT-BEFORE-ORDERING

**Decision:** I1 prospective measurement shadow must precede I2 ordering shadow.

**Rationale:** A feature must survive exact outcomes, Prophet overlap, valid nulls, calibration, access, and prospective proof before an order counterfactual is meaningful.

### DEC:CNLI-COVERAGE-ATOMIC-CHALLENGER

**Decision:** A challenger order exists only when every member of the frozen comparison population has a valid prediction under one definition.

**Rationale:** Per-row incumbent fallback mixed with challenger values creates incomparable scales and selection bias.

### DEC:CNLI-ERA-IS-EFFECTIVE-AUTHORITY

**Decision:** Champion era is keyed by board definition plus effective order basis, order mode, score/admission hashes, and candidate schema—not board_definition alone.

**Rationale:** A v4 bake served through v3 fallback is not the same treatment as a complete Intelligence-ordered v4 bake.

### DEC:CNLI-NO-OUTCOME-AUDITION

**Decision:** One preregistered challenger enters each prospective race; any formula/model/threshold change starts a new era.

**Rationale:** Selecting the best of many observed challengers invalidates the forward comparison.

### DEC:CNLI-AUTHORITY-DOES-NOT-CASCADE

**Decision:** Research, prediction, ordering, score, gate, size, and public probability are separate authority grants.

**Rationale:** Success at one level does not establish safety or value at the next.

### DEC:CNLI-PROPHET-LAB-FENCED-ADJACENCY

**Decision:** The new Prophet Operator Lab is an adjacent US/Radar read-only product, not a CN-Limit owner or storage seam.

**Rationale:** It is fixture-based, not live-wired, and has its own detector ontology. Automatic reuse would blur programs and create a shortcut around canonical China planes.

# Appendix A — Exact contracts for the four primary transition features

### `CNLI.TRANS.DOWN_IMPACT_ASYMMETRY` — Downside price-impact decay relative to upside impact

**Method:** `DETERMINISTIC_CONTINUOUS`  
**Owner:** China raw OHLCV owner; CN-Limit defines research projection only  
**Cutoff:** all bars with session <= decision session d; the target session must begin after d  
**Minimum/coverage rule:** At least five valid negative and five valid positive sessions across each comparison window; otherwise NOT_COVERED.

**Frozen anatomy:**
- `window_logic`: W1=d-19..d; W0=d-39..d-20. Calculate impact separately on negative and positive sessions.
- `down_eff_W`: median(max(-r_t,0) / max(value_rel_t,0.05) | r_t<0)
- `up_eff_W`: median(max(r_t,0) / max(value_rel_t,0.05) | r_t>0)
- `primary_value`: log((down_eff_W0+eps)/(down_eff_W1+eps)) - log((up_eff_W0+eps)/(up_eff_W1+eps))
- `interpretation`: Positive means downside impact fell more than upside/general price impact. It is actor-neutral and must never be labeled hidden accumulation.
- `minimum_support`: At least five valid negative and five valid positive sessions across each comparison window; otherwise NOT_COVERED.

**Negative controls:** CNLI.NC.RAW_VOLUME_SPIKE, CNLI.NC.STATIC_WASHOUT_OCCUPANCY

**Falsifier:** Close if the value adds no discrimination beyond carrier + size/liquidity/volatility + Prophet, if the sign is equally strong on upside impact, or if it survives only after using corrected/future bars unavailable at the decision cutoff.

### `CNLI.TRANS.BAD_DAY_RESILIENCE` — Improving residual behavior on objectively bad market or sector sessions

**Method:** `DETERMINISTIC_CONTINUOUS`  
**Owner:** China raw OHLCV + canonical market/sector context owners  
**Cutoff:** all bars with session <= decision session d; the target session must begin after d  
**Minimum/coverage rule:** See the frozen R3 registry.

**Frozen anatomy:**
- `bad_market_session`: r_market,t <= the trailing 252-session 20th percentile computed using data available before t
- `residual_return`: r_i,t - beta_i,m,t-1 * r_market,t
- `primary_value`: median(residual_return on the six most recent bad-market sessions) - median on the preceding twelve bad-market sessions
- `secondary_sector_channel`: Repeat on bad-sector sessions only when PIT sector membership exists; never substitute current membership silently.
- `interpretation`: Positive means the name has become harder to damage during externally bad conditions.

**Negative controls:** CNLI.NC.STATIC_WASHOUT_OCCUPANCY

**Falsifier:** Close if improvement is merely beta compression or market-wide liquidity recovery, if current sector membership is load-bearing, or if there is no effect on matched bad sessions.

### `CNLI.TRANS.CONSTRUCTIVE_VOL_ASYM` — Constructive versus destructive volume effectiveness

**Method:** `DETERMINISTIC_CONTINUOUS`  
**Owner:** China raw OHLCV owner  
**Cutoff:** all bars with session <= decision session d; the target session must begin after d  
**Minimum/coverage rule:** See the frozen R3 registry.

**Frozen anatomy:**
- `constructive_session`: market_excess_t>0 and clv_t>=0
- `destructive_session`: market_excess_t<0 and clv_t<=0
- `constructive_effect`: median(max(market_excess_t,0) / max(value_rel_t,0.05) on constructive sessions in d-39..d)
- `destructive_effect`: median(max(-market_excess_t,0) / max(value_rel_t,0.05) on destructive sessions in d-39..d)
- `primary_value`: constructive_effect - destructive_effect
- `companion_anatomy`: median(value_rel on constructive) - median(value_rel on destructive)
- `interpretation`: The feature measures which side uses volume effectively; it is not a raw volume spike.

**Negative controls:** CNLI.NC.RAW_VOLUME_SPIKE

**Falsifier:** Close if raw volume spike performs equally well, if the signal is simply positive-return persistence, or if destructive effectiveness does not decline before outcomes.

### `CNLI.TRANS.RECLAIM_HOLD_QUALITY` — Completed reclaim speed, hold quality, adverse excursion, and failure history

**Method:** `DETERMINISTIC_STATE_MACHINE`  
**Owner:** China raw OHLCV owner; incumbent Prophet components remain separate  
**Cutoff:** all bars with session <= decision session d; the target session must begin after d  
**Minimum/coverage rule:** See the frozen R3 registry.

**Frozen anatomy:**
- `setup`: A reclaim episode begins after at least five consecutive closes below the lagged 20-session moving average.
- `reclaim`: First close back above the lagged MA20; the MA value is frozen at the reclaim session for episode evaluation.
- `speed`: Sessions from the episode's lowest close to the reclaim close.
- `hold`: Among the next five completed sessions available by d, count closes at or above the frozen reclaim level.
- `mae`: Minimum low relative to the frozen reclaim level over the completed hold window.
- `states`: {'NO_COMPLETED_RECLAIM': 'no episode completed by d', 'PENDING_RECLAIM': 'reclaim occurred but the five-session hold window has not completed', 'FAST_HELD': 'speed<=3, hold>=4, mae>-0.02', 'SLOW_HELD': 'held criteria met but speed>3', 'FAILED': 'two or more closes below the level or mae<=-0.02', 'MIXED_HISTORY': 'multiple completed episodes with conflicting states'}
- `primary_output`: latest completed state plus component vector; no hidden scalar score.

**Negative controls:** CNLI.NC.STATIC_WASHOUT_OCCUPANCY

**Falsifier:** Close if state value vanishes after the incumbent Prophet entry/signal variables, or if only pending episodes appear predictive through future hold information.
