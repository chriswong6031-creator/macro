## Study Design

**Stratum definition:** A fire is IN the k-day blackout window iff the NEXT
Item-2.02 8-K filing_date for that ticker falls within k trading days strictly
after (or on) the fire date. A fire on the announcement date itself (k=0) is
included in every k stratum. Trading-day arithmetic uses the actual NYSE/NASDAQ
calendar derived from the deep-panel price files (no calendar-day approximation).

**Coverage exclusion:** Fires whose ticker is absent from the 8-K store are
excluded from BOTH the treatment and control arms. They are counted and printed
here — never silently treated as non-blackout.

**Primary endpoint:** k=3 POOLED (deep + baskets), stop5 and mae63.
**Sensitivity:** k=1 and k=2; separate deep/baskets panels.
**Era analysis:** Runs on the POOLED deep+baskets fire set per masterplan §3 F1.

**Hygiene bar (masterplan §5):** CI-excluding-0 degradation on stop5 OR mae63
(pooled FE, k=3 primary). Vetoed volume ≤10% of fires. RUL-7 caveat: bare 2pp
point-estimate rarely clears CI-excluding-0 at minimum n — the CI clause is operative.
