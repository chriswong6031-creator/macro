## The red-team's central ruling (adopted verbatim as design law)

The absorption thesis (D4-01/02/04/07) is, at mechanism level, **PEAD conditioned on the
sign of the initial reaction** (Chan/Jegadeesh/Lakonishok 1996) fused with cross-sectional
momentum: "price holds through 10 sessions of supply" is a positive-early-momentum
selection filter, and under any momentum DGP the [t,t+10] winners are mechanically more
likely to be [t+10,t+31] winners. The window split passes a naive PIT check while still
manufacturing a result. A "momentum-matched baseline" is insufficient. **The only
convincing neutralization: controls matched on the SAME [t,t+10] return path + realized
vol + size, plus residualization on an announcement-return-conditioned drift factor.**
This is now the flagship's decisive gate, not an afterthought.
