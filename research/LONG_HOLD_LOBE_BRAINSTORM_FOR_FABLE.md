## Executive ruling

The highest-value next move is **not another compounder selector**. The local evidence has already said that broad fundamental quality at entry is close to zero on the available ruler, and the ratifying long-horizon sample is not mature. The most useful build is therefore a **Falsifier Contract and Evidence Packet**: for any public ticker or Winner Autopsy episode, show what would break each applicable hold archetype, when that evidence was last observable, whether it is fresh, and which independent facts have actually deteriorated. It must never say “hold,” “trim,” or “exit” by itself.

The single surface most likely to change a real decision on a name up 50–150% is a **Fundamental Delivery Waterfall** beside that packet: how much of the run is explained by revenue-per-share, margin, and cash-flow delivery, and how much is multiple expansion. This is an accounting decomposition, not a causal story and not a valuation sell rule.

The hypothesis budget should remain unspent now. Two ideas are worthy of a future reservation, in order:

1. a **persistent, peer-relative thesis-break event** tested at one 126-day ruler among already-established breakaway episodes; and
2. a **pre-onset material-event hardening ladder** tested against the full Winner Autopsy census.

Neither is registered here. Each must first clear a free coverage census and the existing WA-R8/A2 firewall. Sample engineering itself deserves **zero** of the remaining 11 slots.

One current-state amendment changes the shape of this brainstorm: since the snapshot described in the prompt, `origin/main` has shipped `research/LEADER_RADAR_MASTERPLAN_BY_FABLE.md` and `research/LEADER_LIFECYCLE_FIELD_GUIDE.md`. Leader Radar already owns pre-breakaway lifecycle states, RS turns, revision-positive context, rerating-watch conditions, index/crowding chips, and two Pick Lab forward books. Therefore this paper proposes **no second anticipation scanner, no second state machine, and no new lobe**. Winner-anticipation ideas below are additive PIT columns or joins into Winner Autopsy/Leader Radar.

## A. Duration extension

### A1. Falsifier Contract + evidence freshness packet

**Mechanism.** A discretionary holder usually makes the worst mistake when absence of bad news is mistaken for positive confirmation. Give each applicable archetype a small contract: the specific claim, its observable, the last public observation, expected cadence, evidence age, current raw value, prior value, peer context, and status `not_observed | no_break_observed | challenged | broken | unverifiable`. This is a presentation and provenance layer over existing sensors, not an active-thesis ledger. It can run for any ticker or public Winner Autopsy episode; it must not know which names the operator owns.

The compact decision packet should answer five questions at a glance:

```text
1. Has the tactical entry clock expired?
2. What was the latest fundamental confirmation date?
3. Which predeclared falsifiers fired, and on what filing?
4. Which required observables are overdue or unavailable?
5. What is the next scheduled or event-driven evidence window?
```

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Reuse `engine/long_hold_clocks.py`, `engine/moat_falsifiers.py`, `engine/capital_allocation.py`, `engine/thesis_funnel.py`, filing acceptance timestamps, Form 4 dates, and existing price stores. SEC submissions and standardized XBRL facts are free and update from public filings without authentication through the [SEC EDGAR APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces). A filing-text LLM may extract a cited fact or mark a contract unverifiable; it cannot mark a thesis intact, originate a break, or escalate action.

**Tier claim.** Context/display. Zero hypothesis slots. No stored `active_thesis`, no portfolio list, and no new authority state.

**Falsifier.** At the name level, stale or missing evidence must produce `unverifiable`, never “intact.” At the program level, kill the packet if blinded operator reviews show that it does not alter what evidence is checked, reduce forgotten falsifiers, or shorten review time. Preserve the raw underlying sensors even if the packet format fails.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** on discipline value, **not applicable** as alpha. This faithfully translates the repo’s own LH-R6 doctrine and the practitioner emphasis on revisiting business, management, and reinvestment rather than reacting to price. Akre’s formal process keeps those three legs separate ([Akre investment process](https://www.akrefund.com/investment-process/)); Fundsmith’s manual emphasizes sustained cash returns on operating capital and long holding periods ([Fundsmith Owner’s Manual](https://www.fundsmith.co.uk/media/mv3abv1h/owner-s-manual.pdf)).

### A2. Quarterly peer-relative double-confirmation

**Mechanism.** The shipped moat sensors are annual and can fire during normal cyclicality. For quality and secular-growth archetypes, compute the same raw conditions at filed-quarter cadence where coverage permits, but challenge the thesis only when the **same named observable** deteriorates in two consecutive public filings **and** the same-sector/archetype peer median does not show the same deterioration. That separates company-specific breakage from a common input-cost or recession shock. No sensor votes with another sensor and no score is created.

Examples:

```text
pricing_power_break = margin compression despite revenue growth in Q(t) AND Q(t-1)
                      AND peer median gross-margin change is not comparably negative

collection_break    = receivables growth > revenue growth + locked spread in Q(t) AND Q(t-1)
                      AND peer median spread remains below the threshold
```

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Quarterly EDGAR/XBRL revenue, gross profit, receivables, inventory, CFO, capex, operating income, diluted shares, exact filing acceptance, and PIT sector membership. Require adjacent fiscal periods, acquisition/recast flags, minimum peer coverage, and explicit issuer-extension provenance. The SEC warns that official filings remain the source of record, so anomalous XBRL values should link back to the filing rather than silently drive a break.

**Tier claim.** Context/display. The repeated-filing gate is a deterministic de-escalation contract, not a statistical claim. A future outcome test is the first hypothesis-budget candidate, but it is not registered here.

**Falsifier.** A fire is resolved if the series returns to its pre-flag trajectory in the next two filings without restatement or if the peer adjustment reveals a sector-wide shock. Retire the persistent-break interpretation if most fires resolve this way. Do not tune the spread until fires look persuasive.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **Medium-high** as falsifier design, **low** as untested return prediction. Gross profitability and cash-based operating profitability have substantial external cross-sectional evidence ([Novy-Marx, 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2013.01.003); [Ball et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2587199)), but the repo’s own quality-at-entry evidence is near-null. That conflict is precisely why this remains a break monitor rather than a positive factor.

### A3. Fundamental Delivery Waterfall

**Mechanism.** After a 50–150% run, the most important context is whether the business has delivered enough to support the price move. Use multiplicative identities, not attribution theater. For profitable firms:

```text
share price ≈ revenue per diluted share × operating margin × after-tax/other bridge × P/E
```

Report log changes from the mechanical breakaway anchor or a user-selected public date as separate bars: revenue-per-share delivery, margin delivery, cash-conversion bridge, and residual multiple change. For loss-making firms, use EV/revenue and show net debt/share issuance separately. Label the residual “valuation/mix/accounting residual,” not “sentiment.” This operationalizes the old contradiction concept without reviving a fused `multiple_expansion_only` admission verdict.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Existing price stores plus filed revenue, operating income, CFO, debt, cash, and diluted shares. Every endpoint uses only filings public by the chosen `as_of`; restatements get a visible amendment marker. Negative denominators, large acquisitions, spin-offs, and accounting-standard changes force the affected bridge to `unavailable`.

**Tier claim.** Context/display. Zero slots. It explains what happened; it does not forecast or say valuation is too high.

**Falsifier.** The identity is unusable when denominator sign changes, merger accounting dominates, or the residual is too large to interpret. If a case cannot reconcile within a predeclared tolerance, show the raw components and refuse the waterfall. At the program level, reject it if operators repeatedly read the residual as a causal market-sentiment estimate despite the warning.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** decision value and accounting feasibility for covered profitable names; **medium** coverage across pre-revenue and acquisitive names. Fundsmith emphasizes cash returns on capital rather than EPS alone, while Akre stresses free-cash-flow-per-share compounding; the waterfall translates those practices into a receipt rather than a sell rule. It also directly answers the repo’s prior “price up, fundamentals did not improve” thesis-contradiction concern.

### A4. Reinvestment-runway decay map

**Mechanism.** A quality company stops being a compounding machine when it can no longer deploy incremental capital at attractive returns. Keep four separate series: incremental operating profit per incremental invested dollar, incremental gross profit per R&D+capex dollar, FCF per share, and net share issuance. A challenge requires a predeclared two-period deterioration in one economic return measure plus a separate evidence-of-capital-use observation; it may never be summarized as “reinvestment score.”

For example:

```text
reinvestment_break = incremental NOPAT / incremental invested capital <= 0
                     for two filed annual periods
                     AND capex + capitalized R&D continues to rise
```

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** EDGAR revenue, operating income, tax proxy, debt, cash, PPE, capex, R&D, SBC, CFO, acquisitions where tagged, and shares. Annual cadence is safer than quarter-to-quarter because working capital and acquisitions make incremental-return ratios unstable. Stamp acquisition contamination and refuse comparisons across large scope changes.

**Tier claim.** Context/display. Zero slots. It extends the shipped compounder columns; it does not recreate a quality ranker.

**Falsifier.** A supposed break is killed when the next two periods show the capital was a deliberate build whose profits arrive with the disclosed lag. If acquisition accounting or inconsistent R&D tagging makes the denominator non-comparable, the result is unavailable. Retire universal thresholds if sector-specific capital intensity dominates the signal.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** practitioner relevance, **medium-low** measurement precision. Akre’s third leg is disciplined reinvestment with a long runway ([Akre](https://www.akrefund.com/investment-process/)); Fundsmith likewise seeks firms able to replicate superior returns on newly invested capital ([Owner’s Manual](https://www.fundsmith.co.uk/media/mv3abv1h/owner-s-manual.pdf)). Those are mechanism priors, not proof that this ratio predicts returns.

### A5. Contracted-demand conversion clock

**Mechanism.** For software, aerospace/defense, infrastructure, and other contracted-revenue archetypes, revenue growth can stay healthy while the forward book quietly depletes. Display RPO/backlog replenishment, near-term conversion, contract liabilities, contract assets, and receivables as separate series. The useful break is not “RPO down.” It is **two filings of weak replenishment plus deteriorating conversion or collection quality** while headline revenue still looks fine. This is an archetype-scoped contract, not the forbidden universe KPI registry.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Standard XBRL facts such as remaining performance obligations, contract assets/liabilities, and revenue recognized from opening contract liabilities, plus the filing’s revenue-recognition block when standard tags are absent. Company extensions and practical-expedient omissions are common; coverage must identify them. LLMs may extract a stated amount, horizon, and exact citation, then the deterministic engine computes deltas. Cadence is each 10-Q/10-K, with one- to two-quarter plausible lead time. The [SEC XBRL APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) include standard facts but deliberately exclude issuer-specific custom concepts from aggregated comparability, so the system must not pretend full cross-company coverage.

**Tier claim.** Context/display. Zero slots.

**Falsifier.** At the name level, a lone RPO decline is not a break because rapid delivery can lower backlog. Require the separate conversion/collection deterioration. Kill the lane if applicable-name coverage is too sparse, issuer tags cannot be reconciled over time, or forward reviews show no added information beyond revenue and receivables.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **Medium-high** for contracted archetypes, **low** as universal alpha. Deferred-revenue changes have been associated with later operating performance in accounting research ([Prakash and Sinha](https://digitalcommons.cwu.edu/cobfac_accounting/12/)), but that is nearest evidence only; it does not validate a long-hold signal.

### A6. Archetype-routed SEC hard-stop bus

**Mechanism.** Some thesis breaks are not slow ratios; they are legally defined public events. Route existing EDGAR item codes only to archetypes they can actually injure:

| Public event | Applicable thesis | Display response |
|---|---|---|
| Item 1.02 material-agreement termination | Contract/partnership thesis | Named-contract review |
| Item 2.04 acceleration/default | Leveraged turnaround | Solvency review |
| Item 4.02 non-reliance or newly disclosed material weakness | Accounting-dependent quality thesis | Evidence challenged |
| Item 5.02 key CEO/CFO/founder departure | Owner-operator thesis | Succession review |
| Item 3.01 listing failure | Capital-access/pre-revenue thesis | Financing review |
| Item 1.03 bankruptcy/receivership | Any common-equity thesis | Verified terminal-risk event |

These events open a review packet; they do not order a sale. The same 8-K cannot break an unrelated thesis.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** EDGAR submissions already carry 8-K item codes, filing acceptance, and primary documents. The [official Form 8-K instructions](https://www.sec.gov/files/form8-k.pdf) define the events and their disclosure requirements. Periodic internal-controls disclosures provide remediation state. An LLM may extract which agreement or officer is affected, with a citation; only item code, filing date, and predeclared archetype route can change the display state.

**Tier claim.** Context/display infrastructure. Zero slots. Consume the existing Special Situations taxonomy rather than rebuild SLF-034.

**Falsifier.** A routed event is false when it is immaterial to the registered archetype, promptly cured, or merely administrative. Kill any route whose forward audit is dominated by such cases. At the name level, only a hard stop explicitly named in the contract may produce `broken`; all other events produce `review`.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** observability, **medium** decision value. Material weaknesses are associated with poorer accrual quality and higher risk in the accounting literature ([Doyle, Ge, and McVay](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=959295); [Ashbaugh-Skaife et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1475-679X.2008.00315.x)), supporting de-escalation rather than an automatic exit.

### A7. Owner-operator succession and exposure fracture

**Mechanism.** An owner-operator thesis is partly a bet on the allocator. CEO departure alone is not bearish, and insider selling alone is noisy. The stronger contract is a leadership discontinuity followed by per-share or balance-sheet deterioration, with insider exposure depletion only as confirmation. Separate scheduled Rule 10b5-1 sales, gifts, tax withholding, and option exercises from unscheduled open-market dispositions.

Possible independent gates:

```text
succession_stress = key operator departure within 365d
                    AND FCF/share declines in two successive public periods

allocator_stress  = capital_allocation_delta == dilutive
                    AND net_debt/EBITDA worsens by a locked amount
                    AND FCF/share declines

alignment_confirm = non-10b5-1 open-market sales deplete >= locked share of direct holdings
                    AND succession_stress
```

Insider activity never fires alone and is used only negatively; this does not revive 13F or a positive insider signal.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Items 5.02 and 2.03, Form 4 XML transaction codes and post-transaction holdings, DEF 14A beneficial ownership, and existing capital-allocation/SUE fields. SEC amendments require Forms 4/5 filed from April 1, 2023 to identify transactions intended to rely on Rule 10b5-1 and disclose the plan adoption date ([SEC rule summary](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2022-222)); earlier planned-versus-discretionary status must be `unknown`. LLM extraction may reconcile officer names but cannot originate stress.

**Tier claim.** Context/display. Zero slots.

**Falsifier.** Planned succession followed by stable or improving per-share economics must not fire. Missing post-change quarters mean `awaiting evidence`. Retire the alignment leg if plan tags, holdings, or derivative conversions cannot be reconciled reliably.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** practitioner relevance, **medium** machine coverage. Akre explicitly makes management one of three independent legs. Giverny’s 2025 sell postmortems tie long-held exits to leadership change plus deteriorating economics and debt, not leadership change alone ([Giverny 2025 letter](https://givernycapital.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/giverny-capital-annual-letter-2025-1.pdf)).

### A8. Capital-cycle rollover contract

**Mechanism.** Cyclical winners often look cheapest and report their best earnings near the top. Their hold thesis breaks when high industry returns attract supply while the company’s incremental returns begin to roll over. Scope the contract only to `cyclical` and `commodity_sensitive`; it is not a revival of the generic investment/CMA factor.

Keep the legs independent:

```text
cohort_investment_boom = peer median capex/revenue > own trailing 5y p80
                         AND >= 3pp above two years earlier

company_capacity_build = company capex/revenue rises twice AND net PPE rises

return_rollover        = operating margin falls AND ROIC proxy falls

late_cycle_damage      = all three named conditions, printed line by line
```

The existing inventory-build sensor sits beside the contract as confirmation, not another vote in a score.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** EDGAR capex, revenue, PPE, operating income, ROIC proxy, acquisition flags, PIT subindustry cohorts, and existing price context. Annual cadence is adequate. Require at least 15 covered peers and print acquisition/accounting contamination.

**Tier claim.** Context/display. Zero slots initially.

**Falsifier.** If a cohort investment boom persists for eight reported quarters without cohort margin/ROIC deterioration, the late-cycle interpretation is wrong for that industry. Large acquisitions or reclassifications make the company leg unavailable.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** mechanism confidence, **medium** generic EDGAR feasibility. Marathon’s institutional process explicitly links high returns to incoming capital/competition and treats management’s project, acquisition, issuance, and buyback decisions as decisive ([Marathon capital-cycle philosophy](https://www.marathon.co.uk/the-home-of-capital-cycle-investing/)).

### A9. Milestone integrity + financing runway

**Mechanism.** Clinical, regulatory, pre-commercial, and partnership winners do not have a normal margin/ROIC falsifier. Their thesis is a dated sequence of public milestones plus enough cash to reach them without destroying per-share value. The packet should list only company-disclosed milestones, their original dates/ranges, subsequent delay/amendment, cash and quarterly burn, debt maturities, filed issuance capacity, and actual dilution. A disclosed primary-endpoint failure, clinical hold, partner termination, or inability to finance the next milestone is a hard review event; a volatile share price is not.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** SEC 8-K/6-K, 10-Q/10-K/20-F filing text, Items 1.01/1.02, cash/CFO/R&D/debt/shares from XBRL, S-3/424B5/ATM capacity, and actual issuance. This stays inside the permitted free sources; it does not assume paid trial databases, transcripts, or consensus. LLMs may extract a company-stated milestone and citation, but only a deterministic date change, filed outcome, agreement termination, or runway calculation may de-escalate.

**Tier claim.** Context/display. Zero slots. Archetype-specific, not a universal KPI registry.

**Falsifier.** If milestone language is too vague to bind to a date/range, mark it unverifiable. A financing that extends runway without material per-share damage is not a break. Kill the lane if extracted milestones cannot be reproduced from cited filings or if amendments are routinely missed.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** practical relevance for the Winner Autopsy clinical/partnership sub-species; **medium-low** automation coverage. Baillie Gifford’s long-term philosophy explicitly accepts asymmetric uncertainty but says long-termism is not an excuse for ignoring execution detail ([Baillie Gifford](https://www.bailliegifford.com/en/uk/individual-investors/about-us/actual-investors/actual-investors-think-in-decades-not-quarters/)). That is a practitioner prior, not a return study.

## B. Winner anticipation — additive columns, not another radar

### B1. Material-event hardening ladder

**Mechanism.** Winner Autopsy already counts 8-K density and Special Situations already owns taxonomy. The missing episode column is **topology**: did the pre-onset story progress from soft narrative disclosure to legally or financially hard evidence? Keep separate fields such as:

```text
hard_event_count_126d   # Items 1.01, 2.01, 2.02 before t0
soft_event_count_126d   # Items 7.01, 8.01 before t0
soft_then_hard          # a transparent sequence flag
days_soft_to_hard
hard_event_reversed     # subsequent Item 1.02, descriptive outcome
```

The mechanism is that durable reratings may be more likely when narrative becomes a contract, completed transaction, or financial confirmation before the price break, whereas blow-offs may remain soft-event-heavy. This is not yet evidence.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Reuse the existing `material_8k_events`, earnings 8-K dates, and Special Situations item taxonomy. Every event must have `accepted_at < t0`; the mechanical Detector-D onset remains frozen. LLMs may annotate what the event concerns, but item codes and sequence fields are deterministic. [Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/files/form8-k.pdf) provides the public item definitions.

**Tier claim.** Context/descriptive first. **Genuinely worth one of the 11 slots later**, but only if a free census shows high PIT coverage, at least 25 independent issuer/onset-month clusters in both outcome sides, and enough hard events before—not after—`t0`. The eventual test must freeze one sequence and one 126-day ruler under WA-R8; no family of variants.

**Falsifier.** Do not spend the slot if most hard events occur after onset, if coverage is era/size-biased, or if durable winners and blow-offs show indistinguishable pre-onset topology. A failed study kills predictive framing but not the case chronology.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **Medium** mechanism confidence, **unproven** return confidence. Its case rests on orthogonality, exact public dates, and the existing cross-case “multi-rung ladder” observation—not on a claimed academic anomaly.

### B2. Self-funding crossover

**Mechanism.** Generic quality levels have been locally null. A more specific pre-breakaway context is the moment an operating-leverage company stops depending on friendly capital markets: TTM operating cash flow turns positive, covers capex, revenue is still growing, diluted shares are not expanding materially, and the improvement is not simply receivables/inventory/payables timing. Store all legs separately; `self_funded_transition` may be a transparent AND flag.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Filed quarterly CFO, capex, revenue, accounts receivable, inventory, payables/contract liabilities, cash, debt, and diluted shares. Use true acceptance dates and require a subsequent filing for confirmation. Apply only where CFO is economically meaningful; clinical/pre-revenue names are not “failed,” they are out of scope.

**Tier claim.** Context/display. Zero slots because broad fundamental quality already has a high local burden of proof.

**Falsifier.** The transition fails if the next filing reverses, if temporary working-capital extraction explains the cash turn, or if the firm immediately issues equity to fund normal operations. Retire anticipation language if it is equally common before blow-offs.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **Medium** context confidence, **low-to-medium** anticipation confidence. Cash-based operating profitability has stronger external evidence than accrual-inclusive measures ([Ball et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2587199)), but applying a crossover to breakaway anticipation is an explicit new inference.

### B3. Filing-native share-supply state

**Mechanism.** Extend—not relaunch—the existing dilution, capital-allocation, SLF-029/030, and IPO-lockup work into a Winner Autopsy context join. Keep legal capacity and actual supply separate:

- active S-3/424B5/ATM capacity as a percentage of current float;
- S-8 shares registered;
- Item 3.02 actual issuance;
- convertible if-converted shares where terms are explicit;
- actual quarterly diluted-share change;
- repurchases executed and net of SBC;
- IPO/secondary lockup dates from the existing event-priors path.

A terminated/exhausted ATM plus halted dilution is useful context. Shelf capacity alone is optionality, never bearish supply. A new financing after a 100% run is duration risk only when it becomes actual or clearly live.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Forms S-3, S-8, 424B5, 8-K Items 2.03/3.02, Item 703 repurchase tables, and filed shares/SBC. An LLM may extract a prospectus amount or conversion term with provenance; deterministic logic maintains `active | used | terminated | unknown`. Refuse “remaining capacity” when amendments do not reconcile. SEC form instructions are public ([S-3](https://www.sec.gov/files/forms-3.pdf); [S-8](https://www.sec.gov/files/forms-8.pdf)).

**Tier claim.** Context/display. Zero slots. This is a consumption/integration idea, not a new net-issuance signal.

**Falsifier.** Capacity without issuance cannot break a thesis. Abandon derived remaining-capacity fields if they fail to reconcile to later filed shares. Kill the context join if it merely duplicates `capital_allocation_delta` without improving supply visibility.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** conceptual value, **medium** parsing feasibility. Net share issuance has extensive external evidence ([Pontiff and Woodgate](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2008.01335.x)), but the proposal deliberately makes no claim that every shelf, S-8, or announced buyback predicts returns.

### B4. Index eligibility and actual-announcement amplifier

**Mechanism.** The case library already shows index inclusion as an amplifier, not the origin of the business rerating. Add two separate contexts:

1. published eligibility conditions—float, liquidity, listing age, GAAP profitability, and size—displayed only as “eligible for consideration”; and
2. actual public announcement/effective dates, with passive-demand capacity relative to normal dollar volume and onset timing.

Do not calculate an inclusion probability. Committee discretion, sector balance, vintage methodology changes, and the declining modern index effect make that dishonest.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Existing PIT S&P membership, price/fundamental stores, archived public index announcements, and the current [S&P U.S. Indices methodology](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/methodologies/methodology-sp-us-indices.pdf). Historical eligibility requires the methodology vintage in force at the time; without it, the eligibility card is forward-only. Actual-announcement fields are PIT-clean.

**Tier claim.** Context/display. Zero slots. Consume the existing index-reconstitution study and Leader Radar chips rather than build another flow engine.

**Falsifier.** Frequent eligible-but-not-selected cases kill prediction language, not the card. If actual additions do not change volume/excess-return behavior in the census, retain only provenance. Eligibility must never be treated as a positive thesis.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** context confidence, **low** alpha confidence. S&P’s own research reports that the classic index-addition effect has declined over time ([S&P, “What Happened to the Index Effect?”](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/research/article/what-happened-to-the-index-effect-a-look-at-three-decades-of-sp-500-adds-and-drops/)).

## C. Growing honest N without laundering dependence

There is no lawful statistical shortcut from four honest G1 compounder clusters to 25. For G1, new N means new 2025+ frozen-population `missed_hold` episode-clusters with a matured 252-day outcome. The right architecture is two ledgers: leave G1 untouched, and build explicitly different context cohorts that answer useful questions sooner.

### C1. Prospective all-listed Detector-D satellite

**Mechanism.** Run the already-frozen Winner Autopsy Detector D prospectively over every liquid, exchange-listed, SEC-reporting common equity resolved in the existing full-market price store, not only the PIT S&P 1500. This creates genuinely new issuer-onsets without changing thresholds. Keep nested strata separate: S&P 1500, non-S&P U.S. common stocks, and U.S.-listed foreign private issuers/ADRs. It grows the breakaway census, never G1.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Archive public Nasdaq/other-exchange symbol directories from the first prospective date, map securities to CIK through EDGAR submissions, collapse share classes/ADRs to one issuer, and retain tombstones after delisting. Exclude ETFs, ETNs, funds, preferreds, warrants, units, and pre-deal SPACs using metadata known at `t0`. Do not reconstruct a historical universe from today’s file. Sources are free and public: [Nasdaq symbol-directory definitions](https://nasdaqtrader.com/Trader.aspx?id=SymbolDirDefs), [SEC submissions/XBRL APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces), and existing prices.

**Tier claim.** Infrastructure plus context/display. Zero slots.

**Falsifier.** Kill this as an N-expansion lane if a blinded roster audit maps less than a predeclared 95% of eligible common stocks, terminal outcomes remain disproportionately absent among failures, or incremental episodes concentrate in a handful of issuers/rally months. Keep FPIs separate because 20-F is annual, can use IFRS, and has different lag.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** that it raises honest breakaway-event N; **medium** transportability to the operator’s S&P-heavy population; **zero** that it accelerates G1. The statistical unit is `(CIK, Detector-D onset)` after the frozen cooldown, with distinct issuer, episode, and onset-month counts printed.

### C2. Recurrent-event calendar risk sets

**Mechanism.** Winner names can break away repeatedly. Use later Detector-D onsets only after the frozen 63-trading-day cooldown, treating each issuer as an at-risk process that exits and later re-enters the risk set. This is legitimate within-name reuse; treating every day or every threshold crossing as a new episode is not. It answers the frequency and conditional durability of breakaway onsets, not multi-year compounder selection.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Existing price, volume, benchmark, Detector-D, and event artifacts suffice. Features change only when public and remain behind the A2 firewall. Delisting, bankruptcy, and acquisition are competing events, not benign censoring.

**Tier claim.** Descriptive/context method. Zero slots by itself. A later test of one named feature still costs one slot through WA-R8.

**Falsifier.** The information gain is false if recurrent episodes are concentrated in a few issuers, a one-onset-per-CIK-per-252-day sensitivity collapses the result, or apparent separation disappears under calendar control. Require at least 25 distinct onset-month blocks before inferential language.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** methodological legitimacy, **medium** incremental information, **low-to-medium** relevance to long-hold worthiness. Use calendar time as the clock, issuer and onset-month dependence, and the project’s within-month episode-label permutation as the primary null—not a ticker bootstrap and not the small-block pivot bootstrap. Panel residuals can be correlated across firms and time ([Petersen](https://www.nber.org/papers/w11280)); multiway clustering addresses non-nested dependence ([Cameron, Gelbach, and Miller](https://www.nber.org/papers/t0327)). Recurrent-event modeling has a formal counting-process foundation ([Andersen and Gill](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176345976)).

### C3. Price-free EDGAR operating follow-through cohorts

**Mechanism.** The price hole does not block a different question: after a public filing landmark, did the business continue improving? Build historical reference cohorts from original filings and report future operating primitives separately—revenue change, operating-margin change, diluted-share change, and net-debt change. Do not fuse them into a compounder label or use them as a substitute for returns.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Read original accession documents with SEC acceptance timestamps. Reconstruct listed-equity status from each historical 10-K/20-F cover page rather than today’s ticker roster. At `t0`, retain only facts public in that accession; do not overwrite them with later-restated Companyfacts values. Outcome periods must begin after `t0`. Failure to file, Form 25 removal, acquisition, or bankruptcy is an explicit outcome state. FPIs remain annual-only.

**Tier claim.** Context/display. Zero slots. A feature becomes a hypothesis only if it is formally claimed to predict one of the separate operating primitives.

**Falsifier.** Abandon inference if original-accession extraction is sparse, nonfiling cannot be classified, or issuer/calendar controls remove the apparent persistence. Retain raw base rates if the predictive interpretation dies.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** as a business-falsification surface, **low** as standalone alpha. Sloan shows cash-flow and accrual components have different future-earnings persistence ([Sloan](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2598)); Chan, Karceski, and Lakonishok find little reliable persistence in exceptional long-term growth ([paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/1540-6261.00540)). The cautious literature supports measuring outcomes without promising selection power.

### C4. Corporate-action resolution graph

**Mechanism.** Some missing outcomes can be converted into verified competing outcomes by following the issuer through cash acquisition, stock consideration, ticker change, spin-off, bankruptcy, or deregistration. Extend the existing bankruptcy/acquisition classifier and Special Situations term parser into a CIK-rooted successor graph. This does not fill the permanent daily-price gap; it reduces informative attrition and stops acquisition exits from masquerading as organic compounders.

**Data source and PIT feasibility.** Form 25, 8-K Items 1.03/2.01/5.01, S-4/F-4, merger proxies, Form 15, and exchange corporate-action records. Future transaction terms are outcome labels only. LLM extraction can propose ambiguous consideration/successor mappings for deterministic or human review; it cannot originate a signal. [SEC Form 25](https://www.sec.gov/files/form25.pdf) and [Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/files/form8-k.pdf) provide the core public events.

**Tier claim.** Infrastructure/context. Zero slots.

**Falsifier.** Kill the N-expansion claim if less than roughly 10% of unresolved episodes become verifiably classified or a blinded manual audit cannot reach 98% correct issuer/consideration mapping. Never assign −100% to an unknown delisting; use verified recovery or bounds. The graph may survive as a missingness audit even if evaluable N barely moves.

**Confidence and nearest evidence.** **High** for bias reduction, **medium** for increasing evaluable episodes, **near zero** for repairing the 2021-10→2025-01 path hole. Missing delisting returns can materially bias results ([Shumway](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1997.tb03818.x); [Beaver, McNichols, and Price](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=958763)).

### What explicitly does not count as honest N

- The 113,542 raw fires; inference uses independent episode-clusters.
- Every day within an episode, every threshold variant, or every horizon on the same episode.
- Multiple share classes or an ADR and local share for one issuer.
- More matched controls; they improve counterfactual precision, not treated-event count.
- Bootstrap draws, permutations, posterior simulations, synthetic controls, or hierarchical shrinkage.
- Recurrent episodes without issuer and calendar-time controls.
- Re-labeling a 126-day breakaway as a 252-day compounder.
- Treating an acquisition premium as organic business compounding.
- Filling unknown delistings with last price, zero, or an average imputation.
- Substituting latest-restated XBRL values into historical feature snapshots.
- Backcasting today’s survivor roster.
- Pooling another country without PIT security membership, corporate actions, adjusted prices, and dead-name terminal coverage.
- Changing cooldowns or outcomes because the original event count was too low.
- LLM-selected winner cases; they are selected on the dependent variable.

Other local markets should therefore wait. Free live quotes or present-day constituents in Canada, Hong Kong, or Europe would reproduce the same survivorship error under a new flag. The only near-term cross-border extension with a common disclosure spine is the separate FPI/ADR stratum inside C1, and even that is transportability context rather than pooled evidence.

## D. Field guide — how real practitioners turn evidence into patience, review, or exit

### The main institutional lesson

The best practitioners do not share one sell rule because they do not own the same species of investment. Akre’s three-legged stool asks whether the business, people, and reinvestment runway remain exceptional. Fundsmith’s “do nothing” discipline resists selling intact compounders. Baillie Gifford accepts many failures because a few asymmetric winners dominate. Oakmark sells when price reaches a revised intrinsic-value target. Marathon watches the supply response to high returns. O’Neil manages a price campaign and protects gains when late-run demand exhausts.

Neural Web should preserve that disagreement as explicit context. A high valuation, a broken business, and damaged price support are different facts with different clocks.

### Checkable archetype cards

The words below are operator playbook prompts, not stored authority states. “No registered break observed” is deliberately used instead of “hold.”

| Archetype | Patience / no-break display | Trim-review context | Exit-review context | Cadence and plausible lead time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Quality compounder** | Evidence fresh; no persistent A2 break; reinvestment and FCF/share not deteriorating | Valuation extreme on own and peer history **plus** two-filings fundamental-pace break; valuation alone means “no-add,” not “sell” | Two independently named legs break: business + reinvestment, or business + allocator | Quarterly/annual; usually one to two filings |
| **Owner-operator / allocator** | Operator present; capital allocation non-dilutive; FCF/share stable | Planned succession plus one deteriorating public period; wait for confirmation | A7 succession or allocator stress confirmed; unscheduled exposure depletion can confirm but never originate | Event-driven 8-K/Form 4, then one to two filings |
| **Turnaround / distressed rerating** | Net debt, interest coverage, cash burn, and self-funding transition continue to improve | Improvement stalls for two public periods while valuation has already rerated | Item 2.04/default, self-funding reversal plus renewed dilution, or two-period solvency deterioration | Event-driven + quarterly; days for hard stop, one to two quarters for operating break |
| **Contracted/platform growth** | RPO/backlog replenishes, conversion holds, receivables do not outrun revenue | Two-filings replenishment decline plus conversion/collection stress | Named material agreement terminates or demand conversion breaks twice with company-specific peer divergence | Quarterly; one to two quarters; immediate for Item 1.02 |
| **Clinical/milestone / pre-revenue** | Company-stated milestones unchanged; disclosed cash runway reaches the next milestone without destructive issuance | Milestone delay plus shortening runway or newly live financing capacity | Filed endpoint/regulatory failure, clinical hold, partner termination, or financing failure that invalidates per-share case | Event-driven; days for outcome, quarterly for runway |
| **Cyclical / commodity-sensitive** | Industry investment restrained; company incremental returns and inventory remain disciplined | Cohort investment boom plus company capacity build | A8 late-cycle damage: supply build and company margin/ROIC rollover | Annual/quarterly; typically two to eight quarters |
| **Position campaign after breakaway** | Existing Exit/Trim system shows campaign support intact; Long-Hold merely displays business evidence beside it | A late run of roughly 25%+ in one to three weeks after an extended advance plus an O’Neil-style climax marker | Heavy-volume 10-week/50-day support loss, deep break, or prolonged failure—owned by Exit/Trim, not Long-Hold | Daily/weekly; days to weeks |

### Practitioner evidence behind the cards

- **Akre:** the formal process evaluates business, management, and reinvestment separately and seeks “compounding machines” ([Akre](https://www.akrefund.com/investment-process/)).
- **Fundsmith:** its Owner’s Manual seeks sustainably high cash returns on operating capital and long holding periods; its stated sanction for bad or illogical management is not to own the shares ([Owner’s Manual](https://www.fundsmith.co.uk/media/mv3abv1h/owner-s-manual.pdf)). Terry Smith has also documented the cost of selling good companies too early ([five-year review](https://www.fundsmith.co.uk/news/2015/2072-financial-times-terry-smith-what-i-have-learnt-at-fundsmith-in-the-past-five-years/)).
- **Berkshire:** Buffett’s published policy was to hold indefinitely while underlying economics and management remained satisfactory and the market had not materially overvalued the business ([1987 letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1987.html)).
- **Baillie Gifford:** long-term asymmetric winners can overwhelm mistakes, but patient ownership still requires detailed attention to execution ([investment philosophy](https://www.bailliegifford.com/en/uk/individual-investors/about-us/philosophy-and-values/)).
- **Oakmark:** analysts set and continuously revise buy/sell targets around intrinsic value, commonly selling near 90–100% of estimated value ([Oakmark process](https://oakmark.com/who-we-are/)). This belongs on the expectation-burden axis; it should not override a different operator’s compounder mandate.
- **Marathon:** high industry returns attract capital and competition, so supply response and management’s allocation decisions determine the capital cycle ([Marathon](https://www.marathon.co.uk/the-home-of-capital-cycle-investing/)).
- **O’Neil/IBD:** the official sell guide defines late-run climax evidence and large-volume support breaks as campaign sell conditions ([climax-top booklet](https://shop.investors.com/images/promotional/ClimaxTop_Booklet.pdf); [20 Rules](https://shop.investors.com/images/promotional/20-Rules_102808.pdf)). These are practitioner rules, not survivorship-correct validation, and must stay in the existing Exit/Trim lane.
