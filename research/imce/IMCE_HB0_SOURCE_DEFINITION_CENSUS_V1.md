## 3b. Canonical denominator selection and printed conversion

**Canonical denominator selected per issuer (one per issuer, per the standing rule — not one per family):**

- DHI, PHM, KBH, NVR: canonical denominator = **gross orders in the period** (4 of 5 reporting issuers already use this convention natively; no conversion needed).
- TOL: canonical denominator = **gross signed contracts in the period** (TOL's own "signed contracts" basis), chosen for cross-issuer comparability with the DHI/PHM/KBH/NVR gross-orders convention, since "gross orders" and "gross signed contracts" are the same underlying event (a signed sales contract in the period) under different issuer-specific labels. TOL's **alternate** reported basis — beginning-of-period backlog — is registered as the mandatory alternate-convention sensitivity leg (§3c), not discarded.
- LEN: no canonical denominator is frozen — the cell class itself is excluded (§7.2 condition 1); no conversion is needed or possible.

**Printed conversion (definitional, not computed on any data):**

For an issuer reporting on the backlog basis (TOL's alternate basis), the algebraic relationship to the gross-orders/signed-contracts basis is:

```
cancellation_rate_backlog_basis  = cancellations_in_period / beginning_backlog_units
cancellation_rate_orders_basis   = cancellations_in_period / gross_signed_contracts_in_period
```

These two ratios share the same numerator (cancellations in the period) but different denominators (a stock measured at period-start vs. a flow measured during the period), so they are **not** algebraically convertible into one another by a fixed multiplicative factor — the conversion factor is `beginning_backlog_units / gross_signed_contracts_in_period`, which is itself a **ratio that varies by issuer, quarter, and cycle phase** (it compresses when orders are surging relative to backlog and expands when orders are falling relative to backlog — i.e., it is regime-dependent). This document prints the relationship and flags it as regime-dependent; it does not compute or fit the ratio for any period, issuer, or cell — that is fitting.

## 3c. Registered alternate-convention sensitivity plan (plan only — compute nothing)

Per the standing rule: "a mandatory alternate-convention sensitivity re-run is required; a result that flips under the alternate convention is not a pass." This document registers the plan for that future re-run; it performs none of it now.

- **Primary convention (registered):** gross-orders/signed-contracts basis, applied uniformly to DHI/PHM/TOL/KBH/NVR (LEN excluded per §7.2(1)).
