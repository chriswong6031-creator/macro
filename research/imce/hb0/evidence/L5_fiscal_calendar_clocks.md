# IMCE-HB-0 — Frozen Fiscal-to-Calendar-to-Macro Time Map

Generated by SEC EDGAR submissions API + spot-checked primary filings. No price/return/outcome data included anywhere below.

## Method note (deviation from literal brief, disclosed)
Parts 1-3 were built from `https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK##########.json` (recent + all archived
`.../CIK##########-submissions-NNN.json` pages) for each issuer — one bulk pull per issuer, not per-quarter
page scraping. This gives form, filingDate and reportDate (period of report) for EVERY filing 2001-present in a
handful of HTTP requests, exactly as the SOURCE STANDARD recommends. Fiscal-year-end was then additionally
VERIFIED against the literal cover-page text of the latest (FY2025) 10-K for all six issuers (see citations),
not just the structured `fiscalYearEnd` field, and cross-checked against the full 2005-2026 run of 10-Q/10-K
`reportDate` values (65 quarter-end dates per issuer) to confirm no fiscal-year-end change and no 52/53-week
drift. Earnings-release dates (Part 3) were identified programmatically from the SAME submissions JSON using
its `items` field: an 8-K whose `items` contains `2.02` ("Results of Operations and Financial Condition") is
the earnings-release 8-K. This is exhaustive matching (144 issuer-quarters), not a hand sample, and is more
reliable than guessing which 8-K of several filed in a window is the earnings release. One 8-K EX-99.1
exhibit (DHI FY2025 Q4) was opened directly to spot-check what operating detail is disclosed at reported_at
vs. held back to the 10-K (Part 5 / Q5 finding).

## Part 4 — Alignment hazard table

### Hazard A — identical fiscal-quarter LABEL, non-overlapping calendar windows
Worked example: "FY2024 Q1" measurement windows, all VERIFIED from the FY2024 rows in the Part 3 table above:

| Issuer | FY2024 FQ1 measurement_start | measurement_end |
|---|---|---|
| DHI | 2023-10-01 | 2023-12-31 |
| TOL | 2023-11-01 | 2024-01-31 |
| LEN | 2023-12-01 | 2024-02-29 |
| KBH | 2023-12-01 | 2024-02-29 |
| PHM | 2024-01-01 | 2024-03-31 |
| NVR | 2024-01-01 | 2024-03-31 |

DHI's "FQ1" (Oct-Dec 2023) and PHM/NVR's "FQ1" (Jan-Mar 2024) share ZERO calendar days — a full 3-month /
one-full-macro-quarter offset despite an identical fiscal-quarter label. A naive join keyed only on
`(issuer, fiscal_quarter_number)` without translating to calendar months would silently pair DHI's Q4-2023
operating activity with PHM/NVR's Q1-2024 operating activity under the same nominal "Q1" bucket.
Misalignment size: 3 months (one full quarter) at the extremes; TOL and LEN/KBH sit at intermediate 1-2 month offsets.

### Hazard B — identical CALENDAR quarter, different fiscal-quarter labels across issuers
Worked example: calendar Q4 2023 (Oct 1 - Dec 31, 2023):

| Issuer | Fiscal label whose window best matches Oct-Dec 2023 | Match quality |
|---|---|---|
| DHI | FY2024 FQ1 (Oct 1 2023 - Dec 31 2023) | Exact — full 3-month overlap |
| PHM | FY2023 FQ4 (Oct 1 2023 - Dec 31 2023) | Exact — full 3-month overlap |
| NVR | FY2023 FQ4 (Oct 1 2023 - Dec 31 2023) | Exact — full 3-month overlap |
| TOL | No exact match: FY2023 FQ4 covers Aug-Oct 2023 (only Oct overlaps, 1mo); FY2024 FQ1 covers Nov 2023-Jan 2024 (Nov-Dec overlap, 2mo) | Split across 2 fiscal quarters, best coverage 2 of 3 months |
| LEN | No exact match: FY2023 FQ4 covers Sep-Nov 2023 (Oct-Nov overlap, 2mo); FY2024 FQ1 covers Dec 2023-Feb 2024 (Dec overlap, 1mo) | Split across 2 fiscal quarters, best coverage 2 of 3 months |
| KBH | Same as LEN | Split across 2 fiscal quarters, best coverage 2 of 3 months |

So for the SAME calendar quarter, DHI's number is labeled fiscal-"Q1" while PHM/NVR's number for the SAME
calendar months is labeled fiscal-"Q4" of the PRIOR fiscal year — a naive label-based join ("always use each
issuer's Q4" or "always use each issuer's Q1") gets 2 of 6 issuers systematically wrong by a full fiscal year
offset, and TOL/LEN/KBH never have an exact single-quarter match to any calendar quarter at all — any join to a
calendar quarter for those three necessarily blends two fiscal quarters or accepts up to a 1-month spillover.

## Part 5 — The five stamps, per issuer

For all six issuers the fields are populated as follows unless noted:
- **measurement_start / measurement_end**: the calendar start/end dates of the fiscal quarter per the Part 2 map (measurement_end = the 10-Q/10-K `reportDate` from EDGAR, VERIFIED).
- **fiscal_period**: `{TICKER}-FY{yyyy}-Q{n}` where yyyy is the fiscal year LABEL used on the issuer's own 10-K/10-Q cover (i.e. the calendar year in which the fiscal year ENDS) and n in {1,2,3,4}.
- **reported_at**: the filing date of the 8-K carrying the Item 2.02 earnings-release exhibit (EX-99.1) — the FIRST public disclosure of headline results. This is a SEC-timestamped event (`acceptanceDateTime` in the submissions JSON, generally same calendar day the press release itself goes out).
- **available_at**: for headline financial/operating metrics (revenue, net income, EPS, homes closed, net sales orders, backlog units/value, cancellation rate, gross margin, segment revenue/pre-tax by geography) available_at = reported_at, because the spot-checked DHI FY2025 Q4 EX-99.1 exhibit contains all of these at the region level. For metrics NOT found in the earnings-release exhibit (full audited financial statements, detailed debt maturity schedules, income-tax-rate reconciliation detail, off-balance-sheet/JV entity detail, warranty-reserve rollforward, related-party and legal-contingency detail, MD&A risk discussion) available_at = the 10-Q/10-K filing_date, which is materially later than reported_at (see Part 3 filing_lag vs release_lag columns — the gap is 0-23 additional days for Q1-Q3 and 9-30+ additional days for Q4/annual across the six issuers).
- **calendar_bucket**: the calendar month (or quarter, per the consuming lane's convention) containing measurement_end, OR — if the consuming methodology requires full-window containment rather than endpoint containment — the calendar quarter that contains the FULL measurement_start-to-measurement_end span; per Part 4, DHI/PHM/NVR always have such a fully-containing calendar quarter, but LEN/KBH/TOL do NOT for any fiscal quarter (their quarters straddle two calendar quarters), so an endpoint-containment rule and a full-containment rule will disagree for those three issuers on every single quarter.
- **knowledge_cutoff**: same value as available_at at the metric level above — i.e. there is no single knowledge_cutoff per fiscal_period, there are (at minimum) TWO: one at reported_at for headline metrics, one at the 10-Q/10-K filing_date for everything else first disclosed there.

### Where reported_at and available_at diverge, per issuer
This was spot-checked on ONE filing (DHI FY2025 Q4 EX-99.1, accn 0000882184-25-000044) rather than
systematically across all 144 issuer-quarters (out of budget for this wave) — treat the metric-level claim as
a SOURCE CLAIM / spot-check generalized to the cohort, not a per-filing VERIFIED FACT for LEN/PHM/NVR/KBH/TOL.
The DHI exhibit DID include: net income, EPS, pre-tax income and margin, consolidated/segment revenue, homes
closed, net sales orders (units and $, by region), ROE, ROA, debt-to-capital, book value/share, cash flow by
segment, share repurchases, effective-tax-rate figure, warranty and backlog line items ("SALES, CLOSINGS AND
BACKLOG" table by region). It did NOT include full consolidated balance sheet/income statement/cash-flow
statement line-item detail, footnote-level debt maturity schedule, or segment total assets — those exist only
in the 10-K. This pattern (headline KPIs including backlog/orders in the press release; full statements and
footnote detail only in the 10-Q/10-K) is standard homebuilder-industry disclosure practice and is a reasonable
prior for the other five issuers, but was NOT independently opened and confirmed for LEN/PHM/NVR/KBH/TOL in
this wave — flagged in Gaps.

### Q5 — retro-disclosure lag finding
Within the ONE filing pair spot-checked (DHI FY2025 Q4), no metric was found that was TOTALLY ABSENT from the
earnings release and appeared for the first time only in the 10-K with a lag anywhere near the 13-month
cautionary exhibit cited in the brief's WHY section (which the brief itself attributes to "another issuer",
i.e. outside this six-name cohort — this wave found NO evidence of, and makes no claim about, that other
issuer's case). What WAS found, and IS a real (if much smaller) retro-disclosure lag worth naming: full audited
statement detail, tax-rate reconciliation, debt-maturity schedules and off-balance-sheet/JV detail are
structurally absent from every quarter's earnings-release 8-K and structurally first-public only at the
10-Q/10-K filing_date — i.e., an EVERY-QUARTER, EVERY-ISSUER retro-disclosure lag of the filing_lag-minus-
release_lag magnitude shown in Part 3 (roughly 0-25 additional days for Q1-Q3, and 9-31+ additional days for
Q4/annual, per issuer — see the per-issuer table). This is a STRUCTURAL, RECURRING lag (same categories every
quarter), not an anomalous one-off — but it is much smaller than a 13-month case and applies to
balance-sheet/footnote detail, not to the headline operating KPIs that typically drive a macro join.
