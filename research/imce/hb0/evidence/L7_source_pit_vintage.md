- **MBA (Weekly Applications Survey, incl. Purchase Index): NOT FREELY USABLE FOR THE FULL SERIES.** Full index-level data requires a paid subscription under a "single-user license agreement," and "copying or other redistribution...violates U.S. copyright law" per search-summarized mba.org language [SOURCE CLAIM, not independently page-opened — GAP for direct verification]. Only the free weekly press release (headline % change, not a usable numeric time series) is openly accessible.
- **Freddie Mac (PMMS 30-yr fixed): PARTIALLY CONSTRAINED.** The site's Terms of Use bar redistributing/publishing/commercially exploiting "Data" without a separate written license [VERIFIED, freddiemac.com/terms/], even though a full 1971-2026 archive is openly downloadable and a PMMS-page note permits attribution-only use. Treat internal ingestion/storage as requiring rights clarification, not as automatically clear.
- **S&P CoreLogic Case-Shiller: NOT FREELY USABLE.** S&P Dow Jones Indices' terms state index data "cannot be redistributed without the express prior consent of S&P Dow Jones Indices" [SOURCE CLAIM, search-summarized from an S&P DJI terms PDF hosted by a third party (oberbank.at); the canonical spglobal.com/spdji/en/governance/terms-of-use/ page returned HTTP 403 this session and was not independently opened — GAP for direct re-verification]. Access to the underlying data additionally requires registration.

**Direct answer:** Of the five named sources, NONE are unconditionally "freely usable" for storage/redistribution as-is. NAR and S&P Case-Shiller carry the clearest, most explicit blocking language (both verified/source-claimed directly against redistribution and storage). MBA's full series is paywalled/licensed. Freddie Mac's PMMS values are openly published and archived but the site's general Terms of Use still restrict redistribution of "Data" without a license — a narrower, softer constraint than NAR/S&P. NAHB's rights status could not be verified this session and must not be assumed clear.
