## Addendum — Q5 (Centex merger entry into PHM's reported series), stated directly

**VERIFIED (opened filing):** PulteGroup FY2009 10-K (filed 2010-02-19, acc 0001193125-10-034418), Note 2 "Centex merger": "On August 18, 2009, the Company completed the acquisition of Centex through the merger of Pulte's merger subsidiary with and into Centex... As a result of the merger, Centex became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Pulte. **Accordingly, the results of Centex are included in the Company's consolidated financial statements from the date of the merger.** For the period from August 19, 2009 to December 31, 2009, Centex's revenues and net loss were $1.1 billion and $442.9 million, respectively." The transaction was accounted for under ASC 805 (Business Combinations, acquisition method), with Pulte as accounting acquirer and acquired assets/liabilities recorded at estimated fair value.

**Answer: PROSPECTIVE ONLY, not restated.** Centex's orders, backlog, community count, and inventory entered PHM's reported operating series starting with the post-close stub period (Aug 19–Dec 31, 2009); PHM's FY2009 10-K explicitly isolates Centex's contribution in that stub period (e.g., "Centex contributed 435 active communities at December 31, 2009," line 3499) rather than presenting a combined pro forma or restated history. No language in the FY2009 10-K describes restating PRIOR (pre-2009) periods' operating metrics to include Centex on a combined/pooled basis — pooling-of-interests accounting was not used (not permitted under ASC 805 for this era). **The filings do not state whether any pro forma/as-if-combined operating-metric history was published anywhere else** (e.g., an investor-relations supplemental deck) — that is `missing`, not confirmed absent, since only the 10-K was checked for this question.

Community-count methodology was separately reset in the same year: "the criteria for determining active communities was modified during 2009... The active community counts for prior periods have been recalculated to conform to the current presentation" (FY2009 10-K, lines 3418-3419) — this is a METHODOLOGY restatement of the community-count metric itself, distinct from (and not caused by) the Centex consolidation entry; both events landed in FY2009 and must not be conflated.
