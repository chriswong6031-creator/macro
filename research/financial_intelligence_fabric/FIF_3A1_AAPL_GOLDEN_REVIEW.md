2. **Balance-sheet share-count rows.** Presentation includes `Common stock, shares outstanding` and `shares issued` as their own rows (`14773260000`). HTML puts share counts in the equity line's parenthetical caption.
3. **Commitments and contingencies** is an `xsi:nil` fact (`quality_state=nil`, value null) rather than a dollar amount.
4. **Definition linkbase is retained and unused.** Reconstruction is undimensioned presentation+calculation+label. Product/service dimensional facts are not projected onto the primary tree.
5. **Agreeing duplicate iXBRL occurrences** (visible + hidden) are collapsed to a representative with `occurrence_count>1`. Values agree; this is not first-row-wins of a conflict.
6. **Scale/display.** HTML millions vs reconstructed whole USD. Scale is on the cell; the value is not re-rounded for display.
