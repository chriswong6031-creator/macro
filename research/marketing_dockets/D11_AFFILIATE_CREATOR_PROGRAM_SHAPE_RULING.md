# MKT-D11 — Affiliate + Creator Distribution Program Shape Ruling

- **Status:** RECOMMENDED FOR OPERATOR RATIFICATION
- **Canonical deliverable:** `research/marketing_dockets/D11_AFFILIATE_CREATOR_PROGRAM_SHAPE_RULING.md`
- **Prepared:** 2026-07-20
- **Scope:** Macro Dashboard + Terminal as one commercial relationship; creator, affiliate, newsletter, and community distribution
- **Depends on:** D07 attribution join, D11 Allies ledger, MNZ billing truth
- **Does not authorize:** outreach, price changes, commission issuance, external claims, or payouts

---

## 0. Direct verdict

Launch a **dated Founding 20 partner program**, not a generic affiliate page.

The opening offer should be:

> **Founding partners earn 100% of commissionable net revenue from a referred subscriber's first paid month, then 40% for paid months 2–12.** Their audience receives one consistent launch benefit. The creator receives full product access, ready-to-publish product artifacts, and attribution embedded in every shared Dashboard or Terminal object.

Add a quality unlock:

> **Months 2–12 rise from 40% to 50%** after a partner produces 10 retained paid subscribers, has at least 60 days of observable cohort history, and stays below the refund/chargeback ceiling.

This is aggressive without signing away the company forever. At full retention, the base founding offer pays the creator 45% of first-year commissionable net revenue; the quality-unlocked offer pays 54.2%. It front-loads the creator's reward, preserves recurring alignment, and ends after month 12. No lifetime tail.

The bigger decision is not whether the opening cut is 40% or 50%. It is whether **attribution lives inside the objects people naturally share**. TrendSpider's best move is not its 30% headline; it is automatically carrying affiliate identity in shared charts. Mastermind already has better raw distribution objects—`What changed`, regime cards, watchlists, sector maps, and ticker deep links—but their creator identity is not yet preserved through the funnel.

The thesis:

```text
creator uses the product
  -> product generates a useful, attributable object
  -> audience opens, clones, or follows the object
  -> Macro context hands off into the Terminal
  -> one account remembers the originating creator
  -> paid retention, not clicks, funds the creator
  -> fresh changes give the creator another reason to publish
```

This should be marketed as **a creator distribution system**, not “an affiliate program with banners.”

## 1. The offer being distributed

### 1.1 One subscription, one workflow, two surfaces

Do not ask creators to explain two products or two subscriptions.

Use one sentence:

> **Macro Dashboard tells you what market you are in. Terminal tells you what changed in the names you care about. One account, one subscription, one research workflow.**

The handoff is the story:

| Surface | Job | Natural creator artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Macro Dashboard | Regime, liquidity, risk, sector rotation, cross-asset context | Regime card, “what changed” receipt, sector map, event exposure map |
| Terminal | Single-name workspace, trading charts, watchlists, live-options layer | Ticker dossier, chart, watchlist, alert, before/after autopsy |
| Bundle | Move from market context to a name without switching research systems | “From regime to ticker” episode and cloneable creator watchlist |

### 1.2 The current offer truth must be reflected precisely

The ratified MNZ record currently says:

- Insider: **$59 monthly** or **$588 annually** ($49 monthly equivalent);
- Pro: **$89 monthly** or **$828 annually** ($69 monthly equivalent);
- Terminal core is public; the paid entitlement includes the full Dashboard and Terminal live options.

Therefore creator copy should say **one account and one paid workflow across Dashboard + Terminal**, not “two separately paid products for the price of one.” If the operator intends to gate the entire Terminal as part of the subscription, MNZ must be amended before creators say so.

There is also a live repo conflict: the MNZ masterplan specifies a 7-day trial, while D07 and current chart CTAs say 14 days. Affiliate assets must consume a single dated offer-truth object rather than hardcode either number. Resolve this before the first external invitation.

### 1.3 The audience promise

Creators should sell a repeated job, not feature volume:

> **See what changed, understand why it matters, and carry the market context into the ticker workspace.**

The subscription is not “285 pages plus charts.” It is a compressed loop:

1. What changed in the market?
2. Which sectors and names are exposed?
3. What does the Terminal show for the name?
4. What would weaken the read?
5. What changed next?

## 2. The dated launch program

### 2.1 Founding 20 window

Recommended dates, assuming ratification and implementation begin now:

| Clock | Rule |
|---|---|
| Applications open | 2026-08-03 |
| Founding enrollment closes | 2026-10-31, or when 20 useful partners are active, whichever occurs first |
| Founding acquisition window | Referred subscriber's first paid invoice must occur by 2027-03-31 |
| Commission life per subscriber | First 12 paid months; pauses during nonpayment; never extends beyond 12 collected invoices |
| Founding terms sunset | New referrals first paid after 2027-03-31 use the then-current standard program |

If implementation slips, move all dates together. Do not quietly shorten the promised acquisition window.

“20” is intentionally small. With no users, Mastermind needs hands-on partners who will actually use the system, reveal conversion friction, and teach the product how creators work. A public directory of 500 inactive affiliates would create administrative theater, not distribution.

### 2.2 Founding economics

| Component | Founding creator / newsletter | Community partner | Reporter / journalist |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| First paid month | **100%** of commissionable net revenue | **40%** | **0%** |
| Paid months 2–12 | **40%** | **40%** | **0%** |
| Quality unlock | **50%** for months 2–12 after gate | Case-by-case, same ceiling | Not applicable |
| After paid month 12 | **0%** | **0%** | **0%** |
| Product access | Full partner seat while active | Admin/demo seat as needed | Capped research/media access |
| Audience benefit | Standard creator launch offer | Standard community launch offer | No purchase inducement |

The quality gate should require all of the following:

- at least 10 referred paid subscribers;
- at least 60 days since the first referred paid conversion;
- cohort day-60 paid retention at or above 70%;
- refunds plus chargebacks below 8% of collected invoices;
- no unresolved program-rule or disclosure breach.

When the gate is met, 50% applies prospectively to that partner's eligible months 2–12. It does not retroactively rewrite paid commissions.

### 2.3 Standard program after the founding window

The durable public program should be simpler and lower:

| Level | Requirement | Share |
|---|---|---:|
| Partner | Approved participant | 30% of commissionable net revenue for first 12 paid months |
| Builder | 10 active paid referred subscribers + quality floor | 40% for first 12 paid months |
| Desk | 50 active paid referred subscribers + quality floor | 50% for first 12 paid months |

Do not make a new partner earn a low rate because they are new. Their default 30% should be real from the first conversion. Volume raises the rate; it does not unlock the right to be treated seriously.

### 2.4 Define the money once

**Commissionable net revenue (CNR)** should mean subscription cash actually collected from the referred customer, less:

- discounts, credits, and refunds;
- chargebacks and unrecovered disputes;
- sales, VAT, GST, and similar taxes;
- payment-processing fees;
- separately itemized pass-through data charges, if any.

It should not be reduced by general payroll, hosting, research, or company overhead. Creators need an auditable base, not a definition that can be hollowed out later.

For an annual invoice, calculate the full 12-month founding commission at the same economics:

```text
base founding annual share
  = CNR * ((1 month * 100%) + (11 months * 40%)) / 12
  = CNR * 45%

quality-unlocked annual share
  = CNR * ((1 month * 100%) + (11 months * 50%)) / 12
  = CNR * 54.1667%
```

Hold annual-plan commission until the refund window closes. Do not dribble a fully collected annual invoice across 12 payouts unless the creator explicitly prefers that cash-flow profile.

### 2.5 Illustrative economics at current annual pricing

The table below is an illustration, not a forecast. It assumes no extra audience discount and approximately 3% payment processing; replace that assumption with actual Stripe economics.

| Plan | Annual cash | Approx. CNR after 3% fee | Base founding creator | Company before variable service cost | Quality-unlocked creator |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Insider | $588.00 | $570.36 | $256.66 | $313.70 | $308.94 |
| Pro | $828.00 | $803.16 | $361.42 | $441.74 | $435.05 |

This is why the launch terms are rational: an acquired customer still contributes cash on the first invoice, while a customer who retains past month 12 becomes 100% company revenue. The high share is a variable acquisition budget, not a permanent gross-margin haircut.

### 2.6 Audience benefit

Give partners one standard benefit, not permission to invent offers:

- recommended: **15% off the first annual invoice**; or
- if a monthly buyer refuses annual: **20% off the first three monthly invoices**.

Never stack the two. Keep the trial at the single canonical duration once the 7-day/14-day conflict is resolved.

The creator chooses a memorable code, but the code is a cross-device attribution fallback—not the main distribution mechanism and not proof of disclosure.

### 2.7 Milestone upside without paying for noise

Use retained-customer bounties instead of impression fees:

- $250 after 10 referrals reach day 60 paid;
- $1,000 after 50 referrals reach day 90 paid;
- one permanent founder product seat after 25 referrals reach day 90 paid, subject to pass-through data-cost limits.

No bounty for raw clicks, free signups, views, followers, or posts. Those can all be manufactured without creating a customer.

## 3. The product-native distribution engine

### 3.1 The current leak

Two existing paths are unusually valuable and currently lose partner identity:

1. `templates/watchlist.js` builds a share URL as `.../watchlist.html#wl=<payload>`. URL fragments are not sent to the server, so the list is shareable but the acquisition system cannot observe the payload or creator unless a normal query/signed slug is added.
2. `templates/theme.js` sends ticker clicks to `https://app.mastermind-x.com/terminal?sym=<ticker>&from=macro`. The handoff does not preserve `partner_id`, campaign, or artifact identity.

The first affiliate build should repair these two flows. Buying affiliate software before fixing them would automate the least important links while leaving the natural product loop dark.

### 3.2 Attribution envelope

Every shareable public object should carry a signed, non-personal envelope:

```yaml
schema: marketing.partner_attribution.v1
partner_id: cr_...
campaign_id: cmp_...
artifact_id: art_...
artifact_type: what_changed | regime | ticker | watchlist | sector_map | receipt
created_at: 2026-07-20T00:00:00Z
terms_version: founding20.v1
signature: hmac_...
```

Public URLs may use a short signed token such as `?via=alex&a=K7P3Q`. The server resolves the token to the envelope. Never place payout terms, email addresses, or customer information in the URL.

### 3.3 Attribution policy

Use **first approved partner touch**, because the creator who caused discovery deserves predictable protection:

- attribution window: 365 days;
- repeat touch from the same partner refreshes the window;
- direct and branded visits do not overwrite partner attribution;
- a later affiliate click does not overwrite a locked first touch;
- an explicit valid creator code entered by the buyer before first payment may override the browser touch;
- signup copies attribution to the user account so cross-device conversion is possible;
- first payment freezes the commission owner for that subscription;
- record assisted touches for analysis, but pay one partner unless a written co-production split exists.

Do not depend on a third-party cookie. Store consented first-party attribution, then lock it server-side on signup.

### 3.4 The six objects to build in order

#### Object 1 — Creator watchlist

This is the fastest route because share-link behavior already exists.

- creator publishes a named list;
- follower opens or clones it;
- the list updates as underlying data changes;
- the follower can inspect any member in Terminal;
- the creator gets an automatic “three things changed” recap to publish later.

The referral is attached to an enduring object, not a coupon.

#### Object 2 — What Changed card

The home page already has a `What changed` stream. Add one-click image/link sharing with:

- timestamp and freshness;
- one bounded fact;
- why it matters;
- the condition that would weaken the read;
- creator identity and paid-partner disclosure when relevant;
- signed canonical URL.

#### Object 3 — Ticker dossier / Terminal chart

Every shared ticker object should open directly at the relevant symbol, preserve `via`, and expose a public preview before the subscription ask.

#### Object 4 — Regime receipt

Export the current regime, confidence, transition state, leading drivers, and “what would change this” condition. This is the Macro Dashboard's recurring signature object.

#### Object 5 — Sector rotation map

Creators covering semiconductors, energy, uranium, China, Canada, options, or macro should get a cropped, mobile-safe sector object rather than a screenshot of the full dashboard.

#### Object 6 — Followable creator desk

A creator desk is a maintained public page containing their watchlists, recent shared objects, upcoming live session, and transparent partner relationship. Followers can clone objects and receive updates. This becomes a lightweight network inside the product without pretending creators are independent analysts employed by Mastermind.

### 3.5 Product loop and activation

An attributed visitor should not land on a generic pricing page.

```text
shared object
  -> useful public preview
  -> clone / follow / inspect in Terminal
  -> account or canonical trial
  -> activation across both surfaces
  -> paid plan
```

Define bundle activation as all three within seven days:

1. opens one Macro Dashboard `What changed` or regime object;
2. opens at least one symbol in Terminal;
3. saves, clones, follows, or alerts on at least one object.

This is more meaningful than “logged in” and gives the partner an honest activation funnel.

## 4. Creator program, not affiliate inventory

### 4.1 Who to recruit first

Recruit 20 useful distribution partners across distinct audiences:

| Lane | Why Mastermind is additive | Best seed object |
|---|---|---|
| Macro / rates / liquidity | Strongest Dashboard-native story | Regime receipt + what-changed card |
| Swing trading / technicals | Natural Dashboard→Terminal handoff | Ticker dossier + creator watchlist |
| Options | Terminal live-options is a paid entitlement | Options context card + event watchlist |
| Sector / thematic investors | Existing sector and basket depth | Rotation map + followable theme list |
| China / Hong Kong | Real differentiation from US-only tools | Bilingual China/HK desk |
| Canada / TSX | Underserved regional wedge | TSX macro-to-ticker watchlist |
| Fundamental / long-horizon | Research reports and falsifiers | Thesis stress test + ticker dossier |
| Finance-tool reviewers | Can demonstrate the whole workflow | “from regime to ticker” teardown |

Do not use follower count as the primary filter. Prefer repeat audience attention, topic fit, trust, and willingness to show the product in use. Ten retained customers from a 5,000-person niche newsletter are worth more than 100,000 indifferent views.

### 4.2 What each founding partner receives

- full partner product access while active;
- a 45-minute founder onboarding and workflow build;
- a co-branded creator desk;
- three seed objects tailored to their existing content;
- a cloneable watchlist and event page;
- live conversion, activation, paid, retention, refund, and commission reporting;
- one joint live session or webinar in the first 60 days;
- priority requests for new share formats, not priority over signal truth;
- the dated Founding 20 economics.

The opening pitch is not “please review our software.” It is:

> **We built a live [topic] desk around the names your audience already follows. It is ready to publish under a transparent partner label. Your audience can clone it, you own the attribution, and you receive 100% of month one plus recurring revenue for the rest of year one.**

### 4.3 Content franchises the product can keep feeding

1. **From Regime to Ticker** — start with the Macro state; finish inside one name in Terminal.
2. **What Changed in 90 Seconds** — creator narrates one automatically generated change card.
3. **Follower Watchlist Clinic** — followers submit symbols; the creator publishes a cloneable list and a later change recap.
4. **Before / After Receipt** — freeze the pre-event read and reopen it after 1, 5, and 20 sessions.
5. **Break My Thesis** — creator submits the bull or bear case; Mastermind prints evidence, counterevidence, and falsifiers.
6. **Regional Desk Night** — China/HK, Canada, semiconductors, energy, uranium, or options-specific session.
7. **The Miss Ledger** — reopen a failed or weakened read. Trust is distribution in finance.

Creators should receive fresh objects automatically. The program dies if every post requires them to construct a new funnel or wait for the company to design a custom image.

### 4.4 Creator portfolio of offers

Use three levels of relationship:

| Relationship | What they do | What Mastermind provides | Economics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Affiliate | Recommends an existing product workflow | Links, codes, product access, standard assets | Standard program |
| Creator partner | Repeatedly publishes native product objects | Creator desk, watchlists, seed packs, joint sessions | Founding or negotiated within approved ceiling |
| Distribution partner | Community/newsletter/product embeds maintained objects | Co-branded page, embed/feed, audience onboarding | 40% for 12 months or written fixed equivalent |

Reporters receive data and chart support, not commissions. Product reviewers may use affiliate economics but must retain editorial freedom and disclose compensation.

## 5. Systems and implementation choice

### 5.1 Buy commodity accounting; own attribution truth

Use affiliate software for portals, tax forms, commission accounting, refunds, and payouts. Keep Mastermind's artifact and attribution ledger first-party.

Recommended launch stack:

- **Stripe** remains payment truth;
- **FirstPromoter Starter** is the default operational layer;
- D07 remains the internal acquisition and cohort ledger;
- D11 remains the partner identity, approval, and materials ledger;
- signed product artifacts write `partner_id`, `campaign_id`, and `artifact_id` into D07 events;
- Stripe subscription/invoice events settle commission eligibility.

FirstPromoter is the better fit than a hand-built payout system at launch because its current $49 plan advertises link and coupon tracking, W-9/W-8BEN collection, refund/chargeback handling, self-referral detection, mass payouts, API/webhooks, and an embedded/brandable partner dashboard. Rewardful is a credible alternative and also starts at $49, but the existing TrendSpider precedent and FirstPromoter's tax/fraud administration make it the narrower default here.

Do not let the vendor become the only attribution database. Product artifacts, cross-subdomain handoffs, activation events, and assisted touches are Mastermind-specific.

### 5.2 Build packets

| Packet | Owner | Deliverable | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| AFF-00 Offer truth | Funnel + Allies | One versioned offer/commission file with dates, tier prices, trial, discounts, terms | All creator assets render from it; CI catches stale literal price/trial claims |
| AFF-01 Program terms | Allies | Founding 20 + standard clickwrap agreements; definitions and prohibited traffic | Test partner can accept terms; terms version stored |
| AFF-02 Partner identity | Allies | Approved `partner_id`, code, payout/tax status in D11 ledger | No code issues without approved identity |
| AFF-03 First-party attribution | Funnel | Signed envelope, 365-day first-touch storage, signup lock | Fixture survives anonymous→signup→Stripe customer |
| AFF-04 Cross-surface continuity | Funnel + Terminal | Preserve attribution through Dashboard→Terminal and auth | `via` survives `?sym=&from=macro`, signup, and checkout |
| AFF-05 Watchlist repair | Workshop | Query/signed slug plus existing `#wl` payload; clone/follow event | Shared list produces attributable view and clone without exposing private data |
| AFF-06 Share cards | Studio | What Changed, ticker, regime, sector mobile cards | Card + canonical link include the same signed artifact id |
| AFF-07 Creator desk | Workshop + Allies | Co-branded page, seed objects, clone/follow | One partner can publish without custom engineering |
| AFF-08 Partner portal | Allies + Funnel | Clicks, activations, paid cohorts, retained revenue, commission | Numbers reconcile to Stripe and D07 fixture |
| AFF-09 Settlement | Funnel | Refund window, clawback, monthly/annual payout logic | Refund and chargeback reverse unpaid/next payout correctly |

- `engine/marketing/links.py` and `engine/marketing/attribution.py` already define the D07 outbound-link and conversion-join direction. Add product-artifact fields to that spine.
- `templates/watchlist.js` owns the current share-link behavior.
- `templates/theme.js` owns Dashboard→Terminal symbol routing.

- `engine/marketing/chart_render.py` already prints the chart CTA/brand bar; make it consume offer truth and artifact attribution rather than introducing a separate affiliate renderer.

## 6. Measurement: reward distribution that stays

### 6.1 North star

Use:

> **Day-90 retained paid subscribers per 1,000 attributed product-object opens.**

This connects share quality, landing relevance, bundle activation, conversion, and retention. Raw affiliate revenue can hide discounts, refunds, and a creator who attracts high-support churn.

### 6.2 Partner scorecard

Every partner should see:

- artifact opens and unique visitors;
- clone/follow/Terminal-open activation;
- trial starts;
- paid conversions by Insider/Pro and monthly/annual;
- day-30, day-60, and day-90 paid retention;
- collected CNR;
- matured, pending, reversed, and paid commission;
- refund and chargeback rate;
- assisted conversions, separately from paid commission ownership.

The internal scorecard should add variable service cost and contribution profit. Creators do not need the company's full cost ledger, but they do need a stable, auditable CNR base.

### 6.3 Ninety-day pilot gates

Treat these as planning gates until 30 paid conversions exist, then recalibrate from real cohorts:

| Measure | Continue / expand | Investigate | Pause partner or format |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bundle activation among attributed trials | ≥40% | 20–39% | <20% |
| Trial-to-paid among activated users | ≥25% | 10–24% | <10% |
| Day-60 paid retention | ≥70% | 50–69% | <50% |
| Refund + chargeback rate | <8% | 8–12% | >12% |
| Attributable retained contribution | Positive after two payout cycles | Small/uncertain N | Persistently negative |

Do not punish creators for small samples. Print the cohort N and wait for enough exposure.

### 6.4 Incrementality

Affiliate attribution answers “who gets credit,” not “would this customer have arrived anyway?”

Once volume exists:

- keep a small randomized holdout on company retargeting to partner-referred users;
- compare creator-specific landing pages against the generic landing page;
- measure branded-search and direct-return lift after creator events;
- keep direct/branded traffic from overwriting the creator, but report it as a later touch;
- review contribution after two complete payout cycles before raising a partner's commercial ceiling.

## 7. Program rules—the short, load-bearing version

High commissions invite useful creators and useless arbitrage. Terms must exclude:

- self-referrals and controlled-account referrals;
- cookie stuffing, forced clicks, invisible redirects, or attribution interception;
- unauthorized cashback, coupon-directory leakage, or sub-affiliate networks;
- spam, unsolicited mass DMs, bought traffic, fake engagement, or fake accounts;
- paid search on Mastermind/MastermindX, Macro Dashboard, Terminal, or confusing variants;
- claims of guaranteed profits, verified trading performance without evidence, or product capabilities that do not exist;
- presenting a creator as an employee, adviser, or independent reviewer when that is untrue;
- retroactively adding a creator code after the first paid invoice;
- publishing gated/raw data outside the permitted share objects.

Operational rails:

- commissions mature after the applicable refund window, with a 45-day default;
- minimum payout: $50;
- refunds and chargebacks reverse commission;
- tax and identity information required before payout;
- Mastermind may request the URL or recording of a promotion tied to a disputed claim;
- termination stops future referrals but does not erase already matured, legitimate commission;
- material terms changes require notice and never retroactively cut earned commission.

### 7.1 Disclosure and claims

Every compensated post, email, image, video, or live segment must make the relationship obvious where the endorsement occurs. A code or the words “affiliate link” alone may not be enough. Use plain language such as:

> **Paid Mastermind partner. I receive a commission if you subscribe through this link.**

Put it before a “more” fold, near the link, in the same language as the promotion, and visually plus audibly for video when practical. Product-generated partner cards should include a compact paid-partner label by default; creators should still disclose in the surrounding post or segment.

The FTC says material relationships should be clear and hard to miss, and the Competition Bureau of Canada says a discount code or affiliate link alone is unlikely to be sufficient. If Mastermind is or becomes an investment adviser registered or required to register in the US, the SEC adviser marketing rule can add promoter disclosure, oversight, written-agreement, disqualification, and recordkeeping duties. That classification question is a pre-launch legal gate, not something affiliate copy can solve.

## 8. Launch sequence

### Days 0–14 — make credit trustworthy

- ratify economics, dates, CNR, audience benefit, and quality gate;
- reconcile 7-day versus 14-day trial truth;
- choose FirstPromoter or the operator-approved equivalent;
- ship AFF-00 through AFF-05;
- generate the first partner terms and disclosure kit;
- run refund, annual-plan, cross-device, and Dashboard→Terminal attribution fixtures.

### Days 15–30 — five design partners

- recruit one creator each in macro, swing/technicals, options, China/HK, and Canada/sector investing;
- build three seed objects per partner;
- run one private reverse demo per partner;
- observe which object they actually want to publish;
- fix the share and activation loop before expanding.

### Days 31–60 — public Founding 20 sprint

- open the dated page and applications;
- publish the first creator desks;
- run two “From Regime to Ticker” sessions;
- issue weekly creator change packs;
- show real cohort Ns in the partner portal;
- recruit up to 20, stopping earlier if onboarding quality falls.

### Days 61–90 — compound or cut

- reopen every partner cohort at day 60;
- activate the 50% quality unlock where earned;
- stop low-trust or high-refund traffic sources;
- turn the three best creator formats into automatic product outputs;
- decide whether the standard 30/40/50 program opens in 2027.

## 10. What not to do

- Do not offer lifetime 40–60% revenue share. A zero-user launch problem should not become a permanent margin encumbrance.
- Do not split Dashboard and Terminal into separate affiliate products or codes.
- Do not make creators screenshot dense pages; generate mobile-native objects.
- Do not launch an open auto-approved program before attribution, refunds, and identity checks work.
- Do not pay for follower count, impressions, clicks, free accounts, or a required positive review.
- Do not let coupon sites overwrite the creator who caused discovery.
- Do not ask creators to promote before giving them a useful desk built around their own audience.
- Do not let affiliate software become the only ledger of why a subscriber converted.
- Do not hardcode prices, trial duration, discounts, or commission terms across chart templates.
- Do not market the public Terminal as paid-exclusive unless the ratified entitlement model changes.

## 13. Final ruling

**ACCEPT the affiliate/creator channel, but build it around maintained, attributable product objects.**

The recommended launch shape is:

```text
Founding 20
2026-08-03 through 2026-10-31
qualifying first payment by 2027-03-31
100% of CNR in paid month 1
40% in paid months 2–12
50% months 2–12 after the quality gate
365-day first approved partner touch
no lifetime tail
one account / one Dashboard-to-Terminal workflow
```

The commission gets attention. The embedded attribution loop creates compounding distribution. The latter is the asset.
