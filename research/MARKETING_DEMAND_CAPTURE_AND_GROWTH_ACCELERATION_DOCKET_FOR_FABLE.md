## 1. Executive ruling

The Marketing lobe should stop treating more content as the main constraint.

Content Studio, chart production, six editorial identities, mover posts, signal posts, macro cards, Stocktwits planning, and roughly 1,500 ticker dossiers now create a credible supply of public attention objects. The new constraint is what happens after a stranger sees one.

Today, a visitor can land on a deep ticker page, inspect a great deal of intelligence, copy the URL, open the analyzer, or browse other stocks. The page does not yet let the visitor hire Mastermind for the recurring job implied by the visit:

> **Watch this ticker for me and tell me only when the picture materially changes.**

That missing action is more important than another post format.

The next growth phase should therefore be built around one operating law:

> **Give away the answer. Capture the object of interest. Charge for memory, breadth, speed, depth, and coordination.**

Do not send public attention to a generic homepage or generic signup. Send every person to a useful object that already knows what they care about: a ticker, portfolio, catalyst, theme, claim, creator watchlist, or event. Let them receive value immediately. Then let them preserve or monitor that object with one small commitment.

The fastest compounding loop is:

1. a post, search result, embed, AI assistant, creator, or community answer exposes a useful Mastermind object;
2. the visitor gets the useful answer without paying;
3. the object offers one exact continuation: follow this ticker, save this watchlist, monitor this catalyst, reopen this claim, or rerun this portfolio X-ray;
4. Mastermind delivers a second useful event later;
5. the event carries a permanent shareable receipt;
6. the user shares, clones, embeds, or invites somebody into the object;
7. breadth, freshness, history, private monitoring, and higher-cost intelligence create the paid bridge;
8. attribution, retention, refunds, and contribution data teach Fable which objects create durable customers.

This docket recommends a temporary reallocation of build priority:

1. **Funnel plus Beacon first:** turn ticker pages into free monitoring entry points and repair attribution.
2. **Workshop second:** ship the Portfolio X-Ray and event war-room objects that create high-intent leads.
3. **Allies third:** make watchlists, charts, alerts, and research packets clonable and co-brandable so other people distribute them.
4. **Broadcast and Studio continue operating, but do not receive the majority of marginal build effort until the capture loop works.**

The contrarian point is simple: the best guerrilla campaign is not a louder post. It is a free product fragment that keeps working after the post dies.

- D07 attribution, D10 public tools, and D11 creator/community infrastructure as written but not fully built.

### 2.1 The conversion leak on the ticker pages

The shipped ticker template has a useful Copy link action and a generic final call to action:

- Open Analyzer
- Browse All Stocks

Those actions move people around the product. They do not capture the visitor's declared intent.

A visitor on the NVDA page has already told us something more valuable than a generic email lead: **NVDA matters to them now.** The site should not throw away that information.

The primary action should become:

> **Watch NVDA free**

The secondary actions can remain:

- Ask why it is moving;
- compare it with a peer;
- copy or embed the current view;
- open the full analyzer.

### 2.2 Commercial truth currently conflicts

The Marketing rollout index and D07 describe a chart CTA for a free 14-day trial. The ratified Monetization and Access masterplan describes a 7-day card-required trial. This is not a copy nit. It is a commercial-truth split at the exact moment Broadcast is preparing to send traffic.

Before external scale, Fable must compile one machine-readable offer truth and make every chart, page, post, plan page, lifecycle message, and affiliate kit consume it.

Recommended product distinction:

- **Free utility:** one monitored ticker forever, no card;
- **Full-product trial:** one ratified duration and card posture, set in configuration;
- **Founding experiment:** a separately named, capped cohort offer that may use different terms without silently changing the standard plan.

Do not use “free trial” to describe the free one-ticker utility. It is a durable product tier, not a countdown.

### 2.3 The priority map is now stale

D10 Workshop and D11 Allies are currently P3 while more content formats are P1/P2. That ordering made sense when the system lacked launch-worthy public objects. It is now backward.

The lobe has enough content to test distribution. It does not yet have enough paths from content to recurring value. Fable should promote the smallest parts of D07, D10, and D11 that close that path.

## 3. First-principles growth model

Raw traffic is not the scarce asset. Remembered intent is.

For this system, durable growth can be decomposed as:

**Durable growth = useful first touches × intent capture × second-value delivery × share coefficient × paid expansion × retention ÷ marginal cost**

The current build is strongest at useful first touches. It is weakest at intent capture, second-value delivery, and portable sharing.

### 3.1 The four moments that matter

#### Moment 1 — Instant proof

The visitor receives a complete answer now. No fake blur, no empty tease, no generic hero copy.

Examples:

- why the ticker is moving;
- which causal assumption changed;
- hidden portfolio concentration;
- the next catalyst and exposed names;
- the historical receipt for a prior claim.

#### Moment 2 — Tiny commitment

The visitor preserves exactly one useful object.

Examples:

- watch one ticker;
- save one event;
- clone one public watchlist;
- subscribe to one claim's outcome;
- receive one portfolio X-ray by magic link.

This is a better lead than “join our newsletter” because the preference is explicit and immediately useful.

#### Moment 3 — Unprompted second value

Mastermind contacts the user only when it has earned the interruption.

Examples:

- the causal picture changed;
- an invalidation condition fired;
- earnings or a macro release moved the exposure map;
- the claim matured and was graded;
- the portfolio acquired a new concentration or contradiction.

The second useful event is the real activation event. Signup is merely account creation.

#### Moment 4 — Natural expansion

The user wants more of the same job:

- more tickers;
- faster alerts;
- full history;
- portfolio-wide monitoring;
- more sophisticated Brain questions;
- live options;
- exports, API, team, or creator infrastructure.

That is where payment belongs. The paywall should sit across scope and operating leverage, not across the first proof.

### 3.2 Every public object must pass the GIFT test

Every ticker page, post, chart, tool, report, and bot answer should answer:

- **G — Gives:** what useful value is complete before signup?
- **I — Identifies:** what exact interest did the visitor reveal?
- **F — Follows up:** what future event will legitimately bring them back?
- **T — Travels:** how can the result be shared, cloned, embedded, cited, or called by another tool?

Objects that fail two or more letters should not receive more distribution.

## 4. The highest-leverage systems

## 4.1 Ticker Guardian — turn every dossier into a free sensor

**Verdict:** build first. This is the shortest bridge from the shipped SEO estate to recurring leads.

Add a persistent action near the ticker hero and again after the first proof block:

> **Watch AAPL free — get an alert only when the read materially changes**

The free product:

- one ticker for an anonymous or magic-link user;
- up to three tickers for a signed-in free account;
- a daily or event-triggered digest, never noise for its own sake;
- alerts only on named material changes: regime, thesis, evidence, invalidation, catalyst, options posture, or major contradiction;
- a permanent receipt URL showing before, after, why it changed, and what remains unresolved;
- one-click pause, change cadence, or unsubscribe.

The paid bridge:

- larger watchlists and portfolios;
- intraday or faster freshness where the data supports it;
- deeper history and causal graph;
- full outcome/revision history;
- cross-ticker and portfolio consequences;
- more Brain usage and premium surfaces.

Why this can scale:

- Beacon already created the landing inventory;
- the user self-segments by ticker;
- every later alert creates a return visit;
- every receipt is shareable;
- the free tier has a clean marginal-cost cap;
- the paid expansion is obvious without crippling the free answer.

Owners: Beacon + Funnel + Workshop + Engine Room.

Primary metrics:

- dossier view to monitor-start;
- monitor-start to confirmed delivery;
- confirmed delivery to second useful event;
- alert open to receipt view;
- receipt share or clone rate;
- free monitored ticker to trial and paid expansion;
- marginal inference and delivery cost per activated monitor.

Hard acceptance:

- no alert fires when only the page timestamp changed;
- every alert names the changed field and prior state;
- users can understand why they received it;
- a user can stop it in one action;
- a holdout cohort proves the Guardian increases second sessions without materially worsening page engagement.

## 4.2 Portfolio X-Ray — weaponize the anxiety users already have

**Verdict:** build second. This should be the strongest broad-market lead magnet.

Position it provocatively:

> **Your 12 stocks may be only three bets. Find the hidden overlap.**

Alternative campaign names:

- Bagholder Rescue Clinic;
- Portfolio Blind-Spot Scan;
- Hidden Bet Detector;
- What Can Hurt Everything You Own?

The free flow:

1. paste tickers and optional weights, upload a CSV, or choose equal weight;
2. run locally where possible and discard raw holdings unless the user explicitly saves;
3. return factor duplication, sector and theme concentration, macro and event exposure, earnings clustering, liquidity sensitivity, and named contradictions;
4. show one high-value “what changed recently” observation;
5. create a privacy-safe share card that never exposes tickers unless the user opts in;
6. offer “monitor this portfolio” as the exact continuation.

The result should diagnose structure, not issue personalized trade instructions. The useful promise is:

> **We show what your portfolio is actually exposed to and when that exposure picture changes.**

Why this can spread:

- it begins with pain and curiosity, not a feature tour;
- the result is personalized enough to feel valuable;
- sanitized cards are socially shareable;
- creators can run public community X-Ray sessions;
- every scan seeds watchlist and portfolio monitoring;
- paid value is continuous, not a one-off report.

Owners: Workshop + Funnel + Sentinel + Lab.

Do not require account creation before showing the first useful diagnosis. Ask for identity only to save, monitor, or receive the result.

## 4.3 Catalyst War Rooms — own burst demand instead of renting it

**Verdict:** build a reusable template, not one-off event pages.

For earnings, CPI, FOMC, tariffs, policy shocks, and other scheduled or breaking events, create one URL whose state changes through time:

1. **Before:** what matters, consensus-sensitive variables, exposure map, historical analogues, and named invalidations;
2. **Live:** the actual release, first-order and second-order consequences, winners/losers by mechanism, contradictions, and confidence class;
3. **After:** what persisted, what faded, which thesis changed, and which prior claims were graded.

Each War Room offers:

- follow this event;
- add the catalyst to a calendar;
- monitor an exposed ticker;
- clone the exposure watchlist;
- embed the live matrix;
- receive the postmortem.

Why it wins:

- the event creates the traffic spike for free;
- one durable URL accumulates links before, during, and after;
- Broadcast posts all point to the same compounding object;
- reporters and creators can cite or embed the map;
- the postmortem creates a second value event and public accountability.

Use accurate sitemap last-modified values and notify participating search engines of genuinely changed URLs. Google says accurate lastmod fields help communicate updates; IndexNow accepts immediate added, updated, or deleted URL notifications in batches. These are freshness signals, not indexing guarantees. See [Google crawl guidance](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawl-budget) and [IndexNow documentation](https://www.indexnow.org/documentation).

Owners: Radar + Workshop + Beacon + Broadcast + Studio.

## 4.4 Cloneable Watchlists — make users and creators carry the product

**Verdict:** build the collaboration primitive before building a standalone community.

Any ticker set should become a permanent object:

- creator watchlist;
- event exposure basket;
- “AI infrastructure bottlenecks” list;
- “earnings this week” list;
- user's private or public watchlist;
- analyst bull case versus bear case basket.

Every list gets:

- a stable URL;
- current changes across members;
- clone/follow action;
- update digest;
- creator or source identity;
- optional co-branding;
- referral identity;
- a clear statement of methodology and last update.

The loop:

1. a creator publishes a useful watchlist;
2. followers clone it;
3. Mastermind monitors it;
4. a change alert brings followers back;
5. the creator receives a fresh chart or receipt to discuss;
6. more followers clone the list.

This is stronger than an affiliate link because the referral is attached to a maintained object users actually want.

Owners: Workshop + Allies + Funnel + Beacon.

## 4.5 Thesis Stress Test and Open Ledger Challenge

**Verdict:** use productive conflict as acquisition.

Let a user submit a concise thesis:

> “NVDA margins remain durable because inference demand absorbs supply.”

Mastermind returns:

- evidence for;
- evidence against;
- hidden assumptions;
- the strongest counter-case;
- a concrete falsifier;
- a check-by date;
- the related tickers and exposures;
- a permanent private page, or a public page by explicit opt-in.

Then create two guerrilla formats.

### Bring Us Your Worst Thesis

A recurring clinic where people submit the position or thesis they are least certain about. Mastermind tries to break it. The useful result is delivered before any sales ask.

### The 100-Day Open Ledger

Invite creators, analysts, AI systems, and willing users to publish falsifiable claims under a standard contract. Grade them on calibration, evidence quality, correction speed, and outcome—not popularity or raw returns alone.

The stunt is:

> **We are not asking you to trust our AI. We are asking everyone, including our AI, to leave receipts.**

Why this can win attention:

- it is adversarial and entertaining without becoming empty outrage;
- it turns Mastermind's governance architecture into a public product;
- participants distribute their own challenge pages;
- mistakes create content instead of being hidden;
- creators receive a credible way to demonstrate process quality.

Public challenge pages can later use legitimate author/profile and discussion markup where the page truly contains first-person profiles or public discussion. Google supports ProfilePage and DiscussionForumPosting markup for qualifying community pages, but markup must describe the visible page and does not guarantee special search treatment. See [Google profile-page guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/profile-page) and [discussion markup guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/11/discussion-and-profile-markup).

Owners: Workshop + Sentinel + Allies + Beacon + Lab.

## 4.6 Mastermind Anywhere SDK — distribute answers, not links

**Verdict:** portable intelligence is a growth channel. Build one read-only answer contract and expose it through several thin adapters.

Initial surfaces:

- embeddable What Changed card;
- embeddable event exposure matrix;
- per-ticker RSS or Atom change feed;
- calendar feed for catalysts;
- Discord and Telegram commands;
- signed JSON endpoint;
- Google Sheets custom function or downloadable sheet template;
- read-only MCP server for AI assistants;
- a small public API allowance.

Initial MCP tools:

- what_changed(ticker);
- why_is_it_moving(ticker);
- get_claim_receipt(receipt_id);
- get_event_impact(event_id);

Every result should include:

- as-of time;
- freshness state;
- evidence class;
- a permanent canonical page;
- the next update trigger;
- optional monitor or clone link.

MCP is an open protocol supported by multiple AI clients, and its tool model supports structured inputs and outputs. That makes one server a plausible distribution adapter across AI workflows rather than a one-client integration. See the [MCP introduction](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/getting-started/intro), [server guide](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/develop/build-server), and [tool specification](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-06-18/server/tools).

Embeds are not speculative as a finance distribution pattern. TradingView explicitly packages copy-paste charts, tickers, watchlists, screeners, calendars, and symbol-detail widgets for finance sites. Mastermind should attack a different layer: explanatory change, evidence, falsifiers, and receipts. See [TradingView widget documentation](https://www.tradingview.com/widget-docs/getting-started/).

Owners: Workshop + Allies + Engine Room + Funnel.

The free API and MCP allowance is not charity. It makes Mastermind the cited upstream tool inside somebody else's workflow. Paid plans expand rate, freshness, history, private watchlists, and commercial embedding.

## 4.7 Demand-Backed Dossier Factory

**Verdict:** let real demand choose the next expensive public object.

Every ticker dossier should have:

> **Request a deeper investigation**

The visitor selects the unresolved question:

- why margins changed;
- what the market is pricing;
- the next catalyst;
- ownership and positioning;
- competitive exposure;
- options structure;
- bull versus bear evidence.

The request creates:

- a privacy-safe demand event;
- an optional notification lead;
- a public queue count;
- a research commission when demand, strategic value, or partner importance crosses a threshold;
- a notification to every requester when the answer ships.

This does three jobs:

1. converts low-intent SEO traffic without pretending every visitor wants a subscription now;
2. prevents programmatic content waste by letting demand allocate research;
3. creates an anticipation loop before the report exists.

The lobe may also allow users to spend earned research credits to accelerate a request. Credits come from retained referrals, useful falsifications, or paid plans—not raw social engagement.

Owners: Beacon + Radar + Workshop + Funnel.

## 4.9 Creator Reverse Demo — do the work before asking for the meeting

**Verdict:** replace generic outreach with a working co-branded proof object.

For each qualified creator, community, newsletter, or small research firm, generate a private preview containing:

- the five tickers or themes they cover most;
- a sample What Changed brief;
- one original chart;
- one upcoming-event map;
- a co-branded clonable watchlist;
- a transparent recurring affiliate offer;
- the actual public receipt history relevant to their beat.

The message is:

> **We built the research desk your audience could use. Keep it free if it is useful. If retained subscribers result, you share the economics.**

Standard low-risk partner provisioning should graduate to G4 autonomy under a machine-readable offer and standard terms. Do not require the operator to approve every ordinary creator email forever. Human/root authority is appropriate for non-standard contracts, binding signatures, unusual economics, regulated referral structures, or reputation-sensitive institutions—not routine provisioning inside a ratified playbook.

This is an explicit recommended amendment to D11's all-outreach operator gate. The scaffold can begin conservative, but a sovereign Marketing lobe needs an earned path to standard outreach and provisioning.

Owners: Allies + Workshop + Funnel + Sentinel.

## 4.10 Founding Compute Club — convert early believers into runway

**Verdict:** sell participation in the build, not a lifetime discount.

Offer a capped founding cohort:

- 100–250 seats;
- price locked for 24 months, not forever;
- a visible monthly “what your subscription funded” compute and capability receipt;
- early access to new monitored objects;
- a vote or allocation of research credits toward the next public tool or dossier;
- a founding badge and a limited number of invite codes;
- monthly open-ledger briefing showing shipped improvements, mistakes, and next experiments.

This matches the actual company story: early revenue buys more compute and accelerates autonomous improvement. Make that causal link visible.

Avoid:

- fake scarcity that resets every week;
- giant discounts that anchor the product as cheap;
- promises that founding members control market conclusions or signal authority.

Owners: Command + Funnel + Engine Room + Lab.

## 5. Guerrilla campaigns built on the systems

The systems above are the durable assets. The campaigns below are fast ways to make them visible.

### 5.1 “Reply with one ticker”

Flagship post:

> Reply with one ticker. We will return what changed, the strongest contradiction, and the next condition that matters.

This is valuable opt-in engagement, not unsolicited reply harvesting. X permits automation that broadcasts useful information and permits automated replies when users clearly requested contact, subject to its conditions; AI reply bots and brand auto-response campaigns require prior approval, and non-API website scripting is prohibited. Run manually or via the official API until the correct approval exists. See [X automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation).

The reply result links to a complete free ticker object, not a sales page.

### 5.2 The Bagholder Amnesty Week

Invite people to submit the position they no longer know how to think about. Publish opt-in stress tests, strongest counter-cases, and what evidence would repair or break the thesis.

The emotional hook is powerful because it addresses embarrassment and uncertainty that ordinary bullish content ignores.

Conversion path:

- free stress test;
- follow the thesis or ticker;
- monitor the invalidation;
- expand to portfolio monitoring.

### 5.3 “We changed our mind” Fridays

Every Friday, publish:

- three claims strengthened;
- three weakened;
- one killed;
- one thing the system still cannot resolve.

Each item links to its before/after receipt. This is the fastest way to turn the correction ledger into a recognizable brand ritual.

### 5.4 Earnings Cliff Map

Before the busiest earnings weeks, publish:

- the most crowded reporting windows;
- theme and supplier/customer clusters;
- names exposed to the same variable;
- the sequence in which evidence will arrive;
- a clonable calendar and watchlist.

Creators and newsletters can embed it. Users can follow one event cluster.

### 5.5 The Portfolio Doppelganger

After an X-Ray, show:

> Your 14-stock portfolio behaves most like this three-factor basket.

The precise result remains private. The user can share a sanitized “I thought I owned 14 bets; I owned three” card.

### 5.6 Open Research Request Board

Display the most requested unresolved questions. Let users follow a question, contribute evidence, or spend a research credit. When the answer ships, all followers return.

This is demand generation, lead capture, product research, and community formation in one object.

### 5.7 Market Myth Autopsies

Take a popular market claim and test its actual construction:

- “rate cuts always help growth stocks”;
- “insider buying is bullish”;
- “options flow predicts the move”;
- “earnings beats guarantee upside.”

Publish evidence, counterexamples, and a falsifiable surviving version. The user can request the next myth.

### 5.8 Creator-vs-Machine Thesis Duels

A creator supplies the bull case; Mastermind supplies the counter-case or vice versa. Both sides declare falsifiers and dates. The outcome reopens automatically.

The duel page carries both identities, both audiences, and the later receipt. Do not pay for views or directional excitement; share retained subscription economics.

### 5.9 Free local finance-club kits

Give university investment clubs, meetup hosts, and small Discord communities:

- one clonable watchlist;
- one event calendar;
- one meeting-ready chart pack;
- one monthly X-Ray;
- one co-branded receipt page.

The community gets a useful operating surface. Mastermind gets repeated exposure inside a trusted group.

### 5.10 Research object QR and image fingerprints

Every exported chart, PDF, slide, or physical event card should carry a small searchable receipt ID and QR pointing to the living object. The static image becomes a route back to current evidence.

### 5.11 Free “one question” Brain result

On a ticker dossier, allow one narrow, pre-scoped question without opening a general expensive chat:

- What changed?
- What is the strongest contradiction?
- What is the next catalyst?
- What would invalidate the current read?

The response is cached against the public artifact and includes evidence. Identity is requested to save, monitor, or ask a second question.

### 5.12 Outcome-triggered referral unlocks

Member referral:

- one referred user must activate, not merely sign up;
- two activated referrals unlock three extra monitored tickers for 90 days;
- five unlock a portfolio X-Ray refresh or research credits;
- no cash for ordinary members.

Creator/partner referral:

- cash share based on net collected revenue;
- refunds, discounts, and chargebacks deducted;
- quality and retention affect future allocation;
- no lifetime tail.

This rewards useful distribution rather than spam.

## 6. Channel-specific operating doctrine

## 6.1 X

Use X to start or accelerate an object loop:

- hot-ticker post to Ticker Guardian;
- event thread to War Room;
- receipt post to outcome page;
- creator duel to challenge page;
- “reply with one ticker” to a requested answer.

Do not use browser scripting for automated actuation. X's April 2026 automation rules explicitly prohibit non-API website scripting, duplicative posts across accounts, unsolicited automated replies, and automated likes. They allow multiple automated accounts for distinct, non-duplicative uses and useful informational broadcasting. X separately permits up to ten accounts for different non-duplicative purposes while prohibiting coordinated self-amplification and substantially similar cross-posting. See [X automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation) and [X authenticity rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/authenticity).

The six desks remain strategically valid when each creates a different object and serves a different audience. Their value comes from beat specialization, not mutual amplification.

## 6.2 Stocktwits

Treat Stocktwits as an on-platform value lane:

- post the useful ticker-specific observation in full;
- link only when the free destination genuinely completes the promised idea;
- keep product promotion a small minority of posts;
- do not use multiple accounts to amplify;
- do not post nearly identical messages;
- do not use unauthorized managed or automated access.

Stocktwits' current rules cap product/service promotion at 10% of posts, restrict promotional tagging in the first 50 posts, welcome valuable automated data feeds, and reject bots used for spam or scaled promotion. Its June 2026 best-practices page also rejects links whose purpose is to drive signups or pull discussion away from ticker streams. Its July 2026 terms prohibit unauthorized managed or automated access. See [Stocktwits Community Guidelines](https://stocktwits.com/about/rules/), [Best Practices](https://help.stocktwits.com/c/best-practices/articles/best-practices), and [Terms](https://stocktwits.com/about/legal/terms/).

The useful tactic is therefore counterintuitive: give away the full observation on Stocktwits. Let the brand, receipt ID, profile, and occasional free public object do the acquisition work.

## 6.3 Search

Do not measure Beacon by pages generated.

Measure:

- qualified impressions;
- non-brand clicks;
- view-to-monitor conversion;
- second sessions;
- request-board demand;
- links and embeds earned;
- paid expansion.

Search actions:

- add accurate last-modified values only when meaningful visible content changed;
- notify IndexNow participants only for genuine additions, updates, or deletions;
- build internal links around user jobs: compare, monitor, event, exposure, contradiction;
- create high-demand pair pages such as NVDA versus AMD only when the comparison is substantive;
- noindex or omit pages that cannot deliver differentiated value;
- preserve fast static delivery;
- give every page a useful exact continuation, not a generic footer CTA.

Google states that merely updating a sitemap is adequate for most ordinary sites and that not every crawled page is indexed; popularity, page quality, uniqueness, staleness, and serving health affect crawl demand. More URLs are not the strategy. See [Google crawl-budget guidance](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawl-budget).

## 6.4 Email and push

The newsletter should become a router, not a newspaper.

Each user receives:

- changes only for followed objects;
- one ranked “you may have missed” item;
- one outcome reopen;
- one exact next action.

Do not send the same giant briefing to everybody. A free one-ticker monitor with a 40% open rate and repeated return behavior is more valuable than a large undifferentiated list with no declared intent.

## 6.5 Creators and communities

Lead with infrastructure:

- co-branded watchlist;
- custom event map;
- free X-Ray session;
- private reverse demo;
- embed;
- outcome grading;
- revenue share on retained users.

Do not ask a creator to “review Mastermind” before Mastermind has made their work easier.

## 7. Offer architecture

## 7.1 Free public

- all useful ticker dossiers that pass the indexation governor;
- current public receipts and corrections;
- delayed or bounded event War Rooms;
- public indices and selected data drops;
- one-off Portfolio X-Ray with no saved private state;
- read-only public embeds, feeds, and low-rate API/MCP access.

## 7.2 Free remembered

- one ticker without a full account if secure magic-link delivery is available;
- up to three monitored tickers for a verified free account;
- one saved public watchlist;
- followed claims, events, and research requests;
- narrow cached Brain questions;
- daily digest rather than instant alerts.

## 7.3 Full-product trial

The standard trial duration and card posture must be ratified once and consumed from the offer registry. Do not let chart copy, landing pages, and Stripe disagree.

Recommended early experiment:

- keep the ratified 7-day card-required trial as the commercial control;
- run a capped founding cohort with a 14-day no-card activation preview;
- start the preview clock at the first meaningful saved-object event, not account creation;
- compare retained paid contribution, abuse, support load, and activation—not only trial-to-paid percentage.

At zero audience, a no-card founding preview may teach more because it produces a larger sample of genuine product use. It should remain capped until economics are known.

## 7.4 Paid

Charge for:

- more monitored objects;
- faster updates;
- deeper history;
- portfolio-wide and cross-asset consequences;
- premium intelligence and live options;
- larger Brain budgets;
- private collaboration;
- export, API, team, creator, and commercial embed rights.

Do not charge merely to see the first answer that proves the product works.

## 7.5 Founding cohort

Use:

- finite seats;
- 24-month price lock;
- visible build and compute receipts;
- invite codes;
- research credits;
- early tool access.

Do not use:

- lifetime service;
- permanent unlimited AI;
- fake deadlines;
- control over investment conclusions.

## 11. Measurement constitution

## 11.1 North star

**Marketing-attributable retained contribution profit from users who received at least two useful product events.**

The “two useful events” clause prevents the lobe from celebrating accidental checkout or discount-driven signups that never experience the product.

## 11.2 Activation

A user is activated when all are true:

1. they received a complete first answer;
2. they preserved an object;
3. Mastermind later delivered a material second answer;
4. they opened, acted on, or meaningfully consumed it.

Account creation alone is not activation.

## 11.3 Object scorecard

For every Guardian, X-Ray, War Room, watchlist, challenge, embed, and API tool:

- qualified first touches;
- intent-capture rate;
- median time to first value;
- median time to second value;
- second-value consumption;
- share, clone, embed, or install coefficient;
- trial and paid expansion;
- day-30 and day-90 retention when mature;
- refunds and support burden;
- creator or partner payout;
- inference, data, delivery, and infrastructure cost;
- correction and complaint rates.

## 11.4 Provisional experiment floors

These are starting tripwires, not external benchmarks or forecasts:

- if fewer than 2% of high-intent ticker visitors express monitor intent after 500 eligible sessions, rewrite or relocate the offer before adding traffic;
- if more than 20% of first Guardian alerts are judged non-material in blinded audit, narrow the detector before expanding;
- if fewer than 20% of delivered Guardian users consume a second value event after 100 delivered alerts, investigate relevance and cadence;
- if an object creates shares but no preserved intent, classify it as media, not acquisition;
- if a creator or channel produces signups but materially worse activation, refund, or retention than direct cohorts, reduce its economics;
- if an embed, feed, or MCP tool produces sustained use but no canonical visits or preserved objects, redesign the returned continuation before expanding free quota;
- no paid scaling from a cohort without mature contribution and refund data.

The Lab may replace these floors after enough evidence. It may not move them after seeing a result without recording the change.

## 11.5 Incrementality

Use:

- page-level or session-level CTA holdouts;
- delayed rollout by eligible ticker cohort;
- creator watchlist holdouts;
- digest cadence experiments;
- offer cohorts with identical traffic sources;
- geographic or time-block tests where appropriate.

Do not infer lift from before/after traffic alone when Studio and search inventory are changing simultaneously.

1. **Stop sending high-intent ticker traffic to a generic homepage.**
2. **Stop using Open Analyzer as the only meaningful CTA on dossiers.**

4. **Stop calling an email capture a lead when no explicit interest object is attached.**

6. **Stop giving every free user the same newsletter.**
7. **Stop building isolated reports with no follow, monitor, clone, or outcome-reopen path.**
8. **Stop measuring Beacon by generated URLs.**

10. **Stop requiring the operator to approve routine standard outreach forever; make earned G4 autonomy real.**

13. **Stop using browser scripting as an X or Stocktwits automation plan; official API or allowed human actuation is the durable route.**

## 13. Hard risks that actually matter

### 13.1 Alert fatigue

The Guardian dies if “material change” means “nightly numbers moved.” Start narrow, stateful, and explainable. The product promise is less noise.

### 13.2 Commercial truth drift

Conflicting trial claims destroy trust and attribution. MKT-ACC-00 is a release blocker for external scale.

### 13.3 Data and redistribution rights

Open datasets, widgets, APIs, and embeds must use fields the product can lawfully redistribute. Public exposure indices should be derived artifacts with explicit methodology and source rights.

### 13.4 Personalized-advice boundary

Portfolio X-Ray should explain concentration, exposure, contradictions, and change. It should not quietly become individualized sizing or execution instruction without a separate posture.

### 13.5 Private data leakage

Watchlists, portfolio uploads, and creator previews need public/private types. Public share cards default to sanitized output. Public adapters never infer access from a URL alone.

### 13.6 Platform actuation

The creative strategy is not constrained by generic fear. It is constrained by specific enforceable tripwires:

- official API versus prohibited website scripting;
- distinct desk purposes versus duplicative account networks;
- requested replies versus unsolicited automation;
- useful on-platform Stocktwits content versus signup-driving spam;
- authorized bots versus managed access that current terms prohibit.

Build around the actual rules and keep moving.

### 13.7 Premature success claims

Early conversions will be tiny and noisy. Fable should call them observations until cohorts mature.

## 15. Final verdict

Mastermind does not need to choose between giving away value and making money.

It needs to give away the **first completed unit of intelligence** and charge for the operating system that remembers, monitors, connects, updates, and expands it.

That one job can connect SEO, X, Stocktwits, creators, communities, embeds, email, AI assistants, referrals, trials, and subscriptions into a single measurable flywheel.

Build that before building more megaphones.
