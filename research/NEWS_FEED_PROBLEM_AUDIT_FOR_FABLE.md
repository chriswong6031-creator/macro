# News Feed & Narrative Signals — Problem Audit for Fable

*Dated 2026-07-02. This is an Opus adversarial audit of what the news/narrative subsystem SHIPS TODAY — not the roadmap, not the docstrings' aspirations, but the bytes in the committed artifacts. It was produced by six parallel deep-read audits (ingestion & sourcing; the noise filter; the LLM qualitative layer; the quant-sentiment bridge; narrative engines → signals; the structural qual→quant gap & accountability), each followed by an independent adversarial verification pass that re-checked every citation against the live repo and committed data, then synthesized. Every claim below carries a file:line or a quoted artifact and has been checked against the running code. Findings the verifiers REFUTED have been dropped or softened; miscited line numbers have been corrected; confirmed missed problems have been folded in. Deliverable path: `research/NEWS_FEED_PROBLEM_AUDIT_FOR_FABLE.md`. The purpose is to hand Fable a rigorously-grounded problem statement so it can design novel solutions — not to prescribe them.*

## 0. The one-sentence diagnosis

**The news subsystem is three layers that never meet: a headline-only qualitative feed (no article body anywhere), a hand-authored non-AI noise filter that ranks by keyword-density and source-prestige (never by whether a headline preceded a move), and a thin set of lexical/vendor sentiment indices that either feed a score at un-backtested round-number weights or are firewalled out of scoring entirely — so there is no live, validated, accountable path by which qualitative news becomes a quantitative signal, and the one "narrative signal" that reaches allocation is price momentum wearing a narrative costume.**

Concretely, what ships today:

- **No article body exists anywhere in the pipeline.** GDELT artlist returns only `{title,url,domain,seendate}` (`news_common.py:594`); Google-News descriptions are deliberately discarded (`news_rss.py:177` `summary = ""`); the template itself concedes "no article body exists in the feed" (`templates/news.html.j2:16-17`). Every downstream "meaning" operation runs on ~10-15 words of headline.
- **The flagship financial feed silently oscillates between dark and rich.** On the audit snapshot `site/news/financial.json` shipped `"providers": {"polygon": false, "finnhub": false}`, `"counts": {"raw": 317, "tagged": 143, "tickers_covered": 17}`, only **16 of 45 baskets** with headlines — with `degraded_reason: null`. By the 2026-07-02 `main` build it had recovered (`polygon/quiver/gdelt: true`, `"counts": {"raw": 926, "tagged": 746, "tickers_covered": 667}`, **37/45 baskets**) — yet `finnhub` still reads `false` and `degraded_reason` is *still* `null`. So the only leg that routes news to baskets/sectors/tickers can vanish at build, and the artifact reports "healthy" either way.
- **The only scored news→signal hop has zero validated observations.** `site/basketdata/radar_ic.json`: `n_snapshots: 2489, n_matured: 0, ic_all: null`. `radar_track_record.json`: `scored_total: 0, open: 95`. After 2,489 accrued snapshots, no news-derived call has ever been graded against an outcome.
- **The narrative-quant "extraction spine" is a permanent no-op stub.** `news_vector.py:467-479` `extract_structured` returns neutral placeholders (`surprise:None, direction_claimed:'unknown', reversibility:'unknown', llm_extracted:False`); `config.yml:2496` sets `llm_extract: false`. `site/news/narrative.json` items carry only `{title,domain,theme,tier,scheduled_ref,url}`.
- **The one LLM that reads news headlines contributes zero bytes.** `news_llm.py:216-217` sets `llm_importance`/`llm_tone`, but `grep -rl 'llm_importance|llm_tone' site/` is **empty**; `macro.json` headlines carry only deterministic `importance_score`/`intelligence_score`, `summary:None`.
- **The Narrative Brain (the LLM durability judge) ships degraded.** `site/basketdata/narrative_brain.json`: `assessments: [], rotation: null, degraded_reason: "no_usable_reply"`.
- **The keyword-count uncertainty index was tested for a scored role and FAILED.** `reports/narrative-regime-phase0.md` verdict "NO-GO for a scored leg → DISPLAY-ONLY"; incremental IC over VIX = -0.064/-0.084/-0.088/-0.129 at h=5/10/21/63, all FDR-reject on the wrong side. `narrative_regime.py:130-131` `gate_multiplier: 1.0, gate_status: "pinned_off"`.
- **The event store feeding the scored velocity leg is stale.** `data/news_vector/events.parquet`: 60 rows, newest event 2026-06-20 (12 days before the 2026-07-02 build), zero events in the live 7-day window, 46/60 (77%) bucketed "geopolitics", only 4/60 tier-1.

## 1. The keystone — the qual→quant translation gap has no outcome loop that could ever learn what matters

The single deepest problem is not any one broken engine. It is that **the subsystem is structurally incapable of learning "what matters" from results**, and every symptom below cascades from that.

Trace the intended pathway and watch it break at every accountability point:

1. **The raw material is capped at headlines.** With no body (`news_common.py:594`, `news_rss.py:177`), you cannot extract a magnitude, disambiguate negation/sarcasm, or verify that a headline maps to a market-relevant fact. Every "importance" score, entity tag, and theme classifier operates on ~10-15 words. This is the hard ceiling: qual→quant ambition is capped at keyword-in-headline before any modeling begins.

2. **"Importance" is asserted, never measured.** The core "what matters vs noise" decision is a static additive keyword scorer: `macro_news.py:461-492` — base 22, `+min(40,14*len(hits_hi))`, `+min(18,7*len(hits_med))`, theme bonuses +16/+8, bands high≥72/medium≥48, floor `min_importance_score=34` (`config.yml:2461`). Every literal is hand-authored. A grep for `backtest|calibrat|IC` across `macro_news.py` returns nothing. The output ranks the feed and gates inclusion — but `importance_score`/`intelligence_score` are read only inside the news engines and are never regressed against a forward move anywhere in `engine/` or `scripts/`.

3. **The one scored hop is diluted, price-contaminated, and unvalidated.** News velocity fuses into `theme_activity.fused_obs_z` at `NEWS_WEIGHT = 0.5` (`theme_activity.py:54`), explicitly barred from qualifying a basket alone ("only ENRICHES a basket that already has hard activity data — it never qualifies one on its own", `theme_activity.py:338-339`). That feeds `radar.py`'s divergence, `edge_score` (round-number literals, `radar_plus.py:241-246`), and finally `theme_validation.val_upgrade`.

4. **The scoreboard is empty by construction.** `radar_ic.json` shows `n_matured: 0` over 2,489 snapshots because `edge_snapshots.jsonl` began 2026-06-20 (12 days before as-of) against a 21-day maturation horizon (`radar_ic.py:50` `_HORIZON_D=21`) — maturation is mathematically impossible. Separately, the falsifiable theses carry the `HORIZON_D = 63` constant (`radar.py:44`, referenced at `:250/:255`) with `check_by = asof + 91d` (`radar.py:242`), so the two clocks disagree and both read n=0.

5. **There is no leg-level attribution even in principle.** `radar_ic.py` grades the *fused* `edge_score`, never the news leg's marginal contribution. So even after maturation, you could never learn whether news adds or subtracts edge versus the hard-data legs.

The result: **the subsystem can DECIDE what to show and can LOG a promise to grade it, but it has never once closed the loop from a news call to a graded outcome.** Because nothing is graded, the hand-authored importance weights cannot improve, the 0.20/0.50 fusion weights cannot be earned, and "noise" remains defined as "matched no keyword" rather than "did not precede a move." The user's entire thesis — filter noise, use what's useful, translate qual→quant — is foreclosed at the foundation. `news_vector.py:5-8` even makes this explicit: the substrate "makes no forward-return claim, so it cannot be falsified by one … measurement integrity, not alpha." Every stage built on it inherits that unfalsifiability.

## 2. Ingestion ceiling — headline-only, dark providers, stale stores

**Severity: CRITICAL (dark providers) + HIGH (no body, stale stores).**

The subsystem ingests from five source families — keyless RSS/Atom + Google-News (the self-described PRIMARY channel), GDELT DOC 2.0 artlist, Polygon `/v2/reference/news`, Finnhub, and akshare Chinese wires — and everything arrives as headline + metadata. The two structural facts that cap everything downstream are (a) no body ever, and (b) the ticker-tagged corpus can silently ship dark while reporting healthy.

- **NF (ingestion): the ticker-tagged corpus can silently go dark in the shipped feed.** On the audit snapshot `site/news/financial.json` had `providers.polygon:false, finnhub:false`; `counts.raw:317, tagged:143, tickers_covered:17`; **16/45 baskets**; `degraded_reason:null`. By 2026-07-02 `main` it recovered (`polygon/quiver/gdelt:true`, `raw:926, tagged:746, tickers_covered:667`, **37/45 baskets**) but `finnhub` stays `false` and `degraded_reason` stays `null`. `financial_news.py:144` `_polygon_news` and `:184` `_finnhub_news` return `[]` when a key/fetch fails; basket pools at `financial_news.py:491` depend on `tagged`, which then degrades to RSS name-fan-out (megacap only). Meanwhile `data/polygon/news_sentiment.parquet` IS fresh (snapshot max 2026-07-02, 1435 rows, 126 tickers) — so the *collector* keeps running even when the *feed* path's provider fetch is dark. The single documented mechanism for turning news into per-basket/per-ticker structure can silently vanish, and the artifact looks healthy in both states.
- **The health boolean is degenerate.** `financial_news.py:508` sets `providers.finnhub = bool(fh_market or fh_company)` and `polygon = bool(poly)` — i.e. "did this leg return rows," not "did the fetch run/succeed." Both `_polygon_news` and `_finnhub_news` catch all exceptions and return empty with only a `log.warning`. So "no key," "fetch failed (rate-limit/500/schema drift)," and "genuinely no news" all collapse to the same `false`, and `degraded_reason` is never set from them.
- **Zero article body — the hard ceiling.** `news_common.py:594` appends only `{title,url,domain,seendate}`; `news_rss.py:177` `summary = ""  # google-news descriptions are just a relink anchor`; `templates/news.html.j2:16-17` "no article body exists." All "meaning" extraction is therefore headline-string pattern matching or a vendor's opaque label.
- **The scored velocity store is 12 days stale and theme-skewed.** `data/news_vector/events.parquet`: shape (60,9), `first_seen_utc` max 2026-06-20; theme dist `{geopolitics:46, monetary:8, industrial_policy:3, growth:2, inflation:1}` — zero "trade" rows though `news_flow.py:44-46` maps reshoring/critical_minerals/semicap_equipment to "trade." `news_vector.py:18` keep-FIRST "NEVER overwritten," so a silently-halted ingest freezes the store while the build succeeds. Feeds `theme_activity.py:335` at `NEWS_WEIGHT=0.5`.
- **China blended sentiment ships 10 days stale.** `site/chinanews/sentiment.json` `asof:"2026-06-22"` (10 days before build), `z:-2.06`, `n_days:10`, consumed as a scored family in `china_validation.py:462` (`_SIGN_EXPECTED=-1` at `:68`) and `conditions.py:221-252` (the additive display block). A z-band over ~10 sparse days that is itself 10 days stale is fed as an actionable "cautious/risk-tilted lean." *(Correction per verifier: the underlying `data/china_news/cctv_tone.parquet` and `data/china_news/wire_tone.parquet` are actually fresh to 2026-07-01 (idxmax 2026-07-01 on both) — only the blended shipped `sentiment.json` is stale. Note the path: the fresh tone parquets live under `data/china_news/` (underscore), whereas the stale `sentiment.json` lives under `data/chinanews/` (no underscore) — do not conflate the two. The "compounding staleness of the underlying series" framing is dropped; the stale-artifact + tiny-n=10 concern stands.)*
- **Entity tagging is megacap-alias-only.** `news_common.py:352-362` `_MEGACAP_ALIASES` (31 keys); `match_entities` (`:515-534`) matches only those aliases plus standalone uppercase tickers already in the map; `build_entity_map` docstring at `:430` says "megacaps only." For everything except ~31 megacaps and rare literal-ticker headlines, qualitative news never connects to the universe — the text-tagging that is supposed to route a headline to a basket is a near-no-op, and the shipped feed corroborates it (the only baskets with headlines are megacap-reachable).
- **Polygon sentiment watchlist is first-appearance-capped at 120.** `collectors/polygon_news.py:27` `MAX_TICKERS=120`, `:74` `watch=basket_members(cap=MAX_TICKERS)`; `collectors/universe.py` orders by "first appearance across baskets (membership.json order)" then `out[:cap]`. Which names get a news-sentiment channel is decided by JSON ordering, not importance/liquidity/news-volume — a silent, non-random hole no downstream consumer can see.
- **Source-tiering is brittle substring matching.** `news_common.py:93-104` `source_tier` does `any(s in d for s in TIER1_SOURCES)`; `news_rss.py:170-178` derives the domain from the Google `<source url>` element or a `news.google.com` redirect. A syndicated pick-mill mis-tiered to tier-1 gets a near-100 quality and top slot — the exact noise the tiering is meant to suppress. *(Severity LOW.)*

→ **Light solution directions for Fable:**
- Treat body acquisition (fetch+extract the linked article, or license a body-bearing feed) as the enabling precondition for real NLP, and scope which few high-value events justify the fetch cost.
- Replace the degenerate `bool(rows)` health flag with a tri-state per-provider contract: fetch-attempted / fetch-succeeded / rows-returned, and set `degraded_reason` + a coverage-floor alarm when tagged/basket coverage drops below a threshold.
- Add a freshness/liveness guard on the event store (max-age + per-theme minimum coverage) that degrades or flags the news_flow leg instead of fusing a frozen count; gate blended China sentiment on a minimum fresh-day count and surface asof-age.
- Build entity aliases from the full profiles/name table (leak-free, precision-gated) — or explicitly scope text-tagged news to the ~31 names it can tag and label everything else "provider-tag-only."
- Select the news watchlist by news-volume/liquidity/cross-basket importance, not membership.json order, and record exclusions.

## 3. The noise filter — uncalibrated hand rules that re-admit the noise

**Severity: CRITICAL (importance = term-counting) + HIGH (macro-lane pollution, false-negatives, no novelty measure).**

The "noise filter" is a two-stage deterministic screen — a source allowlist plus a keyword-bucket theme gate (`macro_news.py:48-88` `MACRO_THEMES`) — followed by a hand-weighted additive importance score. It is genuinely non-AI, as advertised, but it does not separate signal from noise: it theme-buckets everything mentioning any of ~150 keywords and ranks by term density.

- **"Importance" is un-backtested term-counting.** `macro_news.py:461-492` (details in §1). Every threshold is a hand-authored literal never checked against realized forward |return|/vol. The noise filter's own definition of noise is asserted, not measured.
- **"Macro news" is dominated by single-stock noise.** The shipped `site/news/macro.json` is damning: of 18 items, **only 1 carries a true-macro theme** (growth); 15 are equity-tape (stocks:8/analyst:4/earnings:2/deals:1), 8 are `stock_wire`. Top headlines: "Stocks close out Q2, Nike earnings… Morning Squawk," "Micron's monster post-earnings rally," "Jim Cramer says the AI trade has shifted," "Coca-Cola eyes $1B IPO." `MACRO_THEMES` includes earnings/guidance/analyst/deals/capital_return/stocks (`macro_news.py:75-88`); `filter_headlines:549` keeps `stock_wire` items on source alone (theme gate bypassed), and `_source_weight` returns +22 for `stock_wire` (`:402`).
- **The second gate is also defeated for exactly this noise.** The +22 `stock_wire` bonus plus the base 22 (`macro_news.py:464`) puts every stock-wire item at importance 44 — already above `min_importance_score=34` — so **a stock-wire item cannot be dropped by the importance floor on content grounds** (the only lever that can pull it under is the `-34` `_sec_noise` regulatory-plumbing de-boost at `macro_news.py:485-487`, which drops a stock-wire item to 10; ordinary equity-tape content has no such de-boost). The same +22 inflates `intelligence_score` (the sort key), so it can't be pushed below the fold either. The two-stage screen has no lever that can suppress equity tape on the macro surface.
- **False-negative by construction.** `classify_theme` matches the TITLE only (`macro_news.py:303-310`); `filter_headlines:549` keeps tier-3/quality items only if `theme is not None`; a reputable-source no-keyword fallback sets `theme='macro'` then `_importance` applies -10 "low title-level macro specificity" (`:484-486`). The code even concedes "reputable headlines rarely put CPI/Fed in the title" (`:260-262`). Genuine macro reporting whose title omits a keyword is dropped or penalized — the inverse of the goal.
- **"Noise" has no operational definition beyond keyword-absence + a blocklist.** `classify_theme→None` drops the item; `is_low_value` is a set of literal regexes (`_ROUNDUP_RE`, `_ADVICE_OPENER_RE`, `_PREVIEW_RE`, `_CLICKBAIT`, `news_common.py:131-234`). Dedup is a 60-char normalized-title prefix exact match (`macro_news.py:553`), so 10 paraphrased wire re-writes of one Fed line pass as 10 distinct "signals." There is no measure of informativeness, novelty, or redundancy.
- **The hard-drop filter is untestable and unlogged.** `news_rss.py:186` drops roundup/advice items outright at ingestion (not demote), with no drop ledger. The module's own comment concedes the real picks "live untaggably in the body" — so a mis-firing regex removes genuine single-name news permanently and invisibly, and the filter can only ever be QA'd against the very headlines it operates on (the same body-ceiling that caps the signal caps the filter's own validation). *(Severity LOW.)*
- **Source tier is an editorial-quality proxy doing signal-selection work.** Tier weights (`news_common.py:84` `{1:1.0, 2:0.82, 3:0.58}`) and +12/+22 importance bonuses assume prestige correlates with market-relevance — never calibrated. A prestigious outlet's lifestyle piece outranks a lower-tier market-moving scoop. *(Severity MEDIUM.)*
- **Ticker tagging fabricates "related" signals.** `TICKER_RULES` (`macro_news.py:168-191`) maps generic words to ETFs ('oil'→USO+XLE, 'dollar'→UUP, 'ai'→QQQ, 'gold'→GLD, 'bank'→XLF); any headline with a common word gets "related tickers" + a `ticker_bonus` (`:504-505`) and inflates the synthesis "top tickers" (`:606`). The would-be qual→quant bridge is generated by lexical coincidence. *(Severity MEDIUM; a `_TICKER_STOPWORDS` guard blunts the standalone-token path somewhat.)*
- **Recency is fused into the importance scalar.** `_freshness_points` (`macro_news.py:445-458`) adds 7/5/3/1 into `intelligence_score` (`:505`), and `quality_score` multiplies by `(0.45+0.55*recency)`. The kept list sorts by `intelligence_score` first — so importance and recency are summed into one number and cannot be distinguished. *(Severity LOW.)*
- **Two divergent keyword taxonomies.** `MACRO_THEMES` (theme gate) and a second near-duplicate theme→keyword map used for `_channels` have already drifted (the second adds energy/consumer/housing buckets absent from the first, and re-defines analyst/deals with different keyword sets), so an item's gating theme and displayed channel can contradict. *(Severity LOW.)*

→ **Light solution directions for Fable:**
- Grade importance bands against realized forward |return|/vol of tagged tickers (or SPY); let the data set the cutoffs, and freeze the current bands as explicitly *provisional* until then.
- Split a strict macro-only lane (monetary/inflation/labor/growth/credit/fiscal) from the equity tape; do not let `stock_wire` skip the theme gate or clear the importance floor on the macro surface.
- Add event-level clustering + novelty scoring (shingling or embeddings) so redundant restatements collapse to one weighted event — the cheapest real noise filter.
- Gate the theme classifier on entity presence or body/summary text, not the title string; keep a drop ledger so hard-drops are auditable.
- Decouple "trusted-not-spam" (binary allowlist) from "market-relevant" (measured); keep importance and recency as separate axes.

## 4. The LLM layer — prose for humans, not signal; no citation guard where it counts

**Severity: CRITICAL (news-reading LLM ships zero bytes) + HIGH (extraction stub, no hallucination guard, no graded news call).**

The suite runs three LLM tiers, but almost none of them read the news. The user-facing "AI Daily Brief" synthesizes the dashboards' own deterministic quant state; the one LLM that reads headlines is dark; the structured-extraction stage is a stub; and the only grounded doc→field path ships neutral.

- **The only LLM that reads news headlines is dark in every artifact.** `news_llm.py:216-217` sets `h['llm_importance']`/`h['llm_tone']`; `build_news.py:71-85` propagates them. But `grep -rl 'llm_importance|llm_tone' site/` is empty, and `macro.json` headline[0] carries `summary:None, llm_importance:None, llm_tone:None`, only deterministic `importance_score:81/intelligence_score:100`. `news_llm.enabled()` (`:59-63`) needs a token that didn't resolve in the committed render, so `annotate()` no-op'd. The one component literally reading headlines contributes zero bytes.
- **`news_vector` / `china_news_intel` structured extraction is a permanent no-op stub.** `news_vector.py:467-479` returns `{**record, **neutral}` with `{category_llm:'unknown', scope:'unknown', direction_claimed:'unknown', surprise:None, reversibility:'unknown', llm_extracted:False}`, gated on `enabled AND llm_extract` (`config.yml:2496` `llm_extract:false`). Stronger than first framed: **both** the gated-off branch and the "future" branch return the identical neutral dict — even if enabled, no model is ever called. `narrative.json` items carry zero extracted fields. The designed qual→quant bridge for narrative events is aspirational.
- **`news_llm` has no hallucination/citation guard — safety is a prompt string only.** `news_llm.py:40-52` SYSTEM says "drawn ONLY from the given headline/blurb — no invented facts," but there is no code-side grounding check: `_summarise_batch` (`:135-174`) only clamps importance to 0-100 and truncates summary to 200 chars. Contrast `catalyst_tone.py`, which does verbatim citation verification in code (`_verify`, ~`:198-206`). If `news_llm` were ever enabled, a fabricated summary and importance would silently shape display order (`build_news.py:53`) with no fabrication trap.
- **The "AI Daily Brief" reads quant state + pre-digested policy prose, not the raw news corpus.** `master_brain.gather_state` (from `~:826`) reads `data/regime/latest.json`, bond/forex/commodity `latest.json`, vector `signals.parquet` — all quant — plus text-DERIVED digests: `_policy_intel_summary` (`master_brain.py:641/944`) and `fed_stance` guidance (`:937-943`), tagged `_tape_family:'policy_text'`. `synthesize()` sends `json.dumps(state)` (`:1246`). It reads pre-digested policy text but never the raw news corpus, so it cannot filter noise or extract signal from qualitative flow. *(Softened per verifier: the page copy at `aibrief.html.j2:86` is fairly honest — "narrate the regime… nothing here feeds a score" — so "label oversells" is weaker than originally framed; the substantive "does not touch the news corpus" point stands.)*
- **`catalyst_tone` — the one grounded doc→structured path — ships neutral, and its live consumers are dormant on neutral data.** `data/regime/latest.json` `catalyst_tone`: `tone_score:0.0, guidance_direction:'on_hold', shock_reversible:'unknown', confidence:'medium', degraded_reason:None` — a *genuine* FOMC digest that resolved to neutral on a hold (with verbatim evidence citations), not a degraded stub. It routes into `dislocation.catalyst_narrative` as a string that "NEVER changes verdict" (`dislocation.py:218`), and — importantly — into `engine/spvector_overlay.py` `live_overlay`, a wired knife-veto that CAN change the live recommended weight when `shock_reversible=='persistent'` with adequate confidence (`:86-101`). *(Correction per verifier: the earlier "context-only by construction / no wired promotion" framing is wrong — a bounded veto-only qual→weight path exists and is forward-logged. It is inert TODAY only because the shipped value is 'unknown', which is a data condition, not an architectural firewall. Fable should not be told "no qual→weight path exists.")*
- **Accountable LLM leans exist but have graded ZERO news-derived calls.** `data/master_brain/track_record.json`: `scored_total:0, open:4, "No cross-asset leans scored yet"`. `data/stock_desk/track_record.json`: `scored_total:0, open:76`. The only actually-scored ledger is `ai_desk/scored.jsonl` with n=2 (1 hit, 1 miss). Grading exists but has never touched a news-text-derived claim.
- **`china_news_intel` importance/sentiment are round-number literals barred from scoring by fiat.** `china_news_intel.py:258-267` `importance_score = TIER_W + 0.30(scheduled) + 0.40(surprise) + 0.50(high-impact-kw) + (THEME_WEIGHT-0.5) + min(0.30,0.10*n_baskets)`, decayed by `exp(-hrs/48)`; bands 1.6/0.9 literals; sentiment is a raw `(pos-neg)/(pos+neg)` lexicon count. Disclaimer (`:116`): "Nothing here is an input to any score, signal, regime or allocation." The suite's most concrete qual→quant attempt is a calibration-free heuristic, display-only by fiat. *(Severity MEDIUM.)*
- **The paid brief runs daily with an empty accountable output.** `config.yml:2694` `interval_days:1`; `:2701` `deepseek-v4-pro` "reasoning tier ($0.435/$0.87 per 1M tok)"; `daily.yml:474` runs it every build. `_MACRO_THESES_TAIL` (`master_brain.py:90-91`) instructs theses "usually EMPTY"; all shipped briefs `theses:[]`. Daily reasoning-model spend whose only falsifiable output is empty. *(Severity LOW.)*
- **A latent scale-mismatch in the (dark) re-rank.** `build_news.py:53` `_blend_key = 0.6*quality + 0.4*(llm_importance or quality)` mixes a `quality` scalar with a 0-100 `llm_importance` without asserting commensurable scales. In the dark state `imp is None` so it sorts on `quality`; the moment the LLM lights up, the 40% term could swamp or vanish — enabling the LLM could inject noise into ordering, not filter it. *(Severity LOW.)*

→ **Light solution directions for Fable:**
- Decide whether the news-reading LLM is a real capability or dead code; if real, make its presence in the artifact a build assertion (not a silent no-op), and port `catalyst_tone`'s code-side citation-floor to `news_llm` before its output can influence ordering.
- Treat `news_vector` Stage-C as unbuilt; scope a minimal citation-verified extraction (surprise/reversibility) with a confidence floor, and forward-grade it before trusting any field.
- Use `catalyst_tone` as the proven doc→typed-field pattern and expand coverage beyond FOMC — but budget for the honest yield: on routine holds the verified path correctly returns "unknown," so information rate is low and lumpy (it only pays on genuine surprises).
- Stand up a news-specific falsifiable-lean ledger and let the sample mature before any news-LLM output is surfaced as decision-grade; gate the paid synthesis behind a materiality trigger rather than every-build cadence.

## 5. The quant-sentiment bridge — lexical ≠ importance, coincident, US-only, one leg tested-and-failed

**Severity: CRITICAL (only scored leg is dark + unvalidated) + HIGH (EPU/GPR failed, SF-Fed firewalled, keyword gate collapses 77% into one bucket).**

## The Fable payload — the hard open questions

These are the genuinely unresolved tensions. They are the point of this document; the findings above exist to ground them.

**1. Can a headline-only feed EVER support event-importance modeling — or is body acquisition a hard precondition?**
The no-body ceiling (§2) caps everything: you cannot extract a magnitude, verify a fact, or resolve negation from ~10-15 words. The only "scored meaning" today is Polygon's opaque per-article `insights` label (a positive/negative/neutral COUNT with no article date, no decay, no provenance — trivially dominated by one heavy-coverage day) and a tier-weighted article COUNT velocity. Both are proxies that never read the news's meaning. **Is any headline-only signal worth validating, or does the whole "turn news into signal" thesis require acquiring article bodies (or a body-bearing licensed feed) before any modeling is even sensible? And if bodies are out of reach, should the ambition collapse to "events over vibes" — extract discrete, dated, verifiable EVENTS (guidance cut, plant fire, contract award) whose market mapping can be backtested — rather than sentiment/velocity of headlines?**

**2. What is the minimal viable outcome-labeled dataset to learn "what matters"?**
Every importance/tier/band threshold (§3) is a hand-authored literal never regressed on realized forward |return|/vol. The tagged history is thin: `data/radar` snapshots begin 2026-06-20, `events.parquet` is 60 rows, and the 21-day maturation horizon means any calibration is >21d away with contradictory 21d/63d clocks (§7). **Is there enough labeled history to fit even a crude "does high-importance precede larger moves" relationship? What is the smallest event-study / per-headline forward-abnormal-return scheme that turns the 22/14/7 keyword score into a learned, falsifiable function without leaking — and should the bands be frozen as explicitly provisional until the sample matures? How do you attribute a move when many headlines co-occur on the same clock?**

**3. Lexical-regime vs event-level extraction — which target has structural information advantage?**
phase0 (§5) killed EPU/GPR on forward *vol* incremental over VIX (negative IC) but never tested them on a target VIX cannot price. There is a validated-but-tiny contrarian residual (VIX-orthogonal text-uncertainty precedes LOWER realized vol) sitting unused and pointed the wrong way. **What is the ONE target where a lexical/keyword text index has a structural edge over market-implied gauges (cross-sectional dispersion? tail skew? the timing of a fade?)? Is a bounded complacency/fade factor built on that residual the highest-value salvage — and how would it be forward-graded so it never repeats the radar's n_matured:0 fate? Or is the future entirely in discrete event extraction, making the lexical-regime legs a dead end to retire rather than re-aim?**

**4. How do you grade a qualitative call at all?**
The accountability chassis is total-vacuum (§7): 0 scored, 0 matured, degraded Brain, and even the "validated" vendor family is n_obs:0. Half of logged theses are ungradeable for lack of a price proxy. `catalyst_tone` proves a citation-verified doc→typed-field path works but yields "unknown" on a routine hold — so the information rate is low and lumpy, paying only on genuine surprises. **What is the minimum forward-grading protocol (regime not level, incremental over price+VIX, block-bootstrap) that would license moving ANY news leg from display to a bounded scored weight, and should it be a hard, code-enforced precondition? How should the thesis generator be constrained to only emit proxy-mappable calls — and what is lost when it is? How do you represent "promise-to-grade" vs "actually-graded" so the UI never launders one as the other?**

**5. Semantic dedup / novelty as the real noise filter.**
The shipped feed is full of near-duplicate wire re-writes of one event, and the only dedup is a 60-char title-prefix exact match (§3), so 10 paraphrases pass as 10 signals. The keyword classifier admits Eurovision and sneakers as macro themes (§6). **What is the cheapest event-clustering primitive (shingling vs embeddings) that collapses restatements into one novelty-weighted event WITHOUT an LLM in the hot path — and without reintroducing a look-ahead/restamp hazard the keep-FIRST design was built to avoid? Is a defensible, backtestable definition of event IMPORTANCE (novelty × cross-source breadth × realized co-move) achievable without a hand-maintained keyword list, and can it be validated before it ever touches a weight?**

**6. Per-asset vs macro resolution — what is the honest ceiling?**
The bridge is US-only, English-only, coincident, macro-scoped, with no ticker resolution (§5), and the theme→basket map is a coarse shared-bucket broadcast so correlated baskets get identical z (§6). Text-tagging is megacap-alias-only (§2). **Even a perfectly validated leg at this resolution can at best CONFIRM, never anticipate, and cannot discriminate between baskets sharing a macro bucket. Should Fable scope ambitions to confirmation/veto use cases where coincident macro-scope text is defensible — or is per-asset alpha only reachable by moving to entity-level extraction over bodies (Q1) that resolves to specific names? And where should the qual→quant firewall (`build_site.py:2764`) actually sit: total for headline news, porous only for a per-series IC-gated feature, or is the honest answer that the whole subsystem is best reframed as accountable display context until a body-bearing event pipeline exists?**

**7. The costume problem.**
The flagship "narrative" product is price momentum relabeled (§6); the one genuine qual→weight path (the `spvector_overlay` knife-veto) is dormant on neutral data, not architecturally firewalled (§4-5). **Is there ANY defensible definition of "narrative signal" in this codebase that is neither price momentum relabeled nor a display-only text panel? If not, should "narrative" be reserved exclusively for the text pipeline — and what does a controlled, forward-graded experiment that lets ONE news feature bind a small, capped weight look like, so the qual→quant claim is tested rather than perpetually deferred?**
