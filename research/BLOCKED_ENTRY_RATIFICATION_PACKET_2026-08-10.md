## §3 Honest weaknesses (read before ratifying)

1. **Episode honest-N is the true uncertainty:** 8 independent regimes at 25% (14 at 30%); the
   episode-clustered CI [0.305, 2.196] is wide. The premium is real across every robustness cut we
   could construct, but its magnitude is uncertain by ~3×.
2. **Mixture disclosure:** baskets map only 12.5% of names; 53.4% of the cell comes through the
   GICS-sector fallback. Checked: basket-only and sector-only arms BOTH pass independently
   (+1.10R / +1.14R @25%) — not a mixture artifact, but the thematic mechanism that admits the
   exemplars contributes a minority of the statistics.
3. **Survivorship, measured but weakly:** dead-name arm correction −0.048R (−11% relative) on a
   store that is mostly index-exit acquisitions (103 acq vs 14 bankruptcies; price→0 tail nearly
   unrepresented; non-index micro-cap deaths unmeasured). LEVELS remain optimistic; the
   cell-vs-complement CONTRAST is the robust quantity.
4. **CN/HK out of scope:** no US peer sets for 9988/600547/002716 — the CN/HK arm needs its own
   basket-peer construction + prereg before any live change there.
5. **Stop-outs dominate trade count at every setting** (median R = −1.000 exactly): this pays as a
   portfolio of stop-bounded entries; roughly 2 of 3 trades lose 1R even inside the cell.
