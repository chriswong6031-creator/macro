## 4. What the blinded map added

The blinded map was materially additive. Its strongest constructions not already identical to the frozen SOL map are:

1. **T+1 inventory-vintage stack:** estimate legally sellable cohorts, cost bases, and supply released at each unlock rather than merely enforcing T+1 on exits.
2. **Two-sided band traversal:** model upper/lower touches and their path order as forced-transfer and disagreement states; daily bars that touch both remain `path_order_unknown`.
3. **Immutable cadence grammar:** distinguish `B-N-B`, `B-R-B`, `F-N-B`, and partial-band sequences rather than reducing trajectory to consecutive-board count.
4. **Unfillable-leader spectator substitution:** keep a locked leader as context with a cash-zero return while building the tradable book from eligible followers.
5. **Post-close fixed-price execution:** treat the post-2026-07-06 fixed-price venue as a distinct entry clock, never as the 15:00 close.
6. **Observability-selection model:** measure coverage propensity and overlap sensitivity rather than merely warning that the local universe covers 36.09% of canonicalized vendor-pool names and 38.27% of its event rows.

Its operational additions are an N0→N9 fill funnel, separate `p_path` and `p_fill` calibration with covariance, and explicit path ambiguity for daily bars that touch both bands.
