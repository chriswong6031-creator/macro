# A-share limit-move research — universe expansion and microstructure data decision

**Decision date:** 2026-08-08
**Authority:** research/data-procurement decision only; no trading or Prophet/Neural Web authority
**Bottom line:** expand the point-in-time daily security spine now; use the 10,000-point TuShare tier, historical A-share minute data, and the inexpensive recent/forward pre-market share feed; buy the auction add-on only after its historical endpoint scope is confirmed, and pilot before buying Level-2.

## 1. Decision

The present 1,842-name nominal-OHLCV store is a curated large/mid-cap research slice, not the A-share market. It is adequate for constructing and debugging hypotheses, but not for an all-market limit-up/limit-down verdict. Full point-in-time daily coverage is a prerequisite, not an optional model enhancement.

The recommended sequence is:

1. Restore the TuShare credential and move to **10,000 points**, not 15,000.
2. Build an effective-dated full-market security/session/rule spine and nominal daily panel from TuShare regular endpoints.
3. Accrue the TuShare 打板 endpoints and the 2018+ modeled chip-distribution endpoints already covered at 10,000 points.
4. Add the recent/forward pre-market share-capital feed, then separately license historical minute and auction data, subject to confirmation of exact history and commercial-use rights.
5. Run an ablation ladder: daily-only → derived 打板 metadata → auction/minute → Level-2.
6. Buy historical Level-2 only if a schema-complete sample materially improves locked-holdout probability, fill, or execution measurements.

This modifies the proposed `TuShare → Wind/Level-2 → Yahoo` architecture in one important way: complete the cheap point-in-time denominator before adding an expensive tape. Yahoo/free feeds remain fallbacks and cross-checks, never the authority for exchange sessions, risk-warning identity, IPO no-limit windows, official bands, halts, or queue execution.

## 3. What “100% of A-shares” must mean

It cannot mean “download today's active tickers.” The research denominator must include:

- every SSE, SZSE, and BSE common-equity identity with listing, daily halt/resumption, paused-listing, relisting, code-change, and delisting intervals;
- `stock_basic` statuses `L`, `D`, `P`, and `G`—listed, delisted, paused-listing, and approved-but-not-yet-traded—plus a separate daily halt/resumption plane;
- an authoritative exchange-session calendar, with a missing security row represented as a halt/no-trade state rather than skipped to the next observed row;
- unadjusted nominal OHLCV and amount, plus a separate adjustment-factor/corporate-action plane for features;
- exchange-validated daily upper/lower prices and an explicit `no_daily_band` state;
- effective-dated risk-warning/ST identity and band rules;
- board and rule eras, including STAR, the 2020-08-24 ChiNext reform, 2023-04-10 main-board registration listings, and the 2026-07-06 risk-warning and post-close changes;
- point-in-time float, share-count, unlock, and suspension states;
- BSE in the data spine but in a separate 30%-band/liquidity model, never pooled with the main-board primary model.

The existing per-ticker parquet-in-Git layout must not simply grow from 1,842 to more than 5,500 files: nightly whole-file rewrites scale poorly and create unnecessary Git history. The full panel belongs in monthly/yearly Parquet partitions in object storage, with only schemas, manifests, hashes, and compact research receipts committed.

### 4.2 Add-ons: buy historical minute and pre-market shares; confirm auction scope first

Points do not unlock the independently licensed feeds. TuShare's table lists:

- historical 1/5/15/30/60-minute A-share data from 2009 at RMB 2,000/year for individuals;
- pre-market daily share capital at RMB 500/year, with only a rolling approximately two-year history;
- same-day opening auction at RMB 500/year for individuals.

**Historical A-share minute data is the clear first add-on.** It is the cheapest layer that can measure minute first-touch, break/reseal cadence, and a non-circular post-auction execution proxy. **Pre-market share capital is also worth the small fee** for recent and forward work: `stk_premarket` is trade-date-keyed and makes total/float share, prior close, and vendor-reported band information available before the open; those bands must still be exchange-validated. It is not the long-horizon PIT solution—the published history is only about two years, while regular `daily_basic`, `bak_basic`, corporate-action, and unlock endpoints remain necessary for older periods.

The documentation separately exposes `stk_auction_o` and `stk_auction_c`, each returning collapsed full-market opening/closing-auction OHLCV, amount, and VWAP, but describes them as separately permissioned. Before payment, TuShare must confirm in writing whether the RMB 500 entitlement includes both historical endpoints and their full available date range, or only the same-day `stk_auction` endpoint.

## 5. Level-2: useful, but only after a paid-data gauntlet

The proposed wall-behavior thesis is mechanistically credible: a static closing wall is a stock, while replenishment, depletion, cancellations, absorption, break/reseal cadence, and auction imbalance are flows. Chinese order-book research also finds that depth and order intensity react differently to liquidity shocks and that resiliency can be measured over order-book updates. That supports testing dynamic wall behavior; it does not establish profitable next-day continuation.

### 5.2 Do not buy a full history first

Request a no-cost schema/sample for at least ten deliberately chosen sessions, then a bounded paid pilot. The sample must include:

- early lock, late lock, one-price lock, multiple break/reseal, failed seal, and down-limit release cases;
- both SSE and SZSE, main and 20%-band boards;
- at least one high-breadth and one low-breadth session;
- sessions before and after 2026-07-06;
- complete non-event controls matched on board, cap, turnover, gap, and sector heat.

Reject the vendor before purchase if the sample cannot prove:

1. exact exchange timestamps, channel sequence, and missing-message detection;
2. add/cancel/trade event typing and stable order IDs or documented exchange-specific limitations;
3. official band and security-status files;
4. auction indicative price plus matched/unmatched quantity, not merely the final auction print;
5. order-queue and top-of-book depth adequate to reconstruct the wall;
6. full-file coverage manifests and correction policy;
7. legal internal-model, derived-feature, retention, and commercial-use rights;
8. feasible transfer, storage, and annual refresh costs.

The paid pilot should then cover either six complete months or every limit/failed-limit candidate plus matched controls over 2024 onward, depending on whether the vendor can filter licensed files by name. Only buy the full 2021+ order-by-order history after the pilot's incremental ablation clears the research gate.

## 6. Dedicated A-share microstructure engine

Raw Level-2 should land as an immutable event plane, not as hundreds of directly tuned columns. A normalized event should preserve exchange, security, session, timestamp, channel, sequence, event type, order/trade identifiers, side, price, quantity, and source/version provenance.

The first stable feature registry should contain roughly 50–100 features in eight preregistered families:

1. **Seal lifecycle:** first touch, final seal, touch count, break count, reseal latency, locked-time share, close state.
2. **Wall state:** wall/free-float, wall/ADV, queue-ahead estimate, depth slope and concentration.
3. **Wall dynamics:** depletion, replenishment, cancellation/pull rate, recovery half-life, absorption under sell waves.
4. **Auction:** indicative-price path, matched/unmatched imbalance, imbalance acceleration, opening/closing-auction strengthening.
5. **Order/trade flow:** signed flow, aggressor imbalance, large-order flow, flow around touch/break, quote bursts.
6. **Fillability:** queue conversion, observed prints at the band, partial-fill proxy, first executable post-auction interval.
7. **Ecology:** sector diffusion, leader/follower timing, ladder topology, failed-leader redistribution, down-limit breadth.
8. **Inventory/supply:** turnover before seal, float/unlocks, T+1 sellable vintages, volume-at-price supply.

Every feature must carry `known_at`, board/rule era, missingness state, source hash, and a construction ID. Static wall size is a baseline comparator, not a presumed weak feature. The dynamic families earn priority because they explain whether displayed demand survives adverse flow, but their incremental value must be measured rather than narrated.

## 7. Purchase earns its keep through ablation

The locked evaluation ladder is:

1. daily nominal bars + official limit prices + point-in-time identity;
2. add TuShare `limit_list_d`/`kpl_list`/ladder/theme fields;
3. add `cyq_perf`/`cyq_chips` as separately identified vendor-modeled supply features;
4. add opening/closing auction and 1-minute execution features;
5. add Level-2 snapshots only;
6. add tick trades;
7. add tick orders/queues and dynamic-wall families.

Each step must report:

- added eligible dates/names and all missingness transitions;
- continuation/onset Brier and log loss versus board/count/era ladders;
- date-weighted top-K precision and calibration;
- fill/no-fill/queue classification and observed execution slippage;
- all-candidate cash-book return at the frozen cost grid;
- month/date-block intervals and regime stability;
- storage, compute, licensing, and human-maintenance cost.

Level-2 does not earn a broader purchase because a feature is intuitive. It earns it only if the order/queue layer adds stable locked-holdout information or materially corrects false daily/minute fills after simpler layers are present.

## 8. ORE ledger / untested variants

This decision does not claim that small cap, wall replenishment, auction imbalance, or any particular vendor field is profitable. Open ore includes:

- exact point-in-time event incidence and returns by free-float cap, liquidity, and listing age;
- delisted-name and historical ST coverage;
- pre-2016 ST membership, which TuShare's daily `stock_st` history cannot backfill;
- BSE 30%-band behavior;
- KPL/THS field availability dates, null rates, revisions, and commercial rights;
- chip-model definition drift and incremental value over independently computed volume-at-price supply;
- whether the separate auction entitlement includes both historical open and close feeds;
- one-minute versus tick-level first-executable-price error;
- exchange-specific order-ID and cancellation comparability before 2021-04-26;
- dynamic-wall incremental lift over static wall size;
- Level-2 capacity/fill modeling under real order size;
- derived-feature publication rights under the selected license.

No daily-slice null kills those families, and no vendor purchase promotes them.
