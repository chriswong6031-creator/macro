## 1. Executive ruling

The Market Ontology program is not complete when it has enough engines, contracts,
artifacts or pages. It is complete only when lawful capabilities become a coherent,
premium research product.

The Macro & Monetary suite contains the twelve closed F01 workspaces plus the two
Chairman-authorized beyond-F01 expansions `rates_curves` and `trade_flows`, fourteen
total at the reviewed estate. Their snapshots, composers, manifests, nightly lane,
tests and static pages are valuable backbone. They are not a finished product
experience. Their present direct-route, backend-first shape demonstrates the failure
this law exists to prevent: valid data and careful diagnostics can still produce a
surface that ordinary users cannot discover, orient within or use to finish a
research task. The exact workspace identities remain owned by
`engine/market_os/macro_workspaces/registry.py`, not by this document's count.

From this ruling forward:

1. **Backend completion and product completion are distinct capability states.**
2. **A producer, schema, artifact, registry row, test suite or technical page does
   not earn an admitted product route by existing.**
3. **Product surfaces are user workflows, not mirrors of backend modules.**
4. **F01–F13 are capability owners and contextual lenses, not thirteen equal
   navigation destinations or thirteen independent mini-products.**
5. **A real user or machine consumer must complete the promised task through the
   real path before a capability is accepted.**
6. **Exact sources, clocks, calculations and receipts remain inspectable, but
   ordinary users are not forced to read implementation structure before they
   understand the decision.**
7. **A builder may implement only the product surface frozen in its handoff. It
   may not infer product architecture from a JSON schema or expose every available
   field because the field exists.**
8. **No product claim advances market, causal, forecast, rank, sizing, trade,
   compliance or rights authority beyond its canonical owner and tested ceiling.**

The capability vocabulary remains:

`PROVEN_LIVE`, `BUILT_NOT_PROVEN`, `PARTIAL`, `DARK_OR_DISCONNECTED`,
`BROKEN`, `SPEC_ONLY`, `NOT_BUILT`, `REJECTED_BY_DESIGN`.

A merged page without live proof is `BUILT_NOT_PROVEN`. An architecture record is
`SPEC_ONLY`. A working producer without a useful consumer is `PARTIAL` or
`DARK_OR_DISCONNECTED`. Green CI is evidence, not user acceptance. These are
current-evidence classifications, not an irreversible promotion ladder: a previously
live capability can become degraded or broken. Composition approval is a design gate,
not another capability-state enum, and source can be built before it is merged.

## 2. One product, composed from many canonical owners

The program carries a researcher through this conceptual workflow spine:

```text
development / event / market state
  -> exact evidence, identity and clocks
  -> transmission and affected entities
  -> security context and financial consequences
  -> thesis, assumptions, valuation, scenario or expression
  -> portfolio impact and monitoring
  -> revision, outcome and evaluation
```

This is a product composition, **not a new schema, graph, store or authority plane**.
Each noun above continues to be owned by its accepted system.

The six experience families below are composition lenses, not top-level navigation:

1. **Research Home / Orientation**  
   What changed, what deserves attention, what is stale or unknown, and where the
   researcher should continue.

2. **Explore / Market Map**  
   Macro, policy, geopolitical, event, capital and material developments traced
   through direct and second-order relationships with visible evidence and
   uncertainty.

3. **Security Workspace**  
   One resolved issuer/security/listing with current state, changes, events,
   financials, valuation, ownership/capital/options context, evidence and open
   research.

4. **Thesis / Scenario / Expression**  
   Human-authored thesis versions, assumptions, valuation cases, scenarios,
   expression research, risks and what would change the view.

5. **Portfolio / Monitor**  
   Holdings and watchlists, exposures, affected positions, conditions, alerts,
   change history and follow-up.

6. **Research Library / Team / Programmatic Access**  
   Saved research, studies, decisions, evidence, collaboration, governed export,
   API and administration.

## 3. Public-route admission uses the existing page registry

`data/product_experience/page_registry.json` remains the sole cross-repository route
census. `config/product_experience/page_registry_overrides.yml` remains its sole
hand-maintained judgment overlay. This law does not create a second registry.

**Scope is prospective and proportionate.** Full admission below applies to new or
materially recomposed Market Ontology workflows and to an explicit new claim that a
previously held workflow is product-complete. It does not retroactively shut down
existing routes, impose a fleet-wide owner backfill as a prerequisite to unrelated
work, or force the full admission process for maintenance under §7.1. Inherited
registry omissions and unassigned fields remain visible migration debt; they do not
become fabricated ownership or evidence of product completion.

A census is authoritative as the designated inventory, not infallible as a snapshot.
Before an affected-route admission decision, the implementing owner must reconcile
actual rendered routes, source templates/builders and the current registry. A missing
row is **not** proof that the page does not exist. A stale generation is not proof
of current coverage. The independent review of this candidate found that the
fourteen Macro & Monetary pages were omitted from the then-current census; §9
therefore requires their inclusion before suite admission.

Use the existing generator `scripts/build_product_page_registry.py` with an explicit
`--as-of <ISO8601>` and the required source checkouts/refs for a reproducible rebuild.
Record the actual source SHAs and run its existing `--check` and derivation/schema
checks. Preserve unrelated rows and source-availability facts; do not turn unavailable
sister-repository input into a silent deletion. Repair an affected derivation gap in
the existing generator if necessary, never invent a row's derived fields in YAML or
hand-edit the generated JSON. This documents the product vertical's requirement;
it does not regenerate the registry in this records-only PR.

Judgment fields, including `owner`, `primary_user_question`, priority and archetype,
are written only through `config/product_experience/page_registry_overrides.yml`,
with evidence identifying the real responsibility. The canonical domain owner
supplies the ownership fact; the current action-authoritative F00/Sol integration
owner resolves cross-lane ambiguity through the existing carrier and §10. A builder
may record that explicit assignment, not assign itself by inference. Unknown ownership
stays `unowned` and holds the affected new admission, not the entire legacy estate.
No assignment here transfers runtime, data, release or another owner's authority.

### 3.2 Admission evidence

A route can exist without being admitted as a primary product workflow. Every proposed
new or materially recomposed Market Ontology route or route family must have current
evidence for the registry's existing fields:

- stable `page_id`, `repo`, `route`, `route_kind`, source template and builder;
- truthful `access_shell` and `payload_tier`;
- a non-`none` `nav_family` for an admitted primary workflow;
- a named, current `archetype`;
- a non-empty `primary_user_question`;
- a named canonical `owner` assigned through §3.1;
- truthful `lifecycle`, `generated`, `bilingual`, `themes` and `locales`;
- governing `known_contracts`, exact `source_evidence` and material caveats.

Until a future reviewed registry version carries richer admission fields, the
route's PR or companion acceptance record must also name:

- primary persona and observable user job;
- complete entry-to-outcome journey;
- real producer and real human or machine consumer;
- loading, valid-empty, warmup, stale, partial, unavailable, rights-blocked,
  unauthorized, correction, replay and error behavior where relevant;
- supported breakpoints, themes, locales and accessibility evidence;
- instrumentation for task start, reached value and failure;
- production proof reference and truthful capability state.

The affected route is held from primary workflow admission when any of these is true:

- `nav_family=none`, unless the route is explicitly a direct-only lab, reference,
  inspector or fallback and is not presented as a discoverable workflow;
- empty primary user question or `owner=unowned`;
- no real consumer or no next action;
- no applicable browser/production proof;
- design-system or archetype noncompliance;
- unsupported locale/theme/breakpoint claims;
- a default view dominated by schemas, formulas, clocks, health diagnostics,
  owner wiring or implementation state;
- a promised action has no real service or accepted continuation;
- the route exists only because a backend module or artifact exists.

A candidate may be built, reviewed and merged as `BUILT_NOT_PROVEN` before the
production acceptance receipt exists, subject to its own release law. Candidate
construction is not final product admission. `lifecycle=live` records route lifecycle;
it does not by itself mean the product job is useful, current, admitted or
`PROVEN_LIVE`.

## 4. Disclosure depth within GLANCE / HOVER / STUDY

The **only customer copy tiers** remain Doctrine §1's **Tier 1 GLANCE, Tier 2 HOVER,
Tier 3 STUDY**. The four levels below describe analytical depth, not another tier
numbering, component system or permission to exceed existing budgets.

| Analytical depth | Canonical presentation home |
|---|---|
| Level 1: decision surface | Tier 1 GLANCE; Doctrine title/subtitle/row/footer limits remain unchanged |
| Level 2: analytical drivers | Brief definitions or receipts may use Tier 2 HOVER, within its approximately 80-word limit; full comparisons, scenario ranges, histories and analogs use Tier 3 STUDY tabs/detail views |
| Level 3: audit and evidence | Tier 3 STUDY evidence/method views; a concise claim-level receipt may use Tier 2 HOVER, not a full audit panel inside a tooltip |
| Level 4: producer/operator inspection | Authorized operator surfaces outside the customer copy-tier system; explicitly permitted customer technical detail uses Tier 3 STUDY, never a new global tier |

Design System §9 rules 9–10 govern drawers, tabs and table placement. Detail views
still have a GLANCE-compliant top header. Essential limitations remain plain words
at the point of use; disclosure must not make a misleading claim appear clean.

### Level 1 — decision surface, default

The ordinary user sees:

- what is happening now;
- what changed;
- why it matters, with a plain-language evidence/authority distinction;
- what is affected;
- compact freshness, coverage and uncertainty;
- one to three real next actions.

### Level 2 — analytical drivers

The user can open:

- component contributions;
- comparisons and contradictions;
- transmission paths;
- scenario/sensitivity ranges;
- historical context and analogs;
- related catalysts, risks and what would change the view.

### Level 3 — audit and evidence

The user can inspect:

- exact source and evidence records;
- observation, release, availability, known-at, collection, revision, calculation
  and render clocks;
- assumptions, units, currency/share basis and exclusions;
- calculation definitions and method versions;
- correction and version history;
- limitations, rights and ownership.

### Level 4 — producer/operator inspection

Explicit technical inspection can expose, within existing access and disclosure rules:

- schema fields and contract versions;
- parser/collector state;
- pipeline and component health;
- raw diagnostics, coverage matrices and execution logs;
- unresolved owner wiring and internal receipts.

Level 4 is never the default customer product experience. Operator records and private
logs do not become public merely because a drawer is labelled “Technical details.”
Existing public-internals and access boundaries continue to govern what may be shown.

Progressive disclosure may hide complexity, but it may not alter truth. The same
canonical fact, null, correction and authority ceiling must survive every level.

## 5. Screen-composition and interaction law

The global Design System and route archetype set the exact module budget. **The
archetype budget wins on conflict.** The list below is a set of composition functions,
not six mandatory panels or a minimum card count: a monitor's four-module budget
and a utility's one-card focus remain intact. For a primary workflow, the composition
normally includes:

- one page orientation block below the existing global header;
- a compact decision brief containing **State / What changed / Why it matters /
  Next action**;
- one dominant work object, visualization or record;
- a small number of decision cards with clear hierarchy;
- one compact evidence/freshness affordance;
- contextual continuation into a related workflow.

Large equal-weight card grids, giant filter walls and full diagnostic matrices are
not acceptable as the first experience. A user should not need internal filenames,
schema vocabulary or owner knowledge to orient.

Additional rules:

1. **One question per viewport.** The first viewport answers the route's registered
   primary user question before methodology or diagnostics.
2. **One dominant object.** Competing charts/tables cannot all be primary.
3. **Nesting depth follows the Design System.** Do not solve density with boxes
   inside boxes.
4. **Actions must be real.** A dead tab, speculative button, unimplemented alert,
   fake save or invented scenario service is a release defect.
5. **Heavy evidence is lazy.** Do not eagerly load every workspace, country shard,
   study artifact or diagnostic payload for a bounded first view.
6. **Mobile is a composition, not a shrink.** Preserve the primary question,
   hierarchy, next action, focus order, 44px targets and intended local overflow
   only inside explicit chart/table containers.
7. **Bilingual parity is semantic.** English and Chinese must carry the same
   state, authority, action and failure meaning; untranslated enum tokens are not
   acceptable product copy.
8. **Dark and light are separate art directions of one system**, governed by the
   Design System rather than page-local themes.
9. **Only the two existing global header families are permitted.** Product pages
   use `templates/_site_nav.html.j2` and its `_navlinks.html.j2` inventory;
   anonymous/corporate pages use `templates/_public_nav.html.j2`. Suite navigation
   is page-local chapter navigation below the appropriate family, not a third
   header or a page-local restyling of global geometry.

A designed view-model or BFF may reorder and compress canonical owner output for the
task. It may not reimplement producer math, silently fill nulls or create a page-local
source of truth.

## 6. Data, time, null, correction and authority behavior

Every admitted workflow states which clocks matter. The default view shows the one
effective “as of” and a plain-language freshness/coverage state; Level 3 preserves
the full clock set.

Binding behavior:

- missing is never zero;
- warmup/first print is not unchanged;
- last successful output after a newer failed attempt is degraded, not current;
- partial coverage names included, excluded and unknown denominators;
- rights-blocked is distinct from source failure;
- unavailable is distinct from valid empty;
- a corrected source preserves prior-known history and identifies the correction;
- replay is idempotent and does not duplicate an event, alert, study or version;
- an unknown modifying effect stays unknown until its canonical owner reconciles it;
- model prose never supplies an exact missing fact, identity, price, causal link,
  rank, size, trade or rights decision.

Every visible interpretation identifies its class. The contract/evidence vocabulary
and its possible plain-language presentation are different layers:

| Exact analytical class | Plain-language presentation, adapted to the actual claim |
|---|---|
| deterministic fact or calculation | Reported fact / Calculated from these inputs |
| descriptive context | Current context |
| mechanism-supported interpretation | How this could matter |
| historical association | What happened in similar past cases |
| calibrated forecast | Forecast, with its applicable uncertainty and limits |
| human-authored assumption or thesis | Your assumption / Your view |
| model-generated explanation/hypothesis | AI explanation / AI hypothesis |

These examples do not authorize a forecast or a causal claim and do not prescribe
extra chips on every row. Preserve exact classes in contracts and evidence; translate
them into GLANCE-compliant words at the claim. Do not print technical classification
enums as first-viewport copy. Use “what would change the view” or “risks and what
we're watching” for customer conditions, not internal falsifier/refutation vocabulary.
The classes may coexist. They may not be visually or semantically collapsed.

## 7. Builder authority and handoff contract

Every bounded Market Ontology commission declares exactly one:

```text
PUBLIC_PRODUCT_SURFACE: NONE
```

or

```text
PUBLIC_PRODUCT_SURFACE: <one named admitted or candidate workflow>
```

`PUBLIC_PRODUCT_SURFACE: NONE` permits contracts, producers, ingestion, artifacts,
tests, machine projections and operator inspection within the commission's existing
authority. It does not permit a new customer route merely because the backend is ready.
An operator-only interface remains subject to applicable design, access and proof
requirements; `NONE` is not an exemption from correctness or security.

### 7.1 Proportionate change classification

The handoff or PR states whether it is **MAINTENANCE** or a **new/materially recomposed
product surface**, and identifies the affected contract and existing proof. This is
human-readable scope/evidence classification, not a new runtime lifecycle or gate store.

MAINTENANCE preserves the registered primary user question, information hierarchy,
actions, data/authority classes, access and privacy boundaries, and promised time/null/
correction behavior. Examples include a faithful translation correction or a local
presentation defect fixed without changing the workflow. It names the existing surface,
reuses its accepted composition/contract, and owes only affected regression evidence,
current required CI and the applicable live readback. A copy fix does not commission a
new BFF, a new design packet, a new telemetry system or a complete flagship screenshot
matrix. Prior proof is reusable only with its source identity and an explicit account
of why unchanged paths, behavior and current integration leave it applicable.

Small diff size is not a safety exemption. Changes to numerical meaning, rights,
security, authorization, privacy, identity, freshness, null/correction behavior or
user actions require the corresponding owner review and discriminating proof even
when one line changes. A material workflow/hierarchy change takes the full product
path below. An in-scope correctness repair may use a focused evidence set only when
that set actually proves the affected promised behavior; it may not hide a contract
change as cosmetic maintenance.

### 7.2 New or materially recomposed surfaces

For a new or materially recomposed named public surface, the handoff contains:

- observable mission and why it matters;
- authority precedence and current estate;
- exact scope, repository and path ceiling;
- explicit non-goals;
- primary persona and complete entry-to-outcome journey;
- real-data desktop/tablet/mobile reference compositions;
- information hierarchy and actions;
- view-model/BFF contract where the workflow uses one;
- data/time/null/correction/rights behavior;
- deterministic, statistical, human-authored and model-generated boundaries;
- all relevant failure and unauthorized states;
- instrumentation through the existing owner;
- acceptance tests, browser evidence and real production proof;
- stop condition and continuation handoff.

Backend specialists do not invent product architecture. Frontend specialists do not
invent analytical semantics. The Fable/Sol principal freezes the product and owner
boundaries, then routes bounded implementation to the least-scarce capable worker.

## 8. Product acceptance law

A new or materially recomposed user-facing workflow is accepted only when the
applicable evidence below binds the immutable candidate and current integration.
For MAINTENANCE, §7.1 controls the affected subset and justified proof reuse; omission
of an irrelevant state is explained by the contract, not silently waived. The real
consumer requirement is human **or** machine as commissioned, both only when the
mission promises both. It does not manufacture a machine consumer for a utility page.

1. real entry from navigation or a real preceding workflow;
2. real production-shaped data, not fixtures alone;
3. the primary task is understandable without internal architecture knowledge;
4. complete, loading, warmup, valid-empty, stale, partial, unavailable,
   rights-blocked, unauthorized, correction, replay and error states where applicable;
5. exact evidence and clocks remain reachable;
6. keyboard, focus, screen-reader basics, reduced motion, contrast and mobile target
   evidence;
7. English/Chinese and dark/light parity where claimed;
8. no dead destination or unsupported action;
9. browser proof at relevant desktop, tablet and mobile widths;
10. bounded network/performance behavior;
11. task-start, reached-value and failure instrumentation without private free text;
12. production deployment receipt and live-path readback when release owes it;
13. a named human or machine capability that now exists end to end.

Existing telemetry, publication, auth and owner-native evidence are reused. A future
cross-suite health or tenancy enhancement does not automatically become a prerequisite
to every otherwise complete vertical; only a demonstrated dependency in that vertical's
contract gates it. For example, team sharing requires its tenant authorization proof,
whereas existing per-user isolation is tested through its current canonical owner.

Green CI, merge, deployment and production acceptance are separate facts. Architecture
records can be accepted as `SPEC_ONLY`. A merged implementation without live proof is
`BUILT_NOT_PROVEN`. A technically present but undiscoverable or unusable feature is
not accepted merely because its route responds. Independent review findings must be
resolved under their actual severity and release law; no adjective substitutes for
evidence that a blocker is closed.

Independent review must ask:

- Can the primary persona complete the primary task?
- Does the first viewport answer the registered question?
- Is backend structure being presented as product?
- Is any claimed action dead or unsupported?
- Are missing, stale, corrected and rights-blocked states honest?
- Did the worker create a duplicate owner or page-local truth?
- What real capability exists now that did not exist before?
- Is the result visible on the real production path?

## 10. Cross-lane navigation and shared-path ownership

Multiple lanes need `_navlinks.html.j2`, `_public_nav.html.j2`, Caddy/public access,
route registry, shared build and product-shell paths. Shared files are not
first-come-first-served, and a file appearing in several PRs is not by itself proof
that their exact hunks or semantics conflict.

Before a child writes a shared path, it must:

1. fresh-census open PRs, branches, worktrees and current main;
2. identify the exact owning hunk and semantic dependency;
3. preserve immutable candidate work from other lanes;
