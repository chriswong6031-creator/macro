## 1. Executive ruling

The Market Ontology program is not complete when it has enough engines, contracts,
artifacts or pages. It is complete only when lawful capabilities become a coherent,
premium research product.

The fourteen F01 Macro & Monetary workspace snapshots, composers, manifests, nightly
lane, tests and static pages are valuable backbone. They are not a finished product
experience. Their present direct-route, backend-first shape demonstrates the failure
this law exists to prevent: valid data and careful diagnostics can still produce a
surface that ordinary users cannot discover, orient within or use to finish a
research task.

From this ruling forward:

1. **Backend completion and product completion are distinct capability states.**
2. **A producer, schema, artifact, registry row, test suite or technical page does
   not earn an admitted product route by existing.**
3. **Product surfaces are user workflows, not mirrors of backend modules.**
4. **F01–F13 are capability owners and contextual lenses, not thirteen equal
   navigation destinations or thirteen independent mini-products.**
5. **A real user or machine consumer must complete the promised task through the
   real path before a capability is accepted.**
6. **Exact sources, clocks, calculations and receipts remain inspectable, but
   ordinary users are not forced to read implementation structure before they
   understand the decision.**
7. **A builder may implement only the product surface frozen in its handoff. It
   may not infer product architecture from a JSON schema or expose every available
   field because the field exists.**
8. **No product claim advances market, causal, forecast, rank, sizing, trade,
   compliance or rights authority beyond its canonical owner and tested ceiling.**

The capability vocabulary remains:

`PROVEN_LIVE`, `BUILT_NOT_PROVEN`, `PARTIAL`, `DARK_OR_DISCONNECTED`,
`BROKEN`, `SPEC_ONLY`, `NOT_BUILT`, `REJECTED_BY_DESIGN`.

A merged page is usually `BUILT_NOT_PROVEN`. An architecture record is `SPEC_ONLY`.
A working producer without a useful consumer is `PARTIAL` or `DARK_OR_DISCONNECTED`.
Green CI is evidence, not user acceptance.

## 2. One product, composed from many canonical owners

The program carries a researcher through this conceptual workflow spine:

```text
development / event / market state
  -> exact evidence, identity and clocks
  -> transmission and affected entities
  -> security context and financial consequences
  -> thesis, assumptions, valuation, scenario or expression
  -> portfolio impact and monitoring
  -> revision, outcome and evaluation
```

This is a product composition, **not a new schema, graph, store or authority plane**.
Each noun above continues to be owned by its accepted system.

The six primary experience families are:

1. **Research Home / Orientation**  
   What changed, what deserves attention, what is stale or unknown, and where the
   researcher should continue.

2. **Explore / Market Map**  
   Macro, policy, geopolitical, event, capital and material developments traced
   through direct and second-order relationships with visible evidence and
   uncertainty.

3. **Security Workspace**  
   One resolved issuer/security/listing with current state, changes, events,
   financials, valuation, ownership/capital/options context, evidence and open
   research.

4. **Thesis / Scenario / Expression**  
   Human-authored thesis versions, assumptions, valuation cases, scenarios,
   expression research, risks and invalidation conditions.

5. **Portfolio / Monitor**  
   Holdings and watchlists, exposures, affected positions, conditions, alerts,
   change history and follow-up.

6. **Research Library / Team / Programmatic Access**  
   Saved research, studies, decisions, evidence, collaboration, governed export,
   API and administration.

The families describe journeys, not a mandate to create six new routes or six new
stores. Existing Macro, Terminal and other product routes are extended through their
canonical owners. Public orientation can lawfully live on the Macro site when that
is its accepted host. Authenticated interactive/private workflows can lawfully live
in Terminal or another accepted host. A lane must not create a second surface merely
because another repository is more convenient.

## 3. Public-route admission uses the existing page registry

`data/product_experience/page_registry.json` remains the sole cross-repository route
census. `config/product_experience/page_registry_overrides.yml` remains its sole
hand-maintained judgment overlay. This law does not create a second registry.

A route can exist without being admitted as a primary product workflow. Every proposed
Market Ontology route or route family must have current evidence for the registry's
existing fields:

- stable `page_id`, `repo`, `route`, `route_kind`, source template and builder;
- truthful `access_shell` and `payload_tier`;
- a non-`none` `nav_family` for an admitted primary workflow;
- a named, current `archetype`;
- a non-empty `primary_user_question`;
- a named canonical `owner`;
- truthful `lifecycle`, `generated`, `bilingual`, `themes` and `locales`;
- governing `known_contracts`, exact `source_evidence` and material caveats.

Until a future reviewed registry version carries richer admission fields, the
route's PR or companion acceptance record must also name:

- primary persona and observable user job;
- complete entry-to-outcome journey;
- real producer and real human or machine consumer;
- loading, valid-empty, warmup, stale, partial, unavailable, rights-blocked,
  unauthorized, correction, replay and error behavior;
- supported breakpoints, themes, locales and accessibility evidence;
- instrumentation for task start, reached value and failure;
- production proof reference and truthful capability state.

A route is held from primary workflow admission when any of these is true:

- `nav_family=none`, unless the route is explicitly a direct-only lab, reference,
  inspector or fallback and is not presented as a discoverable workflow;
- empty primary user question or `owner=unowned`;
- no real consumer or no next action;
- no browser/production proof;
- design-system or archetype noncompliance;
- unsupported locale/theme/breakpoint claims;
- a default view dominated by schemas, formulas, clocks, health diagnostics,
  owner wiring or implementation state;
- a promised action has no real service or accepted continuation;
- the route exists only because a backend module or artifact exists.

`lifecycle=live` means the route exists. It does not by itself mean the product job
is useful, current, admitted or `PROVEN_LIVE`.

## 4. Four levels of progressive disclosure

### Level 1 — decision surface, default

The ordinary user sees:

- what is happening now;
- what changed;
- why it matters, with its evidence/authority class;
- what is affected;
- compact freshness, coverage and uncertainty;
- one to three real next actions.

### Level 2 — analytical drivers

The user can open:

- component contributions;
- comparisons and contradictions;
- transmission paths;
- scenario/sensitivity ranges;
- historical context and analogs;
- related catalysts, risks and invalidators.

### Level 3 — audit and evidence

The user can inspect:

- exact source and evidence records;
- observation, release, availability, known-at, collection, revision, calculation
  and render clocks;
- assumptions, units, currency/share basis and exclusions;
- calculation definitions and method versions;
- correction and version history;
- limitations, rights and ownership.

### Level 4 — producer/operator inspection

Explicit technical inspection can expose:

- schema fields and contract versions;
- parser/collector state;
- pipeline and component health;
- raw diagnostics, coverage matrices and execution logs;
- unresolved owner wiring and internal receipts.

Level 4 is never the default customer product experience. It belongs in operator
surfaces, staff inspection, evidence drawers or an explicit “Technical details”
mode.

Progressive disclosure may hide complexity, but it may not alter truth. The same
canonical fact, null, correction and authority ceiling must survive every level.

## 5. Screen-composition and interaction law

The global Design System and route archetype set the exact module budget. For a
primary workflow, the default composition normally includes:

- one orientation header;
- a compact decision brief containing **State / What changed / Why it matters /
  Next action**;
- one dominant work object, visualization or record;
- a small number of decision cards with clear hierarchy;
- one compact evidence/freshness affordance;
- contextual continuation into a related workflow.

Large equal-weight card grids, giant filter walls and full diagnostic matrices are
not acceptable as the first experience. A user should not need internal filenames,
schema vocabulary or owner knowledge to orient.

Additional rules:

1. **One question per viewport.** The first viewport answers the route's registered
   primary user question before methodology or diagnostics.
2. **One dominant object.** Competing charts/tables cannot all be primary.
3. **Nesting depth follows the Design System.** Do not solve density with boxes
   inside boxes.
4. **Actions must be real.** A dead tab, speculative button, unimplemented alert,
   fake save or invented scenario service is a release defect.
5. **Heavy evidence is lazy.** Do not eagerly load every workspace, country shard,
   study artifact or diagnostic payload for a bounded first view.
6. **Mobile is a composition, not a shrink.** Preserve the primary question,
   hierarchy, next action, focus order, 44px targets and intended local overflow
   only inside explicit chart/table containers.
7. **Bilingual parity is semantic.** English and Chinese must carry the same
   state, authority, action and failure meaning; untranslated enum tokens are not
   acceptable product copy.
8. **Dark and light are separate art directions of one system**, governed by the
   Design System rather than page-local themes.

A designed view-model or BFF may reorder and compress canonical owner output for the
task. It may not reimplement producer math, silently fill nulls or create a page-local
source of truth.

## 6. Data, time, null, correction and authority behavior

Every admitted workflow states which clocks matter. The default view shows the one
effective “as of” and a plain-language freshness/coverage state; Level 3 preserves
the full clock set.

Binding behavior:

- missing is never zero;
- warmup/first print is not unchanged;
- last successful output after a newer failed attempt is degraded, not current;
- partial coverage names included, excluded and unknown denominators;
- rights-blocked is distinct from source failure;
- unavailable is distinct from valid empty;
- a corrected source preserves prior-known history and identifies the correction;
- replay is idempotent and does not duplicate an event, alert, study or version;
- an unknown modifying effect stays unknown until its canonical owner reconciles it;
- model prose never supplies an exact missing fact, identity, price, causal link,
  rank, size, trade or rights decision.

Every visible interpretation identifies its class:

- deterministic fact or calculation;
- descriptive context;
- mechanism-supported interpretation;
- historical association;
- calibrated forecast;
- human-authored assumption or thesis;
- model-generated explanation/hypothesis.

The classes may coexist. They may not be visually or semantically collapsed.

## 7. Builder authority and handoff contract

Every bounded Market Ontology commission declares exactly one:

```text
PUBLIC_PRODUCT_SURFACE: NONE
```

or

```text
PUBLIC_PRODUCT_SURFACE: <one named admitted or candidate workflow>
```

`PUBLIC_PRODUCT_SURFACE: NONE` permits contracts, producers, ingestion, artifacts,
tests, machine projections and operator inspection. It does not permit a new
customer route merely because the backend is ready.

A named public surface is lawful only when the handoff contains:

- observable mission and why it matters;
- authority precedence and current estate;
- exact scope, repository and path ceiling;
- explicit non-goals;
- primary persona and complete entry-to-outcome journey;
- real-data desktop/tablet/mobile reference compositions;
- information hierarchy and actions;
- view-model/BFF contract;
- data/time/null/correction/rights behavior;
- deterministic, statistical, human-authored and model-generated boundaries;
- all failure and unauthorized states;
- instrumentation;
- acceptance tests, browser evidence and real production proof;
- stop condition and continuation handoff.

Backend specialists do not invent product architecture. Frontend specialists do not
invent analytical semantics. The Fable/Sol principal freezes the product and owner
boundaries, then routes bounded implementation to the least-scarce capable worker.

## 8. Product acceptance law

A user-facing PR is accepted only when all applicable evidence exists on the same
immutable candidate and current integration proof:

1. real entry from navigation or a real preceding workflow;
2. real production-shaped data, not fixtures alone;
3. the primary task is understandable without internal architecture knowledge;
4. complete, loading, warmup, valid-empty, stale, partial, unavailable,
   rights-blocked, unauthorized, correction, replay and error states;
5. exact evidence and clocks remain reachable;
6. keyboard, focus, screen-reader basics, reduced motion, contrast and mobile target
   evidence;
7. English/Chinese and dark/light parity where claimed;
8. no dead destination or unsupported action;
9. browser proof at relevant desktop, tablet and mobile widths;
10. bounded network/performance behavior;
11. task-start, reached-value and failure instrumentation without private free text;
12. production deployment receipt and live-path readback when release owes it;
13. a named human or machine capability that now exists end to end.

Green CI, merge, deployment and production acceptance are separate facts. Architecture
records can be accepted as `SPEC_ONLY`. A merged implementation without live proof is
`BUILT_NOT_PROVEN`. A technically present but undiscoverable or unusable feature is
not accepted merely because its route responds.

Independent review must ask:

- Can the primary persona complete the primary task?
- Does the first viewport answer the registered question?
- Is backend structure being presented as product?
- Is any claimed action dead or unsupported?
- Are missing, stale, corrected and rights-blocked states honest?
- Did the worker create a duplicate owner or page-local truth?
- What real capability exists now that did not exist before?
- Is the result visible on the real production path?

## 10. Cross-lane navigation and shared-path ownership

Multiple lanes need `_navlinks.html.j2`, Caddy/public access, route registry, shared
build and product-shell paths. Shared files are not first-come-first-served.

Before a child writes a shared path, it must:

1. fresh-census open PRs, branches, worktrees and current main;
2. identify the exact owning hunk and semantic dependency;
3. preserve immutable candidate work from other lanes;
4. return an ordering/integration proposal when hunks or semantics overlap;
5. never use a broad regeneration or merge to erase another lane's source;
6. re-prove navigation, access, registry and generated parity on the integrated
   candidate.

Feature workers should own new feature files and frozen contracts first. Shared
navigation/admission wiring occurs through one explicit current integration owner.
A route is not made discoverable by a worker racing a nav file.

## 11. Non-copying, rights and moat law

Competitor research may identify jobs-to-be-done, workflows, information architecture,
interaction patterns and public facts. Mastermind uses original code, original design,
original language and first-party, public, licensed or otherwise lawful data.

Do not copy proprietary code, text, visual assets, private corpora, hidden interfaces,
datasets or brand identity. Preserve concrete useful jobs rather than blurring the
reference product into generic concepts.

The moat is not page count. It is the combination of:

- correction-safe and rights-safe truth;
- canonical identity, evidence and time;
- useful relationships and structured intelligence;
- connected premium research workflows;
- human and machine consumption from the same truth;
- privacy-safe learning and separate evaluation of promoted claims.

## 12. Program stop condition

Market Ontology productization is complete only when:

- **Truth:** inputs are fresh, source-bound, correction-safe, rights-safe and visibly
  degraded when they are not;
- **Intelligence:** facts, comparisons, relationships, transmission, studies and
  synthesis are useful without authority overclaim;
- **Product:** a researcher completes connected tasks through coherent, discoverable
  workflows across real states;
- **Learning:** instrumentation shows whether users reached value, while qledger/
  Evaluation OS separately measures any promoted forecast or claim authority.

The program remains open while any admitted lawful capability has no owner/disposition,
no real consumer, no accepted product journey, no production proof or no truthful
rejection/rights ruling.

No worker, PR, page count, CI run or deployment may close this program by declaration.
F00/Sol final acceptance requires the full capability ledger, integrated experience,
real production proof and durable Agent OS closeout.
