## 2.2 `MO-DELTA-005` — Decision Zones

### Identity adjudication

`Decision Zones` is **not a new canonical intelligence owner or separate database**. It is an alias/projection for the downstream research-action composition inside Ontology Explorer and Market OS:

```text
selected observed path or explicit assumption
+ first blocking leg / invalidators / dislocation / incorporation
+ private user exposure where available
+ lawful research-priority context
-> bounded research actions: investigate, monitor, compare inverse, wait for confirmation, archive/refute
```

It does not own market truth, trade decisions, position sizing, alerts, theses or portfolio state. Those actions route to their existing owners in later bounded waves.

### Acceptance

The user can move from one catalyst/path to explicit non-authoritative next research actions, each grounded in the selected evidence and change conditions. No action is generated from an opaque confidence score; no trade/alert/thesis mutation occurs without the canonical target workflow and explicit user action.

### Non-goal

Do not create `decision_zones.json`, a Decision Zones engine, a second task/alert queue or a model-generated action ranker.
