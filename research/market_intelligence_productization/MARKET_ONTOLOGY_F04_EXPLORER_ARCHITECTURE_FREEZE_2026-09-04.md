## 3. Chairman outcome and 10/10 end state

### 3.1 Primary persona

An investor, analyst or portfolio manager trying to understand how one market shock, release, policy change, theme or company event can travel through assets, sectors, themes, companies and their own holdings.

### 3.2 Primary user job

> Something changed — or I want to test an explicit assumption. Show me how the change connects through markets, what has actually happened, what has not happened, which links are observed versus hypothesized, what would invalidate the path, and where my own exposure sits.

### 3.3 Machine/intelligence job

Compose owner-native facts and relationships with incompatible clocks, units, horizons, rights, correction semantics and epistemic status into one deterministic read model without:

- converting similarity into causality;
- converting a historical association into a forecast;
- converting an assumption into an observation;
- multiplying unrelated coefficients into false precision;
- copying canonical truth into a new graph or private store;
- allowing model prose to create privileged facts.

### 3.4 End-state journey

A paid user can:

1. open `/ontology.html` and see a truthful current trace rather than a generic dashboard;
2. search for a node, path or plain-language question;
3. select a real observed root or switch to an explicitly counterfactual scenario;
4. inspect a bounded directed path across macro, rates, FX, commodities, credit, equities, themes and securities;
5. see every node's current value, unit, owner, clocks, freshness, rights and correction state;
6. see every edge's relationship class, method class, horizon, evidence, uncertainty and invalidator;
7. distinguish what is active, waiting, expressed, halted, expired, unavailable or merely historically common;
8. compare the selected path with its inverse, alternatives and strongest invalidators;
9. overlay their canonical Portfolio or Watchlist without exposing or duplicating private state;
10. ask Mastermind to explain the selected evidence and receive exact source/owner citations;
11. save, reopen, share or replay the view where the canonical user and point-in-time owners support those actions;
12. observe honest abstention wherever the system cannot support the requested relationship or numerical response.

Completion is not a visually convincing graph. Completion is an evidence-governed research workflow that remains useful when the answer is uncertain or unavailable.

## 8. Epistemic architecture

### 8.1 Live Trace and Scenario are separate modes

#### `LIVE_TRACE`

Reports what current canonical owners actually observe. It may show owner-native measurements, chain states, historical context and current invalidators.

#### `SCENARIO`

Evaluates an explicit user assumption. The assumption never overwrites an observed node, never appends to an owner ledger, never opens a TXI episode and never becomes evidence that the market moved.

A scenario result is a pure derived evaluation tied to:

- assumption identity;
- owner generation/model versions;
- effective as-of;
- knowledge cutoff;
- method class;
- unit and horizon compatibility;
- scenario path definition.

`COMPARE TO LIVE` displays the assumption beside current state. It does not replace current state.

### 8.2 Relationship classes remain distinct

| Class | Meaning | May prove economic propagation? |
|---|---|---:|
| Graph 1 | Owner-backed economic/exposure/contractual mechanism | Only within native owner claim strength |
| Graph 2 | Similarity, thematic comparability or shared characteristics | No |
| Graph 3 | Residual market context/co-movement | No |
| TXI path | Versioned staged mechanism with observable tests and falsifiers | Context/hypothesis unless a native owner grants stronger status |

Graph 2 or Graph 3 may appear as contextual alternatives, but neither may fill an absent Graph 1 edge. Theme co-membership cannot become an economic relationship.

### 8.3 Method classes

Every numerical or relational assertion carries exactly one primary method class:

| Method class | Meaning | Permissible output |
|---|---|---|
| `OBSERVED_STATE` | Current native owner value/state | Native value and receipt |
| `DETERMINISTIC_IDENTITY` | Exact identity, membership or declared reference | Relationship only |
| `OWNER_MODEL` | Native owner's accepted model output | Output within that owner's contract |
| `EMPIRICAL_SAME_WINDOW_BETA` | Same-window historical co-movement sensitivity | Illustrative estimate with window/model caveat |
| `HISTORICAL_TRANSITION_RATE` | Frequency that a declared next condition followed a prior condition | Probability with N, span and regime cell; not causal confidence |
| `HISTORICAL_OUTCOME_DISTRIBUTION` | Outcomes following frozen historical episodes | Distribution/interval and concentration, not trade recommendation |
| `THRESHOLD_STATE_ONLY` | Assumption satisfies or fails a declared deterministic condition | State transition or partial/unknown only |
| `HYPOTHESIS_MECHANISM` | Versioned mechanism, condition and invalidator without numerical response authority | Direction/mechanism prose, no fabricated estimate |
| `UNAVAILABLE` | Missing identity, data, rights, method, unit, horizon or owner acceptance | Typed abstention |

A future nonlinear full-revaluation owner may add a new method class only through a separate architecture/owner ruling. F04 itself may not invent it.

### 8.4 Why `Conf >= 50%` is rejected

The reference control collapses different epistemic objects into one number. Mastermind instead filters by:

- observed / owner model / historical / hypothesis / unavailable;
- economic / similarity / residual context;
- active / waiting / expressed / halted / expired / unavailable;
- exact horizon and freshness.

A historical transition frequency of 69%, a model coverage score, an identity-resolution state and an LLM narrative confidence are not the same quantity and must never share one threshold control.

## 9. Scenario contract

### 9.1 Assumption shape

A scenario assumption contains:

```json
{
  "assumption_id": "content hash",
  "node_ref": "owner-native node identity",
  "shock_definition": "terminal_change_over_horizon",
  "magnitude": 20.0,
  "unit": "percent",
  "horizon": "3m",
  "path_shape": "terminal_only",
  "origin_class": "unspecified",
  "effective_as_of": "date/time",
  "knowledge_cutoff": "date/time"
}
```

Closed `shock_definition` examples:

- `terminal_change_over_horizon` for price/index changes;
- `absolute_change_bp_over_horizon` for yields/spreads;
- `average_level_shift` only where an owner explicitly supports it;
- `event_impulse` only where an event owner defines the impulse.

A magnitude without a path shape does not imply an intermediate moving-average slope, timing path, volatility path or source cause.

### 9.2 Propagation law

For each requested hop:

1. resolve exact input node and owner;
2. validate scenario definition, unit, horizon, range and origin requirements;
3. read the owner-native method and exact version;
4. evaluate that hop independently;
5. return an estimate, distribution, threshold state, mechanism-only result or typed abstention;
6. preserve uncertainty and missingness;
7. never recursively multiply arbitrary edge coefficients.

A combined path estimate is allowed only when one accepted owner explicitly models the joint path and its dependence structure. Otherwise the product shows hop-level results and states why a path-level point estimate is unavailable.

### 9.3 Current-correlation stress is not causality

An empirical factor/correlation propagation may be displayed only as an explicitly named stress method with:

- estimation window;
- current model version;
- covariance/factor source;
- shock compatibility diagnostics;
- supported instrument coverage;
- nonlinear/proxy limitations;
- no causal language.

### 9.4 Scenario side effects are forbidden

Running, comparing, saving or sharing a scenario may not:

- append TXI chain episodes;
- mutate current market state;
- modify Portfolio or Watchlist truth;
- originate a Prophet candidate;
- trigger an alert or trade;
- alter a calibration ledger;
- become a forward observation.

Any later transition from a saved scenario into a monitored thesis/condition is a separate explicit user action through the existing thesis/monitor owners.

### 12.1 Clock law

`effective_as_of` and `knowledge_cutoff` are separate.

- current Live Trace normally uses the latest owner-effective state knowable at build/read time;
- historical replay requires owner facts effective by D **and** beliefs/observations known by the selected knowledge cutoff;
- a row reconstructed later does not become historically knowable merely because its `valid_from` is old;
- an owner without a lawful PIT reader returns `PIT_UNAVAILABLE` rather than current data backdated into the replay.

### 12.2 Correction law

F04 never edits owner truth. The derived projection:

- references owner generation/content identity;
- exposes amended, superseded, retracted and stale states;
- does not mutate a saved scenario when owner truth is corrected;
- on reopen, compares saved owner references to current superseding references and presents the delta;
- never silently replaces a historical replay's knowledge cutoff with current corrected truth.

### 12.3 Null law

Missing is not zero. Unknown is not neutral. Unsupported is not safe.

Every omitted value requires a typed reason. A path may remain useful even when one leg abstains, but the UI cannot imply a complete numeric propagation.

- rights/display tier;

- relationship and method class;
- source and target native identities;
- owner and native construct;
- mechanism, condition and lag window;
- units/horizon;
- historical N/span/regime cells or model version;
- estimate/distribution only where method permits;
- invalidators and expiry;
- why a stronger claim is unavailable;
- authority ceiling.

### 13.3 Trace Narrative

Narrative generation is deterministic first:

- state summary;
- observed/assumed root;
- most advanced confirmed hop;
- first unresolved or failed hop;
- strongest current invalidator;
- user exposure coverage;
- one sentence of owner-approved mechanism copy.

Brain may expand or answer questions over that packet. Brain prose is never stored as an edge or owner fact.

## 15. Portfolio and Watchlist integration

The explorer consumes canonical Market OS identities and weights. It does not own them.

For each covered holding/watch item it may show:

- exact path membership/exposure source;
- owner-native sensitivity or blast-channel membership;
- portfolio weight where the authenticated user has supplied it;
- book-context measures from RiskCore where relevant;
- coverage and missingness.

It may not show projected dollar P&L unless an accepted owner provides a compatible instrument response model for that scenario. RiskCore's `whatIf` answers how book structure changes when a position is added; it is not a macro shock propagation model.

Partial coverage is visible as:

- covered market value/count;
- uncovered market value/count;
- unresolved identities;
- owner/method reason for each abstention.

Telemetry never transmits raw holdings, weights, free-text research or full scenario payloads.

### 16.2 Reopen

On reopen:

- reproduce the saved view against its frozen references where available;
- show owner corrections/supersessions since save;
- allow `open as saved` versus `refresh to current` as explicit choices;
- do not mutate the saved revision merely because current truth changed.

### 16.3 Share

A share is an immutable, read-only, entitlement- and rights-safe projection. It must remove private portfolio/watchlist context unless the user explicitly creates an allowed sanitized share. It must never expose gated owner payloads to an unentitled recipient.

### 16.4 Replay

Replay uses separate effective and knowledge clocks. A source that cannot support historical knowledge state is visibly unavailable. Current membership or correction state may never be backfilled into an older replay.
