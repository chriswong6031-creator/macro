**Date:** 2026-08-23

## K1 Evidence Foundation rider

Preserve the existing K1 law:

- references over canonical owners;
- no universal evidence warehouse;
- no fifth PIT vocabulary;
- no duplicate source or identity plane.

Add the following required product contract:

```text
EvidenceBlock
  owner/source ref
  observation/source clock
  point-in-time availability
  coverage/freshness
  uncertainty class
  correction state
  economic-episode relation
  next observable
  permitted consumer/authority
```

`EvidenceRecipe` is a composition of owner-backed EvidenceBlocks for one research/decision job.

A recipe is not a store.

## K3 Opportunity Semantics rider

```text
WHAT IS OBSERVED

```

Do not create one composite score that hides these legs.

Any aggregate prints its denominator receipt and dominant degradation state.

## Cross-program invariants learned from authenticated testing

1. Every aggregate prints included/excluded denominator.
2. Every failed execution persists as a durable failure state.
3. Every object update is idempotent and version-bound.
4. Every saved object must reconcile with its canonical library.
5. Confidence/probability requires derivation/calibration receipt.
6. Partial computation cannot look complete.
7. Analog retrieval remains display/research-only until PIT/outcome gates.
8. Options outputs fail closed by output-specific prerequisites.
