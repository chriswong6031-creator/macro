# 1. Executive decision

## 1.1 Build the complete suite, but do not build twelve isolated dashboards

The product is a **Macro Causal Operating System** inside Market OS. Its purpose is not to reproduce a competitor's visual shell or add another generic macro page. It must let a user move through one continuous research loop:

```text
orient to the current state
-> identify what changed
-> inspect the drivers and source clocks
-> distinguish fact, association, mechanism, and hypothesis
-> replay honest history
-> test a bounded scenario
-> create a governed alert
-> connect the state to themes, securities, and the user's portfolio
-> ask the Analyst with the exact evidence packet attached
```

Every workspace is independently useful, but all twelve use one shared contract, one evidence grammar, one navigation system, one alert lifecycle, one user-state owner, and one cross-domain context model.

## 1.2 Product ownership ruling

The suite is an **F01 Market OS product-composition responsibility**, not a new workstream and not a new macro truth owner.

- `WS:MARKET-OS` owns the coherent product journey and cross-domain composition.
- The existing F01 operation remains the lane identity: `marketontology-f01-macro-markets-20260826-fable-001`.
- Existing domain programs retain their facts, formulas, identities, revisions, and acceptance gates.
- F01 may project and compose domain facts. It may not absorb or silently replace the owners that produced them.
- Housing, consumer, capital-structure, liquidity, and fiscal pages may consume capabilities whose implementation rows live in other Market Ontology lanes. Product composition does not transfer implementation authority.

## 1.3 Primary product surfaces

Mastermind's repository roles remain intact:

```text
Macro repository
  canonical market/macro data and deterministic intelligence owners
  -> versioned, correction-safe workspace projection artifacts

Terminal repository
  authenticated premium presentation and interaction plane
  -> /macro and /macro/[workspace]
  -> existing auth, entitlement, alert, Brain, portfolio, layout, and analytics owners

Mastermind portfolio/reasoning repository
  optional read-only Market View / portfolio context consumers
  -> no direct raw producer reads and no new decision authority
```

The primary premium suite UI belongs in Terminal because Terminal is the canonical responsive presentation/control plane. Macro remains the producer and publication authority. A full second implementation of the same twelve pages in the static Macro site is not part of V1. Public summary cards may be added later through the existing Reactive Projection Platform, but they must not become a duplicate product family.

## 1.4 Initial geographic boundary

V1 is **complete for the United States**, with explicit global views only where the current owner and source rights support them:

- Monetary Policy: US plus global policy-rate comparison.
- Liquidity & Central Banks: US plus governed global central-bank state after the existing W-LIQ producer is repaired and accepted.
- All other workspaces: US first.

A region selector lists only supported regions. Unsupported regions render `NOT_COVERED`; they are never silently mapped to US data or displayed as empty zeroes.

# 2. Why the suite was not already being built

The original F01 plan was directionally correct but never reached an execution start. The current state is not a lack of strategy; it is a broken conversion from strategy into a visible product program.

## 2.1 What existed

The durable F01 packet already required:

- coherent macro and cross-asset state;
- rates, FX, commodities, credit, growth, inflation, labor, and liquidity context;
- exact release, observation, known-at, freshness, null, and correction behavior;
- daily/premarket consumption;
- no second macro truth store;
- no LLM-originated regime, rank, gate, size, or trade authority.

F00B and F00C then created a complete current capability crosswalk. F00C drove the current 130-row `ADMITTED_NOW` denominator to zero unassessed rows at the planning tier.

## 2.2 What did not happen

- The original F01 operation has no proven lane ACK or START.
- Linear `MAS-142` remained Todo/unstarted.
- F00 execution concentrated on parity accounting, semantic closure, MOR Reference, F09 slices, and continuity control rather than making the twelve-workspace suite an explicit product outcome.
- The 130-row closure was records and planning truth, not product implementation. At closure, most rows were still `NOT_BUILT`, `PARTIAL`, or `SPEC_ONLY`.
- Existing macro engines remained fragmented across pages, files, contexts, and workstreams.
- No accepted cross-repository contract joined Macro owner output to a single premium Terminal suite.
- Complete screenshots were not admitted as a governed reference corpus. Liquidity and Monetary Policy have strong visual references; the other ten do not yet have a verified complete state matrix.
- Several adjacent programs were left parked or uncomposed, including the W-LIQ global-liquidity producer and its downstream quant/reader work.

## 2.3 Corrective ruling

Priority no longer controls whether the Macro & Monetary suite exists. It controls **the order of complete verticals**. The program must preserve all twelve outcomes while building and proving them in bounded waves.

# 5. Product thesis and value model

## 5.1 Primary persona

An institutional-quality active investor, analyst, portfolio manager, or serious self-directed user who needs to understand the macro environment without manually reconciling dozens of disconnected charts and release calendars.

## 5.2 Primary user job

Within five minutes, answer:

1. What macro state are we in?
2. What changed since the prior accepted print?
3. Which underlying components drove the change?
4. Which facts are current, revised, stale, unavailable, or disputed?
5. What mechanism could transmit this state into rates, credit, sectors, themes, securities, and my portfolio?
6. What would change the conclusion?
7. What should I monitor next?

## 5.3 Machine and intelligence job

Publish typed, point-in-time, correction-safe context that can be reused by:

- Morning Orientation and the Daily Brief;
- Market OS Control Room;
- Analyst/Brain;
- Neural Web and Theme Graph context;
- security dossiers;
- portfolio risk/context;
- alerts;
- research studies and historical analogue retrieval;
- future calibrated models, without granting them authority at birth.

## 5.4 Moat

The moat is not chart count. It is the combination of:

- exact source, release, first-known, revision, calculation, and render clocks;
- reproducible historical-as-known states;
- correction-safe snapshots and method-version lineage;
- cross-domain causal channels and contradiction tracking;
- one continuous investigation workflow across all twelve domains;
- user portfolio/security context without copying user truth;
- forward learning about which states, transitions, and workflows were useful.

## 5.5 Commercial value

The suite becomes a high-frequency return reason and premium entitlement anchor:

- morning and pre-event orientation;
- macro decision support before security research;
- source-grounded differentiation from chart aggregators;
- personal relevance through portfolio exposure;
- reusable Analyst context that reduces time to a defensible view;
- alert and scenario workflows that create retention without claiming trade automation.

## 6.2 Production navigation law

- Internal QA may access a direct route after the page's exact vertical is built.

- No decorative tab or menu item is permitted.

## 6.3 Shared page grammar

Every workspace follows the same research grammar while preserving domain-specific visualizations:

1. **Context header**
   - workspace, region, supported frequency/window;
   - page build time;
   - required-source availability, freshness, coverage, and last accepted source cut;
   - explicit update action that cannot relabel stale data as fresh.

2. **Causal implications ribbon**
   - up to three evidence-grounded implications;
   - evidence class, confidence basis, horizon, channels, contradictions, and `Trace` action;
   - deterministic text or reviewed templates only in the core surface.

3. **Headline state**
   - one named domain state;
   - exact effective date and method version;
   - major axes or native KPIs;
   - prior accepted state and transition distance when comparable.

4. **Tabs**
   - `Current`
   - `Drivers`
   - `History`
   - `Scenario` only when the workspace has an accepted scenario contract
   - `Alerts` only when the workspace has real eligible conditions in the existing alert service

5. **Dominant visualization**
   - page-specific state map, decomposition, curve, diffusion, burden, or balance;
   - never a generic chart substituted for the domain's analytical job.

6. **Diagnostics**
   - constraints, supports, disagreements, missing components, source issues, and method warnings.

7. **What changed**
   - current accepted snapshot versus prior accepted snapshot under the same method version;
   - method changes appear as method changes, not numeric deltas.

8. **Component histories**
   - chart-ready owner projections with per-series clocks, units, basis, revisions, and source references.

9. **Evidence drawer**
   - source, release, first-known, correction, transform, owner, rights, definition, and exact artifact references.

10. **Analyst action**
    - injects a bounded, schema-validated evidence packet into the existing Brain/Analyst context path;
    - no raw unrestricted repository context and no hidden model authority.

## 6.4 Fixed curated layout before customization

The first production version of each workspace uses a curated, immutable layout. User customization does not block launch.

Later customization must extend the existing `workspace_layout.v1` contract and Terminal `chart_layouts` store. It may add reviewed macro widget types, link-group entity types, and scenario assumption payloads only through that owner. It may not create `macro_layouts`, `dashboard_configs`, or a parallel persistence service.

## 6.5 Reference and ontology trace

Every metric, axis, state, and scenario assumption has:

- a stable public Reference ID under `mastermind.market_reference/v1`;
- an owner reference;
- a source/evidence reference;
- unit/basis and direction semantics;
- caveats and authority ceiling;
- a `Trace in Ontology` path that opens the evidence drawer or a related owner surface.

The public Reference definition does not store the live value and cannot originate market state.

- Source corrections append or supersede under the source owner's existing law.
- The original first-known snapshot remains reproducible.
- A rebuilt current snapshot points to its predecessor and lists changed source/definition fingerprints.
- A method-version change creates a new comparable series only where an explicit bridge exists.
- `What changed` refuses a numeric comparison when definition versions are not comparable.
- Historical replay defaults to `as known at the time` when vintages exist. Final-revised history is labeled explicitly and cannot be used as if it were live historical evidence.

- weights or aggregation law;
- minimum component count and coverage floor;
- frequency and lag alignment;
- thresholds and hysteresis;
- definition and data versions;
- revision behavior;
- validation or descriptive-only ceiling.

No page contributes to a universal Mastermind macro score. Each axis remains domain-specific and descriptive unless a separate evaluation and promotion process grants more authority.

# 8. Causal implications, historical analogues, and AI law

## 8.1 Evidence classes

Core causal language uses a closed evidence class:

```text
DESCRIPTIVE
HISTORICAL_ASSOCIATION
MECHANISM_SUPPORTED
MODEL_HYPOTHESIS
```

- `DESCRIPTIVE` states what changed.
- `HISTORICAL_ASSOCIATION` names the sample, horizon, and instability.
- `MECHANISM_SUPPORTED` requires a governed transmission edge plus current supporting observations.
- `MODEL_HYPOTHESIS` is shown only inside research/Analyst surfaces and carries no core-page authority.

The screenshot-style label `historical analog` may be used only for `HISTORICAL_ASSOCIATION`, not as proof of causality.

## 8.2 Confidence

Confidence is decomposed, not invented by an LLM:

```text
data_coverage
source_health
revision_risk
method_stability
evidence_breadth
contradiction_state
```

A single `HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW` display label, if used, is a deterministic presentation of those fields and links to the decomposition.

## 8.3 Core text generation

Core ribbon text is deterministic or human-reviewed template output over accepted facts. The Analyst may generate a richer explanation but must:

- cite the exact snapshot and evidence refs;
- preserve unknowns and contradictions;
- label interpretation separately from fact;
- never originate a state, score, rank, forecast probability, gate, size, recommendation, or trade.

# 9. Scenarios and alerts

## 9.1 Scenario architecture

A scenario is a transient counterfactual over one immutable workspace snapshot.

```text
current snapshot + allowed assumption overrides
-> owner-native pure scenario function
-> mastermind.macro_workspace_scenario_result.v1
-> no canonical write and no forecast authority
```

Each workspace declares a closed assumption vocabulary. The server rejects arbitrary fields, unsupported units, excessive shocks, stale snapshot IDs, and method-version mismatches.

Scenario output separates:

- direct mechanical effects;
- model-estimated effects;
- unsupported downstream effects;
- unchanged owner facts;
- uncertainty and limitations.

A user may save assumption values only inside the existing `workspace_layout.v1` payload. There is no scenario database.

## 9.2 Alert architecture

Alerts extend the existing Terminal alert lifecycle. Eligible conditions are declared by the workspace artifact:

- named state transition;
- metric threshold crossing;
- axis boundary crossing;
- source becoming stale/failed/current;
- material source revision;
- scheduled release approaching;
- contradiction appearing or resolving.

The alert stores the stable condition identity and user configuration, not a copy of macro truth. A page does not show the Alerts tab until the existing service can create, list, evaluate, and delete the page's real conditions.

# 10. The twelve workspace blueprints

## 10.1 Liquidity Regime Monitor

### User job

Understand whether funding is becoming easier or tighter, whether balance sheets are supplying or withdrawing support, what changed, and which component could force the next regime transition.

### Current owner substrate

- US net-liquidity quantity and history;
- liquidity quality and Treasury plumbing;
- `engine/regime.py` liquidity overlay;
- `engine/conditions.py` funding/financial stress;
- `engine/neuralweb/liquidity_plumbing.py` display/shadow context;
- bank credit, reserves, RRP, TGA, repo/funding and credit spread sources.

The page does **not** depend on accepting global W-LIQ for its US V1. Global context remains absent or explicitly partial until W-LIQ is repaired.

### Headline model

```text
x-axis: funding pressure, easier -> tighter
y-axis: balance-sheet support, weaker -> stronger
state: one of four descriptive quadrants with hysteresis
```

Candidate inputs are adopted from current owners rather than recomputed ad hoc. Each axis publishes components, signs, weights, coverage, freshness, and method version.

### Required product composition

- current quadrant and one-month vector;
- nearest valid boundary in score units;
- constraints/supports with native readings;
- prior accepted print and method-comparable changes;
- net liquidity, reserve balances, TGA, RRP, bank credit, funding/repo stress and relevant money/credit histories;
- evidence-grounded implications;
- scenarios for TGA rebuild/drawdown, RRP depletion, reserves, Fed balance-sheet change, and funding-spread stress;
- alerts for quadrant transition, boundary approach, component shock, source failure, and revision.

### Failure states

Insufficient axis coverage, stale required funding data, negative RRP floor behavior, revision mismatch, method change, no comparable prior print, contradictory quantity/quality signals, and unsupported global view.

### Completion proof

Real US input through the production build; deterministic snapshot; Terminal desktop/tablet/mobile; source drawer; one stale-source and one contradictory-state proof; scenario and alert journeys; exact deployment receipt; no hidden global-liquidity claim.

## 10.2 Growth & Real Economy

### User job

Distinguish current growth level, direction, breadth, and leading-versus-coincident evidence before the narrative consensus catches up.
