## 1. Executive amendment

The 2026-08-23 masterplan remains the architectural base, but its scope and execution model are amended in two material ways.

First, the authenticated Market Ontology research corpus is now a **complete capability-adoption program**, not only a source of the eight strongest transferable ideas. The authenticated subscription census closed at **88/88 advertised paid capabilities**. Every one of those 88 rows is now inside the Mastermind closure boundary. The broader public 1,556-row capability inventory remains the feature/state/method opportunity reserve and must be reconciled into families rather than treated as 1,556 separate engines.

Second, the project is no longer operated as a serial `Sol → one Fable → Sol → one next wave` loop. Fable is the sustained COO/principal-builder for the remainder and may run many bounded, disjoint operator lanes concurrently. One canonical system does **not** mean one operator at a time.

**Priority controls sequencing, not scope.** A P2 feature is still owed if it is useful. It may be implemented after a P0 feature, or in parallel when dependencies and owner surfaces are disjoint. A feature does not disappear merely because it is not immediately alpha-bearing.

## 2. Lawful parity, not proprietary copying

"Parity" means independently reproducing the useful **job, workflow, object contract, interaction law or contextual capability** inside Mastermind with original code, original design and lawful first-party/public/licensed inputs.

Do not copy Market Ontology proprietary code, private corpora, copyrighted prose, screenshots/assets, branding, hidden APIs, credentials or vendor-only datasets. The research archive is evidence for product/method understanding and red-team comparison, not an implementation source tree.

Where Market Ontology's implementation is weak or unsafe, Mastermind reproduces the useful job with stronger canonical law rather than reproducing the defect.

## 4. Product thesis

The end state is not "clone a competitor UI." It is a denser Mastermind intelligence operating system:

`truth → relationship → market path → company consequence → valuation/portfolio consequence → human research object → monitoring/revision → distribution → learning`

The same canonical facts and intelligence should be usable through:

- compact retail/security views;
- deep Desk/company research;
- portfolio and scenario workflows;
- research studies and analog navigation;
- thesis/RMS/collaboration;
- alerts/briefings/chat;
- API/webhook/export projections;
- machine consumers.

Context has value even before it earns alpha authority. A geopolitical map, source library, capital-markets tape, historical analog navigator, options surface or policy tracker may improve research, discovery and synthesis without being allowed to rank or originate trades.

Sol reviews milestone bundles and final acceptance. Chairman is not the routine transport layer.

## 9. Per-PR law

The program is broad; each carrier is narrow.

Each implementation PR must deliver one independently useful capability or tightly coupled vertical, with:

`producer/source → canonical contract → real consumer → failure states → tests → real production proof`

Foundation-only PRs are accepted only when they unlock an immediately named consumer and are followed by that consumer without pretending infrastructure is product completion.

## 10. Completion standard

The Market Ontology parity program is complete only when all four dimensions are true:

**Truth:** all adopted data/context inputs have exact source, identity, clock, correction, coverage and rights behavior.  
**Intelligence:** relationships, comparisons, scenarios and synthesis are governed, typed and useful without overstating causality.  
**Product:** the 88/88 capability jobs have a lawful Mastermind disposition and the adopted jobs work through coherent Retail/Desk/portfolio/research/collaboration/delivery experiences.  
**Learning:** usage and evaluation show whether each family improves research, discovery, retention or decisions; alpha-null does not erase product-useful context, and product usefulness does not grant alpha authority.

Final closeout additionally requires zero unowned paid rows, zero silently skipped useful capability families, production proof at the claimed authority tier, and a final Sol adversarial review against the Chairman's complete-parity intent.
