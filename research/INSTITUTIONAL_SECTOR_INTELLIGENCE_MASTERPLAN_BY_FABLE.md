# Institutional Sector Intelligence — Masterplan by Fable

**Program goal.** Close the verified 20-25% gap between the ChatGPT "capital migration detector" proposal and what the repo already runs ([audit](INSTITUTIONAL_SECTOR_INTELLIGENCE_PROBLEM_AUDIT_FOR_FABLE.md)), and — more importantly — fix the structural finding the audit surfaced only implicitly: **the Thematic Foresight Desk feeds zero scored paths.** The end-state is a desk that (a) ingests the one genuinely-missing elite data layer (Federal Register / regulatory pipeline), (b) converts it into *dated, known-in-advance catalysts* rather than narrative vibes, (c) surfaces where money is arriving before narrative (the front-run cell), and (d) has a pre-built, dark-until-validated promotion contract into the **one scored seam that exists**: `engine/spotlight.py → stock_score._axis_tailwind`.

**Provenance.** Built from an 8-stream / 11-agent investigation (2026-07-03, workflow `wf_bd7daa7f-d5d`): 6 gap-verification streams (Sonnet), a novel-alpha hunt and a red-team (both Opus), plus adversarial verification of the three speculation-prone streams (38 claims upheld, 1 refuted-in-our-favor). Every file:line and API claim below was independently reproduced; the three keystone contracts (`_queue_pull` reads `total_gw_yoy`; `theme_tilt/blend` clip to [-1,1]; `_axis_tailwind` at `engine/stock_score.py:844`) were re-verified by the orchestrator directly.

## 1. The two discoveries that reshape the plan

**D1 — The dead interconnection-queue leg.** `engine/power_scarcity.py:48-63 _queue_pull()` has been wired into the graded power composite since it shipped, reading `data/eia/interconnection_queue.json` — **and nothing in `collectors/` writes that file.** It has silently returned `None` forever. One tiny keyless collector emitting exactly `{"total_gw_yoy": <float, PERCENT units — 18.5 means +18.5%/yr, NOT 0.185>}` arms a multi-year-lead physical-capacity read the desk already believes it has (the reader divides by 20, so a fraction-unit bug would produce a silently-dead ~0 read — the exact dark failure the leg has today). It also carries the cleanest validation story in the whole program: the leg is already inside a forward-graded composite, so "did arming it improve the grade" is measured for free.

**D2 — The US sector-ETF flow collector already exists.** `collectors/sponsors.py SectorFlowAdapter` has been pulling SSGA daily shares-outstanding-derived flows into `data/flows/*.parquet` since 2026-06-09, and a multi-window flow board already renders on sector_central. The audit's "one real Layer-3 gap" is actually just a missing kinetics transform (velocity/accel) — an S-sized wrap, not a data build. The red-team then correctly demolished its alpha claim (see R3).

## 2. Rulings

- **R1 — KILL: Policy Surprise Monitor & Policy Latency Score (as modeled).** Both need an "expected" baseline that cannot be honestly grounded; the repo categorically refuses fabricated forecasts (theme_activity is self-referential YoY everywhere). *Resurrection clause:* PRORULE→RULE latency gets measured **empirically** (join on `regulation_id_numbers`) inside W1b — a latency *measurement*, never a latency *score*.
- **R2 — KILL: Buzzword-Tourist per-stock revenue screen.** Segment/product-axis revenue XBRL is not collected and structurally sparse (companyconcept is consolidated-only). The theme-level discipline already exists structurally as `TEXT_ONLY_CAP=50` in foresight_score. Also killed on the same evidence: **N-PORT** (no usable public monthly dissemination edge over 13F for our purposes) and **words-to-money segment ratio**.
- **R3 — DOWNGRADE: US sector-ETF flow kinetics.** Buildable (D2) but ships display-only with the CN null **pre-registered on the panel** (CN per-name fund-flow rank-IC ≈ −0.008; `flow_velocity.py` carries this honesty gate in code). Positioning lens, never a thesis input. Deprioritized to W2; velocity is statistically immature until ~80 obs (~Sep 2026) — render an "accruing" watermark, `min_obs` gate.
- **R4 — REFRAME: Federal Register is a catalyst calendar, not a narrative heatmap.** The ChatGPT doc's "Policy Narrative Heatmap / Elite Consensus Delta" framing repeats the exact laundering pattern the desk's evidence hierarchy exists to prevent. `comments_close_on` / effective dates / Entity-List additions are **hard, dated, structured, known-in-advance events**. Build the calendar + stage-pipeline (PRORULE→RULE) + reg-velocity leg. The "Elite Consensus Delta" label is banned; the honest board is **Regulatory-Action Convergence** (agency×theme co-occurrence), and only as a cheap secondary view on top of the same data.
- **R5 — INTEGRATION CONTRACT (the ruling that matters).** Nothing in this program ships as another orphan display board. Every new signal declares, at design time, its promotion path: a bounded [-1,1] theme tilt consumable by `spotlight.theme_tilt()/blend()` (→ `stock_score._axis_tailwind`, weight 0.10, over-extension-clamped), OR a triage entry via `theme_alerts → alert_triage`. The wiring ships **dark** (config-gated off) with a **two-sided** regression test: (a) flag OFF → stock_score output byte-identical to pre-PR baseline, AND (b) flag ON with a fixture tilt → `_axis_tailwind` shifts by the expected clamped amount for ≥1 member — a dark test that can't distinguish "correctly gated" from "never connected" is verification theater. Promotion requires a matured forward ledger clearing Phase-0 (walk-forward, FDR-corrected) — house law, no exceptions.
- **R6 — Evidence-class stamping.** Every new read carries its class per the desk's hierarchy: Federal Register events = **dated-structured** (real-time, uncapped-eligible after validation); USAspending/XBRL money metrics = **fingerprint-class** (annual/lagged, ≤60); text-derived anything = ≤50. New confirmers NEVER change cascade stages (existing contract in `foresight_cascade`).
- **R7 — No new pages.** All surfaces land as sections on `foresight.html` (divergence board, policy calendar) or existing hubs (sector_central for ETF kinetics, stock.html chips for money metrics). The audit's anti-fork warning stands.
- **R8 — Backfill before Phase-0, off the nightly path.** Federal Register backfill (2015→, RULE+PRORULE, ~1 req/s polite) runs as a one-time script; the nightly collector is incremental (`conditions[publication_date][gte]`). The 67-minute render budget is a real constraint; nothing here adds heavy nightly work.
- **R9 — Known data gotchas are law for builders.** (a) USAspending recipient→ticker matching is curated-alias, lossy for subsidiaries — on-page caveat required. (b) rr-surprise must zero its blend weight in the specific fiscal months where the **LAG-adjusted recent window** overlaps Sep 1–Oct 31 (fiscal-year-end surge); use the same `LAG_MONTHS` constant the obligations leg uses — never hardcode the calendar months — and add a hermetic test asserting rr_surprise==0 on a Sept-heavy synthetic frame. (c) SAM opportunity $ is ~20-30% populated — pipeline-to-award is count-based. (d) FedReg two-stage filter (term AND agency-slug, OR across full agencies list incl. parents) — term-only pollutes, agency-only drops. (e) mktcap denominator is price-live vs award-numerator lagged — disclose. (f) FedReg `significant` has a high null rate and can be revised post-publication — store the value at `_first_seen` (PIT-authoritative) and treat null as NOT-significant in any weighting; never coerce null→true.

## 4. What "institutional access / front-running" honestly means here
Not MNPI, not scraped sell-side (standing NO). Operationally: (1) **structured government data at publication time** — Federal Register events are PIT-stamped the day they publish, weeks-to-months before money lands and before financial media narrates; (2) **filing-time 8-K contract magnitude before consensus moves** (W1c pre-drift window); (3) **physical-capacity leads** (queue buildout, the desk's existing bottleneck stack); (4) **the divergence board's hidden-opportunity cell** — money arriving while narrative is quiet — with every such call forward-graded. The moat is the translation layer the repo already has (themes→members→evidence classes→graded ledgers); this program feeds it the missing earliest-layer inputs and finally gives it a scored outlet.
