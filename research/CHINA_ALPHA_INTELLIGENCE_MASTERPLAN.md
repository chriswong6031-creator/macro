## 0. Executive decision

The China program is not “add more alternative datasets.” It is the construction of a **real-world corporate sensing and professional-discovery system** whose job is to improve the ordering of Prophet candidates without contaminating Prophet's timing/admission logic.

The system must answer, for every China listed company:

1. **Reality:** what materially changed in the company's economic world?
2. **Professional Belief:** who is researching, revising, allocating, or changing conviction?
3. **Recognition:** how much has broader market consensus already absorbed?
4. **Tradeability / Path:** does Prophet offer a lawful favorable entry now?

The core product law remains:

> **Intelligence decides which lawful Prophet candidates deserve priority. Prophet decides whether they may be acted on now.**

Current `cn_prophet_v4` remains the live champion until a challenger earns promotion.

The target moat is not possession of one secret feed. It is:

> **China-specific first-party/licensed data × exact identity × point-in-time provenance × cross-source logical-event linkage × actor/economic independence × AI extraction × prospective outcome learning.**

- Why is this name unusually interesting now?
- What actually changed?
- Who is acting?
- Is the evidence independent or repeated reporting of one event?

without letting an LLM originate trade authority.

## 4. Evidence architecture

### 4.1 Data owners keep payload truth
Raw visits remain in the visit source plane.
Q&A remains in existing Q&A stores.
Fund holdings remain in a fund-ownership plane.
Projects remain in project-domain storage.
Procurement remains in its domain store.
Documents remain in document stores.

### 4.2 Evidence Mesh is optional runtime plumbing, not a mandatory warehouse
GROK-A0's strongest ruling supersedes any instinct to build a universal mesh store immediately.

The lawful construction is a **typed pointer/reference layer** over owner-native objects.

No raw payload copy.
No new identity registry.
No second contradiction engine.
No authority increase.

### 4.3 “Boring baseline” law
Do not build a persistent Evidence Mesh merely because the architecture can name one.

The baseline is:
> call the owner-native readers directly.

A persistent cross-store pointer index is justified only when a funded consumer demonstrably needs to enumerate native objects across multiple owner stores without importing all of those engines.

For the first Institutional Discovery vertical, a universal Mesh runtime is **not a blocker**.

FABLE-A K1 should therefore freeze:
- reference contract;
- logical-event linkage contract;
- independence lineage contract;
- adapter law;
- as-of bundle law;
and may ship only golden fixtures / contract code unless a concrete cross-store consumer meets the flip condition.

### 4.4 Canonical evidence objects
Conceptually distinguish:
1. ObservationRef
2. ObservationVersion / correction
3. StructuredAssertion
4. LogicalEvent
5. ManifestationLink
6. IndependenceLineage
7. IntelligenceFamilyState
8. CompanyIntelligenceState
9. AsOfBundle

### 4.5 Three independence axes
Never compress into “N signals”:
- economic-event independence;
- actor-decision independence;
- source-lineage independence.

A fourth descriptive axis is carrier class:
- real world;
- professional behavior;
- corporate disclosure;
- policy/regulatory;
- market attention;
- price/volume;
- model-derived.

### 4.6 LLM law
Allowed:
- schema extraction;
- entity/date/amount extraction;
- topic classification;
- commitment extraction;
- contradiction proposal;
- logical-event linkage proposal.

Required:
- exact source span;
- model/schema version;
- extraction confidence;
- deterministic numeric/date/entity checks where possible.

Forbidden:
- model prose → bullishness score → Prophet.
