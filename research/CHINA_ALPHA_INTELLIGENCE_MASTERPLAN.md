## 0. Executive decision

The China program is not “add more alternative datasets.” It is the construction of a **real-world corporate sensing and professional-discovery system** whose job is to improve the ordering of Prophet candidates without contaminating Prophet's timing/admission logic.

The system must answer, for every China listed company:

1. **Reality:** what materially changed in the company's economic world?
2. **Professional Belief:** who is researching, revising, allocating, or changing conviction?
3. **Recognition:** how much has broader market consensus already absorbed?
4. **Tradeability / Path:** does Prophet offer a lawful favorable entry now?

The core product law remains:

> **Intelligence decides which lawful Prophet candidates deserve priority. Prophet decides whether they may be acted on now.**

Current `cn_prophet_v4` remains the live champion until a challenger earns promotion.

The target moat is not possession of one secret feed. It is:

> **China-specific first-party/licensed data × exact identity × point-in-time provenance × cross-source logical-event linkage × actor/economic independence × AI extraction × prospective outcome learning.**
