# LEU 2025 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

LEU's 2025 alpha was a sovereign-fuel scarcity re-rating, not a simple uranium-price trade: a profitable but lumpy fuel broker with a scarce, already-operating U.S.-origin centrifuge platform showed a 67% first-quarter revenue increase and a sharp gross-profit inflection, then the May 23 nuclear executive orders enlarged the perceived addressable market and made domestic enrichment a national-security bottleneck; DOE's $110 million HALEU extension and the 900-kilogram delivery converted policy optionality into operating evidence, while the KHNP/POSCO framework added customer and private-capital support. The tape was correspondingly broad and persistent—+58.86 percentage points versus SPY over 21 sessions at the May 23 onset, repeated new highs, and eventually billion-dollar turnover days—but price ultimately ran far ahead of near-term contracted economics: the stock peaked at $436 on October 15 and finished 2025 at $242.76, down 44.32% from that peak despite retaining a 264.45% calendar-year gain. In short, capital correctly re-underwrote Centrus from reseller to prospective strategic domestic producer, then over-capitalized long-dated, funding-contingent capacity before financing and execution caught up.
