# LEU 2025 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

LEU's 2025 alpha was a policy-led scarcity rerating that became company-specific rather than a uranium-price proxy: the May 23 nuclear executive orders explicitly called for expanded domestic enrichment, but Centrus then converted that opening into a ladder of operating and financing proof—strong Q1 contract economics, a $110 million HALEU extension, delivery of the 900-kilogram Phase II target, relief on 2026–2027 Russian-fuel waivers, potential Korean capital, a disclosed multi-billion-dollar Ohio expansion, and an October NNSA notice of intent to sole-source AC100 work ([White House, published 2025-05-23](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/05/reinvigorating-the-nuclear-industrial-base/); [Centrus, published 2025-06-20](https://investors.centrusenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/centrus-energy-secures-contract-extension-department-energy); [Centrus, published 2025-06-25](https://investors.centrusenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/centrus-achieves-key-production-milestone-delivery-900-kilograms); [SEC, filed 2025-08-19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065059/000106505925000078/leu-20250804.htm); [FedConnect, published 2025-10-08](https://www.fedconnect.net/FedConnect/default.aspx?ReturnUrl=%2FFedConnect%2F%3Fagency%3DDOE%26doc%3D89233125RNA000264&agency=DOE&doc=89233125RNA000264)). The market was capitalizing a possible strategic enrichment franchise long before its cash earnings resembled one: quarterly margins stayed contract-mix-lumpy, much of the expansion backlog was contingent, and runway came heavily from an expanded equity program and $805 million of zero-coupon converts. That explains both outcomes. LEU ended 2025 up 264.45%, beating URA by 204.89 percentage points and SPY by 248.10 points, yet it also fell 44.32% from its October 15 close high as late positive gaps stopped holding and Q3 disclosed a gross loss plus another $1 billion equity program ([Centrus, published 2025-11-05](https://investors.centrusenergy.com/node/20461); [Centrus, published 2025-11-06](https://investors.centrusenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/centrus-announces-market-equity-offering-program)).

## 5. Fundamentals arc

**Repo coverage is null.** `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` each contain zero LEU rows. There is therefore no local PIT-gated revenue, margin, cash-flow, debt, or EPS series to quote. The arc below is reconstructed only from timestamped issuer releases; it is not silently substituted into the repo panel.

**Before the durable leg.** FY2024 revenue was $442.0 million, up 38.0%, while gross profit was nearly flat at $111.5 million, a 25.23% gross margin versus 35.01% in 2023. Unrestricted cash was $671.4 million, but that balance followed $402.5 million of 2024 converts. This was a growing top line with no aggregate gross-profit growth, already dependent on capital-market runway. [Centrus release, published 2025-02-06](https://investors.centrusenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/centrus-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2024-results).

Q1 2025, the last report before t0, made the earnings bridge look much better: revenue rose 67% to $73.1 million and gross profit rose from $4.3 million to $32.9 million, a 45.01% gross margin. Yet management explicitly said LEU results vary with delivery timing and contract composition, and $11.8 million of the quarter's net-income bridge came from debt extinguishment. Cash was $653.0 million and backlog was $3.8 billion through 2040. [Centrus release, published 2025-05-07](https://investors.centrusenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/centrus-reports-first-quarter-2025-results).

**During and after the run.** Q2 revenue declined 18% to $154.5 million, but gross profit increased 48% to $53.9 million, a 34.89% margin; cash reached $833.0 million after approximately $114 million of net Q2 equity proceeds, and backlog was $3.6 billion. Thus the economic proof was margin mix plus HALEU execution, not smooth revenue growth. [Centrus release, published 2025-08-05](https://investors.centrusenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/centrus-reports-second-quarter-2025-results).

Q3 then showed the model's lumpiness: revenue increased 30% to $74.9 million, but gross profit swung to a $4.3 million loss, a -5.74% margin, because LEU segment cost exceeded revenue under the quarter's contract mix. Nine-month operating cash flow was $99.4 million and capex $10.1 million, while financing cash flow was $841.4 million; cash reached $1.63 billion largely after $782.4 million of net zero-coupon-convert proceeds and $139.7 million of year-to-date equity proceeds. Backlog reached $3.9 billion, but $2.3 billion was contingent on expansion milestones and substantial funding. Runway was therefore ample, but finance-heavy and paired with execution and dilution risk. [Centrus release, published 2025-11-05](https://investors.centrusenergy.com/node/20461).

The fundamentals arc is not “earnings compounded into the price.” It is “lumpy incumbent cash flows and external capital financed an option on a strategically favored build-out.” The market's willingness to capitalize that option drove alpha; the Q3 gross loss and serial financing helped puncture the October extreme.
