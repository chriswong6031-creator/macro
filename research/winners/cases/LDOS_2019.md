# LDOS 2019 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

LDOS's 2019 alpha was an execution-and-duration rerating, not a single contract headline: a business that had produced almost no FY2018 revenue growth but sharply better margins and cash conversion began showing accelerating organic growth, expanding bookings/backlog, and repeated guidance increases. The [April 30 first-quarter filing (2019-04-30)](https://investors.leidos.com/node/8931/html) was the true economic breakaway—earnings, cash flow, bookings, and guidance all improved together, the stock cleared a 252-session closing high on a durable high-volume gap—and the June 4 mechanical detector date arrived after much of that relative move was already in the tape. The [July 30](https://investors.leidos.com/static-files/b62e5ed0-73ef-4c16-8d0e-0101d9dc6054) and [October 29](https://investors.leidos.com/node/8986/html) releases extended the earnings runway; the [S&P 500 addition announced 2019-08-01](https://press.spglobal.com/2019-08-01-Leidos-Holdings-IDEX-Set-to-Join-S-P-500-Grubhub-Foot-Locker-to-Join-S-P-MidCap-400-National-Beverage-to-Join-S-P-SmallCap-600) forced a large but mechanically contaminated liquidity event; and the [Dynetics agreement announced 2019-12-17](https://investors.leidos.com/static-files/7801c5f9-a6f7-4284-b663-81b6c374ead4) added higher-technology growth optionality. Consequently, the full-year result was large (+88.82% total return, +59.74 percentage points versus XLI), but alpha measured *forward* from June 4 was initially absent and only became material by 63 sessions. That timing distinction explains both the winner and its apparent failure to sustain large short-horizon alpha from the detector date.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

The filing anchors in `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` are 2019-02-19, 2019-04-30, 2019-07-30, and 2019-10-29. `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` has no LDOS rows from 2019—its LDOS coverage begins in 2020—so that absence is a local coverage gap, not evidence that no material event occurred.

1. **2019-02-07 — NASA NEST contract publication.** NASA publicly awarded Leidos a potential ten-year, $2.9 billion ceiling contract for agency end-user IT services. This established a long-duration Civil revenue rung before earnings, although a protest still remained. [NASA publication, 2019-02-07](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-awards-contract-for-end-user-it-services-technologies/)

2. **2019-02-19 — FY2018 results filed.** Full-year revenue was nearly flat at $10.19 billion, but operating margin rose to 7.3% from 5.5%, operating cash flow reached $768 million, and bookings were $13.7 billion (1.3× book-to-bill). Capital could begin separating operating improvement from the weak top-line history. [Leidos FY2018 filing/release, 2019-02-19](https://investors.leidos.com/sec-filings/sec-filing/10-k/0001336920-19-000004)

3. **2019-02-21 — non-core cyber divestiture completion reported.** A public report said Leidos had closed the previously disclosed sale of its commercial cybersecurity business to Capgemini, sharpening the portfolio around government and highly regulated markets. This was a low-durability portfolio-cleanup rung, and its later accounting gain must not be confused with recurring earnings. [Washington Technology, published 2019-02-21](https://www.washingtontechnology.com/2019/02/leidos-closes-sale-of-commercial-cyber-business/326464/)

4. **2019-02-21 — $200 million accelerated repurchase announced.** Leidos announced a $200 million accelerated share-repurchase agreement expected to complete by the end of April. This was a finite capital-return rung, not an operating catalyst. [Leidos exhibit filed/published 2019-02-21](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336920/000133692019000007/pressrelease22119.htm)

5. **2019-04-30 — first-quarter earnings and first guidance step-up.** Revenue rose 5.5% to $2.577 billion, operating margin expanded to 7.5% from 6.5%, operating cash flow was $288 million versus $22 million, bookings were $3.3 billion, and backlog was $21.5 billion. Diluted EPS rose to $1.29 from $0.66, but the quarter included an $88 million pre-tax gain on the cyber divestiture; the cleaner non-GAAP comparison was $1.13 versus $1.03. Management increased the non-GAAP EPS range to $4.30–$4.65 and the operating-cash-flow floor to $825 million from $725 million. The tape's first 252-session high and durable +9.88% earnings gap make this the principal re-underwriting date. [Leidos 8-K/release, 2019-04-30](https://investors.leidos.com/node/8931/html)

6. **2019-05-10 — Investor Day preview filed.** Leidos publicly scheduled a May 14 management forum to discuss its businesses and strategic outlook, with presentation materials to be made public that day. The current archive does not expose those May 14 materials, so the exact point-in-time target set is **null** here; this rung records a public re-underwriting forum, not a claimed positive surprise. [Leidos 8-K, filed 2019-05-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336920/000133692019000026/form8-kitem701.htm)

7. **2019-05-30 — NASA protest denied.** GAO denied the Enterprise Services protest over the NEST award, removing a public contract-contingency overhang between the April breakaway and the June detector date. [GAO decision, published 2019-05-30](https://www.gao.gov/products/b-417329%2Cb-417329.2%2Cb-417329.3)

8. **2019-07-30 — second-quarter earnings, revenue/EPS guidance increase, and dividend increase.** Revenue rose 7.9% to $2.728 billion (8.8% organic after adjusting for a divestiture), bookings were $3.0 billion, and backlog reached $21.7 billion. Management increased revenue guidance to $10.65–$10.95 billion, non-GAAP EPS guidance to $4.50–$4.75, and the quarterly dividend by 6% to $0.34; the 7.7% GAAP operating margin was slightly below the prior-year 7.9%, an important non-linear detail. [Leidos release, 2019-07-30](https://investors.leidos.com/static-files/b62e5ed0-73ef-4c16-8d0e-0101d9dc6054)

9. **2019-08-01 — second $200 million accelerated repurchase announced.** Leidos entered another $200 million accelerated agreement and said approximately 80% of the shares would be received during August. This separate reduction in available shares overlapped with the index-inclusion window and is another reason not to treat August liquidity as purely fundamental discovery. [Leidos 8-K, filed 2019-08-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1336920/000133692019000045/a8-kcoverpage.htm)

10. **2019-08-01 — S&P 500 inclusion announced.** S&P Dow Jones Indices announced that LDOS would replace Anadarko in the S&P 500 before the August 9 open. The August 8 $2.61 billion dollar-volume spike is therefore best read as forced index demand and positioning, not an independent fundamental discovery. [S&P Global announcement, 2019-08-01](https://press.spglobal.com/2019-08-01-Leidos-Holdings-IDEX-Set-to-Join-S-P-500-Grubhub-Foot-Locker-to-Join-S-P-MidCap-400-National-Beverage-to-Join-S-P-SmallCap-600)

11. **2019-10-29 — third-quarter earnings and broad guidance increase.** Revenue rose 10.1% to $2.835 billion, bookings reached $5.2 billion (1.8× book-to-bill), and backlog rose to $23.9 billion. Management increased revenue, adjusted EBITDA-margin, non-GAAP EPS, and operating-cash-flow guidance; the new EPS range of $4.90–$5.10 was well above July's $4.50–$4.75. The +6.91% gap held through ten sessions. [Leidos 8-K/release, 2019-10-29](https://investors.leidos.com/node/8986/html)

12. **2019-12-17 — Dynetics acquisition announced.** Leidos announced a definitive agreement to acquire Dynetics for $1.65 billion in cash, describing it as immediately accretive to revenue growth, EBITDA margins, and non-GAAP EPS upon closing. The deal broadened the public growth narrative into applied research and national-security technology, while also introducing financing and integration risk. [Leidos announcement, 2019-12-17](https://investors.leidos.com/static-files/7801c5f9-a6f7-4284-b663-81b6c374ead4); the related 8-K was filed on [2019-12-18](https://investors.leidos.com/node/9001/html).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present, but broad rather than tight.** From September's 2018 high to the December low, adjusted LDOS fell 28.60%; September 4 through year-end was -25.24%. That was a market-linked reset around a nearly flat-revenue FY2018 story, not a narrow volatility coil immediately before April. The stock had already rebounded 25.39% from 2018-12-24 through 2019-02-15 (`data/yahoo/LDOS.parquet`).

- **Catalyst ladder — present.** The [NASA award published 2019-02-07](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-awards-contract-for-end-user-it-services-technologies/), portfolio/capital-allocation actions, the [2019-04-30](https://investors.leidos.com/node/8931/html), [2019-07-30](https://investors.leidos.com/static-files/b62e5ed0-73ef-4c16-8d0e-0101d9dc6054), and [2019-10-29](https://investors.leidos.com/node/8986/html) earnings disclosures with higher guides, [index inclusion announced 2019-08-01](https://press.spglobal.com/2019-08-01-Leidos-Holdings-IDEX-Set-to-Join-S-P-500-Grubhub-Foot-Locker-to-Join-S-P-MidCap-400-National-Beverage-to-Join-S-P-SmallCap-600), and [Dynetics announced 2019-12-17](https://investors.leidos.com/static-files/7801c5f9-a6f7-4284-b663-81b6c374ead4) each changed a different underwriting input. The ladder was additive rather than one-day binary.

- **Relative breakaway — present.** April 30 produced the first 252-session closing high and a +9.88% durable gap. By the June 4 mechanical t0, trailing 42-session excess was already +25.17 pp versus XLI; calendar-2019 excess finished at +59.74 pp (`data/yahoo/LDOS.parquet`; `data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`).

### During the run

Strict local PIT treatment matters:

- `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` is usable by its `asof_date`: diluted EPS was $1.29 in Q1 (available 2019-04-30), $0.93 in Q2 (2019-07-30), and $1.11 in Q3 (2019-10-29), versus $0.66, $0.94, and $0.96 in the comparable 2018 quarters. Q1 GAAP EPS included the cyber-divestiture gain; the [2019-04-30 company release](https://investors.leidos.com/node/8931/html) reported non-GAAP EPS of $1.13 versus $1.03, so the raw EPS doubling is not a clean recurring-growth measure.
- `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet` contains the period/filed-date quarterly statements, but its `as_of` is the 2026 ingestion date. It is therefore not treated as strict episode-PIT evidence. The contemporaneous company releases—published/filed [2019-04-30](https://investors.leidos.com/node/8931/html), [2019-07-30](https://investors.leidos.com/static-files/b62e5ed0-73ef-4c16-8d0e-0101d9dc6054), and [2019-10-29](https://investors.leidos.com/node/8986/html)—supply the public quarterly figures instead: revenue growth accelerated from 5.5% to 7.9% to 10.1%; GAAP operating margin progressed from 7.5% to 7.7% to 8.8%; bookings were $3.3 billion, $3.0 billion, and $5.2 billion; and backlog moved from $21.5 billion to $21.7 billion to $23.9 billion.
- `data/edgar/statements.parquet` has no episode-usable LDOS annual row; its only LDOS row is FY2020 with a 2026 `as_of`. This is a coverage gap.

The runway was real but not frictionless. Backlog provided multi-year visibility and the guidance sequence rose at every quarterly disclosure, while Q2 margin showed that growth and profitability would not improve in a straight line. The FY2019 annual row in `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`—$11.094 billion revenue, $912 million operating income (8.22%), and $992 million operating cash flow—was only available as of 2020-05-02 and is therefore post-window confirmation, not evidence capital possessed during 2019. The company first published the full-year figures on [2020-02-18](https://investors.leidos.com/node/9051/html), after this case's run window.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

### Required false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** The public ladder is multi-quarter and tied to contracts, bookings, margins, cash flow, higher guides, and a later index rebalance. Short-interest and social-activity histories are null, so the ruling rests on the observed mechanism rather than a claim that speculative activity was absent.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** April 30, June 4, August 8, October 29, and December 18 qualifying gaps each held through 3, 5, and 10 sessions, and the re-underwriting arrived through multiple disclosures.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** Calendar-2019 excess was +59.74 pp versus XLI and +57.60 pp versus SPY; trailing excess was already +25.17 pp versus XLI over 42 sessions at t0.
- **options_mirage — possible.** No 2019 options history exists, so options amplification cannot be excluded. It is not required to explain the observed earnings, guidance, contract, index, and tape evidence.

### Episode and current hazards

- **Index-flow contamination:** likely for the August 8 liquidity spike; the [S&P 500 inclusion announced 2019-08-01](https://press.spglobal.com/2019-08-01-Leidos-Holdings-IDEX-Set-to-Join-S-P-500-Grubhub-Foot-Locker-to-Join-S-P-MidCap-400-National-Beverage-to-Join-S-P-SmallCap-600) explains why that day should not be generalized as organic demand.
- **Extended after vertical move:** possible by 2019 year-end after an +88.82% calendar-year total return. A current extension assessment is null because this study does not reconstruct a 2026 trend regime.
- **Catalyst exhaustion:** possible by late 2019 after three quarterly guidance steps and an acquisition announcement raised the expectation bar.
- **Backlog conversion / contract protest / recompete:** persistent operating hazards in a government-services model; the [GAO decision published 2019-05-30](https://www.gao.gov/products/b-417329%2Cb-417329.2%2Cb-417329.3) shows that an award is not identical to immediate revenue.
- **Acquisition integration and leverage:** possible after the [$1.65 billion cash Dynetics agreement announced 2019-12-17](https://investors.leidos.com/static-files/7801c5f9-a6f7-4284-b663-81b6c374ead4); the strategic runway came with financing and execution burden.
- **Call-wall overhead:** present in the 2026-07-18 model snapshot at $110, 3.31% above spot. This is current context only and says nothing about 2019.
