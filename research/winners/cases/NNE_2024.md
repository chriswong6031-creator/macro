# NNE 2024 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

NNE's October move was a real price/liquidity breakaway but a weak fundamental re-underwriting: Google and Amazon made advanced nuclear newly legible as an AI-power theme just as a tiny, newly listed, pre-revenue microreactor developer issued a dense stream of appointments, technology agreements and capital raises. That combination produced scarcity, narrative torque and abundant trading liquidity, not evidence that NNE had crossed the hard gates that distinguish durable nuclear developers—licensed design, financed deployment, contracted revenue or operating margin. The tape therefore escaped both SPY and the company's published fundamental model, but the ladder culminated in equity-and-warrant issuance rather than commercial proof; the November 22 peak then retraced 25.9% by year-end, and the repo census records a blow-off, `clean_hold: false`, and negative 126-day forward excess.

**FAILED BREAKAWAY:** this case is `case_type: failed_breakaway`; the run did not sustain, and the failure—not the temporary alpha—is the object of the study.
