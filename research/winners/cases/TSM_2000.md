# TSM 2000 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

TSM's early-2000 alpha came from several reinforcing facts rather than one headline: the pure-play foundry model was taking share as fabless chip companies outsourced production; fourth-quarter 1999 sales, operating income, and net income accelerated sharply at 107% utilization; 0.18-micron production and the coming 0.15-micron node supported mix; and the TSMC-Acer and Worldwide Semiconductor transactions offered scarce near-term capacity into an undersupplied market ([TSMC results, published 2000-01-27](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/english/1999/Q4/4q99fr.pdf); [WSMC merger release, published 2000-01-07](https://pr.tsmc.com/japanese/news/2196)). What failed was not the contemporaneous business acceleration—2000 revenue and net income later rose 127.3% and 165.1%—but the durability of the price re-rating: by the 2000-02-03 onset the ADR was already +202.13% over 126 sessions and +52.50% YTD, the strongest public evidence was fully visible, and the tape immediately began discounting a semiconductor-cycle peak and the technology-bubble unwind well before reported fundamentals rolled over. TSM underperformed SPY at every forward horizon from 5 through 252 sessions, and late-2000 inventory cuts showed why peak utilization and capacity scarcity were dangerous to extrapolate ([TSMC FY2000 results, published 2001-02-06](https://pr.tsmc.com/schinese/news/2302); [Taipei Times, published 2000-10-31](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/biz/archives/2000/10/31/0000059372)).

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`): the run did not sustain.**

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** TSM entered t0 after +93.65% over 63 sessions, +202.13% over 126 sessions, and a sequence of January highs. Any earlier 1999 base had already resolved; t0 was late-cycle extension (`data/yahoo/TSM.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present but exhausted at onset.** The WSMC capacity transaction and Q4 report materially upgraded earnings, utilization, node mix, and runway. Almost all high-quality positive information was public by 2000-01-27, one week before t0.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed immediately.** TSM led SPY by +41.45pp/21 sessions and +68.88pp/42 sessions into t0, but lagged it at every forward horizon, including -32.26pp at 63 sessions (`data/yahoo/{TSM,SPY}.parquet`; `data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`).
- **Liquidity confirmation — present but distributional.** The episode had z21=1.66 and five-day/60-day dollar volume of 2.19; the largest subsequent turnover occurred below t0. Liquidity arrived, but it did not hold price.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2000 local options history exists. Per-ticker history begins in 2026-06, so options cannot explain or rescue this episode.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The PIT-gated repo surfaces have a complete coverage gap: `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` contain **zero TSM rows**. Accordingly, all repo-native fundamental fields are **null**. The following external figures use only documents public on the stated dates:

- **Before/t0 (published 2000-01-27):** Q4 1999 revenue was NT$23.691 billion, +104% year over year; operating income was NT$7.681 billion, +166%; net income was NT$8.311 billion, +229%; utilization was 107%; shipments were 551,000 eight-inch-equivalent wafers. These numbers show real operating leverage, not a narrative-only move ([TSMC Q4 release, published 2000-01-27](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/english/1999/Q4/4q99fr.pdf)).
- **Runway visible near t0:** the 1999 annual report said 1999 wafer shipments rose 50% to about 1.8 million, 0.18-micron technology was in volume production, 0.15-micron availability was planned for early 2000, and acquisitions plus fab expansion were expected to lift total 2000 capacity to 3.4 million wafers ([TSMC annual report, published 2000](https://investor.tsmc.com/sites/ir/annual-report/1999/annual1999.pdf)).
- **During/after the failed run (published 2001-02-06):** FY2000 revenue reached NT$166.229 billion, +127.3%; net income NT$65.106 billion, +165.1%; EPS NT$5.70, +128.9%. Q4 utilization remained above 100%, and advanced 0.25/0.18-micron-and-beyond products were about half of quarterly sales. Yet management identified market normalization since November ([TSMC FY2000 release, published 2001-02-06](https://pr.tsmc.com/schinese/news/2302)).

The arc explains the false positive: revenue, profit, utilization, technology, and capacity all improved after t0, but their **rate of change was already capitalized**. Peak fundamentals were a lagging confirmation of peak expectations. Gross-margin data suitable for a clean before/during comparison are **null in the repo**; no interpolated margin is supplied.

## 7. Research echo

The repo contains no analyst-target history, and accessible contemporaneous target-level evidence is insufficient; consensus price target, target revisions, and target dispersion are therefore **null**. The available publication record still shows an expectations gap:

- On 2000-01-27, public coverage described quarterly profit as more than tripling while management forecast approximately 20% 2000 sales growth ([CNNfn, published 2000-01-27](https://money.cnn.com/2000/01/27/asia/taiwan_chips/)).
- By 2001-02-06, reported FY2000 sales had actually risen 127.3% and exceeded the company's later formal forecast, yet the ADR was already 56.86% below t0 after 252 sessions ([TSMC, published 2001-02-06](https://pr.tsmc.com/schinese/news/2302); `data/yahoo/TSM.parquet`).

Thus “price escaped the published model” has a precise read here: price ran ahead of the modest growth framing into t0, then escaped **downward** even as reported results surpassed it. The market shifted from valuing current scarcity to discounting future inventory, cyclicality, and tech-multiple compression. Exact analyst target revisions remain unknown rather than reconstructed.

- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The move developed over at least 126 sessions and multiple publications; no single binary event accounts for it.
- **sector_beta — possible.** The sector ETF field is blank and period cohort data are null, so semiconductor-beta cannot be directly removed. TSM's +68.88pp 42-session excess versus SPY proves index-relative alpha, but not foundry-relative alpha.
- **options_mirage — ruled_out.** No episode options evidence exists, so options cannot be asserted as the mechanism. Absence of data also prevents testing whether options amplified cash-market movement.

**Current hazards (local data through 2026-07-22):**

- **Extension:** possible. TSM closed at $421.21, 11.80% below its 2026-06-30 trailing-252-session closing high of $477.57; this is not the vertical t0 condition, but the level remains close enough to the annual high for valuation/expectation sensitivity to matter (`data/yahoo/TSM.parquet`).
- **Call walls:** unknown. All three specified current options surfaces are absent for TSM.
- **Catalyst exhaustion:** unknown for the current company; likely for the 2000 episode. The original scarcity ladder was exhausted by t0 and later replaced by inventory correction. No claim about the current catalyst calendar is made from a 2000 autopsy.
