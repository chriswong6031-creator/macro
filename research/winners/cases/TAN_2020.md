# TAN 2020 Winner Autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

TAN's 2020 alpha was a layered repricing, not a single-news jump: the March liquidation compressed a basket that had already been strong in 2019; renewables then proved unusually resilient in the real economy, solar-heavy holdings preserved margins and liquidity better than feared, policy probabilities rose in both Europe and the United States, and increasingly large ETF inflows made the theme self-reinforcing. The evidence says broad clean-energy beta supplied most of the direction—TAN's 21/42-day excess ending at t0 was `+11.25/+18.50 pp` versus ICLN but `+20.92/+35.44 pp` versus SPY—while TAN's concentrated exposure to SolarEdge, Enphase, First Solar, and Sunrun supplied the cohort alpha. The re-underwriting ladder ran from the IEA's evidence that renewables were the only energy source expected to grow in 2020 ([published 2020-04-30](https://www.iea.org/news/global-energy-demand-to-plunge-this-year-as-a-result-of-the-biggest-shock-since-the-second-world-war)), through the Biden campaign's `$2 trillion` clean-infrastructure plan ([published 2020-07-14](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/biden-campaign-press-release-the-biden-plan-build-modern-sustainable-infrastructure-and)), into constituent earnings, the election, tax-credit extension, and Georgia Senate runoffs. Liquidity confirmed the thesis—TAN's dollar volume expanded from `$47.43 million` at t0 to `$826.99 million` on 2021-01-06—but eventually outran the published component models. The visible run held from 2020-03-23 through 2021-02-09; it did not persist indefinitely, as the local tape is `-55.54%` from that peak through 2026-07-17.

## 4. Stage anatomy

**Compressed prior — present, but short-cycle.** `data/yahoo/TAN.parquet` shows a `-46.92%` fall from 2020-02-20 to 2020-03-23, followed by a `+120.79%` recovery to t0. Calendar 2019 was already `+66.53%`, so this was a violent macro reset rather than a long neglected base.

**Catalyst ladder — present.** Resilient renewable demand, Sunrun/Vivint scale, U.S. and EU policy proposals, SolarEdge/Enphase/First Solar earnings, the election, tax-credit extension, and Georgia Senate control gave capital multiple publication-dated chances to revise duration, margins, and policy probability.

**Relative breakaway — present, with sector-beta caveat.** At t0 TAN was ahead of SPY by `+20.92 pp` over 21d and `+35.44 pp` over 42d, but its excess over ICLN was `+11.25/+18.50 pp`; at 21d it was only about `+10-11 pp` ahead of PBW/QCLN. The breakaway was real, but it combined theme beta with solar concentration.

**Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 dollar-volume z21 was `+1.34`; the 2020-11-05 election-probability session reached `$345.79 million`; 2021-01-06 reached `$826.99 million` and z60 `+5.88`. More than half of greater-than-1% gaps held for five sessions, although several peak-period high-volume days were negative.

**Options convexity — unknown.** No 2020 per-ticker options history exists in the repo; history begins in 2026-06. Current TAN options snapshots are also absent. No historical convexity or signed-flow claim is supported.
