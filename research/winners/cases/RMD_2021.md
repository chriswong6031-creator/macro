# RMD 2021 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

RMD's 2021 episode was a competitive-dislocation re-rating with a product-cycle amplifier, not a generic Health Care rally: ResMed entered June with strong pre-pandemic cash generation but flat latest-quarter revenue, a tax-distorted reported loss, and a stock that had fallen 14.51% from January 11 through April 30; Philips' April 26 foam-quality warning became a 3–4 million-device recall on June 14, FDA escalation extended the likely supply gap, and ResMed had the AirSense 11 launch plus recovering sleep-patient starts with which to absorb displaced demand ([Philips, published 2021-04-26](https://www.usa.philips.com/a-w/about/news/archive/standard/news/press/2021/20210426-philips-first-quarter-results-2021.html); [Philips, published 2021-06-14](https://www.usa.philips.com/a-w/about/news/archive/standard/news/press/2021/20210614-philips-issues-recall-notification-to-mitigate-potential-health-risks-related-to-the-sound-abatement-foam-component-in-certain-sleep-and-respiratory-care-devices.html); [ResMed, published 2021-08-16](https://investor.resmed.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/68/resmed-launches-airsense-11-pap-series-advancing-digital-health-in-sleep-apnea-treatment)). The cash tape confirmed a multi-session repricing and reached +29.81 percentage points versus XLV from the first June high to the September 8 peak. The move was real but not permanent: component and freight constraints limited incremental FY2022 device revenue to an estimated $300–$350 million and compressed gross margin, Philips began remediation on September 1, and the stock peaked at ResMed's September 8 investor day as the published model caught up ([ResMed call, public 2021-08-05](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_fe008488bc9ed6e9f9701fa7c8aba84e/resmed/db/2294/21499/webcast_transcript/RMD-USQ_Transcript_2021-08-05.pdf); [Philips, published 2021-09-01](https://www.usa.philips.com/a-w/about/news/archive/standard/news/press/2021/20210901-philips-starts-repair-and-replacement-program-of-first-generation-dreamstation-devices-in-the-us-in-relation-to-earlier-announced-recall-notification.html); [ResMed investor-day materials, public 2021-09-08](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_2638450cb20e3187b5f401d19264b32d/resmed/db/2271/21738/pdf/RMD-Investor-Day_08SEP21_MASTER-Presentation_vFINAL.pdf)). Forward excess versus XLV was still +15.17 points at 63 sessions but was −1.09 at 126 and −12.98 at 252: a genuine share-shift window whose scarcity premium and relative alpha were not sustained.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** From 2021-01-11 through the post-results close on April 30, RMD fell 14.51%; April 30–May 19 then held inside a 4.17% closing range. The latest public quarter had flat revenue and a tax-distorted GAAP loss, despite positive underlying cash generation.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** The April Philips warning, June recall, June/July FDA escalation, August quantified revenue opportunity, provider survey, AirSense 11 launch, and September investor day supplied distinct rungs. Philips remediation on September 1 was the first clear counter-rung.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** Mechanical t0 carried +20.62 pp of 21-day excess versus XLV and strict 63/126/252-day closing highs. From June 9 to September 8, excess expanded to +29.81 pp versus XLV and +33.21 pp versus SPY.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0's `z21=0.99` was borderline, but the five-day/60-day dollar-volume ratio was 1.62 and recall week produced four $193m–$285m sessions. The recall-day gap was small, yet its pre-gap line held through ten sessions.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2021 per-ticker options history exists in the repository. Current files cannot be projected backward, and no historical options contribution is claimed.

## 7. Research echo

The clean “price escaped the published model” moment occurred immediately. On 2021-06-17, a public estimate put the average analyst target at $217 while spot was about $236, an 8% shortfall after the recall repricing ([Forbes/Trefis, published 2021-06-17 at 09:00 ET](https://www.forbes.com/sites/greatspeculations/2021/06/17/despite-philips-recall-resmed-stock-appears-fully-priced/)). By June 21, Needham had raised its NYSE target from $229 to $267 and framed recall-driven incremental sales scenarios of $106m–$372m, showing research converting an operational shock into explicit estimates ([MD+DI, published 2021-06-21](https://www.mddionline.com/regulatory-quality/is-philips-loss-resmed-s-gain-in-sleep-apnea-devices-)). Yet RMD's local raw close reached $297.36 on September 8, 11.37% above that revised target.

Research continued to catch up after the peak. A September 28 Australian-market review said Morningstar raised fair value 68% to A$34 while the CDI traded near A$37.29; Ord Minnett moved its target from A$28.50 to A$32.00; and Morgans raised FY2021–FY2023 earnings estimates 18% and its target to A$41.34 ([Morningstar Australia, published 2021-09-28](https://www.morningstar.com.au/stocks/opportunity-for-resmed-in-philips-recall)). These are ASX CDI figures, not NYSE targets, so they are not merged numerically with the U.S. series. The mosaic read is still consistent: targets and earnings models rose because the recall changed market-share assumptions, but spot moved first and by the investor day much of the scarcity case was already embedded.

The repository has no analyst-target history. All target observations in this section are external, qualitative case evidence only.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

Historical and current hazards:
