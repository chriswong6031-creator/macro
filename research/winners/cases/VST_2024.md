# VST 2024 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Vistra's September-2024 breakaway was the second leg of a broader merchant-power rerating: Energy Harbor had already added roughly 4 GW of nuclear generation and about one million retail customers on 2024-03-01 ([Vistra, published 2024-03-01](https://investor.vistracorp.com/2024-03-01-Vistra-Completes-Energy-Harbor-Acquisition)), S&P announced index inclusion on 2024-05-03 ([S&P Global, published 2024-05-03](https://press.spglobal.com/2024-05-03-Vistra-Set-to-Join-S-P-500-Aaon-to-Join-S-P-MidCap-400-Marathon-Digital-Holdings-to-Join-S-P-SmallCap-600)), and Q2 showed a 40% rise in ongoing-operations adjusted EBITDA ([Vistra, published 2024-08-08](https://investor.vistracorp.com/2024-08-08-Vistra-Reports-Second-Quarter-2024-Results)). The mechanical t0, 2024-09-20, coincided with Constellation's 20-year Microsoft agreement to restart Three Mile Island Unit 1—not a Vistra contract, but a public price-discovery event for scarce, around-the-clock nuclear output ([Constellation, published 2024-09-20](https://investors.constellationenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/constellation-launch-crane-clean-energy-center-restoring-jobs)). Vistra then supplied company-specific confirmation on 2024-11-07 through higher 2024 guidance, 2025 guidance, another $1B repurchase authorization, and greater ownership of its zero-carbon/retail subsidiary ([Vistra filing exhibit, filed 2024-11-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1692819/000119312524252608/d852941dex991.htm)). The mosaic was scarce dispatchable generation, integrated retail hedging, acquired nuclear scale, rising forward power value, and capital return—not simply “AI electricity demand.”

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2024-03-01 - M&A close:** Vistra completed the Energy Harbor acquisition, adding about 4,000 MW of 24/7 nuclear generation and roughly one million retail customers ([Vistra, published 2024-03-01](https://investor.vistracorp.com/2024-03-01-Vistra-Completes-Energy-Harbor-Acquisition)).
- **2024-05-03 - index inclusion:** S&P Dow Jones Indices announced that Vistra would join the S&P 500 effective before the 2024-05-08 open ([S&P Global, published 2024-05-03](https://press.spglobal.com/2024-05-03-Vistra-Set-to-Join-S-P-500-Aaon-to-Join-S-P-MidCap-400-Marathon-Digital-Holdings-to-Join-S-P-SmallCap-600)).
- **2024-08-08 - earnings/guidance:** Q2 ongoing-operations adjusted EBITDA increased $406M to $1.414B, Energy Harbor contributed to the gain, and 2024 EBITDA guidance remained $4.55B-$5.05B ([Vistra, published 2024-08-08](https://investor.vistracorp.com/2024-08-08-Vistra-Reports-Second-Quarter-2024-Results)).
- **2024-09-20 - peer contract read-through:** Constellation announced a 20-year Microsoft PPA supporting the planned restart of Three Mile Island Unit 1. This was not a Vistra agreement; it provided a public market reference for nuclear output value ([Constellation, published 2024-09-20](https://investors.constellationenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/constellation-launch-crane-clean-energy-center-restoring-jobs)).
- **2024-11-07 - earnings/guidance/capital return:** Vistra reported $1.444B Q3 ongoing-operations adjusted EBITDA, raised and narrowed 2024 EBITDA guidance to $5.0B-$5.2B, initiated $5.5B-$6.1B 2025 guidance, and authorized another $1B of repurchases ([Vistra filing exhibit, filed 2024-11-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1692819/000119312524252608/d852941dex991.htm)).
- **2025-02-27 - earnings/guidance:** full-year ongoing-operations adjusted EBITDA reached $5.656B, adjusted free cash flow before growth reached $2.888B, and 2025 guidance remained $5.5B-$6.1B EBITDA and $3.0B-$3.6B adjusted FCF before growth ([Vistra, published 2025-02-27](https://investor.vistracorp.com/2025-02-27-Vistra-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2024-Results)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior - present, but earlier than t0.** The major base belonged to late 2023/early 2024 before Energy Harbor closed. By September the stock was already extended, so t0 is a second-leg breakout rather than the beginning of the whole rerating.
- **Catalyst ladder - present.** Acquisition close, index inclusion, Q2 operating proof, peer nuclear price discovery, and Q3 capital-return/guidance confirmation accumulated across the year.
- **Relative breakaway - present.** VST beat XLU by +42.41pp at 42 days and +24.39pp at 126 days.
- **Liquidity confirmation - present.** T0 traded $2.520B and held; the Q3 gap also held. January's larger volume was two-sided stress, not clean accumulation evidence.
- **Options convexity - unknown.** No episode-timely trade-level archive supports directional option-flow claims.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Before t0, Q2 net income was $467M, ongoing-operations net income $492M, and ongoing-operations adjusted EBITDA $1.414B, up $406M year over year because of Energy Harbor, fleet optimization, and retail performance. Vistra had hedged 94% of expected remaining-2024 generation, 86% of 2025, and 55% of 2026 as of 2024-08-05 ([Vistra, published 2024-08-08](https://investor.vistracorp.com/2024-08-08-Vistra-Reports-Second-Quarter-2024-Results)). The local PIT filing row for 2024-06-30 shows $3.845B revenue, $808M operating income, $365M net income, and $1.624B cash (`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`).

During the run, Q3 GAAP net income was $1.837B, ongoing-operations adjusted EBITDA $1.444B, and cash flow from operations $1.702B. Management raised/narrowed 2024 EBITDA and initiated a higher 2025 range; it also disclosed 100% hedging for the balance of 2024, 96% for 2025, and 64% for 2026 as of 2024-09-30 ([Vistra filing exhibit, filed 2024-11-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1692819/000119312524252608/d852941dex991.htm)). The local Q3 filing row shows $6.288B revenue, $2.588B operating income, $1.888B net income, and $905M cash (`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`).

Full-year 2024 then produced $2.812B GAAP net income, $4.563B operating cash flow, $5.656B ongoing-operations adjusted EBITDA, and $2.888B ongoing-operations adjusted free cash flow before growth. Vistra attributed the $1.516B adjusted-EBITDA increase partly to Energy Harbor and estimated nuclear production tax credits ([Vistra, published 2025-02-27](https://investor.vistracorp.com/2025-02-27-Vistra-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2024-Results)). “Cash runway” is not the right mature-company lens; hedge coverage, free cash flow, debt service, nuclear operations, and capital return are. Coverage gap: commodity and hedge mark-to-market accounting can make GAAP revenue and profit volatile, so non-GAAP measures are useful but must remain reconciled to filings.

## 8. Ownership context (context-only)

**Context-only; descriptive background, explicitly not a signal.** The local `data/quiver/sec13f.parquet` snapshot does not provide a complete 2024 VST holder panel, so holder-breadth change is **unknown**. Index inclusion is separately observable from the 2024-05-03 S&P announcement; it must not be disguised as inferred 13F demand. Form 13F holdings can become public up to 45 days after quarter-end ([SEC FAQ, dated 2023-05-24 and updated 2026-03-06](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/division-investment-management-frequently-asked-questions/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-13f)). Episode insider records are incomplete locally and must be keyed to Form 4 filing dates, generally within two business days ([SEC ownership-report rule release, published 2002-08-27](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2002/08/ownership-reports-trading-officers-directors-principal-security-holders)). Neither is used as a positive input.

- **one_day_binary - ruled_out.** T0 and the Q3 results gap both held at 3/5/10 sessions.
- **sector_beta - ruled_out.** VST exceeded XLU by +42.41pp at 42 days and +24.39pp at 126 days.
- **options_mirage - ruled_out.** The mechanism does not depend on options evidence, which is unknown.
