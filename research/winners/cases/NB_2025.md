# NB 2025 — failed breakaway winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

NB's 2025 alpha was a re-underwriting of *project-financing probability*, amplified by policy scarcity and attention, rather than a re-underwriting of near-term earnings: EXIM advanced Elk Creek into independent technical review, UKEF supplied a preliminary non-binding expression of interest, and the Pentagon funded drilling and alloy work ([NioCorp, 2025-02-04](https://niocorp.com/exim-advances-niocorp-elk-creek-critical-minerals-project-to-independent-technical-review/); [NioCorp, 2025-05-16](https://niocorp.com/british-export-credit-agency-issues-expression-of-interest-to-niocorp-for-potential-debt-guarantee-of-up-to-200-million-for-the-elk-creek-critical-minerals-project/); [DoD, 2025-08-05](https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4264389/department-of-defense-awards-10-million-to-develop-a-domestic-mine-to-master-al/)). Capital then solved the immediate runway problem—cash rose from $1.3 million at March 31 to a preliminary $162.8 million at September 30—but did so through repeated equity issuance, not operating cash flow ([SEC, filed 2025-05-08](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1512228/000153949725001285/n2574_x261-10q.htm); [NioCorp, 2025-10-12](https://www.niocorp.com/niocorp-provides-preliminary-unaudited-financial-results-for-the-three-month-period-ended-september-30-2025/)). At the October 14 peak the company still had no operating revenue, no updated feasibility study, no final EXIM commitment, no construction financing, and an old $1.141 billion upfront-capex reference; the stock was already +422% YTD at the October 3 detector date ([SEC, filed 2025-09-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1512228/000153949725002331/n2574_x280-10k.htm); local prices below). The peak coincided with the densest liquidity and a new $150.2 million issuance at $9.34, while legacy warrants were exercisable at $11.50 ([SEC, filed 2025-10-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1512228/000153949725002714/n2574_x297-8k.htm); [SEC, filed 2025-09-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1512228/000153949725002331/n2574_x280-10k.htm)). Thus the tape confirmed attention but not scarce supply: the market capitalized a sequence of contingent financing steps, the issuer monetized that repricing, and no harder economic milestone arrived quickly enough to absorb the new paper.

**FAILED BREAKAWAY:** this is a `failed_breakaway` case; the run did not sustain, and the distinction from a durable winner is the combination of contingent project economics, equity-created runway, peak liquidity that migrated into reversal days, and no completed financing/feasibility/offtake gate during the episode.
