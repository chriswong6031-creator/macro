# OKLO 2024 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Oklo's late-2024 breakaway was a pre-revenue advanced-nuclear probability reset: regulatory progress reduced fuel/deployment uncertainty just as hyperscale data-center power demand made future baseload contracts more economically legible. The exact t0 catalyst was the 2024-10-15 Department of Energy approval of the Aurora fuel-fabrication facility's conceptual safety design ([Oklo, published 2024-10-15](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2024/DOE-Approves-Conceptual-Safety-Design-Report-for-Oklos-Aurora-Fuel-Fabrication-Facility/default.aspx)). It was followed by letters of intent for up to 750 MW of data-center power, an expanded 2.1-GW customer pipeline ([Oklo, published 2024-11-13](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2024/Oklo-Secures-Partnerships-for-Up-to-750-Megawatts-of-Power-for-U.S.-Data-Centers/)), a proposed radioisotope acquisition ([Oklo, published 2024-11-14](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2024/Oklo-Announces-Proposed-Acquisition-of-Atomic-Alchemy-to-Expand-into-Radioisotope-Market/default.aspx)), and a non-binding 12-GW framework with Switch ([Oklo, published 2024-12-18](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2024/Oklo-and-Switch-Form-Landmark-Strategic-Relationship-to-Deploy-12-Gigawatts-of-Advanced-Nuclear-Power-One-of-the-Largest-Corporate-Clean-Power-Agreements-Ever-Signed/default.aspx?os=w)). The move was not justified by current revenue—there was none—but by a large change in perceived licensing, fuel, customer, and commercialization pathways. That makes this a powerful winner example and a high-hazard one.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2024-10-15 - regulatory decision:** DOE approved the conceptual safety design report for the Aurora fuel-fabrication facility at Idaho National Laboratory, advancing the recovered-fuel pathway ([Oklo, published 2024-10-15](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2024/DOE-Approves-Conceptual-Safety-Design-Report-for-Oklos-Aurora-Fuel-Fabrication-Facility/default.aspx)).
- **2024-11-13 - partnership:** Oklo announced letters of intent with two data-center providers for up to 750 MW, taking the stated customer pipeline to about 2.1 GW ([Oklo, published 2024-11-13](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2024/Oklo-Secures-Partnerships-for-Up-to-750-Megawatts-of-Power-for-U.S.-Data-Centers/)).
- **2024-11-14 - M&A announcement:** Oklo proposed a $25M all-stock acquisition of Atomic Alchemy to add radioisotope production and a potential earlier revenue path ([Oklo, published 2024-11-14](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2024/Oklo-Announces-Proposed-Acquisition-of-Atomic-Alchemy-to-Expand-into-Radioisotope-Market/default.aspx)).
- **2024-12-18 - partnership:** Oklo and Switch announced a **non-binding** master agreement framework for up to 12 GW through 2044, with binding project agreements still to be negotiated at milestones ([Oklo, published 2024-12-18](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2024/Oklo-and-Switch-Form-Landmark-Strategic-Relationship-to-Deploy-12-Gigawatts-of-Advanced-Nuclear-Power-One-of-the-Largest-Corporate-Clean-Power-Agreements-Ever-Signed/default.aspx?os=w)).
- **2025-01-17 - partnership:** Oklo and RPower announced an MOU for phased data-center power that would begin with gas generation and transition to Aurora powerhouses ([Oklo, published 2025-01-17](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2025/Oklo-and-RPower-Join-Forces-to-Accelerate-Power-Availability-for-Data-Centers/default.aspx)).
- **2025-02-25 - government program:** Oklo announced participation in a DOE voucher program with Oak Ridge National Laboratory for advanced structural-material evaluation ([Oklo, published 2025-02-25](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2025/Oklo-Collaborates-with-the-U.S.-DOE-and-Oak-Ridge-National-Laboratory-to-Advance-Next-Generation-Materials-Development/default.aspx)).
- **2025-03-05 - M&A close:** Oklo announced that it had closed the Atomic Alchemy acquisition, making it a subsidiary; the market-knowable date is the 2025-03-05 publication date, not the disclosed 2025-02-28 closing date ([Oklo, published 2025-03-05](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2025/Oklo-Closes-Acquisition-of-Radioisotope-Producer-Atomic-Alchemy/default.aspx)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior - present.** The de-SPAC had only five months of OKLO trading history, no revenue, and unresolved licensing/fuel/timing risk. T0 was its first new 3-month close high, but a full 12-month base did not exist.
- **Catalyst ladder - present.** DOE fuel-facility progress, data-center LOIs, the Switch framework, RPower, and Atomic Alchemy each changed a different commercialization assumption.
- **Relative breakaway - present.** OKLO exceeded NLR by +97.65pp at 21 days and +103.77pp at 126 days.
- **Liquidity confirmation - present.** T0 and the next session sharply expanded volume, while January-February dollar volume exceeded $1B on five sessions.
- **Options convexity - unknown.** No trade-level episode archive supports claims about directional options demand.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

This was not a conventional revenue-and-margin arc. Oklo was pre-revenue throughout the core episode. Before t0, its 2024-08-13 Form 10-Q reported $294.6M of cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities at 2024-06-30, a six-month operating loss of $25.1M, net loss of $53.4M, and operating cash use of $17.0M ([Oklo 10-Q, filed 2024-08-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849056/000141057824001386/oklo-20240630x10q.htm)). The filing also stated that OKLO trading began on 2024-05-10.

During the run, the 2024-11-14 Form 10-Q reported $288.5M of cash, cash equivalents, and marketable debt securities at 2024-09-30, nine-month operating loss of $37.4M, net loss of $63.3M, and operating cash use of $24.9M ([Oklo 10-Q, filed 2024-11-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849056/000162828024047875/oklo-20240930.htm)). A public B. Riley report on 2024-11-15 summarized the same $288.5M liquidity, zero debt, and zero estimated 2024-2026 revenue while raising its model on customer and regulatory progress ([B. Riley, published 2024-11-15](https://octanecdn.com/brileyfincom/brileyfincom_252665509.pdf)).

The fundamental “improvement” was therefore balance-sheet runway plus probability-weighted project progress, not current sales or margin. The cash balance could fund development work, but project finance, licensing, fuel fabrication, construction, and conversion of non-binding instruments remained open. The correct nulls are revenue: zero; gross margin: not applicable; operating margin: negative; commercial reactor cash generation: absent.

## 7. Research echo

Published research repeatedly chased a faster-moving probability model. B. Riley moved its target from $10 to $27 on 2024-11-15, citing the 2.1-GW customer pipeline and regulatory progress; OKLO closed that day at $18.00 ([B. Riley, published 2024-11-15](https://octanecdn.com/brileyfincom/brileyfincom_252665509.pdf)). Wedbush moved to $45 from $26 on 2025-01-24 while OKLO closed at $41.82 ([Investing.com, published 2025-01-24](https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/wedbush-raises-oklo-stock-target-to-45-on-ai-datacenter-outlook-93CH-3829661)). On 2025-02-07, B. Riley moved to $58 from $27 while OKLO closed at $55.49 ([Investing.com, published 2025-02-07](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/oklo-shares-surge-on-upgraded-price-target-by-b-riley-3856934)). Complete consensus history is unavailable, but each dated revision left only limited same-day headroom. That is the “price escaped the published model” pattern in an early-stage name: targets roughly doubled, yet the market approached them almost immediately because small changes in assumed project probability produced large equity-value changes.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze - possible.** OKLO was a recent de-SPAC with large narrative sensitivity; however, DOE and customer-path milestones were real public events.
- **one_day_binary - ruled_out.** The run survived multiple gaps and still showed +103.77pp excess versus NLR at 126 days, though individual November and February gaps failed.
- **sector_beta - ruled_out.** NLR was negative over the 63- and 126-day windows while OKLO remained sharply positive.
- **options_mirage - ruled_out.** The case does not depend on options evidence, which is unknown.

Current hazards were `pre_revenue`, `low_float`, `extended_after_vertical_move`, `licensing_timeline`, `first_of_a_kind_construction`, `fuel_availability`, `future_project_financing`, `nonbinding_customer_pipeline`, `policy_dependency`, and `catalyst_exhausted`. The non-binding nature of major agreements and the late failed gaps are especially important death-pattern checks for future analogs.
