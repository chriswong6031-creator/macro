# MNSO 2021 — failed breakaway autopsy

## Bottom line

MNSO's January 2021 alpha was a post-IPO, low-history rerating built from several inputs rather than a new January breakthrough: a $20 October IPO established a scarce public tape; the December report showed sharp sequential recovery, continued store growth, and an art-toy adjacency; Goldman then raised its target; and reopening/value-retail expectations pulled the stock above both the index and broad consumer discretionary. The tape was real—MNSO led SPY by 70.16 percentage points over 42 sessions at the January 25 onset, made a 63-session high, and traded at +2.43 z21 dollar volume—but the operating evidence beneath it was still a recovery from a damaged base. At the last report available before t0, revenue was down 30.7% year over year, gross margin had fallen to 25.2% from 31.4%, and operating profit had become a small loss; the later February report confirmed that revenue, operating profit, and adjusted profit remained far below the prior year. The price had also already moved above Goldman's $28.80 published model, there was no same-day company catalyst, and MNSO had only 68 trading observations. It peaked just 10 sessions after t0, then the combination of incomplete international recovery, margin/mix pressure, COVID recurrence, China-ADR risk, and ordinary post-IPO price discovery overwhelmed the reopening narrative. The breakaway therefore described a fast re-rating of expectations, not a durable earnings inflection.

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`): the run did not sustain.**

## Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** MNSO had only 68 trading observations, was already +78.80% over 42 sessions, and had printed a +16.53% target-day jump on 2020-12-21. This was post-IPO price discovery, not a long, tight base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present, but stale at t0.** IPO funding, December sequential recovery, TOP TOY, and Goldman's target increase formed a coherent ladder; no new company publication was found on January 25, and February results weakened the fully recovered interpretation.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0, MNSO led SPY by 17.86 pp over 21 sessions and 70.16 pp over 42, and led XRT by 4.07 pp and 45.95 pp. Forward SPY excess was already -19.28 pp at 21 sessions.

## Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** T0 fell inside the January 2021 retail-trading dislocation and MNSO was a new, short-history issue, but local historical short interest and trade-level evidence are unavailable. The pre-t0 December earnings/target leg means “pure meme” is too strong.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** T0 gained 11.88%, but the 42-session return was +78.80% and the December earnings/target events created distinct earlier legs. The failure was a blow-off, not a single binary announcement.
- **sector_beta — possible.** XRT was already +18.46% over 21 days and later accelerated in the meme-retail episode; MNSO's 21-day excess over XRT was only +4.07 pp. Yet its 42-day excess over XRT was +45.95 pp, so retail beta cannot explain the full move.
- **options_mirage — ruled_out.** Options are not credited as confirmation because 2021 history is unavailable. This verdict rules out the *claim*, not historical options activity itself.
