# NET 2020 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Cloudflare's October 2020 breakaway was a platform rerating built from three mutually reinforcing facts: internet traffic and security demand were increasing, the company kept growing revenue near 50% while adding large customers, and its product surface expanded from content delivery and DDoS defense into developer compute and enterprise Zero Trust networking. Workers Durable Objects made stateful serverless applications possible on Cloudflare's edge, while Cloudflare One packaged access, gateway, and network services into a coherent enterprise architecture. The 2020-10-09 mechanical `t0` preceded the October 12 Cloudflare One announcement, so that public launch is confirmation rather than retroactive cause. Q3 then delivered 54% revenue growth and roughly 100 net new large customers, while Q4 sustained 50% growth and a 119% dollar-based net retention rate. Free cash flow remained negative and valuation became demanding, but the move had a multi-product, multi-quarter operating mechanism. Sources: Cloudflare's [Q2 release, published 2020-08-06](https://www.cloudflare.com/en-gb/press-releases/2020/cloudflare-announces-second-quarter-2020-financial-results/), [Durable Objects introduction, published 2020-09-28](https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-workers-durable-objects/), [Cloudflare One release, published 2020-10-12](https://cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2020/Cloudflare-Announces-Cloudflare-One-a-Platform-to-Connect-and-Secure-Companies-and-Remote-Teams-Anywhere-On-Any-Device/default.aspx), and [Q3 release, published 2020-11-05](https://cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2020/Cloudflare-Announces-Third-Quarter-2020-Financial-Results/default.aspx).

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2020-08-06 - earnings:** Q2 revenue grew 48% to $99.7 million, operating margin improved 450 basis points to negative 24.8%, operating cash flow turned positive at $4.0 million, and Cloudflare added a record number of large and paying customers. [Company release, published 2020-08-06](https://www.cloudflare.com/en-gb/press-releases/2020/cloudflare-announces-second-quarter-2020-financial-results/).
- **2020-09-28 - product launch:** Cloudflare introduced Workers Durable Objects in beta, adding globally coordinated state and storage to its serverless platform and enabling classes of real-time applications that stateless Workers could not support alone. [Company engineering post, published 2020-09-28](https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-workers-durable-objects/).
- **2020-10-12 - product launch:** Cloudflare announced Cloudflare One, combining Zero Trust access, secure web gateway, browser isolation, and network connectivity into a single global platform for distributed work. [Company release, published 2020-10-12](https://cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2020/Cloudflare-Announces-Cloudflare-One-a-Platform-to-Connect-and-Secure-Companies-and-Remote-Teams-Anywhere-On-Any-Device/default.aspx).
- **2020-11-05 - earnings:** Q3 revenue rose 54% to $114.2 million, gross margin was 76.3%, Cloudflare added about 100 net new large customers and its first customer above $10 million of annualized revenue, while full-year revenue expectations increased. [Company release, published 2020-11-05](https://cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2020/Cloudflare-Announces-Third-Quarter-2020-Financial-Results/default.aspx).
- **2021-02-11 - earnings:** FY2020 revenue grew 50% to $431.1 million; Q4 revenue grew 50% to $125.9 million, dollar-based net retention reached 119%, and GAAP operating margin improved materially year over year, though free cash flow remained negative. [Company release, published 2021-02-11](https://cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2021/Cloudflare-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2020-Financial-Results/default.aspx).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior - present.** The company had only been public for about a year, remained GAAP-loss-making, and was still commonly modeled as a CDN/security vendor rather than an integrated edge platform.
- **Catalyst ladder - present.** Q2 execution, stateful serverless compute, Cloudflare One, Q3 large-customer evidence, and Q4 retention accumulated across six months.
- **Relative breakaway - present.** NET exceeded IGV by 40.74 percentage points at 21 sessions and 64.44 points at 42 sessions.
- **Liquidity confirmation - present.** The October product window contained four of the five largest dollar-volume sessions, and the post-Q3 gap held at 3/5/10 sessions.
- **Options convexity - unknown.** A public recap reported concentrated calls before `t0`, but full NBBO, open/close status, aggregate position history, and dealer state are unavailable.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Before the breakaway, Q2 established both demand and the cost of expansion. Revenue was $99.7 million (+48%), GAAP gross profit was $75.6 million at a 75.8% margin, GAAP operating loss was $24.7 million, operating cash flow was positive $4.0 million, free cash flow was negative $20.2 million, and cash plus available-for-sale securities was $1.069 billion. Management increased full-year revenue expectations to $404-$408 million while continuing to invest through the pandemic. [Company release, published 2020-08-06](https://www.cloudflare.com/en-gb/press-releases/2020/cloudflare-announces-second-quarter-2020-financial-results/).

Q3 extended the high-growth pattern: revenue was $114.2 million (+54%), gross margin was 76.3%, GAAP operating loss was $21.3 million, operating cash flow was positive $2.0 million, free cash flow was negative $17.9 million, and cash plus securities was $1.051 billion. The approximately 100 net new large customers and first customer above $10 million of annualized revenue helped shift the model from traffic utility to enterprise platform. [Company release, published 2020-11-05](https://cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2020/Cloudflare-Announces-Third-Quarter-2020-Financial-Results/default.aspx).

For FY2020, revenue reached $431.1 million (+50%), GAAP operating margin improved by 1,280 basis points to negative 24.8%, free cash flow was negative $92.1 million, and cash plus securities ended at $1.032 billion. Q4 revenue was $125.9 million (+50%) and dollar-based net retention was 119%. [Company release, published 2021-02-11](https://cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2021/Cloudflare-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2020-Financial-Results/default.aspx). The arc was strong growth, customer expansion, and improving loss ratios with ample runway; positive free cash flow was not yet established.

## 6. Options context

The public evidence supports isolated activity magnitude only. An OptionsHawk recap dated 2020-08-17 reported 6,500 near-term August $42 calls active and 500 February $45/$50 call spreads. [OptionsHawk recap, published 2020-08-17](https://optionshawk.com/wp-content/uploads/Market-Recap-8-17-20.pdf). The recap's directional wording is not a complete trade-level NBBO record; opening-versus-closing status, aggregate open-interest change, counterparty direction, volatility surface, and dealer hedge state cannot be established for the episode. Because the cited activity also preceded `t0` by nearly two months, options convexity remains **unknown** and is not used as the primary mechanism.

## 7. Research echo

Published models moved quickly but still lagged the tape. On 2020-10-05, Baird's $48 target sat above a reported $41.75 spot, while the cited 12-analyst average was $48.22 and range was $42-$55. [Investing.com, published 2020-10-05](https://www.investing.com/news/robert-w-baird-stick-to-their-buy-rating-for-cloudflare-inc-2315336). On 2020-11-06, after Q3, Oppenheimer set $77 while reported spot was $58; the cited nine-analyst average was only $55.88, already below spot, with a $42-$77 range. [Investing.com, published 2020-11-06](https://www.investing.com/news/oppenheimer-stick-to-their-buy-rating-for-cloudflare-2342311). The stock then traded through the high end during the visible run. The research echo moved from a CDN/security growth model toward a larger edge-compute and Zero Trust platform model, but the price escaped the published average before consensus fully reset.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze: ruled_out.** The episode followed a multi-product and multi-quarter enterprise-software ladder with large-customer growth and no evidence here that short-covering was the primary mechanism.
- **one_day_binary: ruled_out.** Product launches and two earnings prints extended the move across more than four months; the post-Q3 gap held at 3/5/10 sessions.
- **sector_beta: ruled_out.** At 42 sessions, NET exceeded IGV by 64.44 percentage points and SPY by 61.03 points.
- **options_mirage: possible.** Concentrated calls were reported before the run, but signed flow and dealer state are unknown; the platform and financial evidence stands independently.
- **Current episode hazards:** `extended_after_vertical_move`, `multiple_compression`, `negative_free_cash_flow`, `hyperscaler_competition`, `platform_execution`, and `network_outage_or_security_tail_risk`.
