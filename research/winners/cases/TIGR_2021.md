# TIGR 2021 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

TIGR's 2021 alpha was a two-step platform re-rating that eventually collided with the
regulatory fragility embedded in the model. The January move did not follow a new earnings
release: capital extrapolated the already-public Q3 2020 turn to 148% revenue growth,
profitability, funded-account growth, and overseas expansion into a record retail-trading
cycle, while the “Robinhood of China” discovery narrative and the simultaneous move in FUTU
amplified a small, volatile ADR. Harder evidence arrived after the tape had broken away — a
Xiaomi-affiliate-led financing, Singapore account growth, FY2020 results, MSCI inclusion, and
Q1 2021 revenue up 255.5% — and created a second May–June leg. This was real operating
re-underwriting, but not durable full-year alpha against the index: TIGR was still +33.13
percentage points ahead of SPY at the lab's 126-trading-day ruler, then China's overseas-listing,
data, and unlicensed cross-border-brokerage scrutiny attacked the addressable-market premise;
TIGR ended 2021 down 38.16% while SPY gained 28.73%. The source mosaic is the local tape below,
the [Q3 release published 2020-11-25](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/11/25/2133433/0/en/UP-Fintech-Holding-Limited-Reports-Unaudited-Third-Quarter-2020-Financial-Results.html),
the [Q1 release filed 2021-05-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1756699/000117184321003744/0001171843-21-003744-index.htm),
and the direct regulatory warnings published on
[2021-10-14](https://kelo.com/2021/10/14/chinese-online-brokers-futu-and-up-fintech-face-regulatory-risks-peoples-daily-website/)
and [2021-10-28](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/china-warns-unlicensed-online-brokerages-they-are-breaking-the-law-2658481).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — false at t0.** A flatter, underfollowed base existed earlier in 2020,
  but the 2021-01-12 onset came after TIGR had already advanced 122.04% in 21 sessions and was
  making 63d/126d/252d highs. This was a late breakaway from the detector's perspective, not a
  compressed entry.

- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Q3's growth/profit turn and the KPMG/Aurora items preceded t0;
  Xiaomi financing, Singapore growth, FY2020/Q1 results, and MSCI inclusion supplied successive
  public rungs. July–October policy events formed an equally important negative ladder.

- **Relative breakaway — present.** Forward excess was +100.54 pp versus KWEB and +125.31 pp
  versus SPY at 21 days; it remained +69.67 pp and +33.13 pp at 126 days, respectively.

- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 z21 was 2.05 and five-day/60-day turnover was 3.46;
  repeated $400m–$600m days confirmed capacity, although the later high-volume down days show
  that confirmation eventually became distribution.

- **Options convexity — unknown.** Per-ticker options history begins in 2026-06 and TIGR has no
  current repo snapshot. There is no 2021 open-interest, volatility, or dealer-state evidence.

It was financing-supported runway, not locally evidenced free-cash-flow generation; CFO is
null in the repo and is not inferred. By Q2, client growth and assets still rose, but revenue
fell sequentially, expansion costs exceeded net revenue, and the attributable loss included a
$13.7m fair-value change on convertibles. The operating proof strengthened through Q1, while
per-share dilution, cyclical trading activity, and regulatory permission remained the weak
links.
