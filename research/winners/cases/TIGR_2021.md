# TIGR 2021 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

TIGR's 2021 episode was a real online-brokerage platform re-rating wrapped in a much faster attention and liquidity shock. The public information available at the 2021-01-12 mechanical t0 already showed a credible business inflection—Q3 2020 revenue +148.2% YoY, funded accounts +110.7%, client assets +188.1%, a swing to operating profit, and licensed expansion beyond China—but it did not by itself explain a +122.04% trailing-21-session stock return ([UP Fintech, published 2020-11-25](https://ir.itigerup.com/static-files/baf16825-afe8-4e89-aa36-a92540577edd)). The missing layer was reflexivity: a low-dollar-volume China-broker shelf became an easy “Robinhood of China” narrative during a retail-trading boom; public long research, sparse coverage, FUTU's parallel move, and cash-volume expansion accelerated the price faster than new company facts ([InvestorPlace, published 2021-02-02](https://investorplace.com/2021/02/robinhood-of-china-8-things-for-tigr-stock-investors-to-know-as-tiger-brokers-roars-today/); [The Motley Fool, published 2021-02-03](https://www.fool.com/investing/2021/02/03/why-up-fintech-holding-stock-nearly-doubled-in-jan/)). The business then earned part of the move: FY2020 delivered a first annual profit, Q1 2021 revenue grew 255.5%, MSCI inclusion and Singapore licensing widened the addressable story, and a follow-on offering funded expansion ([UP Fintech, published 2021-03-26](https://ir.itigerup.com/static-files/1aeb2da7-b13a-46f6-8539-3f321cfb50e2); [UP Fintech, published 2021-05-25](https://ir.itigerup.com/static-files/1ea0fd7d-ae5d-476b-ad5a-1305603ebf9c); [UP Fintech, published 2021-05-12](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/up-fintech-holding-limited-to-be-added-to-the-msci-china-all-shares-index-301290434.html); [UP Fintech, published 2021-06-09](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2021/06/09/2244095/0/en/up-fintech-announces-pricing-of-follow-on-public-offering-of-american-depositary-shares.html)). That was enough for +33.13 percentage points of excess return versus SPY through 126 sessions, but not for calendar-year durability: Q2 produced a net loss as revenue fell sequentially and marketing expense surged, then Chinese authorities publicly challenged the legality of mainland-facing cross-border online brokerage and data practices ([UP Fintech, published 2021-09-10](https://ir.itigerup.com/static-files/3463105c-1c04-438a-a72e-355c66f1fc42); [People's Daily, published 2021-10-28](https://finance.people.com.cn/BIG5/n1/2021/1028/c1004-32267651.html)). TIGR therefore won over the detector's six-month horizon but failed to sustain that alpha through year-end; it was neither a pure one-day event nor pure sector beta.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — false at t0.** A low-dollar-volume shelf existed from September through November 2020, but it was not a quiet statistical compression: annualized daily volatility was about 78.65%. By t0 TIGR had already gained 122.04% in 21 sessions and 75.98% since December 15 (`data/yahoo/TIGR.parquet`).

- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Q3 2020 supplied profit/account-growth proof; FY2020, Q1 2021, MSCI inclusion, the offering, and Singapore/Hong Kong licensing renewed the thesis. Q2 deterioration and October regulatory commentary then became negative rungs.

- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 TIGR made 63-/126-/252-session closing highs and led XLF by 112.45pp and SPY by 118.32pp over 21 sessions. Forward excess remained +31.61pp versus XLF and +33.13pp versus SPY at 126 sessions (`data/yahoo/{TIGR,XLF,SPY}.parquet`).

- **Liquidity confirmation — present, later two-sided.** T0 dollar volume was +2.05z/+3.53z versus 21/60 sessions; repeated $400m-$600m sessions confirmed a new participation regime. February and June retention failures and high-volume down days showed distribution as well as demand (`data/yahoo/TIGR.parquet`).

- **Options convexity — unknown.** The repo has no 2021 per-ticker options history, no current TIGR GEX snapshot, and no trade-level NBBO evidence.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The required local normalized surfaces are a complete coverage gap for this issuer: `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` each contain **zero TIGR rows**. Consequently, no local `asof_date`/`as_of` row can be projected into the episode. Public 6-K/20-F-era releases fill the chronology, but they remain externally sourced company figures rather than repo-normalized factors.

**Before the run:** Q3 2020, published November 25, was the inflection available at t0. Revenue was $38.0m (+148.2% YoY), operating income was $7.4m versus a prior-year loss, attributable net income was $3.8m versus a loss, funded accounts were 214.7k (+110.7%), and account balance was $10.9b (+188.1%) ([UP Fintech, published 2020-11-25](https://ir.itigerup.com/static-files/baf16825-afe8-4e89-aa36-a92540577edd)). Calculated on reported revenue, operating margin was about 19.5% and attributable net margin about 10.1%. Cash plus term deposits were $86.9m. That was genuine operating leverage, though one profitable quarter did not establish cycle-normal margins.

**During the first leg:** FY2020 results, not published until March 26, showed Q4 revenue of $47.2m (+136.5% YoY), attributable net income of $8.5m, and FY revenue of $138.5m (+136.1%) with $16.1m of attributable net income ([UP Fintech, published 2021-03-26](https://ir.itigerup.com/static-files/1aeb2da7-b13a-46f6-8539-3f321cfb50e2)). Calculated attributable net margins were about 18.0% in Q4 and 11.6% for the year. Funded accounts reached 258.7k and account balance $16.0b. The report proved the business but came after the February 16 peak; the stock's -11.93% report-day return showed that proof and price expectations were no longer synchronized.

**Second-leg proof:** Q1 2021 was the strongest public print: revenue $81.3m (+255.5% YoY), attributable net income $21.1m, funded accounts 376.0k (+180.4%), account balance $21.4b (+289.8%), and cash plus term deposits $193.4m ([UP Fintech, published 2021-05-25](https://ir.itigerup.com/static-files/1ea0fd7d-ae5d-476b-ad5a-1305603ebf9c)). The calculated attributable net margin was about 26.0%. The close impulse held ten sessions, so this was not merely narrative renewal.

**Runway and break:** the June 9 offering priced 6.5m ADS at $24.50 for $159.25m gross proceeds ([UP Fintech, published 2021-06-09](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2021/06/09/2244095/0/en/up-fintech-announces-pricing-of-follow-on-public-offering-of-american-depositary-shares.html)). Relative to the approximately 143m ADS-equivalent shares disclosed for March 31, the base deal was roughly 4.5% before the underwriter option; that calculation is approximate because share classes and later exercise can change the denominator. Q2 cash plus term deposits rose to $313.2m, but revenue fell 26.0% sequentially to $60.2m, attributable net loss was $21.5m, and marketing expense reached $23.7m versus $12.8m in Q1 ([UP Fintech, published 2021-09-10](https://ir.itigerup.com/static-files/3463105c-1c04-438a-a72e-355c66f1fc42)). Marketing expense rose from about 15.7% to 39.4% of revenue, and the attributable net margin fell from +26.0% to -35.7%. The capital raise reduced near-term financing pressure; it did not prove that acquisition economics or broker margins would remain at Q1 levels.

**Late-year adaptation:** Q3 revenue was $60.8m (+59.6% YoY) and non-GAAP net income $5.3m, an 8.7% calculated margin, but account balance fell about 14.1% sequentially and trading volume fell about 9.2%; more than 80% of newly funded accounts came from outside China ([UP Fintech, published 2021-11-30](https://ir.itigerup.com/static-files/0aaf2eb6-7927-4671-87bc-0613718c49ce)). Overseas diversification was real, but it was not yet large enough to neutralize the mainland regulatory discount. A conventional “cash divided by burn” runway is not decision-useful for a broker whose regulatory capital, client balances, clearing deposits, and growth spending differ from an industrial company's cash burn; simple runway is therefore **null**.
