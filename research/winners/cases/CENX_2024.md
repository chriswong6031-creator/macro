# CENX 2024 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Century Aluminum's February-to-April alpha was not one headline: a depressed, high-cost primary-aluminum producer acquired control of upstream Jamalco, began receiving Section 45X manufacturing credits, was selected to negotiate up to $500 million of federal support for the first new U.S. primary smelter in 45 years, and then received an external aluminum-price shock when the United States and United Kingdom restricted new Russian metal on global exchanges ([Century Aluminum, published 2024-02-21](https://centuryaluminum.com/investors/press-releases/press-release-details/2024/Century-Aluminum-Company-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-2023-Results/); [Century Aluminum, published 2024-03-25](https://centuryaluminum.com/investors/press-releases/press-release-details/2024/Century-Aluminum-Selected-by-U.S.-Department-of-Energy-to-Receive-500-Million-Investment-to-Build-New-Green-Aluminum-Smelter-to-Accelerate-Industrial-Decarbonization/default.aspx); [U.S. Treasury OFAC, published 2024-04-12](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20240412_33)). That mosaic made CENX a much higher-beta expression of policy-supported domestic aluminum than XLB or SPY, but it did not yet make the business structurally higher-margin: at the April 19 onset the latest reported full-year revenue and shipments were down, the latest quarter's EBITDA improvement leaned on a full-year tax-credit catch-up, the DOE selection was only an invitation to negotiate, and spot aluminum still governed near-term earnings. Liquidity confirmed the preceding run on a smoothed basis rather than with an exceptional t0 session. The result was a vertical re-rating that exhausted its near-term public catalysts before contemporaneous operating results could carry it: CENX gave back its relative lead by day 42 and was still behind both benchmarks at day 126. Stronger earnings and final 45X rules later in 2024 caused a distinct second re-underwriting, explaining the +50.08% calendar-year return without rescuing the April episode as a durable breakaway ([Century Aluminum, published 2024-11-04](https://centuryaluminum.com/investors/press-releases/press-release-details/2024/Century-Aluminum-Company-Reports-Third-Quarter-2024-Results/)).

**Case classification: failed breakaway. The run did not sustain.**

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** CENX is a volatile, commodity-sensitive small/mid-cap and rose 79.44% in roughly nine weeks. There is no PIT short-interest series to separate a squeeze contribution, but the multi-event policy and commodity ladder means the move was not purely social.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The run accumulated across February earnings/45X, March DOE selection, rising aluminum, and April sanctions. No single session explains the full impulse.
- **sector_beta — possible.** CENX massively exceeded broad Materials at onset, so XLB beta alone is insufficient; however, aluminum-price and trade-policy beta were core mechanisms, and XLB is a broad rather than aluminum-pure cohort.
- **options_mirage — ruled_out.** Historical options evidence is absent and is not needed to explain the cash-market move. No options claim supports the case.

Episode hazards were `extended_after_vertical_move`, `commodity_price_reversal`, `policy_headline_overreach`, `conditional_grant`, `one_time_accounting_gain`, `thin_gross_margin`, `cash_earnings_lag`, `high_operating_leverage`, `power_and_input_costs`, and `catalyst_exhausted`. The false-positive pattern was treating a policy-supported cyclical producer as if the DOE selection and sanctions had immediately converted into funded capacity and recurring margin.

Current hazards are context-only. In `data/yahoo/CENX.parquet` through 2026-07-22, spot is $45.33, down **26.17% over 63 sessions** and **34.08% below its trailing 252-session closing high** despite being up 104.19% over 252 sessions: extension has partially unwound but remains a relevant long-horizon hazard. **Call-wall overhead is unknown** because current options files are absent. **Catalyst exhaustion is unknown** because this study does not reconstruct the 2026 public-event ladder.
