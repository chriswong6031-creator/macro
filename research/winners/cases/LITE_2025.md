# LITE 2025 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Lumentum’s 2025 alpha was a real operating turnaround that the market progressively recast as an AI-optics bottleneck, not a single NVIDIA-association headline and not merely technology-sector beta. The compressed prior was a company coming off fiscal 2024 revenue contraction, an 18.5% GAAP gross margin and a $546.5M loss; the re-underwriting mosaic then combined a new operator, named roles in NVIDIA’s silicon-photonics ecosystem, hyperscaler sampling of optical circuit switches, repeated guidance increases, capacity investment, and—most importantly—quarterly proof that cloud demand was converting into revenue and extreme margin repair. The key estimate path moved from $425.2M of fiscal Q3 revenue and a 10.8% non-GAAP operating margin to $480.7M/15.0%, then $533.8M/18.7%, while the next-quarter revenue outlook jumped to $630M-$670M ([leadership transition, published 2025-02-03](https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2025/Lumentum-Announces-Leadership-Transition/default.aspx); [NVIDIA ecosystem release, published 2025-03-18](https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2025/Lumentum-Selected-as-an-NVIDIA-Silicon-Photonics-Ecosystem-Partner-to-Advance-AI-Networking-at-Scale/default.aspx); [fiscal Q3 release, published 2025-05-06](https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2025/Lumentum-Announces-Fiscal-Third-Quarter-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx); [fiscal Q4 release, published 2025-08-12](https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2025/Lumentum-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Fiscal-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx); [fiscal Q1 2026 release, published 2025-11-04](https://investor.lumentum.com/financial-news-releases/news-details/2025/Lumentum-Announces-First-Quarter-of-Fiscal-Year-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). The June breakaway initially advanced slowly, but the August and November proof points turned it into a sustained rerating: 126-session excess reached +254.58pp versus XLK and +261.99pp versus SPY, and LITE finished 2025 up 339.06%.
