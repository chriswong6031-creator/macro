# APLD 2024 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Applied Digital's spring-to-July alpha was an attempted platform re-rating: a deeply impaired crypto-hosting/data-center equity was recast as scarce, power-rich AI infrastructure after a Together AI contract, a Garden City divestiture, NVIDIA Blackwell positioning, a 400 MW hyperscaler LOI, successive construction financings, and a June investor deck ([Applied Digital, published 2024-03-14](https://ir.applieddigital.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/88/applied-digital-secures-contract-with-ai-customer-together); [SEC exhibit, filed 2024-03-15](https://ir.applieddigital.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001144879-24-000058/apld-gardencitysalepr.htm); [Applied Digital, published 2024-03-19](https://ir.applieddigital.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/90/applied-digital-among-first-cloud-service-providers-to-use); [Applied Digital, published 2024-04-11](https://ir.applieddigital.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/94/applied-digital-reports-fiscal-third-quarter-2024-results); [SEC filing, filed 2024-06-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1144879/000114487924000151/apld-20240607.htm); [SEC exhibit, filed 2024-06-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1144879/000114487924000175/investorpresentationfina.htm)). The mosaic was more fragile than the headlines: the lease that mattered was still unsigned, much of the “up to $200 million” CIM facility was conditional, the capital carried warrants or variable-price conversion exposure, the latest public quarter still had a negative gross margin and a $62.8 million loss, and t0 made only a 63-day—not a 126- or 252-day—closing high on ordinary same-day dollar volume. The July 9 filing of a new $125 million ATM exposed the equity-financing dependency and broke the move ([SEC, filed 2024-07-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1144879/000114487924000186/apld-20240709.htm)); APLD was down 25.23% at 21 sessions and 41.23% at 42. The September 5 NVIDIA/Related-backed private placement caused a separate, exceptional-volume reset ([Applied Digital, published 2024-09-05](https://ir.applieddigital.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/107/applied-digital-announces-160-million-strategic-financing)), explaining the later 63/126-day recovery without turning the July onset into a durable breakaway.

**Case classification: failed breakaway. The run did not sustain.**

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** APLD closed at $2.39 on 2024-04-18, and at t0 was still 36.52% below the prior 252-day closing high. Transformer outages, crypto-hosting exposure, an expired first anchor negotiation, and repeated bridge needs created a genuinely impaired prior.
- **Catalyst ladder — present, low durability.** Contracts, the Garden City divestiture, a new 400 MW LOI, CIM financing, and the June deck changed the story; however, the economically decisive lease and full project financing remained conditional while each bridge added claims on future equity.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed.** The census records +50.45pp/21d and +131.31pp/42d trailing excess versus SPY and a new 63-day high. Forward excess was already -17.77pp at five days and -43.62pp at 42 days.
- **Liquidity confirmation — absent at the July onset.** T0 dollar-volume z21 was -0.06; the 5d/60d ratio of 2.32 reflected prior sessions. The largest initial-episode turnover arrived on the July 9 ATM decline. Strong liquidity confirmation appeared only on the separate September 5 reset.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** The permitted APLD options surfaces begin in June 2026, so no 2024 option-positioning history exists and no opening/closing or customer/dealer direction can be inferred.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** APLD was a small, volatile AI/crypto-adjacent equity whose value depended on distant capacity and financing. Real contracts and filings existed, but no PIT short-interest series is available to rule out squeeze contribution.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The pre-t0 move accumulated over weeks across several public events. The episode failed on a funding/dilution sequence, not on one binary scientific or regulatory outcome.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** At onset APLD exceeded SPY by the supplied +50.45pp/21d and +131.31pp/42d, and the direct SRVR comparison is similarly large. Its forward collapse also occurred while SRVR and SPY rose.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Historical options context is null, so it cannot explain the move and cannot be cleanly falsified. Cash-market evidence alone is sufficient to classify the failure.

Episode hazards were `unsigned_hyperscaler_lease`, `conditional_project_financing`, `equity_dilution`, `warrant_overhang`, `convertible_financing`, `negative_gross_margin`, `cash_burn`, `crypto_customer_exposure`, `execution_delay`, and `catalyst_exhausted`. The main false-positive pattern was confusing access to power plus a non-definitive lease with contracted, financed cash flow.

Current hazards are context-only and not a continuation of the 2024 case. As of 2026-07-17, **extension is possible but not measurable from the permitted current options files alone**; spot had retraced below the latest full-store close, while IV remained above 100%. **Call-wall overhead is possible** at $28.50, but the file labels that wall weak; the nearer $25 put wall and very high expected move make the setup two-sided. **Catalyst exhaustion is unknown** because this study did not reconstruct the 2026 catalyst ladder. The 2024 lesson remains current: financing announcements can extend runway while simultaneously increasing claims on the equity.
