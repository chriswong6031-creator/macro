# ILMN 2025 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Illumina's 2025 result is two different stories that must not be blended: the stock lost 16.35 percentage points to XLV and 19.57 points to SPY over the full calendar year, but the mechanical 2025-06-20 breakaway subsequently earned 27.07 points of 126-session excess versus XLV and was labeled a clean, durable episode in the local census. The first leg was a washout recovery from a 51.3% January-to-April collapse after China's restrictions and a weak revenue outlook; Q1 then showed that cost cuts, free cash flow and repurchases could protect earnings even as revenue guidance fell, while the May tariff pause, PromoterAI and the proposed SomaLogic acquisition reopened a multiomics/platform narrative ([Q1 release, published 2025-05-08](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110803/000111080325000044/a1q25earningsrelease.htm); [U.S.-China joint statement, published 2025-05-12](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2025/05/joint-statement-on-u-s-china-economic-and-trade-meeting-in-geneva/); [SomaLogic release, filed 2025-06-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110803/000119312525144290/d66921dex991.htm)). That early alpha was fragile—the Q2 guide increase still produced a 9.84% negative opening gap—but BioInsight and the 5-base launch broadened the model before Q3 delivered flat total revenue, a return to ex-China growth, 24.5% non-GAAP operating margin and another EPS-guide increase; the next session rose 24.78%, and China's later removal of the instrument export ban supplied a second held gap ([BioInsight release, published 2025-10-01](https://www.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2025/bac27a69-7339-4f9f-9c1e-1fbc262895d4.html); [5-base release, published 2025-10-15](https://www.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2025/ed906867-8ddf-4219-b15f-b8aa8facbd11.html); [Q3 release, published 2025-10-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110803/000111080325000071/a3q25earningsrelease.htm); [China update, published 2025-11-05](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/illumina-responds-to-positive-updates-from-chinese-ministry-of-commerce-302606280.html)). The mosaic is therefore a margin-and-cash-flow turnaround plus platform expansion and partial China relief, not broad health-care beta; but most final 2025 episode alpha depended on one Q3 binary, and the top line itself had not yet become a high-growth story.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks
