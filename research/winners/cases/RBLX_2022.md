# RBLX 2022 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

RBLX's summer 2022 alpha was a reflexive re-underwriting of a badly compressed, post-lockdown platform: May data still showed bookings down 9%–11%, but July suddenly paired 26% DAU growth and 25% engagement growth with bookings up 8%–10%, giving capital a credible “engagement is becoming monetization again” bridge just as the stock had rebounded from a roughly $22 trough. The tape confirmed that narrative with repeated 63-day highs and multi-billion-dollar volume. But the headline recovery hid the durability problem: Q2 bookings were still down 4%, ABPDAU was down 21%, free cash flow was negative, and infrastructure, safety, developer, and R&D spending kept profitability remote. Thus price escaped the contemporaneous published models before cash economics had turned; the August 12 peak was followed by a 44% fall by year-end, while forward excess versus SPY reached −27.06 percentage points at 63 sessions. The move was genuine platform-usage reacceleration amplified by compression and liquidity, but it failed because monetization per user and operating leverage never caught up to the vertical re-rating.

**Format-contract classification: this is a failed breakaway case; the run did not sustain.**

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2022-05-10 — Q1 results established the compressed prior.** Revenue grew 39%, but bookings fell 3%; April bookings were estimated down 8%–10% and ABPDAU down 25%–26%. This made the later inflection meaningful. Publication date: 2022-05-10. [Roblox Q1 release](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-Reports-First-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results/default.aspx).
- **2022-06-15 — May monthly metrics showed audience resilience but continued monetization decay.** DAUs grew 17% and hours 10%, while bookings fell 9%–11% and ABPDAU 23%–24%. Publication date: 2022-06-15. [Roblox May metrics](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-Reports-May-2022-Key-Metrics/default.aspx).
- **2022-07-12 — Q2 reporting date announced.** Roblox explicitly said the release would include July metrics, concentrating the next re-underwriting point. Publication date: 2022-07-12. [Roblox earnings-date release](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-to-Report-Fiscal-2022-Second-Quarter-Financial-Results-on-August-9-2022/default.aspx).
- **2022-08-09 — Q2/July inflection disclosure, the hard catalyst.** Q2 revenue grew 30%, but bookings fell 4%, ABPDAU fell 21%, and FCF was −$57.3m. The new information was July: bookings +8%–10%, DAUs +26%, and hours +25%. Publication and 8-K filing date: 2022-08-09. [Roblox release](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-Reports-Second-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results/default.aspx); [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509822000121/rblx-20220809.htm).
- **2022-09-07 — Investor Day announced.** Management promised long-term product/technology discussion and August metrics, creating a second expectations node after the peak. Publication date: 2022-09-07. [Roblox announcement](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-to-Host-2022-Investor-Day/default.aspx).
- **2022-09-15 — August metrics moderated the July acceleration.** DAUs rose 24% and bookings 5%–7% (9%–11% constant currency), but ABPDAU remained down 14%–16%. Publication date: 2022-09-15. [Roblox August metrics](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-Reports-August-2022-Key-Metrics/default.aspx).
- **2022-10-17 — September metrics produced a tradable echo, not durable repair.** Bookings rose 11%–15% and DAUs 23%, but ABPDAU remained down 7%–10%; Roblox also revised July and August estimated revenue down by about $43m and $38m after extending estimated paying-user life. Publication date: 2022-10-17. [Roblox September metrics](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-Reports-September-2022-Key-Metrics/default.aspx).
- **2022-11-09 — Q3 exposed the cost of growth.** Bookings grew 10% and DAUs 24%, but revenue grew only 2%, FCF was −$67.7m, ABPDAU fell 11%, and net loss attributable to common holders widened to $297.8m from $74.0m. Publication date: 2022-11-09. [Roblox Q3 release](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-Reports-Third-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results/default.aspx).

Repo anchor coverage is absent: searches of [`data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet) and [`data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet) return zero RBLX rows. The SEC filing above supplies the 2022-08-09 filing anchor without pretending the local panels cover it.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** RBLX was −49.84% YTD at t0 and −29.68% over 126 sessions; May bookings and ABPDAU were still contracting sharply.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Monthly reporting moved from May contraction to July bookings growth, followed by August/September evidence that audience growth persisted but monetization lagged.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 RBLX had +22.79 pp 21-day and +70.32 pp 42-day excess versus SPY and had begun a sequence of 63-day closing highs.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 dollar volume was $2.563bn with z21 +2.34; the prior day was $3.113bn with z21 +3.94.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2022 per-ticker options history exists in the repo, and price/volume cannot identify an options mechanism.

Publications available at the time show the arc. Q2 revenue was $591.2m (+30%), bookings $639.9m (−4%), operating cash flow $26.5m, FCF −$57.3m, and net loss $176.4m; infrastructure/trust-and-safety expense rose to $158.2m from $109.0m and R&D to $211.8m from $124.7m ([2022-08-09 release](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-Reports-Second-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). Q3 then delivered bookings growth but not leverage: revenue $517.7m (+2%), bookings $701.7m (+10%), FCF −$67.7m, and net loss $297.8m ([2022-11-09 release](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-Reports-Third-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). Cash of $3.075bn at 2022-06-30 provided runway, but the sequence was self-funded capacity without demonstrated margin conversion—not a near-term earnings turn ([2022-08-09 release](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-Reports-Second-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results/default.aspx)).

## 7. Research echo

The research tape was divided and generally behind spot. On 2022-07-07, a reported 16-analyst average target was about $37, with roughly half positive, while RBLX closed $38.85 that day—already around/above the published center ([PriceTargets.com, published 2022-07-07](https://www.pricetargets.com/news/roblox-has-struggled-this-year-but-could-bounce-back/); spot from [`data/yahoo/RBLX.parquet`](../../../data/yahoo/RBLX.parquet)). On 2022-08-01, MKM raised its target from $28 to $40 on faster bookings trends, but kept a neutral stance; spot closed $42.19 ([Motley Fool, published 2022-08-01](https://www.fool.com/investing/2022/08/01/why-roblox-stock-rallied-on-monday/)). After Q2, Morgan Stanley raised its target from $25 to $32 and stayed cautious on scale/profitability; KeyBanc carried $40, while spot closed $48.05 on 2022-08-10 ([Benzinga, published 2022-08-10](https://www.benzinga.com/news/22/08/28435182/analysts-remained-cautious-over-roblox-post-q2-with-more-challenging-comps-expected-in-upcoming-quar)). Morningstar cut fair value from $80 to $75 on weaker bookings per user and infrastructure/security investment even while calling shares undervalued ([Morningstar, published 2022-08-11](https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/roblox-posts-poor-q2-earnings-bookings-users-decline)). This is the “price escaped the published model” read: the marginal targets clustered below a $48–$51 tape, while the one high valuation depended on long-duration economics the contemporaneous FCF did not yet demonstrate. The repo contains no analyst-target dataset.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** RBLX was a volatile, heavily discussed post-IPO growth stock and compression could amplify covering/speculation, but the catalyst and multiweek relative move had operating data underneath it; local PIT short-interest evidence is absent.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The move developed across June–August, multiple 63-day highs, and several high-dollar-volume days, not a single session. The failure was nonetheless rapid after peak.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** At t0, 42-day RBLX return was +78.53% versus +3.44% for XLC and +8.21% for SPY.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Options may have amplified convexity, but 2022 options history is unavailable; price/volume alone cannot establish it.
- **Current/episode hazards:** `extended_after_vertical_move` was likely (+106.76% in 63 sessions); `catalyst_exhausted` was likely after July metrics were capitalized and August data moderated; `call_wall_overhead` is unknown because no historical chain exists. Additional hazards were `monetization_lag`, `negative_free_cash_flow`, `expense_intensity`, and `accounting_estimate_sensitivity`.
