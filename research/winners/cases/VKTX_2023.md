# VKTX 2023 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

VKTX's 2023 alpha was a two-asset re-underwriting, not a generic biotech rally: the March 28 publication of 28-day Phase 1 VK2735 data made Viking a credible GLP-1/GIP obesity-platform option, same-day initiation of an oral formulation widened the modeled opportunity, the ensuing equity offering converted the re-rating into a much longer cash runway, and May's positive VK2809 VOYAGE readout added a second metabolic-disease asset ([Viking, published 2023-03-28](https://ir.vikingtherapeutics.com/2023-03-28-Viking-Therapeutics-Announces-Results-from-Phase-1-Clinical-Trial-of-Dual-GLP-1-GIP-Receptor-Agonist-VK2735); [Viking oral-study release, published 2023-03-28](https://ir.vikingtherapeutics.com/2023-03-28-Viking-Therapeutics-Announces-Initiation-of-Phase-1-Study-to-Evaluate-Oral-Formulation-of-Dual-GLP-1-GIP-Receptor-Agonist-VK2735); [offering close 8-K, filed 2023-04-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1607678/000095017023011636/vktx-20230403.htm); [Viking VOYAGE release, published 2023-05-16](https://ir.vikingtherapeutics.com/2023-05-16-Viking-Therapeutics-Announces-Positive-Top-Line-Results-from-Phase-2b-VOYAGE-Study-of-VK2809-in-Patients-with-Biopsy-Confirmed-Non-Alcoholic-Steatohepatitis-NASH)). The breakaway failed because the market had capitalized early, short-duration efficacy before comparative or later-stage evidence existed: the mechanical April 3 onset inherited its liquidity test from the March 28 shock rather than receiving fresh volume, the May catalyst gap failed by session 10, and the June reversal arrived on very large dollar volume as an FDA rejection reset the NASH evidence bar and Lilly published stronger, longer-duration oral and triple-agonist obesity benchmarks ([Intercept, published 2023-06-22](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2023/06/22/2693358/0/en/Intercept-Receives-Complete-Response-Letter-from-FDA-for-Obeticholic-Acid-as-a-Treatment-for-Pre-Cirrhotic-Fibrosis-due-to-NASH.html); [Lilly orforglipron release, published 2023-06-23](https://investor.lilly.com/node/49091); [Lilly retatrutide release, published 2023-06-26](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lillys-phase-2-retatrutide-results-published-new-england-journal)). The result was real alpha into t0 and through 42 sessions, but not durable price discovery: VKTX led XBI by 56.33 percentage points over the 21 sessions into t0, then lagged it by 18.54 points over the next 63 sessions and by 30.26 points over 126 sessions.

This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`).
