# BIDU 2006 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Baidu's 10 May 2006 breakaway was a real but over-compressed re-underwriting of an
exceptional operating trajectory: the February earnings step-up, April's Baidu Baike
launch, and a first-quarter revenue beat culminated in second-quarter guidance roughly
31%–36% above the then-published Wall Street estimate, while improving monetization and a
rapidly expanding advertiser base showed search economics scaling. The tape therefore
produced enormous index alpha and authentic liquidity confirmation. It did not sustain
because price capitalized much of that runway in one session, the catalyst ladder lacked
another independent near-term rung, the close had not cleared the post-IPO 252-day high,
and July's very strong results supplied only an approximately in-line forward outlook.
The May close then lost 16.4% over 63 trading days while SPY lost 3.8%; the later
October–December advance was a separate re-breakaway, not continuation of the May event.

**Format-contract classification: this is a FAILED breakaway case
(`case_type: failed_breakaway`); the run did not sustain.**

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2006-02-21 — earnings.** Q4 2005 revenue rose 167.7% year over year to RMB114.9m,
  above prior guidance; GAAP operating profit rose 105.6% sequentially. This established
  that search monetization and operating leverage were already inflecting before May.
  Publication: 2006-02-21.
  [Baidu investor relations](https://ir.baidu.com/news-releases/news-release-details/baidu-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2005-results/).
- **2006-04-20 — product launch.** Baidu publicly launched the test version of Baidu
  Baike, extending search into a community-generated knowledge product. This broadened
  engagement optionality but had no disclosed near-term financial contribution.
  Publication: 2006-04-20.
  [Sina Tech contemporaneous report](https://tech.sina.com.cn/i/2006-04-20/1324911598.shtml).
- **2006-05-09 — earnings and guidance.** Q1 revenue was RMB135.6m, +196.8% year over
  year and above the top of company guidance; Q2 guidance was RMB186m–193m. Management
  attributed performance to milder seasonality and monetization-algorithm improvement.
  Publication: 2006-05-09.
  [Baidu investor relations](https://ir.baidu.com/news-releases/news-release-details/baidu-announces-first-quarter-2006-results).
- **2006-05-10 — research echo.** A contemporaneous report put Q2 guidance at
  $23m–$24m versus a $17.6m Wall Street estimate and noted a 23% after-hours move to
  $75.42 on the then-current, pre-split quote scale. Piper Jaffray moved its rating to
  Market Perform and target to $75. Publication: 2006-05-10.
  [TheStreet earnings reaction](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/todays-early-winners-and-losers-10284708);
  [TheStreet analyst action](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/analysts-actions-a-daily-roundup-of-street-research-10284769).
- **2006-07-26 — earnings/guidance, failure reveal.** Q2 revenue reached RMB191.6m,
  +41.3% sequentially and +174.9% year over year; operating profit more than doubled
  sequentially. Yet Q3 guidance of RMB238m–246m represented about 26%–30% sequential
  growth, no longer a giant expectation reset. The next close fell 20.9% on $723m of
  dollar volume. Publication: 2006-07-26 at 17:01 ET.
  [Baidu Q2 release](https://ir.baidu.com/static-files/60add814-0d7a-4cf1-9729-cc65abc3cf5d);
  [TheStreet contemporaneous interpretation](https://www.thestreet.com/technology/slowdown-slams-baidu-10299686).
- **2006-10-31 — earnings, separate reset.** Q3 revenue rose 24.9% sequentially and
  169.1% year over year; net income rose 902.5% year over year. This event belongs to the
  later 252-day-high re-breakaway, not the failed May continuation. Publication:
  2006-10-31.
  [Baidu investor relations](https://ir.baidu.com/index.php/news-releases/news-release-details/baidu-announces-third-quarter-2006-results).

Repo filing anchors are **null**: BIDU has zero rows in both

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** At t0 the 126-day return was only +15.67% despite
  +71.04% over 42 days: the stock had spent months digesting its volatile 2005 IPO and
  then rapidly compressed expectations into the May print.
- **Catalyst ladder — present, but shallow.** February results and April's product launch
  led into May's earnings/guidance shock. The economically decisive rung was still one
  earnings event; no independent post-May milestone bridged the wait to Q2.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed.** +39.84pp/21d and +67.55pp/42d versus
  SPY with 63d/126d highs, but no 252d high; 63-day forward excess reversed to -12.58pp.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** May 10 dollar volume was $1.258bn, z21 4.32 in
  the episode census and 4.48× the 60-day baseline. Confirmation was highly concentrated
  in the event day rather than persistent accumulation.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2006 options history exists. Per-ticker repo history
  begins 2026-06, and BIDU also lacks a current repo snapshot.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The repo's PIT-gated US issuer surfaces have no BIDU rows:
`data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`,
`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` are all
**null for BIDU**. The following external figures use only what was public on each
publication date; the full-year 2006 figures are retrospective context and were not
available at t0.

Before the run, the 2006-02-21 release showed FY2005 revenue of RMB319.2m, +171.8%, and
GAAP operating profit of RMB35.8m, +225.7%; Q4 revenue was RMB114.9m and operating
profit RMB14.2m. At the 2006-05-09 trigger, Q1 revenue was RMB135.6m, +18.0%
sequentially and +196.8% year over year, while implied Q1 operating profit was about
RMB26.9m (the July release later stated Q2's RMB58.0m was +115.4% sequentially).
The re-underwriting rested on two runway variables: more paying search customers and
better monetization per customer.

During the run, the 2006-07-26 release confirmed Q2 revenue of RMB191.6m (+41.3%
sequentially), active online-marketing customers above 90,000 (+21.6% sequentially),
revenue per customer of RMB2,081 (+19.3%), and operating margin of approximately
30.3% (RMB58.0m / RMB191.6m), versus approximately 19.8% in Q1. Cash was RMB1.1bn.
The business did not fail; the *breakaway* failed because the rate of expectation
change decelerated.

The 2006 Form 20-F, filed 2007-05-29 and therefore not t0 evidence, later showed FY2006
revenue RMB837.8m (+162.5%), operating profit RMB262.9m (+633.4%), and net income
RMB301.8m (+533.9%). It also showed customers rising from about 76,000 in 2005 to above
143,000 in 2006 and annual revenue per customer from about RMB4,044 to RMB5,800.
Publication/filing: 2007-05-29.
[SEC Form 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1329099/000119312507125387/d20f.htm).

## 7. Research echo

At the trigger, spot escaped the published model in one jump: Q2 revenue guidance of
$23m–$24m stood 31%–36% above the cited $17.6m estimate, while the post-release quote
of $75.42 approximately met Piper's newly raised $75 target immediately. Consensus was
therefore reacting to, not leading, the tape. Publication: 2006-05-10.
[TheStreet earnings reaction](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/todays-early-winners-and-losers-10284708);
[TheStreet analyst action](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/analysts-actions-a-daily-roundup-of-street-research-10284769).

The echo later caught up in stages rather than underwriting an uninterrupted May run:
Piper moved to Outperform with a $110 target on 2006-07-14, before Q2; after the later
Q3 event, Goldman moved from its negative rating to Neutral with a $93 target on
2006-11-01, even though Q3 fell short of consensus. Publications: 2006-07-14 and
2006-11-01.
[TheStreet July revision](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/analysts-actions-a-daily-roundup-of-street-research-10296856);
[TheStreet November revision](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/wednesdays-analysts-upgrades-and-downgrades-10319170).
The repo has no analyst-target dataset; these are point-in-time external observations.
