# SMCI 2024 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Super Micro Computer's 2024 breakaway was an AI-server operating-leverage and index-liquidity rerating. The first leg came when the company preannounced stronger fiscal Q2 revenue and EPS on 2024-01-18, turning a known AI infrastructure beneficiary into a near-term earnings revision story. The second leg was mechanical and attention-driven: the S&P 500 inclusion announcement on 2024-03-01 pulled a newly large but still volatile AI-server name into a major index flow channel. By 2024-03-13, SMCI had outperformed XLK by +237.13pp over 42 trading days and +304.85pp YTD.

- one_day_binary: ruled_out. The January gap began the move, then earnings confirmation and index inclusion added separate rungs.
- sector_beta: ruled_out. XLK was up 8.93% over the 42d window while SMCI was up 246.06%.

- Current hazards: extension after a vertical move, margin sustainability, AI-server competition, accounting/filing-risk sensitivity, and current call-wall overhead.
