# LNG 2011 failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

LNG's 2011 alpha was the public market capitalizing a business-model option faster than Cheniere could de-risk it: an underused U.S. import terminal could become the first lower-48 LNG export platform, using existing tanks, berths, and pipelines; DOE's FTA authorization, a string of customer MOUs, a financing adviser, and a FERC application progressively made that option imaginable ([project announcement, 2010-06-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/3570/000000357010000026/exhibit_99-1.htm); [DOE Order 2833, 2010-09-07](https://hgeo.energy.gov/ng_regulation/orders-2010-index); [financial-adviser announcement, 2010-12-13](https://cqpir.cheniere.com/news-presentations/press-releases/detail/80/cheniere-engages-societe-generale-as-financial-advisor-for); [FERC notice, 2011-02-18](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2011-02-18/pdf/2011-3670.pdf)). But the February tape was a late-stage capitalization of nonbinding interest, not a contemporaneous earnings inflection: at the mechanical 2011-02-28 t0, LNG had risen 323.67% in 126 trading days while the export project still lacked binding offtake, final construction authority, final investment decision, and project financing. The unchanged t0 close also followed a 14.57% surge on February 25 for which no same-day public catalyst was found. A creditor's default/accounting allegations then exposed the balance-sheet and governance fragility on a 35.77% down day ([8-K filed 2011-03-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/3570/000000357011000031/ceicenterbridge8k.htm)); DOE's non-FTA approval produced a spectacular but binary May rebound ([DOE publication, 2011-05-20](https://www.energy.gov/hgeo/articles/energy-department-approves-gulf-coast-exports-liquefied-natural-gas)), followed by equity financing rather than operating confirmation ([offering pricing, 2011-06-01](https://lngir.cheniere.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/104/cheniere-announces-upsizing-and-pricing-of-public-offering)). The decisive durability milestone—the first binding 20-year SPA—was not filed until October 26, after the 126-day failure window ([BG agreement 8-K, 2011-10-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1383650/000138365011000070/cqp8-kbgliquefactionagreem.htm)). Thus the long-run export thesis eventually advanced, but the February breakaway itself ran ahead of executable evidence and failed to sustain alpha.

This is a **FAILED breakaway case** (`case_type: failed_breakaway`).

Dates below are publication or filing dates. - **2010-06-03 — export-project pivot (`other`; high durability).** Cheniere Partners proposed up to four liquefaction trains at Sabine Pass, using existing acreage, storage, berths, regasification facilities, and pipeline connections. It estimated a $1.40–$1.75/MMBtu fee but made construction contingent on approvals, satisfactory construction contracts, long-term customer contracts, financing, and a final investment decision. [SEC-furnished issuer release, 2010-06-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/3570/000000357010000026/exhibit_99-1.htm).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** At the visible-run start on 2010-11-08, LNG was already up 28.32% over 21 sessions and the trailing 21-session high/low range was 39.34%; at t0 it was up 323.67% over 126 sessions. This was a volatile repair and vertical extension, not a compressed base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** The export pivot, DOE FTA approval, non-FTA process, successive MOUs, financing adviser, and FERC filing progressively increased project plausibility. The weakness was quality: customer interest was nonbinding, financing was advisory, and construction/FID remained conditional.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 LNG led XLE by 46.83pp over 21 days and 77.16pp over 42, and led SPY by 54.37pp and 87.84pp, while making 63/126/252-day closing highs.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, but low quality.** The five-day/60-day dollar-volume ratio was 1.84 at mechanical t0, and the likely February 25 tape onset reached z21 2.54 and z60 3.34. Yet t0 itself was only 0.22/0.89 on an unchanged close, the first still-larger subsequent volume arrived on a 35.77% collapse, and May's constructive expansion was tied to a single regulatory decision before financing pressure.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2011 per-ticker options history exists. Current files begin in 2026-06 and cannot be projected backward.
