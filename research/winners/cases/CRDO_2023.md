# CRDO 2023 failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

CRDO's 2023 burst was the market pulling forward a plausible AI-data-center interconnect recovery, not the discovery of contemporaneous revenue acceleration. A February customer-driven forecast collapse reset expectations and price; Nvidia's May 24 data-center forecast then supplied the cohort-wide AI shock, while Credo's May 31 results reframed its high-speed connectivity platform for generative-AI demand and Mizuho mapped that narrative to potential hyperscaler AEC adoption the next day—even though quarterly revenue had just fallen 40.9% sequentially ([Credo 8-K, filed 2023-02-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1807794/000162828023003504/crdo-20230214.htm); [Nvidia, published 2023-05-24](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2023/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-Fiscal-2024/default.aspx); [Credo, published 2023-05-31](https://investors.credosemi.com/news-events/news/news-details/2023/Credo-Technology-Group-Holding-Ltd-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2023-Financial-Results-05-31-2023/default.aspx); [Benzinga, published 2023-06-01 13:39 ET](https://www.benzinga.com/analyst-ratings/analyst-color/23/06/32676141/analyst-ups-credo-technology-price-target-sees-fy24-upswing-despite-macro-headwinds)). Cash participation was real—two of three qualifying opening gaps held for ten sessions and June 1 dollar volume reached $154.45m—but the mechanical June 12 t0 came seven sessions after that peak confirmation, with only a +0.16 trailing-21-day dollar-volume z-statistic. CRDO had gained 114.99% in 21 sessions and led SPY by 109.73 percentage points, yet still lagged SOXX over 126 sessions and YTD; after peaking June 20 it lost 25.0%, trailed SPY by 11.51 points over the next 63 sessions, and did not make 126- or 252-session closing highs until November and December. The failed persistence therefore came from narrative timing outrunning booked revenue, extreme customer concentration, late/stale liquidity confirmation, and a recovery path that was too slow to support the vertical repricing—not from the AI/AEC thesis being wholly fictitious.

This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`).
