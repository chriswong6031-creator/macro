# CRDO 2023 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

CRDO's mid-2023 alpha was a two-step re-underwriting of *time horizon*, not a contemporaneous earnings inflection: the 2023-02-14 disclosure that its largest customer had cut demand, taking fiscal-Q4 guidance to $30M-$32M and fiscal-2024 growth to roughly flat, first crushed the price and expectations ([SEC 8-K, filed 2023-02-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1807794/000162828023003504/crdo-20230214.htm)); NVIDIA's 2023-05-24 $11B next-quarter outlook then turned AI-cluster connectivity into a scarce-capacity narrative ([NVIDIA, published 2023-05-24](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-first-quarter-fiscal-2024/)); and Credo's own 2023-05-31 release explicitly connected its low-power SerDes, DSP and active-electrical-cable portfolio to generative AI ([Credo, published 2023-05-31](https://investors.credosemi.com/news-events/news/news-details/2023/Credo-Technology-Group-Holding-Ltd-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2023-Financial-Results-05-31-2023/default.aspx)). That mosaic was real enough to lift CRDO 115.0% in the 21 sessions into mechanical t0, but it arrived while quarterly revenue had fallen from $54.3M to $32.1M, GAAP operating margin was -51.1%, and management still guided only $33M-$35M for the next quarter. The later $35.1M and $44.0M quarters proved a recovery, not the immediate step-change that the vertical tape had discounted ([Credo, published 2023-08-24](https://www.globenewswire.com/de/news-release/2023/08/24/2731590/0/en/Credo-Technology-Group-Holding-Ltd-Reports-First-Quarter-of-Fiscal-Year-2024-Financial-Results.html?f=22&fvtc=5&fvtv=37025363); [Credo, published 2023-11-29](https://investors.credosemi.com/news-events/news/news-details/2023/Credo-Technology-Group-Holding-Ltd-Reports-Second-Quarter-of-Fiscal-Year-2024-Financial-Results-11-29-2023/default.aspx)). The move therefore failed through a horizon mismatch: sector read-through and long-lead product optionality repriced immediately, while reported revenue, customer diversification and operating leverage needed several more quarters; by t0 the best liquidity shock was already eleven days old, published targets had been overtaken, and later high-volume sessions included distribution and a discounted equity offering.

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`).** “Failed” refers to this 2023-06-12 detector episode: the repo marks it failed at 63 trading days. It does not erase the separate later CRDO episodes recorded in `data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`.

The subsequent tape waited for fundamentals, lost relative strength, and only repaired its long-lookback highs late in the year.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** The repo has no PIT short-interest or social-attention history for this episode. Multiple hard catalysts argue against a purely reflexive move, but a small-cap short-cover contribution cannot be ruled out.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The impulse had distinct May 25, May 30 and June 1 gaps, and each was above its pre-gap close after 3/5/10 sessions. Failure emerged over 21-63 sessions rather than through an immediate full-gap reversal.
- **sector_beta — possible.** NVIDIA's forecast produced a 6.8% one-day semiconductor-index rally, making the first gap a genuine cohort read-through; however, CRDO's +92.56pp trailing-21-day excess versus SOXX and the separate June 1 issuer reaction show stock-specific amplification. The false positive was *partial sector narrative plus over-discounted duration*, not pure beta.
- **options_mirage — possible.** There is no 2023 options history and no trade-level NBBO. Convexity may have amplified the vertical tape, but it cannot be measured and is not required for the cash-market explanation.

Episode hazards were `customer_concentration`, `revenue_trough_not_growth`, `narrative_ahead_of_reported_revenue`, `liquidity_confirmation_stale_at_t0`, `sector_readthrough_dependency`, `negative_gaap_operating_leverage`, and `secondary_share_supply`.
