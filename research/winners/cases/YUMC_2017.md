# YUMC 2017 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

YUMC's 2017 alpha was a two-step post-spin turnaround re-rating, not a simple China-consumer or restaurant-sector move: a quiet price-discovery base after the October 2016 separation met February evidence that an independent Yum China could turn a flat same-store-sales year into 31% operating-profit growth and return cash, then the April 5 first-quarter release converted that premise into a 63-day high on exceptional liquidity as positive comps, a 23.0% restaurant margin, digital reach, and free cash flow arrived together. [Company releases published 2017-02-07 at 4:31 p.m. EST](https://ir.yumchina.com/news-releases/news-release-details/yum-china-reports-full-year-operating-profit-us640-million-31) and [2017-04-05 at 4:30 p.m. EDT](https://ir.yumchina.com/news-releases/news-release-details/yum-china-achieved-strong-first-quarter-same-store-sales-growth/). The quality was mixed: retail tax reform supplied much of the early margin lift, reported Q2 revenue was nearly flat, and the July print caused the year's largest dollar-volume decline. [Company release published 2017-07-05 at 4:30 p.m. EDT](https://ir.yumchina.com/news-releases/news-release-details/yum-china-reports-q2-same-store-sales-growth-3-and-operating?ID=2284757&c=254434&p=irol-newsArticle). The move survived because October supplied the missing organic proof—6% same-store-sales and 10% system-sales growth, 120 million loyalty members, more than 45% mobile-payment penetration, an initial dividend, and a larger repurchase authorization—while the new management team framed a debt-free, cash-generative runway. [Company Q3 release published 2017-10-05 at 4:30 p.m. EDT](https://ir.yumchina.com/static-files/0ef55c6a-f4d7-416e-958e-4da93c7b163b); [Investor Day filing published 2017-10-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1673358/000156459017019559/yumc-8k_20171017.htm). The tape therefore says durable broad-benchmark alpha with a mid-run fundamental failure test, not uninterrupted alpha: YUMC peaked on October 9, the Investor Day did not start another leg, and year-end retained +30.78 percentage points versus XLY and +32.10 points versus SPY.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2017-01-09 — Taco Bell entered China (product launch).** The first Shanghai restaurant made a third global brand a visible, if still small, unit-growth option. [Yum China release, published 2017-01-09](https://yumchinaholdingsinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/yum-china-launches-taco-bell-brand-china).
- **2017-02-07 — Q4/FY2016 results and $300 million repurchase authorization (earnings/capital return).** System sales grew 5% excluding currency, 575 restaurants opened, restaurant margin expanded 270 bp to 15.3%, and operating profit rose 31% despite flat same-store sales; the board authorized up to $300 million of repurchases. This made margin conversion and cash return visible before top-line acceleration. [Yum China release, published 2017-02-07 at 4:31 p.m. EST](https://ir.yumchina.com/news-releases/news-release-details/yum-china-reports-full-year-operating-profit-us640-million-31); [SEC 8-K filed 2017-02-07 at 5:07 p.m. EST](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1673358/000156459017001091/yumc-8k_20170208.htm).

[SEC 8-K filed 2017-02-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1673358/000156459017001351/yumc-8k_20170207.htm).
- **2017-04-05 — Q1 results created the economic breakaway (earnings).** Same-store sales turned +1%, system sales grew 4% excluding currency, 133 restaurants opened, restaurant margin reached 23.0% (+370 bp), operating profit rose 22%, and net income rose 21%. The next session closed +9.16% on $450.8 million. Tax reform was a major margin contributor, so this was strong but not fully organic proof. [Yum China release, published 2017-04-05 at 4:30 p.m. EDT](https://ir.yumchina.com/news-releases/news-release-details/yum-china-achieved-strong-first-quarter-same-store-sales-growth/); [SEC 8-K filed 2017-04-05 at 4:45 p.m. EDT](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1673358/000156459017006073/yumc-8k_20170405.htm).

The disclosure introduced execution risk soon after breakaway but did not terminate the price trend. [SEC 8-K filed 2017-04-21](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1673358/000119312517132662/d307602d8k.htm).

At the economic break, Q1 revenue was $1.284 billion, down 2% reported, but system sales rose 4% excluding currency and same-store sales turned +1%. Operating profit was $254 million (+22%), restaurant margin 23.0% (+370 bp), operating cash flow $357 million, and capital spending $74 million; ending cash was $1.074 billion before short-term investments. Digital made the apparently mature footprint look more like a data-enabled platform: loyalty was approximately 93 million, delivery represented about 12% of sales, and management said the 550-600 gross-new-unit plan could accompany double-digit operating-profit growth excluding currency. Q2 supplied scale but also the failure case. Revenue of $1.594 billion was nearly flat year over year, even as system sales rose 7%, same-store sales 3%, restaurant margin 270 bp to 15.3%, and operating profit 64% to $143 million. Loyalty passed 100 million, mobile payment exceeded 40%, delivery reached 13%, and the Daojia investment added delivery know-how; year-to-date operating cash flow of $473 million still exceeded $177 million of capital spending. [Company release published 2017-07-05](https://ir.yumchina.com/news-releases/news-release-details/yum-china-reports-q2-same-store-sales-growth-3-and-operating?ID=2284757&c=254434&p=irol-newsArticle). The 12.91% next-session decline was therefore a quality/durability reset, not a contradiction of the reported profit number.

By Q3, the arc was more organic: revenue rose to $2.038 billion from $1.883 billion, same-store sales grew 6%, system sales 10%, restaurant margin 80 bp to 20.0%, and operating profit 11% to $317 million. Year-to-date revenue was $4.916 billion (+3%) and operating profit $714 million (+23%); loyalty exceeded 120 million, mobile payment exceeded 45%, and delivery exceeded 14%. [Company Q3 release published 2017-10-05](https://ir.yumchina.com/static-files/0ef55c6a-f4d7-416e-958e-4da93c7b163b). The October 17 financial presentation then showed $987 million of January-August operating cash flow, $1.6 billion of cash and short-term investments, zero debt, and ongoing operating targets. [Investor Day financial presentation published 2017-10-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1673358/000156459017019559/yumc-ex996_7.htm). This was ample self-funded runway; the live questions had shifted to Pizza Hut execution, inflation, and whether digital engagement could keep comps positive after the one-time tax benefit lapped.
