# NVDA 2004 winner autopsy

## Bottom line

NVDA's 2004 alpha was a recovery in the market's estimate of product competitiveness, mix and future design-win runway, not simply semiconductor beta or a one-session earnings squeeze. The August quarter exposed weak desktop demand and collapsed the stock; the GeForce 6600 and 6200 then extended the new Shader Model 3.0 architecture from enthusiast into mainstream and value price points; an October 25 preannouncement showed that GeForce 6 mix and share gains were already lifting revenue; and the November 4 report paired a large EPS beat with 470bp year-over-year gross-margin expansion and above-consensus forward revenue. The December 7 Sony next-generation console GPU agreement then replaced part of the fading Xbox narrative with a longer-duration platform opportunity. Price and liquidity corroborated the sequence: by the 2004-11-05 onset NVDA was +43.18% over 42 sessions versus +10.95% for XLK, dollar volume was 3.86 standard deviations above its 21-day history, and the earnings jump held over 3/5/10 sessions. Yet NVDA remained -23.97% YTD at onset and had not made a 252-day closing high, showing that this was an expectations repair escaping a damaged prior model, not an already-crowded annual leader.

## Catalyst ladder

- **2004-08-07 — Q2 reset / damaged prior.** NVIDIA reported Q2 revenue of $456.1m and net income of $5.1m; the weak desktop result created the low-expectation base from which later evidence was re-underwritten. Publication date: 2004-08-07: [ITmedia](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/0408/07/news010.html).
- **2004-08-12 — GeForce 6600 launch.** NVIDIA brought the GeForce 6 feature set into the sub-$200 mainstream market, expanding the addressable tier below the 6800. Publication date: 2004-08-12: [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/nvidia-goes-mainstream-with-geforce-6600-and-6600-gt/1100-6104700/).
- **2004-10-11 — GeForce 6200 launch.** The 6200 completed the architecture's move into the value segment; boards were scheduled for November shipment. Publication date: 2004-10-11: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/on-prem/2004/10/11/nvidia-pushes-geforce-6200_at_value_end/944016).
- **2004-10-25, filed 2004-10-27 — preliminary Q3 raise.** NVIDIA lifted expected Q3 revenue to $510m-$515m from $470m-$502m and explicitly attributed growth to GeForce 6 mix and share gains. Publication date: 2004-10-25; SEC filing date: 2004-10-27: [SEC exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581004000028/prelimpressrelease.htm). The filing anchor is also present in [`data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet); [`data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet) has no 2004 NVDA coverage.
- **2004-11-05 — final Q3 beat and raised forward frame.** Revenue was $515.6m versus $509m consensus; EPS was $0.15 versus $0.09 consensus; gross margin was 32.3% versus 27.6% a year earlier; and management's next-quarter revenue range was above the then-published consensus. Publication date: 2004-11-05: [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/technology/profit-surges-at-nvidia-10192784). The associated 8-K was filed 2004-11-04 (accepted 21:34 UTC in the local EDGAR anchor), as recorded in [`data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet).
- **2004-12-07 — Sony next-generation console GPU partnership.** Sony Computer Entertainment and NVIDIA disclosed joint development of the GPU for Sony's next-generation entertainment system, including tools and middleware cooperation. Publication date: 2004-12-07: [Sony Interactive Entertainment](https://sonyinteractive.com/jp/press-releases/2004/041207-2/).

## Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** The August 6 session fell 35.23% on the third-largest dollar-volume day of 2004, and NVDA was still -23.97% YTD at onset. From September 1 through October 25 it gained only 5.04% and suffered a 14.60% maximum drawdown: damaged expectations, then stabilization.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** 6600 and 6200 launches expanded the GeForce 6 stack; the October 25 preannouncement demonstrated product/share translation; November 4 proved earnings and margin leverage; December 7 added a multi-year console design win.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At onset the 42-day gain was 43.18%, or +32.23pp versus XLK and +38.57pp versus SPY, with the first 63-day closing high on that date.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** Onset dollar volume was the year's largest, z21 was 3.86, and 5-day dollar volume was 2.14 times the 60-day average; the close-to-close information jump held for at least ten sessions.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** The repo's per-ticker options history begins in 2026-06. No defensible 2004 options evidence exists.

Consequently, every 2004 repo-panel value is **null** and none is backfilled into the machine evidence.

Public filings still reveal the point-in-time arc. Before the run, FY2004 revenue had declined to $1.823bn from $1.909bn, net income fell to $74.4m from $90.8m, and Q4 gross margin was 29.3% ($138.2m gross profit on $472.1m revenue), published 2004-02-12: [SEC earnings exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581004000005/fourthquarterprfinancials.htm). The August quarter then printed only $456.1m revenue and $5.1m net income (article published 2004-08-07: [ITmedia](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/0408/07/news010.html)). During the run, Q3 revenue reached $515.6m (+6% year over year), gross margin expanded to 32.3% from 27.6%, and EPS reached $0.15 versus $0.04 a year earlier and $0.09 consensus (article published 2004-11-05: [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/technology/profit-surges-at-nvidia-10192784)). The runway changed qualitatively too: GeForce 6 occupied high, mainstream and value tiers by October, and Sony's December 7 announcement added next-generation console development. The honest read is a margin/mix recovery with widening product runway; local PIT panels cannot independently quantify it for this era.

## Research echo

Consensus was behind the operating turn. On November 5, the published frame was $509m revenue and $0.09 EPS; NVIDIA delivered $515.6m and $0.15 after having already raised the revenue range on October 25. UBS moved from “reduce” to “neutral” on November 5, while American Technology Research described the quarter as dramatically better than expected and lifted the next-quarter consensus frame. Publication date: 2004-11-05: [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/technology/profit-surges-at-nvidia-10192784), [EDN](https://www.edn.com/analysts-couch-nvidias-outlook-bucks-trend/).

The “price escaped the published model” read is therefore grounded in expectations rather than invented targets: the stock rose 14.47% on November 5, held the prior close for ten sessions, and reached its first 126-day closing high on December 7 while brokerage research was only moving from a negative rating toward neutral and revising estimates after the fact.

- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** At onset NVDA exceeded XLK by 32.23pp over 42 sessions and SPY by 38.57pp; forward 252-session excess was +90.01pp versus XLK.
- **options_mirage — ruled_out.** No 2004 options evidence is used; the options stage is unknown rather than inferred.
- **Current extension hazard — possible.** The 2026-07-16 spot is 7.77% above the modeled gamma flip, but this is a current snapshot unrelated to the historical entry.
- **Current call-wall/pin hazard — possible.** Current modeled magnets at $210/$215 sit close to spot and `pin_risk` is true; this is model context, not signed flow.
- **Catalyst exhaustion — possible after the run.** By early 2005 the October earnings repair and December Sony announcement were known; persistence then required shipment, mix and margin execution. It was not yet evident at onset.
