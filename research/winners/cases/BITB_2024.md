# BITB — 2024 winner autopsy

## Bottom line

BITB's 2024 move was overwhelmingly a bitcoin repricing transmitted through a newly approved spot ETP, not durable security-selection alpha inside the spot-bitcoin cohort. The January regulatory opening created the wrapper and a low 0.20% fee helped BITB gather assets; the April supply halving, September monetary easing, an increasingly explicit pro-crypto Trump platform, heavy pre-election cohort inflows, and the November 6 election result successively reduced access, scarcity, liquidity, and policy discounts. That mosaic explains the 22.29 percentage points of 21-trading-day excess return over SPY at the November 7 onset. But BITB lagged the closest locally available peer, IBIT, by 0.23 points over the same window and by 0.45 points since their common January 11 launch. The autopsy therefore finds large index alpha, essentially zero cohort alpha, and no evidence that options caused the onset. BITB did sustain the move: the repo labels it a clean hold and durable winner, with +37.70 points of 126-day forward excess return versus SPY.

## Catalyst ladder

- **2024-01-10 — regulatory access.** The SEC approved exchange rule changes allowing spot-bitcoin ETP shares to list and trade, removing the central U.S. access barrier. Publication date: 2024-01-10. [SEC statement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/gensler-statement-spot-bitcoin-011023).
- **2024-01-11 — product launch and fee positioning.** BITB began trading with a 0.20% management fee, waived for six months on the first $1 billion. Publication date: 2024-01-11. [Bitwise launch release](https://bitwiseinvestments.com/newsroom/the-bitwise-bitcoin-etf-bitb-begins-trading).
- **2024-03-11 — early asset-gathering proof.** Bitwise reported BITB had exceeded $2 billion of AUM two months after launch, showing that the wrapper had achieved institutional scale. Publication date: 2024-03-11. [Bitwise release](https://bitwiseinvestments.com/newsroom/bitb-crosses-2-billion-in-assets).
- **2024-04-18 — halving became imminent public information; 2024-04-19 — protocol event.** AP explained the imminent 50% reduction in miner rewards before it occurred; LSEG later records the halving on April 19. Publication dates: 2024-04-18 and 2024-04-19. [AP explainer](https://apnews.com/article/d6053474e647114220648902fb02be5a), [LSEG research](https://www.lseg.com/en/ftse-russell/research/bitcoin-halving).
- **2024-07-27 — policy optionality.** Trump publicly promised that the federal government would retain its bitcoin and form the core of a strategic national stockpile, and promised a change in SEC leadership if elected. Publication date: 2024-07-27. [Axios report](https://www.axios.com/2024/07/27/trump-bitcoin-strategic-reserve-fire-gary-gensler-nashville).
- **2024-08-30 — potential asset consolidation.** BITB filed an 8-K announcing an agreement to acquire the assets of Osprey Bitcoin Trust. This was BITB-specific but low durability because closing remained conditional and the transaction was later terminated. Filing date: 2024-08-30. [SEC filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1763415/000095017024102165/0000950170-24-102165-index.html).
- **2024-09-18 — easier liquidity regime.** The FOMC cut the federal-funds target range by 50 basis points to 4.75%-5.00%. Publication date: 2024-09-18. [Federal Reserve statement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20240918a.htm).
- **2024-10-18 — derivatives-access expansion.** The SEC approved listing and trading of options on BITB and BTC on NYSE American. Publication date: 2024-10-18. [SEC rulemaking record](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/nyseamer/2024/34-101386.pdf).
- **2024-11-01 — pre-election flow confirmation.** Reuters reported $917.2 million of one-day net inflows into U.S. spot-bitcoin ETPs on October 30, the largest since March, as investors positioned for the election. Publication date: 2024-11-01. [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/crypto-etfs-see-big-inflows-ahead-of-us-election-traders-brace-for-volatility-3697889).
- **2024-11-06 — election-policy resolution.** AP reported Trump's victory and bitcoin's nearly 8% move above $75,000 to a record, explicitly tying the repricing to expectations of a more favorable crypto regime. Publication/filing date: 2024-11-07. [CoinNess flow report](https://coinness.com/en/news/1110828), [SEC 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1763415/000095017024124134/bitb-20240930.htm).

## Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** After March's high-volume peak, BITB spent roughly seven months below its March closing high; the first full-window 63d high did not arrive until October 18. The prior is short because the fund launched in January.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Regulatory approval, launch/AUM proof, halving, rate easing, pro-crypto campaign commitments, derivatives access, cohort inflows, and the election result progressively changed the distribution of access, supply, liquidity, and policy outcomes.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0, BITB was +22.29pp versus SPY over 21td and +23.22pp over 42td, with fresh 63d and 126d closing highs. Relative breakaway versus IBIT was absent: -0.23pp over 21td.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** The 5d/60d dollar-volume ratio was 1.71 and November 6 printed $229.11m, although t0's own z21 was -0.53. Confirmation was episodic rather than a single t0 burst.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** Options listing approval preceded t0 by 20 calendar days, but the repo has no 2024 chain, GEX, or trade-level NBBO history for BITB. Timing permits a contribution; evidence does not identify one.

## Fundamentals arc

The requested PIT-gated corporate surfaces contain no BITB observations: [`data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet), [`data/edgar/statements.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/statements.parquet), [`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet), and [`data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet). Consequently revenue, gross margin, operating margin, EPS, and cash runway are null. They are also poor economic descriptors for a grantor trust whose sole investment is bitcoin.

The correct PIT arc is balance-sheet scale and fee drag. At launch on 2024-01-11 BITB charged 0.20%, temporarily waived on the first $1 billion. By the 2024-11-07 filing, the September 30 snapshot showed $2.502 billion of net assets, 39,435.5408 BTC, 72.38 million shares, and NAV of $34.57; inception-to-date net creations were $2.143 billion, while investment income was zero and net expenses were $1.942 million. This was not operating-margin expansion: it was asset gathering plus bitcoin appreciation, offset slightly by sponsor fees. The later 2025-03-17 annual filing is excluded from the t0 inference but supplies outcome context: at December 31 the trust held 40,289.1335 BTC and $3.762 billion of net assets, with $3.671 million of net expenses and $2.175 billion of net capital-share inflows for the year. Publication dates: 2024-11-07 and 2025-03-17. [September 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1763415/000095017024124134/bitb-20240930.htm), [2024 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1763415/000095017025040572/bitb-20241231.htm).

## Research echo

There is no analyst-target concept for a pass-through bitcoin ETP and the repo has no target data. The relevant echo was bitcoin-price research. On 2024-09-12, Standard Chartered published an approximately $125,000 end-2024 bitcoin target under a Trump win and $75,000 under a Harris win. Spot was still below its March record before the election, so the bullish published model had not yet been reached; after the November 6 result bitcoin broke above $75,000, escaping the bearish-election branch but remaining well below the bullish $125,000 branch at t0. Publication dates: 2024-09-12 and 2024-11-06. [Target report](https://www.investing.com/news/cryptocurrency-news/standard-chartered-sets-125000-2024-yearend-target-for-bitcoin-if-trump-wins-3614067), [AP spot report](https://apnews.com/article/bitcoin-crypto-donald-trump-election-c2e2a1a895288c5e9c0df2721012a5bb).

For BITB itself, price did **not** escape the cohort model: its 21d, 42d, 126d, and launch-to-t0 returns were within 0.23pp, 0.03pp, 0.04pp, and 0.45pp of IBIT. The published-model escape occurred in bitcoin versus conventional risk assets, not BITB versus near-identical spot wrappers.

## Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** BITB is an open-ended spot ETP with creation/redemption arbitrage, and its returns closely matched IBIT. Speculative sentiment existed, but no low-float equity squeeze mechanism is needed.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The election caused a large jump, yet the ladder began in January and the repo records positive 21d/63d/126d forward excess, a clean hold, and no blow-off.
- **sector_beta — likely.** Interpreting "sector" as the spot-bitcoin ETP cohort, this is the dominant explanation: BITB differed from IBIT by only -0.23pp over 21d and -0.45pp since launch through t0.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Options approval preceded t0, but there is no historical GEX or trade-level evidence. Assigning the move to options would exceed the data.

Current hazards are substantial extension after a vertical move, bitcoin's 24/7 price discovery versus BITB's exchange hours, fee and tracking drag, custody/operational concentration, policy-expectation reversal, and catalyst exhaustion after approval/halving/election resolution. Call-wall overhead is **unknown** because no current BITB GEX snapshot exists. The later repo outcome mitigates but does not erase the ex-ante extension hazard: +37.70pp 126d forward excess and a clean hold were outcomes unavailable at t0.
