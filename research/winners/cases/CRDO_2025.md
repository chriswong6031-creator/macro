# CRDO 2025 failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

CRDO's apparent January 2025 breakaway was the late, liquid aftershock of a real earnings-led platform rerating, not a fresh information event: the decisive public reset was the December 2, 2024 disclosure that fiscal-Q2 revenue had grown 64% year over year and that fiscal-Q3 revenue was expected to jump to $115-$125 million, which made Active Electrical Cable (AEC) demand for AI clusters modelable ([Credo, published 2024-12-02](https://investors.credosemi.com/news-events/news/news-details/2024/Credo-Reports-Second-Quarter-of-Fiscal-Year-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). By the mechanical January 17 t0, CRDO was already +116.33% over 63 sessions, had spent six weeks digesting a 47.89% earnings gap, and was only +2.42 percentage points ahead of SOXX over 21 sessions; a same-day target increase to $90 from $80 was the only identifiable public rung ([TipRanks/The Fly, published 2025-01-17 10:56 ET](https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/credo-technology-price-target-raised-to-90-from-80-at-barclays?mod=mw_quote_news)). Real cash participation and all three 63/126/252-session closing highs made the setup look complete, but the evidence was late and fragile: DeepSeek's efficiency shock hit the AI complex on January 27 ([AP, published 2025-01-27](https://apnews.com/article/52c54e361616509280bd2775674b6b4b)), CRDO fell 30.91% that day versus SOXX's 7.48%, and the March filing later showed that one end customer had become 86% of quarterly revenue and one hyperscaler supplied more than 95% of the year-over-year product-sales increase ([SEC 10-Q, filed 2025-03-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1807794/000162828025011738/crdo-20250201.htm)). The business did execute—revenue reached $135.0 million and GAAP operating income $26.2 million—but the January price had already capitalized the inflection before customer breadth, cash conversion, or a new company-specific catalyst could de-risk it. The result was a classic late-stage blow-off: the thesis was economically real, yet this entry episode failed because extension, catalyst staleness, concentrated growth, and two-sided liquidity overwhelmed the otherwise exceptional headline fundamentals.

This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`).
