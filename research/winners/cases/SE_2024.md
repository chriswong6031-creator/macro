# SE 2024 winner autopsy

## Bottom line

SE's 2024 alpha was a staircase re-underwriting of Sea from a cash-burning, competitively threatened e-commerce story into a three-engine profitable-growth platform, not a one-day binary event. March supplied the balance-sheet and annual-profit floor; May showed that Shopee volume, Garena engagement, and SeaMoney credit could all grow again; August raised Shopee GMV guidance and pulled forward its expected EBITDA inflection; and November converted that promise into positive Shopee EBITDA alongside faster group revenue, a growing but still controlled loan book, and stronger Free Fire bookings. The September 23 mechanical onset was therefore a tape confirmation between earnings dates, not a same-day corporate catalyst: by then SE was already +135.81% YTD and +114.15 percentage points versus SPY, but fresh long-horizon highs and expanded dollar volume showed capital continuing to accept the combined growth-plus-profit model. The move sustained because subsequent operating evidence improved the model again; it was not merely XLC or China-internet beta, though a favorable late-September risk backdrop likely helped the final breakaway.

## Catalyst ladder

The repo filing-anchor panels [`data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet) and [`data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet) contain **zero SE rows**. Sea is a foreign private issuer that furnishes 6-Ks and files a 20-F, so absence from an 8-K-specific surface is expected coverage failure, not evidence that no disclosure occurred.

- **2024-03-04 — earnings/guidance:** Sea published Q4/FY2023 results: its first full year of net profit after the IPO, $8.5 billion of cash/investments, an expectation that 2024 would also be profitable, high-teens Shopee GMV guidance, expected second-half Shopee adjusted-EBITDA profitability, and double-digit Free Fire user/bookings growth. This reset solvency and competitive-decline assumptions. [Sea release, published 2024-03-04](https://cdn.sea.com/webmain/static/resource/seagroup/website/investornews/4Q2023/STWdlYqdqwmJntN4lrDk/2024.03.04%20Sea%20Fourth%20Quarter%20and%20Full%20Year%202023%20Results.pdf).
- **2024-04-26 — annual filing:** Sea filed its FY2023 Form 20-F, giving the market audited detail behind the first profitable year and the balance-sheet reset. This was confirmatory rather than a new operating inflection. [SEC Form 20-F, filed 2024-04-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1703399/000114036124022429/ef20015280_20f.htm).
- **2024-05-14 — earnings:** Q1 revenue grew 22.8%; Shopee GMV grew 36.3%; SeaMoney revenue grew 21.0% with its loan book up 28.7% and 90-day NPL stable at 1.4%; Garena bookings returned to 10.8% growth. Shopee EBITDA was still negative, so this rung established renewed growth while leaving profitability execution to prove. [Sea release, published 2024-05-14](https://cdn.sea.com/webmain/static/resource/seagroup/website/investornews/1Q2024/RaOK5KBcEryjDR0lKovo/2024.05.14%20Sea%20First%20Quarter%202024%20Results.pdf).
- **2024-08-13 — earnings/guidance:** Q2 revenue grew 23.0%; Shopee GMV grew 29.1%; management raised full-year Shopee GMV guidance to mid-20% growth and expected positive adjusted EBITDA from Q3. Garena bookings grew 21.1%, SeaMoney's loan book grew 39.5% with 1.3% 90-day NPL, and Brazil reached positive contribution margin per order. This was the decisive estimate-upgrade rung immediately preceding the September breakaway. [Sea release, published 2024-08-13](https://cdn.sea.com/webmain/static/resource/seagroup/website/investornews/2Q2024/TWIFPvNsKrWONCEaa711/2024.08.13%20Sea%20Second%20Quarter%202024%20Results.pdf).
- **2024-11-12 — earnings/guidance:** Q3 revenue accelerated to 30.8%; Shopee revenue rose 42.6% and adjusted EBITDA turned positive in both Asia and Brazil; SeaMoney's loan book grew 73.2% with 90-day NPL improving to 1.2%; and Free Fire's full-year bookings outlook moved above 30% growth. This extended the run because the promised profit inflection arrived without sacrificing growth. [Sea release, published 2024-11-12](https://cdn.sea.com/webmain/static/resource/seagroup/website/investornews/3Q2024/RP8KKr0FKNxCwFHgBOUJ/2024.11.12%20Sea%20Third%20Quarter%202024%20Results.pdf).

No company-specific publication was found on 2024-09-23. Treating that date itself as a fundamental catalyst would introduce a false precision; it is a market-confirmation date in a ladder whose nearest major public rungs were 2024-08-13 and 2024-11-12.

## Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** SE entered 2024 at $38.45 after the 2021-2023 collapse and before the March profitability reset. The base was compressed in price and expectations, although the local file cannot measure valuation multiples.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Four sequential earnings publications moved the model from annual survival/profitability, to renewed growth, to raised Shopee guidance, to demonstrated simultaneous growth and segment profit.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At 2024-09-23, 42-day excess return was +33.47 pp versus SPY and the close was a fresh 63/126/252-session high.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** Dollar volume was +2.67 standard deviations versus the trailing 21 sessions at `t0`; each major earnings rung also appears among 2024's largest dollar-volume days.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** The repo has no 2024 per-ticker options history and no current SE snapshot. Price/volume behavior cannot establish an options mechanism.

## Fundamentals arc

Repo-native PIT coverage is **null for SE**: [`data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet), [`data/edgar/statements.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/statements.parquet), [`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet), and [`data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet) each contain zero SE rows. The arc below therefore uses only publication-dated company releases and the filed 20-F; it is not represented as repo-derived structured data.

Before the run, FY2023 revenue was $13.1 billion (+4.9%), gross profit $5.83 billion (+12.5%; about 44.7% gross margin), net income $162.7 million versus a $1.66 billion 2022 loss, and adjusted EBITDA $1.2 billion versus negative $878.1 million. The quality caveat was that Q4 revenue grew only 4.8%, Shopee adjusted EBITDA was negative $225.3 million, and Garena bookings were still far below 2022; cash and treasury investments of $8.5 billion nevertheless supplied substantial runway. [Sea release, published 2024-03-04](https://cdn.sea.com/webmain/static/resource/seagroup/website/investornews/4Q2023/STWdlYqdqwmJntN4lrDk/2024.03.04%20Sea%20Fourth%20Quarter%20and%20Full%20Year%202023%20Results.pdf).

During the run, quarterly group revenue growth stepped from 22.8% in Q1 to 23.0% in Q2 and 30.8% in Q3. Gross profit was $1.6 billion, $1.6 billion, and $1.9 billion respectively; using reported unrounded revenue, gross margin was about 42.8%, 41.6%, and 43.4%, so the thesis was not simple gross-margin expansion. It was operating leverage and mix: Shopee adjusted EBITDA improved from negative $21.7 million in Q1 to negative $9.2 million in Q2 and positive $34.4 million in Q3, while its revenue accelerated; SeaMoney adjusted EBITDA remained positive as loans expanded from $3.3 billion to $4.6 billion and NPL stayed 1.2%-1.4%; Garena bookings growth accelerated from 10.8% to 21.1% to 24.3%. Cash/investments rose from $8.6 billion at Q1 to $9.0 billion at Q2 and $9.9 billion at Q3, although $429 million of the Q3 increase came from capped-call settlement proceeds. Sources: [Q1 release, 2024-05-14](https://cdn.sea.com/webmain/static/resource/seagroup/website/investornews/1Q2024/RaOK5KBcEryjDR0lKovo/2024.05.14%20Sea%20First%20Quarter%202024%20Results.pdf), [Q2 release, 2024-08-13](https://cdn.sea.com/webmain/static/resource/seagroup/website/investornews/2Q2024/TWIFPvNsKrWONCEaa711/2024.08.13%20Sea%20Second%20Quarter%202024%20Results.pdf), and [Q3 release, 2024-11-12](https://cdn.sea.com/webmain/static/resource/seagroup/website/investornews/3Q2024/RP8KKr0FKNxCwFHgBOUJ/2024.11.12%20Sea%20Third%20Quarter%202024%20Results.pdf).

The tension in the arc matters: Q1-Q2 adjusted EBITDA remained below the prior-year periods because Sea reinvested, especially in e-commerce marketing. The multiple expanded because evidence increasingly suggested those investments were funding faster growth without reopening an existential cash-burn loop; Q3 was the first clean simultaneous proof.

## Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out:** the move unfolded over four earnings rungs, with accelerating reported revenue and segment economics; local short-interest history is unavailable, so this rules out a meme-first explanation, not all squeeze contribution.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out:** the largest liquidity days span March, May, August, and November, while the September detector onset had no same-day company event.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out:** at `t0`, SE exceeded XLC by 31.85 pp over 42 sessions and 111.83 pp YTD. Late-September macro beta may have helped, but cannot explain the year-long magnitude or earnings-day volume pattern.
- **options_mirage — possible:** options evidence is absent, so options cannot be credited as a driver or conclusively excluded; the equity/fundamental evidence stands independently.

Current hazards are **extended_after_vertical_move** (SE had already risen 135.81% YTD at `t0`), **catalyst_exhausted** (four estimate-resetting prints increase the hurdle for incremental surprise), **credit_cycle_exposure** (SeaMoney's loan book grew 73.2% by Q3 and credit-loss provisions rose), and **competitive_reinvestment** (Shopee economics can reverse if subsidies or logistics spending re-escalate). **call_wall_overhead is unknown** because no SE options surface exists.
