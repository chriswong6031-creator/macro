# NEO 2020 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

NeoGenomics' 2020 alpha was a platform-and-runway re-rating rather than a COVID-testing trade or a single binary event: the pandemic first compressed an otherwise growing oncology-testing franchise, then the April capital raise removed near-term financing risk while the May Inivata partnership and June 29 launch converted “liquid biopsy” from a slide-deck option into a commercial product suite. The tape broke away immediately after that launch, but the move persisted because subsequent public evidence showed the core testing business recovering, Pharma Services backlog compounding, and the new balance sheet funding expansion despite depressed near-term margins. NEO therefore finished 2020 up 84.07%, versus 13.30% for XLV and 18.33% for SPY; the mosaic is recovery plus a larger addressable market plus runway, not sector beta, meme attention, or one day's news.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** NEO fell from $31.78 on 2020-01-13 to $19.89 on 2020-03-18 and did not regain a 63-day closing high until 2020-07-01 (`data/yahoo/NEO.parquet`). Q1 filed data showed gross margin compression and an operating loss (`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Business continuity and Q1 growth were followed by a large capital raise, Bayer access, Inivata commercial rights, and the June 29 three-test launch; Q2 and Q3 later supplied recovery evidence.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At 2020-07-01, NEO was +27.94pp versus XLV over 21 sessions and +24.41pp over 42, with a first post-compression 63-day closing high (`data/yahoo/{NEO,XLV}.parquet`).
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** Dollar volume reached $57.62 million at `t0`, z21 1.67, after $66.96 million on June 26 and $61.86 million on June 30 (`data/yahoo/NEO.parquet`). This confirms capacity, not buyer identity.

The Q1 2020 statement in `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet` was filed 2020-04-29 and was therefore known at `t0`: revenue $106.0 million versus $95.6 million in Q1 2019 (+10.9%); gross profit $46.4 million versus $47.1 million, gross margin 43.7% versus 49.3%; operating income -$5.3 million versus +$2.5 million; net income -$7.0 million; CFO -$6.9 million; cash $86.3 million. The April 30 offerings then added about $300.4 million gross before costs and overallotments ([company release, 2020-04-30](https://ir.neogenomics.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/143/neogenomics-announces-pricing-of-public-offerings-of-common-stock-and-convertible-senior-notes)), changing runway more than near-term earnings power.

During the run, Q2 was published 2020-07-28: revenue $87.0 million, gross profit $28.0 million (32.2% margin), operating loss $18.9 million, and net loss $6.8 million in `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`; the accompanying release attributed the pressure to COVID-related volume loss and retaining employees, while reporting a 63% Pharma Services backlog increase to $173 million ([filed exhibit, 2020-07-28](https://ir.neogenomics.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001077183-20-000158/a99-106302020earningsr.htm)). Q3, filed 2020-10-29, rebounded to revenue $125.4 million, gross profit $54.1 million (43.1% margin), operating income $4.7 million, and net income $2.6 million (`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`). Thus the arc was growth → pandemic margin trough → volume/margin recovery, financed from strength.

No unstamped later facts are treated as pre-`t0` knowledge.

## 7. Research echo

The published model initially sat near the tape. The July 29 revision record identifies Needham's prior target as $33; NEO had already closed at $33.26 on 2020-07-01 (`data/yahoo/NEO.parquet`), effectively consuming that modeled upside as the liquid-biopsy suite launched ([24/7 Wall St., published 2020-07-29](https://247wallst.com/investing/2020/07/29/top-analyst-upgrades-and-downgrades-amd-amgen-dexcom-ebay-fireeye-kraft-heinz-3m-pfizer-seagate-sonos-starbucks-t-mobile-and-more/2/)).

Research caught up after results rather than before the breakaway: on 2020-07-29, Needham raised its target to $39 from $33, versus the prior $35.80 close ([24/7 Wall St., published 2020-07-29](https://247wallst.com/investing/2020/07/29/top-analyst-upgrades-and-downgrades-amd-amgen-dexcom-ebay-fireeye-kraft-heinz-3m-pfizer-seagate-sonos-starbucks-t-mobile-and-more/2/)); the same day's archived headlines record Benchmark to $40, Raymond James to $40, alongside the Needham change ([ChartExchange archive, entries dated 2020-07-29](https://chartexchange.com/symbol/nasdaq-neo/news/)). Historical point-in-time consensus breadth and estimate revisions are otherwise **null** because the repo has no analyst-target history and accessible archives are incomplete. The defensible read is “price escaped the April published model; targets followed,” not that target revisions caused the June 29–July 1 breakaway.
