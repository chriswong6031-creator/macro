# ATI 2025 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

ATI's 2025 alpha was a sequence, not a single headline: March index inclusion broadened mechanical demand, the May 1 quarter showed that years of aerospace-capacity investment were becoming earnings and margin, and the subsequent Airbus and Boeing agreements made the utilization runway more contract-backed and less hypothetical. The tape separated from both XLI and SPY before capital had a complete published model: at the May 2 onset ATI was already +22.22 percentage points versus XLI over 21 sessions, yet the more consequential evidence arrived afterward—Airbus more than doubled ATI's prior support, Pageland came online, Boeing expanded its titanium agreement, and Q3 delivered 21% aerospace-and-defense growth plus a large guidance increase. Thus the durable mechanism was a capacity-led aerospace cycle upswing with improving mix and margins; index flow and analyst target catch-up amplified it but do not explain the full-year 108.54% return.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present, qualified.** ATI closed at $55.03 on 2025-01-02, made a 63d high on February 4, then fell to $41.97 on April 8 before recovering. This was a volatile reset rather than a quiet narrow base; the April low sat 37.0% below the eventual t0 close.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Q1 earnings initiated the relative break, while Airbus, Pageland, Boeing, and Q3 guidance successively increased visibility into capacity utilization, customer share, margins, and cash generation.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 ATI led XLI by 22.22pp over 21 sessions and 15.96pp over 42 sessions, and closed at first 63d/126d highs; it also led SPY by 22.97pp over 21 sessions.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 dollar-volume z21 was 2.21 (episode census: 2.16) and the supplied 5d/60d ratio was 1.53; later earnings/contract and guidance days again registered z-scores above 3 on both relevant windows.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No ATI current snapshot exists in the three permitted repo surfaces, and ticker options history begins only in 2026-06. Price/volume cannot establish an options mechanism.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

PIT discipline materially changes what was knowable. [`data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet) makes FY2024 available on 2025-04-28: revenue rose 4.5% to $4.362bn, gross margin expanded from 19.23% in 2023 to 20.59%, operating margin from 11.17% to 13.96%, and CFO rose from $85.9m to $407.2m. The same annual panel does not make FY2025 available until 2026-04-27, so that row is excluded from the 2025 causal read.

[`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet), gated by `filed`, shows the during-run progression. Q1 2025 (filed May 1) revenue grew 9.7% year over year to $1.144bn, gross margin rose to 20.60% from 18.93%, and operating margin to 12.84% from 11.05%. Q2 (filed July 31) revenue grew 4.1%, gross margin improved to 21.26% from 20.76%, and operating margin to 14.12% from 13.02%. Q3 (filed October 28) revenue grew 7.1%, gross margin reached 22.68% from 21.39%, and operating margin 14.43% from 13.53%. This is the core arc: modest consolidated sales growth but accelerating aerospace mix and conversion.

[`data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet) reports GAAP EPS of $0.67, $0.70, and $0.78 for Q1-Q3 2025, but its mechanical `asof_date` lags the actual earnings filings by roughly 28 days; the earnings 8-K dates above are therefore the correct public timing anchors. ## 7. Research echo

The echo lagged the tape. On May 13, Deutsche Bank raised its target to $93 from $76, while ATI had already crossed the $65 area and the mechanical breakaway had occurred; on May 20 KeyBanc downgraded to Sector Weight and removed its $55 target, showing that consensus was not uniformly chasing the move. [MarketScreener recommendation history, entries published 2025-05-13 and 2025-05-20](https://uk.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/ATI-INC-11627/consensus/). [Dow Jones/Webull, published 2025-08-01](https://www.webull.com/news/13261759218213888); [TipRanks/The Fly, published 2025-08-01](https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/ati-price-target-raised-to-92-from-75-at-jpmorgan-thefly).

[Susquehanna/Benzinga, published 2025-10-29](https://www.sahmcapital.com/news/content/susquehanna-maintains-positive-on-ati-raises-price-target-to-120-2025-10-29); [MarketScreener, Deutsche Bank entry published 2025-10-29](https://uk.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/ATI-INC-11627/consensus/); [Reuters/Webull, published 2025-10-29](https://www.webull.com/news/13763227273339904). ## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** The move persisted across three quarterly publications and two major-airframer contracts, with rising revenue and margins; no repo float/short-interest history is available, so this rules out the label behaviorally, not through positioning data.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** ATI advanced from $66.57 at t0 to $114.76 at year-end, and distinct May, July, October, and December liquidity confirmations occurred.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** ATI beat XLI by 22.22pp over the 21 sessions into t0 and by 88.78pp for full-year 2025.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Historical options evidence is structurally unavailable, so options cannot be ruled in or out; importantly, the cash earnings, contracts, and tape do not require an options explanation.

Current hazards as of the repo's 2026-07-16 close are **extension** (ATI at $185.51, 61.7% above its 2025 year-end close and 178.7% above t0), **catalyst exhaustion** (possible after repeated contract and guidance re-underwriting), and **call walls** (unknown because ATI options surfaces are absent). Raw-material availability/pricing, aerospace build-rate delays, qualification timing, execution on new capacity, and customer concentration remain operating hazards; ATI itself enumerated these risks in its [Q3 release, published 2025-10-28](https://ir.atimaterials.com/news-events/news-details/2025/ATI-Announces-Third-Quarter-2025-Results/default.aspx).
