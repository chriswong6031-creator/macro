# EDU 2019 winner autopsy

## Bottom line

EDU's 2019 alpha was a multi-quarter re-underwriting of growth quality, not a one-print squeeze: a stock still 33.2% below its June 2018 adjusted high entered 2019 with regulation, rapid facility growth, and margin pressure embedded in expectations; the 2019-01-22 release showed 27.8% revenue growth, 38% K-12 growth, a 69.2% increase in adjusted net income, and 25%–28% next-quarter revenue guidance even though the seasonal GAAP loss worsened. Capital then received successively harder evidence that new centers could mature profitably—April operating income +64.1% and margin +260 bp, July another quarter of margin expansion, and October operating income +52.6% with margin +420 bp—while Koolearn's Hong Kong listing separately surfaced an online asset. The tape confirms a genuine relative breakaway and repeated liquidity renewal: at the 2019-01-22 onset EDU was +27.56 percentage points versus SPY over 21 sessions on a 3.86-sigma dollar-volume day, then produced +35.89 pp forward excess at 126 sessions and +59.03 pp at 252 sessions. It did not lead its closest locally available China-tutoring peer over every lookback (it trailed TAL over 63 sessions at onset), but the later margin ladder made the company-specific case progressively stronger and sustained the move through year-end.

## Catalyst ladder

- **2019-01-22 — Q2 FY2019 earnings and guidance (publication date).** Revenue grew 27.8%, K-12 revenue about 38%, and adjusted net income 69.2%; management guided the next quarter to 25%–28% revenue growth. The counterweight was real: GAAP operating loss widened to $28.6m and operating margin fell to -4.8% from -2.8%. This was the breakaway catalyst because demand and adjusted earnings survived the capacity/regulatory burden. [Company release, published 2019-01-22](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-second-fiscal-quarter-ended-0).
- **2019-03-27 — HKEX published notice of Koolearn's Hong Kong listing (publication date).** The notice specified that Koolearn would begin trading on 2019-03-28. The online subsidiary completed its offering and raised about HK$1.78bn net, earmarked for hiring, course and technology development, marketing, acquisitions/investments, and working capital. This gave public investors a separately priced online-growth asset, though EDU continued consolidating it. [HKEX participant notice, published 2019-03-27](https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/Services/Circulars-and-Notices/Participant-and-Members-Circulars/HKSCC/2019/ce_HKSCC_DNS_069_2019.pdf); offering proceeds were later disclosed in the [company's 2019-04-23 release](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-third-fiscal-quarter-ended/).
- **2019-04-23 — Q3 FY2019 earnings (publication date).** Revenue grew 28.9%, operating income 64.1%, and operating margin reached 12.0% from 9.4%; management guided Q4 revenue to $820.6m–$840.6m. The enrollment headline (+82.3%) was not fully comparable because regulatory compliance split spring enrollment recognition across quarters, an important false-positive guard. [Company release, published 2019-04-23](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-third-fiscal-quarter-ended/).
- **2019-07-23 — Q4/FY2019 earnings (publication date).** Quarterly revenue rose 20.2% to $842.9m, just above April's range; operating income rose 36.0% and operating margin expanded to 9.1% from 8.1%. FY2019 revenue grew 26.5% while FY operating margin fell to 9.9% from 10.7%, showing that the rerating depended on the improving exit rate rather than a clean full-year margin record. Q1 FY2020 revenue guidance was 22%–25%. [Company release, published 2019-07-23](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-fourth-fiscal-quarter-and-3).
- **2019-10-22 — Q1 FY2020 earnings (publication date).** Revenue grew 24.6%, enrollments 50.4%, operating income 52.6%, and operating margin reached 23.0% versus 18.8%; next-quarter revenue guidance called for 26%–29% growth. This was the clearest late-stage proof that facility utilization could convert enrollment growth into margin. [Company release, published 2019-10-22](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-first-fiscal-quarter-ended-0/).

## Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** EDU's adjusted close peaked at $104.00 on 2018-06-12, fell to $49.41 on 2018-12-24, and remained 33.2% below that high at t0. The compression coincided with costly expansion and reported margin deterioration documented on 2019-01-22.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** January demand resilience was followed by the Koolearn listing, April operating leverage, July confirmation, and October acceleration; the case could be revised on multiple public dates instead of one binary event.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 EDU had +27.56 pp 21-day and +30.98 pp 42-day excess versus SPY and a 63-day high. Relative leadership versus TAL was positive over 5/21/42 days but negative over 63 days, so the earliest break was partly cohort recovery and partly company-specific.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 dollar volume was 3.86 standard deviations above its trailing 21-day mean and the 5d/60d ratio was 1.51; July and October earnings produced two more of 2019's largest dollar-volume days.
