# RBRK 2024 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Rubrik's November-2024 breakaway was a post-IPO platform rerating around cyber resilience, subscription scale, and operating leverage. The proof began before t0: fiscal Q2 subscription ARR grew 40% to $919.1M, revenue grew 35%, the ARR contribution margin improved to -8% from -22%, and full-year guidance rose ([Rubrik, published 2024-09-09](https://ir.rubrik.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/Rubrik-Reports-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-Year-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). A Microsoft Ignite launch then extended cyber posture and recovery to Azure Blob Storage on 2024-11-19 ([Rubrik, published 2024-11-19](https://ir.rubrik.com/news-events/press-releases/news/details/2024/Rubrik-Delivers-Cyber-Resilience-Solution-for-Microsoft-Azure-Blob-Storage/default.aspx)); the mechanical t0 followed on 2024-11-21. Fiscal Q3 supplied the decisive financial rung: ARR crossed $1B, revenue grew 43%, free cash flow turned positive, and guidance rose again ([Rubrik, published 2024-12-05](https://ir.rubrik.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/Rubrik-Reports-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-Year-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). The mechanism was not generic AI excitement. It was a widening data-security control plane converting cloud workloads and cyber-recovery demand into subscription ARR while the cost structure moved toward cash generation.

- **Relative breakaway - present.** RBRK exceeded IGV by +42.80pp at 42 days and +92.95pp at 126 days.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Before t0, fiscal Q2 subscription revenue grew 50% to $191.3M, total revenue grew 35% to $205.0M, and subscription ARR grew 40% to $919.1M. The ARR contribution margin improved 14 percentage points to -8%, but free cash flow was still -$32.0M versus -$13.4M a year earlier; cash and short-term investments totaled $601.3M ([Rubrik, published 2024-09-09](https://ir.rubrik.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/Rubrik-Reports-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-Year-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). This was a high-growth, loss-making transition with improving unit leverage—not yet a mature cash compounder.

Fiscal Q3 moved the proof forward. Subscription revenue rose 55% to $221.5M, total revenue rose 43% to $236.2M, and subscription ARR reached $1.002B (+38%). The ARR contribution margin improved to -3% from -14%, operating cash flow reached $23.1M, and free cash flow reached $15.6M. Cash and short-term investments totaled $632.0M. GAAP operating loss was still $124.8M and net loss $130.9M, heavily affected by post-IPO stock-based compensation, so cash improvement should not be confused with GAAP profitability ([Rubrik, published 2024-12-05](https://ir.rubrik.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2024/Rubrik-Reports-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-Year-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx)).

Fiscal Q4 then showed subscription revenue of $243.7M (+54%), total revenue of $258.1M (+47%), subscription ARR of $1.093B (+39%), and an ARR contribution margin of +2% versus -12%. Operating cash flow reached $83.6M and free cash flow $75.2M; full-year free cash flow was $21.6M versus -$24.5M. Cash and short-term investments ended at $705.1M ([Rubrik, published 2025-03-13](https://ir.rubrik.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2025/Rubrik-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). The durable bridge was recurring revenue plus improving contribution economics, while cloud and recovery products widened cross-sell. Coverage gaps include a complete product-level ARR bridge, gross retention by cohort, and a clean split between recurring stock compensation and IPO-triggered expense.

After fiscal Q4, Piper Sandler moved to $87 from $83 on 2025-03-14, citing cloud transition, cyber resilience, cross-sell, and improving free-cash-flow expectations ([Investing.com, published 2025-03-14](https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/piper-sandler-raises-rubrik-stock-price-target-to-87-93CH-3929831)); RBRK closed at $70.64. ## 8. Ownership context (context-only)

**Context-only; descriptive background, explicitly not a signal.** RBRK listed in April 2024, and the local `data/quiver/sec13f.parquet` extract does not provide a complete post-IPO holder-breadth panel. Breadth change is therefore **unknown**. Form 13F can be filed up to 45 days after quarter-end ([SEC FAQ, dated 2023-05-24 and updated 2026-03-06](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/division-investment-management-frequently-asked-questions/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-13f)). Local insider records are incomplete and require filing-date rather than trade-date treatment; Form 4 is generally due within two business days ([SEC ownership-report rule release, published 2002-08-27](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2002/08/ownership-reports-trading-officers-directors-principal-security-holders)). Neither lane is used positively.
