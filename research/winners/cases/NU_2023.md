# NU 2023 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

NU's 2023 alpha came from a multi-quarter profitability and operating-leverage reset, not from customer growth or a single earnings surprise in isolation: the platform kept adding customers while ARPAC rose, cost to serve stayed below $1, gross margin expanded, reported profit compounded, and excess capital funded new products and Mexico/Colombia expansion. The February results made profitability credible; the May 15 Q1 release turned it into a repeatable model and produced the May 18 mechanical breakaway; subsequent customer, product, and earnings disclosures extended the re-underwriting. The distinction that matters for this specified episode is that the calendar-year stock was a very large winner (+104.67%), while the post-2023-05-18 leg was a slower clean hold: +22.85% over 126 sessions and +14.67 percentage points versus SPY, just below the lab's +15-point durable-winner threshold. The Q2 positive opening gap failed immediately and the Q3 report produced a persistent negative gap, showing that rising expectations and credit/regulatory concerns limited the rate of further alpha even as the fundamental arc kept improving. Public operating anchors are the [Q4/FY2022 release filed 2023-02-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1691493/000129281423000433/nu20230214_6k1.htm), [Q1 release filed 2023-05-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1691493/000129281423002286/nufs1q23_6k.htm), [Q2 release filed 2023-08-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1691493/000129281423003591/nufs2q23_6k.htm), and [Q3 release filed 2023-11-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1691493/000129281423004660/nu20231114_6k1.htm).
