# SMMT 2025 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

SMMT's 2025 alpha was an expectation-driven re-rating of one asset, ivonescimab, rather than an operating inflection: investors stacked FDA Fast Track status, a Pfizer combination-study collaboration, China Phase III success, an imminent second China approval, and the prospect that a PD-1/VEGF bispecific could displace entrenched checkpoint therapy. The tape reached an all-time closing high on April 24 before Summit's April 25 filing disclosed the approval and immature HARMONi-2 overall-survival hazard ratio, so much of the scientific and regulatory upside was already capitalized before the publication anchor. The same concentration that created convexity killed durability: the April 25 session rejected the news, and the first global Phase III HARMONi readout on May 30 delivered excellent progression-free survival but missed statistical significance on overall survival (HR 0.79, p=0.057). With no product revenue, rising trial spend, a single-asset valuation, and no demonstrated options-history support, the stock could not sustain the prior model of a near-Keytruda-scale franchise. The episode is therefore best understood as expectation saturation followed by endpoint de-risking in the wrong direction, not sector beta.

**Format contract — failed case:** this is a `failed_breakaway`; the run did not sustain.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

Dates below are publication or filing dates, never later-revealed effective dates.

1. **2025-02-24 — FDA Fast Track, global trial progress, and Pfizer collaboration.** Summit reported Fast Track designation for ivonescimab plus chemotherapy in post-TKI EGFR-mutant NSCLC, completion of HARMONi enrollment, HARMONi-7 site activation, and a Pfizer collaboration to test ivonescimab with multiple vedotin ADCs. These widened the perceived regulatory and combination-platform opportunity while putting the global HARMONi readout into mid-2025. [Summit release, published 2025-02-24](https://smmttx.com/news/press-releases/news-details/2025/Summit-Therapeutics-Reports-Financial-Results-and-Operational-Progress-for-the-Fourth-Quarter-and-Twelve-Months-Ended-December-31-2024-02-24-2025-12-00-AM/default.aspx); [Pfizer collaboration release, published 2025-02-24](https://smmttx.com/news/press-releases/news-details/2025/Summit-Therapeutics-Announces-Clinical-Trial-Collaboration-with-Pfizer-to-Evaluate-Ivonescimab-in-Combination-with-Pfizer-Antibody-Drug-Conjugates-ADCs-02-24-2025-12-00-AM/default.aspx).
2. **2025-04-25 — second China approval and immature OS signal.** Summit filed that China approved ivonescimab monotherapy for first-line PD-L1-positive NSCLC based on HARMONi-2 and disclosed an interim OS HR of 0.777 at 39% maturity. This was commercially and scientifically constructive, but it arrived one day after the mechanical t0/all-time close and therefore cannot explain the already-completed run without lookahead. [Summit 8-K, filed 2025-04-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1599298/000159929825000066/smmt-20250425.htm).
3. **2025-05-01 — Q1 results preserved runway but exposed burn.** Cash and short-term investments fell to $361.3 million from $412.3 million at year-end; Q1 net loss was $62.9 million and non-GAAP R&D rose to $47.1 million from $28.5 million. [Business Wire release, published 2025-05-01](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250501292458/en/Summit-Therapeutics-Reports-Financial-Results-and-Operational-Progress-for-the-First-Quarter-Ended-March-31-2025); [Summit 8-K, filed 2025-05-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1599298/000159929825000088/smmt-20250501.htm).
4. **2025-05-30 — global HARMONi readout broke the peak thesis.** PFS was statistically significant and clinically meaningful, but the primary OS analysis did not achieve statistical significance. Later full disclosure quantified the primary result as HR 0.79, 95% CI 0.62-1.01, p=0.057. The stock was down about 31% intraday, consistent with expectations being set above a merely positive PFS result. [Summit 8-K, filed 2025-05-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1599298/000159929825000099/smmt-20250530.htm); [Motley Fool market report, published 2025-05-30](https://www.fool.com/investing/2025/05/30/why-summit-therapeutics-plunged-today/).
5. **2025-08-11 — detailed HARMONi figures and conditional BLA intent.** Summit disclosed PFS HR 0.52 (p<0.00001) and OS HR 0.79 (p=0.057), said it intended to seek a BLA subject to review and FDA discussions, and reported $297.9 million of cash plus short-term investments at June 30. This restored a regulatory path but did not erase the missed primary OS threshold. [Summit Q2 release, published 2025-08-11](https://smmttx.com/news/press-releases/news-details/2025/Summit-Therapeutics-Reports-Financial-Results-and-Operational-Progress-for-the-Second-Quarter-Ended-June-30-2025/default.aspx).
6. **2025-09-07 — longer western follow-up improved the OS trend.** At WCLC, Summit reported HR 0.78 and nominal p=0.0332 after additional western follow-up, with North American HR 0.70. Because Asian patients remained locked and the p-value was explicitly nominal, this was supportive follow-up, not a rewrite of the prespecified primary analysis. [Summit WCLC release, published 2025-09-07](https://www.smmttx.com/news/press-releases/news-details/2025/Longer-Term-Follow-Up-of-Western-Patients-Showed-Improving-Favorable-Trend-in-Overall-Survival-in-Global-Phase-III-HARMONi-Clinical-Trial-for-Ivonescimab-Plus-Chemotherapy-in-2L-EGFRm-NSCLC/).
7. **2025-10-19/20 — HARMONi-6 detailed PFS and BLA plan.** China-only HARMONi-6 showed median PFS of 11.14 versus 6.90 months and HR 0.60 against tislelizumab plus chemotherapy; the following day Summit said it planned a Q4 2025 BLA submission based on global HARMONi. These events rebuilt some regulatory optionality but arrived after the failed episode and left global front-line translation unresolved. [Summit HARMONi-6 release, published 2025-10-19](https://smmttx.com/news/press-releases/news-details/2025/Ivonescimab-with-Chemotherapy-Reduced-the-Risk-of-Disease-Progression-or-Death-by-40-Compared-to-Tislelizumab-PD-1-Inhibitor-Plus-Chemotherapy-in-1L-Treatment-of-Patients-with-Squamous-NSCLC-in-the-HARMONi-6-Study-Conducted-by-Akeso-in-China/default.aspx); [Summit Q3 release, published 2025-10-20](https://www.smmttx.com/news/press-releases/news-details/2025/Summit-Therapeutics-Reports-Financial-Results-and-Operational-Progress-for-the-Third-Quarter-and-Nine-Months-Ended-September-30-2025/).

The fundamental conclusion is narrow: the 2025 move could only be sustained by a higher probability and/or larger commercial value for ivonescimab. There was no contemporaneous revenue, margin, or self-funding transition to absorb disappointment in the clinical model.
