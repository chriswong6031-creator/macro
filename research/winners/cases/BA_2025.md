# BA 2025 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

BA's 2025 episode was a turnaround re-rating with a finite shelf life, not a one-order pop and not simply Industrials beta. The prior combined a 2024 strike/safety reset, deeply negative margins and cash burn with an April tariff drawdown; capital then received several independent reasons to change the distribution of outcomes: Boeing won the F-47 program ([U.S. Department of Defense, published 2025-03-21](https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/4131804/trump-hegseth-announce-air-forces-next-generation-fighter-platform/)), agreed to monetize Digital Aviation assets for $10.55 billion, reported 18% Q1 revenue growth and sharply better operating performance ([Boeing release exhibit, published 2025-04-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000119312525093515/d938289dex991.htm); [Boeing, published 2025-04-23](https://investors.boeing.com/investors/news/press-release-details/2025/Boeing-Reports-First-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)), disclosed record-setting widebody demand ([Boeing, published 2025-05-14](https://investors.boeing.com/investors/news/press-release-details/2025/Boeing-and-Qatar-Airways-Announce-Historic-Order-for-up-to-210-Widebody-Airplanes/default.aspx)), and reached 737 output of 38 per month while bringing quarterly free-cash use close to zero ([Boeing, published 2025-07-29](https://investors.boeing.com/investors/news/press-release-details/2025/Boeing-Reports-Second-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)). That mosaic drove a 73.79% Apr. 4-to-Sep. 2 rebound, 45.74 percentage points ahead of XLI. It did not produce durable cohort alpha: the October disclosure paired positive quarterly free cash flow and approval for 42 monthly 737s with a $4.9 billion 777X charge and first delivery pushed to 2027 ([Boeing, published 2025-10-29](https://investors.boeing.com/investors/news/press-release-details/2025/Boeing-Reports-Third-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)). BA therefore finished 2025 only 3.32 points ahead of XLI, and its forward excess return from the mechanical onset turned negative by 126 sessions. The autopsy is “real operating re-underwriting, over-capitalized before development and cash-conversion risk had cleared.”

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present, by crisis/reset rather than quiet base.** FY2024 revenue fell 14.5%, GAAP operating margin was -16.10% and CFO was -$12.08 billion; after an early-2025 rebound, tariff stress took BA to $136.59 on April 4, 27.46% below the prior 252-session high. This was a wide, event-driven reset, not low-volatility compression.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** F-47, delivery recovery, the $10.55 billion divestiture, Q1 operating improvement, a multi-customer order ladder, legal-tail compression and the 38/month production milestone each changed a different leg of the model. The ladder was diverse enough that no single headline is sufficient.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then attenuated.** At t0 BA led XLI by 20.71 points over 21 sessions and 26.17 over 42, while printing its first 252-session high of 2025. From the April trough to September peak it led XLI by 45.74 points; full-year excess narrowed to 3.32 points and forward 126-session excess was -10.50 points.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, mixed.** T0 turnover was $2.941 billion at `z21=2.32/z60=1.99`; F-47 printed $4.774 billion at `z21=3.64/z60=4.55`, and both April positive gaps held 3/5/10 sessions. The largest run-day liquidity was nevertheless the AI171 decline, and Q2 earnings reversed lower on a high-volume session.
- **Options convexity — unknown for 2025.** Per-ticker options history begins in 2026-06, after the episode. No 2025 IV, open-interest wall, gamma or trade-level NBBO history exists in the permitted repo surfaces, so options cannot be assigned a causal role.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** The move persisted across F-47, delivery, divestiture, earnings, order, legal and production publications, with Q1/Q2 revenue acceleration and repeated multi-billion-dollar cash sessions. - **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The April 8 and April 23 gaps both held 3/5/10 sessions, t0 did not occur until May 8, and the closing high arrived September 2. Several later events separately added or removed value.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out for the breakaway; possible for the residual year.** BA led XLI by 20.71/26.17 points over 21/42 sessions at t0 and by 45.74 points from the April trough to September peak. By year-end the lead was only 3.32 points, so the late-year residual looked much more like cohort exposure.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Historical options evidence is structurally absent. Cash-market and fundamental evidence is sufficient to explain the run, but an options accelerant cannot be excluded without 2025 chain and trade-level NBBO data.
