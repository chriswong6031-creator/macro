# ASTS 2024 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

AST SpaceMobile's 2024 breakaway was a partnership-and-commercial-credibility rerating for a pre-revenue satellite-to-phone platform. The stock began the episode as a speculative space/telecom equity with financing and execution doubts. The May 2024 AT&T definitive commercial agreement, the May 2024 Verizon strategic partnership, and the August business update around first commercial satellites gave the market a ladder from concept to carrier-backed deployment path. By 2024-08-15, ASTS had outperformed XLC by +204.36pp over 42 trading days, +1,207.65pp over 63 trading days, and +526.93pp YTD.
