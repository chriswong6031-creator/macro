# BROS 2024 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Dutch Bros' November-2024 breakaway was an operating-inflection rerating in a high-unit-growth restaurant system. The 2024-11-06 third-quarter report paired 27.9% revenue growth with positive system and company-operated transaction growth, 38 openings, completion of mobile-order capability, and higher full-year revenue and adjusted-EBITDA guidance ([Dutch Bros, published 2024-11-06](https://investors.dutchbros.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx/1000/)). T0 on 2024-11-07 was the cash market's response: a +24.92% opening gap, +28.13% close-to-close gain, $919.8M of dollar volume, and the first 3/6/12-month closing high. The 2025-02-12 annual report then showed the broader engine—151 openings, 32.6% revenue growth, a 130-basis-point company-shop gross-margin gain, and a 150-basis-point contribution-margin gain—and the next session gapped +30.68% ([Dutch Bros, published 2025-02-12](https://investors.dutchbros.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx/1000/)). The mechanism was simultaneous same-shop stabilization, transaction tools, improving site selection, rapid unit growth, and operating leverage—not store count alone.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2024-11-06 - earnings/guidance:** Q3 revenue reached $338.2M (+27.9%), system same-shop sales and transactions rose 2.7% and 0.8%, company-operated same-shop sales and transactions rose 4.0% and 2.4%, and management raised 2024 revenue and adjusted-EBITDA guidance ([Dutch Bros, published 2024-11-06](https://investors.dutchbros.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx/1000/)).
- **2024-11-06 - product/operations:** Dutch Bros completed mobile-order capability rollout while opening 38 shops; management connected the next unit-growth phase to stronger site selection and development capacity ([Dutch Bros, published 2024-11-06](https://investors.dutchbros.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx/1000/)).
- **2025-02-12 - earnings/outlook:** fiscal-2024 revenue reached $1.28B (+32.6%), 151 shops opened, full-year company-shop gross margin reached 22.3%, contribution margin reached 29.7%, and 2025 adjusted-EBITDA guidance was $265M-$275M ([Dutch Bros, published 2025-02-12](https://investors.dutchbros.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx/1000/)).
- **2025-05-07 - earnings:** Q1 revenue reached $355.2M (+29.1%), system transactions rose 1.3%, company-operated transactions rose 3.7%, and 30 shops opened; company-shop contribution was $96.1M at a 29.4% margin ([Dutch Bros, published 2025-05-07](https://investors.dutchbros.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx/1000/)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior - present.** BROS remained below its November-2021 first public close through 2024-11-06 despite rapid unit growth. The missing proof had been whether transactions, shop economics, and overhead leverage could support that expansion.
- **Catalyst ladder - present.** Q3 supplied transaction growth and a guide-up; Q4 showed full-year margin expansion; Q1 2025 preserved transaction and unit momentum.
- **Relative breakaway - present.** Excess return versus XLY widened from +9.54pp at day 21 to +54.90pp at day 126.
- **Liquidity confirmation - present.** The Q3 and Q4 reactions were the two largest dollar-volume sessions and both held at 3/5/10 days.
- **Options convexity - unknown.** No historical trade-level archive supports a claim about opening call demand, put sales, overwriting, or hedging.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The t0 report showed both strength and an unresolved margin issue. Q3 revenue rose 27.9% to $338.2M, net income rose to $21.7M from $13.4M, and adjusted EBITDA rose 20.3% to $63.8M. Company-operated transactions rose 2.4%, and adjusted SG&A fell to 14.9% of revenue from 15.2%. Yet company-shop gross margin declined 190 basis points to 22.2% and contribution margin declined 150 basis points to 29.5%, including pre-opening costs ([Dutch Bros, published 2024-11-06](https://investors.dutchbros.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx/1000/)). The breakaway therefore priced a forward operating inflection, not uniformly perfect contemporaneous margins.

Full-year results supplied the missing bridge. Revenue rose 32.6% to $1.28B; 151 shops opened; company-shop gross profit rose to $260.0M from $180.2M; gross margin improved 130 basis points to 22.3%; and company-shop contribution rose 43.1% to $346.8M with a 150-basis-point margin gain to 29.7%. System same-shop sales grew 5.3%, while transactions were nearly flat at -0.1%; the better company-operated cohort posted +1.5% transaction growth ([Dutch Bros, published 2025-02-12](https://investors.dutchbros.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx/1000/)). This is why the case is an inflection rather than a simple consumption boom: the system still needed traffic tools to make growth less price-dependent.

By Q1 2025, revenue growth remained 29.1%, system and company-operated transactions were positive, 30 new shops had opened, and company-shop contribution grew 29.8% to $96.1M. Gross and contribution margins were roughly flat to slightly lower year over year as pre-opening costs and coffee inflation persisted ([Dutch Bros, published 2025-05-07](https://investors.dutchbros.com/financials/quarterly-results/default.aspx/1000/)). Coverage gap: aggregate reports do not expose full vintage-level returns for every shop, so new-unit productivity is inferred from system openings, management disclosures, same-shop activity, and segment margins rather than a complete cohort ledger.

## 6. Options context

Episode options convexity is **unknown**. The repo does not contain BROS trade-level options history for the episode, and current chain data cannot be back-projected. Large earnings gaps can create both speculative call buying and hedging demand, but neither can be signed without trades and quotes. This case rests on cash-market persistence and public operating results.

## 7. Research echo

Models chased the Q3 repricing. On 2024-11-07, Wedbush moved to $55 from $45 and JPMorgan to $48 from $44 ([Benzinga, published 2024-11-07](https://www.benzinga.com/news/24/11/41820151/dutch-bros-stock-hits-52-week-highs-whats-going-on)); BROS closed at $44.77, leaving the JPMorgan model only modestly above spot. UBS then moved to $65 from $44 on 2024-12-06 as the stock traded near $54, citing new-unit productivity, mobile order, same-shop drivers, and margin potential ([Investing.com, published 2024-12-06](https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/ubs-maintains-buy-rating-on-dutch-bros-sees-strong-upside-potential-from-sales-drivers-93CH-3759281)). After Q1, Piper Sandler cut its target to $63 from $70 on 2025-05-08 while retaining a neutral stance and flagging softer near-term same-shop guidance ([Investing.com, published 2025-05-08](https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/piper-sandler-cuts-dutch-bros-price-target-to-63-from-70-93CH-4032400)); BROS closed at $64.45 that day. The sequence is useful precisely because it includes both catch-up and caution: published targets repeatedly converged on a cash price that had already moved.

## 8. Ownership context (context-only)

**Context-only; descriptive background, explicitly not a signal.** The local `data/quiver/sec13f.parquet` extract does not provide a complete BROS holder panel across the episode, so breadth change is **unknown**. Form 13F can become public up to 45 days after quarter-end ([SEC FAQ, dated 2023-05-24 and updated 2026-03-06](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/division-investment-management-frequently-asked-questions/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-13f)). Local insider records are also incomplete and would need filing-date reconstruction; Form 4 is generally due within two business days, but filing and trade dates are distinct ([SEC ownership-report rule release, published 2002-08-27](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2002/08/ownership-reports-trading-officers-directors-principal-security-holders)). Neither ownership lane is used positively.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze - ruled_out.** The move was anchored to two major earnings gaps, repeat transaction evidence, unit expansion, and full-year margin improvement.
- **one_day_binary - ruled_out.** Both the Q3 and Q4 gaps remained far above their pre-gap closes after 3/5/10 sessions.
- **sector_beta - ruled_out.** BROS produced +54.90pp excess versus XLY at 126 days.
- **options_mirage - ruled_out.** Historical options direction is unknown and not used in the thesis.

Current hazards were `extended_after_vertical_move`, `premium_multiple_duration`, `coffee_and_labor_inflation`, `new_unit_cannibalization`, `site_selection_execution`, `transaction_slowdown`, `mobile_order_operational_complexity`, `franchise_company_store_divergence`, and `expectations_gap`. The failed 2025-03-24 gap and May target cut show that a durable winner can still contain short-lived breakaways and softer forward bars.
