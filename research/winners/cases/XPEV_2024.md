# XPEV 2024 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

XPeng's late-2024 breakaway was a product-cycle and unit-economics rerating, not simply a rebound in China ADRs. The low-priced MONA M03 opened a much larger volume pool, monthly delivery reports showed that demand converting into records, the P7+ supplied a second model rather than a one-product spike, and vehicle margin improved as volume and cost reductions flowed through. Public releases progressed from 14,036 August deliveries to 21,352 in September, 30,895 in November and 36,695 in December ([August report, published 2024-09-01](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/zh-hans/news-releases/news-release-details/xpeng-announces-vehicle-delivery-results-august-2024); [September report, published 2024-10-01](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/node/9211/pdf); [November report, published 2024-12-01](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/news-releases/news-release-details/xpeng-announces-vehicle-delivery-results-november-2024); [December report, published 2025-01-01](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/news-releases/news-release-details/xpeng-announces-vehicle-delivery-results-december-and-fourth-2)). The market began the relative breakaway on 2024-09-19 before all those confirmations arrived; their sequential arrival is precisely what sustained the episode. XPEV was still down for calendar 2024, so the case is about a durable inflection from a damaged prior, not a full-year momentum winner.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2024-08-20 — earnings:** Q2 revenue was RMB8.11B, gross margin 14.0%, vehicle margin 6.4% and cash resources RMB37.33B ([company release, published 2024-08-20](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/zh-hant/news-releases/news-release-details/xpeng-reports-second-quarter-2024-unaudited-financial-results)).
- **2024-08-27 — product launch:** XPeng publicly launched MONA M03 after announcing the event on 2024-08-20 ([event page, published 2024-08-27](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/events/event-details/xpeng-10th-anniversary-gala-night-mona-m03-launch-event)).
- **2024-10-01 — delivery report:** September deliveries reached a record 21,352, up 39% year over year, and first-month MONA M03 deliveries exceeded 10,000 ([company report, published 2024-10-01](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/node/9211/pdf)).
- **2024-11-01 — delivery report:** October deliveries reached 23,917, another monthly record, with MONA above 10,000 for a second month ([company release, published 2024-11-01](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/news-releases/news-release-details/xpeng-announces-vehicle-delivery-results-october-2024)).
- **2024-11-07 — product launch:** XPeng launched the P7+ and began a second model ramp ([company event, published 2024-11-07](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/events/event-details/xpeng-p7-launch-event)).
- **2024-11-19 — earnings:** Q3 revenue rose to RMB10.10B, gross margin to 15.3% and vehicle margin to 8.6%, though the net loss remained RMB1.81B ([company release, published 2024-11-19](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/zh-hans/news-releases/news-release-details/xpeng-reports-third-quarter-2024-unaudited-financial-results)).
- **2025-01-01 — delivery report:** December deliveries were 36,695, up 82% year over year; Q4 deliveries of 91,507 exceeded the upper guidance bound ([company release, published 2025-01-01](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/news-releases/news-release-details/xpeng-announces-vehicle-delivery-results-december-and-fourth-2)).
- **2025-03-18 — earnings:** FY2024 revenue was RMB40.87B, up 33.2%, full-year gross margin reached 14.3%, and year-end cash resources were RMB41.96B ([company release, published 2025-03-18](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/news-releases/news-release-details/xpeng-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2024-unaudited)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** XPEV entered September below its 2024 opening price after an extended EV price-war and loss-making prior; calendar-year performance remained negative even after the first leg (`data/yahoo/XPEV.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** MONA launch, monthly delivery records, P7+ launch and successive margin disclosures converted a forecast into observed production and economics.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** Excess versus DRIV was +35.51pp at 42 sessions and +126.96pp at 126 sessions; SPY comparison was nearly identical.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** The P7+ day and the March results/product cluster printed $0.48B-$0.77B sessions, while several major gaps retained their pre-gap levels.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2024-25 trade-level historical options dataset is available locally; cash ADR volume cannot identify net option demand.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Before t0, Q2 2024 revenue was RMB8.11B, gross profit RMB1.14B, gross margin 14.0%, vehicle margin 6.4%, net loss RMB1.28B and cash resources RMB37.33B ([company release, published 2024-08-20](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/zh-hant/news-releases/news-release-details/xpeng-reports-second-quarter-2024-unaudited-financial-results)). During the run, Q3 revenue increased to RMB10.10B, gross profit to RMB1.54B, gross margin to 15.3% and vehicle margin to 8.6%; net loss widened sequentially to RMB1.81B and cash resources fell to RMB35.75B ([company release, published 2024-11-19](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/zh-hans/news-releases/news-release-details/xpeng-reports-third-quarter-2024-unaudited-financial-results)). That mixed quarter is important: the rerating depended on product scale and margin direction, not current net profitability.

By Q4, revenue reached RMB16.11B, vehicle margin 10.0%, net loss narrowed to RMB1.33B and cash resources recovered to RMB41.96B; full-year deliveries were 190,068 and revenue RMB40.87B ([company release, published 2025-03-18](https://ir.xiaopeng.com/news-releases/news-release-details/xpeng-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2024-unaudited)). Cash runway was therefore substantial relative to quarterly loss, but China EV price competition and continued R&D spending remained real. Coverage gap: U.S. ADR filings do not map cleanly into the repo's normalized quarterly SEC panel, so the company 6-K/20-F releases are the numerical source.

## 6. Options context

Episode-timely options evidence is **unknown**. This checkout does not contain reconstructable 2024-25 XPEV option trades with contemporaneous NBBO, nor historical open-interest surfaces sufficient to infer dealer positioning. The study therefore makes no signed-flow claim. ADR volatility and large gaps make options amplification possible, but that remains a hazard rather than a mechanism finding.

## 7. Research echo

The echo lagged the tape. On 2025-03-19, after the FY2024 release, Bernstein raised its target from $14 to $18 while retaining a neutral stance, citing stronger delivery expectations ([Investing.com report, published 2025-03-19](https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/bernstein-raises-xpeng-stock-price-target-to-18-93CH-3936406)). XPEV closed that day at $23.80 (`data/yahoo/XPEV.parquet`), 32% above the new target; a separate report said Barclays lifted its target from $7 to $20 after the same results ([Investing.com report, published 2025-03-20](https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/barclays-lifts-xpeng-stock-price-target-to-20-from-7-3938821)). Full PIT consensus is unavailable locally, but both revisions remained behind spot, a direct published-model escape.

## 8. Ownership context (context-only)

**Context-only — descriptive background, explicitly not a signal.** Local `data/quiver/sec13f.parquet` and `sec13f_changes.parquet` contain XPEV records, but their `_first_seen` dates begin in 2026 and cannot reconstruct 2024 knowledge. Foreign-issuer and ADR ownership also complicate holder breadth. Under the local winner-library PIT rule, 13F material is usable only from its later filing/publication date. Episode-timely holder changes and insider transactions are therefore unknown.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible:** a volatile China ADR can attract speculative positioning, but repeated vehicle deliveries and margin steps provide a non-meme core.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out:** records accumulated from September through March across several independent publications.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out:** XPEV exceeded DRIV by +126.96pp and SPY by +127.25pp at 126 sessions.
- **options_mirage — possible:** historical derivatives evidence is absent, so convexity amplification cannot be excluded.
- **Current hazards:** `china_price_war`, `continued_net_losses`, `adr_geopolitical_risk`, `model_launch_execution`, `extended_after_vertical_move`, `gap_failure_after_march_peak`.
