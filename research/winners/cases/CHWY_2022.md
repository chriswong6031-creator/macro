# CHWY 2022 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

CHWY's 2022 alpha burst was a rebound from a severely compressed, heavily shorted
post-pandemic prior, sparked by one better-than-feared quarter and then amplified by an
analyst upgrade and a broad growth-stock rebound—not a durable re-underwriting ladder.
The June 1 release restored belief that double-digit sales growth and modest profitability
could coexist, but its own evidence still showed lower year-over-year EBITDA and margin;
by July 8 the stock had gained 54.19% in 21 sessions and beaten XLY by 59.02 percentage
points, yet remained down 23.23% YTD, had made only a 63-day high, and lacked persistent
60-day liquidity expansion. Price then escaped the near-term published model, Wedbush
downgraded it on customer-growth/valuation risk, and the August quarter supplied the
failure mechanism: better margins but weaker sales expectations. The breakaway therefore
failed because capital had repriced survival and near-term execution much faster than the
evidence could support a lasting growth-duration thesis.

**Format-contract classification: this is a FAILED breakaway case
(`case_type: failed_breakaway`); the run did not sustain.**

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2022-03-29 (filing/publication) — earnings, negative prior reset.** The FY2021
  earnings 8-K was filed at 20:04:29Z; the next-day report described a wider-than-expected
  loss, revenue miss, labor-cost pressure, shrinking margins, and ten target cuts. This
  deepened the compressed prior from which the later rebound launched.
  [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1766502/000176650222000008/chwy-20220329.htm)
  (2022-03-29);
  [contemporaneous report](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2022/03/30/chewy-stock-plummets-after-dismal-quarterly-report)
  (2022-03-30). Filing anchor:
  `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`.
- **2022-06-01 (filing/publication) — earnings, primary positive rung.** Fiscal Q1 net
  sales grew 13.7% to $2.43bn and GAAP net income was $18.5m, but gross margin slipped
  10bp, adjusted EBITDA fell 21.8%, and adjusted EBITDA margin fell 110bp. The mosaic
  read was “better than feared and sequentially improving,” not a clean acceleration.
  [Chewy release](https://investor.chewy.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2022/Chewy-Announces-First-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results/default.aspx)
  (2022-06-01);
  [SEC exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1766502/000176650222000016/chwyq12022exhibit992.htm)
  (filed 2022-06-01). Filing anchor:
  `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`.
- **2022-06-21 (publication) — research echo, secondary rung.** Wedbush upgraded CHWY
  from Neutral to Outperform and raised its target from $30 to $35, citing spending and
  retention; the shares were reported up 13.3% intraday. The same report put short
  interest at 23.8% of available float, making squeeze amplification plausible.
  [Schaeffer's](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2022/06/21/chewy-stock-gets-boost-after-analyst-upgrade)
  (2022-06-21).
- **2022-07-26 (publication) — adverse research rung.** After price outran the June
  target, Wedbush reversed to Neutral. It raised the target to $44 but saw limited upside,
  forecast only 2% FY22 active-customer growth versus 4% consensus, and cited downside
  after the run.
  [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/chewy-stumbles-following-wedbush-downgrade-432SI-2854357)
  (2022-07-26).
- **2022-08-30 (filing/publication) — earnings, failure reveal.** Fiscal Q2 sales grew
  12.8%, gross margin expanded 60bp, and adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 230bp; however,
  the subsequent selloff reflected weaker revenue guidance. The combination showed why
  the move could not compound: cost execution improved while the customer/revenue
  duration premise weakened.
  [Chewy release](https://investor.chewy.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2022/Chewy-Announces-Second-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results/default.aspx)
  (2022-08-30);
  [contemporaneous guidance report](https://investorplace.com/2022/08/chewy-chwy-stock-falls-5-as-revenue-outlook-disappoints/)
  (2022-08-31). Filing anchor:
  `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** Before the rebound CHWY had fallen to $23.49 on June 1,
  was down sharply YTD, and the March earnings reset had produced ten target cuts.
- **Catalyst ladder — weak/present.** Q1 earnings and one upgrade supplied two public
  rungs, but only the earnings release changed company information; there was no product,
  strategic, or recurring operating ladder.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At July 8, excess return was +59.02pp/21d and
  +68.29pp/42d versus XLY, with the first 63-day closing high of 2022.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present but concentrated.** The earnings response printed
  $1.43bn of dollar volume, but July 8 was only `z60 = 0.88` and 5d/60d dollar volume only
  `1.20×`; participation did not keep broadening.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2022 per-ticker history exists. Contemporaneous
  reporting showed high short interest and bearish put/call volume, but that cannot
  establish option-driven convexity or signed flow.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

PIT rule: only observations whose `asof_date`, `as_of`, or `filed` timestamp was public by
the date discussed are used. `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet` shows the pre-run
fiscal Q3 2021 quarter (filed 2021-12-09) at $2.212bn revenue, 26.44% gross margin,
-1.44% operating margin, and -1.46% net margin. Fiscal Q1 2022 (filed 2022-06-01) moved
to $2.428bn revenue, 27.50% gross margin, +0.77% operating margin, +0.76% net margin,
$82.4m CFO, and $604.8m cash. The public release added the crucial qualifier: revenue
grew 13.7% YoY, while adjusted EBITDA and its margin fell 21.8% and 110bp YoY. Thus the
runway improved from “loss and compression” to “growth with thin profitability,” not to
durable margin expansion.

During the run, fiscal Q2 (filed 2022-08-30) held revenue nearly flat sequentially at
$2.431bn, lifted gross margin to 28.09%, operating margin to 0.89%, and net margin to
0.92%. That margin progress was genuine, but 12.8% YoY sales growth and weaker forward
sales expectations failed to support the valuation reset. Fiscal Q3 (filed 2022-12-08)
revenue rose to $2.532bn and gross margin to 28.44%, while operating margin returned to
-0.02%.

Annual `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet` confirms that FY2021, public by
2022-05-30, had roughly $8.97bn revenue, $2.39bn gross profit, a $73.6m operating loss,
and a $75.2m net loss. `data/edgar/statements.parquet` contains annual CHWY data, but its
`as_of` is a 2026 ingestion timestamp, so it is not used as contemporaneous PIT evidence.
`data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` has no CHWY rows: quarterly EPS coverage is **null**.
The annual panel and statements disagree modestly on FY2021 revenue, another reason to
anchor the episode to filed quarterly rows rather than blend sources.

## 7. Research echo

On 2022-06-21, published coverage was almost evenly split—nine favorable ratings and
eight neutral ratings—with a $43.76 consensus target while spot was reported at $32.52. Wedbush's upgrade
to a $35 target helped price the recovery, but CHWY closed at $44.13 by July 8: price had
escaped the newest published model. The revealing follow-up came July 26, when Wedbush
raised its target to $44 but downgraded the shares with the prior close at $41.75, citing
limited upside and customer-growth risk. This was research catching up numerically while
becoming less constructive in substance.
[Schaeffer's](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2022/06/21/chewy-stock-gets-boost-after-analyst-upgrade)

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** The move had real earnings evidence, but reported short
  interest was 23.8% of float and the follow-on June 21 jump coincided with an upgrade;
  forced covering could have amplified a thin ladder.
- **one_day_binary — likely.** The June 2 session contributed the only $1bn-plus positive
  dollar-volume event and followed the sole company-information catalyst before `t0`.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** CHWY beat XLY by 59.02pp/21d and SPY by 60.30pp/21d.
  Sector beta cannot explain the magnitude, though a growth-factor rebound likely helped.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Contemporaneous put/call and short-interest observations
  suggest positioning fuel, but historical GEX/IV/OI and trade-level NBBO are unavailable.

Episode hazards were `extended_after_vertical_move`, `catalyst_exhausted`,
`customer_growth_deceleration`, and `liquidity_confirmation_not_persistent`. Current
extension is **unknown** for purposes of this historical pull; current call walls are
**null** because no CHWY snapshot exists; current catalyst exhaustion is **unknown**
because the requested evidence window is 2022 and no current catalyst census was
performed.
