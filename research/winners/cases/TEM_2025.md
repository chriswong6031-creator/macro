# TEM 2025 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

TEM's first 2025 breakaway was a real operating story wrapped in a reflexive price event: a post-IPO drawdown created the spring, the FDA-approved xT launch and Ambry acquisition expanded the addressable platform, and later contracts showed that the data asset had commercial value, but the stock reached $83.75 after a 163.1% 21-session rise before hard financial evidence could support that speed ([xT launch, published 2025-01-15](https://investors.tempus.com/news-releases/news-release-details/tempus-announces-national-launch-fda-approved-xt-cdx-test); [Ambry completion, published 2025-02-03](https://investors.tempus.com/news-releases/news-release-details/tempus-completes-acquisition-ambry-genetics)). The ignition mixed company news with a public congressional disclosure of call purchases and broad AI attention ([U.S. House filing, filed 2025-01-17](https://disclosures-clerk.house.gov/public_disc/ptr-pdfs/2025/20026590.pdf); [William Blair valuation report, published 2025-02-12](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/tempus-ai-stock-downgraded-william-150631468.html)); by February 13 the IFLI collaboration carried no disclosed economics, the best reviewed published target of $74 was already below spot, and the next full results would guide to only $5M of adjusted EBITDA on $1.24B of acquisition-inflated revenue ([IFLI release, published 2025-02-13](https://investors.tempus.com/news-releases/news-release-details/tempus-announces-collaboration-ifli-aimed-supporting-development/); [TD Cowen report, published 2025-02-05](https://m.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/tempus-ai-stock-rating-upgraded-pt-raised-to-74-by-td-cowen-93CH-3850538?ampMode=1); [FY24 results, published 2025-02-24](https://investors.tempus.com/news-releases/news-release-details/tempus-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2024-results)). The February 13 gap held for three sessions but failed by day five, TEM lost 40.5% over the next 21 sessions, and the largest later dollar-volume day was a 19.2% decline on a short-positioned report ([Spruce Point report, published 2025-05-28](https://www.sprucepointcap.com/research/tempus-ai-inc)). The mosaic says the platform thesis survived, but the first price discovery did not: narrative velocity, valuation escape, acquisition financing, cash burn, and delayed proof distinguished this episode from a durable winner.

This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** TEM was a recent IPO and closed at $31.83 on 2025-01-14, 59.0% below its 2024-11-11 closing high. The setup was compressed, though still highly volatile rather than orderly (`data/yahoo/TEM.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present, but economically back-loaded.** xT CDx, Olivia, Ambry, IFLI, and repeated collaborations gave the market many reasons to retell the platform story. Before the break, only Ambry materially changed the financial base; the $200M AstraZeneca/Pathos contract arrived after the break.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed.** The +158.39pp 21-session excess versus SPY into t0 became -33.47pp over the next 21 sessions, -22.38pp by 63, and -18.06pp by 126 (`data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`).
- **Liquidity confirmation — present initially, then distributive.** T0 dollar volume was $2.449B at z21 2.25 and z60 3.49, but the peak-day gap failed and the window's largest dollar-volume session was the May 28 decline.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** The public congressional call disclosure plausibly increased attention, but it is not options-chain history, signed flow, or evidence of dealer hedging. The repo has no TEM episode options history.
