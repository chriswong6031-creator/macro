# TEM 2025 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

TEM's early-2025 alpha was a reflexive platform re-rating, not one clean earnings surprise: improving core growth and gross margin, the addition of Ambry's profitable hereditary-testing franchise and data, a rapid sequence of AI/product announcements, and the public disclosure of Nancy Pelosi-linked call exposure gave investors several ways to extrapolate a much larger healthcare-data platform. The break visible in the tape began on January 21, after the [Olivia launch published that day](https://www.tempus.com/news/pr/tempus-announces-national-launch-of-olivia-its-ai-enabled-personal-health-concierge-app-for-patients/) and a [public report published January 20 about the call disclosure](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/tempus-ai-shares-set-to-rally-as-pelosi-buys-calls-3820876); by the mechanical February 13 trigger, TEM had already risen 138.3% from its January 17 close and traded above the available analyst target framework. The business did continue to grow, but the price had capitalized that improvement before the [February 24 full-year release](https://investors.tempus.com/news-releases/news-release-details/tempus-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2024-results) exposed the still-negative adjusted EBITDA and cash-flow base, while the Ambry purchase had consumed cash and added expensive secured debt. Liquidity then changed character: the February 13 gap held only three sessions, the February 14 gap held none, and several of the largest subsequent dollar-volume sessions were declines. Thus the failure was not a collapse in the platform story; it was a late mechanical signal after an attention-amplified vertical move, followed by insufficient new economic surprise to sustain a price that had escaped the published model.

**Case classification: failed_breakaway. The run did not sustain; this study focuses on why TEM differed from a durable winner.**
