# TMDX 2021 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

TMDX's 2021 alpha burst was a regulatory-expectation and research-multiple rerating laid over a real but not yet commercial inflection: the mechanical onset arrived on 2021-03-02 after the company scheduled an OCS Heart FDA panel, with trailing excess returns of 75.34pp versus XLV over 21 sessions and 109.47pp over 42, while research targets were being rebuilt around Heart and Liver approvals and a high-growth medtech multiple ([TransMedics, published 2021-03-01](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-announces-scheduling-ocs-heart-fda-advisory/); [Canaccord target report, published 2021-03-15](https://biotuesdays.com/2021/03/15/canaccord-ups-transmedics-pt-to-79-from-46/)). What killed durability was the mismatch between regulatory de-risking and the timing of monetization: the Heart panel was favorable but split 9-7 on safety, Q1 revenue still fell 6%, Q3 revenue fell 24% as Heart and Liver clinical-trial revenue stopped before commercial launches began, and the largest dollar-volume day was a -13.85% session before the panel ([Heart panel release, published 2021-04-06](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-announces-positive-fda-advisory-committee-vote-ocs/); [Q1 release, published 2021-05-04](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-reports-first-quarter-2021-financial-results/); [Q3 release, published 2021-11-09](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-reports-third-quarter-2021-financial-results)). The company ultimately obtained both approvals ([Heart, published 2021-09-07](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-receives-fda-approval-its-ocs-heart-system-enabling); [Liver, published 2021-09-29](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-receives-fda-approval-its-ocs-liver-system/)), but each approval gap failed its 10-session hold test; by 63 sessions TMDX had lost 34.69% and lagged XLV by 41.94pp. The technology thesis was not disproved—the 2021 tape had simply capitalized a later revenue curve too early.

This is a FAILED breakaway case (case_type: failed_breakaway).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present, but stale by t0.** Weekly closes sat roughly between $12 and $15 through much of October-November 2020, and 31 of the 63 sessions ending 2021-01-29 were below the 200-day average. By t0, however, TMDX had already risen 110.78% in 42 sessions (`data/baskets/ohlcv/TMDX.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present, but monetization lagged.** CMS policy, two FDA panels, two PMAs, and DCD data were real reinforcing events ([Heart approval, published 2021-09-07](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-receives-fda-approval-its-ocs-heart-system-enabling); [Liver approval, published 2021-09-29](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-receives-fda-approval-its-ocs-liver-system/)); Q1 and Q3 revenue showed that approval and commercial revenue were not synchronous.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed.** The detector recorded +75.34pp/+109.47pp trailing excess versus XLV at 21/42 sessions; forward excess became -34.70pp at 42 and -41.94pp at 63 (`data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`; `data/yahoo/{TMDX,XLV}.parquet`).
- **Liquidity confirmation — present initially, then distributive.** T0 met both volume tests, but the year's largest dollar-volume session was -13.85% and the positive Heart/Liver regulatory gaps repeatedly failed (`data/baskets/ohlcv/TMDX.parquet`).
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2021 TMDX options history, current TMDX snapshot, or trade-level NBBO evidence exists in the cited repo surfaces.

Near-term runway was therefore not the breakaway's primary failure; the problem was that the price and published models pulled forward a commercial ramp the 2021 income statement could not yet show.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** The unexplained 2021-02-09 +31.46% opening gap, 2.55M shares traded that day, and the later catalyst anticipation are consistent with speculative amplification (`data/baskets/ohlcv/TMDX.parquet`). Historical short-interest and retail-attention coverage are absent, so a squeeze cannot be established.
- **one_day_binary — possible.** Several discrete FDA events produced gaps, but the full move was a ladder spanning policy, research revisions, panels, and approvals rather than one decision. The repeated failure of later gaps is more diagnostic than any single session.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** TMDX led XLV by 75.34pp over the trailing 21 sessions at t0, then lagged it by 41.94pp over the next 63; broad Health Care beta cannot explain that reversal (`data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`).
- **options_mirage — possible.** The verticality could have been derivatives-amplified, but all episode options fields are null and no signed-flow inference is permitted.

Current-snapshot hazards are explicit nulls where coverage is absent. **Extension:** not currently present on price alone; the 2026-07-17 close of $73.98 was 50.82% below the trailing 252-session high (`data/yahoo/TMDX.parquet`). **Call walls:** null because all three TMDX options surfaces are missing. **Catalyst exhaustion:** null for the current company; for the 2021 episode it was likely by 2021-09-29 because the scheduled Heart and Liver approval ladder had completed while commercial proof still lagged. Other episode hazards are `extended_after_vertical_move`, `approval_to_revenue_latency`, `clinical_trial_revenue_cliff`, `liquidity_distribution`, `research_multiple_expansion`, and `operating_loss_expansion`.
