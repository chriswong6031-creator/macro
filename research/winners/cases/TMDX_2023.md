# TMDX 2023 — failed first breakaway after the NOP/aviation re-underwrite

## 1. Bottom line

TMDX's late-2023 alpha was not a single-product headline: capital re-underwrote a vertically integrated transplant platform after three successive guidance increases, 159% Q3 revenue growth, 93% Q2 U.S. OCS-case penetration by the National OCS Program (NOP), and the addition of dedicated air logistics. The November 6 earnings release made that mosaic legible, producing a 51.18% next-session close-to-close move and a 41.83% opening gap that held for 3/5/10 sessions. What prevented the first breakaway from becoming durable was the cost of making the model real: Q3 gross margin fell to 61%, aviation integration introduced execution risk, operating expense expanded, and aircraft investment consumed capital before the logistics network had demonstrated normalized economics. Research targets rose after the tape moved, but the stock failed the repo's 63-session relative-return and clean-hold tests before a separate spring-2024 re-underwrite.

**Format-contract classification: this is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`); the run did not sustain.** The failure is horizon-specific: forward excess versus XLV was -14.02 percentage points at 63 sessions, although a second advance lifted 126-session excess to +72.53 points (`data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`).

- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Three guidance increases, NOP penetration, the Summit agreement/close, and the Q3 guidance leap progressively changed the addressable model ([Q2 release, published 2023-08-03](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-reports-second-quarter-2023-financial-results); [Q3 release, published 2023-11-06](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-reports-third-quarter-2023-financial-results)).
