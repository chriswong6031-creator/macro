# EDU 2023 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

EDU's 2023 alpha was a turnaround rerating, not simply China beta or an e-commerce novelty: four successive public earnings releases moved the investable story from post-regulatory survival, to restored profitability, to demand-led growth, and finally to operating leverage at scale. The January quarter showed that drastic cost removal had nearly erased a catastrophic prior-year loss ([published 2023-01-17](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-second-fiscal-quarter-ended-11/)); April brought 22.8% growth and an 8.8% operating margin ([published 2023-04-19](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-third-fiscal-quarter-ended-13)); July showed 64.2% growth and a full-year swing from a $982.5 million operating loss to $190.0 million of income ([filed 2023-07-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1372920/000119312523194044/d509403dex991.htm)); and October delivered 47.7% growth, an 18.6% margin, $335.8 million of quarterly operating cash flow, and 38.4% deferred-revenue growth ([published 2023-10-25](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-first-fiscal-quarter-ended-9/)). Meanwhile EDU rose as KWEB fell, so the market was capitalizing an idiosyncratic earnings recovery with a large cash runway rather than merely lifting a China cohort. The move was sustained because each report supplied a higher-quality proof point—revenue, margins, bookings proxy, cash, and capacity expansion—although by December the stock had become vertically extended and an East Buy personnel controversy exposed how much narrative value had attached to the livestreaming asset ([published 2023-12-15](https://technode.com/2023/12/15/subsidiary-of-new-oriental-saw-stock-price-slide-nearly-20-after-livestream-dispute/)).

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2023-01-17 — Q2 FY2023 earnings:** revenue was still down 3.1%, but the operating loss narrowed to $2.5 million from $768.1 million, operating cash flow reached $173.7 million, deferred revenue grew 6.9%, and East Buy's half-year revenue rose 590.2%. This changed the question from solvency to earnings recovery. [New Oriental release, published 2023-01-17](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-second-fiscal-quarter-ended-11/).
- **2023-04-19 — Q3 FY2023 earnings:** revenue grew 22.8%, operating margin improved to 8.8% from -23.0%, deferred revenue grew 19.8%, and the company guided to 53%-57% Q4 revenue growth. [New Oriental release, published 2023-04-19](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-third-fiscal-quarter-ended-13).
- **2023-07-26 — Q4/FY2023 earnings and 6-K:** quarterly revenue grew 64.2% to $860.6 million; FY2023 operating income was $190.0 million versus a $982.5 million loss, with a 6.3% margin versus -31.6%. Management attributed the quarter to post-COVID demand recovery plus new initiatives and better-than-expected margins. [SEC exhibit, filed 2023-07-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1372920/000119312523194044/d509403dex991.htm); [PR Newswire release, published 2023-07-26](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-oriental-announces-results-for-the-fourth-fiscal-quarter-and-the-fiscal-year-ended-may-31-2023-301886145.html).
- **2023-10-25 — Q1 FY2024 earnings:** revenue grew 47.7% to $1.10 billion, above the company's prior range; operating income rose 163.0%, operating margin expanded 810 bp to 18.6%, new education initiatives grew 103.3%, deferred revenue rose 38.4%, and quarterly operating cash flow was $335.8 million. [New Oriental release, published 2023-10-25](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-first-fiscal-quarter-ended-9/).
- **2023-10-26 — UBS target revision:** UBS raised its target to $87 from $71 while reiterating its rating, an immediate sell-side model catch-up after the October result. [TipRanks analyst record, published 2023-10-26](https://www.tipranks.com/experts/analysts/felix-liu).
- **2023-11-07 — Morgan Stanley initiation:** Morgan Stanley initiated coverage with an $81 target and Overweight rating, broadening the published institutional model set after the breakaway. [Benzinga ratings history, published 2023-11-07](https://www.benzinga.com/quote/EDU/analyst-ratings/).
- **2023-11-24 — Goldman Sachs initiation:** Goldman Sachs initiated coverage with an $85 target, framing EDU as early in a multi-year scaling and profit-growth period. [MarketScreener/MT Newswires, published 2023-11-24](https://uk.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/NEW-ORIENTAL-EDUCATION-TE-32766/news/Goldman-Sachs-Initiates-New-Oriental-Education-Technology-at-Buy-Rating-With-85-Price-Target-45434245/).

The local filing-anchor files `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` and `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` contain no EDU rows, consistent with EDU being a foreign private issuer that furnished 6-Ks rather than domestic 8-Ks; the ladder therefore uses publication-dated releases and the July SEC exhibit.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present:** after reaching a closing high on 2023-07-28, EDU spent roughly two months below that close and then built a short shelf around $59-$63 before October earnings. This was compression within a larger recovery, not a long low-volatility base (`data/yahoo/EDU.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present:** January cost repair, April growth/guidance, July full-year profit inflection, and October operating leverage progressively reduced turnaround uncertainty; publication dates and links are above.
- **Relative breakaway — present:** at t0 EDU was +13.66 pp versus SPY over 21 sessions and about +27.9 pp over 42, while the KWEB thematic proxy was negative over both horizons (`data/yahoo/*.parquet`; supplied episode context).
- **Liquidity confirmation — present:** October 25 dollar volume reached $315.4 million and z60 5.34; by t0 the five-day/60-day ratio remained 1.75 even though t0 z21 had normalized to -0.35 (`data/yahoo/EDU.parquet`).
- **Options convexity — unknown:** no 2023 options history exists in the repository, and no inference is made from later snapshots.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The repo's PIT-gated fundamentals surfaces—`data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet` (`asof_date`), `data/edgar/statements.parquet` (`as_of`), `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet` (`as_of`), and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` (`asof_date`)—contain **zero EDU rows**, so local annual/quarterly fundamentals are null. This is a material foreign-issuer coverage gap, not evidence of absent fundamentals.

Publicly available releases fill the narrative, but not the repo panel. Before the run, Q2 FY2023 revenue was $638.2 million (-3.1%) and operating margin -0.4%, yet $173.7 million operating cash flow and $4.21 billion of cash, term deposits, and short-term investments showed runway ([published 2023-01-17](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-second-fiscal-quarter-ended-11/)). Q3 then reached $754.2 million revenue (+22.8%), 8.8% operating margin, and $190.5 million operating cash flow ([published 2023-04-19](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-third-fiscal-quarter-ended-13)). FY2023 revenue was still down 3.5% to $3.00 billion, but operating margin swung to 6.3% from -31.6% and operating income to $190.0 million from a $982.5 million loss ([filed 2023-07-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1372920/000119312523194044/d509403dex991.htm)). During the breakaway, Q1 FY2024 revenue reached $1.10 billion (+47.7%), operating margin 18.6%, cash flow $335.8 million, liquid resources about $4.57 billion, and deferred revenue $1.40 billion (+38.4%); the company guided the next quarter to 23%-26% growth ([published 2023-10-25](https://investor.neworiental.org/news-releases/news-release-details/new-oriental-announces-results-first-fiscal-quarter-ended-9/)). The arc was thus demand recovery plus fixed-cost leverage, with a cash balance large enough to fund renewed learning-center expansion.

## 7. Research echo

Consensus was chasing spot rather than leading it. EDU closed at $65.49 on t0 (`data/yahoo/EDU.parquet`); the next day UBS's newly raised $87 target still implied room, but it was a reaction to the October earnings rather than a pre-break signal ([published 2023-10-26](https://www.tipranks.com/experts/analysts/felix-liu)). Morgan Stanley's $81 initiation arrived after the stock had already broken away ([published 2023-11-07](https://www.benzinga.com/quote/EDU/analyst-ratings/)), and Goldman's $85 initiation arrived with spot at $75.40 on the same trading date, leaving only about 13% nominal upside ([published 2023-11-24](https://uk.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/NEW-ORIENTAL-EDUCATION-TE-32766/news/Goldman-Sachs-Initiates-New-Oriental-Education-Technology-at-Buy-Rating-With-85-Price-Target-45434245/); spot from `data/yahoo/EDU.parquet`). Price then reached $82.76 on December 12, nearly consuming Goldman's fresh target within 12 trading sessions. That is the "price escaped the published model" read: estimates were revised upward, but price discovery compressed their forecast horizon. The repository has no historical analyst-target surface, so this section is qualitative and externally sourced.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out:** four quarters of improving revenue/profit evidence and months of sequential highs fit a fundamental rerating better than a socially driven squeeze; PIT short-interest evidence is unavailable.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out:** the ladder began in January and the October earnings gap was followed by continued closing highs through December, rather than a one-session round trip.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out:** at t0 EDU had +33.02 pp 63-day excess versus KWEB and +28.04 pp versus SPY; KWEB itself was down 12.08% over that horizon.
- **options_mirage — ruled_out:** no historical options evidence is used in the thesis; 2023 options context is null.
- **Current/late-episode hazards:** `extended_after_vertical_move` became likely as price rose from $65.49 at t0 to $82.76 on December 12; `catalyst_exhausted` was possible after three post-earnings initiations/raises compressed upside to published targets; `call_wall_overhead` is unknown because EDU options snapshots are absent. The December 15 record dollar-volume down day coincided with reporting that an East Buy dispute had escalated, showing that narrative/personnel risk could abruptly reverse the extension ([TechNode, published 2023-12-15](https://technode.com/2023/12/15/subsidiary-of-new-oriental-saw-stock-price-slide-nearly-20-after-livestream-dispute/)).
