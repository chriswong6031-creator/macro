# ILMN 2014 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Illumina's 2014 alpha was a platform-and-market-expansion re-rating rather than a single product headline: FDA clearance of MiSeqDx had already opened a regulated-clinical lane, then the January 14 simultaneous launch of the $10 million HiSeq X Ten—making the fully loaded $1,000, 30x genome credible at population scale—and the immediately available $250,000 NextSeq 500 filled both the factory and desktop ends of the sequencing market; the January 15 Amgen companion-diagnostic agreement and January 16 market roadmap made the clinical pull-through less hypothetical. The tape broke away on this new architecture, but persistence came from conversion: Q4 2013 showed 25% revenue growth and cash generation, HiSeq X orders rapidly exceeded the original plan, Q1–Q3 2014 revenue grew 27%, 29%, and 35%, gross margin improved through the year, and management repeatedly lifted full-year growth/EPS guidance. In mosaic, capital was not merely paying for cheaper sequencing hardware; it was re-underwriting Illumina as the dominant picks-and-shovels platform across population genomics, ordinary research labs, and regulated diagnostics, with instruments seeding recurring consumables and public contracts/partnerships making the enlarged addressable market measurable.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2013-11-19 — regulatory decision.** FDA granted marketing authorization for the MiSeqDx platform, the first high-throughput genomic sequencer authorized for clinical use, creating a regulated base for later diagnostic partnerships. Publication/decision date: 2013-11-19. [FDA device record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/denovo.cfm?ID=DEN130011); [NIH/NHGRI contemporaneous account](https://www.genome.gov/27555431/2013-news-feature-fda-clears-nextgeneration-sequencer-for-routine-medical-care).
- **2014-01-14 — product launch.** Illumina introduced HiSeq X Ten, a ten-instrument, $10 million system capable of tens of thousands of 30x whole genomes annually at a fully loaded cost below $1,000; initial customers included Broad, Garvan, and Macrogen, and Q1 shipment was promised. Publication and 8-K filing date: 2014-01-14. [Illumina release](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2014/Illumina-Introduces-the-HiSeq-X-Ten-Sequencing-System/default.aspx); local anchor: `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`.
- **2014-01-14 — product launch.** Illumina simultaneously launched the immediately available $250,000 NextSeq 500, bringing high-throughput whole-genome capability and sub-day workflows to a desktop instrument. Publication date: 2014-01-14. [Illumina release](https://emea.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2014/1890697.html).
- **2014-01-15 — partnership.** Amgen and Illumina agreed to develop and commercialize a multi-gene companion diagnostic for Vectibix on the FDA-cleared MiSeqDx, linking the newly regulated platform to a marketed oncology therapy. Publication date: 2014-01-15. [Illumina release](https://www.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/press-release-details.html?newsid=89762cec-582a-4a9c-bb19-2c41c49190a3).
- **2014-01-16 — investor day/market roadmap.** At the mechanical t0, management described expansion into reproductive health, oncology, and other clinical markets and additional workflow products around the two new instruments. Publication date: 2014-01-16. [Illumina release](https://jp.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2014/1891655.html).
- **2014-01-28 — earnings/guidance.** Q4 revenue grew 25% to $387m, GAAP gross margin was 66.9%, and free cash flow was $100m; initial FY2014 guidance called for 15%–17% revenue growth and $2.00–$2.06 non-GAAP EPS. Publication and 8-K filing date: 2014-01-28. [Illumina release](https://emea.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2014/1894434.html); local anchor: `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`.

Publication and 8-K filing date: 2014-04-22. [Illumina release](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2014/Illumina-Reports-Strong-Start-to-Fiscal-Year-2014/default.aspx); local anchor: `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`.
- **2014-07-23 — earnings/guidance.** Q2 revenue grew 29% to $448m, GAAP gross margin reached 67.1%, and FY guidance rose again to 25%–26% revenue growth and $2.26–$2.28 non-GAAP EPS. Publication and 8-K filing date: 2014-07-23. [Illumina release](https://emea.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2014/1950453.html); local anchor: `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`.

[Illumina release](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2014/UK-to-Become-World-Number-One-in-DNA-Testing-with-Plan-to-RevolutioniseFight-Against-Cancer-and-Rare-Diseases/default.aspx).

Publication date: 2014-08-21. The quarterly local surface is useful but has a critical vintage limitation: `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet` contains 2013–14 periods and filing dates, but its common `as_of` is a 2026 extraction timestamp rather than a historical PIT-gate. Used as a reconstructed filing-aligned record—not as proof the repo held the data in 2014—it shows revenue of $331.0m, $346.1m, and $356.8m in 2013 Q1–Q3, with gross margin falling from 66.3% to 64.6% to 58.8% (Q3 included a non-core impairment). The contemporaneous January 28 release then reported Q4 revenue $387m (+25%), GAAP gross margin 66.9%, CFO $127m, and FCF $100m. Publication date: 2014-01-28. [Illumina Q4 release](https://emea.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2014/1894434.html).

During the run, reported revenue accelerated from $421m in Q1 (+27%) to $448m in Q2 (+29%) and $481m in Q3 (+35%). GAAP gross margin improved from 66.1% to 67.1% to 69.5%, operating income in the reconstructed quarterly surface rose from $92.1m to $100.2m to $119.5m, and cash/short-term investments reported by the company remained ample ($1.09bn after Q1 and $1.27bn after Q3). The model runway expanded in four public steps: initial 15%–17% FY revenue growth guidance on January 28, 21%–23% on April 22, 25%–26% on July 23, and about 30% on October 20; non-GAAP EPS guidance moved from $2.00–$2.06 to $2.63–$2.65. Publication dates and figures: [Q1, 2014-04-22](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2014/Illumina-Reports-Strong-Start-to-Fiscal-Year-2014/default.aspx), [Q2, 2014-07-23](https://emea.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2014/1950453.html), [Q3, 2014-10-20](https://emea.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2014/1979486.html).

Thus the cleanest episode-era fundamentals are the dated company releases and filing anchors, while the local quarterly statements provide a reconciliation check with a present-day extraction-vintage caveat.

## 7. Research echo

At launch, the published research echo was enthusiastic but the numeric consensus-vs-spot record is incomplete. Fierce Biotech reported on 2014-01-15 that Wells Fargo analyst Tim Evans titled his note “Not Just A Splash—A Cannonball” and expected HiSeq X Ten to secure Illumina's high-throughput dominance for an extended period; the article also recorded a greater-than-6% share-price response. Publication date: 2014-01-15. [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/data-management/illumina-hits-1-000-genome-mark-a-big-data-cannonball). A reliable contemporaneous January consensus target and full broker target ladder are **null** after web review, so this case does not reconstruct them from later aggregators.

The “price escaped the published model” read is strongest in the operating model. Initial January guidance contemplated 15%–17% 2014 revenue growth and $2.00–$2.06 non-GAAP EPS; by October those ranges had become approximately 30% and $2.63–$2.65. Meanwhile ILMN rose 55.14% from t0 to its November 3 high. The price initially escaped a model that did not yet capitalize adoption of the new platforms, then the public model caught up through successive resets. Dates: [2014-01-28 initial guidance](https://emea.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2014/1894434.html), [2014-04-22 revision](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2014/Illumina-Reports-Strong-Start-to-Fiscal-Year-2014/default.aspx), [2014-07-23 revision](https://emea.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2014/1950453.html), and [2014-10-20 revision](https://emea.illumina.com/company/news-center/press-releases/2014/1979486.html). Price source: `data/yahoo/ILMN.parquet`.

Later in the year, expectations again chased the tape: on 2014-10-21 Janney changed its rating and raised its target from $165 to $192, while six other firms raised targets and JMP went to $245, according to a contemporaneous report. Publication date: 2014-10-21. [Schaeffer's Research](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2014/10/21/analyst-upgrades-illumina-inc-synaptics-incorporated-and-grubhub-inc). This is research-echo context, not a repo-derived target series.
