# MU 2017 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

MU's 2017 alpha was principally a memory-cycle operating-leverage re-underwriting, not an unexplained momentum event and not merely Information Technology beta. Rising DRAM and NAND pricing met Micron's technology-node cost reductions, Inotera consolidation, and a richer server/cloud/SSD mix: reported quarterly revenue advanced from $3.97 billion to $6.14 billion while gross margin moved from 25.5% to 50.7% and operating margin from 9.0% to 40.8%. The 2017-09-26 fiscal-Q4 release converted that story into hard earnings, free cash flow, deleveraging, and another higher outlook; the next session's 6.09% opening gap, 8.51% close-to-close gain, simultaneous 63/126/252-day closing highs, and $3.14 billion of dollar volume supplied the tape confirmation. The mechanical detector fired on 2017-09-28, one session after that economic break. The result was durable through 126 sessions and reached a second high on 2018-05-29 as guidance rose again, but it did not sustain one-year alpha against XLK: the market began discounting shorter cycle duration before the reported business deteriorated. That distinction—correct earnings re-underwriting, followed by an early multiple/duration reversal—is the central autopsy finding. Fundamental figures are from `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet` and the [Micron fiscal-Q4 release, published 2017-09-26](https://investors.micron.com/node/35186); tape figures are from `data/yahoo/MU.parquet`, `data/yahoo/XLK.parquet`, and `data/yahoo/SPY.parquet`.

## 4. Stage anatomy

1. **Compressed prior — absent.** MU was already up 68.34% YTD at the mechanical detector date, traded above its 200-day average throughout the prior 63 sessions, and sat close to a one-year high. A summer drawdown existed, but the measured setup was a volatile continuation rather than a compressed shelf.
2. **Catalyst ladder — present.** Four successive earnings releases lifted reported revenue, margins, cash generation, and forward guidance; management change, Inotera integration, and deleveraging broadened the thesis beyond spot memory pricing. The 2017-09-26 release was the decisive public reset.
3. **Relative breakaway — present.** By 2017-09-28, MU led XLK by 19.12 points over 21 sessions and 31.93 points over 42; it led SPY by 17.83 and 33.04 points. All three new-high windows broke together on 2017-09-27.
4. **Liquidity confirmation — present.** The economic break carried $3.14B of dollar volume at +3.50/+5.32 21/60-day z-scores, and its 6.09% opening gap held through 3/5/10-session path tests. The detector-day five-session dollar-volume rate was 1.98× its 60-session baseline.
5. **Options convexity — unknown.** The repository has no per-ticker 2017 options history. Current-positioning files begin in June 2026, so there is no defensible evidence that options created or amplified the 2017 move.

### Economic interpretation

The earnings arc had three reinforcing components:

- **Price and industry supply:** DRAM average prices rose 5%, 21%, 14%, and 8% sequentially across fiscal Q1–Q4; Q4 NAND average price rose 5%. Management repeatedly described demand strength against limited supply growth. [Fiscal Q1 release, published 2016-12-21](https://investors.micron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-technology-inc-reports-results-first-quarter-fiscal-2017) [Fiscal Q2 release, published 2017-03-23](https://investors.micron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-technology-inc-reports-results-second-quarter-fiscal-2017) [Fiscal Q3 release, published 2017-06-29](https://investors.micron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-technology-inc-reports-results-third-quarter-fiscal-2017) [Fiscal Q4 release, published 2017-09-26](https://investors.micron.com/node/35186)
- **Cost and mix:** technology migrations and 3D NAND, plus server/cloud/SSD exposure and full Inotera consolidation, caused incremental revenue to convert at very high margins. Q4 prepared remarks said cloud-server memory content had risen about 50% and server/SSD revenue had more than tripled year over year. [Micron prepared remarks, published 2017-09-26](https://investors.micron.com/static-files/f4d25135-ee9a-4c18-8b3e-935a7479c6e1)
- **Runway and balance sheet:** fiscal-2017 free cash flow was about $3.3B, roughly $1.6B had gone to deleveraging, management targeted gross debt of $8B–$9B, and fiscal-2018 plans called for no new wafer capacity—an important supply-discipline claim. [Micron prepared remarks, published 2017-09-26](https://investors.micron.com/static-files/f4d25135-ee9a-4c18-8b3e-935a7479c6e1)

The later evidence separates business execution from tape duration. Fiscal 2018 ultimately produced $30.39B of revenue, 58.9% gross margin, $17.4B of operating cash flow, and $9.2B of free cash flow, while debt fell to $4.64B and the company ended in a net-cash position. [Micron release, published 2018-09-20](https://investors.micron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-technology-inc-reports-results-fourth-quarter-and-full-0) Thus, MU's one-year XLK-relative reversal was not caused by contemporaneous income-statement collapse; expectations and the valuation of cycle duration turned first.
