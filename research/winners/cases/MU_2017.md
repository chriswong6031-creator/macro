# MU 2017 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Micron's 2017 alpha was a memory-cycle earnings re-underwrite, not simply technology-sector beta or one earnings-day surprise: limited DRAM/NAND supply met improving demand and Micron's cost reductions, turning fiscal-2016 losses into rapidly compounding revenue, gross margin, and EPS; four consecutive public earnings releases progressively demonstrated that the upcycle was both larger and lasting longer than the market's old cyclical model allowed. A July–August reset compressed the tape even while the operating evidence improved, then September estimate/target revisions and the September 26 fiscal-Q4 release forced the price to catch up. MU gained 20.58% in the 21 sessions through the mechanical 2017-09-28 onset versus 1.46% for XLK and 2.75% for SPY, on nearly twice its normal dollar-volume run rate, then advanced another 30.87% to November 24. The move therefore produced large alpha because earnings power, margins, and cycle duration were repeatedly revised upward; it failed to hold the entire late-2017 vertical leg because the stock remained priced as a cyclical and finished December 17.19% below its November peak, though still 87.60% above its 2016 close.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2016-12-21 — earnings/guidance.** Fiscal-Q1 revenue grew 23% sequentially and 19% year over year; management tied the inflection to favorable demand, limited supply, and the newly closed Inotera acquisition. Publication: 2016-12-21, 4:05 p.m. EST. [Micron release](https://investors.micron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-technology-inc-reports-results-first-quarter-fiscal-2017).
- **2017-03-23 — earnings/guidance.** Fiscal-Q2 revenue rose 58% year over year to $4.65B; GAAP gross margin reached 36.7% from 19.7% a year earlier, with a 21% sequential increase in DRAM average selling prices and cost reductions in both NAND and DRAM. Publication: 2017-03-23, 4:04 p.m. EDT. [Micron release](https://investors.micron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-technology-inc-reports-results-second-quarter-fiscal-2017).
- **2017-04-27 — management change.** Micron appointed SanDisk co-founder and former CEO Sanjay Mehrotra as CEO, bringing a public record in flash storage, enterprise products, and NAND manufacturing partnerships. Publication: 2017-04-27, 8:00 a.m. EDT; the repo filing anchor is 2017-04-28, item 5.02, in `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet`. - **2017-06-29 — earnings/guidance.** Fiscal-Q3 revenue reached $5.57B, GAAP gross margin 46.9%, and EPS $1.40, versus 17.2% gross margin and a $0.21 loss a year earlier; prepared remarks described sustained healthy supply-demand dynamics. Publication/8-K filing: 2017-06-29 after close, anchored by `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` (accepted 20:01:25Z). Publication/8-K filing: 2017-09-26, 4:00 p.m. EDT, anchored by `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` (accepted 20:00:30Z) and `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` (item 7.01). ## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** MU corrected 15.42% from its 2017-06-07 adjusted closing high to its 2017-08-10 low and spent nearly three months below the June high (`data/yahoo/MU.parquet`), even as fiscal-Q3 results showed sharply higher margins and earnings.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Four successive earnings publications demonstrated the transition from loss to high profitability; the CEO transition and public target revisions added management and expectations rungs.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** On `t0`, MU had +19.12pp 21d and +31.93pp 42d excess return versus XLK, plus a post-reset 252d closing high from September 1.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** Inclusive dollar-volume z21 was 1.34, z60 was 2.45, and 5d/60d turnover was 1.98×; the post-earnings session was the largest dollar-volume day of the leg.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2017 options history exists. Per-ticker options history begins in 2026-06, so a 2017 convexity claim would be invented.

## 7. Research echo

Consensus was divided on cycle durability before the breakaway. On 2017-05-08, Goldman Sachs downgraded MU to Neutral and lowered its target to $30 from $32, arguing the DRAM upturn was already mid-to-late cycle ([Fox Business, published 2017-05-08](https://www.foxbusiness.com/markets/micron-technology-shares-slide-2-as-goldman-downgrades-to-neutral)). That was the stale model the tape escaped: MU closed at an unadjusted $37.96 on 2017-09-28 (`data/yahoo/MU.parquet`), above that target and above the $35 Credit Suisse target reported on March 22 ([Benzinga, published 2017-03-22](https://www.benzinga.com/analyst-ratings/analyst-color/17/03/9201419/credit-suisse-sees-40-upside-in-micron-shares)).

The revisions chased spot upward. Susquehanna moved to $50 from $40 on September 19 ([Schaeffer's, published 2017-09-19](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2017/09/19/analyst-eyes-new-record-highs-for-apple-stock)); after earnings, Credit Suisse and JPMorgan moved to $50 from $40 and Rosenblatt to $76 from $50 ([TheStreet, published 2017-09-27](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/micron-shares-could-double-14321752)). “Price escaped the published model” is therefore literal: spot first outran bearish/early-cycle targets, then the strongest targets were reset above spot as the margin and duration evidence accumulated. The repo contains no analyst-target dataset, so this section is qualitative external evidence only.

| sector_beta | ruled_out | MU exceeded XLK by 19.12pp over 21d and 31.93pp over 42d at `t0`, and exceeded SPY by 17.83pp and 33.04pp. |
| options_mirage | possible | Historical options data is absent; options cannot explain or disprove the 2017 episode. |

Episode hazards were **extension** and **catalyst exhaustion**: by November 24 the post-August move was 77.37%, and the stock subsequently retraced 17.19% into year-end as the next earnings catalyst approached. The core fundamental hazard was cycle mean reversion—limited supply and high ASPs could induce supply or fade with demand—which explains why published models remained contested even amid record margins.

Current hazards are non-comparable context only. The 2026-07-18 options snapshot shows IV30 above 100%, an overhead $900 magnet roughly 6% above spot, and assumed short-gamma conditions; that is consistent with extension/call-wall and options-mirage risk today, not in 2017. Catalyst exhaustion for the current company is **unknown** because this study did not inventory 2026 catalysts.
