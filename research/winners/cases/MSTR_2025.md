# MSTR 2025 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

MSTR's spring 2025 alpha was the equity premium on a financing flywheel, not a software re-rating and not merely Bitcoin beta: from the April 8 tariff-panic close to the May 8 mechanical `t0`, MSTR rose 74.15%, beating the conventional software-sector ETF IGV by 48.87 percentage points, SPY by 60.33 points, and the economically closer Bitcoin ETF IBIT by 41.96 points as Strategy resumed large Bitcoin acquisitions, added 8%–10% perpetual preferred layers, launched another $21 billion common-stock ATM, raised its Bitcoin-per-share KPI targets, and Bitcoin reclaimed $100,000. Yet the structure that created the excess also capped its durability. There was no compressed prior, only a roughly 50% drawdown with triple-digit realized volatility; `t0` cleared the 63-day high but not the 126- or 252-day ceiling; the `t0` gap failed its floor by session 10; and the stock closed below `t0` two sessions later. Even when Bitcoin reached a record in July, MSTR managed only 10.02% maximum post-`t0` upside before common/preferred issuance, fixed claims, and premium-to-asset-value compression dominated. The company itself disclosed $6.8 billion of Q2 financing proceeds, only 2.7% software revenue growth, and a $14.0 billion unrealized Bitcoin gain; later it permitted common issuance below 2.5x mNAV and funded a $1.44 billion dividend-and-interest reserve with common shares ([Q1 release, published 2025-05-01](https://www.strategy.com/press/strategy-announces-first-quarter-2025-financial-results_05-01-2025); [Bitcoin-$100,000 report, published 2025-05-08](https://www.investing.com/news/forex-news/bitcoin-tops-100000-on-trade-deal-optimism-4033755); [VanEck structural note, published 2025-05-22](https://www.vaneck.com/us/en/blogs/digital-assets/matthew-sigel-deconstructing-strategy-mstr-premium-leverage-and-capital-structure/); [Q2 release, published 2025-07-31](https://www.strategy.com/press/strategy-announces-second-quarter-2025-financial-results_07-31-2025); [equity-guidance filing, filed 2025-08-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000095017025109566/mstr-20250818.htm); [USD-reserve release, filed/published 2025-12-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312525303157/d69948dex991.htm)). The failure discriminator was therefore not lack of catalysts or liquidity; it was that repeated capital formation and Bitcoin appreciation no longer produced incremental common-equity alpha once the premium and incomplete high stack stopped expanding.

**FAILED BREAKAWAY:** this case is `case_type: failed_breakaway`; the run did not sustain, and the failure—not the temporary alpha—is the object of the study.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** On 2025-04-08 MSTR was 49.78% below its prior 252-day close high, but 21-day annualized realized volatility was 107.6% and the March 10–April 8 range was 43.65%. This was a violent damaged prior, not compression.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Authorized-share expansion, STRK/STRF/STRD/STRC, a zero-coupon convertible, serial Bitcoin acquisitions, Q1 KPI increases, and Bitcoin's $100,000/$123,000 milestones formed a dense ladder. The weakness is that `t0` itself had no hard MSTR filing and later rungs increasingly repeated financing-to-acquire rather than adding a new economic engine.
- **Relative breakaway — present but incomplete.** At `t0`, MSTR led IGV/SPY/IBIT by 48.87/60.33/41.96pp over 21 days, but cleared only the 63-day high. It did not make a 126-day high until July 10 and never made a 252-day high in 2025.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present but non-durable.** `t0` dollar volume was $9.173 billion with z21 1.76 and z60 2.13. Its gap held three and five sessions but not ten; later peak-phase gaps also failed, and large dollar volume appeared on May 23, July 18, and August 1 declines.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** Per-ticker listed-options history starts in June 2026. Corporate convertibles clearly embedded optionality, but they are not a substitute for trade-level MSTR option-flow history.

The five-stage sequence therefore broke in three places: there was no compressed prior, the high stack was incomplete, and liquidity did not defend the new level. Catalyst density alone was insufficient.
