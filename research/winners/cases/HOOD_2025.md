# HOOD 2025 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Robinhood's 2025 breakaway was a platform rerating with a later index-flow accelerator. The starting point was no longer a pandemic-era trading app: Q1 showed 50% revenue growth, record net deposits, rapid Gold adoption and material GAAP profit; the 2025-06-02 Bitstamp close added an international and institutional crypto exchange; the 2025-06-30 tokenization launch expanded the product narrative beyond transaction commissions; and Q2 confirmed 45% revenue growth while trading, deposits and subscriptions all remained strong ([Q1 release, published 2025-04-30](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-first-quarter-2025-results); [Bitstamp close, published 2025-06-02](https://investors.robinhood.com/static-files/4183f04d-6658-438d-9045-64ffa8e7402d); [tokenization release, published 2025-06-30](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-launches-stock-tokens-reveals-layer-2-blockchain-and-expands-crypto-suite-in-eu-and-us-with-perpetual-futures-and-staking/); [Q2 release, published 2025-07-30](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-second-quarter-2025-results)). S&P 500 inclusion, announced later on 2025-09-05, created forced passive demand but did not originate the run. The mechanism was an earnings-backed broadening of the franchise, with index flow as a second-stage liquidity event.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2025-04-30 — earnings/capital return:** Q1 revenue was $927M, up 50%; net income was $336M; net deposits reached $18.0B; Gold subscribers reached 3.2M; and the repurchase authorization increased to $1.5B ([company release, published 2025-04-30](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-first-quarter-2025-results)).
- **2025-06-02 — M&A activity:** Robinhood closed Bitstamp for about $200M cash; the acquired exchange had more than 50 active licenses/registrations and served retail and institutional customers ([company release, published 2025-06-02](https://investors.robinhood.com/static-files/4183f04d-6658-438d-9045-64ffa8e7402d)).
- **2025-06-30 — product launch:** Robinhood launched stock and ETF tokens in the EU, announced a planned Layer 2 blockchain and expanded crypto products ([company newsroom, published 2025-06-30](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-launches-stock-tokens-reveals-layer-2-blockchain-and-expands-crypto-suite-in-eu-and-us-with-perpetual-futures-and-staking/)).
- **2025-07-30 — earnings:** Q2 revenue was $989M, up 45%; net deposits were $13.8B; Gold subscribers reached 3.5M; and diluted EPS rose to $0.42 ([company release, published 2025-07-30](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-second-quarter-2025-results)).
- **2025-09-05 — index inclusion:** S&P Dow Jones Indices announced Robinhood would join the S&P 500 effective before the 2025-09-22 open ([S&P DJI release, published 2025-09-05](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/indexnews/announcements/20250905-1479730/1479730_sept2025-1500rebalance.pdf)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** HOOD had spent years below its 2021 IPO price, but the 2024 profit transition and Q1 2025 metrics had begun to dissolve that prior before t0 (`data/yahoo/HOOD.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Q1 profitability, Bitstamp, tokenization, Q2 execution and index inclusion supplied five distinct public rungs.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** Excess versus XLF reached +27.14pp at 21 sessions and +71.47pp at 126; SPY excess was +60.62pp at 126.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** Tokenization and index events produced five sessions near or above $9.5B of dollar volume, with the S&P announcement gap holding through ten sessions.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** The company reports customer options activity as a business metric, but that says nothing about signed trading in HOOD options. Episode-timely underlying option-flow evidence is absent.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Before t0, Q1 revenue reached $927M, transaction-based revenue $583M, net income $336M, adjusted EBITDA $470M, total platform assets $221B and cash $4.4B ([company release, published 2025-04-30](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-first-quarter-2025-results)). The locally normalized 10-Q row, filed 2025-05-01, matches $927M revenue, $336M net income and $4.416B cash (`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`). The pre-run financial prior was therefore profitable and liquid, not dependent on raising emergency capital.

During the run, Q2 revenue rose to $989M, net income to $386M, adjusted EBITDA to $549M, platform assets to $279B and Gold subscribers to 3.5M; cash remained $4.2B ([company release, published 2025-07-30](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-second-quarter-2025-results)). Growth was broad across equity, options and crypto activity, but the revenue mix remained exposed to market engagement and crypto cycles. Bitstamp added institutional reach and licenses, not an immediate margin miracle: the 2025-06-02 release expected near-term adjusted-EBITDA neutrality ([company release, published 2025-06-02](https://investors.robinhood.com/static-files/4183f04d-6658-438d-9045-64ffa8e7402d)). Coverage gap: gross profit is not a meaningful standardized line for this brokerage model in the local panel.

## 6. Options context

Historical HOOD-option direction is **unknown**. Customer options contracts traded on Robinhood rose 46% year over year in Q1 and 32% in Q2, but those are platform operating metrics, not trades in Robinhood's own shares ([Q1 release, published 2025-04-30](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-first-quarter-2025-results); [Q2 release, published 2025-07-30](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-second-quarter-2025-results)). This checkout lacks a 2025 trade-level HOOD options history with NBBO. No net-call-buying, dealer-gamma or call-wall claim is made.

## 7. Research echo

Research chased a rapidly moving spot. Barclays reportedly raised its target from $57 to $102 on 2025-07-10; HOOD closed at $98.70 that day (`data/yahoo/HOOD.parquet`; [contemporaneous report, published 2025-07-10](https://www.webull.com/news/13135580052677632)). After Q2, a Reuters item recorded Barclays lifting the target again from $102 to $120 on 2025-07-31, when HOOD closed at $103.05 ([Reuters-distributed item, published 2025-07-31](https://www.itiger.com/news/2555888332)). Full PIT consensus is unavailable locally. The sequence shows models being revised sharply upward while spot repeatedly approached the new numbers; it does not establish that the revisions drove the cash move.

## 8. Ownership context (context-only)

**Context-only — descriptive background, explicitly not a signal.** Local Quiver files contain HOOD holder-change and insider rows, but their `_first_seen` dates begin in 2026, after this episode. Under the local winner-library PIT rule, 13F information is usable only from its later filing/publication date. The September index event is known from S&P's dated public release and should not be conflated with lagged discretionary-holder filings. Episode-timely 13F breadth and insider context are therefore unknown.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible:** HOOD has retail-trading reflexivity and crypto sensitivity, but Q1/Q2 profits, deposits and product expansion provide a fundamental core.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out:** the run had distinct April, June, July and September rungs.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out:** +71.47pp 126-session excess versus XLF and +60.62pp versus SPY.
- **options_mirage — possible:** no underlying episode options tape is available, while the stock's reflexivity makes amplification plausible.
- **Current hazards:** `crypto_volume_cycle`, `transaction_revenue_reflexivity`, `regulatory_change`, `index_flow_exhausted`, `extended_after_vertical_move`, `integration_execution`.
