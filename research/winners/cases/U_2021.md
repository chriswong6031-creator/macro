# U 2021 — failed breakaway autopsy

This is a FAILED breakaway case (case_type: failed_breakaway).

## 1. Bottom line

Unity's November 2021 breakaway failed because a broad real-time-3D platform narrative outran the reliability of the advertising engine that funded it. Q3 revenue grew 43%, management raised guidance, and the Weta Digital agreement expanded the creation-platform vision ([Q3 release, published 2021-11-09](https://investors.unity.com/news/news-details/2021/Unity-Announces-Third-Quarter-2021-Financial-Results/); [Weta announcement, published 2021-11-09](https://investors.unity.com/news/news-details/2021/Unity-Announces-Intent-to-Acquire-Weta-Digital/default.aspx)). The stock initially printed a multi-window high and another week of upside, but the capital-intensive acquisition and a growth-stock multiple made the thesis dependent on continued Operate execution. On 2022-05-10 Unity disclosed monetization-product challenges, cut full-year guidance and showed a 53%-of-revenue GAAP operating loss; analyst reporting later identified targeting-model data and calculation problems ([Q1 release, published 2022-05-10](https://investors.unity.com/news/news-details/2022/Unity-Announces-First-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results/default.aspx); [Benzinga report, published 2022-05-11](https://www.benzinga.com/analyst-ratings/analyst-color/22/05/27138708/why-these-analysts-cut-unity-software-price-target-by-50)). The failure was operational: healthy Create adoption could not offset a damaged monetization engine quickly enough.

- **2022-02-03 — earnings/guidance:** FY2021 revenue reached $1.1B, up 44%, and management guided 2022 growth of 34%-36% ([company release, published 2022-02-03](https://investors.unity.com/news/news-details/2022/Unity-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — false.** U was already near an all-time high after a strong 2021 run; the setup was expansion from elevation, not compression (`data/yahoo/U.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — false after initial rungs.** Q3/Weta and FY2021 looked reinforcing, but the May monetization disclosure broke the operating bridge.
- **Relative breakaway — failed.** +9.95pp excess versus IGV at five sessions became -16.21pp at 21 and -42.72pp at 126.
- **Liquidity confirmation — failed.** Peak early volume marked the top, and the later $1.541B May session confirmed downside rather than support.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No episode-timely option trades or historical open-interest surface is available.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Q3 revenue was $286.3M, up 43%; Create revenue grew 34% to $83.7M and Operate revenue grew 54% to $185.0M ([company release, published 2021-11-09](https://investors.unity.com/news/news-details/2021/Unity-Announces-Third-Quarter-2021-Financial-Results/)). FY2021 revenue later reached $1.1B, but annual free cash flow was negative $153.4M ([company release, published 2022-02-03](https://investors.unity.com/news/news-details/2022/Unity-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). Weta expanded assets and technical scope but also increased integration and cash-allocation complexity.

Q1 2022 revenue reached $320.1M, up 36%, while GAAP operating loss widened to $171.2M, net expansion eased to 135%, and cash/restricted cash stood at $1.2B. Unity cut full-year revenue guidance to $1.35B-$1.425B because of monetization challenges ([company release, published 2022-05-10](https://investors.unity.com/news/news-details/2022/Unity-Announces-First-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). This was not immediate insolvency; it was an execution and unit-economics break inside a highly valued platform. Normalized local SEC quarterly coverage is absent, so company releases are the source.

## 7. Research echo

Analyst models broke with the operating disclosure. On 2022-05-11 Morgan Stanley reportedly cut its target from $110 to $50 and Piper Sandler from $145 to $55; both cited Operate/targeting issues ([Benzinga, published 2022-05-11](https://www.benzinga.com/analyst-ratings/analyst-color/22/05/27138708/why-these-analysts-cut-unity-software-price-target-by-50)). Daiwa reportedly cut its target from $110 to $34 ([Investing.com, published 2022-05-11](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/unity-software-shares-drop-22-on-weak-outlook-analyst-downgrades-to-neutral-432SI-2822394)). U closed at $30.30 that day (`data/yahoo/U.parquet`), below even the harshest new target. Full PIT consensus is unavailable; the relevant echo is an abrupt model reset after the tape had already fallen for months.

- **one_day_binary — possible:** Q3/Weta created the breakaway, but the thesis died through both ongoing derating and a later operational disclosure.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out:** U lagged IGV by -42.72pp and SPY by -65.73pp at 126 sessions.
- **options_mirage — possible:** no historical derivatives record exists to test convexity.
