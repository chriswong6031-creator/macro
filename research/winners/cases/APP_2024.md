# APP 2024 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

AppLovin's 2024 breakaway was an AI-adtech earnings-quality rerating, not just a software momentum chase. The company entered 2024 with improving advertising engine evidence, but the November 2024 quarter made the market re-underwrite AXON as a high-margin growth platform with operating leverage. The tape confirms a step-change: by 2024-12-06, APP had outperformed IGV by +125.58pp over 21 trading days, +334.81pp over 63 trading days, and +895.80pp YTD.
