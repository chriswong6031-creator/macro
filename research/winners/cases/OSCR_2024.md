# OSCR — 2024 Winner Autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

OSCR's 2024 alpha was a fundamental turnaround with a policy-duration overlay, not a generic health-sector move: a [2024-01-10 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1568651/000119312524005574/d581645d8k.htm) put 2024 adjusted-EBITDA profitability and roughly 1.3 million members into the public model, the [2024-02-07 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1568651/000156865124000004/oscr-4q23xpressrelease.htm) converted that aspiration into $125-$175 million of guidance, and the [2024-05-07 quarter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1568651/000156865124000013/oscr-1q24xpressrelease.htm) showed that membership growth, disciplined pricing, medical-cost control, and fixed-cost leverage could coexist. That combination changed the object being valued—from a serially loss-making insurtech into a scaled, profitable ACA insurer—and drove OSCR 96% from the mechanical trigger to its May peak. It did not fully sustain: the [2024-11-06 post-election ACA analysis](https://www.kff.org/quick-insights/what-trumps-2024-victory-means-for-the-affordable-care-act/) reintroduced subsidy duration, and the [2024-11-07 quarter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1568651/000156865124000029/oscr-3q24xpressrelease.htm) exposed higher Special Enrollment Period risk-adjustment transfers and medical costs. OSCR still finished 2024 up 46.9%, 44.4 percentage points ahead of XLV and 22.0 points ahead of SPY, but 42.3% below its May peak; the alpha was real, while the peak multiple assumed more durability than the year-end evidence supported.
