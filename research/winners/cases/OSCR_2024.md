# OSCR 2024 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

OSCR's 2024 alpha was a turnaround re-rating built from three reinforcing facts, not one headline: 2023 had already shown that disciplined pricing and administrative leverage could sharply narrow losses; January's enrollment evidence and tape action pulled the profitability inflection forward; then the February outlook and May quarter converted a fragile forecast into observable revenue growth, lower loss ratios, positive net income and adjusted EBITDA. The stock's 2024-01-25 detector onset was therefore late relative to the operating turn but early relative to the strongest earnings proof. Liquidity confirmed the May leg, yet the move did not remain vertical: OSCR peaked at $23.28 on 2024-05-21 and ended 2024 at $13.44, 42.3% below that peak. August still raised revenue and adjusted-EBITDA guidance, so the fade reads less like an immediate fundamental reversal than expectations and published valuation models catching up to a volatile, policy-sensitive insurer whose first-half profitability is seasonally stronger.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent at mechanical t0.** By 2024-01-25 OSCR had already gained 133.6% over 63 sessions and made its first 63/126/252-day high on 2024-01-08. Compression existed earlier in 2023, but the requested onset caught a developed trend.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** November's profitability commitment, February's quantified guide, May's earnings proof and August's raised guide repeatedly reduced uncertainty about whether growth and margins could coexist.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 OSCR led SPY by 26.48 pp over 21 days and 32.19 pp over 42 days; the XLV-proxy gaps were 26.96 pp and 33.24 pp.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, delayed.** T0's z21 was only about 0.58, but the five/60-day dollar-volume ratio was 1.52; the February and May result days later printed z60 above 5.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** Per-ticker history starts in 2026-06, so no 2024 convexity claim is supportable.

[Oscar Q1 release, published 2024-05-07](https://ir.hioscar.com/news-events-presentations/news-press-releases/news-details/2024/Oscar-Health-Announces-Results-for-First-Quarter-2024/default.aspx). Runway is **unknown from the requested repo stores**; inferring it from adjusted EBITDA alone would ignore statutory-capital and seasonality constraints.

## 7. Research echo

The echo lagged the tape, then chased it. OSCR was already $16.68 on 2024-02-08, versus $11.89 at t0, before Wells Fargo raised its target from $12 to $20 while retaining Overweight on 2024-02-15. [StreetInsider, published 2024-02-15](https://www.streetinsider.com/Analyst%2BPT%2BChange/Oscar%2BHealth%2BInc%2B%28OSCR%29%2BPT%2BRaised%2Bto%2B%2420%2Bat%2BWells%2BFargo/22785727.html). BofA then raised its target from $22 to $25 on 2024-05-20, citing greater confidence in exchange-market stability and utilization; OSCR closed at $23.28 the next day, essentially consuming most of the revised published upside immediately. [Investing.com, published 2024-05-20](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/oscar-health-share-price-target-raised-by-bofa-on-market-stability-93CH-3447618). ## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible:** the 63-day move was 133.6% and later high-volume legs reversed, but the multi-quarter earnings ladder and continuing revenue/margin improvement argue against a purely social squeeze. Float/short-interest history is unavailable.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out:** excess return accumulated across 21/42/63-day windows, with multiple new highs and separate February/May/August information events.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out:** OSCR beat XLV by 27.0 pp over 21 days and 33.2 pp over 42 days at t0; SPY-relative results were similar.
- **options_mirage — ruled_out for the causal account:** no 2024 options history exists, and the thesis does not use options to explain the move.

Current hazards are **extension/catalyst exhaustion possible** because the original profitability inflection is now historical, and **call-wall overhead possible** because the 2026-07-18 snapshot places a strong $30 call wall about 3.1% above $29.10 spot. These current options conditions must not be back-projected into 2024. The 2024 episode itself demonstrated extension risk: the year-end close was 42.3% below the May peak even though August raised guidance.
