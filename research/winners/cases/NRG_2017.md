# NRG 2017 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

NRG's 2017 alpha was a staged turnaround re-underwriting, not ordinary utilities beta and not a single earnings surprise. The tape began escaping its cohort before the January 17 activist filing: the market first priced a credible route to change when Elliott and Bluescape disclosed a combined 9.4% position and sought operational, financial, strategic, and board action; the February cooperation agreement then installed two directors and a board review committee. February results showed that the underlying enterprise still produced substantial adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow despite GAAP impairments, while June's GenOn Chapter 11 filing ring-fenced and deconsolidated a troubled subsidiary. The decisive July 12 step was a quantified Transformation Plan—recurring cost and margin improvement, major divestitures, and radical deleveraging—whose scale made the equity's prior enterprise-value discount newly actionable. The stock's 29.4% July 12 jump on approximately $1.13 billion of dollar volume, followed by higher closes, shows capital underwriting the plan rather than merely reacting to a headline. In short: activist-created governance credibility plus self-help, liability separation, and a measurable capital-allocation algorithm turned a levered merchant generator from an opaque asset collection into a simpler cash-flow and deleveraging story.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

Dates below are publication or filing dates, never later effective dates.

1. **2017-01-17 — activist thesis became public.** Elliott and Bluescape filed a Schedule 13D describing a joint position; contemporaneous Reuters reporting put the combined interest at 9.4% and described demands for operational and financial improvement, strategic initiatives, and possible board representation. [SEC Schedule 13D, filed 2017-01-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/904495/000101359417000060/nrg13d-011717.htm); [Reuters, published 2017-01-17](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/elliott-teams-ex-txu-ceo-155114757.html).
2. **2017-02-13 — governance mechanism installed.** NRG entered cooperation agreements, added C. John Wilder and Barry Smitherman to the board, changed the chair, and formed a five-person Business Review Committee covering cost, portfolio, capital structure, and broader strategy. [NRG release, published 2017-02-13](https://investors.nrg.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nrg-announces-cooperation-agreement-elliott-management-and); [SEC 8-K index, filed 2017-02-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1013871/0001104659-17-008383-index.htm).
3. **2017-02-28 — cash-generation bridge survived weak GAAP optics.** NRG reported 2016 adjusted EBITDA of $3.257bn and free cash flow before growth of $1.209bn, said cost reductions exceeded the $400m target by more than $100m, reaffirmed 2017 guidance, and identified a $1.2bn non-cash impairment. This earnings filing is present in `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` with acceptance time 11:31:32Z. [NRG release, published 2017-02-28](https://investors.nrg.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nrg-energy-inc-reports-2016-results-reaffirms-2017-financial).
4. **2017-06-14 — GenOn liability separation.** GenOn entities entered Chapter 11; NRG subsequently treated GenOn as a discontinued operation and deconsolidated it from June 14, putting an estimated zero value on the investment. [NRG 8-K, filed 2017-06-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1013871/000110465917040434/a17-15329_18k.htm). The event is absent from `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` in this checkout, a coverage gap rather than evidence of no filing.
5. **2017-07-12 — quantified Transformation Plan.** NRG targeted $1.065bn of recurring cost savings and margin enhancement, up to $4bn of net divestiture proceeds, and consolidated debt reduction from about $19.5bn to about $6.5bn. [NRG event materials, published 2017-07-12](https://investors.nrg.com/events/event-details/nrg-transformation-plan); [NRG Q3 10-Q, filed 2017-11-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1013871/000101387117000027/nrg2017093010q.htm). This was the liquidity-confirmed step: the stock gained 29.39% that day.
6. **2017-11-02 — execution evidence extended the move.** The Q3 filing recapitulated the plan and disclosed continuing implementation; the date is anchored as an earnings 8-K at 10:40:06Z in `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`. The stock rose 4.55% on $453.9m of dollar volume that day and another 6.35% the next session (`data/yahoo/NRG.parquet`). [SEC filing index, filed 2017-11-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1013871/000101387117000024/0001013871-17-000024-index-headers.html).

`data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` contains no NRG rows in the 2016-07-01 through 2018-01-31 interval, while `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` contains 2017 anchors on February 28, May 2, August 3, and November 2. That archive asymmetry is printed as coverage, not interpreted economically.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** At t0, 126-session NRG performance was -5.15%, essentially matching XLU and trailing SPY by 11.50 pp, even as the 42-session window had turned sharply positive. The stock had not yet exceeded its prior 126-session closing high.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** The January public activist thesis led to a February board-level review mechanism, June GenOn separation, and July quantified self-help plan; November price/volume supplied a later execution echo.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At 2017-01-13 NRG led XLU by 14.11 pp over 21 sessions and 25.48 pp over 42, with a new 63-session closing high (`data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`).
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, delayed.** Initial confirmation was broad rather than explosive (z21 0.20; five-day/60-day ratio 1.61). Definitive confirmation arrived July 12 at roughly $1.13bn dollar volume and z21 38.31, with the close still 16.97% higher ten sessions later.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2017 per-ticker options history exists; current-only surfaces begin in 2026-06 and cannot establish an options mechanism for this episode.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

PIT discipline matters here. `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet` dates FY2015 data to 2016-04-29, FY2016 data to 2017-04-30, and FY2017 data to 2018-04-30. Therefore, at the January t0 the latest annual panel observation was FY2015: revenue $12.328bn, operating loss $4.051bn, net loss $6.382bn, and cash from operations $1.349bn. FY2016 numbers were **not** panel-available until April 30, although the company publicly reported results on February 28. The panel then showed revenue $8.915bn, operating income $33m, net loss $774m, and cash from operations $1.908bn. The arc was not revenue growth or clean GAAP margin expansion; it was stronger cash conversion beneath impairments and a shrinking/reshaping asset base.

`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, gated by `filed`, shows the last pre-t0 reported quarter (2016 Q3, filed 2016-11-04) at $3.952bn revenue, $1.159bn gross profit (29.3% gross margin), $755m operating income, and $402m net income. During the run, 2017 Q1 (filed 2017-05-02) fell to $2.759bn revenue, $634m gross profit (23.0%), $47m operating income, and a $163m net loss; 2017 Q2 (filed 2017-08-03) reported $2.701bn revenue, $864m gross profit (32.0%), $343m operating income, and a $626m net loss; Q3 (filed 2017-11-02) reported $3.049bn revenue, $893m gross profit (29.3%), $376m operating income, and $171m net income. This volatility reinforces that the rerating was attached to prospective self-help and simplification rather than a smooth income-statement inflection.

`data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` is coarsely PIT-gated at quarter-end plus roughly 60 days: 2016 Q3 EPS was 1.27; 2016 Q4 -3.12; 2017 Q1 -0.52; Q2 -1.98; and Q3 0.53. `data/edgar/statements.parquet` is unsuitable for reconstructing the episode because the NRG rows carry a 2026 `as_of` extraction stamp and begin at FY2020. Gross profit, capex, long-term debt, and equity are missing in relevant annual `fundamentals_panel` rows; quarterly long-term debt is also null. Runway therefore cannot be quantified consistently from the designated local panel. The contemporaneous February release provides the more appropriate public bridge: 2017 adjusted EBITDA guidance of $2.7-$2.9bn and free cash flow before growth guidance of $0.8-$1.0bn, published 2017-02-28 at the URL above.

What is observable is a lagging narrative reset. Reuters reported on 2017-01-17 that the newly disclosed 9.4% activist group called the shares deeply undervalued and sought operational, financial, and strategic change; NRG closed at $15.47 that day. [Reuters, published 2017-01-17](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/elliott-teams-ex-txu-ceo-155114757.html). The "price escaped the published model" read is therefore qualitative: before July, the model was commodity-sensitive merchant generation plus complicated renewables/GenOn exposures; after July, investors could capitalize explicit cost savings, proceeds, debt retirement, and returned capital. Because exact contemporaneous analyst targets are unavailable, it would be overclaiming to say when or by how much formal consensus caught up.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** NRG was a large, liquid operating company; the move unfolded through several public governance and restructuring steps and the repo labels the episode a clean hold, not a blow-off. Historical short-interest data are unavailable, so this does not rule out some short covering.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** January relative strength preceded July, and the July event close held and advanced over 3/5/10 sessions. The 126-session forward excess versus XLU was 48.33 pp.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** At t0 NRG led XLU by 25.48 pp over 42 sessions; over the next 126 sessions it led by 48.33 pp. XLU gained only 9.22% while NRG gained 57.55%.
- **options_mirage — ruled_out.** No historical options evidence is being used. Current 2026 surfaces are explicitly separated from the 2017 explanation.

Current hazards are not episode causes: **extension** is possible because the 2026-07-17 spot in `site/gex/NRG.json` is far above 2017 levels and current valuation/fundamentals are outside this study; **call-wall overhead** is possible at the modeled $143 wall, about 7.7% above the 2026-07-17 spot; **catalyst exhaustion** is possible because the 2017 activist/self-help ladder is long completed. These current options observations are snapshot-only, model-dependent, and not directional flow evidence.
