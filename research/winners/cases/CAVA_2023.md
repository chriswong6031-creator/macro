# CAVA 2023 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

CAVA's late-2023 breakaway was the resolution of an IPO-era argument about whether rapid unit growth would dilute restaurant economics. It was not one isolated headline: the 2023-11-07 third-quarter release paired 49.5% CAVA revenue growth, 14.1% same-restaurant sales growth, 7.6% traffic growth, a 25.1% restaurant-level margin, and 11 openings ([CAVA, published 2023-11-07](https://investor.cava.com/news/news-details/2023/CAVA-Group-Reports-Third-Quarter-2023-Results/default.aspx)); the 2024-02-26 annual release then showed nearly 60% full-year CAVA revenue growth, 17.9% same-restaurant sales growth, 72 openings, and three consecutive profitable quarters ([CAVA, published 2024-02-26](https://investor.cava.com/news/news-details/2024/CAVA-Group-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-Fiscal-2023-Results/default.aspx)); and the 2024-05-28 first-quarter release added the first positive-free-cash-flow quarter and higher full-year guidance ([CAVA, published 2024-05-28](https://investor.cava.com/news/news-details/2024/CAVA-Group-Reports-First-Quarter-2024-Results/default.aspx)). The mosaic was therefore a portable restaurant format, traffic-led demand, new-unit productivity, and a margin/profit inflection arriving together. The local episode census starts the relative breakaway on 2023-12-13, five weeks after the first operating proof point; that difference is useful because it shows capital needed time to absorb the evidence.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2023-11-07 - earnings:** CAVA reported 49.5% CAVA revenue growth, 14.1% same-restaurant sales growth including 7.6% traffic growth, a 25.1% restaurant-level margin, and 11 net openings ([company release, published 2023-11-07](https://investor.cava.com/news/news-details/2023/CAVA-Group-Reports-Third-Quarter-2023-Results/default.aspx)). This was the operating proof point preceding the 2023-12-13 mechanical t0.
- **2024-02-26 - earnings/guidance:** the full-year release reported 59.8% CAVA revenue growth, 17.9% same-restaurant sales growth, 72 net openings, a 24.8% restaurant-level margin, and three consecutive quarters of positive net income; it also introduced 2024 guidance ([company release, published 2024-02-26](https://investor.cava.com/news/news-details/2024/CAVA-Group-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-Fiscal-2023-Results/default.aspx); [8-K, filed 2024-02-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1639438/000162828024007007/cava-20240226.htm)).
- **2024-05-28 - earnings/guidance:** first-quarter CAVA revenue grew 30.3%, same-restaurant sales grew 2.3%, 14 restaurants opened, restaurant-level margin reached 25.2%, and management raised its 2024 openings, same-sales, margin, and adjusted-EBITDA ranges ([company release, published 2024-05-28](https://investor.cava.com/news/news-details/2024/CAVA-Group-Reports-First-Quarter-2024-Results/default.aspx)).
- **2024-05-29 - research echo:** Morgan Stanley increased its published target to $85 from $68 after the first-quarter report ([Investing.com, published 2024-05-29](https://uk.investing.com/news/company-news/morgan-stanley-raises-cava-group-shares-target-on-profit-beat-93CH-3520337)). This is an echo, not a causal operating event.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior - present.** From the 2023-06-15 first close through 2023-12-12, CAVA was down 7.81% in the local Massive bars and still below its first-day close. The market was balancing a rich new-issue narrative against early evidence of traffic, margins, and new-unit portability.
- **Catalyst ladder - present.** The 2023-11-07 operating proof point was followed by 2024-02-26 annual confirmation and a 2024-05-28 beat-and-raise release; each was public on the stated date and each reduced a different uncertainty.
- **Relative breakaway - present.** The 21-day lead was only +7.30pp versus XLY, but it widened to +54.46pp by day 63 and +116.56pp by day 126.
- **Liquidity confirmation - present.** Four of the five largest post-t0 dollar-volume sessions occurred during the 2024-05-28 release week, and the February results gap held at all three checkpoints.
- **Options convexity - unknown.** No episode-timely trade-level options archive was found in the repo, so neither initiation nor direction of options trades can be established.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The before-state was already better than the compressed tape implied. On 2023-11-07, CAVA disclosed third-quarter revenue of $175.6M, CAVA revenue growth of 49.5%, a 25.1% restaurant-level margin, net income of $6.8M versus a $11.9M prior-year loss, and $340.4M of cash and cash equivalents ([CAVA, published 2023-11-07](https://investor.cava.com/news/news-details/2023/CAVA-Group-Reports-Third-Quarter-2023-Results/default.aspx)). The local PIT statement row filed 2023-11-08 agrees on $175.6M revenue, $44.5M gross profit, $2.8M operating income, $6.8M net income, and $340.4M cash (`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`).

During the run, the 2024-02-26 annual release reported fiscal-2023 revenue of $728.7M, up 29.2% on a consolidated basis and 59.8% for the CAVA segment; net income was $13.3M versus a $59.0M loss, adjusted EBITDA was $73.8M versus $12.6M, and year-end cash was $332.8M ([CAVA, published 2024-02-26](https://investor.cava.com/news/news-details/2024/CAVA-Group-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-Fiscal-2023-Results/default.aspx)). On 2024-05-28, first-quarter revenue was $256.3M, the restaurant-level margin was 25.2%, net income was $14.0M, adjusted EBITDA was $33.3M, and free cash flow was positive for the first time; management raised adjusted-EBITDA guidance to $100M-$105M from $86M-$92M ([CAVA, published 2024-05-28](https://investor.cava.com/news/news-details/2024/CAVA-Group-Reports-First-Quarter-2024-Results/default.aspx)).

The mechanism was therefore not revenue growth alone. Traffic and new openings supported top-line durability, restaurant-level margin stayed near 25% despite expansion, corporate profitability crossed positive, and a post-IPO cash balance above $300M reduced near-term financing pressure. Coverage gap: the available filings do not disclose mature-vintage economics for every opening cohort, so portability is inferred from aggregate same-sales, traffic, margin, and opening cadence rather than a complete store-level panel.

## 7. Research echo

A reliable point-in-time consensus/spot snapshot at 2023-12-13 was not located, so the initial consensus comparison is **unknown**. The clearest model-catch-up appeared after the May release. On 2024-05-29, when CAVA closed at $88.16 in the local Massive bar, Morgan Stanley raised its target to $85 from $68 ([Investing.com, published 2024-05-29](https://uk.investing.com/news/company-news/morgan-stanley-raises-cava-group-shares-target-on-profit-beat-93CH-3520337)) and Loop Capital raised its target to $80 from $53 ([Investing.com, published 2024-05-29](https://ph.investing.com/news/company-news/loop-capital-lifts-CAVA-Group-share-price-target-in-light-of-positive-Q1-By-Investing.com-93CH-1281918)). Both freshly increased models were already below spot. That is a literal “price escaped the published model” pattern: analysts increased operating assumptions after the evidence, but the cash market repriced faster.

## 8. Ownership context (context-only)

**Context-only; descriptive background, explicitly not a signal.** A Form 13F is due up to 45 days after quarter-end, so any holder-breadth observation must be lagged to its public filing date ([SEC Form 13F FAQ, dated 2023-05-24 and updated 2026-03-06](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/division-investment-management-frequently-asked-questions/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-13f)). The local `data/quiver/sec13f.parquet` snapshot has no CAVA rows for this episode, so point-in-time holder-breadth change is **unknown**, not zero. The local insider table also does not provide complete 2023-2024 episode coverage. No insider transaction is elevated here without a filing-date-complete reconstruction. Form 4 reports are generally due within two business days, but transaction date and filing date must still remain distinct ([SEC ownership-report rule release, published 2002-08-27](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2002/08/ownership-reports-trading-officers-directors-principal-security-holders)).

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze - ruled_out.** The 5-day return was negative and the move broadened over 126 sessions alongside repeated operating releases; that is inconsistent with a purely reflexive short burst.
- **one_day_binary - ruled_out.** The February gap held after 3/5/10 sessions, and the May release generated a separate high-dollar-volume cluster.
- **sector_beta - ruled_out.** CAVA beat XLY by +54.46pp at 63 days and +116.56pp at 126 days.
- **options_mirage - ruled_out.** The case does not depend on options evidence; the relevant historical direction is unknown.

Current hazards were `extended_after_vertical_move`, `valuation_duration`, `new_unit_cannibalization`, `traffic_slowdown`, `food_and_labor_inflation`, and `execution_at_faster_opening_cadence`. The incomplete 12-month lookback and missing PIT ownership breadth remain evidence hazards, not reasons to fill the gaps with present-day data.
