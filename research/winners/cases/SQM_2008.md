# SQM 2008 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

SQM's 2008 alpha was real but terminal: the company could monetize the same potassium-rich resource both directly as potassium chloride and indirectly through higher-value specialty plant nutrients, so the world potash-price shock widened revenue and margins even while several product volumes fell; a 10-for-1 ADR ratio change broadened access, a China potassium-nitrate joint venture extended the capacity story, and three rapid brokerage-model resets fed a reflexive final leg. By the mechanical 2008-06-17 onset, however, the stock was already +196.45% YTD, +134.35% in 63 sessions, above the published targets described contemporaneously, and one session from its terminal high. Liquidity confirmed attention rather than durable absorption: the year's largest adjusted-dollar-volume day was 2008-06-19, the first down day after the peak, and most of the next-largest sessions were also reversals. Earnings continued to accelerate after the price failed, which distinguishes this from an earnings miss; the evidence is most consistent with the market stopping its capitalization of peak fertilizer prices and beginning to discount a sector demand/credit reversal plus a $1 billion capacity program conceived at the top. The mosaic is therefore operating leverage + cycle beta + accessibility + research reflexivity, followed by multiple compression and a fertilizer-cycle unwind—not a lithium headline and not a one-day corporate binary ([Q1 release published 2008-04-29, filed 2008-04-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909037/000114420408024948/v112191_6-k.htm); [contemporaneous “bubble or justified?” review published 2008-06-21](https://www.df.cl/economia-y-politica/el-fenomeno-sqm-burbuja-o-alza-justificada); [USDA fertilizer-cycle review published 2009-02-13](https://www.ers.usda.gov/publications/35825)).

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`): the run did not sustain.**

## 7. Research echo

The repository has no analyst-target history, so this section is external and qualitative. The echo was unusually reflexive and contemporaneously visible:

- 2008-06-03 — Celfin and LarrainVial repeated positive recommendations; the cited local target moved to CLP23,500 from CLP14,500, a 62% reset. The same report said Banchile had removed SQM from a late-May update because it considered the shares overvalued ([published 2008-06-03](https://www.mch.cl/celfin-y-larrainvial-recomiendan-por-segunda-vez-comprar-sqm-b/)).
- 2008-06-09 — Banchile returned with a low-risk positive recommendation and CLP26,000 end-2009 target; SQM-B rose 8.89% that day ([published 2008-06-09](https://www.latercera.com/noticia/bolsa-chilena-cierra-estable-pese-a-subida-de-sqm/)).
- 2008-06-19 — a local market report put SQM-B at CLP27,012 after a 201% YTD rise and attributed the move to potassium prices, projects, research recommendations, the commodity boom, and speculation; June turnover represented more than 17% of the main Chilean exchange's traded value ([published 2008-06-19](https://www.mch.cl/tras-nueva-alza-acciones-de-sqm-ya-triplican-su-valor-en-bolsa-en-2008/)).
- 2008-06-21 — Diario Financiero wrote that the stock had exceeded every published target and that even supportive analysts acknowledged speculation. It recorded Celfin's ADR target at $52 from $36.50 and Banchile's sharply higher 2008/2009 EBITDA assumptions based on potassium-price growth ([published 2008-06-21](https://www.df.cl/economia-y-politica/el-fenomeno-sqm-burbuja-o-alza-justificada)).

The “price escaped the published model” read is literal. `data/yahoo/SQM.parquet` shows the ADR at a raw $53.30 close on 2008-06-18, already above the $52 Celfin target reported three days later; the local share price reported on 2008-06-19 also exceeded Banchile's CLP26,000 target. Consensus was not stale because analysts ignored the cycle—it was stale because each potassium-price assumption was overtaken before the revised report could anchor the tape. That is a terminal reflexivity signature, especially when the cited article simultaneously records speculation and split foreign/local institutional flows.
