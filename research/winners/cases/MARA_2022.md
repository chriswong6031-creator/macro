# MARA 2022 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

MARA’s 2022 breakaway was a deeply compressed, levered Bitcoin-proxy rebound that acquired a company-specific story—new hosting agreements, resolution of the Texas energization bottleneck, roughly 40,000 installed miners beginning to come online, and a reiterated path to 23.3 EH/s—but never acquired self-funding economics. The mechanical 2022-08-10 signal coincided with a softer CPI print and a broad crypto/risk-on burst, after MARA had already risen 206% from June 30; hard information available by then showed Q2 revenue of only $24.9M, negative mining margin after depreciation, a $191.6M net loss, declining cash, a delayed capacity target, and new borrowing secured by Bitcoin. In other words, price capitalized promised future hash rate while present production was impaired and the balance sheet had become more reflexive to Bitcoin. XLK made the move look idiosyncratic, but MARA’s 63-session pre-t0 return correlation was 0.88 with RIOT versus 0.61 with XLK; once the crypto rebound stalled, weakening gap persistence, Compute North’s bankruptcy, collateral pressure, and the FTX liquidity shock exposed that mismatch. The result was a blow-off, not a durable re-underwriting: MARA peaked two sessions after t0, lost 39.1% over the next 63 sessions and 63.6% over 126, and finished 2022 down 89.6% ([Q2 results, published 2022-08-08](https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1292/marathon-digital-holdings-reports-second-quarter-2022-results-along-with-bitcoin-production-and-mining-operation-updates-for-july-2022); [July CPI release, published 2022-08-10 at 08:30 ET](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_08102022.htm); [Compute North consequence, published 2022-09-23](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2022/09/23/bitcoin-miner-marathon-digital-downgraded-at-btig-on-headwinds-from-compute-norths-bankruptcy); [FTX liquidity report, published 2022-11-08 at 11:26 ET](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/binance-to-acquire-ftx-unit-to-help-with-liquidity-crunch--ceo-2936487); [collateral call, filed/published 2022-11-14](https://ir.mara.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001493152-22-031756/form10-q.htm)).

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`).**

## 3. Catalyst ladder

Local filing anchors were checked first. `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` contains 2022 MARA rows on June 22, August 3, September 13, October 17, and November 28. Dates below are publication or filing dates, never later-disclosed effective dates.

- **2022-05-04 — profitable-looking production baseline, already delayed:** Q1 revenue reached $51.7M, production 1,259 BTC, total margin $25.3M, and cash $118.5M. Management said Texas approvals were in hand and still aimed for 23.3 EH/s by early 2023, while acknowledging that the deployment schedule had already shifted by 45 days ([company results, published 2022-05-04 at 16:05 ET](https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1285/marathon-digital-holdings-reports-first-quarter-2022-results)). ## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** MARA was down 50.21% YTD at t0 and had fallen to $5.34 on June 30 after the Hardin outage and Texas delays. The compression was genuine, but it also created extreme elasticity to Bitcoin and any energization update (`data/yahoo/MARA.parquet`; MARA Massive file).
- **Catalyst ladder — present, low durability.** July 7, July 18, August 3, and August 8 supplied successive public rungs, but most value lay in future hosting, construction, energization, and credit. The hard Q2 economics contradicted the soft capacity story, and the host later failed.
- **Relative breakaway — present at t0, then failed.** The detector’s +98.60pp/21d and +104.55pp/42d excess versus XLK were real. Forward excess became negative at every measured horizon and reached -59.27pp at 126d; versus RIOT and COIN the later company-specific deficit was about 26pp.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, then distributive.** The five-day/60-day dollar-volume ratio was 1.73 and July 19–20 produced the year’s two largest turnover days. Yet the largest day was negative, t0 z21 was only 0.92, and peak-adjacent gaps failed by day ten.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** There is no 2022 per-ticker options history or trade-level NBBO in the repo. Convexity may have amplified the vertical cash tape, but presence, direction, and causal importance are null.
