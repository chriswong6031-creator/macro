# CRWD 2023 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

CrowdStrike's 2023-2024 breakaway was a platform-consolidation and profitable-growth rerating, not simply an “AI security” headline. The 2023-11-28 release showed revenue and annual recurring revenue (ARR) both growing 35%, record $223.1M net-new ARR, an 80% non-GAAP subscription gross margin, and a swing to GAAP operating and net profit ([CrowdStrike, published 2023-11-28](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/crowdstrike-reports-third-quarter-fiscal-year-2024-financial)). The 2024-03-05 release then accelerated net-new ARR growth, produced a fourth consecutive quarter of record GAAP net income, and demonstrated 31% full-year free-cash-flow margin while module adoption continued to deepen ([CrowdStrike, published 2024-03-05](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/crowdstrike-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2024)). Alongside those numbers, an expanded Dell managed-detection relationship and the Flow Security acquisition widened the credible platform surface ([CrowdStrike/Dell, published 2024-03-05](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/dell-technologies-and-crowdstrike-expand-partnership-delivering/); [CrowdStrike fiscal-Q1 filing, filed 2024-06-05](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/static-files/950964e0-7bf2-4367-a8da-72f4727459ed)). The mosaic was durable ARR, improving incremental economics, multi-module consolidation, and distribution/product expansion arriving together.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2023-11-28 - earnings/guidance:** fiscal-Q3 revenue reached $786.0M, ARR reached $3.15B, net-new ARR reached $223.1M, GAAP operating income was $3.2M versus a $56.4M loss, and management increased full-year guidance ([company release, published 2023-11-28](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/crowdstrike-reports-third-quarter-fiscal-year-2024-financial)).
- **2024-03-05 - M&A announcement:** CrowdStrike publicly announced its agreement to acquire Flow Security, extending the platform into cloud data-runtime security ([company release, published 2024-03-05](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/crowdstrike-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2024)). A later filing states that the agreement was signed on 2024-03-03, but strict PIT treatment keeps the catalyst on the 2024-03-05 public-announcement date ([fiscal-Q1 filing, filed 2024-06-05](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/static-files/29e71f45-3c39-4c2c-9159-5e7bb9f3315b)).
- **2024-03-05 - partnership:** Dell and CrowdStrike expanded their relationship so Dell's managed detection and response service would use Falcon XDR ([joint release, published 2024-03-05](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/dell-technologies-and-crowdstrike-expand-partnership-delivering/)).
- **2024-03-05 - earnings/guidance:** fiscal-Q4 revenue reached $845.3M, ARR $3.44B, net-new ARR $281.9M, GAAP operating income $29.7M, and free cash flow $283.0M; fiscal-2024 revenue grew 36% and free-cash-flow margin reached 31% ([company release, published 2024-03-05](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/crowdstrike-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2024)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior - present.** The stock had already recovered during 2023, but the pre-t0 model still had to reconcile slower percentage growth at scale with improving net-new ARR and profit. The day before t0 closed below the 3-, 6-, and 12-month highs that t0 cleared simultaneously.
- **Catalyst ladder - present.** November established ARR and GAAP-profit momentum; March added faster net-new ARR, deeper module adoption, a distribution partnership, and an adjacent acquisition.
- **Relative breakaway - present.** CRWD was ahead of XLK by +19.95pp at 63 days and +19.69pp at 126 days despite a strong technology benchmark.
- **Liquidity confirmation - present.** Both earnings reactions were among the five largest dollar-volume sessions, and the March cluster reached $7.597B on its peak day.
- **Options convexity - unknown.** No trade-level episode archive supports a directional options conclusion.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The pre-breakaway comparison was fiscal Q3 one year earlier, when CrowdStrike reported $580.9M of revenue, $2.34B of ARR, a GAAP operating loss of $56.4M, and a GAAP net loss of $55.0M ([company release, published 2022-11-29](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/crowdstrike-reports-third-quarter-fiscal-year-2023-financial)). On 2023-11-28, the same fiscal-quarter frame had advanced to $786.0M revenue (+35%), $3.15B ARR (+35%), a 78% GAAP subscription gross margin, $3.2M GAAP operating income, and $26.7M GAAP net income ([company release, published 2023-11-28](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/crowdstrike-reports-third-quarter-fiscal-year-2024-financial)). The filing row dated 2023-11-29 in `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet` shows $2.969B cash, compared with $2.467B in the prior-year fiscal Q3.

During the run, fiscal Q4 extended rather than reversed that arc. Revenue was $845.3M (+33%), ARR was $3.44B (+34%), GAAP subscription gross margin was 78% versus 75%, GAAP operating income was $29.7M versus a $61.5M loss, and GAAP net income was $53.7M versus a $47.5M loss. Operating cash flow was $347.0M, free cash flow $283.0M, and cash plus short-term investments $3.47B ([CrowdStrike, published 2024-03-05](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/crowdstrike-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2024)). For full fiscal 2024, revenue rose 36% to $3.06B, GAAP operating loss narrowed to $2.0M from $190.1M, GAAP net income became $89.3M from a $183.2M loss, and free cash flow reached $938.2M.

The fundamental bridge was thus ARR growth plus a large incremental-margin swing and cash generation, not accounting profit in isolation. Coverage gap: company module-adoption percentages show cross-sell breadth, but public filings do not provide cohort-level ARR retention or unit economics for every module, so the exact contribution of each product surface is unknown.

## 6. Options context

Historical options convexity is **unknown**. The repo has no CRWD trade-level NBBO archive covering this 2023-2024 window, and the study did not find a dated public report sufficient to determine opening versus closing activity or buyer versus writer initiation. The large March opening gap and cash-market volume are directly observable; signed option flow is not. Current implied volatility, open interest, or dealer-exposure snapshots are not substituted for episode evidence.

## 7. Research echo

The sell-side model initially moved in small steps behind the tape. After the November report, Needham raised its target only to $250 from $245 while CRWD closed 2023-11-29 at $234.44 ([Investing.com/Benzinga, published 2023-11-29](https://uk.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/crowdstrike-analysts-increase-their-forecasts-after-upbeat-earnings-3253037)). After the March report, the revisions became much larger: RBC moved to $420 from $357 ([Investing.com, published 2024-03-06](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/crowdstrike-shares-target-raised-to-420-on-strong-outlook-93CH-3327056)), Macquarie to $370 from $285 ([Investing.com, published 2024-03-06](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/macquarie-raises-crowdstrike-stock-target-to-370-93CH-3327072)), and Evercore ISI to $405 from $255 ([Investing.com, published 2024-03-06](https://in.investing.com/news/crowdstrike-shares-target-raised-to-405-on-strong-outlook-93CH-4052298)). CRWD closed that day at $329.57. The “price escaped the published model” read is strongest at the transition: t0 rapidly consumed the modest post-Q3 target headroom, and only after the second earnings rung did several models jump well above spot.

## 8. Ownership context (context-only)

**Context-only; descriptive background, explicitly not a signal.** Form 13F holdings may become public as late as 45 days after quarter-end ([SEC FAQ, dated 2023-05-24 and updated 2026-03-06](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/division-investment-management-frequently-asked-questions/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-13f)). The local `data/quiver/sec13f.parquet` extract is not a complete 2023-2024 CRWD holder panel, so breadth change for this episode is **unknown**. The same applies to episode-timely insider interpretation: a transaction must be keyed to its Form 4 filing date, generally within two business days, rather than inferred from later aggregates ([SEC ownership-report rule release, published 2002-08-27](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2002/08/ownership-reports-trading-officers-directors-principal-security-holders)). No ownership or insider observation is used as a positive input here.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze - ruled_out.** The move followed repeated ARR/profit releases at large cash-market volume and persisted for six months.
- **one_day_binary - ruled_out.** Both November and March gaps held at 3/5/10 sessions.
- **sector_beta - ruled_out.** CRWD beat an already strong XLK by +19.69pp at 126 days.
- **options_mirage - ruled_out.** The thesis does not require options evidence, which remains unknown.

Current hazards were `extended_after_vertical_move`, `premium_multiple`, `net_new_arr_deceleration`, `platform_execution`, `cybersecurity_competition`, `large_deal_timing`, and `acquisition_integration`. A security-platform winner can still reverse sharply if ARR conversion or retention falls short; absence of episode options and complete ownership data should remain explicit nulls.
