# CVNA 2023 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Carvana's 2023 breakaway was a distressed-turnaround rerating where liquidity survival, gross-profit improvement, and debt restructuring hit at the same time. The stock entered the year priced for existential risk; then first-quarter unit-economics improvement, short interest pressure, and the July 2023 debt-exchange agreement let capital re-underwrite survival odds. By 2023-08-03, CVNA had outperformed XLY by +222.38pp over 42 trading days, +616.00pp over 63 trading days, and +986.21pp YTD.
