# CENX 2010 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

CENX's 2010 breakaway was chiefly a high-operating-leverage aluminum-cycle re-rating, with company-specific cost repair and capacity optionality amplifying—but not independently creating—the move. The stock entered 2010 with 2009 revenue down 54%, negative gross profit, curtailments, and unresolved Hawesville power/labor issues; by the 2010-09-24 mechanical onset, LME cash aluminum had risen 12.7% in 21 trading days as Chinese power curbs tightened the supply narrative, while CENX had already restored positive gross profit, accumulated cash, and refinanced its revolver ([Century release published 2010-02-23](https://centuryaluminum.com/investors/press-releases/press-release-details/2010/Century-Reports-2009-Financial-Results/default.aspx); [China power-curb report published 2010-09-09](https://news.metal.com/es/newscontent/100012597-China-Power-Cuts-to-Trim-Steel-Aluminum-Production-Drive-Metal-Prices); [LME observations dated 2010-08-25 and 2010-09-24](https://www.westmetall.com/en/markdaten.php?action=table&field=LME_Al_cash&year=2010); [Q2 release published 2010-07-27](https://centuryaluminum.com/investors/press-releases/press-release-details/2010/Century-Aluminum-Reports-Second-Quarter-2010-Earnings/default.aspx)). QE2, the Hawesville potline restart, a five-year labor agreement, and FY2010 cash-flow/margin repair prolonged the re-underwriting into a 2011-04-07 peak ([Federal Reserve statement published 2010-11-03](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20101103a.htm); [restart published 2010-12-07](https://centuryaluminum.com/investors/press-releases/press-release-details/2010/Century-Aluminum-Announces-Restart-of-Hawesville-KY-Potline/default.aspx); [FY2010 release published 2011-02-15](https://centuryaluminum.com/investors/press-releases/press-release-details/2011/Century-Reports-2010-Earnings/default.aspx)). But the cohort test is the autopsy's decisive qualification: CENX's +43.32% forward-126-day return nearly matched Alcoa's +40.64%, and its 252-day return fell to -27.51% as LME aluminum dropped 21.7% from 2011-04-28 to 2011-09-23 and Hawesville required remediation ([LME observations dated 2011-04-28 and 2011-09-23](https://www.westmetall.com/en/markdaten.php?action=table&field=LME_Al_cash&year=2011); [Q2 release published 2011-08-04](https://centuryaluminum.com/investors/press-releases/press-release-details/2011/Century-Aluminum-Reports-Second-Quarter-2011-Earnings/default.aspx)). The large early alpha was real; its inability to survive the commodity reversal shows that aluminum beta was the engine and CENX-specific repair was the torque.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** Low expectations and damage were present, but price compression was not: on 2010-08-25 CENX was 47.04% below its prior-252-day high, yet its trailing-63-day range was 31.05% and annualized 63-day close volatility was 69.62%. At t0, the 21-day range had expanded to 34.00%. This was a volatile rebound from a low, not a tight base (`data/yahoo/CENX.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Q1/Q2 recovery and liquidity improvement created runway; Chinese power curbs and QE2 repriced the commodity; Q3 exposed price leverage; Hawesville restart, labor resolution, and FY2010 cash generation converted the cycle story into operating optionality.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** T0 was the first 63-day closing high, with +20.76pp 21-day excess versus XLB and +23.36pp versus SPY. CENX also led AA and KALU into t0, although AA nearly closed the gap by the 126-day horizon.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, moderate at onset and strong later.** T0 z21 1.83, z60 1.14, and 5d/60d 1.17 were constructive but not extreme. The close jump held ten sessions, and later event sessions reached z60 6.44 on 2010-12-17 and 3.85 on 2011-02-16. True opening-gap evidence is **null**.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** The repo has no 2010 per-ticker options history and no current CENX snapshot. No options mechanism can be inferred.
