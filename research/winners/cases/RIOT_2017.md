# RIOT 2017 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

RIOT's 2017 alpha was a reflexive repricing of a tiny, nearly revenue-less former life-sciences company into a listed crypto proxy at the exact crest of Bitcoin's institutionalization narrative: the October name-and-business pivot supplied scarcity value, a $3 million Coinsquare stake and majority TessPay stake supplied exchange/payments optionality, the Kairos acquisition supplied 1,200 physical miners, repeated 8-Ks kept shortening the narrative half-life, and the December 11 TessPay public-merger LOI plus Cboe/CME futures launches turned that option basket into a liquid trading vehicle. The move was not an operating inflection. At the December 12 detector onset RIOT had gained 294% in 21 sessions and 724% YTD, but its last public quarter contained only $24,175 of fee revenue, no mining revenue, and a $5.3 million net loss; price was capitalizing future crypto exposure while the company used appreciated equity and then a $37 million placement to fund that exposure. Bitcoin's reversal removed the sector tailwind, the placement enlarged supply at $22.50 just as RIOT peaked at $38.60, the strongest gaps stopped holding, and governance/listing scrutiny converted narrative uncertainty into an adverse re-underwriting. This is why RIOT could generate spectacular late-2017 alpha yet fail the durable-winner test: the tape confirmed attention and liquidity, but economics, disclosure quality, and self-funded runway never caught up ([pivot filing, filed 2017-10-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1167419/000107997317000569/bioptix_8k.htm); [Q3 10-Q, filed 2017-11-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1167419/000107997317000667/riot_10q-093017.htm); [TessPay release, published 2017-12-11 and filed 2017-12-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1167419/000107997317000731/ex99x1.htm); [private placement, filed 2017-12-19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1167419/000107997317000747/riot_8k.htm)).

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`): the run did not sustain.**

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present at the origin, absent at t0.** From 2017-06-14 through 2017-09-13, adjusted closes stayed between $3.10 and $3.68 and median dollar volume was only $0.030 million (`data/baskets/ohlcv/RIOT.parquet`). By t0 the stock was +665.67% over 63 sessions, so the scanner found the climax phase rather than the first release.
- **Catalyst ladder — present, but optionality-heavy.** The name change, Coinsquare, TessPay, Kairos miners, Verady, portfolio marks, and futures-market legitimacy repeatedly widened the possible future. At t0, only the miner fleet represented a producing asset, and no mining revenue had yet been reported publicly.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed.** RIOT led SPY by +290.49pp over the 21 sessions into t0 and still gained 36.88% over the next five sessions, but excess reversed to -27.12pp by day 21 and -76.98pp by day 63 (`data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`).
- **Liquidity confirmation — present as attention, not durable depth.** T0 dollar volume was $1.030 billion with z21=3.61, but the billion-dollar sessions were concentrated in the final ascent, the t0 gap failed its hold line the next day, and the fourth-largest dollar-volume day was negative.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2017 per-contract options history, open-interest history, or trade-level NBBO tape exists in the repo. Options cannot be used to explain the episode.
