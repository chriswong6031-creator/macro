# OKTA 2019 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Okta's 2019 breakaway was an identity-platform rerating built from recurring growth, large-enterprise adoption, and improving cash conversion rather than a single security headline. Before the run, the 2018-12-05 quarter had already shown 58% revenue and subscription growth, 55% growth in customers above $100,000 of annual recurring revenue, and the company's first positive quarterly free cash flow; after the 2019-01-14 mechanical breakaway, the 2019-03-07 annual release showed 56% fiscal-year revenue growth and a swing to positive operating cash flow, while Oktane19 broadened the product story from single sign-on toward an extensible identity control plane. The 2019-05-30 quarter then delivered 52% subscription growth and 53% growth in large customers. The mechanism was therefore a sequence: cloud and Zero Trust demand increased the strategic value of independent identity, the enterprise mix extended revenue durability, and improving cash flow reduced the discount attached to high growth. Sources: Okta's [Q3 FY2019 release, published 2018-12-05](https://investor.okta.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2018/Okta-Announces-Record-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-2019-Financial-Results-12-05-2018/), [FY2019 release, published 2019-03-07](https://investor.okta.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2019/Okta-Announces-Record-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2019-Financial-Results-03-07-2019/), and [Q1 FY2020 release, published 2019-05-30](https://investor.okta.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2019/Okta-Announces-Record-First-Quarter-Financial-Results-05-30-2019/).

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2018-12-05 - earnings:** Q3 FY2019 revenue and subscription revenue each grew 58%; customers above $100,000 ARR grew 55%, operating cash flow was $6.4 million, and free cash flow turned positive for the first time. [Company release, published 2018-12-05](https://investor.okta.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2018/Okta-Announces-Record-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-2019-Financial-Results-12-05-2018/).
- **2019-03-07 - earnings and guidance:** FY2019 revenue rose 56% to $399.3 million, subscription revenue rose 57%, annual operating cash flow became positive at $15.2 million, and Okta guided FY2020 revenue to $530-$535 million. [Company release, published 2019-03-07](https://investor.okta.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2019/Okta-Announces-Record-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2019-Financial-Results-03-07-2019/).
- **2019-03-07 - M&A announcement:** Okta announced an agreement to acquire no-code workflow platform Azuqua, extending identity into cross-application workflow automation. [Business Wire release carried by VentureBeat, published 2019-03-07](https://venturebeat.com/technology/okta-to-acquire-azuqua-to-connect-business-applications-in-the-enterprise).
- **2019-04-02 - product launch:** At Oktane19, Okta introduced Identity Engine as a customizable set of building blocks for workforce and customer identity use cases, broadening the story beyond a point solution. [Todd McKinnon's Oktane19 presentation storyboard, published 2019-04-02](https://speakerdeck.com/tmckinnon/storyboard-04-1-2019-external).
- **2019-05-30 - earnings and guidance:** Q1 FY2020 revenue rose 50% to $125.2 million, subscription revenue rose 52%, large-customer count grew 53%, operating cash flow reached $21.3 million, and Okta raised full-year revenue expectations to $543-$548 million. [Company release, published 2019-05-30](https://investor.okta.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2019/Okta-Announces-Record-First-Quarter-Financial-Results-05-30-2019/).
- **2019-08-28 - earnings:** Q2 FY2020 revenue grew 49% and subscription revenue grew 51%; Okta also cited its 2019 Gartner Access Management leadership placement. [Company release, published 2019-08-28](https://investor.okta.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2019/Okta-Announces-Strong-Second-Quarter-Financial-Results-08-28-2019/).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior - present.** OKTA entered 2019 below its September 2018 high after a growth-stock drawdown, while GAAP losses and the durability of enterprise identity demand still constrained the model.
- **Catalyst ladder - present.** Two 50%+ subscription-growth prints, positive cash-flow milestones, workflow M&A, and the Oktane19 platform expansion accumulated over eight months.
- **Relative breakaway - present.** The temporary 42-day underperformance reversed to +66.14 percentage points versus IGV and +73.78 points versus SPY at 126 sessions.
- **Liquidity confirmation - present.** The two largest episode dollar-volume days followed the May 30 result; the March 8 session was the third largest and followed the March 7 result.
- **Options convexity - unknown.** Public reports describe concentrated call activity, but trade-level NBBO, full open/close history, and dealer hedge state are not available.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Before the mechanical breakaway, Okta had reached a useful combination of scale and improving unit economics. On 2018-12-05, Q3 revenue was $105.6 million (+58%), subscription revenue was $97.7 million (+58%), GAAP gross profit was $78.6 million, GAAP operating margin improved to negative 27.0% from negative 51.6%, operating cash flow was $6.4 million, and free cash flow was $1.4 million. [Company release, published 2018-12-05](https://investor.okta.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2018/Okta-Announces-Record-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-2019-Financial-Results-12-05-2018/).

During the run, the 2019-03-07 annual release showed FY2019 revenue of $399.3 million (+56%), subscription revenue of $370.9 million (+57%), GAAP gross profit of $285.8 million, GAAP operating loss of $119.6 million, operating cash flow of positive $15.2 million versus negative $25.2 million in FY2018, and $563.8 million of cash and short-term investments. [Company release, published 2019-03-07](https://investor.okta.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2019/Okta-Announces-Record-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2019-Financial-Results-03-07-2019/). The 2019-05-30 quarter extended top-line growth but also showed the investment burden: revenue was $125.2 million (+50%), GAAP operating loss was $51.8 million, operating cash flow was $21.3 million, free cash flow was $13.2 million, and cash plus investments were $547.5 million. [Company release, published 2019-05-30](https://investor.okta.com/news-and-events/news-releases/news-details/2019/Okta-Announces-Record-First-Quarter-Financial-Results-05-30-2019/).

The arc was not GAAP-profitability conversion; it was recurring-revenue scale plus stronger cash conversion while management continued to spend into enterprise and platform expansion. Local PIT coverage includes 28 OKTA rows in `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, ten in `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, and 40 in `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`.

## 6. Options context

The public evidence supports activity magnitude only. An OptionsHawk market recap dated 2019-08-12 reported more than 1,300 August 30 $135 calls transacting that day, an average analyst target of $105 against a roughly $131 spot reference, and 6.9% short interest; the public PDF is not a complete trade-level NBBO record and cannot establish net buyer versus seller for the episode. [OptionsHawk recap, published 2019-08-12](https://optionshawk.com/wp-content/uploads/Market-Recap-8-12-19.pdf). Historical dealer gamma, implied-volatility term structure, and aggregate open-interest evolution are unknown. Options convexity is therefore not used to explain the primary breakaway.

## 7. Research echo

The price escaped the published model during the run. A public 2019-08-12 recap put OKTA near $131 against an average target of $105 and recorded Needham's 2019-07-17 increase to a Street-high $154 after Oktane19 momentum. [OptionsHawk, published 2019-08-12](https://optionshawk.com/wp-content/uploads/Market-Recap-8-12-19.pdf). On 2019-08-21, Cowen initiated at $150 while the reported average target was about $126 and BMO's high was $155. [Motley Fool, published 2019-08-21](https://www.fool.com/investing/2019/08/21/okta-gets-bullish-start-at-cowen.aspx). On 2019-08-29, after another revenue-guidance increase, JPMorgan raised its published target to $168 from $127. [Forbes, published 2019-08-29](https://www.forbes.com/sites/robertdefrancesco/2019/08/29/okta-shares-fall-4-despite-another-revenue-guidance-boost/). The research echo moved from catching up to the price to debating how long 30%+ growth could persist.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze: ruled_out.** OKTA was a liquid enterprise-software growth name with only 6.9% short interest in the cited August report and a multi-quarter operating ladder.
- **one_day_binary: ruled_out.** The move survived a mid-run software pullback and reaccelerated across March and May earnings plus the April product expansion.
- **sector_beta: ruled_out.** At 126 trading days, OKTA exceeded IGV by 66.14 percentage points and SPY by 73.78 points.
- **options_mirage: possible.** Public call activity appeared late in the run, but signed flow and dealer positioning are unknown; the platform and earnings evidence stands independently.
- **Current episode hazards:** `extended_after_vertical_move`, `multiple_compression`, `platform_execution`, `large_customer_concentration`, `security_incident_tail_risk`, and `options_mirage`.
