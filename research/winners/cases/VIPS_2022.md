# VIPS 2022 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

VIPS's late-2022 alpha came from three interacting repricings, not one headline: China first reduced the lockdown/consumption discount and then the U.S.-listing audit discount, while Vipshop supplied company-specific proof that a shrinking top line could still produce materially better gross, operating, and net margins through merchandising discipline, much lower marketing intensity, and large share repurchases ([NHC, published 2022-11-10](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/wjw/mtbd/202211/a3b0fdce56344090bf59cdccb3c94cdd.shtml); [PCAOB, published 2022-12-15](https://pcaobus.org/news-events/news-releases/news-release-detail/fact-sheet-pcaob-secures-complete-access-to-inspect-investigate-chinese-firms-for-first-time-in-history); [Vipshop, published 2022-11-22](https://ir.vip.com/news-releases/news-release-details/vipshop-reports-unaudited-third-quarter-2022-financial-results)). The distinction matters because the first five sessions into t0 were mostly China beta—VIPS lagged KWEB by 0.62 percentage points—but over 21 sessions it beat KWEB by 18.04 points and an equal-weight BABA/JD/PDD cohort by 15.37 points as Q3 gross margin rose 230 bp, operating margin rose 220 bp, and reported net income rose 168.4% despite a 13.3% revenue decline ([Vipshop, published 2022-11-22](https://ir.vip.com/news-releases/news-release-details/vipshop-reports-unaudited-third-quarter-2022-financial-results)). Liquidity arrived in repeated policy and earnings waves, every qualifying 5% opening gap in the core November break held through ten sessions, and the 2022-12-02 close became the first 252-session high of 2022. The move did sustain, though it attenuated: census excess versus SPY was +31.18/+23.16/+31.30/+13.20 points at 21/63/126/252 forward sessions, with `clean_hold=true`; the February 2023 results gap then failed its hold test and published targets began chasing price ([MarketScreener, published 2022-11-24](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/latest/Analyst-recommendations-Apple-Dollar-Tree-GSK--42401672/), with spot dates from the local price file), but May 2023's return to revenue and customer growth supplied a final hard fundamental confirmation ([Vipshop, published 2023-05-23](https://ir.vip.com/news-releases/news-release-details/vipshop-reports-unaudited-first-quarter-2023-financial-results)).

## 3. Catalyst ladder

The local filing-anchor check is null: VIPS has zero rows in [`data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet) and [`data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet). VIPS produced 2022's largest dollar-volume session and a +31.88% close, but the later retracement shows that this was a soft de-risking precursor, not sufficient company evidence.
