# A 2000 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Agilent's 2000 breakaway was a real operating acceleration wrapped in a fragile market structure. The November 1999 offering left only 72 million shares public while Hewlett-Packard retained 380 million, and the prospectus disclosed that those retained shares would become freely saleable after the separation ([final prospectus filed 1999-11-18](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1090872/000101287099004313/0001012870-99-004313.txt)). First-quarter orders, revenue, and earnings then accelerated ([published 2000-02-18](https://www.wirelessnetworksonline.com/doc/agilent-technologies-net-earnings-increase-77-0002)), so capital began valuing Agilent as an optical/communications platform rather than a diversified HP carve-out; the March 6 photonic-switch launch turned that rerating into a 47.2% one-session jump in the repository tape ([published 2000-03-06](https://compoundsemiconductor.net/article/80393/Agilent_Technologies_unveils_breakthrough_optical_switching_technology)). The move failed because the detector arrived after a 166.9% 63-session rise, the product announcement marked the terminal peak, HP's announced distribution expanded potential public supply by more than five times the offering float ([published and filed 2000-04-07/2000-04-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1090872/000101287000002034/0001012870-00-002034.txt)), operating margins did not rise consistently with revenue, and the July warning exposed component shortages, capacity inefficiency, and weak non-communications units ([published 2000-07-20](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/agilent-warns-of-lower-profits-stock-plunges-25-1009342)). The supplied XLV comparison exaggerates the cohort gap because contemporaneous Agilent was principally communications/electronics, but the breakaway was still very large versus XLK and QQQ; the later collapse also underperformed those technology proxies. This was therefore genuine alpha at inception but not a durable winner: low float, thematic valuation, one-day convexity, supply normalization, and cycle reversal mattered more than the still-strong full-year headline.

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`): the run did not sustain.**

That absence is useful: the price escaped the published model before the fundamental forecasts failed, then converged below it once supply normalized and the cycle weakened.
