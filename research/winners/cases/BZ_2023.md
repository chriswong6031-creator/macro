# BZ — 2023 failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

BZ's apparent January 2023 alpha was a fast re-rating of a previously impaired China internet/recruiting asset, not yet a demonstrated earnings-duration story: reopening expectations removed a macro constraint, the November result showed user growth and positive adjusted earnings despite weak paid-customer trends, and the December Hong Kong dual-primary listing reduced one channel of listing anxiety. Those ingredients landed on a depressed, liquid ADR and produced an 84.5% 42-session rise through January 9, but the tape had already pulled forward the recovery before the operating proof arrived. At the breakaway close, 126-session relative performance was still negative, no 252-day closing high had printed, the price-volume burst was moderate rather than exceptional, and the next results revealed flat Q4 revenue, falling billings, fewer paid enterprise customers, heavy spending, and a GAAP loss. Stronger 2023 user, revenue, and margin evidence eventually arrived, but too late to defend the January price: BZ underperformed SPY at every forward horizon and finished 2023 32.3% below its January 9 close.

**Format-contract classification: this is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`); the run did not sustain.**

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** BZ entered the 42-session impulse after a long impairment, and even after the impulse its 126-session return was -0.64%; however, the January price was already 49.2% above its 63-session starting point. The base was depressed, but no longer compressed at t0.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Reopening speculation, formal policy easing, Q3 results, and the HKEX listing arrived in sequence. Most of the early ladder removed macro/regulatory discount rather than proving multi-quarter monetization.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed.** The supplied/recomputed 21- and 42-session excess values were +32.85 pp and +81.98 pp, with a 63-day high. Forward relative returns were negative at every measured horizon.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present but not exceptional.** Dollar-volume z-scores were +1.44/+1.70 and the 5d/60d ratio was 1.46; confirmation cleared the episode threshold but did not display an overwhelming, persistent expansion.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2023 per-ticker options history exists locally, and the current options surfaces have no BZ row/snapshot.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Repo-native PIT coverage is **null** for BZ: there are zero ticker rows in `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet`. Consequently, the following arc is taken only from company publications on their publication dates, not backfilled into a false local PIT series.

Before t0, the cash runway was strong but the operating setup was mixed. The 2022-11-29 Q3 release reported RMB13.923bn of cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments as of September 30, providing ample runway. The next observable disclosure, published 2023-03-20, showed FY2022 revenue of RMB4.511bn (+5.9%), but FY billings down 8.1%; Q4 revenue was RMB1.082bn (flat), Q4 adjusted net income fell to RMB59.5m from RMB348.5m, Q4 GAAP net loss was RMB184.8m, and Q4 operating costs/expenses were RMB1.426bn versus RMB1.082bn revenue. Paid enterprise customers fell 10% even while MAU rose 26.6%. [Q3 release, 2022-11-29](https://ir.zhipin.com/zh-hans/news-releases/news-release-details/kanzhun-limited-announces-third-quarter-2022-financial-results/); [Q4/FY release, 2023-03-20](https://ir.zhipin.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kanzhun-limited-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2022).

During the failed run's aftermath, fundamentals did turn—but sequentially, not at t0. Q1 revenue grew 12.3% and adjusted income doubled, while reported operating loss of RMB77.3m implied a -6.1% GAAP operating margin. Q2 revenue growth accelerated to 33.7%, billings to 65.4%, and adjusted net income to RMB568.5m. Q3 revenue grew 36.3%; reported operating income of RMB261.0m implied a 16.2% GAAP operating margin, with paid enterprise customers up 32.4%. Thus the business moved from weak monetization and negative GAAP operating margin toward strong growth and profitability, but the January tape demanded that recovery months before the publications supplied it. [Q1 release, 2023-05-24](https://ir.zhipin.com/zh-hans/news-releases/news-release-details/kanzhun-limited-announces-first-quarter-2023-financial-results); [Q2 release, 2023-08-29](https://ir.zhipin.com/zh-hant/news-releases/news-release-details/kanzhun-limited-announces-second-quarter-2023-financial-results); [Q3 release, 2023-11-14](https://ir.zhipin.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kanzhun-limited-announces-third-quarter-2023-financial-results).

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** The 84.5% 42-session move and immediate reversal resemble squeeze geometry, but there is no local 2023 short-interest or trade-level evidence to make it the primary mechanism.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The impulse accumulated across many sessions and several public events; no single day accounts for the move.
- **sector_beta — likely.** The catalyst ladder was dominated by China reopening and policy normalization. The sector field is blank, preventing a direct China-internet cohort calculation, but the broad China-equity rally is documented and SPY is an imperfect comparator.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Historical options evidence is structurally absent. It cannot explain the episode, but neither can a convexity contribution be excluded.
- **Current/episode hazard: extended_after_vertical_move.** By t0 BZ had gained 84.5% in 42 sessions and 21.8% in five.
- **Current/episode hazard: catalyst_exhausted.** Macro discount removal and listing completion were substantially reflected before operating acceleration was published.
- **Current hazard: call_wall_overhead — null.** No BZ GEX snapshot exists.
- **Current hazard: fundamental_price_disconnect.** Real 2023 improvement failed to regain the January high, suggesting the initial re-rating had over-discounted speed or durability.
