# IONQ 2025 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

IONQ's May 2025 alpha was a real but non-durable re-underwriting from a small trapped-ion computing vendor into a broader quantum-platform story: FY2024 revenue had nearly doubled and management paired a CEO change with ID Quantique, Lightsynq, Capella, and later Oxford Ionics to add networking, interconnect, space, and alternative ion-trap capabilities; DARPA Stage A and a $22 million EPB agreement gave the story government and commercial anchors ([2025-02-26 results](https://www.ionq.com/news/ionq-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2024-financial-results), [2025-02-26 CEO announcement](https://investors.ionq.com/news/news-details/2025/IonQ-Names-Niccolo-de-Masi-as-President--Chief-Executive-Officer/default.aspx), [2025-04-03 DARPA announcement](https://www.ionq.com/news/ionq-selected-by-darpa-for-quantum-benchmarking-initiative-qbi-to-advance), [2025-04-25 EPB announcement](https://investors.ionq.com/news/news-details/2025/IonQ-Announces-22M-Deal-with-EPB-Establishing-Chattanooga-Tennessee-as-the-First-Quantum-Computing--Networking-Hub-in-the-U-S-/default.aspx)). But the mechanical breakaway on May 22 was not tied to a new hard IONQ filing: it coincided with a promotional CEO comparison and a broad pure-play quantum rally after D-Wave's May 20 product launch ([Barron's, 2025-05-22](https://www.barrons.com/articles/ionq-quantum-computing-nvidia-02c0d4ea), [Investopedia, 2025-05-22](https://www.investopedia.com/why-ionq-and-other-quantum-computing-stocks-soared-thursday-11740534), [D-Wave, 2025-05-20](https://www.dwavequantum.com/company/newsroom/press-release/d-wave-announces-general-availability-of-advantage2-quantum-computer-its-most-advanced-and-performant-system/)). The tape therefore contained both genuine company alpha and likely sector beta: IONQ beat SPY by 61.95 percentage points over 21 trading days, yet trailed an equal-weight QBTS/RGTI/QUBT proxy by 32.03 points, made only a 63-day—not 126- or 252-day—high, and added just 4.91% before reversing. Flat Q1 revenue, a 43.0% gross margin, heavy operating cash consumption, share-funded acquisitions, and a price above most contemporaneous published targets left too little near-term economic proof for the valuation step. Liquidity confirmed attention, not durable ownership: the May 22 session was the year's largest dollar-volume transfer, but there was no opening gap to defend and forward excess versus SPY was negative at 21, 63, and 126 days.

**FAILED BREAKAWAY:** this case is `case_type: failed_breakaway`; the run did not sustain, and the failure—not the temporary alpha—is the object of the study.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** IONQ closed at $51.07 on January 6, fell 39.00% on January 8, and spent the next four months in wide two-sided swings. The May break came from a volatile repair and was already +70.86% over 21 days, not from a quiet compressed base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** FY2024 growth/CEO transition → ID Quantique/ATM runway → DARPA → $22 million EPB contract → AI demonstrations → Q1 plus Lightsynq/Capella → Einride formed a coherent platform ladder. Quality deteriorated at t0: the actual May 22 impulse was media and cohort-driven, with no same-day hard filing.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed.** IONQ beat SPY by 61.95pp/73.74pp over 21/42 days and made a 63-day high, but did not clear the 126-/252-day January high. Forward excess was -15.14pp, -24.12pp, and -23.01pp at 21/63/126 days.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** Dollar-volume z21 was 4.23, z60 was 7.13, and 5d/60d turnover was 2.51×. The capital transfer was real, but the t0 move had no opening gap, produced only 4.91% additional upside, and then reversed.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** Per-ticker history starts in June 2026. No episode-date gamma, wall, IV, or trade-direction evidence exists, so options cannot be credited with or excluded from the 2025 acceleration.
