# UAMY 2025 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

UAMY's 2025 move began with a real operating and policy inflection—China restricted U.S.-bound antimony, Washington prioritized domestic critical-mineral capacity, UAMY restarted and expanded its smelters, higher realized prices turned gross profit positive, and the Defense Logistics Agency later awarded a five-year antimony contract—but the equity converted those facts into far more certainty than the disclosed throughput supported. The mechanical 2025-09-19 trigger arrived after a 266.7% year-to-date rise and two sessions before the DLA announcement; the contract was an indefinite-delivery vehicle with a $245 million ceiling, only an approximately $10 million initial order, and no recognized revenue through 2025-09-30. From the trigger, UAMY rose another 169.2% to $17.47, implying at least a $2.28 billion equity value against $17.5 million of first-half revenue, while repeated equity issuance and an attempted all-stock Larvotto acquisition changed the debate from scarcity leverage to dilution, execution, and capital allocation. The largest turnover then clustered around the top—including a $572.5 million, -21.2% reversal day—and Larvotto's rejection broke the acquisition rung; by 63 sessions the stock had fallen 74.3% from its peak and lagged XME even though the mining cohort remained positive.

This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** The first 63/126/252-day closing highs had appeared on 2025-03-24; by the trigger UAMY was +137.7% over 63 sessions and +266.7% YTD. This was an extension from an earlier rerating, not a fresh base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present, but mixed durability.** China policy, U.S. policy, improving gross profit, restart/expansion plans, and the DLA award formed a real ladder. Its hardest rung arrived after the detector fired, and its face value exceeded guaranteed economics; feedstock delays, financing, and acquisition scope were adverse rungs.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed.** At 21 sessions UAMY led XME by 88.09 percentage points and SPY by 105.79. By 63 sessions those spreads were -44.15 and -32.74 points, respectively.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present initially, non-durable.** The trigger's dollar-volume z-scores were 4.04/5.78 versus 21/60-day history, but four of the five largest turnover days clustered from October 10–20 and included the large October 15 reversal. Later gaps did not hold.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** The repo has no UAMY options history for the 2025 episode, and current snapshots cannot be projected backward.
