# NXT 2025 Winner Autopsy

## 1. Bottom line
NXT produced large 2025 alpha because the market had to re-underwrite the company from a solar-tracker component supplier into a high-margin solar infrastructure platform with backlog visibility, domestic-content/tax-credit positioning, adjacent eBOS/foundations/software products, and international demand. The local tape confirms the initial breakaway around 2025-05-12, with the May 14, 2025 8-K and earnings release converting the move into a capital re-rating: record Q4 FY25 revenue, FY26 guide above the prior run-rate, cash/no-debt runway, backlog above $4.5 billion, and Bentek/eBOS expansion. The move was not perfectly linear: the first 63 trading days lost relative momentum versus XLI/SPY, but the July 29, 2025 beat-and-raise and the October 23, 2025 beat/guide/backlog confirmation restarted the episode and left YTD alpha very large.

## 4. Stage anatomy
Compressed prior: present. NXT traded through a January pop, a March/April reset, and then the 2025-05-12 mechanical breakaway. The 5/12 close-to-close impulse was +8.79% on dollar-volume z21 `+5.73` from Yahoo, while the supplied episode context marks new_high_63d `True`.

Catalyst ladder: present. January raised profit outlook/backlog, May FY25/FY26 guide plus Bentek/eBOS, May analyst revisions, India demand evidence, July beat-and-raise, and October backlog/guide confirmation created repeated publication-date opportunities for capital to reprice the name.

Relative breakaway: present but with a mid-run stall. NXT beat XLI by +12.40 pp at 5 trading days and +15.99 pp at 21 trading days from Yahoo bars, while the supplied episode context shows +20.51 pp at 21d. It then underperformed at 63d before the 126d leg produced +101.49 pp vs XLI.

Liquidity confirmation: present. Dollar volume on 2025-05-12 was `$218.36 million` with local z21 `+5.73`; 2025-05-15 was the third-largest 2025 dollar-volume day at `$505.03 million`; later July and October earnings confirmations also landed among the largest dollar-volume days.

Options convexity: unknown for 2025 history. Current snapshots exist, but per-ticker options history starts 2026-06, and repo doctrine forbids signed-flow claims without trade-level NBBO.

During the run, reported fundamentals kept the re-rating alive. The 2025-05-14 SEC release reported FY25 revenue of `$2.959 billion`, net income attributable to Nextracker Inc. of `$509 million`, adjusted free cash flow of `$622 million`, cash and equivalents of `$766 million`, and no long-term debt as of March 31, 2025. The 2025-07-29 release reported Q1 FY26 revenue of `$864 million`, gross profit of `$282 million`, net income of `$157 million`, backlog over `$4.75 billion`, cash of `$743 million`, and no debt. The 2025-10-23 release reported Q2 FY26 revenue of `$905 million`, gross profit of `$293 million`, backlog over `$5 billion`, cash of `$845 million`, and no debt. The fundamentals arc therefore moved from “good margins with policy/tariff questions” to “visible backlog, cash runway, and repeated guide lifts.”
