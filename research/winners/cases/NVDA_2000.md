# NVDA 2000 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

NVDA's June 2000 breakaway was the last, most liquid repricing of a genuine product-and-distribution step-change: the March Xbox selection and $200 million advance widened the addressable market beyond PC add-in boards; GeForce2 GTS demonstrated rapid high-end execution; May 16 results reported 109% year-over-year revenue growth and a stock split; and June's GeForce2 MX brought the architecture to a mass-market price point. Capital therefore underwrote a much larger platform while NVDA was growing far faster than the technology cohort. The failure was not that the commercial story was fictional. It was that nearly all of the re-underwriting had already been compressed into a 220% YTD rise by June 16, the stock made its ultimate 2000 high three sessions later, the next product announcements could not create a second estimate shock, and the late-cycle semiconductor multiple was exposed when the technology tape rolled over. The clean mosaic is real company-specific acceleration plus extreme anticipation, followed by catalyst exhaustion and sector/multiple compression; the 126-session result was a 44.49% NVDA loss and 10.93 percentage points of underperformance versus XLK.

**Format-contract classification: this is a failed breakaway case. The run did not sustain.**

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2000-03-07 — Xbox design win became public.** EE Times reported that Microsoft had selected NVDA for the Xbox graphics architecture; the agreement ultimately included a $200 million prepayment and potential multiyear processor sales. Publication date: 2000-03-07. [EE Times](https://www.eetimes.com/microsoft-pairs-with-nvidia-on-x-box-gaming-console-2/)
- **2000-04-19 — Microsoft advance received.** NVDA said it had received the $200 million contractual advance, converting the Xbox headline into funded development economics. Publication date: 2000-04-19. [EE Times](https://www.eetimes.com/nvidia-receives-200-million-advance-payment-from-microsoft/)
- **2000-04-26 — GeForce2 GTS launched.** The high-end NV15/GeForce2 GTS introduced per-pixel shading and was positioned at roughly three times the prior GeForce rendering performance. Publication date: 2000-04-26. [PC Watch](https://pc.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/article/20000426/winhec05.htm)
- **2000-05-16 — fiscal Q1 results and 2-for-1 split.** NVDA reported $148.5 million revenue, up 109% year over year and 15.6% sequentially, and $18.3 million net income, while authorizing a split. Publication date: 2000-05-16. [EDN](https://www.edn.com/nvidia-reaps-109-revenue-increase-in-fiscal-q1-splits-stock/)
- **2000-06-14 — Q1 Form 10-Q filed.** This is the filing anchor for the results in the preceding event. The local earnings-8-K table has no 2000 row because earnings 8-K coverage for NVDA begins in 2004; `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` likewise begins in 2020. Filing date: 2000-06-14. [SEC's later order identifies the original filing date and reported metrics](https://www.sec.gov/enforcement-litigation/administrative-proceedings/34-48480)
- **2000-06-28 — GeForce2 MX launched.** NVDA moved the architecture into mainstream home/business PCs around a $119 board price, with NEC and Fujitsu Siemens design wins. Publication date: 2000-06-28. [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/nvidia-launches-a-value-3d-chip/1100-2596187/)
- **2000-07-26 — Quadro2 family announcement reported.** Quadro2 Pro extended GeForce2-generation technology into professional workstations. Publication date: 2000-07-26. [contemporaneous product report](https://www.hartware.de/2000/07/26/nvidia-stellt-quadro2-familie-vor/)
- **2000-08-21 — fiscal Q2 results.** Historical earnings metadata records the publication date and $170.4 million reported revenue, but a stable contemporaneous release URL was not recovered; this event is therefore lower-confidence and is not used as a primary causal anchor. Publication date: 2000-08-21. [historical earnings record](https://www.earningslabs.com/stock/NVDA/earnings)

No 2000 anchors were found in `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` or `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`; that absence is a repository coverage fact, not evidence that no events occurred.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** After the Xbox surge, NVDA fell 53.9% from its trailing high by 2000-04-14, then rebuilt for roughly two months. This was a volatile V-shaped reset, not a quiet low-volatility base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Xbox funding, GeForce2 GTS, a 109% reported revenue increase and split, then MX/Quadro product breadth provided distinct public opportunities to expand the model.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** On t0, trailing excess was +30.64 pp over 21 sessions and +102.90 pp over 42 sessions versus XLK, with a new 63-day high.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 dollar-volume z21 was 3.50 in the episode census (3.64 on the Yahoo reconstruction), and the five-day/60-day ratio was 1.64. However, the March Xbox impulse carried even larger dollar volume.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No historical options surface exists. Per-ticker options history begins in 2026-06; any assertion about 2000 gamma or call demand would be fabricated.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The local PIT-gated surfaces do **not** cover the episode: `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet` begins with NVDA fiscal 2009 and an `asof_date` of 2010-05-31; `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet` begins at fiscal 2009 Q2; `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` begins in 2008; and the NVDA rows in `data/edgar/statements.parquet` are later annual history with a 2026 extraction `as_of`, not evidence available in 2000. Accordingly, all episode-era fundamentals below are filing/publication anchored externally, not presented as local PIT panel data.

Before the run, reported fiscal Q4 revenue was about $128.5 million. On 2000-05-16, NVDA reported fiscal Q1 revenue of $148.483 million, +109% year over year and +15.6% sequentially, with reported gross profit of $55.508 million (37.4% reported gross margin) and net income of $18.287 million. The 2000-06-14 filing was the public runway proof: GeForce demand was broadening, Xbox supplied a funded new platform, and the split increased trading accessibility. [EDN, 2000-05-16](https://www.edn.com/nvidia-reaps-109-revenue-increase-in-fiscal-q1-splits-stock/) [SEC order describing the 2000-06-14 filing, published 2003-09-11](https://www.sec.gov/enforcement-litigation/administrative-proceedings/34-48480)

There is a crucial later-discovered accounting qualification, but it cannot be used as a 2000 trading catalyst: in 2002 NVDA restated fiscal 2001 Q1 gross profit to $51.519 million, gross margin to 34.7%, and net income to $14.695 million. The SEC later said the originally reported gross profit was overstated 6.4% and net income 15.3%. This weakens the retrospective quality of the apparent margin inflection but was not public during the run and therefore did not cause the June–December 2000 failure. [restated 10-K filed 2002-04-29](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000101287002002262/d10k.htm) [SEC order published 2003-09-11](https://www.sec.gov/enforcement-litigation/administrative-proceedings/34-48480)

The durable runway was product cadence and OEM reach; the weak point was the price already paid for it. By t0 the stock had risen 219.9% YTD, while the next two public product launches did not push the close above the 2000-06-21 high. Exact contemporaneous consensus revenue/EPS series are unavailable, so the magnitude of estimate revision is null.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** The episode predates modern social-meme mechanics and had a multi-event commercial ladder; no local short-interest history is available, so a minor squeeze contribution cannot be measured.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** Xbox was the largest discrete repricing, but the June onset followed earnings, funding, product, and distribution events across three months and a multiweek advance.
- **sector_beta — likely.** Company-specific excess was enormous into t0, ruling out beta as the origin of the move; however, the failure phase coincided with the technology unwind, and by 126 sessions NVDA and XLK had fallen 44.49% and 33.56%, respectively. Sector/multiple compression was a major failure amplifier.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Historical options evidence is absent. The correct verdict is possible rather than ruled out because convexity cannot be tested, but no claim should be inferred from the 2026 snapshot.

Current hazards are context-only and not linked causally to 2000: **extension** is possible after any vertical move; **call-wall overhead** is present at $220 in the 2026-07-19 snapshot; and **catalyst exhaustion** is possible because current options and price surfaces do not supply a catalyst calendar. For the historical episode, extension and catalyst exhaustion were likely: t0 arrived after +220% YTD, and the stock peaked three sessions later.
