# NVDA 2015 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

NVIDIA's 2015 alpha was a two-engine platform rerating, not a one-headline forecast of the much later generative-AI cycle: Maxwell/GeForce and high-end PC gaming supplied the immediate revenue and earnings engine, while GRID, CUDA/deep learning, cloud GPU availability and DRIVE made that engine look reusable across several long-duration markets. The August 6 quarter showed that gaming and automotive demand plus broad platform adoption could offset the deliberate Icera modem exit and a SHIELD recall charge ([NVIDIA, published 2015-08-06](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-second-quarter-fiscal-2016)); Microsoft Azure then announced NVIDIA GPU instances on the mechanical breakaway date ([NVIDIA, published 2015-09-29](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-gpus-to-accelerate-microsoft-azure)); and the November quarter converted the narrative into record revenue, margin recovery, a strong next-quarter outlook and a larger capital-return commitment ([NVIDIA, published 2015-11-05](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-third-quarter-fiscal-2016-6622461)). Facebook's adoption of Tesla M40 for its Big Sur deep-learning system then supplied a named production customer ([NVIDIA, published 2015-12-10](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-gpus-power-facebook-s-new-deep-learning-machine)). The tape distinguishes the cash engine from the optionality: the August, November and February earnings gaps all held through 10 sessions, while the January 2016 DRIVE PX 2 gap failed all three hold tests. Capital therefore re-underwrote a profitable gaming company as a broader accelerated-computing platform; auto and AI widened duration, but repeated financial delivery sustained the move.

This was optionality, not yet a financial inflection. [NVIDIA, published 2015-01-04](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-paves-way-for-tomorrow-s-cars-with-nvidia-drive-automotive-computers).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** Before the August 6 release, the prior 21 sessions spanned only 6.58%; even at mechanical t0, NVDA remained 36.43% below its October 2007 adjusted high. The August earnings gap then consolidated for seven weeks before the first simultaneous 63/126/252-session high.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Financial proof (August, November, February) alternated with distribution and customer proof (GRID, Azure, Facebook, Volvo), allowing each claim to be re-underwritten before the next arrived. The failed January auto gap shows the ladder was discriminating rather than a sequence of uniformly positive headlines.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 the 42-session excess versus XLK was +27.86pp; forward excess widened to +28.90pp at 63 sessions and +35.70pp at 126 sessions. Full-2015 excess was +61.65pp versus XLK and +65.88pp versus SPY.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0's z21 of 1.40 was moderate, then the post-Q3 session reached $897.66M with z60 5.92 and the post-Q4 session reached $782.11M with z60 4.18. Both gaps held for 10 sessions.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2015 per-ticker options history or trade-level NBBO exists in-repo. Current positioning begins in 2026-06 and cannot be projected backward; options amplification is therefore null for the episode.
