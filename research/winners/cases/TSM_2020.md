# TSM 2020 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

TSM's 2020 move was a scarcity-and-capability rerating, not a single-news spike: the company first showed that leading-edge demand and margins could absorb the Huawei shock, then Intel's disclosed 7nm delay made the value of an independent leading-edge foundry easier to underwrite, and subsequent 5nm, revenue, margin, and capital-spending disclosures kept replacing optionality with evidence. TSM reported Q2 revenue growth of 28.9% and a 53.0% gross margin on **2020-07-16** ([TSMC](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/2726)); Intel disclosed a roughly six-month shift in its 7nm product timing on **2020-07-23** ([Intel](https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1402/intel-reports-second-quarter-2020-financial-results)); and TSM later paired a 5nm ramp with a US$25-28 billion 2021 capital budget on **2021-01-14** ([TSMC](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/2781)). The tape is more qualified than the headline return: TSM beat SPY by 57.49 percentage points through 126 sessions and still led it by 19.68 points after 252 sessions, but trailed SOXX by 2.91 points at 252 sessions. The autopsy therefore finds a real company-specific rerating whose sector-relative alpha was large through the visible run but was not fully sustained after the semiconductor cohort caught up.
