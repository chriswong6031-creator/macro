# TSM 2020 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

TSM's 2020 alpha was not simply pandemic semiconductor beta and not a one-session Intel sympathy move. The prior was already being revised by two quarters of resilient revenue, expanding margins, an N5 volume-production ramp, higher capital intensity, and an Arizona commitment that made TSMC more strategically legible to U.S. customers. The decisive re-underwriting came when Intel publicly disclosed on 2020-07-23 that its 7nm product timing had slipped and process yield was roughly twelve months behind its internal target: TSMC was suddenly priced less as one strong foundry inside a favorable cycle and more as scarce leading-edge manufacturing infrastructure with an enlarged potential customer and share runway. The tape fits that mosaic: by 2020-07-24 TSM had gained 30.61% in 21 sessions and 46.50% in 42, beating SPY by 25.09 and 38.74 percentage points and SOXX by 25.78 and 34.20 points, while roughly $3.05 billion of ADR dollar volume printed at a 3.94 trailing-21-session z-score. The move did sustain through year-end, but its marginal driver evolved from the Intel/node-lead shock into delivered N5/7nm growth and broad digital demand; that makes the durable mechanism a platform re-rating with cyclical support, not a permanent straight-line Intel revenue transfer.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

Repo filing anchors: **null**. `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` and `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` contain zero TSM rows, and this checkout does not provide a TSM event history in those surfaces; public company releases therefore anchor the ladder.

- **2020-04-16 — earnings/guidance.** TSMC reported Q1 revenue of US$10.31B, gross margin of 51.8%, and operating margin of 41.4%, all above the top of its prior guidance ranges; it guided Q2 revenue to US$10.1–10.4B despite the first pandemic shock. Publication date: 2020-04-16. [TSMC Q1 results](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2020/q1).
- **2020-05-15 — capital commitment/geopolitical de-risking.** TSMC announced its intention to build a 5nm Arizona fab with 20,000 wafers/month capacity and approximately US$12B of spending over 2021–2029. This did not create near-term earnings, but it made leading-edge U.S. capacity and government alignment explicit. Publication date: 2020-05-15. [TSMC release](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/2033).
- **2020-07-16 — earnings/guidance.** Q2 revenue was US$10.38B, gross margin 53.0%, and operating margin 42.2%; Q3 revenue guidance stepped up to US$11.2–11.5B, supported by demand for 5nm and 7nm. Publication date: 2020-07-16. [TSMC Q2 results](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2020/q2).
- **2020-07-23 — competitor process failure / runway expansion.** Intel disclosed that its 7nm CPU timing was shifting about six months and that process yield was trending roughly twelve months behind its internal target. The public call also introduced third-party manufacturing contingency speculation; the durable fact at publication was Intel's process slippage, while any TSMC award remained unannounced. Publication timestamp: 2020-07-23 4:00 p.m. EDT. [Intel Q2 release](https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1402/intel-reports-second-quarter-2020-financial-results).
- **2020-07-24 — market interpretation.** Contemporary coverage connected TSM's Friday ADR surge to Intel's disclosure and the prospect that AMD's gains would enlarge TSMC's addressable manufacturing demand. Publication timestamp: 2020-07-24 1:07 p.m. EDT. [Motley Fool contemporaneous report](https://www.fool.com/investing/2020/07/24/why-taiwan-semiconductor-stock-jumped-today.aspx).
- **2020-07-27 — external-foundry optionality becomes the explicit narrative.** Contemporary coverage reported an additional >11% ADR move as investors considered TSMC a contender for Intel contingency production; the report carefully noted that Intel had not named a foundry. Publication timestamp: 2020-07-27 11:08 a.m. EDT. [TheStreet contemporaneous report](https://www.thestreet.com/technology/tsmc-tsm-stock-intel-intc-production-delays).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present, but shallow.** Before the final breakaway, TSM had already recovered from the March shock and made a 63-day high on 2020-06-05; compression is better described as a brief June digestion than a deep neglected base. The ADR was only +25.18% YTD at t0 despite two strong quarters.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Q1 resilience, Arizona strategic alignment, Q2 beat/guide-up, and Intel's process slip successively changed earnings visibility, geopolitical framing, and the perceived leading-edge runway.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0, TSM led SOXX by +25.78pp over 21 sessions and +34.20pp over 42, while closing at a 252-session high.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** Roughly $3.05B traded on t0 at z21 3.94; the following session was the largest dollar-volume day of 2020 at $3.32B. This confirms capacity and attention, not investor identity or direction.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2020 per-ticker options history exists. Repo history begins in 2026-06 and current TSM snapshots are also absent.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The repo's PIT-gated fundamental surfaces have **no TSM coverage**: zero rows in `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet`. Accordingly, the following figures come from publication-dated TSMC materials and are not represented as repo-derived PIT factors.

Before the run, Q1 2020 already showed operational resilience: US$10.31B revenue, 51.8% gross margin, and 41.4% operating margin, versus prior guidance of US$10.2–10.3B, 48.5–50.5%, and 37.5–39.5%. Publication date: 2020-04-16. [TSMC Q1 results](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2020/q1).

During the run, Q2 revenue reached US$10.38B, up 28.9% year over year; gross margin expanded to 53.0% and operating margin to 42.2%. The Q3 revenue guide of US$11.2–11.5B implied roughly 9% sequential growth at midpoint, although guided margins of 50–52% acknowledged ramp dilution. Publication date: 2020-07-16. [TSMC Q2 results and release](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2020/q2).

Runway rested on technology mix and capacity, not merely unit recovery. Later-reported full-year evidence—**not available at t0 and used only to assess sustainment**—showed 2020 revenue up 25.2%, gross margin at 53.1% versus 46.0% in 2019, operating margin at 42.3% versus 34.8%, advanced technologies at 58% of wafer revenue versus 50%, and N5 entering volume production in Q2 before a strong second-half ramp. Publication year: 2021 annual-report cycle. [TSMC 2020 annual report](https://investor.tsmc.com/static/annualReports/2020/english/index.html). This later evidence explains why the re-rating held; it must not be treated as onset-day knowledge.

## 7. Research echo

At the breakaway, research appears to have been **catching the tape rather than leading it**. A historical ratings log records Credit Suisse moving TSM from Neutral to Outperform on 2020-07-27, after the 2020-07-24 onset and concurrent with another double-digit ADR move; the log does not expose a reliable contemporaneous target level, so consensus-versus-spot and target upside are **null** rather than backfilled. Publication/record date: 2020-07-27. [EarningsAhead historical action log](https://history.earningsahead.com/upgrades/tsm/).

The defensible “price escaped the published model” read is qualitative: TSM closed at $73.90 on 2020-07-24 and $83.25 on 2020-07-27 in local data while the notable rating change arrived on July 27. Contemporary reporting described the Monday move as >11% and driven by a newly considered Intel contingency-manufacturing opportunity, not an already contracted revenue stream. Publication timestamp: 2020-07-27 11:08 a.m. EDT. [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/technology/tsmc-tsm-stock-intel-intc-production-delays). Repo source for spot: `data/yahoo/TSM.parquet`. Because the repo has no analyst-target data and the accessible historical sources do not establish a complete consensus series, no claim is made about average target, dispersion, or exact upside.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** The move had company-reported earnings, an identifiable competitor process disclosure, repeated multi-billion-dollar volume, and follow-through; no local short-interest or social data is needed to explain it.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** TSM was already +30.61% over 21 sessions at t0 and produced multiple new highs before and after July 24; Intel accelerated an existing ladder rather than creating the entire move.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** SOXX rose only 4.84% over the same 21 sessions and 12.31% over 42, leaving TSM +25.78pp and +34.20pp ahead.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Historical options evidence is structurally unavailable, so an options amplifier cannot be excluded; it also is not required to explain the cash-tape and catalyst evidence.

Current hazards as of the local 2026-07-17 bar are deliberately separated from the 2020 autopsy. **Extension: possible**—TSM was +69.55% over 252 sessions but 16.58% below its trailing-252-session closing high, so it is not at a fresh vertical peak. **Call walls: unknown** because all current TSM options surfaces are absent. **Catalyst exhaustion: possible** for the historical episode—the July 2020 Intel surprise has long since entered the public model, so any present thesis requires a new catalyst and fundamental set rather than recycling the old shock. Source: `data/yahoo/TSM.parquet` and the options paths listed in section 6.
