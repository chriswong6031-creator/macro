# EME 2024 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

EMCOR's 2024 alpha was a serial earnings-and-margin re-underwriting of a specialty contractor with unusually visible demand, not an Industrials beta move or a one-day squeeze: the 2024-02-28 release paired record 2023 revenue and EPS with $8.85 billion of remaining performance obligations (RPO), initial 2024 EPS guidance of $14–$15, and a dividend increase; the next three reports then raised the earnings range to $15.50–$16.50, $19–$20, and $20.50–$21 as revenue, operating margin, and RPO kept advancing. The tape had already begun anticipating improvement in January, but the February print turned it into a relative breakaway (+30.32 percentage points over XLI in 21 sessions), exceptional dollar volume confirmed that institutions could re-size positions, and continued beat-and-raise execution sustained the move to +111.27% for calendar 2024 versus +17.31% for XLI and +24.89% for SPY. The mosaic is therefore execution leverage plus backlog visibility plus repeated estimate resets, with buybacks helping the per-share bridge; it is not adequately explained by a data-center headline alone, and the absent pre-run compression makes this a less canonical five-stage case.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** On 2024-01-26 the close was only 0.62% below its available 252-day high; EME had already made a 63-day high on 2024-01-11 and returned 37.5% over the 126 sessions into t0. This was acceleration from strength, not release from a stale base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Four dated earnings releases repeatedly lifted the revenue/EPS/RPO model, with a $500 million repurchase increase between Q1 and Q2.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At 2024-02-28 EME exceeded XLI by 30.32pp over 21 sessions and 35.67pp over 42 sessions, with a new 63-day close.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 dollar volume was about $321 million at +2.29σ/21d; later sessions reached roughly $496 million and $1.68 billion. This supports capacity and attention, not a claim about buyer identity.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** Per-ticker options history begins 2026-06, so no 2024 IV/GEX/OI history exists in the permitted surfaces.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** The move had four earnings/guidance rungs, improving reported margins, and multi-month follow-through; no local evidence establishes a meme mechanism or low-float squeeze.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** EME added 14.65% over 21 sessions and 29.05% over 63 sessions after t0, then re-accelerated on later reports.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** T0 excess was +30.32pp/21d and +35.67pp/42d versus XLI; calendar-2024 excess reached +93.96pp.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Historical options evidence is structurally unavailable. Options cannot explain the episode from the permitted data, but their contribution cannot be ruled out.

Current hazards as of the 2026-07-18 snapshot are **extension** (spot and business expectations are far above the 2024 starting point), **call-wall overhead** ($780, about 4.8% above snapshot spot), and **catalyst exhaustion** (possible after a long sequence of beat-and-raise quarters). These are current context only, not retroactive explanations of 2024. The present options surface is assumption-based and should not be mistaken for historical convexity.
