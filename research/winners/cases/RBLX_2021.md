# RBLX 2021 — failed breakaway autopsy

This is a FAILED breakaway case (case_type: failed_breakaway).

## 1. Bottom line

Roblox's November 2021 gap looked like a platform rerating but died because the lead economic indicator did not sustain the headline growth. Q3 revenue doubled and engagement remained strong, producing a 42% one-day cash repricing, yet bookings growth had already slowed to 28%; by the 2022-02-15 publication, January bookings growth was only 2%-3% and average bookings per DAU had fallen 22%-23% year over year ([Q3 release, published 2021-11-08](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2021/Roblox-Reports-Third-Quarter-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx); [Q4 release, published 2022-02-15](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). Reopening and difficult pandemic comparisons exposed that users and hours could grow while monetization per user compressed. The gap held ten sessions, but liquidity concentrated near the top, follow-on monthly data weakened, and RBLX underperformed both the gaming cohort and SPY badly by 63 and 126 sessions. This is what breakaway death looks like when revenue recognition lags a deteriorating bookings engine.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2021-11-08 — earnings:** Q3 revenue was $509.3M, up 102%; bookings were $637.8M, up 28%; DAUs rose 31%; and free cash flow was $170.6M ([company release, published 2021-11-08](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2021/Roblox-Reports-Third-Quarter-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx)).
- **2021-12-15 — operating metrics:** November DAUs rose 35% and hours 32%, but bookings growth slowed to 22%-24% and average bookings per DAU fell 8%-9% ([company release, published 2021-12-15](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2021/Roblox-Reports-November-2021-Key-Metrics/default.aspx)).
- **2022-02-15 — earnings/death confirmation:** Q4 bookings grew 20%, but January bookings grew only 2%-3% and January average bookings per DAU fell 22%-23% ([company release, published 2022-02-15](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** RBLX was a recent listing with limited price history and had pulled back from its June high before the Q3 gap (`data/yahoo/RBLX.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — false after first rung.** Q3 created the breakaway, but November metrics and Q4/January data progressively weakened the monetization thesis rather than reinforcing it.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed.** Excess versus ESPO was +11.48pp at 21 sessions but -23.88pp at 63 and -39.66pp at 126.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present initially, then distributive.** More than $36B of aggregate dollar volume printed across the five largest early sessions, but one of the peaks was a double-digit down day.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No episode-timely trade-level options history or open-interest surface is available.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

At the breakaway, Q3 revenue was $509.3M, bookings $637.8M, net loss attributable to common holders $74.0M, operating cash flow $181.2M and free cash flow $170.6M ([company release, published 2021-11-08](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2021/Roblox-Reports-Third-Quarter-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). Those cash figures made the initial reaction more than pure narrative. Yet bookings—the closer measure of current Robux demand—grew much more slowly than recognized revenue.

Q4 revenue rose 83% to $568.8M, bookings rose 20% to $770.1M and free cash flow was $77.3M; January 2022 then exposed the break: bookings growth slowed to 2%-3% while ABPDAU fell 22%-23% ([company release, published 2022-02-15](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2022/Roblox-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). The failure mechanism was monetization deceleration under reopening comparisons, compounded by a high-growth valuation reset—not a cash-runway crisis. Coverage gap: normalized local SEC quarterly statements are absent for RBLX, so company releases are used.

## 6. Options context

Historical options context is **unknown**. No local 2021-22 RBLX trade-level options dataset supports a signed-flow or dealer-position claim. The large gap and recent-listing volatility make convexity amplification possible, but cash volume alone cannot identify it. Options are therefore a missing stage and a false-positive hazard, not an explanatory fact.

## 7. Research echo

Research remained above spot even as estimates reset. On 2022-02-18, Needham reportedly cut its target from $136 to $83 and BTIG from $133 to $99 ([Benzinga, published 2022-02-18](https://www.benzinga.com/news/earnings/22/02/25724947/why-roblox-shares-are-trading-lower-today)). RBLX had closed at $53.87 on 2022-02-16 (`data/yahoo/RBLX.parquet`), so the published models still sat far above a tape already discounting deeper bookings decay. Full PIT consensus is unavailable; this is a “models chased down but not fast enough” failure echo.

## 8. Ownership context (context-only)

**Context-only — descriptive background, explicitly not a signal.** Local RBLX 13F rows begin with report periods after the breakaway and were first observed in this checkout in 2026. Under the local PIT rule, they cannot be projected backward. Episode-timely holder breadth and insider transactions are unknown; no part of the failure is assigned to institutional selling without dated filings.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible:** recent-listing scarcity and metaverse enthusiasm may have amplified the gap, but Q3 supplied real operating evidence.
- **one_day_binary — likely:** one earnings shock created most of the upside before subsequent data reversed the thesis.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out:** RBLX lagged ESPO by -39.66pp at 126 sessions, so sector weakness explains only part of death.
- **options_mirage — possible:** episode options evidence is absent and the vertical gap could have been convexity-amplified.
- **Current hazards:** `pandemic_pull_forward`, `bookings_deceleration`, `abpdau_compression`, `recent_listing`, `liquidity_distribution`, `growth_multiple_reset`.
