# FCX 2020 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

FCX's late-2020 alpha was a **copper-cycle re-underwriting with company-specific operating torque**, not a lone headline and not merely XLB beta. Vaccine-era reopening expectations and already-recovering Chinese industry pushed copper toward a seven-year high, but FCX also arrived with more earnings convexity than the sector ETF: the Grasberg underground ramp was lifting copper and gold volumes while unit cash costs fell, the Americas were restoring output, and Q3 converted that forecast into reported margin and cash-flow evidence ([FCX Q3 release, published 2020-10-22](https://s22.q4cdn.com/529358580/files/doc_news/2020/FCX_201022_3Q_2020_Earnings_Release.pdf); [China NBS, published 2020-11-16](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202011/t20201116_1803044.html); [copper-market report, published 2020-11-25](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40034971/vaccines-china-demand-and-dollar-drive-copper-to-30-month-peak)). The market could therefore raise both its commodity-price deck and FCX's volume/margin assumptions at once. Kisanfu sale proceeds, Q4 execution, a restored dividend/payout framework and Q1 2021 results then shifted the debate from survival to cash allocation ([Kisanfu filing, filed 2020-12-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/831259/000083125920000042/exhibit991-12142020.htm); [payout policy, published 2021-02-02](https://investors.fcx.com/investors/news-releases/news-release-details/2021/Freeport-McMoRan-Announces-Reinstatement-of-Common-Stock-Dividend-and-Adoption-of-Performance-Based-Payout-Policy/default.aspx)). That ladder explains why FCX beat XLB by 62.42 percentage points over 126 forward sessions and still by 44.18 points after 252: the alpha sustained, although the largest turnover day at the May copper-record climax was a down day and marked late-stage two-sidedness.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** FCX had already triggered a separate mechanical episode on 2020-06-10, was +154.24% over the 126 sessions into t0 and had made strict 252-day highs from the October 22 earnings date. The operating prior was depressed, but the tape prior was not compressed.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Pandemic cost reset → above-plan Q2 execution → Q3 margin/cash-flow inflection → vaccine/China/copper repricing → asset-sale cash → Q4/Q1 proof and a payout framework formed independent public rungs.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 FCX was +21.09 pp/+35.63 pp versus XLB over 21/42 sessions and at a strict 63/126/252-day closing high; excess remained +62.42 pp after 126 forward sessions.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 turnover was $829.75m with z21/z60 2.78/3.96, several later sessions exceeded $1.4bn, and every qualifying 5% upside gap held through ten sessions. The May climax turned liquidity two-sided.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** Per-ticker options history begins in 2026-06. No 2020 trade-level options/NBBO history exists, so episode convexity and signed flow direction are null.
