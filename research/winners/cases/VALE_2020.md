# VALE 2020 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

VALE's apparent 2020 alpha was a real operating re-underwrite wrapped inside an even larger iron-ore cycle: sequential revenue, gross margin, EBITDA, cash conversion and leverage improved as China steel demand met constrained seaborne supply; Vale then added dividends, capacity projects, operating records and liability/logistics milestones, giving capital multiple reasons to raise the cash-flow path rather than trade one headline. The tape confirms a durable break from SPY and broad Materials, but not a durable break from the narrow metals-and-mining cohort: at the 2020-11-25 detector date VALE led XME by only 8.61 percentage points over 21 sessions and 9.77 points over 42, fell 2.46 points behind it over the next 63 sessions, and led it by just 2.38 points over the next 126. The stock nevertheless led SPY by 40.69 points through 126 forward sessions before losing 26.43 points over 252, as the iron-ore price regime reversed. In mosaic terms, the episode was company-specific earnings/cash-flow proof plus payout and de-risking optionality, amplified by sector beta; it produced large index alpha, but failed to sustain large alpha versus its own narrow cohort. Local evidence: `data/yahoo/VALE.parquet`, `data/yahoo/XME.parquet`, `data/yahoo/XLB.parquet`, `data/yahoo/SPY.parquet`, and `data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`. Public operating anchors were published [2020-10-28](https://vale.com/w/3q20-financial-results) and [2021-02-25](https://saladeimprensa.vale.com/w/2020-financial-results); the late-cycle iron-ore record was reported [2021-05-07](https://au.investing.com/news/commodities-news/update-1iron-ore-steel-sizzle-as-china-demand-drives-record-rally-2346935), and the subsequent China steel-output constraint was reported [2021-07-09](https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/currency/UNITED-STATES-DOLLAR-B-CH-35467117/news/China-iron-ore-futures-set-for-4th-week-of-losses-on-steel-output-control-worries-35823493/).
