# SMMT 2024 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Summit Therapeutics' 2024 run was a cancer-asset re-underwriting, not a plain biotech squeeze. A small-cap prior around a single licensed PD-1/VEGF bispecific met public HARMONi-2 evidence that ivonescimab beat pembrolizumab in a head-to-head China Phase 3 setting, then the September WCLC data gave investors a second rung with quantitative PFS detail. The local tape shows a true breakaway: by 2024-09-13, SMMT had outperformed XBI by +181.81pp over 21 trading days and +1,097.16pp YTD.
