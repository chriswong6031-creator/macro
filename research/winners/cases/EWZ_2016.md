# EWZ 2016 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

EWZ's 2016 alpha was a three-engine country rerating: Brazil-specific political odds shifted from continued policy paralysis toward a change of government and a more orthodox fiscal/monetary team; the global backdrop simultaneously turned from dollar/commodity liquidation toward a gentler Fed path, firmer oil and iron ore, and renewed emerging-market demand; and the Brazilian real then translated the local-equity rebound into a much larger U.S.-dollar return. That three-part contemporaneous read was already public by mid-March. [World Economic Forum/Financial Times, published 2016-03-15](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2016/03/whats-driving-brazil-s-currency-rally/). The breakaway therefore did not price an improvement already visible in GDP—the economy was still contracting—but a reduction in the probability of the worst policy path, from a deeply depressed starting point. The later confirmation of Ilan Goldfajn, progress on a federal spending cap, the first Selic reduction, and large gains in state-linked, bank, mining, and energy shares made that expectation less hypothetical. [Reuters, published 2016-06-07](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/brazil%27s-senate-confirms-ilan-goldfajn-as-new-central-bank-chief-407070); [Banco Central do Brasil, published 2016-10-19](https://bcb.gov.br/en/monetarypolicy/copomstatements/2202); [Reuters via UOL, published 2016-12-29](https://noticias.uol.com.br/ultimas-noticias/reuters/2016/12/29/bovespa-sobe-quase-40-em-2016-apos-tres-anos-em-queda-na-maior-alta-desde-2009.htm). Liquidity, repeated gap retention, and progressively longer-window highs show that the move was re-underwritten in stages rather than exhausted on the first impeachment headline. Commodity and broad emerging-market beta mattered, but EWZ's separation from ILF, EEM, and SPY, plus a roughly 21.7% real appreciation from `t0` to the visible September peak, make the Brazil regime/currency channel the residual that explains the unusually large alpha.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

The local filing check is an informative null: EWZ has zero rows in both [`data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet) and [`data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet). As an ETF, it had no operating-company 8-K ladder. The relevant public tape was sovereign politics, macro policy, currency, commodities, and the earnings sensitivity of the underlying market.

- **2016-01-20 — local policy floor.** Banco Central do Brasil published its decision to leave Selic at 14.25%, declining to add further restraint during a severe contraction. It did not repair growth, but it fixed a high-carry starting point and removed an immediate additional hike. [Banco Central do Brasil, published 2016-01-20](https://www.bcb.gov.br/detalhenoticia/15423/nota).
- **2016-03-03 — the washout was quantified.** At 12:18 local time, IBGE reported that 2015 GDP had contracted 3.8%, Q4 had fallen 1.4% sequentially, industry had declined 6.2% for the year, and fixed investment had fallen 14.1%. This was not a positive growth catalyst; it documented how depressed the prior had become. [IBGE, published 2016-03-03](https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/agencia-sala-de-imprensa/2013-agencia-de-noticias/releases/9610-em-2015-pib-cai-3-8-e-totaliza-r-5-9-trilhoes).
- **2016-03-03 — political odds abruptly changed at `t0`.** Reuters reported allegations that Senator Delcídio do Amaral's testimony linked President Rousseff and former president Lula to interference in the Petrobras investigation. The same report recorded a 5.4% Bovespa jump and more than 2% real appreciation as markets priced a higher probability of a change in government. The allegations were contested and not treated here as established fact; their market effect came from changed probabilities. [Reuters via MarketScreener, published 2016-03-03](https://www.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/PETROBRAS-6496795/news/Petroleo-Brasileiro-Petrobras-Brazil-s-Rousseff-attacks-leaks-after-corruption-allegations-21955970/).
- **2016-03-04 — the Car Wash investigation reached Lula directly.** Federal police detained Lula for questioning; Reuters reported a 4% Bovespa gain led by a 10% rise in Petrobras as the episode's first major follow-through gap held. [Reuters via Investing.com, published 2016-03-04](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/brazil%27s-lula-detained-in-corruption-probe%3B-rousseff-objects-387955).
- **2016-03-15 — the macro bundle became legible.** A contemporaneous Financial Times article republished by the World Economic Forum documented a 10.8% one-month real rebound, a 46% year-to-date iron-ore increase, a gentler expected Fed path, and impeachment expectations as overlapping drivers. This is the clearest public mosaic evidence that the move was not politics alone. [World Economic Forum/Financial Times, published 2016-03-15](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2016/03/whats-driving-brazil-s-currency-rally/).
- **2016-03-16 — the global discount-rate rung.** The FOMC held the federal-funds range at 0.25%–0.50%, described policy as accommodative, emphasized global financial risks, and said future increases would be gradual. This reinforced the weaker-dollar/emerging-market channel into the next session's 7.53% EWZ opening gap. [Federal Reserve, published 2016-03-16](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20160316a.htm).
- **2016-03-17 — the local political rung reinforced the Fed impulse.** Reuters reported that released Lula–Rousseff calls intensified the political crisis and that the Chamber would install its impeachment commission; Petrobras was sharply higher with oil also firm. EWZ's $1.496 billion turnover was the episode's largest through the September peak, and the gap remained above its pre-gap close after 3, 5, and 10 sessions. [Reuters via Investing.com, published 2016-03-17](https://br.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/ibovespa-salta-4-com-agravamento-de-crise-politica-170403).
- **2016-03-30 — the governing coalition fractured.** TIME reported the PMDB's decision to leave Rousseff's coalition, increasing the probability that impeachment could advance and that Vice President Michel Temer could take office. ## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present, but not a neat low-volatility base.** EWZ was still 34.27% below its prior 252-session high at `t0`, and contemporaneous research said it had lost more than half its value since the start of 2014. [ETF.com, published 2016-04-01](https://www.etf.com/sections/news/brazil-etf-prime-opportunity-or-trap). The immediate launch was a violent V-bottom—up 36.99% from 2016-01-21 to `t0`—rather than quiet compression.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Investigation disclosures changed impeachment odds; formal votes converted probability into government change; named economic officials, the spending cap, and the first Selic reduction converted political hope into an observable policy path. [Senado Federal, published 2016-08-31](https://www12.senado.leg.br/noticias/materias/2016/08/31/dilma-rousseff-perde-o-mandato-e-temer-e-confirmado-presidente); [Banco Central do Brasil, published 2016-10-19](https://bcb.gov.br/en/monetarypolicy/copomstatements/2202). Fed, dollar, oil, and iron ore were parallel rungs. [World Economic Forum/Financial Times, published 2016-03-15](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2016/03/whats-driving-brazil-s-currency-rally/).
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At `t0`, EWZ was 22.64 pp ahead of SPY over 21 sessions and made its first 63-session high. The first 126-session and 252-session highs followed on 2016-03-10 and 2016-07-14, respectively.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** `t0` dollar volume was z21 3.02/z60 3.88 with a 1.57 five-day/60-day ratio. The March 4 and March 17 sessions each exceeded $1.44 billion, and four of five measured 3% opening gaps remained above the pre-gap close at all 3/5/10-session checks.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2016 per-ticker options history, trade-level NBBO, or dealer-inventory record exists. The equity, FX, policy, and commodity evidence does not require an options explanation.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** EWZ is a broad, highly liquid country basket; the move aligned with local equities, the real, formal political votes, macro policy, and commodities. Short covering or speculative participation cannot be excluded, but a single-name float squeeze is not needed.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The March 4, March 17, April 8, and July 8 gaps held at 3/5/10 sessions, the 252-session high came on July 14, and policy delivery continued through December.
- **sector_beta — possible.** ILF, EEM, commodities, and global risk assets also rose, so beta was a real contributor. It does not fully explain EWZ's 21.60 pp excess over ILF or 37.59 pp excess over SPY from `t0` through 2016-09-07, but the Brazil-specific residual cannot be separated perfectly from shared Latin America/commodity exposure.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Historical options evidence is absent. Options could have amplified some gaps, but the documented equity, FX, policy, and commodity mechanism stands without that assumption.
- **Extension hazard — likely by the visible peak.** EWZ was up 48.35% from `t0` and 103.22% from the 2016-01-21 close by 2016-09-07. Even a durable country rerating contained substantial vertical-move and mean-reversion risk.
- **Call-wall hazard — unknown.** There is no historical or current EWZ GEX snapshot in the repo, so no strike or wall can be asserted.
- **Catalyst-exhaustion hazard — possible after 2016-08-31.** Final removal converted the main political binary from expectation to fact. Further persistence required difficult fiscal legislation, disinflation, rate reductions, coalition stability, and actual activity recovery.
- **Commodity/FX reversal hazard — likely structural.** A stronger dollar, weaker iron ore/oil, or renewed real depreciation could reverse two of the three return engines even if local reform expectations held.
