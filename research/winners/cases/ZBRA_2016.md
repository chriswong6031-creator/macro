# ZBRA 2016 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Zebra's 2016 alpha was a debt-financed-acquisition **turnaround rerating**, not a demand breakout and not a sector ride: after the Motorola Enterprise acquisition left roughly $3 billion of debt, integration expense, weak controls, and slowing sales, the May 2016 reset made expectations unusually low; the August quarter then showed that mix, services margin, product costs, operating expense, working capital, and debt reduction could lift earnings and cash generation even with revenue still slightly down. [Zebra Q1 release, published 2016-05-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/877212/000119312516584752/d185323dex991.htm); [Zebra Q2 release, published 2016-08-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/877212/000119312516675940/d208102dex991.htm). The first gap re-priced that self-help, but the edge nearly disappeared over the next 63 sessions as investors digested a WLAN divestiture and an accounting restatement. What made the move durable was a second public proof set on November 14–15—restated filings delivered on schedule, an Android enterprise-mobile launch addressed the product-cycle debate, Q3 adjusted EBITDA margin expanded, another $90 million of debt was retired, and a new CFO with large-integration experience arrived—followed by another loan repricing in December. [Zebra amended Q2 filing, filed 2016-11-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000877212/000087721216000025/zbra-20160702x10qa.htm); [Zebra Q3 release, published 2016-11-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/877212/000119312516768389/d261651dex991.htm); [Zebra CFO filing, filed 2016-11-15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/877212/000119312516768852/d290144d8k.htm); [Zebra loan-pricing release, published 2016-12-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/877212/000119312516790400/d305587dex991.htm). The tape matches that mosaic: +24.06 percentage points versus XLK over the 21 sessions into `t0`, only +2.48 points of forward 63-day excess during the digestion, then +23.83 and +40.55 points over 126 and 252 days as operating leverage and balance-sheet repair, rather than top-line acceleration, became credible.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

Local filing anchors were checked first. [`data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet) contains ZBRA earnings filings on 2016-02-25, 2016-05-10, 2016-08-09, 2016-11-01, and 2016-11-15. [`data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet) contains 2016 anchors on 2016-06-03, 2016-08-19, 2016-11-01, 2016-11-15, and 2016-12-12. The local material-event extractor omits the 2016-09-20 impairment filing and Form 10-K/A/10-Q/A amendments, so those are explicit coverage gaps rather than absent events.

- **2016-02-25 — earnings / strategy baseline.** Zebra published Q4 2015 results and framed 2016 around $50 million of incremental acquisition synergies, a 17%–18% adjusted EBITDA margin, and at least $300 million of debt reduction. It also disclosed $3.0 billion of long-term debt and only $102.6 million of 2015 operating cash flow, making execution and deleveraging the central underwriting questions. [Zebra 8-K exhibit, published and filed 2016-02-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/877212/000119312516476870/d149807dex991.htm).
- **2016-05-10 — earnings / expectation reset.** Q1 adjusted sales fell 5.5%, adjusted EBITDA margin declined to 15.5%, and non-GAAP EPS fell 28.4%; management reduced full-year sales guidance from +1%–4% to -3%–+1%. ZBRA closed down 17.77% that day. This was a negative rung, but it created the low bar against which August's mix and cost progress could be re-underwritten. [Zebra 8-K exhibit, published and filed 2016-05-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/877212/000119312516584752/d185323dex991.htm).
- **2016-06-02 — capital structure.** Zebra repriced its $1.96 billion term loan by 75 basis points and estimated about $14 million of annualized net interest savings, the first concrete proof that deleveraging could translate into EPS rather than remain a presentation objective. [Zebra 8-K exhibit, published 2016-06-02; filing accepted 2016-06-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/877212/000119312516611466/d206117dex991.htm).
- **2016-08-09 — earnings / `t0`.** Q2 sales fell 1.2%, but gross margin rose 200 basis points, adjusted EBITDA rose 9.9%, adjusted EBITDA margin rose 170 basis points to 16.3%, and non-GAAP EPS rose 30.1% to $1.34. The result exceeded management's May EPS range of $1.00–$1.20 and margin range of 15%–16% while full-year guidance was maintained; first-half operating cash flow reached $122 million and $145 million of term-loan principal had been paid. [Zebra 8-K exhibit, published and filed 2016-08-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/877212/000119312516675940/d208102dex991.htm).
- **2016-08-19 — management/control capacity.** Zebra filed that Colleen O'Sullivan had become Chief Accounting Officer effective August 15, replacing the CFO's interim principal-accounting role. The filing did not eliminate the later control problem, but it documented added accounting capacity before the restatement surfaced. [Zebra 8-K, filed 2016-08-19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/877212/000119312516685601/d242089d8k.htm).
- **2016-09-14 — portfolio focus.** The definitive $55 million WLAN divestiture separated a non-core piece inherited with Motorola Enterprise; contemporaneous coverage identified the assets and the acquisition price. The later impairment charge made this economically mixed, but the action sharpened the core mobile-computing/data-capture story and provided another source of debt reduction. [Network World, published 2016-09-14](https://www.networkworld.com/article/955338/extreme-swallows-zebra-s-wlan-biz-for-55-million.html).
- **2016-10-31 — transaction completion.** Extreme Networks announced completion of the WLAN transaction, converting the September agreement into an executed portfolio change. [Extreme Networks, published 2016-10-31](https://investor.extremenetworks.com/news/news-details/2016/Extreme-Networks-Completes-Acquisition-of-the-Wireless-LAN-Business-from-Zebra-Technologies-10-31-2016/default.aspx).
- **2016-11-01 — accounting shock, bounded by operating guidance.** Zebra said its 2015 and first-half 2016 financial statements could not be relied on because of tax, accrual, and acquired-receivable errors. At the same time, it said there was no impact on current cash or future operations, expected Q3 adjusted EBITDA margin above its prior outlook, expected the midpoint of sales/EPS guidance, and disclosed another $90 million of Q3 debt reduction. This event interrupted the tape but did not remove the operating thesis. [Zebra 8-K and exhibit, filed/published 2016-11-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/877212/000119312516755300/d284671d8k.htm).

The amended Q2 filing made the restated first-half numbers public before Q3 results, reducing uncertainty about scope while leaving a material weakness to remediate. [Zebra Q2 10-Q/A, filed 2016-11-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000877212/000087721216000025/zbra-20160702x10qa.htm).

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** The move occurred in two filing-linked waves, carried institutional-capacity turnover, held the August gap, and persisted to +40.55 pp of 252-day sector excess. Social attention or short covering is not needed to explain it. Historical short-interest coverage is unavailable, so this is not a claim that no covering occurred.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The August gap held at 3/5/10 sessions; a second, larger turnover event arrived on November 15; the 252-session high did not arrive until December 5; and forward 126/252-day excess was strong.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** ZBRA was 24.06 pp ahead of XLK over the 21 sessions into onset and 34.18 pp ahead from onset to the 2016 high. SPY gives nearly the same residual. Broad technology helped the backdrop but cannot explain the magnitude or event timing.
- **options_mirage — possible.** No 2016 options history exists, so convexity amplification cannot be tested. The operating, filing, and equity-liquidity mechanism stands without it, but the missing history prevents a stronger verdict.
- **Episode-end extension — likely.** By 2016-12-20 ZBRA was 39.27% above `t0`, had crossed its prior 252-session high, and had moved above both auditable 2016 research targets. The run remained durable later, but the contemporaneous extension hazard was real.
- **Accounting/control hazard — likely.** The November restatement and continuing material weakness showed that integration complexity affected reporting reliability. On-time amended filings bounded the immediate scope; they did not erase the control risk.
- **Leverage hazard — likely.** Even after Q3 reductions, long-term debt was $2.788 billion against $163 million of cash. The thesis depended on cash conversion, financing access, and continued principal reduction.
- **Catalyst exhaustion — possible after December 7.** By then Q2/Q3 margin proof, the WLAN transaction, restatement completion, product launch, CFO change, and two loan repricings were public. Further persistence required product-cycle adoption, organic revenue stabilization, and more deleveraging.
- **Current short-term extension — possible, not long-term.** At the latest local close on 2026-07-17, ZBRA was up 12.64% over 21 sessions and only 1.93% below its 63-session high, but still 21.67% below its 252-session high. Source: [`data/yahoo/ZBRA.parquet`](../../../data/yahoo/ZBRA.parquet).
