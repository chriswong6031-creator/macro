# ALT 2021 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

ALT's 2021 alpha was a short-window probability rebound in an already-broken vaccine trade, not the start of a durable platform re-rating: after the December 2020 AdCOVID clinical hold, research still assigned large values to an intranasal COVID vaccine and to ALT-801, Guggenheim coverage published at the detector turn put a $36 target against a $21.07 prior close, and the tape anticipated FDA clearance, trial enrollment, and a June human readout while XBI itself was near a cyclical peak ([TheStreet, published 2020-12-24](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/altimmune-alt-stock-covid-vaccine-fda-hold); [24/7 Wall St., published 2021-02-11](https://247wallst.com/investing/2021/02/11/top-analyst-upgrades-and-downgrades-allstate-bed-bath-beyond-simon-property-spirit-airlines-twitter-under-armour-and-more/)). Clearance and enrollment did arrive, but they improved execution timing rather than product efficacy; commercial-scale manufacturing commitments and repeated mouse results then increased the amount of success embedded in price without closing the preclinical-to-human gap ([Altimmune, published 2021-02-17](https://ir.altimmune.com/news-releases/news-release-details/altimmune-announces-fda-clearance-adcovidtm-ind-application); [Altimmune/SEC, filed 2021-03-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326190/000119312521078521/d113879d8k.htm)). The decisive contrast with a durable winner was that t0 had only a 63-day high and five-day turnover confirmation—no 126-day or 252-day high, no onset-day volume shock, no contemporaneous hard 8-K, no product revenue, and no human AdCOVID evidence. ALT peaked six sessions after t0; a mixed NasoShield human readout warned that intranasal antibody quality might not translate, and AdCOVID ultimately produced inadequate human immune responses and was discontinued, overwhelming a genuine but separate ALT-801 obesity result ([Altimmune, published 2021-04-01](https://ir.altimmune.com/news-releases/news-release-details/altimmune-reports-data-its-phase-1b-clinical-trial-nasoshieldtm); [Altimmune, published 2021-06-16](https://ir.altimmune.com/news-releases/news-release-details/altimmune-announces-positive-interim-data-alt-801-phase-1-trial); [Altimmune, published 2021-06-29 at 4:01 p.m. EDT](https://ir.altimmune.com/news-releases/news-release-details/altimmune-announces-update-adcovidtm-phase-1-clinical-trial)).

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`): the run did not sustain.**
