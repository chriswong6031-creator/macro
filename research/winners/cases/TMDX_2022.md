# TMDX 2022 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

TMDX's 2022 alpha was not a sector move or a single FDA headline: it was the first visible proof that three prior approvals could become a scaled service-and-device business through the National OCS Program (NOP). DCD Heart approval widened the usable donor pool, quarterly revenue accelerated from 125% to 151% to 378% year over year, NOP rose to roughly 90% of U.S. revenue, guidance was raised repeatedly, and an August equity raise removed the near-term financing constraint ([TransMedics, published 2022-04-28](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-receives-fda-pma-approval-ocstm-dcd-heart-indication/); [Q2 results, published 2022-08-01](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-reports-second-quarter-2022-financial-results/); [offering pricing, published 2022-08-04](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-announces-upsize-and-pricing-public-offering-common); [Q3 results, published 2022-11-03](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-reports-third-quarter-2022-financial-results)). The standardized 2022-08-25 onset nevertheless arrived after the market had already capitalized much of that re-underwriting: trailing excess was +39.23 percentage points versus XLV over 21 sessions and +64.53 over 42, while onset dollar-volume was only +0.24 standard deviations versus 21 days. Price then fell 25.15% in 21 sessions before a second earnings gap repaired the chart. Thus the episode generated extraordinary calendar-year alpha but failed the durable-winner path: `clean_hold=false`, `blow_off=true`, and 252-session excess decayed to +11.01 points. The failure was not that commercialization was imaginary; it was that a late, extended breakaway depended on serial upside revisions while operating cash flow remained negative and the business was still funding NOP scale.

This is a FAILED breakaway case (case_type: failed_breakaway).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present, but months before t0.** TMDX closed at $10.00 on 2022-02-24 and remained below $20 until mid-April; by the August 25 onset it had already risen more than fivefold from that close (`data/baskets/ohlcv/TMDX.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Liver evidence, DCD Heart approval, three consecutive commercial beats/guidance increases, debt funding, and an equity raise successively converted an approval story into a scale story. The ladder was unusually coherent, but its later rungs became necessary to repair prior drawdowns.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then unstable.** At t0 the census measured +39.23pp 21-day and +64.53pp 42-day excess versus XLV; forward excess was -18.35pp at 21 days before Q3 earnings restored the relative trend (`data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`).
- **Liquidity confirmation — partial.** The five-day/60-day dollar-volume ratio was 1.64, but onset z21 was only +0.24. The truly decisive liquidity days were earlier/later earnings gaps, not t0 (`data/baskets/ohlcv/TMDX.parquet`).

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The point-in-time annual baseline available at t0 is FY2021, `asof_date=2022-04-30`, in `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`: revenue **$30.262M**, gross profit **$21.159M** (69.92% gross margin), operating loss **$39.428M**, operating cash outflow **$28.864M**, long-term debt **$35.197M**, and net loss **$44.2M**. This was a high-margin consumables/device platform but not a self-funded one.

Publication-dated quarterly results show the inflection that the annual panel could not yet contain. Q1 revenue was $15.9M (+125%) with a $10.6M net loss; Q2 was $20.5M (+151%), 70% gross margin, and an $11.5M net loss; Q3 was $25.7M (+378%, or $24.3M excluding the estimate change), 71% reported gross margin, and a narrower $7.4M net loss ([Q1, published 2022-05-03](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-reports-first-quarter-2022-financial-results-0/); [Q2, published 2022-08-01](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-reports-second-quarter-2022-financial-results/); [Q3, published 2022-11-03](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-reports-third-quarter-2022-financial-results)). `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet`, gated by `asof_date`, records Q1 EPS of -$0.38 as of 2022-05-30 and Q2 EPS of -$0.41 as of 2022-08-29; the local EPS row therefore did not yet contain Q2 at the August 25 t0 despite the public August 1 release.

Runway changed materially during the move. Q2 cash, equivalents, and marketable securities were $58.1M, only about 2.0 years of simple coverage against FY2021 operating cash burn, before capex, working-capital changes, or higher scale spending; however, the August 1 $60M debt facility and August equity offering removed that immediate constraint ([Q2, published 2022-08-01](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-reports-second-quarter-2022-financial-results/); [offering price, published 2022-08-04](https://investors.transmedics.com/news-releases/news-release-details/transmedics-announces-upsize-and-pricing-public-offering-common)). By Q3 the company reported $204.5M cash. The later annual row, available only at `asof_date=2023-04-30`, shows FY2022 revenue **$93.459M**, gross profit **$65.272M** (69.84%), operating loss **$31.437M**, operating cash outflow **$45.817M**, and capex **$11.907M** (`data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`). Revenue and loss economics improved, but cash consumption and investment needs rose.
