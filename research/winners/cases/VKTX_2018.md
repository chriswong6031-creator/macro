# VKTX 2018 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Viking Therapeutics' 2018 alpha came from two different re-underwriting events that the tape briefly treated as one durable story: Madrigal's positive THR-beta NASH data on May 31 supplied peer read-through before Viking had reported its own result, then Viking's 45-patient VK2809 Phase 2 readout on September 18 showed large LDL-C and MRI-PDFF liver-fat reductions and caused a second vertical repricing. The mosaic was powerful but fragile. The June 14 mechanical onset occurred after the peer-sympathy gap, with ordinary onset-day dollar volume and no compact prior; the company had no revenue or margin base, the clinical evidence was still a small surrogate-stage study rather than biopsy-based NASH evidence, and Viking repeatedly issued equity into the rerating—most consequentially 9.5 million shares at $18.50 two days after the September data. The May gap held, but forward excess from the June onset was negative at 5, 21, 42, 63, and 126 sessions versus SPY; the September data gap held for 10 sessions but failed by 63, and VKTX fell 60.7% from the September 18 close to year-end. The public sequence and its limits are documented in Madrigal's [May 31, 2018 Phase 2 release](https://ir.madrigalpharma.com/news-releases/news-release-details/madrigals-mgl-3196-achieves-liver-biopsy-endpoints-patients-non), Viking's [May 9, 2018 pre-revenue Q1 release](https://ir.vikingtherapeutics.com/2018-05-09-Viking-Therapeutics-Reports-First-Quarter-2018-Financial-Results-and-Provides-Corporate-Update), Viking's [June 7, 2018 offering-pricing release](https://ir.vikingtherapeutics.com/2018-06-07-Viking-Therapeutics-Announces-Pricing-of-67-5-Million-Public-Offering-of-Common-Stock), its [September 18, 2018 VK2809 release](https://ir.vikingtherapeutics.com/2018-09-18-Viking-Therapeutics-Announces-Positive-Top-Line-Results-from-Phase-2-Study-of-VK2809-in-Patients-with-Non-Alcoholic-Fatty-Liver-Disease-NAFLD-and-Elevated-LDL-Cholesterol), and its [September 20, 2018 offering-pricing release](https://ir.vikingtherapeutics.com/2018-09-20-Viking-Therapeutics-Announces-Pricing-of-175-8-Million-Public-Offering-of-Common-Stock).

**Case classification: `failed_breakaway`. The run did not sustain.**

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** In the 21 sessions through May 30, closes ranged from $4.04 to $5.42, a 34.2% span; the 63-session span was 65.5%. The detector fired after a one-day peer-sympathy repricing, not out of a tight base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present, but discontinuous.** The ladder ran from peer read-through to enrollment and financing, then waited more than three months for Viking's own binary result. The later conference detail repeated the same study rather than introducing a new independent hard catalyst.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed.** At June 14, trailing excess was +145.99pp versus XBI and +148.93pp versus SPY over 21 sessions; forward excess was negative versus SPY at every requested horizon and breached the repo's failure threshold at 63 sessions.
- **Liquidity confirmation — absent at onset.** The May 31 and September 18 binary days each exceeded +4.3 z21, but June 14 itself was only -0.02 z21 and +0.50 z60. Liquidity was event-concentrated rather than recurring through the hold window.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** The repo's per-ticker options history begins in 2026-06; there is no 2018 contract-level history, and the public summary evidence does not supply trade-level NBBO needed to determine direction.

## 7. Research echo

Published research moved in three catch-up steps. On May 31, Maxim raised its target from $8 to $14 while VKTX traded at $8.61 intraday; the report explicitly reduced its VK2809 model risk from 90% to 50% because Madrigal's result supported the THR-beta class. [Maxim research, dated 2018-05-31](https://www.investorvillage.com/uploads/86385/files/VKTXMaximGroup05312018.pdf). On August 13, Zacks carried a $15 valuation at a $10.36 spot. [Zacks research, dated 2018-08-13](https://s27.q4cdn.com/906368049/files/News/2018/Zacks_SCR_Research_08132018_VKTX_Bautz.pdf). Before the September 18 open, the six-firm average target was $14.33 versus the prior $10.39 close; VKTX then closed at $19.46 and traded as high as $24, so price escaped the pre-data published model in one session. [Schaeffer's, published 2018-09-18 at 9:14 a.m. ET](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2018/09/18/option-bulls-cheer-viking-therapeutics-stock-explosion).

By September 24, Zacks had moved its valuation to $25 from the August $15 while spot was $18.20. [Zacks research, dated 2018-09-24](https://s27.q4cdn.com/906368049/files/News/2018/Zacks_SCR_Research_09242018_VKTX_Bautz.pdf). The research echo therefore confirms model catch-up, not a durable price floor: peer data first lowered risk assumptions, own data forced another target revision, and the tape still retraced sharply as the market confronted dilution, small-study evidence, and the long path to a biopsy-based development program. The repo contains no analyst-target dataset; all target observations here are external and publication-dated.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** A publication on the September data morning reported short interest above 36% of float and concentrated near-term calls, so covering and option-related hedging could have amplified the vertical move. There is no cited evidence of social coordination, and the clinical catalyst was real. [Schaeffer's, published 2018-09-18 at 9:14 a.m. ET](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2018/09/18/option-bulls-cheer-viking-therapeutics-stock-explosion).
- **one_day_binary — likely.** The June onset inherited its signal from the May 31 peer gap, while the September direct-data session supplied $1.006 billion of turnover and a 91.77% opening gap. Forward performance after the June onset was weak, and the September gap failed by 63 sessions.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** VKTX's 2018 return exceeded XBI by 103.70pp, and its trailing 21-day excess at onset was +145.99pp; the move was name/class specific rather than a biotech-sector drift.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Public reporting showed unusual 2018 positioning, but there is no trade-level NBBO history to determine direction or dealer hedge state. Price, clinical, and financing evidence stand independently of an options explanation.

Episode hazards are `peer_readthrough_dependency`, `one_day_binary`, `small_trial`, `surrogate_endpoint_risk`, `long_development_path`, `equity_dilution`, `short_squeeze_amplification`, and `catalyst_exhausted`. The repeated equity raises improved cash coverage but made share supply part of the mechanism; later detailed data did not create a new independent rung.

Current hazards are separate from the 2018 diagnosis. From `data/yahoo/VKTX.parquet`, the 2026-07-17 close was up 25.06% over 21 sessions but 11.99% below its July 7 closing high, so `extended_after_vertical_move` remains possible but is no longer at its peak. `site/gex/VKTX.json` shows `call_wall_overhead` at $40, 7.87% above spot, with a very-strong/hard label. `catalyst_exhausted` is **unknown** for the current company because the snapshot files do not establish the current clinical calendar.
