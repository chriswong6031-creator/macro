# NBIS 2025 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

NBIS's January 2025 alpha was a newly relisted, short-history AI-infrastructure story being valued on capacity and exit-rate revenue rather than current earnings: the November U.S. cluster plan, December $700 million NVIDIA/Accel/Orbis financing, and $750 million-$1 billion year-end-2025 ARR ambition converted post-Yandex cash into a credible GPU-cloud buildout. The move was real relative alpha and had broad dollar-volume confirmation, but it was not yet durable fundamental proof: the latest public quarter still showed a deeply loss-making, capex-heavy group, December ARR later proved below prior guidance, the stock had only 64 prior sessions under NBIS, and no sector cohort could be assigned. DeepSeek's January 27 efficiency shock therefore attacked the exact long-duration GPU-demand assumption supporting the multiple; NBIS fell 37.4% in one session on $1.16 billion of turnover, and a same-day service outage added company-specific execution risk. Strong February results briefly reclaimed the trigger, but the tape then failed again and reached 51.9% below the trigger by April 8. The later 2025 breakaways belong to separate episodes, not evidence that this January onset held.

**Format-contract classification: this is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`); the January run did not sustain.**

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present, but unusual:** trading had resumed only in October 2024 after the Russian-business divestment, leaving a cash-rich but newly defined company and just 64 prior sessions at t0. This was informational compression and short listing history, not a mature multi-quarter price base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present:** U.S. capacity, strategic financing, ambitious ARR guidance, and product releases progressively made the AI-cloud identity investable; however, much of the ladder was promise/capacity rather than reported utilization or margin.
- **Relative breakaway — present:** +53.24pp/21d and +99.27pp/42d versus SPY, plus the first strict 63-day closing high. Sector-relative evidence is null because the repository assigned no sector ETF.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, then adverse:** January 24 dollar volume was elevated and the five-day/60-day ratio was 1.61, but the next session's $1.16 billion downdraft overwhelmed it; later high-volume up days did not prevent the 63-day failure.
- **Options convexity — unknown:** per-ticker options history starts 2026-06. No 2025 options inference is possible from the repository, and current positioning cannot be projected backward.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

All four requested PIT stores—`data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet`—contain **zero NBIS rows**. Thus repo-PIT revenue, margin, cash runway and EPS are null. The gap likely reflects the ticker transition/foreign-filer mapping, but that is an inference; the observable fact is zero coverage.

The public information available before t0 was the October 31, 2024 Q3 release: group revenue was $43.3 million, up 1.7× sequentially; core cloud revenue was up 2.7× sequentially; September ARR exceeded $120 million; cash was $2.288 billion; nine-month capex was $387.6 million and nine-month operating cash outflow was $255.3 million. Publication: 2024-10-31. [Nebius Q3 release](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-group-n-v-announces-third-quarter-2024-financial-results). The December financing then added $700 million gross capital and raised the exit-ARR ambition.

The first during/following-run financial print, published February 20, showed the tension that the January price had outrun: Q4 revenue $37.9 million was lower sequentially than group Q3 revenue (mix and timing prevent treating that comparison as pure core-cloud deceleration), while Q4 adjusted EBITDA loss of $75.5 million equaled roughly -199% of revenue. FY2024 revenue was $117.5 million, adjusted EBITDA loss $266.4 million (about -227%), operating cash outflow $319.6 million, capex $808.1 million, and year-end cash $2.450 billion. Simple cash divided by FY operating outflow suggests ample operating runway, but it is misleading for a buildout whose FY capex alone was $808.1 million; cash less one year of FY operating outflow and capex is roughly $1.32 billion before growth, financing and working-capital changes. Publication: 2025-02-20. [Nebius FY2024 results](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-group-n-v-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2024-financial-results). In short: revenue growth and funding were real, margins were deeply negative, capex intensity was extreme, and the valuation depended on a large forward utilization ramp.
