# NBIS 2025 — failed breakaway winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

NBIS's January 2025 alpha was a scarcity-and-credibility rerating of a newly tradable former Yandex remnant into a cash-rich pure-play AI-infrastructure platform, not yet a rerating of demonstrated margins: the Nasdaq return exposed $2.29 billion of cash, $120 million of September cloud ARR and a planned infrastructure buildout; the Kansas City GPU cluster made the U.S. expansion tangible; and the $700 million placement at $21 with NVIDIA, Accel and Orbis raised year-end 2025 ARR ambition to $750 million-$1.0 billion ([Nebius, published 2024-10-17](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-group-announces-planned-resumption-of-trading-on-nasdaq-and-provides-investor-update); [Nebius, published 2024-10-31](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-group-n-v-announces-third-quarter-2024-financial-results); [Nebius, published 2024-11-19](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-expands-in-us-with-first-gpu-cluster-in-kansas-city-offices-in-san-francisco-dallas-and-new-york); [Nebius, published 2024-12-02](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-announces-oversubscribed-strategic-equity-financing-of-usd-700-million-to-accelerate-roll-out-of-full-stack-ai-infrastructure)). A January 13 research initiation and January 22 AI Studio release then helped carry the stock to $41.69 on January 24, +56.08% in 21 sessions, but the tape had no mature base and the operating proof lagged the price ([Insider Monkey, published 2025-01-13](https://www.insidermonkey.com/blog/10-firms-dominate-monday-upsurge-1423178/4); [Nebius, published 2025-01-22](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-ai-studio-introduces-one-of-the-most-cost-effective-suite-for-text-to-image-generation-with-leading-open-source-ai-models)). DeepSeek's challenge to the assumption that AI required ever more expensive compute produced a 37.44% next-session collapse; February's NVIDIA-filing attention restored a new high, but the February 20 results then disclosed December ARR of only $90 million versus the earlier $170 million-$190 million expectation, sequentially lower group revenue, a -199% adjusted-EBITDA margin and quarterly capex eleven times revenue ([Reuters-distributed report, published 2025-01-27](https://m.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/chinas-deepseek-sets-off-ai-market-rout-3831131?ampMode=1); [Reuters, published 2025-02-14](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-cuts-stake-arm-holdings-124814823.html); [Nebius, published 2025-02-20](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-group-n-v-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2024-financial-results)). Thus the move produced real alpha against XLK and SPY, but the alpha was a vertical capitalization of distant capacity and partner credibility; once the compute-demand prior was shocked and reported utilization/revenue lag became visible, heavy liquidity migrated to reversals rather than durable gap retention. Later 2025 gains followed new, separate information and do not make the January episode durable.

**FAILED BREAKAWAY:** this is a `failed_breakaway` case; the run did not sustain. The episode row records `clean_hold=false`, `blow_off=true`, `durable_winner=false`, `failed=true`, and forward excess versus SPY of -15.68 percentage points at 21 sessions and -32.12 points at 63 sessions.

## 4. Stage anatomy

1. **Compressed prior — absent.** NBIS had only 64 prior sessions at t0. Its trailing 21-session high-low range was 71.01%, its close rose 56.08%, and a 33.3-million-share placement had repriced the story only seven weeks earlier. This was a short pause inside price discovery, not a mature compression.

2. **Catalyst ladder — present, but front-loaded and estimate-heavy.** Relisting, Q3 growth, Kansas City, strategic financing, Blackwell pre-orders and AI Studio formed a real ladder. Most economic value still depended on future capacity, utilization and year-end ARR; Q4 publication exposed the timing gap.

3. **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 NBIS led XLK by 54.50 points over 21 sessions and 98.87 over 42; it led SPY by 53.24 and 99.27. January 24 was the first fully seasoned 63-session closing high.

4. **Liquidity confirmation — present for attention, failed for durability.** T0 dollar volume was z21 2.68 and the five-day/60-day ratio was 1.61, but the January 27 shock, February 18 peak and February 20-24 reversal cluster generated the largest dollar-volume days. The February 18 upside gap failed at 3/5/10 sessions.

5. **Options convexity — unknown.** The repository has no episode-timely 2025 NBIS options history. Per-ticker options data begin in June 2026; the current surface cannot be projected backward.

The missing first stage and unknown fifth stage matter. The January episode had the middle of the five-stage pattern—catalysts, relative strength and liquidity—but no seasoned base, no episode options evidence and no reported margin confirmation.

### What arrived during the failed run

The 2025-02-20 publication showed Q4 revenue of $37.9 million, down **12.5% sequentially** from Q3 despite 466% year-over-year growth. Adjusted EBITDA loss was $75.5 million, a calculated **-199.2% margin**; net loss from continuing operations was $136.6 million; quarterly capex was $417.6 million, **11.02x revenue**; and operating cash outflow was $80.4 million. Cash remained $2.450 billion, but December cloud ARR was $90 million rather than the previously expected $170 million-$190 million. Contracts supported at least $220 million March ARR and management retained the $750 million-$1.0 billion year-end target ([Nebius, published 2025-02-20](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-group-n-v-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2024-financial-results)).

This was the fundamental fault line: year-over-year growth and cash runway were genuine, but the price had capitalized a fast path from capacity to revenue while the first post-rerating report showed customer-conversion delay, sequential revenue contraction and spending far ahead of revenue. The May and August publications later showed $55.3 million and $105.1 million of revenue, respectively, and Q2 core adjusted EBITDA turned positive ([Nebius, published 2025-05-20](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-group-n-v-announces-first-quarter-2025-financial-results); [Nebius, published 2025-08-07](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-reports-second-quarter-financial-results-and-raises-arr-guidance-for-2025)). Those later facts explain later reratings; they were not available at January t0.
