# BIDU 2013 winner autopsy

## Bottom line

BIDU's 2013 alpha was a change in what the market believed about mobile, not simply a strong search quarter: the July 16 proposal to acquire 91 Wireless supplied distribution, the July 24 result showed mobile revenue above 10% of company revenue for the first time, and the 39.7%-43.3% third-quarter revenue guide made reacceleration quantifiable even while net income and margins were under investment pressure. Capital could therefore move from treating mobile as a cannibalization risk to underwriting Baidu as a mobile search-and-distribution platform. The July 25 tape supplied unusually broad liquidity confirmation, later acquisitions widened the mobile/local-services story, and October results showed 42.3% revenue growth plus another acceleration guide. The move persisted because sequential evidence improved the revenue runway; it was not an earnings-only relief gap, although the margin sacrifice and acquisition price remained real counterweights.

## Catalyst ladder

- **2013-04-25 — first-quarter results exposed the cost of the transition.** Revenue was RMB5.969bn, 40.0% above the prior year, but operating profit fell 5.3%; management guided second-quarter revenue to 35.1%-38.3% growth and said investment would remain aggressive. This established the compressed expectation set before the breakaway. Publication date: 2013-04-25. [Baidu release](https://ir.baidu.com/static-files/df8138c5-9fdf-48f4-af2e-75ab87b75be2)
- **2013-07-16 — proposed acquisition of 91 Wireless.** Baidu disclosed an MOU to acquire all of the leading third-party app distributor for $1.9bn, giving the mobile strategy a distribution asset rather than only a search product. Publication date: 2013-07-16. [NetDragon announcement](https://www.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2013/0716/ltn20130716055.pdf)
- **2013-07-24 — second-quarter results and reacceleration guide.** Revenue rose 38.6%, mobile exceeded 10% of total revenue for the first time, active online-marketing customers rose 33%, and the third-quarter guide called for 39.7%-43.3% growth. Net income fell 4.5% and operating margin was 38.4%, making this a revenue/runway re-underwriting rather than an immediate profit inflection. Publication date: 2013-07-24. [Baidu release](https://ir.baidu.com/news-releases/news-release-details/baidu-announces-second-quarter-2013-results)
- **2013-08-14 — definitive 91 Wireless agreement.** The signed agreement reduced transaction uncertainty; consideration was adjusted to $1.85bn after a special dividend, and Baidu said the combined services generated 69m daily app downloads in July. Publication date: 2013-08-14. [Baidu release](https://ir.baidu.com/news-releases/news-release-details/baidu-signs-definitive-agreement-acquire-netdragons-subsidiary)
- **2013-08-29 — strategic investment in Nuomi.** Baidu agreed to pay $160m for 59% of Renren's group-buying service, adding a local-commerce leg to search, maps, and mobile distribution. Publication date: 2013-08-29. [Baidu release](https://ir.baidu.com/static-files/ce0ea0ab-780a-4f71-be6b-b803a43811ac)
- **2013-10-29 — third-quarter results extended the evidence chain.** Revenue growth accelerated to 42.3%, mobile search revenue was described as growing rapidly, and fourth-quarter guidance implied 45.5%-49.6% growth; operating profit grew only 1.2%, preserving the investment-versus-margin tension. Publication date: 2013-10-29. [Baidu release](https://ir.baidu.com/news-releases/news-release-details/baidu-announces-third-quarter-2013-results)

## Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** BIDU was only 2.45pp ahead of SPY over the trailing 126 sessions at t0, while April results had paired strong revenue with falling operating profit and investment pressure.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** 91 Wireless proposal → mobile crossed 10% of revenue plus accelerating guide → definitive acquisition → Nuomi → Q3 acceleration.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At July 25 BIDU led SPY by 31.63pp over 21 days and 27.75pp over 42 days and closed at a 63-day/126-day high.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** July 25 was the year's largest dollar-volume day, with z21 3.99 and five-day/60-day ratio 2.22; its closing level held for the following 10 sessions.

## Fundamentals arc

Repo coverage is **null**. BIDU has zero rows in `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet`, so no PIT-gated repo fundamental observation exists for this episode.

The publication-dated company releases show the usable arc. Q1 revenue was RMB5.969bn (+40.0% year over year) while operating profit declined 5.3%; Q2 revenue rose sequentially to RMB7.561bn (+38.6% year over year), operating profit was RMB2.904bn (+3.2%), and operating margin was 38.4%. Q2 net income fell 4.5% as SG&A rose 83.5% and R&D rose 72.6%, evidence that the runway was being purchased through product promotion and engineering. Yet mobile revenue crossed 10% of total revenue, customers grew 33%, operating cash inflow was RMB3.205bn, and cash plus short-term investments were RMB34.069bn, supporting continued investment ([Q2 release](https://ir.baidu.com/news-releases/news-release-details/baidu-announces-second-quarter-2013-results), publication date 2013-07-24).

During follow-through, Q3 revenue reached RMB8.892bn (+42.3%), operating profit RMB3.338bn (+1.2%), and operating margin 37.5%; cash plus short-term investments reached RMB43.300bn and operating cash inflow RMB4.295bn. The Q4 guide of 45.5%-49.6% growth was the clearest runway extension ([Q3 release](https://ir.baidu.com/news-releases/news-release-details/baidu-announces-third-quarter-2013-results), publication date 2013-10-29). Thus revenue growth reaccelerated while margins remained compressed: the market paid for mobile monetization and distribution before profit leverage arrived.

## Research echo

The post-earnings research reaction was a model reset, not merely target chasing. Barclays moved from Equal Weight to Overweight and raised its target from $98 to $153 on 2013-07-25, explicitly raising 2013-2015 revenue forecasts and the target multiple because the Q3 guide suggested reacceleration and mobile monetization was faster than expected ([Benzinga report](https://cdn6.benzinga.com/analyst-ratings/analyst-color/13/07/3783675/update-barclays-capital-upgrades-baidu-on-multiple-posit), publication date 2013-07-25). Against the July 25 spot close of $125.85, $153 still implied 21.6% upside. But BIDU closed at $164.93 on October 30, escaping that newly published model within roughly three months (`data/yahoo/BIDU.parquet`).

Consensus was not uniform: Oppenheimer reiterated a valuation-based Underperform with a $78 target on 2013-07-26, underscoring concern that spending and valuation could overwhelm the mobile narrative ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/stocks/analysts-actions-aap-bidu-do-lll-tsla-11990600), publication date 2013-07-26). The useful read is dispersion: bullish models lifted growth and multiples, while a bearish model stayed anchored to earnings and valuation; subsequent spot prices outran even Barclays' revised target before Q3 results supplied another operating datapoint. The repo contains no analyst-target history, so no complete consensus series is claimed.

Current/hindsight hazards at the end of the visible 2013 run: **extension** was likely after the near-vertical July-December advance; **call walls** are unknown because no BIDU options surface exists; **catalyst exhaustion** was possible once the 91 Wireless/Nuomi transactions and two accelerating quarters were absorbed. Margin compression, acquisition-integration risk, and paying $1.85bn for mobile distribution were fundamental hazards throughout.
