# QFIN 2023 failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

QFIN's large alpha into 2023 was a real but late re-underwriting of a profitable, deeply impaired China consumer-credit ADR: reopening signals widened the macro aperture, QFIN said its regulatory rectification was mostly complete, Hong Kong trading added a second venue, and the PCAOB removed an acute U.S.-listing tail while loan facilitation, capital-light mix, cash generation, and a dividend kept the equity story investable ([State Council, published 2022-11-11](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/topnews/202211/11/content_WS636e31efc6d0a757729e2e63.html); [QFIN, published 2022-11-13](https://ir.qifu.tech/static-files/aeb939c7-9ee3-4b06-8237-9b15ad6ec0cb); [SEC, filed 2022-11-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1741530/000110465922121725/tm2228449d9_ex99-1.htm); [PCAOB, published 2022-12-15](https://pcaobus.org/news-events/news-releases/news-release-detail/pcaob-secures-complete-access-to-inspect-investigate-chinese-firms-for-first-time-in-history)). The mosaic nevertheless lacked the ingredient durable winners usually need at the breakaway: a simultaneous earnings inflection. At the January 9 mechanical anchor QFIN was already 142.4% above its October low, the heaviest constructive dollar-volume days were in December, and the stock was above the two publication-dated price targets observable before t0; the next two results showed revenue and net income still falling year over year, provisions rising, and no immediate take-rate recovery ([QFIN, published 2023-03-09](https://ir.qifu.tech/news-releases/news-release-details/360-digitech-announces-fourth-quarter-2022-and-full-year-2022); [QFIN, published 2023-05-18](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2023/05/18/2672296/0/en/Qifu-Technology-Announces-First-Quarter-2023-Unaudited-Financial-Results-and-Changes-in-Dividend-Policy.html)). QFIN added only 6.4% after t0, then largely followed the China-internet cohort lower: forward excess versus KWEB was -0.08, -2.40, and -3.45 percentage points at 21, 63, and 126 sessions, while SPY excess fell to -35.15 points by 126 sessions. The autopsy is therefore not “the catalysts were fictitious”; it is that policy relief, listing-risk relief, and low-base optimism were consumed before fundamentals confirmed a new earnings slope.

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`).**
