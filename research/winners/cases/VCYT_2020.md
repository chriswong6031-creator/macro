# VCYT 2020 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

VCYT's 2020 alpha was a recovery-plus-platform re-rating rather than one clean binary
event: testing revenue returned to its pre-pandemic level in the September quarter,
reimbursement improved, and investors began capitalizing a 2021 lung-cancer pipeline and
global genomic-diagnostics platform well before those products generated revenue. The
December 3 Lung Cancer R&D Day announcement focused that optionality and the December 16
data, December 22 Bayer collaboration, February 3 preliminary results, and Decipher deal
kept adding rungs. The tape was genuinely exceptional, but the earnings bridge was not:
2020 revenue ultimately fell 2% versus 2019, gross margin fell five points, operating loss
more than doubled, and the peak-phase Decipher transaction was followed by a large equity
issuance. Thus capital first escaped the published model, then discovered that the
platform thesis required product launches, acquisition integration, and much more time
than the vertical price implied. Sources and publication dates: Veracyte Q3 release
([2020-11-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1384101/000143774920022260/ex_197472.htm));
R&D Day announcement
([2020-12-03](https://investor.veracyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/veracyte-host-virtual-lung-cancer-rd-day-wednesday-december-16));
R&D data 8-K
([2020-12-16](https://investor.veracyte.com/static-files/669724b5-1ae5-4615-952c-69bf8b1dfa4f));
Bayer collaboration
([2020-12-22](https://investor.veracyte.com/node/12441));
preliminary results
([2021-02-03](https://investor.veracyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/veracyte-announces-preliminary-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2020));
Decipher announcement
([2021-02-03](https://investor.veracyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/veracyte-acquire-decipher-biosciences));
full-year results
([2021-02-17](https://investor.veracyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/veracyte-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2020-financial)).

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`): the run did not
sustain.**

## 3. Catalyst ladder

Dates below are publication or filing dates. Repo filing anchors are explicitly
separated from web-recovered events.

- **2020-04-16 — other — pandemic reset.** Veracyte withdrew 2020 guidance because
  specimen volume had fallen sharply as clinical visits were deferred. This created
  the compressed prior and made recovery in testing volume the key marginal variable
  ([Veracyte, published 2020-04-16](https://investor.veracyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/veracyte-provides-business-update-and-withdraws-2020-financial)).
- **2020-07-30 — earnings — trough became measurable.** Q2 revenue was $20.7 million
  and the company described improving weekly test volume from the April low
  ([Veracyte, published 2020-07-30](https://investor.veracyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/veracyte-announces-second-quarter-2020-financial-results)).
  Filing anchor: `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`, filing date 2020-07-30.
- **2020-08-04 — other — balance-sheet runway.** Veracyte announced an underwritten
  public issuance, subsequently adding roughly $194 million of net proceeds; it reduced
  near-term funding risk but increased the share base
  ([SEC exhibit, published 2020-08-04](https://investor.veracyte.com/static-files/794eb3aa-1f28-45be-9800-12f8fc6c7d8b)).
- **2020-11-02 — earnings — commercial rebound and reimbursement proof.** Q3 revenue
  recovered to $31.1 million from $20.7 million in Q2; product/testing revenue rose 79%
  sequentially, gross margin was 67%, reported test volume rose 90% sequentially, and
  Envisia received ADLT status and new Medicare pricing
  ([SEC exhibit, published 2020-11-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1384101/000143774920022260/ex_197472.htm)).
  Filing anchor: `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`, filing date 2020-11-02.
- **2020-12-03 — investor_day — a dated pipeline reveal.** The company scheduled a
  December 16 Lung Cancer R&D Day to update its nasal-swab and Percepta Atlas programs,
  both then planned for second-half 2021 launch
  ([Veracyte, published 2020-12-03](https://investor.veracyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/veracyte-host-virtual-lung-cancer-rd-day-wednesday-december-16)).
- **2020-12-16 — clinical_data — preliminary lung-test performance.** At R&D Day the
  company released preliminary data for the noninvasive nasal-swab test and Percepta
  Genomic Atlas, maintaining planned second-half 2021 launches
  ([Veracyte 8-K and exhibit, filed/published 2020-12-16](https://investor.veracyte.com/static-files/669724b5-1ae5-4615-952c-69bf8b1dfa4f)).
  `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` has no VCYT rows, so this anchor is recovered
  from the public SEC-hosted filing; that local archive gap is explicit.
- **2020-12-22 — partnership — Bayer precision-oncology collaboration.** Bayer would
  offer Afirma Xpression Atlas testing to identify actionable alterations in advanced
  thyroid cancer
  ([Veracyte, published 2020-12-22](https://investor.veracyte.com/node/12441)).
- **2021-02-03 — earnings — pre-pandemic level exceeded, but annual contraction
  remained.** Preliminary Q4 revenue was $34-$35 million and volume 13,000-13,200,
  approximately +16% and +14% year over year at the midpoints; preliminary 2020
  revenue of $117-$118 million remained below 2019's $120.4 million
  ([Veracyte, published 2021-02-03](https://investor.veracyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/veracyte-announces-preliminary-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2020)).
  Filing anchor: `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`, filing date 2021-02-03.
- **2021-02-03 — ma_activity — Decipher changed the scale and risk of the thesis.**
  Veracyte agreed to acquire Decipher Biosciences for $600 million; Decipher's
  preliminary 2020 revenue was $39-$40 million, up more than 130%, but the transaction
  introduced integration and financing risk
  ([Veracyte, published 2021-02-03](https://investor.veracyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/veracyte-acquire-decipher-biosciences)).
- **2021-02-04 — other — acquisition financing.** The company priced 7.43 million
  shares at $74, later reporting 8.55 million shares including the overallotment and
  about $593.8 million net proceeds
  ([Veracyte, published 2021-02-04](https://investor.veracyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/veracyte-announces-pricing-upsized-public-offering-7432433);
  [Veracyte full-year release, published 2021-02-17](https://investor.veracyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/veracyte-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2020-financial)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** COVID-19 deferred clinical visits, drove Q2 revenue
  down to $20.7 million, and caused guidance withdrawal; the recovery slope was
  therefore highly convex to reopening.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Q3 recovery/reimbursement, a scheduled R&D Day,
  preliminary lung-test data, Bayer, Q4 recovery, and Decipher repeatedly expanded the
  addressable narrative.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 VCYT was +45.51pp/21d and +65.81pp/42d
  versus XLV, with simultaneous 63/126/252-day closing highs.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, but deteriorated.** T0 turnover was +2.79σ
  versus 21 days and the five-day/60-day ratio was 1.80; subsequent high-turnover
  December moves often failed their 5/10-session close-hold proxy.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2020 per-ticker options history exists in the
  repository, and no current VCYT snapshot exists. Price acceleration alone is not
  evidence of options causality.

published only on 2021-02-17 and therefore not available at t0, showed the mismatch:
2020 revenue $117.5 million (-2%), gross margin 65% versus 70% in 2019, operating loss
$35.4 million versus $15.1 million, net loss $34.9 million versus $12.6 million, and
operating cash usage $9.7 million. This is post-t0 outcome evidence, not input to the
onset thesis
([Veracyte, published 2021-02-17](https://investor.veracyte.com/news-releases/news-release-details/veracyte-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2020-financial)).

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** The move had company-specific recovery,
  reimbursement, product-data, partnership, and M&A rungs; local short-interest and
  social-tape history are null, so “ruled out” means no affirmative meme mechanism is
  needed, not proof that short covering was absent.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** VCYT first set its 126-day and 252-day closing highs
  in July and was +101.56% over 63 sessions at t0; the move was multi-month.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** Excess return was +45.51pp/21d and +65.81pp/42d versus
  XLV, and +41.92pp/+62.15pp versus SPY.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Historical options evidence is structurally absent;
  options causality cannot be established or excluded from price alone.

Current/post-episode hazards are **extension after a vertical move**, **catalyst
exhaustion**, **acquisition integration and equity-issuance dilution**, and **a large
gap between platform narrative and then-current earnings**. Historical call walls are
**null**. The decisive false-positive signature was not weak onset tape; it was
liquidity without durable close-holds plus a valuation re-rate that arrived before the
new products and acquired revenue could prove a self-funded, repeatable earnings
engine.
