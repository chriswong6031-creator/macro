# CCJ 2025 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

CCJ's 2025 move was a two-step re-underwriting, not simply uranium beta: the May 9 onset followed a 34.5% drawdown and Q1 proof that long-term contracts were lifting realized prices even while uranium spot had fallen, then US nuclear-policy orders, a US$170 million Westinghouse EBITDA step-up, and ultimately an at-least-US$80 billion US-government/Westinghouse reactor framework converted Cameco from a cyclical miner in the market's model into the scarce liquid public vehicle spanning uranium, conversion, fuel services, and reactor technology. The tape supports company-specific alpha at onset (+23.80 percentage points over SPY in 21 sessions) and institutional-capacity repricing, but also imposes an important limit: XME is only a broad metals/mining proxy, and CCJ's +75.74% full-year return actually trailed XME's +80.67%, so the durable 2025 result was large index alpha, not demonstrated full-year sector alpha. Publication dates and claims below are point-in-time; the later October framework was a second leg, not information available at the May onset.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2025-02-20 — earnings / prior reset.** Cameco reported 2024 adjusted EBITDA above C$1.5 billion, up about 73%, including C$483 million from Westinghouse; it had roughly 220 million pounds under long-term uranium contracts and had extinguished the Westinghouse acquisition term loan in January. Publication date: 2025-02-20. [Cameco release](https://www.cameco.com/media/news/cameco-reports-2024-fourth-quarter-results).
- **2025-05-01 — earnings / t0 setup.** Q1 net earnings were C$70 million and adjusted EBITDA C$353 million. Crucially, realized uranium pricing improved while average spot uranium had fallen 30% year over year; fuel-services adjusted EBITDA more than doubled and Cameco's share of Westinghouse adjusted EBITDA rose to C$92 million from C$77 million. Guidance was unchanged. Publication date: 2025-05-01. [Cameco Q1 release](https://www.cameco.com/media/news/cameco-reports-2025-first-quarter-results).
- **2025-05-23 — macro policy.** Four US executive orders sought faster reactor licensing and deployment and a stronger domestic nuclear fuel cycle; one called for a plan to expand uranium conversion and enrichment capacity. This broadened the addressable demand narrative but was sector-wide, not CCJ-exclusive. Publication date: 2025-05-23. [White House fact sheet](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/05/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-reinvigorates-the-nuclear-industrial-base/) and [fuel-cycle order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/05/reinvigorating-the-nuclear-industrial-base/).
- **2025-06-06 — partnership/economics.** Cameco disclosed an expected approximately US$170 million increase in its 49% share of Westinghouse's 2025 Q2 and annual adjusted EBITDA, tied to two Dukovany reactors; the five-year Westinghouse adjusted-EBITDA growth outlook remained 6%–10% excluding that step-up. Publication date: 2025-06-06. [Cameco release](https://www.cameco.com/media/news/cameco-reports-expected-increase-in-its-share-of-westinghouse-2025-adjusted-ebitda).
- **2025-07-31 — earnings.** Q2 net earnings were C$321 million and adjusted EBITDA C$673 million, driven largely by Westinghouse and strong uranium/fuel-services results; Westinghouse adjusted EBITDA was C$352 million for Q2. Publication date: 2025-07-31. [Cameco Q2 report](https://www.cameco.com/invest/financial-information/quarterly-reports/2025/q2).
- **2025-10-28 — partnership / second breakaway.** Cameco and Brookfield announced a binding term sheet with the US Department of Commerce under which the government would facilitate financing, permitting, and approvals for at least US$80 billion of new Westinghouse reactors. The government's participation interest was contingent and the definitive-agreement/funding risks remained material. Publication date: 2025-10-28. [Cameco release](https://www.cameco.com/media/news/cameco-and-brookfield-establish-transformational-partnership-with-united-states).
- **2025-11-05 — earnings / confirmation with blemishes.** Nine-month adjusted EBITDA reached C$1.338 billion, up 33%; Westinghouse's nine-month contribution rose to C$569 million from C$320 million and Cameco received a US$171.5 million October distribution. But Q3 adjusted EBITDA fell 5% year over year and McArthur River/Key Lake production guidance had been cut to 14–15 million pounds from 18 million. Publication date: 2025-11-05. [Cameco Q3 release](https://www.cameco.com/media/news/cameco-reports-2025-third-quarter-results).

Repo filing anchors are absent: `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` and `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` contain zero CCJ rows. This is a coverage gap, not evidence that the events did not occur; the company is a Canadian foreign private issuer and its dated issuer releases are used above.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The repo's PIT-gated US fundamentals surfaces have no CCJ observations: `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` all return zero rows. Therefore no repo-derived revenue or margin series can be printed for CCJ, and the following figures come from issuer publications on their stated dates.

Before the run, 2024 adjusted EBITDA was more than C$1.5 billion, up about 73%, but net earnings were C$172 million and Westinghouse still showed a C$218 million accounting loss at Cameco's share because of acquisition accounting. The runway was unusually visible: about 220 million pounds in the long-term uranium book, roughly 28 million pounds of average annual deliveries for 2025–29, more than 85 million kgU of contracted UF6, C$600 million cash, C$1.3 billion debt, and a C$1.0 billion undrawn facility as published 2025-02-20. [Cameco 2024 results](https://www.cameco.com/media/news/cameco-reports-2024-fourth-quarter-results).

During the run, Q1 did not show a volume-led boom—uranium adjusted EBITDA fell 6% and unit cost of sales rose 11%—but did show the economic mechanism: realized contract pricing rose despite a 30% fall in spot, fuel-services earnings and adjusted EBITDA more than doubled, and Westinghouse adjusted EBITDA improved. By the nine months ended September, revenue was C$2.281 billion versus C$1.953 billion (+17%), gross profit C$697 million versus C$533 million (+31%), implying gross margin of 30.6% versus 27.3%; adjusted EBITDA was C$1.338 billion versus C$1.007 billion (+33%), and operating cash flow C$731 million versus C$376 million (+94%). Publication date: 2025-11-05. [Q3 MD&A](https://www.cameco.com/sites/default/files/documents/2025%20Q3%20MDA%20FS%20and%20Notes.pdf). Balance-sheet runway was C$779 million cash, C$1.0 billion debt, and a C$1.0 billion undrawn facility. The counterweight was operational: McArthur River/Key Lake's 2025 production expectation fell to 14–15 million pounds from 18 million, demonstrating that the rerating was not a frictionless mine-execution story.

## 7. Research echo

Research followed the tape rather than leading the first breakaway. On 2025-06-10, after CCJ had closed at US$66.70 on June 9, Goldman Sachs raised its target to US$78 from US$65, explicitly citing the Westinghouse/Korean framework; the new target was only about 17% above spot after the repricing. Publication date: 2025-06-10. [TipRanks/The Fly report](https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/cameco-price-target-raised-to-78-from-65-at-goldman-sachs-thefly). On 2025-07-24, National Bank raised its target to C$110 from C$100 while the cited CCO price was C$108.06—roughly 2% headroom—despite adding Westinghouse value and nuclear/data-center tailwinds. Publication date: 2025-07-24. [Fidelity/MT Newswires](https://fixedincome.fidelity.com/ftgw/fi/FINewsArticle?id=202507241145MIDNIGHTUSEQUITY_A3419757).

The point-in-time read is not that consensus was bearish; it is that prices repeatedly reached or escaped newly published models, forcing targets upward after catalysts. A clean contemporaneous aggregate consensus-versus-spot series is unavailable, because the repo has no analyst-target data and current web consensus pages are not PIT-safe for 2025.
