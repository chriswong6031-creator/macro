# ISRG 2009 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

ISRG's 2009 alpha was a platform and earnings-quality rerating out of a recessionary capital-equipment prior, not a one-day robotics story: investors began the year expecting hospital credit stress to leave 2009 system revenue roughly flat, but procedure volume—the usage meter that drives instruments, accessories, and service—kept compounding; the April da Vinci Si launch then created a replacement/upgrade bridge from that resilient installed-base usage back to capital placements. Q2 made the mismatch undeniable: even after removing a favorable deferred-revenue recognition, revenue exceeded the top of the contemporaneous analyst range, procedures rose about 52%, recurring revenue rose about 33%, and 47 of 76 systems placed were the new Si ([SEC exhibit published 2009-07-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1035267/000119312509153116/dex991.htm); [MassDevice published 2009-07-23](https://www.massdevice.com/intuitive-surgical-wins-expectations-game-with-strong-second-quarter/)). The 26.84% closing jump on record dollar volume was therefore the forced re-underwrite, while Q3's approximately 49% procedure growth, rapid Si mix shift, and November Japan approval supplied enough durability for the stock to keep separating from both Health Care and SPY despite a negative post-Q3 reaction ([SEC exhibit published 2009-10-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1035267/000119312509210349/dex991.htm); [company release published 2009-11-20](https://isrg.intuitive.com/news-releases/news-release-details/da-vinci-approved-japan)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** This was expectation compression, not clean price compression. On t0 the stock remained 34.96% below its prior-252d closing high, but its prior-63d range was 24.38% and 63d annualized close volatility was about 48%; the tape was already recovering.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** A low 2009 capital-placement guide was followed by the Si launch, Q1 proof of procedure resilience, the decisive Q2 expectations gap, Q3 Si adoption, and Japan approval.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 ISRG was +32.77pp vs XLV over 21d and +42.49pp over 42d, with simultaneous new 63d and 126d closing highs.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 printed $1.270bn dollar volume, z21 4.06 and z60 6.78; the closing jump remained above its prior close for 10 sessions. The later high-volume Q3 decline prevents interpreting volume as signed accumulation.
