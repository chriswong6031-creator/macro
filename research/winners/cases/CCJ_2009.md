# CCJ 2009 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

CCJ's 2009 move was a post-crisis uranium-cycle and balance-sheet rerating with company-specific de-risking layered on top, not a clean earnings-acceleration story: the stock entered the mechanical 2009-04-23 onset 54% below its prior-252-day high, then the uranium spot market rebounded from an April average of about US$41.63/lb to US$51 by mid-May as Asian utilities, an investment fund, and Cameco itself bought material, while the next-day Kumtor agreement reduced a long-running Centerra overhang and May's results raised uranium sales expectations ([Scotiabank publication dated 2009-05-20](https://scotiabank.investorroom.com/2009-05-20-Scotiabank-apos-s-Commodity-Price-Index-Tumbles-In-April-Likely-Touching-Bottom); [Kumtor release published 2009-04-24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1009001/000113031909000221/o54939e6vk.htm); [Q1 release published 2009-05-01](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1009001/000113031909000251/o55083exv99w1.htm)). Q2's higher delivery volume and upgraded revenue outlook, the October restart of Cigar Lake dewatering, and December's C$871 million Centerra divestiture then converted optionality into financial and operating runway ([Q2 release published 2009-08-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1009001/000095012309033919/o56613exv99w1.htm); [Cigar Lake release published 2009-10-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1009001/000095012309052675/c91407exv1.htm); [divestiture completion published 2009-12-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1009001/000095012309074101/c94198exv1.htm)). The qualification is decisive: from t0 through year-end CCJ gained 64.57% versus SPY's 32.61% but XME's 74.40%, and at 252 sessions it lagged SPY by 14.76 percentage points and XME by 69.95 points. This was sustained index alpha through 2009 and a strong 126-day large-cap uranium rerating, but not durable broad-mining cohort alpha.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** Expectation and drawdown compression were present, but price compression was not: t0 sat 54.02% below the prior-252-day high while the prior-63-day range was 52.82% and annualized 63-day close volatility was 58.34%. This was a violent recovery from a low, not a tight base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** The first documented rung was one session after mechanical t0: Kumtor settlement and ratification, higher uranium-volume guidance, Port Hope restart, Q2 guidance, Cigar Lake dewatering, and the Centerra exit repeatedly reduced distinct uncertainties.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** T0 was a new 63-day close with +37.64pp 42-day excess versus SPY and +23.66pp versus XME. Relative leadership was weaker at 21 days and disappeared against XME by 126 days.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, but mixed.** T0 z21 was 1.74, yet z60 was only 0.80 and 5d/60d was 0.84. April 30's z21 2.92/z60 3.67 and a close-based 10-session hold supplied the stronger confirmation; true gap evidence is null.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** The repo has no 2009 per-ticker options history. Current files begin in 2026-06 and cannot establish a 2009 mechanism.
