# CLSK 2020 failed-breakaway autopsy

## Bottom line

CLSK’s 2020 alpha came from three narratives arriving faster than its tiny starting revenue base could absorb: real microgrid/switchgear revenue acceleration and a Nasdaq uplisting made the company newly investable; GridFabric and FERC Order 2222 supplied a software/DER policy story; then the ATL acquisition abruptly recast the stock as a bitcoin-mining proxy just as bitcoin accelerated. The tape, however, says this was not one durable institutional re-underwriting. The September 21 breakaway was already the rebound from a summer vertical move, printed exceptional turnover but lost 39% in 21 sessions, and required a second, economically different crypto catalyst to recover. A $40 million equity raise against only $10.0 million of FY2020 revenue improved runway while greatly expanding the share base; reported operating losses and cash burn persisted. Thus narrative breadth, reflexive crypto beta, capital access, and low-base percentage growth produced spectacular calendar-year alpha, but weak fundamental scale, changing identity, dilution, and repeated gap failures kept the move from sustaining.

**Format-contract status: this is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`). The run did NOT sustain.**

## Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** T0 was a new 252d close, but it followed a roughly fivefold June-to-August rise and a pullback, not a long, quiet base. This distinction is central to the blow-off label.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Revenue/backlog updates, GridFabric, Order 2222, new contracts, financing, and then ATL supplied repeated public reasons to change the model—but the final catalyst changed the model itself from microgrid software toward bitcoin mining.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** Prompt/census trailing excess was +26.70 pp over 21d and +177.99 pp over 42d versus XLK. Forward excess then collapsed to −45.46 pp at 21d, showing onset without immediate follow-through.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 dollar volume was $244.78 million, z21 4.27 and z60 3.98; however, similarly extreme turnover had already appeared in May, July, and August, so confirmation was not unique to the durable portion of the run.

## Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — likely.** The stock repeatedly posted enormous turnover and vertical gaps relative to a tiny revenue base; the December crypto identity change amplified thematic/retail reflexivity. Public business events existed, so this was not narrative-free.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** No single adjudication determined value; the ladder spanned listing, contracts, policy, financing, acquisition, and results.
- **sector_beta — likely.** XLK was an administratively correct GICS benchmark but an economically poor cohort after ATL. Clean-energy enthusiasm first and bitcoin-miner beta later supplied much of the factor exposure; CLSK’s 2021 drawdown despite positive index returns shows that beta did not create durable idiosyncratic quality.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Options could have amplified a low-float momentum tape, but 2020 history is absent; any stronger statement would be invention.

Current hazards are extension sensitivity after vertical moves, bitcoin and mining-difficulty exposure, dilution/capital-intensity, and catalyst exhaustion when capacity claims become expected. Current-only options hazards are a $14 call wall, modeled gamma-regime instability between adjacent snapshots, approximately 100% IV30, and pin/volatility risk; they are not historical explanations.
