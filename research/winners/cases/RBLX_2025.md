# RBLX 2025 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

RBLX's 2025 alpha was a platform re-underwriting, not one viral-game headline: the 2025-02-06 guide rejection first reset expectations, the 2025-05-01 beat-and-raise showed that bookings, engagement, and cash generation were accelerating, and Cube, Google advertising, creator-economy changes, and record concurrent use then made a broader discovery/monetization/infrastructure flywheel visible ([FY24 results, published 2025-02-06](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2025/Roblox-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2024-Financial-Results/); [Q1 results, published 2025-05-01](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2025/Roblox-Reports-First-Quarter-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx); [Cube, published 2025-03-17](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/03/introducing-roblox-cube); [Google advertising partnership, published 2025-04-01](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_news/2025/Apr/01/Roblox-Launches-Rewarded-Video-Ads-Partners-With-Google-to-Scale-Immersive-Advertising.pdf); [infrastructure update, published 2025-06-24](https://about.roblox.com/en-au/newsroom/2025/06/roblox-infrastructure-supporting-record-breaking-games)). Grow a Garden was the live proof point rather than the whole thesis: by 2025-06-24 Roblox said the experience had reached 21.6 million simultaneous users and the platform 30.6 million, before Q2 reported bookings growth of 51% and raised the full-year outlook ([infrastructure update, published 2025-06-24](https://about.roblox.com/en-au/newsroom/2025/06/roblox-infrastructure-supporting-record-breaking-games); [Q2 results, published 2025-07-31](https://ir.roblox.com/news/news-details/2025/Roblox-Reports-Second-Quarter-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). The move produced large 63- and 126-session alpha, but it did not survive the full year after the mechanical t0: targets chased the tape, child-safety exposure increased, and the Q3 disclosure paired still-extraordinary growth with declining summer-hit engagement and a warning that 2026 operating margin could fall as creator payouts, safety, and infrastructure spending rose ([Louisiana Attorney General release, published 2025-08-14](https://ag.louisiana.gov/Article/322); [Q3 shareholder letter filed 2025-10-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000326/ex991-q32025shareholderl.htm)). That explains both halves of the autopsy: real operating evidence caused the re-rating, while increasingly expensive growth and a fully escaped valuation model prevented the peak alpha from persisting.
