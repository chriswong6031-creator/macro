# QBTS 2025 — failed breakaway autopsy

## Bottom line

QBTS's March 2025 alpha was a fast, company-specific re-underwriting of three things at once: a peer-reviewed claim that its annealer had crossed a useful-problem classical-computing boundary, the first system-sale/bookings evidence that D-Wave could monetize hardware rather than only small cloud and services contracts, and an equity-funded balance-sheet reset that removed near-term survival risk. The tape confirms that this was not merely SPY or quantum-cohort beta, but the move was too far ahead of the economic proof: by March 17 the stock had gained 141.47% in five sessions, had escaped almost every published analyst target, and was capitalizing a disputed scientific milestone while trailing revenue remained only $8.8 million. The March 17 gap then failed within three sessions and the episode lost 33.18 percentage points versus SPY over the next 21 trading days. Later Q1 system-sale revenue and Advantage2 general availability supplied genuinely new evidence and drove separate May/July waves; they explain why QBTS still finished 2025 much higher, not why the March breakaway itself endured.

This is a failed breakaway case; the run did not sustain.

## Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** QBTS had already risen 1,018% over 126 trading days into t0 and had made 63d/126d/252d highs on January 6. This was a vertical continuation after a volatile reset, not a quiet institutional base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** First system sale → named Jülich deployment → *Science* publication → bookings/runway disclosure formed a coherent ladder, but the most price-sensitive scientific claim arrived with contemporaneous methodological dispute.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed.** The onset cleared all three high windows and beat SPY by 78.98pp/138.95pp over 21/42 days; forward 21-day excess was -33.18pp and the March 17 gap failed inside three sessions.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** Detector z21 was 2.62 and 5d/60d dollar volume was 2.11. Liquidity confirmed attention and price discovery; it did not distinguish durable demand from turnover after a vertical move.
