# IREN 2025 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

IREN's 2025 alpha was a two-engine re-rating that became a capital-intensity stress test: a scaled, low-cost Bitcoin-mining operation first supplied observable cash generation and financing capacity, while management redirected incremental growth toward scarce power, liquid-cooled data centers and GPU cloud. The June 5 operating update made the bridge explicit—record mining economics, nearly full GPU use and $28 million of AI Cloud run-rate revenue—then Blackwell purchases, hardware financing and contracted AI customers repeatedly raised the addressable model ([May update, published 2025-06-05](https://iren.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/iren-may-2025-monthly-update); [multi-year contracts, published 2025-10-07](https://iren.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/iren-secures-new-multi-year-ai-cloud-contracts)). The November 3 Microsoft agreement supplied the missing hyperscaler proof, but also attached a roughly $5.8 billion Dell equipment order and a large construction burden to the thesis ([company release, published 2025-11-03](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/11/03/3178993/0/en/IREN-Secures-9-7bn-AI-Cloud-Contract-with-Microsoft.html)). Thus IREN produced large alpha because the market stopped valuing it as only a Bitcoin miner and began capitalizing power and AI-cloud revenue; it failed to sustain the peak because the cash revenue remained mining-heavy, projected AI ARR ran well ahead of recognized AI revenue, and funding the conversion required repeated converts and equity, culminating in a December recapitalization ([FY25 results, published 2025-08-28](https://iren.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/iren-reports-full-year-fy25-results); [equity pricing, published 2025-12-03](https://iren.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/iren-announces-pricing-ordinary-shares-offering-fund-repurchase)).
