# NET 2021 winner autopsy

## Bottom line

Cloudflare's 2021 alpha was not one headline or a squeeze: capital progressively re-underwrote a fast-growing CDN as a broader edge-network platform. Q1 supplied the economic proof—51% revenue growth, 123% dollar-based net retention, large customers above half of revenue, and sharply narrower operating losses—then the June 8 Fastly outage made the scarcity and strategic importance of global edge infrastructure unusually visible just as NET broke away. Zero Trust launches, accelerating Q2 growth, a $1.125 billion zero-coupon financing, R2 object storage, email security, and an earlier-than-planned non-GAAP profit in Q3 repeatedly enlarged the perceived market while preserving roughly 77%–78% GAAP gross margins. The tape confirms a sustained company-specific rerating: NET rose 142.0% from June 8 to its November 18 peak against 20.2% for software proxy IGV and 12.0% for SPY, with multiple billion-dollar-volume sessions. It did not sustain the full re-rating: the November earnings gap failed, the published target stack lagged the price, and NET's 252-trading-day forward excess versus SPY was -38.79 percentage points, consistent with valuation duration—not a broken 2021 operating arc—becoming the dominant risk.

## Catalyst ladder

- **2021-05-06 — Q1 earnings / guidance.** Revenue grew 51% to $138.1 million, dollar-based net retention reached 123%, large customers grew 70%, and full-year revenue guidance was $612–$616 million. Cloudflare published the release on 2021-05-06; the repo material-event anchor is the 8-K filed the same day (`data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet`, accession `0001477333-21-000024`; `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` has no NET rows). [Cloudflare release](https://www.cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2021/Cloudflare-Announces-First-Quarter-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx) (2021-05-06); [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477333/000147733321000024/) (2021-05-06).
- **2021-06-08 — Fastly's global outage exposed category criticality.** Publication-date reporting identified Fastly, one of the few dominant CDN providers alongside Cloudflare and Akamai, as the source of a widespread outage. This was competitor/category evidence, not a Cloudflare contract or revenue disclosure, but it coincided exactly with mechanical `t0`. [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/fastly-cdn-internet-outages-2021/) (2021-06-08); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2021/06/08/internet-outage-fastly) (2021-06-08).
- **2021-06-10 — Zero Trust private networking launch.** Cloudflare extended Teams policies from public traffic into private networks, supporting the thesis that its network could replace more hardware and point products. [Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/private-networking/) (2021-06-10).
- **2021-06-17 — WARP for Linux and proxy mode.** The release broadened enterprise endpoint reach and allowed per-application routing, another concrete Zero Trust platform extension. [Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/announcing-warp-for-linux-and-proxy-mode/) (2021-06-17).
- **2021-08-05 — Q2 earnings / raised annual outlook.** Revenue accelerated to 53% growth, net retention reached 124%, large customers rose to 1,088, and full-year revenue guidance increased to $629–$633 million. The repo material-event file anchors the same-day 8-K (`0001477333-21-000033`). [Cloudflare release](https://www.cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2021/Cloudflare-Announces-Second-Quarter-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx) (2021-08-05); [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477333/000147733321000033/) (2021-08-05).
- **2021-08-10 — $1.125 billion 0% convertible financing.** The upsized offering added approximately $1.1 billion of net proceeds and materially extended investment runway; the closing-related 8-K was filed 2021-08-13 (`0001193125-21-246066`) in `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet`. [Cloudflare release](https://www.cloudflare.com/en-ca/press/press-releases/2021/cloudflare-inc-announces-upsize-and-pricing-of-offering-of-usd1-125-billion/) (2021-08-10); [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477333/000119312521246066/) (2021-08-13).
- **2021-09-27 to 2021-10-01 — Birthday Week expanded the addressable market.** Cloudflare entered email security and announced R2, an S3-compatible object store without egress charges, moving the narrative beyond CDN/security toward developer storage and a broader edge cloud. The dated official retrospective states Birthday Week ran September 26–October 1 and enumerates the releases. [Cloudflare Birthday Week](https://www.cloudflare.com/birthday-week-2021/) (2021-09-27 week); [Cloudflare innovation-weeks archive](https://blog.cloudflare.com/2021-innovations-weeks/) (published 2021-11-29, describing the public September releases).
- **2021-10-15 — Technology Partner Program.** The program formalized an integration ecosystem around Cloudflare One, supporting enterprise distribution and platform breadth. [Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/technology-partner-program/) (2021-10-15).
- **2021-11-04 — Q3 earnings and earlier profitability milestone.** Revenue grew 51% to $172.3 million; large customers grew 71%; GAAP gross margin was 78.2%; and non-GAAP operating income turned positive a year ahead of the original timeline. The repo material-event file anchors the same-day 8-K (`0001477333-21-000042`). [Cloudflare release](https://www.cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2021/Cloudflare-Announces-Third-Quarter-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx) (2021-11-04); [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477333/000147733321000042/) (2021-11-04).

## Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** NET had already set 63/126/252-day highs on 2021-02-08, then corrected and consolidated; its 126-day return at `t0` was only 16.07% while its 21-day return had reaccelerated to 25.36%.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Q1 economic proof preceded the Fastly outage and June Zero Trust launches; Q2, financing, R2/email launches, and Q3 profitability repeatedly expanded the model.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At `t0`, 21-day excess was 25.32 pp and 42-day excess 20.71 pp versus SPY, with a 63-day high. Supplemental IGV excess was 20.22 pp and 23.29 pp.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** Dollar-volume z21 was about 1.45–1.48 at onset, followed by multiple $1 billion-plus days and z60 readings above 3 later in the run.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** The repo has no per-ticker 2021 options history; current data cannot establish historical convexity.

## Fundamentals arc

The four prescribed repo fundamentals surfaces—`data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet`—contain **zero NET rows**, so PIT-gated local fundamentals are null. The numbers below therefore come only from dated company releases and are not backfilled into the machine block as local facts.

Immediately before `t0`, Q1 revenue was $138.1 million (+51% year over year), GAAP gross margin 76.8%, GAAP operating margin -22.6% versus -39.5% a year earlier, operating cash flow $23.5 million, free cash flow -$2.2 million, and cash plus securities $1.035 billion. Dollar-based net retention was 123%, large customers grew 70%, and management guided 2021 revenue to $612–$616 million. [Cloudflare Q1 release](https://www.cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2021/Cloudflare-Announces-First-Quarter-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx) (2021-05-06).

During the run, Q2 revenue reached $152.4 million (+53%), GAAP gross margin 77.0%, GAAP operating margin -18.9%, operating cash flow $7.5 million, and free cash flow -$9.8 million; annual revenue guidance rose to $629–$633 million. [Cloudflare Q2 release](https://www.cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2021/Cloudflare-Announces-Second-Quarter-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx) (2021-08-05). Q3 revenue reached $172.3 million (+51%), GAAP gross margin 78.2%, GAAP operating margin improved to -15.4%, and non-GAAP operating margin reached +1.3%, but free cash flow deteriorated to -$39.7 million. Cash plus securities rose to $1.814 billion after the convertible financing, leaving ample runway despite continuing GAAP and cash losses. [Cloudflare Q3 release](https://www.cloudflare.net/news/news-details/2021/Cloudflare-Announces-Third-Quarter-2021-Financial-Results/default.aspx) (2021-11-04). The arc was thus stable hypergrowth, high gross margins, improving accounting leverage, and ample financing capacity—not GAAP profitability or self-funded expansion.

The model escaped the published targets before analysts caught up. NET closed at $180.77 on October 18 while much of the target stack was around $122–$141; a November 2 contemporaneous article cited a $130 median target against a roughly $190 spot, a 32% gap. [InvestorPlace](https://investorplace.com/2021/11/net-stock-is-due-for-a-correction-after-its-spectacular-run/) (2021-11-02). The echo therefore corroborated the platform narrative but largely chased the tape.

## Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** The multi-month move followed recurring operating/product evidence and repeated liquidity expansion; local short-interest history is absent, so this is a structural read rather than a short-interest measurement.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The run accumulated across Q1, a competitor outage, product launches, Q2, financing, Birthday Week, and Q3. No single gap explains the 142% move.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** NET beat IGV by 121.76 pp and SPY by 130.02 pp from `t0` to peak; at onset its 21-day excess was 20.22 pp versus IGV and 25.32 pp versus SPY.
- **options_mirage — possible.** 2021 options history is unavailable, so convexity cannot be measured. It is not needed to explain the observed fundamental and cash-tape sequence, but cannot be excluded.

Current hazards are substantial and separate from the 2021 episode. At the latest local close (2026-07-17), NET was up 40.67% over 63 sessions and stood 17.28% above its 50-day average and 31.76% above its 200-day average (`data/yahoo/NET.parquet`), so `extended_after_vertical_move` is present. The 2026-07-18 options snapshot places a hard $300 call wall 7.59% above spot, so `call_wall_overhead` is possible, subject to the snapshot's assumption-based dealer-sign limitation. `catalyst_exhausted` is unknown because this autopsy did not research the 2026 event calendar. The 2021 analog warns that a clean 126-day winner can still reverse sharply after price outruns published models and the catalyst ladder becomes fully capitalized.
