# FN 2025 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Fabrinet's 2025 alpha was a cycle/platform re-rating in an unusually scarce part of the AI stack: the market moved from treating the company as a steady, low-margin contract manufacturer with a near-term datacom air pocket to underwriting it as a capacity-and-execution beneficiary of multiple optical ramps. The sequence mattered more than any headline: February established telecom/DCI strength and future next-generation datacom ramps; May showed record revenue despite the datacom transition; Rosenblatt's June 12 work made the 800G/1.6T and Blackwell production bridge explicit; then August and November reported records, telecom persistence, early high-performance-computing revenue, and better-than-feared datacom. Revenue growth accelerated while gross margin stayed near 12%, so most of the move was a higher growth-duration multiple plus earnings revisions, not margin expansion. The tape anticipated that conversion in June and sustained it: FN returned 106.60% in calendar 2025 versus 24.90% for XLK and 18.01% for SPY, and the repo labels the June 20 episode a clean, durable winner.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

PIT gate: quarterly values are included only after the `filed` date; annual panel values only after `asof_date`. Before the breakaway, fiscal Q2 (filed 2025-02-04) revenue was $833.6m, +17.0% year over year, with 12.10% gross margin and 9.55% operating margin. Fiscal Q3 (filed 2025-05-06) revenue was $871.8m, +19.2%, while gross margin slipped to 11.72% and operating margin to 9.05%; cash was $306.9m and the quarterly panel reports no debt value. That is acceleration without margin leverage.

During the sustained run, full-year fiscal 2025 revenue reached $3.419bn versus $2.883bn in fiscal 2024 (+18.6%); gross margin was 12.09% versus 12.35%, operating margin 9.49% versus 9.63%, and diluted EPS $9.17 versus $8.10. Fiscal Q1 2026, filed 2025-11-04, then accelerated to $978.1m (+21.6%) with 11.90% gross margin and 9.63% operating margin; cash was $305.0m. The runway read is therefore net-cash operating capacity and internally generated cash, but with rising working-capital/capex needs: the annual panel shows FY2025 CFO of $328.4m and capex of $121.1m, versus $413.1m and $47.5m for FY2024.

Sources: `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet` (quarterly filed gate and margins), `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet` (annual `asof_date` gate), `data/edgar/statements.parquet` (annual arc), and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` (EPS coverage). Coverage gaps: the quarterly file lacks fiscal Q4 rows for FN and has no CFO/capex values for fiscal Q2/Q3 2025; `eps_quarterly.parquet` also lacks fiscal Q4. The annual `statements.parquet` extraction carries a 2026 `as_of` timestamp, so its FY2025 values are used only as retrospective run description, not as evidence available at t0.

## 7. Research echo

Consensus was initially split and slow. April 17-22 target cuts took JPMorgan to $220 and Rosenblatt to $230; after May results JPMorgan moved only to $235 and Barclays lowered to $234, while FN closed at $266.19 on t0. Rosenblatt's June 12 target of $290 was the first published model in this set to explain the next-generation ramp bridge, but price then escaped again: FN closed at $355.05 on August 28, above the $329-$360 post-August target cluster. JPMorgan's August 25 upgrade to Overweight and later October target increases were catch-up. After November's record, targets jumped sharply—Needham $440 to $540, Rosenblatt $425 to $550, JPMorgan $430 to $530, and Barclays $329 to $499—while FN closed at $459.60 on November 4. This is the “price escaped the published model” pattern: targets followed successive operating proof points rather than leading the June breakaway. Sources and publication dates: [PriceTargets target history, entries dated 2025-04-17 through 2025-11-05](https://www.pricetargets.com/NYSE/FN/); [Investing.com, 2025-06-12](https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/fabrinet-price-target-raised-to-290-from-250-at-rosenblatt-93CH-4093231); [Sahm/Benzinga, 2025-08-25](https://www.sahmcapital.com/news/content/jp-morgan-upgrades-fabrinet-to-overweight-raises-price-target-to-345-2025-08-25); spot closes from `data/yahoo/FN.parquet`.
