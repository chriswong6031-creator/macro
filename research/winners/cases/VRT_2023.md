# VRT 2023 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Vertiv's 2023 breakaway was a data-center power-and-cooling rerating that moved before the phrase became market shorthand. The April earnings shock and raised outlook established that demand was converting into orders, margin, and cash rather than remaining a thematic story. The August quarter then confirmed acceleration and pushed the stock from "AI infrastructure sympathy" into a higher-quality industrial growth profile. By 2023-10-11, VRT had outperformed XLI by +61.92pp over 63 trading days, +235.77pp over 126 trading days, and +207.21pp YTD.
