# OKLO 2025 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

OKLO's January 2025 breakaway was the last, most reflexive leg of a probability rerating already seeded by late-2024 nuclear-policy enthusiasm, a 12-GW but non-binding Switch framework, and the idea that data-center load would pull first-of-a-kind reactors forward. RPower, Lightbridge, Chris Wright's confirmation as Energy Secretary, and rapidly rising analyst targets kept widening the imagined opportunity set, while cash-market volume confirmed that the story had attracted capital. What the tape could not sustain was a matching conversion of possibility into near-term cash flow or binding project economics: OKLO remained pre-revenue, licensing was still pre-application, 2024 operating cash use rose, the flagship customer pipeline remained largely non-binding, and targets caught up only after price had gone vertical. The stock peaked at $55.49 on 2025-02-07, fell 64.32% to $19.80 by 2025-04-04, and was still −31.10% from t0 after 63 sessions. March licensing and annual-update disclosures were real progress, but not the hard commercial or regulatory decision required to defend the January price. A different May policy shock began a separate breakaway; it does not rescue the January episode from its failed label.

**Format-contract classification: this is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`). The 2025-01-21 run did not sustain.**

## 3. Catalyst ladder

The ladder includes events that changed licensing, customer, fuel, policy, financing/runway, or execution assumptions around this episode. Dates are publication or filing dates.

- **2025-01-17 — RPower MOU / bridge-power concept.** Oklo and RPower published an MOU for gas generation that could transition to Aurora powerhouses for data centers. It improved the time-to-power narrative but was not a binding reactor power contract ([Oklo, published 2025-01-17](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2025/Oklo-and-RPower-Join-Forces-to-Accelerate-Power-Availability-for-Data-Centers/default.aspx)). The local `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` also carries a 2025-01-17 8-K anchor, Item 5.02, which is not the partnership itself.
- **2025-01-28 — Lightbridge MOU.** The companies agreed to study co-location of fuel-fabrication facilities and possible recycling collaboration; the disclosure repeatedly framed the work as feasibility and exploration, not committed capacity ([Oklo, published 2025-01-28](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2025/Lightbridge-and-Oklo-Sign-MOU-to-Explore-Co-Location-of-Commercial-Fuel-Fabrication-Facilities-and-Collaboration-on-Advanced-Fuel-Recycling/default.aspx)).
- **2025-02-05 — listing-compliance filing.** Oklo filed an Item 3.01 8-K, captured in `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet`; the local extractor has no amount, counterparty, or successful text extraction, so its economic content is **unknown** here ([SEC filing index, filed 2025-02-05](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849056/000110465925009756/0001104659-25-009756-index.html)).
- **2025-02-06 — Wright board transition.** Oklo announced that former director Chris Wright had stepped down after confirmation as U.S. Energy Secretary. This strengthened the market's policy-association narrative but was not an Oklo contract, appropriation, or licensing decision ([Oklo, published 2025-02-06](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2025/Oklo-Announces-Board-Transition-Following-Chris-Wrights-Confirmation-as-Secretary-of-Energy/default.aspx)).
- **2025-02-25 — DOE/ORNL materials voucher.** Oklo announced work with Oak Ridge under a DOE voucher to evaluate structural materials. This reduced a technical workstream incrementally, but did not approve a plant or begin revenue ([Oklo, published 2025-02-25](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2025/Oklo-Collaborates-with-the-U-S-DOE-and-Oak-Ridge-National-Laboratory-to-Advance-Next-Generation-Materials-Development/default.aspx)).
- **2025-03-05 — Atomic Alchemy acquisition close.** Oklo published that the approximately $25M all-stock acquisition had closed; it added a possible earlier radioisotope revenue path but immediate operating contribution was still prospective ([Oklo, published 2025-03-05](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2025/Oklo-Closes-Acquisition-of-Radioisotope-Producer-Atomic-Alchemy/default.aspx)).
- **2025-03-24 — annual results and business update.** The filed 8-K/shareholder letter disclosed zero revenue, $275.3M cash and marketable securities, $52.8M operating loss, $73.6M net loss, and $38.4M operating cash use for 2024; it also described a roughly 14-GW customer-interest pipeline and an end-2027 commercial-power goal ([SEC 8-K and shareholder letter, filed 2025-03-24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849056/000110465925027282/tm2510010d1_8k.htm)). `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` has no OKLO row, a local coverage gap.
- **2025-03-24 — NRC pre-application readiness assessment.** Oklo said the NRC would begin reviewing first-phase application material in late March and that a formal combined-license application was planned later in 2025. This was process progress, not application acceptance or license issuance ([Oklo, published 2025-03-24](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2025/Oklo-Engaging-with-U-S--Nuclear-Regulatory-Commission-in-Pre-Application-Readiness-Assessment/default.aspx)).
- **2025-04-22 — chairman transition.** Oklo announced Sam Altman's departure as chairman and said it continued to explore AI-sector partnerships, including potentially with OpenAI. The conditional wording supplied narrative optionality, not a disclosed agreement ([Oklo, published 2025-04-22](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2025/Oklo-Announces-Chairman-Transition/default.aspx)); `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` carries the same-day Item 5.02 filing anchor.
- **2025-05-13 — first-quarter update, after the failed window.** Oklo published Q1 results and reiterated that there was no operating commercial project, while highlighting financing, fuel, regulatory, and PPA risks ([Oklo, published 2025-05-13](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2025/Oklo-Publishes-First-Quarter-2025-Financial-Results-and-Business-Update/default.aspx)). This preceded, but was not itself, the separate 2025-05-23 policy-led breakaway.
- **2025-05-28 — nuclear-policy executive-order response, separate episode.** Oklo publicly supported executive orders aimed at faster advanced-nuclear testing and licensing; the announcement followed the 2025-05-23 signing event and therefore belongs to the later breakaway, not the January causal ladder ([Oklo, published 2025-05-28](https://oklo.com/newsroom/news-details/2025/Oklo-Supports-Executive-Orders-to-Advance-U-S--Nuclear-Energy-Leadership-and-Fuel-Security/default.aspx)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** The 252-day-high condition was true, but it came after a +151% listing-to-2024-year-end move and only three months after the prior October breakaway. This was a stacked extension, not a compressed base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** RPower, Lightbridge, DOE/ORNL, Atomic Alchemy, NRC readiness work, and policy associations touched multiple probability inputs, but most were MOUs, studies, or pre-application steps.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed.** Trailing excess at t0 was +63.48pp/+70.31pp over 21/42 days versus SPY, and forward excess remained +35.30pp at 21 days; it reversed to −18.80pp by day 63.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 dollar volume was +1.35 standard deviations versus its trailing 21-day census window, and five sessions through day 63 exceeded $1.3B. Peak volume clustered around the February top.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** Per-ticker options history begins in 2026-06. No episode-time contract/NBBO archive establishes whether convexity contributed to the 2025 move.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The required local PIT tables—`data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet`—contain **zero OKLO rows**. The following is a primary-filing bridge, explicitly outside those standardized panels.

Before t0, Oklo's 2024-11-14 Form 10-Q reported no revenue, $288.5M of cash, cash equivalents and marketable debt securities at 2024-09-30, a $37.4M nine-month operating loss, a $63.3M net loss, and $24.9M operating cash use ([Oklo Form 10-Q, filed 2024-11-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849056/000162828024047875/oklo-20240930.htm)). Gross margin was not meaningful because revenue was zero; operating margin is therefore null rather than a useful percentage.

During the failure, the 2025-03-24 shareholder letter disclosed full-year 2024 cash and marketable securities of $275.3M, operating loss of $52.8M, net loss of $73.6M, and operating cash use of $38.4M. The company remained pre-revenue and described the 14-GW figure as customer interest, while targeting commercial power by end-2027 ([SEC 8-K/shareholder letter, filed 2025-03-24](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849056/000110465925027282/tm2510010d1_8k.htm)). Cash runway was substantial relative to then-current corporate burn, but not equivalent to fully financing licensing, fuel, construction, and ownership of multiple powerhouses. The arc was therefore worsening reported losses and cash use alongside improving project optionality—no revenue inflection, no gross-margin evidence, and no commercial reactor cash generation to anchor the valuation.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — likely.** The episode combined a recent de-SPAC, a highly legible AI-power narrative, political-personality associations, target chasing, and a 77.57% rise from t0 to the February peak without revenue. Real public milestones existed, but the speed and 64.32% peak-to-trough reversal are consistent with reflexive narrative amplification.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** No single publication explains the entire move; the ladder extended across January and February. The failure was a multiweek exhaustion/reversal, not a one-session binary reset.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** OKLO beat the XLI proxy by +38.45pp at 21 days and then lagged it by 20.03pp at 63 days. The reversal was much larger than broad-sector movement.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Options could have amplified a low-priced narrative equity, but episode history is absent. Because the causal case does not rely on options, this remains a coverage warning rather than an asserted mechanism.

Current/structural hazards: `extended_after_vertical_move`; `catalyst_exhausted`; `pre_revenue`; `nonbinding_customer_pipeline`; `licensing_timeline`; `first_of_a_kind_construction`; `fuel_availability`; `future_project_financing`; `policy_dependency`; `target_chasing`; and `call_wall_overhead`. The current 2026-07-22 $47 call wall is snapshot-only and has no bearing on the 2025 failure. The central death pattern is more general: liquidity and new highs arrived, but hard evidence did not progress at the same speed as price, so increasingly soft catalysts had to carry an increasingly large terminal-value claim.
