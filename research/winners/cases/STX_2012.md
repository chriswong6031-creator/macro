# STX 2012 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

STX's 2012 alpha was a scarcity-profit re-underwriting, not a generic technology rally: Thailand's floods disabled enough industry supply to lift HDD pricing while Seagate's own factories remained operational, the newly closed Samsung HDD acquisition added scale, and a January 4 preannouncement made the resulting margin step-up measurable. A January 26 dividend/buyback package then translated the windfall into per-share value, and January 31 results showed the thesis in reported numbers—31.6% gross margin versus 19.5% in the prior reported quarter, plus guidance for more than $4.3B and $5B of revenue in the next two quarters. The local tape confirms capital progressively accepted that earnings regime: STX was already +26.21 percentage points ahead of XLK over 21 trading days at the January 27 onset, then the February 1 earnings gap held for at least 10 sessions on about $1.93B of dollar volume. The alpha persisted—the repo labels the onset a clean-hold durable winner with +38.06pp forward 126-day excess—but it was not permanently sustainable: a second May 1 episode is labeled a blow-off, consistent with a temporary supply/pricing windfall eventually entering published models rather than a new secular growth rate. Public-event sources and dates: [Thailand update, 2011-10-12](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2011/Seagate-Provides-Update-On-Thailand-Operations/default.aspx), [Samsung closing, 2011-12-19](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2011/Seagate-Completes-Acquisition-of-Samsungs-Hard-Disk-Drive-Business/default.aspx), [preliminary results, 2012-01-04](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2012/Seagate-Technology-Provides-Preliminary-Fiscal-Second-Quarter-2012-Financial-Results/default.aspx), [capital return, 2012-01-26](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2012/Seagate-Technology-plc-Increases-Quarterly-Cash-Dividend-By-39-And-Announces-New-Share-Repurchase-Of-1-Billion/default.aspx), and [quarterly results, 2012-01-31](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2012/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Second-Quarter-Financial-Results/default.aspx).

## 3. Catalyst ladder

Dates are publication or filing dates—never later-discovered effective dates. [`data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet) supplies 2011-10-21, 2012-01-04, 2012-01-31, and 2012-04-17 filing anchors for STX. [`data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet) contains no STX rows in 2011–2013, a coverage gap rather than evidence of no events.

- **2011-10-12 — flood/supply update (other; high durability).** Seagate said all its Thailand factories were operational but component supply was disrupted and HDD supply would be constrained throughout the quarter. This created the core relative advantage: industry scarcity without direct loss of Seagate assembly capacity. [Seagate publication, 2011-10-12](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2011/Seagate-Provides-Update-On-Thailand-Operations/default.aspx).
- **2011-10-20 — fiscal Q1 results and limited guidance (earnings; medium).** Seagate reported $2.8B revenue, 19.5% gross margin, and $0.32 diluted EPS; it withheld normal December-quarter precision because of Thailand. This low-margin baseline made the later scarcity-margin step visible. [Seagate publication, 2011-10-20](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2011/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Fiscal-First-Quarter-Financial-Results/default.aspx); the associated 8-K appears in the local earnings-date table on filing date 2011-10-21.
- **2011-12-19 — Samsung HDD acquisition closed (ma_activity; high).** The $1.4B transaction added Samsung's M8 line, assets, staff, geographic customer access, and an expected reduction in combined operating expense. [Seagate publication, 2011-12-19](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2011/Seagate-Completes-Acquisition-of-Samsungs-Hard-Disk-Drive-Business/default.aspx).
- **2012-01-04 — fiscal Q2 preannouncement (guidance; high).** Seagate quantified approximately 47M drives, $3.1–$3.2B revenue, and at least 30.5% gross margin, including Samsung operations only from December 19. That changed the story from “shortage may help pricing” to a modelable margin shock. [Seagate publication, 2012-01-04](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2012/Seagate-Technology-Provides-Preliminary-Fiscal-Second-Quarter-2012-Financial-Results/default.aspx); local 8-K filing anchor: 2012-01-04.
- **2012-01-26 — dividend increase and repurchase authorization (capital_return; medium).** The board raised the quarterly dividend 39% to $0.25 and added a $1B repurchase authorization, while expecting to use the remaining $0.9B of the prior authorization by fiscal year-end. [Seagate publication, 2012-01-26](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2012/Seagate-Technology-plc-Increases-Quarterly-Cash-Dividend-By-39-And-Announces-New-Share-Repurchase-Of-1-Billion/default.aspx).
- **2012-01-31 — fiscal Q2 results and forward revenue step-up (earnings; high).** Reported revenue was $3.2B, gross margin 31.6%, net income $563M, and diluted EPS $1.28. Contemporaneous reporting recorded non-GAAP EPS of $1.32 versus $1.08 consensus and management guidance above $4.3B for fiscal Q3, above $5B for fiscal Q4, and about $20B for calendar 2012. [Seagate publication, 2012-01-31](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2012/Seagate-Technology-Reports-Second-Quarter-Financial-Results/default.aspx); [Forbes publication, 2012-01-31](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ericsavitz/2012/01/31/seagate-fy-q2-revs-shipments-match-recent-guidance/); local 8-K filing anchor: 2012-01-31.
- **2012-04-17 — fiscal Q3 continuation (earnings; medium).** Contemporaneous reporting recorded $2.64 non-GAAP EPS and $1.2B non-GAAP net income, with management reaffirming revenue targets and providing gross-margin guidance. This extended the re-underwriting but arrived late in the visible run. [TheStreet publication, 2012-04-17](https://www.thestreet.com/story/11499199/1/seagate-blows-past-wall-streets-profit-view.html); local 8-K filing anchor: 2012-04-17.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** On 2011-12-19, STX remained 17.87% below its prior 252-trading-day closing high. The “compression” was primarily a repaired drawdown and uncertainty discount, not a narrow low-volatility base; finer volatility-compression statistics are null.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Flood-driven supply scarcity, Samsung closing, quantified January preliminary results, capital return, reported earnings/guidance, and April continuation successively reduced uncertainty.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At 2012-01-27, STX was +26.21pp versus XLK over 21 trading days and +24.48pp over 42, with a 63-day high; it was also +27.08pp/+22.98pp versus SPY.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** Dollar-volume z21/z60 were 1.46/2.12 at t0; February 1 reached $1.925B and z21 4.19, and its gap held through 10 sessions.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2012 per-ticker options history exists in the repo. Current options data begins in 2026-06 and cannot be projected backward.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The clean PIT source for the episode is the filing-date field in [`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet). Before the run, fiscal Q1 (period ended 2011-09-30, filed 2011-10-27) showed revenue $2.811B, gross profit $549M (19.53% gross margin), operating income $236M (8.40% operating margin), net income $140M, cash $2.474B, and total reported debt $3.484B. Fiscal Q2 (period ended 2011-12-30, filed 2012-02-01) showed revenue $3.195B (+13.7% sequentially), gross profit $1.010B (31.61%), operating income $605M (18.94%), net income $563M, cash $1.825B, and debt $3.485B. Thus the early rerating was margin-led more than unit-growth-led; cash declined and net debt rose around the acquisition, so the immediate runway evidence was earnings capacity and announced capital return, not a cleaner balance sheet. Fiscal Q3 (period ended 2012-03-30, filed 2012-04-30) then showed revenue $4.450B, gross margin 36.88%, operating margin 27.19%, and net income $1.146B—confirmation available only near the run endpoint, not at t0.

Coverage limitations matter. [`data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet) has STX annual observations but 2010–2012 revenue and gross-profit fields are null; its fiscal-2011 row was not available until `asof_date=2012-04-28`, after t0. [`data/edgar/statements.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/statements.parquet) has no STX years before 2020 and its rows carry 2026 extraction timestamps, so it contributes nothing to this episode. [`data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet) reports $0.32 for the September 2011 quarter only as of 2011-11-29 and $1.28 for December 2011 only as of 2012-02-28; it is consistent with the filings but is too delayed to timestamp the January 31 surprise. Quarterly CFO/capex are missing for fiscal Q2 and Q3 in the local table, so precise free-cash-flow runway during the run is null.

## 7. Research echo

The echo lagged the tape. On 2012-01-31, immediately after results, published commentary still described fiscal Q2 revenue and shipments as matching the January preannouncement, while the true model reset was the $1.32 non-GAAP EPS versus $1.08 consensus and forward revenue guidance above $4.3B/$5B ([Forbes, 2012-01-31](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ericsavitz/2012/01/31/seagate-fy-q2-revs-shipments-match-recent-guidance/)). By the February 2 session STX had closed at $25.75, while TheStreet Ratings published a $24.96 target; that same report said Brean Murray had raised its target to $30 from $26 on February 1 ([TheStreet, 2012-02-02](https://www.thestreet.com/opinion/stock-upgrades-seagate-11396645)). In other words, spot had already escaped at least one published model, and the notable sell-side response was to chase the new earnings regime upward rather than anticipate it. The repo has no analyst-target history, so consensus breadth, dispersion, and a complete revision ledger are null; this section is an illustrative public echo, not a census.
