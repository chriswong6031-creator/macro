# NTES 2009 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

NTES's 2009 alpha was a two-layer platform rerating, not a World of Warcraft headline in isolation: the [results published 2009-02-25 and filed 2009-02-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000119312509038037/d6k.htm) showed that the self-developed game engine could grow through the global downturn, then [public reports on 2009-04-15](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/report-shares-in-the9-slide-on-i-wow-i-license-rumors) and the [formal Blizzard agreement published 2009-04-16](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000119312509082053/d6k.htm) added a three-year licensed-franchise runway. The tape began repricing the first layer before the second was public: the economic break was the +9.17% session on 2009-02-27, while the mechanical `t0` on 2009-04-03 marked a simultaneous 63/126/252-session closing high. The move became durable through 126 sessions because Q1 and Q2 proved that Fantasy Westward Journey, Westward Journey II, and Tianxia II—not yet WoW—were funding the expansion. It did not sustain large alpha over 252 sessions because the optionality became a regulatory and margin dependency: Q3 game gross margin fell to 79.9% as WoW ramp costs arrived ([results published/filed 2009-11-19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000119312509237951/d6k.htm)), while the [rule tightening published 2009-10-12](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/china-tightens-regulation-on-foreign-online-game-operation) and [GAPP rejection published 2009-11-02, filed 2009-11-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000119312509221195/d6k.htm) reversed the clean expansion narrative. Thus NTES produced genuine calendar-2009 alpha, but much of the post-April risk-adjusted promise was consumed by execution cost and approval uncertainty.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

The local filing anchors are structurally empty for this foreign private issuer: `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` and `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` contain zero NTES rows. That is an 8-K coverage gap, not an absence of public filings. SEC EDGAR shows the relevant Form 6-K filings on 2009-02-26, 2009-04-20, 2009-05-20, 2009-08-13, 2009-11-03, and 2009-11-19.

- **2009-02-25 — earnings — high durability.** Q4 revenue rose 28.9% year over year to RMB801.7 million, online-game revenue rose 32.6% to RMB672.5 million, game gross margin remained 88.0%, and cash plus time deposits ended 2008 at RMB5.61 billion. The release was public February 25 and filed February 26; the +9.17% tape reaction arrived February 27. [SEC Form 6-K, release published 2009-02-25; filed 2009-02-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000119312509038037/d6k.htm).
- **2009-04-15 — other — medium durability.** Chinese-media reports disclosed that The9 had lost the WoW license and implied NetEase was the successor; this explains why NTES's $142.48 million dollar-volume expansion began one session before the formal issuer release without using later information. [Game Developer, published 2009-04-15](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/report-shares-in-the9-slide-on-i-wow-i-license-rumors).
- **2009-04-16 — partnership — high durability.** Blizzard and NetEase announced a three-year mainland-China WoW license, building on their existing Warcraft III, StarCraft II, and Battle.net relationship. [Issuer release published 2009-04-16; SEC filing 2009-04-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000119312509082053/d6k.htm).
- **2009-05-20 — earnings — high durability.** Q1 online-game revenue rose 30.2% year over year to RMB724.0 million and game gross margin reached 90.0%; the self-developed portfolio was carrying the model before WoW contributed. Cash plus time deposits reached RMB6.0 billion and operating cash flow was RMB567.8 million. [SEC Form 6-K, published/filed 2009-05-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000119312509115277/d6k.htm).
- **2009-07-14 — regulatory panel — low durability, negative rung.** GAPP publicly questioned whether the NetEase/Blizzard arrangement complied with Chinese rules and indicated that WoW could not be promoted without its approval. This made approval risk public before the late-July rally. [Engadget, published 2009-07-14](https://www.engadget.com/2009-07-14-china-investigating-potential-world-of-warcraft-upset-may-ban-g.html).
- **2009-07-22 — regulatory panel — medium durability.** A public report said content examination was complete and GAPP would allow beta testing from July 30. The date coincided with $165.89 million of dollar volume and a 4.76% close-to-close gain. [ChinaTechNews, published 2009-07-22](https://www.chinatechnews.com/2009/07/22/10193-gapp-wow-can-implement-beta-test-from-july-30).
- **2009-08-13 — earnings — medium durability, mixed rung.** Q2 revenue rose 21.8% year over year and game revenue rose 31.3%, but RMB83.3 million of dormant-account revenue flattered the quarter, game gross margin fell to 88.3%, about 500 staff had been added for Battle.net/WoW, and commercial launch still awaited GAPP. [SEC Form 6-K, published/filed 2009-08-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000119312509173606/d6k.htm).
- **2009-09-19 — product launch — high durability at publication, later reversed.** NetEase announced commercial WoW service, including new accounts and paid game time, and said Wrath of the Lich King was ready for submission. [NetEase release, published 2009-09-19](https://ir.netease.com/node/8286).
- **2009-10-12 — macro policy — high durability, negative rung.** GAPP and the National Copyright Administration tightened preapproval requirements, required recertification for game changes, and restricted foreign participation; contemporaneous coverage reported an immediate greater-than-5% NTES decline. [Game Developer, published 2009-10-12](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/china-tightens-regulation-on-foreign-online-game-operation).
- **2009-11-02 — regulatory decision — high durability, negative rung.** GAPP said WoW lacked proper approval, returned the application, directed suspension of charging and new registrations, and considered penalties; NetEase disputed that position. [Issuer release published 2009-11-02; SEC filing 2009-11-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000119312509221195/d6k.htm).
- **2009-11-19 — earnings — medium durability, mixed rung.** Q3 revenue grew 9.0% year over year, Fantasy Westward Journey reached a record 2.5 million peak concurrent users, and WoW had launched, but consolidated gross profit, net profit, and operating cash flow fell sequentially; game gross margin dropped to 79.9% because of server depreciation, custody fees, and royalties. [SEC Form 6-K, published/filed 2009-11-19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000119312509237951/d6k.htm).
- **2010-02-12 — regulatory decision — high durability, aftermath outside the 2009 run.** GAPP ultimately approved The Burning Crusade as online-game published goods. The delayed resolution did not restore relative leadership by the 2010-04-06 252-session endpoint. [NetEase release, published 2010-02-12](https://ir.netease.com/news-releases/news-release-details/neteasecom-announces-receipt-approval-general-administration).

- **Compressed prior — present, qualified.** On 2009-02-26 NTES was 30.39% below its prior 252-session high and had fallen 17.65% from the first 2009 close; its trailing 21-session range was 10.42%, a damaged expectation base rather than a textbook low-volatility shelf (`data/yahoo/NTES.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** The February self-developed-game proof was followed by the April Blizzard license, May earnings confirmation, July beta permission, and September commercial launch; regulation published [2009-10-12](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/china-tightens-regulation-on-foreign-online-game-operation) and [2009-11-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000119312509221195/d6k.htm), plus Q3 margin pressure published/filed [2009-11-19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110646/000119312509237951/d6k.htm), then reversed part of the ladder.

- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** The `t0` session itself was quiet at `z21=-0.46`, but the five/60 ratio was 2.27; the February economic break was above +3.2σ on both windows, and April 15, May 21, July 22, and September 23 supplied repeated capacity-scale expansion.
