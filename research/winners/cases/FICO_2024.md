# FICO 2024 winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

FICO's 2024 alpha was chiefly a scarce-asset/pricing-power rerating, not a rebound in end demand: the Scores franchise converted large unit-price increases into 19%-27% quarterly segment growth even while mortgage-origination volumes and consumer volumes were weak, while the smaller FICO Platform supplied a second runway through roughly 31%-32% platform ARR growth. Two guidance raises, on April 25 and July 31, converted that thesis into reported evidence; buybacks and a falling share count amplified EPS. The tape had already begun re-underwriting the name in May and broke to a 252-session closing high on July 3. Thus the mechanical August 6 onset was liquidity confirmation of an established move, not its economic beginning. The distinction matters: FICO finished 2024 20.30% above the onset versus 16.32% for XLK and 12.97% for SPY, but the repo's fixed-horizon record shows only +1.81pp forward excess at 21 days, +8.56pp at 63 days, and -3.44pp at 126 days. It was a real 2024 winner whose late mechanical signal did not sustain large medium-term alpha.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — false/absent.** FICO was already making repeated 63/126-day highs in January-March and was +31.75% over 126 sessions by August 6. This was continuation from a high base, not release from a compressed price base.
- **Catalyst ladder — true.** April and July each paired double-digit company growth, price-led Scores growth, ~31%-32% platform ARR growth, and a guidance raise; November extended the evidence after onset.
- **Relative breakaway — true.** At August 6, excess return was +22.61pp over 21 sessions and +29.39pp over 42 sessions versus XLK, with a fresh 63-day high.
- **Liquidity confirmation — true.** Census dollar-volume z21 was 2.02; independently reconstructed z21/z60 were 2.07/1.75, and the August 6 close-to-close impulse held 3/5/10 sessions.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2024 per-ticker options history exists. Current option structure cannot be projected backward.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

PIT coverage is uneven. `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet` is annual, uses `asof_date`, and at the August onset contained FY2023 (available 2024-04-29): revenue $1.514bn, operating income $642.8m (42.5% operating margin), net income $429.4m, CFO $468.9m, and $405.5m of repurchases. Versus FY2022, revenue grew 9.9%, operating income 18.5%, and shares fell 1.1%; FY2022 itself had $1.377bn revenue and a 39.4% operating margin. This was already a high-margin, self-funded model rather than an early operating turn.

Quarterly detail in `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet` shows the acceleration, but its `as_of` ingestion timestamp is 2026-07-12, so those rows are **not PIT-eligible local evidence at 2024 t0** under the requested gate. They are included only as later reconstruction and agree with the dated public releases: fiscal Q1/Q2/Q3 2024 revenue was $382.1m/$433.8m/$447.8m, gross margin 78.2%/80.0%/80.3%, and operating margin 39.6%/44.9%/42.5%. `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` is properly announcement-gated and reports GAAP EPS of $4.80 on January 25, $5.16 on April 25, and $5.05 on July 31.

Full FY2024, unavailable at t0 but subsequently present in `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet` with 2025-04-30 `asof_date`, reached $1.718bn revenue (+13.5%), $733.6m operating income (42.7% margin), $513m net income, and $633m CFO; shares fell another 1.8% while repurchases rose to $821.7m. Runway was two-engine but asymmetric: Scores monetization delivered the near-term step-up while platform ARR near 31% supplied a longer-duration path; non-platform software and B2C were weak, and the FY2024 annual row cannot explain an August decision without lookahead. `data/edgar/statements.parquet` has no FICO rows, a stated coverage gap.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** Large-cap, high-dollar-volume rerating accompanied by repeated earnings/guidance evidence; no local evidence of a retail-social burst or low float.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The stock made successive highs from May through August, and the August impulse held 3/5/10 sessions; multiple public events carried the thesis.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** At onset XLK was -14.45% over 21 sessions while FICO was +8.17%; FICO exceeded both XLK and SPY at every requested lookback.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Historical options evidence is unavailable, so it cannot be affirmatively excluded; however, the price/volume and fundamental record does not require options to explain the move.

Current hazards are temporally separate from the 2024 episode. Extension after a vertical move is **not current**: 2026-07-18 spot is far below the 2024 year-end region, though volatility is high. Call-wall overhead is **possible** at $1,250, only 1.32% above current spot, but the snapshot is assumption-based and display-only. Catalyst exhaustion is **possible** because repeated price-led growth raises regulatory/competitive sensitivity and the late-onset episode already faded to -3.44pp forward 126-day excess. The clean-hold label and absence of a 2024 blow-off do not erase that decay in relative follow-through.
