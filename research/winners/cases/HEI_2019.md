# HEI 2019 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

HEICO's 2019 alpha was an earnings-and-capital-allocation rerating layered on a healthy aerospace cycle, not a one-day headline and not merely Industrials beta: strong peer aerospace results first reduced concern about the cycle—Honeywell reported 10% organic Aerospace growth and raised its outlook on 2019-04-18—then HEICO's 2019-05-28 fiscal-Q2 release delivered the decisive company-specific shock, with 20% sales growth, 30% operating-income growth, a 180-basis-point margin expansion, 15% organic growth in Flight Support, 20% organic growth in Electronic Technologies, and simultaneous increases to sales, income, margin, and cash-flow expectations ([Honeywell, published 2019-04-18](https://investor.honeywell.com/news-releases/news-release-details/honeywell-delivers-strong-first-quarter-raises-2019-sales-and); [HEICO Q2 exhibit, published/filed 2019-05-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46619/000004661919000037/q2fy19exhibit991.htm)). The 2019-06-03 REI and 2019-07-02 Bernier acquisitions extended the familiar reinvestment runway rather than creating the move by themselves ([REI, published 2019-06-03](https://reiusa.net/page/2/?cat=0&submit=view); [Bernier, published 2019-07-02](https://www.airframer.com/news_story.html?release=71066)). The move was real but not durable at the original rate: the 2019-08-27 Q3 release still raised guidance, yet quarterly growth had slowed to 14% sales and 18% operating income, ETG organic growth had slowed to 7%, valuation resistance was already public, and the largest later turnover sessions became predominantly negative ([HEICO Q3 exhibit, published/filed 2019-08-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46619/000004661919000059/q3fy19exhibit991.htm); [Schaeffer's/BofA valuation report, published 2019-07-11](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2019/07/11/wall-street-cools-on-these-2-stocks)). HEI therefore retained a strong calendar-2019 gain but surrendered all post-`t0` alpha by year-end and was behind both XLI and SPY by 126 trading days: the operating engine held, while the rate of positive estimate change and the multiple stopped expanding.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

The local filing surfaces have an important coverage hole: `data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` has no HEI rows in 2019, and `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` has no HEI rows at all. The SEC archive shows Q1, Q2, and Q3 earnings 8-Ks filed on 2019-02-26, 2019-05-28, and 2019-08-27, respectively. The SEC dates below therefore replace, rather than confirm, missing local anchors.

1. **2018-12-17 — earnings — fiscal-2018 baseline and balance-sheet runway.** HEICO published fiscal-2018 sales of $1.778 billion (+17%), operating income of $376.2 million (+23%), a 21.2% operating margin versus 20.1%, operating cash flow of $328.5 million, net debt/EBITDA of 1.04×, and no significant debt maturities until fiscal 2023. The 2019 leg began from a proven operating and reinvestment base, not a turnaround. [SEC exhibit, published/filed 2018-12-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46619/000004661918000115/a10312018q4exhibit991.htm). **Durability: high.**

2. **2019-02-01 — M&A activity — Solid Sealing Technology.** HEICO disclosed the all-cash acquisition of 85% of SST, its sixth announced acquisition in twelve months, and expected earnings accretion within a year. SST added high-reliability feedthroughs and connectors across defense, industrial, medical, research, and semiconductor applications. [Business Wire text, published 2019-02-01](https://www.voxmarkets.co.uk/rns/announcement/780f2962-e2a6-4124-ba18-c077e0deebb6). **Durability: medium.**

3. **2019-02-26 — earnings/guidance — first operating proof and guide-up.** Fiscal-Q1 sales rose 15% to $466.1 million, operating income 23% to $97.9 million, and the operating margin to 21.0% from 19.7%; FSG and ETG organic growth were 13% and 12%. HEICO increased expected fiscal-2019 sales growth to 9%-11% from 8%-10%, net-income growth to 11%-13% from about 10%, and operating cash flow to roughly $370 million from $360 million. The next session did not gap and closed -0.74%, so this was fundamental confirmation rather than the tape break. [SEC exhibit, published/filed 2019-02-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46619/000004661919000014/q1fy19earningsreleaseexhib.htm). **Durability: high.**

4. **2019-04-18 — other/peer read-through — aerospace demand remained strong.** Honeywell reported 10% organic Aerospace sales growth, strength in commercial aftermarket and defense, a 260-basis-point Aerospace margin expansion, and raised company sales and earnings guidance. This does not explain HEICO-specific alpha, but it reduced the sector-cycle objection during HEI's April advance. [Honeywell, published 2019-04-18](https://investor.honeywell.com/news-releases/news-release-details/honeywell-delivers-strong-first-quarter-raises-2019-sales-and). **Durability: medium.**

5. **2019-05-28 — earnings/guidance — decisive re-underwriting event.** Fiscal-Q2 sales rose 20% to $515.6 million, operating income 30% to $119.2 million, operating margin to 23.1% from 21.3%, and first-half operating cash flow 72% to $178.3 million. FSG organic growth was 15%; ETG organic growth was 20% and its margin rose to 31.4% from 28.5%. HEICO raised fiscal-2019 sales growth to 12%-13% from 9%-11%, net-income growth to 17%-18% from 11%-13%, margin to 21.5%-22.0%, and cash flow to about $380 million. [SEC exhibit, published/filed 2019-05-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46619/000004661919000037/q2fy19exhibit991.htm). A contemporaneous estimate comparison put EPS at $0.60 versus $0.49 expected and sales at $515.6 million versus $478 million expected. [Zacks via Investing.com, published 2019-05-28](https://www.investing.com/analysis/heico-corp-hei-q2-earnings-top-estimates-guidance-raised-200426077). **Durability: high.**

6. **2019-06-03 — M&A activity — Research Electronics International.** HEICO acquired 75% of REI in cash while owner-managers retained 25%; REI made technical-surveillance countermeasure equipment, and HEICO expected accretion within twelve months. This widened ETG's niche-electronics runway immediately after the earnings break. [REI, published 2019-06-03](https://reiusa.net/page/2/?cat=0&submit=view). **Durability: medium.**

7. **2019-07-02 — M&A activity — Bernier.** HEICO's 3D PLUS subsidiary acquired the French interconnect business in cash and expected accretion within twelve months, extending ETG in defense/aerospace communications components. The stock rose 2.59% that session but did not open with a qualifying 5% gap. [Airframer/HEICO release, published 2019-07-02](https://www.airframer.com/news_story.html?release=71066). **Durability: medium.**

8. **2019-08-27 — earnings/guidance — confirmation, then expectation saturation.** Fiscal-Q3 sales rose 14% to $532.3 million, operating income 18% to $119.4 million, and operating margin to 22.4% from 21.8%; FSG organic growth remained 12%, while ETG organic growth slowed to 7%. HEICO again raised fiscal-2019 expectations—to 14%-15% sales growth, 23%-24% net-income growth, about 22% margin, and about $405 million cash flow. [SEC exhibit, published/filed 2019-08-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46619/000004661919000059/q3fy19exhibit991.htm). The first full post-release session traded the run's largest post-`t0` dollar volume but closed down 0.92%; price peaked one session later. **Durability: high for operations, low for incremental rerating.**

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** May was a 21-session shelf (+0.04% through May 28), but only 1.68% below the prior 252-session high; the first calendar-year 252-session high had already occurred on February 22. This was a continuation base near highs, not a deep compression.

- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Fiscal-2018 proof, Q1 and Q2 guide-ups, healthy peer aerospace data, and three niche acquisitions created distinct rungs; Q3 supplied a final operating confirmation.

- **Relative breakaway — present.** On May 29 HEI cleared the prior 63/126/252-session closing highs, with +17.27 pp/21d excess versus XLI and +16.24 pp versus SPY; forward excess expanded through day 63.

- **Liquidity confirmation — present, then two-sided.** `t0` dollar volume was z21 +2.43/z60 +4.28, and the 8.25% opening gap held through ten sessions. Four of the five largest later run-window turnover sessions were negative, warning that confirmation became distribution-like after the August peak.

- **Options convexity — unknown.** Per-ticker options history begins only in 2026-06. No 2019 chain, open-interest, volatility, or trade-level NBBO record exists locally, so historical convexity cannot be asserted or rejected.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

### Repo coverage

All four requested PIT surfaces are null for HEI: `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` contain zero HEI rows. There is therefore no local `asof_date`/`as_of` series to compute. The figures below come from contemporaneous SEC exhibits and filings, with their publication/filing dates stated explicitly; they are not backfilled into a local panel.

### Before the run

The 2018-12-17 release showed a quality compounder with room to reinvest: fiscal-2018 revenue was **$1.778 billion, +17%**; operating income **$376.2 million, +23%**; consolidated operating margin **21.2%, up 110 basis points**; and operating cash flow **$328.5 million, +14%**. Net debt/EBITDA had fallen to **1.04×** from 1.67×, and significant debt maturities did not begin until fiscal 2023. [SEC exhibit, published/filed 2018-12-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46619/000004661918000115/a10312018q4exhibit991.htm).

The 2019-02-26 Q1 release advanced both engines: consolidated sales/operating income grew **15%/23%**, margin improved **130 basis points**, FSG organic sales grew **13%** on aftermarket replacement and specialty products, and ETG organic sales grew **12%** on defense/aerospace/space demand. Q1 operating cash flow itself was $49.6 million versus $51.9 million, a small year-over-year decline, but full-year cash-flow expectations rose. The reported net-income comparison also included discrete tax benefits in both periods, so operating income and margin are cleaner evidence than headline EPS. [SEC exhibit, published/filed 2019-02-26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46619/000004661919000014/q1fy19earningsreleaseexhib.htm).

### During the breakaway

Q2 was the acceleration point: revenue **$515.6 million, +20%**; operating income **$119.2 million, +30%**; margin **23.1% versus 21.3%**; FSG organic growth **15%**; ETG organic growth **20%**; and first-half operating cash flow **$178.3 million, +72%**. The guide changed on four connected axes—growth, profit, margin, and cash—so capital could lift both the near-term earnings base and the confidence assigned to future acquisitions. [SEC exhibit, published/filed 2019-05-28](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46619/000004661919000037/q2fy19exhibit991.htm).

The Q2 Form 10-Q said HEICO had **$178.3 million** of first-half operating cash flow, a **33.5% debt/equity ratio**, compliance with its covenants, and enough operating cash plus revolver availability to fund at least the next twelve months. [SEC Form 10-Q, filed 2019-05-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46619/000004661919000039/a43019heiform10q.htm). Runway was therefore operationally funded and lightly levered relative to the acquisition strategy; it was not dependent on a near-term equity financing.

Q3 confirmed the business but revealed deceleration at the margin: revenue growth eased to **14%**, operating-income growth to **18%**, ETG organic growth to **7%**, and ETG operating margin to **28.8% from 30.1%**, partly reflecting acquisition-related costs and compensation; FSG organic growth remained **12%** and its margin rose to **20.2% from 19.2%**. Nine-month operating cash flow was **$313.4 million, +46%**; net debt/EBITDA was still only **1.11×** despite six fiscal-year acquisitions. [SEC exhibit, published/filed 2019-08-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46619/000004661919000059/q3fy19exhibit991.htm). The fundamental arc remained positive, but the second derivative no longer matched the valuation's new demands.
