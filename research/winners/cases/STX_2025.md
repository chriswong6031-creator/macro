## 1. Bottom line

Seagate's 2025 alpha was an **HDD-cycle recovery re-underwritten as a durable profit algorithm**, not an AI-storage headline by itself: revenue and gross margin had already rebounded before t0, but the May 22 investor day joined HAMR-led areal-density gains, disciplined unit capacity, long demand visibility, a low-to-mid-teens FY2025-FY2028 revenue target, 40%+ non-GAAP gross margin, >75% free-cash-flow returns and a $5 billion repurchase authorization into one model ([Seagate investor day, published 2025-05-22](https://investors.seagate.com/news/news-details/2025/Seagate-Highlights-Strategy-to-Build-Long-Term-Value-in-Todays-Data-driven-World-at-2025-Investor-and-Analyst-Event/default.aspx)).
