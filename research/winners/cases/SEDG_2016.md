# SEDG 2016 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## Bottom line

SEDG's apparent February 2016 alpha was a genuine but already-late re-underwriting of a newly public, profitable solar-electronics grower: the December 2015 solar-tax-credit extension lengthened the industry runway, fiscal Q2 results published February 3 beat revenue and EPS expectations, margins expanded, HD-Wave and StorEdge began shipping, and next-quarter guidance topped consensus ([Congressional passage reported 2015-12-18](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/updated-congress-passes-11t-omnibus-spending-bill-with-solar-wind-tax-c/411115/); [SolarEdge results republished 2016-02-04](https://ar.enfsolar.com/news/10991/solaredge-announces-fiscal-second-quarter-financial-results); [StreetInsider, published 2016-02-03](https://www.streetinsider.com/Earnings/SolarEdge%2BTechnologies%2B%28SEDG%29%2BTops%2BQ2%2BEPS%2Bby%2B8c%2C%2BQ3%2BGuidance%2BTops%2BViews/11276799.html)). But the mosaic contained its own failure: the stock had already gained 57.13% in 42 sessions, the fiscal Q2 GAAP EPS included a one-time tax asset, sequential revenue guidance implied no further growth, and SolarCity publicly disclosed a sharper-than-usual seasonal slowdown five days later ([SolarCity filing, published 2016-02-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1408356/000156459016012513/scty-ex991_7.htm)). The tape then converted from launch to distribution; flat sequential revenue, softer customer demand, and repeated target cuts overwhelmed still-good margins and cash generation. SEDG did not merely lose its temporary lead: from t0 through 126 sessions it returned -44.21%, trailing XLK by 60.02 percentage points, and it finished 2016 down 55.98% while XLK rose 15.01%.

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`): the run did NOT sustain.**

## Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** SEDG had traded publicly for less than a year, and the 126-session return at t0 was -3.93%, but the relevant prior was no longer compressed: the stock had already risen 57.13% in 42 sessions (`data/yahoo/SEDG.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present, then reversed.** The ITC extension, earnings beat, margin expansion, product shipments, and raised near-term expectations were real; SolarCity's slowdown and two flat sequential SEDG revenue quarters removed incremental runway.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed immediately.** Trailing 42-session excess versus XLK was +63.23 pp at t0; forward excess was -11.97 pp by 21 sessions and -60.02 pp by 126 (`data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`; `data/yahoo/{SEDG,XLK}.parquet`).
- **Liquidity confirmation — present but directionally unstable.** T0 registered z21 3.76, yet the largest annual dollar-volume session was May 10's -15.14% distribution print (`data/yahoo/SEDG.parquet`).
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2016 history or current SEDG snapshot exists in the permitted options surfaces, and no trade-level NBBO record supports signed-flow claims.

These are local normalization gaps, not assertions that public investors lacked the results.

Publication-dated releases fill the arc. Before the episode, FY2015 revenue had grown 144.0% to $325.1M, gross margin rose from 16.5% to 25.2%, operating income swung from a loss to $28.3M, and net income reached $21.1M ([SolarEdge, published 2015-08-12](https://investors.solaredge.com/news-releases/news-release-details/solaredge-announces-fiscal-fourth-quarter-and-year-end-2015)). At the launch, fiscal Q2 revenue grew 70.3% year over year to $124.8M, margin reached 30.9%, operating income was $19.3M, operating cash generation was $13.1M, and liquid resources were $162.0M; GAAP net income of $24.1M included a $6.6M tax asset, so $0.55 GAAP EPS overstated recurring quarter-on-quarter improvement ([issuer release republished 2016-02-04](https://ar.enfsolar.com/news/10991/solaredge-announces-fiscal-second-quarter-financial-results)).

During the unwind, annual fundamentals remained strong while marginal growth faded. FY2016 revenue grew 50.7% to $489.8M, gross margin rose to 31.0%, operating income reached $71.8M, net income reached $76.6M, cash and securities were $186.6M, and debt was zero; however, fiscal Q3 and Q4 revenues were $125.2M and $124.8M, essentially flat with fiscal Q2 ([SolarEdge, published 2016-08-09](https://investors.solaredge.com/node/6636/pdf)). The later PIT annual row in `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet` confirms FY2016 revenue $489.843M, gross profit $151.956M (31.02%), operating income $71.759M (14.65%), net income $76.609M, and operating cash flow $52.530M, but its `asof_date=2017-04-30` means it is a later check, not t0 evidence. Runway was balance-sheet ample; the failed breakaway came from the market capitalizing peak growth before downstream demand and sequential revenue proved durable.

The notable revision was negative rather than confirmatory: Deutsche Bank cut its target from $36 to $27 on May 10, after SEDG had already closed May 9 at $22.59 and then closed May 10 at $19.17 ([Schaeffer's, published 2016-05-10](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2016/05/10/analyst-update-solaredge-technologies-inc-valeant-pharmaceuticals-intl-inc-and-tetra-technologies-inc); `data/yahoo/SEDG.parquet`). This is the inverse “price escaped the published model” read: price broke below the bullish consensus first, then the model followed. The February target premium therefore described hope embedded in the old growth slope, not independent evidence that the move could persist.

## Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** The 57.13% 42-session rise and t0 volume spike permit speculative amplification, but 2016 retail-attention and PIT short-interest evidence are absent; the earnings beat and cash generation show the move was not purely social.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** T0 was earnings-driven, but the premise had accumulated through the ITC extension, FY2015 growth, product launches, and fiscal Q2 execution. Failure unfolded across customer and issuer updates rather than one binary reversal.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** SEDG led XLK by 63.23 pp over the trailing 42 sessions, then lagged it by 60.02 pp over the next 126; broad Information Technology beta cannot explain either magnitude (`data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`).
