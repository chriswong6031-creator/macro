# AMAT 2020 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

AMAT was not a full-calendar-2020 sector-alpha story: it returned 43.29% in 2020, 0.33 percentage points less than XLK, although it beat SPY by 24.96 points. The real episode was the late-year handoff from recovery to a semiconductor-capital-equipment upcycle. Summer product evidence and improving wafer-fab-equipment forecasts built the prior; the November 12 report then showed 25% quarterly revenue growth, a 4.4-point GAAP operating-margin expansion and a higher next-quarter revenue run-rate, while the November 13 gap held. TSMC's January 14 capital budget of $25-$28 billion converted an issuer forecast into customer-side spending evidence, and AMAT's February quarter showed that demand becoming 24% revenue growth, better underlying margins and another guide-up. Product breadth in foundry/logic, memory, advanced packaging and services made AMAT more than a high-beta shortage proxy, but the mechanism remained a capital-spending cycle with execution and product breadth layered on top. The five-month vertical leg finally met expectation saturation around the April AIx launch and investor meeting: the April 5 gap failed at 3/5/10 sessions and the next day produced a large distribution print. Even so, this was digestion rather than episode failure—forward excess versus XLK was 41.43 points at 63 sessions, 56.63 points at 126 sessions and 68.41 points at 252 sessions. Public anchors: [Applied Materials Selective Tungsten release, published 2020-07-20](https://ir.appliedmaterials.com/news-releases/news-release-details/applied-materials-solves-major-bottleneck-continued-2d-scaling/), [SEMI forecast, published 2020-07-21](https://www.semi.org/en/news-media-press/semi-press-releases/mid-year-equipment-forecast), [Applied Materials Sym3 Y release, published 2020-08-07](https://ir.appliedmaterials.com/news-releases/news-release-details/applied-materials-introduces-new-sym3r-etch-system-advanced/), [Applied Materials-Besi release, published 2020-10-22](https://ir.appliedmaterials.com/news-releases/news-release-details/applied-materials-and-be-semiconductor-industries-accelerate/), [Applied Materials Q4 FY2020 release, published 2020-11-12](https://ir.appliedmaterials.com/news-releases/news-release-details/applied-materials-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2020/), [TSMC Q4 release, published 2021-01-14](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/2781), [Applied Materials Q1 FY2021 release, published 2021-02-18](https://ir.appliedmaterials.com/news-releases/news-release-details/applied-materials-announces-first-quarter-2021-results/), [Applied Materials AIx release, published 2021-04-05](https://ir.appliedmaterials.com/node/24181/pdf), and [Applied Materials investor-meeting release, published 2021-04-06](https://ir.appliedmaterials.com/news-releases/news-release-details/applied-materials-showcases-unique-capabilities-accelerate).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** AMAT had a reset, not a tight low-volatility base: from 2020-07-31 through 2020-11-12 the adjusted high-low range was 22.86%, annualized close-to-close volatility was 39.2%, and the stock ended only 2.10% below the November 9 high. The failed August earnings gap was useful expectation repair, but “compression” overstates it.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** The 14 publication-dated, URL-cited rungs in §3 span 2020-07-20 through 2021-04-06 and cover product, industry, earnings, customer-capex, capital-return and long-range-model evidence.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** On 2020-11-18, trailing excess reached +21.06pp/21d and +25.40pp/42d versus XLK; forward excess expanded to +31.60pp/42d and +56.63pp/126d.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** `t0` dollar-volume z-scores were 1.61/1.73 versus 21/60 days, the November 13 and February 19 earnings gaps held all three checkpoints, and four late-run sessions exceeded $2.1 billion.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** Per-ticker options history starts in 2026-06. No 2020-2021 GEX, IV, open-interest surface or trade-level NBBO exists, so options cannot be credited with causing or sustaining the episode.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

Episode hazards:

- **Extension/exhaustion:** from `t0` through 2021-04-05, AMAT rose 89.3%. The April 5 opening gap failed at 3/5/10 sessions, and April 6 printed $2.405 billion of dollar volume on a -2.45% day. The stock then digested rather than immediately beginning a second vertical leg.
- **Cycle and customer concentration:** the FY2020 10-K said the served industries were historically cyclical and that a limited number of geographically concentrated manufacturers represented a substantial portion of business. A customer-capex delay or mix shift could reverse operating leverage quickly. [Form 10-K, filed 2020-12-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/6951/000000695120000048/amat-20201025.htm).
- **Trade/export exposure:** the same filing said about 90% of FY2020 sales were outside the United States and described 2020 export-license changes affecting China-related demand and licenses. [Form 10-K, filed 2020-12-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/6951/000000695120000048/amat-20201025.htm).
- **M&A opportunity cost:** Kokusai ultimately terminated with a $154 million fee; the new repurchase authorization softened the capital-allocation reset but did not replace the lost strategic asset. [SEC exhibits filed 2021-03-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/6951/000119312521088636/d128931dex991.htm) and [2021-03-29](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/6951/000119312521097484/d165560dex991.htm).
