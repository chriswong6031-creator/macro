# STLD 2016 winner autopsy

## Bottom line

STLD's 2016 alpha was a layered re-underwriting, not merely a post-election steel trade: an early-cycle recovery in domestic sheet spreads and trade protection had already repaired earnings, the cash-funded Vulcan acquisition added a small higher-margin downstream leg, and the October quarter showed that realized steel pricing was outrunning scrap costs while liquidity exceeded $2.2 billion. Donald Trump's November 8 victory then converted that improving company setup into a much larger policy-duration story—domestic-content infrastructure, trade protection and lower taxes—causing a 9.96% close-to-close jump on November 9, a simultaneous 63/126/252-session closing breakout, and 3.13-sigma dollar volume. The distinction matters: STLD had gained 29.92% in the 21 sessions into t0 versus 3.78% for XLB, then gained another 18.37% over the next 21 sessions, but its forward excess versus XLB faded from +11.66 percentage points at 21 sessions to -0.28 by 126. The market correctly recognized a real margin recovery and unusually strong balance-sheet runway, but initially capitalized an uncertain policy promise too quickly; December guidance for sequentially lower shipments and compressed spreads exposed that mismatch.

## Catalyst ladder

- **2016-08-01 — downstream acquisition (high durability):** STLD completed the $114 million, cash-funded Vulcan Threaded Products acquisition, described as immediately accretive and a move toward higher-margin downstream products intended to reduce cyclicality. Publication date: 2016-08-01. [Steel Dynamics release](https://ir.steeldynamics.com/steel-dynamics-completes-acquisition-of-vulcan-threaded-products/).
- **2016-09-19 — earnings guidance (medium durability):** third-quarter EPS guidance of $0.63-$0.67 versus $0.58 sequentially and $0.25 a year earlier made the spread recovery legible before the election; management attributed improvement to flat-roll metal-spread expansion while flagging challenged energy/agriculture demand. Publication date: 2016-09-19. [Steel Dynamics release](https://ir.steeldynamics.com/steel-dynamics-provides-third-quarter-2016-guidance-and-announces-third-quarter-2016-cash-dividend/).
- **2016-10-19 / 2016-10-25 filing anchor — earnings (high durability):** adjusted Q3 EPS was $0.65, net sales $2.1 billion, steel operating income rose 13% sequentially, nine-month operating income rose 118% year over year, and liquidity exceeded $2.2 billion. Publication date: 2016-10-19; the repo's Item 2.02 earnings 8-K anchor is 2016-10-25 in `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`. [Steel Dynamics release](https://ir.steeldynamics.com/steel-dynamics-reports-third-quarter-2016-results/).
- **2016-10-26 — research re-underwriting (medium durability):** Bank of America upgraded STLD one day after the earnings filing anchor. Publication/action date: 2016-10-26. [Fintel historical ratings ledger](https://fintel.io/sfo/us/stld).
- **2016-11-09 — election/policy shock (medium durability):** the market repriced steelmakers for prospective infrastructure spending and trade protection after Donald Trump's election; contemporaneous reporting recorded STLD up 8.9% intraday with volume tracking toward an annual high. Publication date: 2016-11-09. [Schaeffer's contemporaneous report](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/options/2016/11/09/call-players-take-aim-at-2-surging-steel-stocks); [Washington Post macro report](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/economy/wall-street-rallies-with-optimism-in-trumps-business-friendly-plans/2016/11/09/46a97e8a-a6b4-11e6-8fc0-7be8f848c492_story.html).

## Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** STLD was already +79.70% YTD and +32.75% over 126 sessions at t0. There was a short September/early-October pause, but not a genuinely compressed long prior.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Accretive downstream M&A, improving Q3 guidance/results, an analyst upgrade, then the election policy shock successively broadened the earnings and duration narratives.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** November 9 was simultaneously a 63/126/252-session closing high; 21-session trailing excess was +26.14 pp to XLB and +28.54 pp to SPY.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 dollar volume was 3.13 standard deviations above the inclusive 21-session baseline and the five-day/60-day dollar-volume ratio was 1.64.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** A contemporaneous article reported heavy call activity, but the repo has no 2016 chain or trade-level NBBO history; direction, dealer positioning, and causal contribution cannot be established.

The weak point was cyclicality, not funding: December 16 guidance showed that realized pricing and scrap spreads could reverse within one quarter.

## Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out:** STLD was a liquid, profitable steel producer; the move followed earnings/spread improvement and a cohort-wide policy repricing. Short-interest evidence is null, so this does not rule out some squeeze contribution.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out:** the election created the largest breakaway day, but the November 9 level held for 10 sessions and STLD added 18.37% over the next 21 sessions.
- **sector_beta — likely:** the policy shock lifted steel broadly, and forward STLD excess versus XLB decayed to -0.28 pp by 126 sessions. However, the +26.14 pp trailing excess at t0 and stronger company margin arc show that sector beta was not the whole pre-t0 story.
- **options_mirage — possible:** contemporaneous calls were unusually active, but historical chains, signed flow, and dealer positioning are unavailable; options causality is therefore unknown.

Current hazards are episode-context nulls rather than live claims: **extension** was likely at the 2016-12-07 peak (+24.50% in 19 sessions from t0); **call walls** are unknown; **catalyst exhaustion** was likely after the election premium was capitalized and December 16 guidance reintroduced near-term spread compression. No current STLD options snapshot exists in the specified repo sources.
