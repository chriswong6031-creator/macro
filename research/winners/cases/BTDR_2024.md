# BTDR 2024 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

BTDR's June–July alpha was a real but premature **platform-optionality rerating layered on bitcoin-miner beta**: a washed-out, unusually quiet prior met a $100 million Tether equity placement, an all-stock ASIC-team acquisition, a multi-generation SEALMINER roadmap, a 570 MW Ohio lease, and rising H100-cloud utilization, letting capital model Bitdeer as a future ASIC vendor plus scarce-power/HPC platform rather than only a miner ([Bitdeer, published 2024-05-31](https://ir.bitdeer.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bitdeer-announces-150-million-private-placement-financing); [Bitdeer, published 2024-06-06](https://ir.bitdeer.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bitdeer-announces-acquisition-desiweminer-all-stock-transaction); [Bitdeer, published 2024-06-28](https://ir.bitdeer.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bitdeer-expands-power-capacity-pipeline-25-gw-570-mw-strategic-0); [Bitdeer, published 2024-07-03](https://ir.bitdeer.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bitdeer-announces-june-2024-production-and-operations-update)). That mosaic explains why BTDR gained 86.59% in 21 sessions to 2024-07-03 while miner ETF WGMI gained 40.20% and SPY 4.82%. It did not sustain because the re-underwriting outran the conversion evidence: June output was only 179 BTC after 184 in May, the proprietary rigs were still samples and future production, the 570 MW was mostly a 2025–26 opportunity, and the t0 volume reading was the tail of a three-week expansion rather than a fresh shock. The later Q2 publication showed sequentially lower revenue and gross profit, post-halving contraction in cloud-hash and hosting lines, and $74.1 million of operating cash use, while an August 15 convertible-note proposal made the financing bridge explicit ([Bitdeer, published 2024-08-12](https://ir.bitdeer.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bitdeer-reported-unaudited-financial-results-second-quarter-2024); [Bitdeer, published 2024-08-15](https://ir.bitdeer.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bitdeer-announces-proposed-offering-us150-million-convertible)). BTDR consequently lost 37.83% over the next 21 sessions and 49.28% over 42; its later November–December recovery was a separate mechanical episode, not a clean hold of the July breakaway.

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`); the run did not sustain.**
