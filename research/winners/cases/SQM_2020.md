# SQM 2020 — winner autopsy

## Bottom line

SQM's 2020 alpha was a re-underwriting of the *next* lithium cycle while the income statement still described the trough: pandemic-hit first-quarter volumes recovered sharply, third-quarter lithium volume rose more than 40% sequentially, management kept expanding low-cost Chilean capacity, and the market began capitalizing EV-driven demand several years forward. The October sustainability plan reduced a company-specific Atacama permitting/ESG discount, while the November 7 U.S. election outcome and November 9 vaccine news strengthened the clean-energy-plus-reopening demand mosaic. That combination explains why SQM beat both XLB and SPY well before earnings visibly recovered. It also explains the limit: the episode was a clean hold, not a fully durable 126-day winner under the repo definition; falling realized lithium prices, weak contemporaneous profits, later equity financing and rapid multiple expansion left the stock dependent on forward demand expectations.

## Catalyst ladder

- **2020-05-19 — first-quarter trough disclosed.** Revenue fell 22.3% year over year to $392.0m and gross margin slipped to 27.5%, but SQM said operations remained normal, held about $1.4bn cash, cut 2020 capex to $350m, and expected lithium volume to improve in the remaining quarters. Publication date: 2020-05-19. [Company release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sqm-reports-earnings-for-the-three-months-ended-march-31-2020-301062297.html).
- **2020-08-19 — sequential volume recovery became visible.** Second-quarter lithium volume exceeded 12,600 tonnes despite lithium revenue remaining 38% below the prior year. Publication date: 2020-08-19. [SQM release PDF](https://ir.sqm.com/system/files-encrypted/nasdaq_kms/news/2024/11/26/10-25-44/PR_2Q20_ing_FINAL.pdf).
- **2020-10-08 — Atacama ESG/permitting discount addressed.** SQM announced immediate water/brine reductions, a 50% brine-reduction goal and carbon-neutral lithium by 2030. This did not create demand, but it made long-duration capacity easier for capital to underwrite. Publication date: 2020-10-08. [SQM announcement](https://sqm.com/en/noticia/sqm-reducira-en-50-la-extraccion-de-salmuera-del-salar-de-atacama-y-en-40-su-consumo-hidrico-en-todas-sus-operaciones-2/).
- **2020-11-07 — U.S. policy regime changed.** Biden was projected president with a climate agenda centered on large clean-energy spending and a carbon-free grid; this was a macro demand-duration catalyst for battery materials, not SQM-specific news. Publication date: 2020-11-07. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2020/11/07/president-joe-biden-climate-agenda).
- **2020-11-09 — vaccine/reopening probability jumped.** Pfizer/BioNTech published greater-than-90% interim efficacy, supporting a broad cyclical recovery interpretation on the exact mechanical t0. Publication date: 2020-11-09. [Pfizer release](https://www.pfizer.com/print/pdf/node/530221).
- **2020-11-18 — volume thesis confirmed, earnings remained weak.** Third-quarter lithium volume exceeded 17,600 tonnes, up more than 40% sequentially, with still-higher fourth-quarter volume expected; nine-month revenue was down 11.4%, gross margin was 26.9%, and net income was depressed by a $62.5m settlement. Publication date: 2020-11-18. [Company release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sqm-reports-earnings-for-the-third-quarter-of-2020-301176662.html).
- **2020-12-24 — expansion funding became explicit, with dilution risk.** SQM planned a $1.1bn raise to help finance a $1.9bn lithium/iodine/nitrates program; fourth-quarter lithium volume was expected near 25,000 tonnes, roughly triple first quarter. Publication date: 2020-12-24. [S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/metals/122420-sqm-to-raise-11-billion-to-fund-lithium-expansion-company).
- **2020-12-30 — customer commitment.** SQM announced an agreement to supply LG 55,000 tonnes of battery-grade lithium carbonate and hydroxide. Publication date: 2020-12-30. [SQM](https://sqm.com/en/noticia/sqm-y-lg-firman-alianza/).

## Stage anatomy

1. **Compressed prior — present.** The March pandemic drawdown reset the stock, but the base was already resolving by June; the first post-crash 252-day high arrived August 12. “Compressed” applies to the economic expectations and lithium price trough more than to the tape immediately before t0.
2. **Catalyst ladder — present.** Sequential lithium-volume recovery, October ESG de-risking, the November policy/reopening reset, and November volume guidance progressively shifted attention from trough earnings to 2021–2025 demand and capacity.
3. **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 SQM led SPY by 15.48 pp over 21 days and 27.62 pp over 42 days; it also led XLB by 13.16/25.51 pp.
4. **Liquidity confirmation — present.** The engine's 5d/60d dollar-volume ratio was 1.75×, and the +10.16% November 5 discontinuity held for 10 sessions. The z21 reading of 0.94 alone was just below the detector threshold.
5. **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2020 per-ticker options history exists in the repo, and no current SQM options snapshot exists either.

Before the run, Q1 revenue was $392.0m (-22.3% YoY), gross profit $107.7m (27.5% margin versus 28.9%), and net income $45.0m versus $80.5m. Average lithium prices were almost 50% below the prior year, but SQM had about $1.4bn cash, no relevant maturity until April 2023, and a reduced $350m 2020 capex plan ([2020-05-19 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sqm-reports-earnings-for-the-three-months-ended-march-31-2020-301062297.html)). During the run, nine-month revenue was $1.303bn (-11.4%), gross profit $350.4m (26.9% versus 28.7%), and net income $97.5m versus $211.2m; Q3 revenue was $452.9m (-4.3%) and gross profit $114.8m. The improving variable was physical lithium volume—over 17,600 tonnes in Q3, more than 40% above Q2—not margin ([2020-11-18 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sqm-reports-earnings-for-the-third-quarter-of-2020-301176662.html)). Runway was then enlarged by the planned $1.1bn raise supporting a $1.9bn investment program ([2020-12-24 report](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/metals/122420-sqm-to-raise-11-billion-to-fund-lithium-expansion-company)); economically constructive for capacity, but dilutive and therefore a two-sided catalyst.

## Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** SQM was a large, established commodity producer; the move unfolded over months with repeated highs and improving physical volumes, not a social-media-only burst. Historical short-interest coverage is null.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The November 5 jump held, but the first post-crash long-window high was August 12 and multiple public steps preceded/followed t0.
- **sector_beta — possible.** Election/reopening and EV enthusiasm lifted materials and battery names broadly. Yet 21/42-day excess of 13.16/25.51 pp versus XLB and 15.48/27.62 pp versus SPY rejects a pure-beta explanation.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Historical options evidence is absent, so convexity cannot be ruled in or out; it must not be inferred from price alone.
- **Current hazards — extension / catalyst exhaustion / call walls.** At the episode peak on 2021-01-12 the stock was 43.6% above t0, making extension likely then; the later fade and sub-threshold 126-day excess show catalyst exhaustion was possible. Historical and current call walls are **null**. As of the local 2026-07-17 snapshot, SQM closed at $69.81, but that current price is context only and not part of the 2020 autopsy.
