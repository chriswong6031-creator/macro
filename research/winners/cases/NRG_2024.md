# NRG 2024 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

NRG's 2024 alpha was a cash-flow and capital-allocation rerating that acquired a second, sector-wide power-scarcity leg: the market first gained confidence that Vivint was integrating better than feared, recurring energy/home margins could fund a large and unusually explicit repurchase program, and leverage was moving toward target; then tightening power markets and prospective data-center load increased the perceived runway for NRG's Texas generation, retail book, and load-management platform. The sequence mattered more than any headline: February results preserved $3.3–$3.55 billion of 2024 adjusted EBITDA guidance and $825 million of planned repurchases, March target revisions chased a stock already making highs, May results showed 31% year-over-year adjusted EBITDA growth and completed a $950 million accelerated repurchase at $50.43, and September finally raised guidance. NRG therefore escaped both XLU and SPY because its per-share cash-flow denominator was shrinking while the earnings-duration story expanded; 2024 ended +80.15%, +58.60 percentage points versus XLU and +54.56 points versus SPY. The move did sustain over the year, although the repo census labels the March episode `clean_hold`, not `durable_winner`, because 126-session forward excess was only +3.28 points.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** The immediate setup was not compressed: by 2024-03-01 the stock had a 70.4% one-year gain and was at a record, and the first 252-session high of 2024 came on January 8. The deeper 2023 valuation skepticism around Vivint and leverage existed, but the local tape says t0 was continuation, not release from compression.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** A June 2023 cash-allocation blueprint was followed by February execution, a dense March target ladder, May operating confirmation/data-center optionality, and a September guidance raise.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 NRG led XLU by 16.40pp over 21 sessions and 25.85pp over 42; it finished 2024 58.60pp ahead of XLU.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 met the engine's z21 threshold; the next session printed $1.096 billion of dollar volume at z21 4.01, and the March 5 and May 9 upside gaps held for 10 sessions.
- **Options convexity — unknown for the episode.** Per-ticker history starts in 2026-06, so neither episode-era positioning nor its causal role can be measured. The contemporaneous March 1 article reported relatively low implied volatility and 6.8% short float, but that cannot establish option-driven convexity.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

PIT discipline changes what was knowable. `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet` has annual NRG coverage, but FY2023 carries `asof_date=2024-04-29`, after t0. The latest annual row available before t0 was therefore FY2022 (`asof_date=2023-04-30`): $31.543 billion revenue, $2.018 billion operating income (6.4% operating margin), $360 million CFO, $367 million capex, $7.976 billion long-term debt, and 229.6 million shares. Revenue is noisy for an energy retailer because commodity pass-through and hedge accounting can obscure underlying margin economics.

The during-run quarterly record is clearer. `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, gated by `filed`, reports Q1 2024 on 2024-05-07: $7.429 billion revenue, $872 million operating income (11.7% margin), $511 million net income, and 208.2 million shares. Q2, filed 2024-08-08, had $6.659 billion revenue, $1.410 billion operating income (21.2% margin), $738 million net income, and 207.5 million shares. The share count was about 9.3% below FY2022's annual weighted figure, consistent with the capital-return mechanism. `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` supplies $2.31 Q1 EPS (`asof_date=2024-05-30`) and $3.37 Q2 EPS (`asof_date=2024-08-29`), but its dates lag the actual earnings filings, so it is not used to date catalysts.

For retrospective outcome only—not information available during the 2024 run—`data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet` shows FY2024 (`asof_date=2025-04-30`) revenue of $28.130 billion, operating income of $2.424 billion (8.6% margin), CFO of $2.306 billion, capex of $472 million, long-term debt of $9.812 billion, 198.6 million shares, and $935 million of repurchases. This versus FY2023's later-filed $28.823 billion revenue, $384 million operating income, negative $221 million CFO, and 208.1 million shares demonstrates why revenue growth alone misses the arc: revenue fell 2.4%, while reported operating income and cash conversion recovered and shares contracted. `data/edgar/statements.parquet` is a current extraction (`as_of` in 2026), not a historical filing-time snapshot, so it is used only as a cross-check. Gross profit/cost-of-revenue coverage is null in the annual panel and quarterly statements; gross-margin history is unavailable locally.

Runway at the time rested on the public $550 million of recurring growth/cost opportunities through 2025, the remaining repurchase authorization, target-credit-metric deleveraging, Smart Home cross-sell, and the newly articulated ERCOT/data-center load opportunity. Those were projections, not booked revenue; the September guidance raise was the first 2024 hard upward reset.

## 7. Research echo

Consensus repeatedly lagged spot. On 2024-03-01 Citi moved to $62 with spot near $55.76; after the March breakaway, published targets rose in quick succession to $65/$78/$85, but the stock kept escaping. The pattern repeated after Q1: Evercore's $74 target on 2024-05-07 sat below that day's $73.40 close only briefly, while May 8–28 revisions ranged from $82 to $110 as NRG traded into the $80s. By 2024-05-31, a contemporaneous review put the eight-analyst mean target at $81.38 versus spot around $84.64—a 3.9% modeled downside—while the high target was $110. Published 2024-05-31: https://www.barchart.com/story/news/26561157/are-wall-street-analysts-predicting-nrg-energy-stock-will-climb-or-sink

The “price escaped the published model” read is therefore literal: analysts retained mixed/neutral stances while repeatedly lifting targets, suggesting their base estimates and/or multiples were being marked to a moving tape rather than leading it. The external record does not expose full estimate models, and the repo has no historical analyst-target dataset, so consensus EPS changes are **unknown**. Notable dated revisions are sourced to the archived tape at https://www.benzinga.com/quote/nrg/analyst-ratings (publication dates listed in Section 3); the data-center rationale for Evercore's May 7 revision is sourced separately to https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/nrg-energy-price-target-raised-to-74-on-data-center-growth-93CH-3425524 (published 2024-05-07).

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** Contemporaneous reporting on 2024-03-01 put short interest at 15.18 million shares, 6.8% of float, so squeeze assistance cannot be excluded; however, repeated earnings/capital-allocation events and year-long excess make a pure meme mechanism unlikely.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** There was no single binary event: the episode built across February results, March revisions, May execution, and September guidance. The largest March dollar-volume day followed through.
- **sector_beta — possible.** The later power/data-center rerating was shared by IPPs, and S&P Global reported NRG, Vistra, and Constellation all rising after 2023 earnings commentary (published 2024-04). But NRG's +16.40pp/21d excess versus XLU at onset and +58.60pp full-year excess rule out ordinary utilities beta as a sufficient explanation.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Episode-era options history is absent. Low implied volatility and moderate short interest were publicly noted, but without trade-level NBBO no signed-direction or dealer-causality claim is supportable.
- **Current extension hazard.** The 2024 case itself ended extended after an 80% year; as of 2026-07-18 spot is $129.11 after a much longer rerating, while IV30 is 56%, so sensitivity to expectation resets is high.
- **Current call-wall hazard.** The current $143 wall is 10.76% above spot and classified weak; it is context, not a deterministic ceiling.
- **Catalyst-exhaustion hazard.** The 2024 ladder transitioned from promised capital returns to delivered margin/cash flow and then to a guidance raise. Once repurchases, deleveraging, and data-center optionality are embedded, further rerating requires new earnings or contracted-load evidence; the local case does not establish that future evidence.
- **Operating hazards.** Commodity/hedge accounting can make GAAP earnings volatile; Texas weather, plant outages, retail churn, Vivint execution, leverage, and data-center development/conversion remain material uncertainties. NRG enumerated these risk categories in its 2024 releases, including https://investors.nrg.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nrg-energy-inc-reports-second-quarter-results-and-reaffirms-2024 (published 2024-08-08).
