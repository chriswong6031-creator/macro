# MARA 2021 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

MARA's large 2021 alpha was a levered Bitcoin-cycle repricing dressed as a mining-capacity rerating: a recapitalized microcap pre-bought a very large future fleet, converted $150 million of newly raised cash into Bitcoin, and explicitly marketed itself as a listed pure-play proxy just as Tesla filed disclosure of a $1.5 billion Bitcoin purchase and planned payment acceptance; the formal XLK comparison therefore measured an economically mismatched cohort. The operating ladder was real—hashrate, mined coins, and revenue subsequently rose—but the 2021-03-31 detector fired after a 360% YTD advance and before most ordered miners were installed. A locally extreme one-day turnover print masked weak multi-day expansion, the March 30 compliant-pool story was reversed within two months, the April 5 production update marked the closing high, and Bitcoin's reversal exposed how little current cash earnings supported the equity value. Q1 later showed only $9.2 million of revenue, a $47.1 million operating loss, and net income dominated by a Bitcoin mark, while Tesla's May 12 reversal and China's May 22 crackdown publication attacked the asset and mining narrative together ([MARA Bitcoin purchase, published 2021-01-25](https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1224/marathon-invests-150-million-in-bitcoin); [Tesla Bitcoin filing, filed 2021-02-08](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000156459021004599/tsla-10k_20201231.htm); [MARA Q1 results, published 2021-05-10](https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1240/marathon-reports-first-quarter-2021-results); [Tesla reversal, published 2021-05-12](https://www.axios.com/2021/05/12/elon-musk-suspend-tesla-bitcoin); [China crackdown report, published 2021-05-22](https://finance.caixin.com/2021-05-22/101716652.html)). Later August and November breakaways explain the still-large full-calendar-year return, but they were separate census episodes, not survival of the March onset.

This is a **FAILED breakaway case** (`case_type: failed_breakaway`).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** MARA was +359.96% YTD and +131.53% over 42 sessions at t0. The detector caught a late vertical recross, not a stale or compressed prior.
- **Catalyst ladder — present, but front-loaded and partly reversible.** Miner orders, two capital raises, the Bitcoin treasury, Tesla's disclosure, and the pool launch repeatedly expanded the model; most value depended on future installation or Bitcoin, and the pool's differentiating filter was abandoned on May 31.
- **Relative breakaway — present at t0, not sustained.** Trailing excess was +37.38pp/21d and +128.31pp/42d versus XLK; forward excess was -28.59pp/21d and -49.91pp/126d. Relative strength versus the formal benchmark concealed high crypto-miner commonality.
- **Liquidity confirmation — absent after onset.** T0 z21 was 3.35, but z60 was only 1.33 and the 5d/60d ratio was 0.89. The largest post-t0 turnover day was a -15.75% session, and three of four April gaps failed by day ten.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2021 trade-level options, NBBO, open-interest history, or dealer-state history exists locally. Cash-tape verticality cannot establish an options mechanism.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible:** retail-style verticality and proxy scarcity may have amplified the move, but PIT short-interest history is unavailable and the company had real financing, treasury, equipment, and production events. “Squeeze” cannot be promoted from possibility without the missing positioning data.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out:** the repricing accumulated across miner orders, two capital raises, a Bitcoin treasury purchase, Tesla's filing, annual results, the pool launch, and monthly operating updates.
- **sector_beta — likely:** broad XLK beta is not the explanation, but XLK is the wrong economic cohort. MARA lagged RIOT by 28.07pp over the trailing 42 days and its daily-return correlation with RIOT was 0.87 before t0 and 0.91 afterward; much of the apparent sector alpha was crypto-miner cycle beta.
- **options_mirage — possible:** the cash tape is consistent with convexity amplification, but 2021 options history and trade-level NBBO are absent. No signed-flow or dealer-hedging direction is supportable.
