# THC 2024 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

THC's 2024 alpha was a balance-sheet-and-business-mix rerating with an earnings engine underneath it, not a single hospital headline: Tenet repeatedly sold relatively low-margin hospitals at attractive proceeds, retained or expanded Conifer relationships, retired debt, shrank the share count, and left investors with greater exposure to the higher-margin USPI ambulatory platform; then Q1, Q2, and Q3 each showed that the retained operations were outperforming while full-year EBITDA expectations rose from $3.285bn-$3.485bn in February to $3.9bn-$4.0bn in October ([Tenet, published 2024-02-08](https://investor.tenethealth.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2024/Tenet-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-FY-2023-Results-Provides-2024-Financial-Outlook/default.aspx); [Tenet, published 2024-10-29](https://investor.tenethealth.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2024/Tenet-Reports-Strong-Third-Quarter-2024-Results-Raises-2024-Financial-Outlook/default.aspx)). The May onset was therefore a second-leg re-underwriting rather than a compressed-prior break: at `t0` the shares were already +53.43% YTD and +117.75% over 126 sessions, but the April 30 earnings gap held and successive July and October gaps confirmed the mechanism. Alpha did sustain through 126 sessions (+30.16pp versus XLV and +19.68pp versus SPY), yet it partially failed late: THC fell 25.09% from its November 7 peak to year-end, leaving May-to-year-end excess of +9.81pp versus XLV but -9.40pp versus SPY as post-election reimbursement-policy uncertainty prompted hospital-sector de-rating and sharp target cuts ([Reuters via Boursorama, published 2024-11-06](https://www.boursorama.com/bourse/actualites/les-operateurs-d-hopitaux-americains-en-baisse-suite-a-la-victoire-de-trump-a-l-election-presidentielle-49ee584ab4ed4191fbb090ad1029abfc); [Wells Fargo coverage reported by Investing.com, published 2024-11-25](https://www.investing.com/news/analyst-ratings/wells-fargo-tempers-optimism-on-tenet-healthcare-stock-amid-policy-uncertainties-93CH-3738947)).
