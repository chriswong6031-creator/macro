# SG 2023 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Sweetgreen's 2023 alpha was a fast, expectation-led re-rating of a depressed post-IPO
restaurant stock: Q1 showed sales growth and narrower losses, the first Infinite Kitchen
made a credible but still one-store labor-productivity story tangible, CAVA's successful
IPO supplied a favorable fast-casual valuation reference, and BofA's July 6 double target
reset turned that narrative into a high-volume gap. What failed was durability, not the
initial mosaic. At the mechanical July 11 onset, the tape had already gained 44.23% in 21
sessions and 97.86% in 63, while the hard evidence still consisted of modest same-store
sales, GAAP losses, cash consumption, and an automation pilot. Q2 subsequently supplied
20% restaurant-level margin and the first positive adjusted EBITDA quarter, but revenue
missed expectations and traffic/mix was negative; the July 28 decline and failure of the
July 31 analyst-upgrade gap showed that published models were chasing a vertical move
rather than underwriting a repeatable earnings engine.

**Format-contract classification: this is a FAILED breakaway case. The run did not
sustain.**

## 3. Catalyst ladder

Dates below are publication or filing dates, never later-disclosed effective dates.
The two repo EDGAR event tables have no SG rows, so the SEC filing links are the filing
anchors and the absence is recorded rather than backfilled locally.

- **2023-03-02 — earnings:** FY2022 revenue grew 38% to $470.1m, but Q4 restaurant-level
  margin was 11%, net loss was $49.3m, and adjusted EBITDA was -$17.9m. This established
  the depressed prior and a large distance to profitability.
  [SEC filing, published 2023-03-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477815/000162828023004819/q422sweetgreenearningsrele.htm).
- **2023-05-04 — earnings:** Q1 revenue rose 22% to $125.1m, restaurant-level margin
  improved to 14%, and adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed to $6.7m; $6.9m of employee-
  retention credits aided reported costs, limiting the quality of the apparent step-up.
  [SEC filing, published 2023-05-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477815/000162828023015732/q123sweetgreenearningsrele.htm).
- **2023-05-10 — product launch:** Sweetgreen opened its first Infinite Kitchen in
  Naperville, turning acquired Spyce technology into a customer-facing automation pilot.
  [Business Wire/company release, published 2023-05-10](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230510005427/en/sweetgreen-Pilots-Automation-at-New-Store).
- **2023-06-15 — sector read-through:** CAVA began trading after pricing its IPO at
  $22, giving public investors a fresh high-growth fast-casual comparison. This is a
  contextual re-rating rung, not an SG operating event.
  [CAVA release, published 2023-06-14](https://investor.cava.com/news/news-details/2023/CAVA-Announces-Pricing-of-Initial-Public-Offering/default.aspx);
  [Axios, published 2023-06-15](https://www.axios.com/2023/06/15/cava-restaurant-ipo).
- **2023-07-06 — research revision:** BofA raised its SG target from $9 to a Street-high
  $17, citing urban demand, menu/loyalty, and labor-
  cost automation. The stock opened 13.96% above the prior close on the episode's
  largest dollar-volume day.
  [Schaeffer's, published 2023-07-06](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2023/07/06/2-food-and-beverage-stocks-set-to-rise-after-upgrades).
- **2023-07-27 — earnings/guidance:** Q2 revenue grew 22% to $152.5m, restaurant-level
  margin reached 20%, and adjusted EBITDA turned positive at $3.3m. Management raised
  FY2023 restaurant-level margin guidance to 16%-18% and adjusted EBITDA to -$10m-$0,
  but same-store sales were only 3%, comprising +4% price and -1% traffic/mix.
  [SEC filing, published 2023-07-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477815/000162828023025929/q223sweetgreenearningsrele.htm).
- **2023-07-31 — research revision:** Piper Sandler upgraded Neutral to Overweight and
  raised its target from $13 to $19, emphasizing improving margins and a reported 26%
  June margin at the Infinite Kitchen. The resulting gap failed within three sessions.
  [Investing.com, published 2023-07-31](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/sweetgreen-gains-as-piper-sandler-upgrades-on-inflecting-margins-432SI-3139344).
- **2023-11-02 — earnings, failure confirmation:** Q3 revenue rose 24% and restaurant-
  level margin reached 19%, but traffic/mix remained -1%; FY revenue guidance narrowed
  to $575m-$585m and adjusted EBITDA guidance remained negative at -$8m to -$3m.
  [SEC filing, published 2023-11-02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477815/000162828023036398/q323sweetgreenearningsrele.htm).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** SG fell from its 2021 post-IPO regime, entered 2023
  below $9, and reached $6.31 in March; FY2022 still carried a 41% operating-loss margin
  and negative adjusted EBITDA. Evidence: `data/yahoo/SG.parquet` and the
  [2023-03-02 SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477815/000162828023004819/q422sweetgreenearningsrele.htm).
- **Catalyst ladder — present, but soft-to-hard sequencing was incomplete at `t0`.**
  Q1 improvement, Infinite Kitchen, CAVA's IPO, and BofA's revision accumulated, but
  repeatable automation economics and company-wide positive EBITDA were not yet public.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At `t0`, SG led XLY by 37.19pp/21d and 60.31pp/42d,
  led SPY by 40.74pp/21d and 68.50pp/42d, and had a 126-day high; the 252-day high was
  absent.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, then contradicted.** The July 6 gap printed
  $123.88m and held ten sessions, but `t0` z21 was only +0.09 and the second-largest
  print, July 28, was a $100.52m down day.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No SG snapshot or history exists in the specified
  option surfaces. Per-ticker option history begins 2026-06; no 2023 direction or
  positioning inference is permissible.

## 7. Research echo

Before the July break, consensus lagged spot: as of 2023-07-06, the average one-year
target was $10.93 versus a $14.10 latest close, a 22.49% implied downside
([Nasdaq/Fintel, published 2023-07-31](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/piper-sandler-upgrades-sweetgreen-inc-sg)).
BofA's 2023-07-06 target increase from $9 to a Street-high $17 was therefore not a
routine revision; it
was the principal public model reset and directly preceded the largest liquidity event
([Schaeffer's, published 2023-07-06](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2023/07/06/2-food-and-beverage-stocks-set-to-rise-after-upgrades)).
Piper followed on 2023-07-31, raising $13 to $19 after Q2
([Investing.com, published 2023-07-31](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/sweetgreen-gains-as-piper-sandler-upgrades-on-inflecting-margins-432SI-3139344)).

The “price escaped the published model” read is **brief and reflexive**, not durable:
spot outran the old $10.93 consensus, but never closed above BofA's $17 or Piper's $19
during the 2023 episode. The first reset catalyzed the breakout; the second could not
hold its own opening gap. External target history is not available in-repo.
