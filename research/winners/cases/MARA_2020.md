# MARA 2020 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

MARA's 2020 breakaway was the equity-market monetization of three nested convexities: Bitcoin price, preordered hash capacity, and financing reflexivity. Bitcoin's advance lifted the modeled revenue of every future machine; fixed-price Bitmain orders and the Beowulf power agreement lowered the prospective unit-cost curve; then the rising share price let MARA issue enough equity to fund a planned 103,060-machine fleet and add direct Bitcoin treasury exposure. The market therefore stopped valuing the then-subscale, loss-making miner on trailing revenue and began capitalizing a future 10.36 EH/s operation plus a liquid Bitcoin proxy. The company-specific scale plan mattered—MARA gained 1,568.44% from the 2020-11-20 close through 2021-04-05 versus 849.12% for RIOT—but the move was not independent of the crypto cycle: Bitcoin gained 217.15% over the same dates. The vertical peak was not sustained, falling 60.01% by the 126-session observation, yet the case remained a large-alpha winner at that horizon because MARA still exceeded XLK by 551.31 percentage points. The critical public re-underwriting steps were the [low-cost power agreement published 2020-10-13](https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1211/marathon-patent-group-announces-joint-venture-with-beowulf-energy-for-105-megawatt-bitcoin-mining-data-center), the [investor model published 2020-11-18](https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1215/marathon-unveils-new-investor-presentation-with-updated-financial-and-operational-metrics), the [70,000-miner order published 2020-12-28](https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1218/marathon-patent-group-purchases-70000-s-19-asic-miners-from-bitmain-for-170-million), the [funding bridge published 2021-01-04](https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1219/marathon-patent-group-completes-200-million-capital-raise-ending-the-year-with-217-6-million-in-cash), and the [Bitcoin treasury purchase published 2021-01-25](https://ir.mara.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1224/marathon-invests-150-million-in-bitcoin).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present, but volatile.** After an August close of $5.09, MARA fell 67.78% to $1.64 by 2020-09-23 and spent the next six weeks below the August high. It was already rebounding into `t0`, so this was a washout-and-rebuild rather than low-volatility compression.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Fleet orders, low-cost power, quarterly disclosure, an explicit operating model, repeated equity financing, direct Bitcoin exposure, and then delivery/production evidence accumulated from June 2020 through April 2021.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** MARA exceeded XLK by 304.28 points at 21 sessions and 873.13 points at 63; the first 252-day closing high after `t0` arrived on 2020-11-25.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** `t0` dollar volume was 1.274 standard deviations above its 21-day mean, the five-day/60-day ratio was 3.807, and five later sessions exceeded $2.1 billion.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2020 trade-level NBBO, open-interest history, volatility surface, or dealer-position history exists in the repository. Current options structure cannot be backcast.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** The repeated 10%+ gaps, billion-dollar turnover, tiny starting revenue base, and fast narrative reset are compatible with reflexive attention. Signed short-interest and episode options histories are absent, while the fleet, power, financing, and treasury events provide a real mechanism beyond attention alone.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** Fifteen material publications accumulated over ten months, and the price held large alpha through 126 sessions despite a severe peak-to-horizon retracement.
- **sector_beta — possible.** XLK and SPY plainly do not explain the move, but XLK is the wrong economic cohort: RIOT gained 849.12% and Bitcoin 217.15% during the selected run. MARA's additional 719 points versus RIOT support company-specific scale/financing effects without removing the dominant crypto-cycle exposure.
- **options_mirage — possible.** Historical options evidence is null. Current GEX payloads disagree on same-date spot/regime, and no signed-flow inference is permitted without trade-level NBBO.
- **Current/episode hazards:** `extended_after_vertical_move` is likely: +1,568.44% through 2021-04-05 was followed by a 60.01% decline to the 126-session observation. `call_wall_overhead` is possible only in the 2026-07-16 snapshot; the site file places a $12.50 wall 9.46% above its $11.42 spot, while the state file gives only 1.71%, so the mismatch itself is a hazard. `catalyst_exhausted` was likely near the April peak because the large fleet, funding, and treasury announcements were already public while most of the 103,120 planned miners remained undeployed. Additional hazards are `equity_dilution`, `miner_delivery_and_energization`, `bitcoin_drawdown`, `network_difficulty`, `power_counterparty_and_regulation`, and `benchmark_mismatch`.
