# HUT 2023 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

HUT's 2023 move was a mosaic of miner-cycle reflexivity and merger optionality, not a clean operating inflection: the stock emerged from a 94.65% drawdown from its 2021 closing high, followed Bitcoin-miner ETF WGMI higher, then acquired idiosyncratic torque from the announced USBTC merger, regulatory progress, USBTC's conditional Celsius mandate, a projected 7.5 EH/s combined platform, Hut 8's 9,000-plus-Bitcoin treasury, a five-year HPC contract, the BlackRock spot-Bitcoin-trust filing, and Bitcoin-collateralized liquidity. Into the mechanical 2023-06-30 t0, HUT had risen 288.24% YTD and led WGMI by 34.93 percentage points over 21 sessions, but the legacy business was moving the other way: Q1 revenue and mining profit had collapsed, adjusted EBITDA was negative, North Bay was suspended, and Drumheller was operating at about 20% of installed hashrate ([Q1 release, published 2023-05-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1731805/000114036123024000/brhc20052631_ex99-1.htm); [Q2 release, published 2023-08-14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1731805/000114036123039398/brhc20057066_ex99-1.htm)). The last vertical leg culminated on 2023-07-13 in a 19.20% session and the episode's largest dollar volume as broader crypto risk appetite surged after the Ripple ruling ([court page, published 2023-07-13](https://nysd.uscourts.gov/securities-and-exchange-commission-v-ripple-labs-inc-et-al-1)); no equivalent hard operating proof arrived at the peak. HUT then fell 40.91% over 63 sessions, lost 12.47 points to WGMI and 37.68 points to SPY, and trailed WGMI by 57.51 points over 126 sessions. The autopsy read is that capital priced the pro forma company before the assets, timing, and unit economics were delivered; very high sector correlation, negative existing-business evidence, a closing delayed until 2023-11-30, and post-peak high-volume reversals distinguished this from a durable winner even though HUT still finished calendar 2023 up 213.88%.

This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`).
