# AVGO 2025 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

AVGO's 2025 alpha was a serial re-underwriting of two reinforcing cash engines, not a one-print semiconductor bounce: VMware converted a large acquired revenue base into faster software growth and high consolidated cash generation while custom AI accelerators plus Ethernet networking moved from a promising adjacency to a visible hyperscaler-scale program. The May breakaway began as investors and research raised the size and durability of that opportunity ahead of Q2 results; June confirmed ten quarters of AI growth, September added a new customer's $10 billion order and accelerated the modeled AI ramp, and October named OpenAI and quantified a 10-GW deployment. This produced repeated high-volume gaps that held and a second leg far beyond XLK and SPY. The move did not fully sustain into year-end: after expectations and targets chased the tape upward, December's otherwise strong results introduced lower-margin AI-mix risk, and the earnings gap failed for 3/5/10 sessions. AVGO still finished 2025 +50.54%, beating XLK by 25.64 percentage points and SPY by 32.53 points, but its final 21-day return was -10.18% versus +0.71% for XLK—the autopsy is a durable fundamental winner with a late expectations/margin reset.
