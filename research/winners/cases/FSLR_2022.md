# FSLR 2022 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

First Solar's 2022 breakaway was a company-specific policy rerating, not merely a solar-beta rally. The July 27 Senate agreement made long-duration U.S. clean-energy and domestic-manufacturing incentives newly probable; the 2022-07-28 mechanical `t0` was the first market session after that public agreement. First Solar's Q2 release came at 4:02 p.m. ET on July 28, after the regular close, so its 44-GW backlog and 27.1 GW of year-to-date bookings confirmed the thesis on the next session rather than causing the initial gap. Enactment on August 16 converted political probability into law, and the August 30 plan to spend up to $1.2 billion on 4.4 GW of additional U.S. capacity made the benefit operational. By February 2023, management explicitly included $660-$710 million of Section 45X credits in 2023 guidance. TAN fell over the 42- and 63-session windows while FSLR rose roughly 50%, isolating domestic manufacturing economics, backlog, and capacity scarcity from generic solar exposure. Sources: the [Senate agreement published 2022-07-27](https://www.democrats.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/senate-majority-leader-chuck-schumer-d-ny-and-sen-joe-manchin-d-wv-on-wednesday-announced-that-they-have-struck-a-long-awaited-deal-on-legislation-that-aims-to-reform-the-tax-code-fight-climate-change-and-cut-health-care-costs), [Q2 release published 2022-07-28](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/07/28/2488302/0/en/first-solar-inc-announces-second-quarter-2022-financial-results.html), [U.S. expansion release published 2022-08-30](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/08/30/2506467/0/en/First-Solar-to-Invest-up-to-1-2-Billion-in-Scaling-Production-of-American-Made-Responsible-Solar-by-4-4-GW.html), and [FY2022 release published 2023-02-28](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2023/02/28/2617641/0/en/first-solar-inc-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2022-financial-results-and-2023-guidance.html).

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2022-07-27 - macro policy agreement:** Senate leaders announced agreement on the Inflation Reduction Act, including approximately $369.75 billion for energy security and climate programs and an emphasis on domestic energy production and manufacturing. [Senate Democratic Caucus release, published 2022-07-27](https://www.democrats.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/senate-majority-leader-chuck-schumer-d-ny-and-sen-joe-manchin-d-wv-on-wednesday-announced-that-they-have-struck-a-long-awaited-deal-on-legislation-that-aims-to-reform-the-tax-code-fight-climate-change-and-cut-health-care-costs).
- **2022-07-28 - earnings:** Q2 net sales were $621 million, cash and securities were $1.9 billion, year-to-date bookings reached 27.1 GW, and expected module shipment backlog exceeded 44 GW, though legacy impairment and yen weakness reduced earnings guidance. [Company release, published 2022-07-28 at 16:02 ET](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/07/28/2488302/0/en/first-solar-inc-announces-second-quarter-2022-financial-results.html).
- **2022-08-16 - macro policy enactment:** H.R. 5376 became Public Law 117-169, converting the Inflation Reduction Act from legislative probability into enacted policy. [Congress.gov bill record, action dated 2022-08-16](https://www.congress.gov/bill/117th-congress/house-bill/5376/titles).
- **2022-08-30 - capacity expansion:** First Solar announced up to $1.2 billion of investment to add 4.4 GW of U.S. manufacturing capacity, including a new 3.5-GW Southeast factory and 0.9 GW of Ohio expansion, targeting more than 10 GW of U.S. capacity by 2025. [Company release, published 2022-08-30](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/08/30/2506467/0/en/First-Solar-to-Invest-up-to-1-2-Billion-in-Scaling-Production-of-American-Made-Responsible-Solar-by-4-4-GW.html).
- **2022-10-27 - earnings and backlog:** Q3 net sales were $629 million, cash and securities remained $1.9 billion, year-to-date bookings reached 43.7 GW, and backlog exceeded 58 GW, while logistics costs drove a quarterly loss and lower 2022 guidance. [Company release, published 2022-10-27](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/10/27/2543346/0/en/First-Solar-Inc-Announces-Third-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results.html).
- **2023-02-28 - earnings and policy bridge:** FY2022 net sales were $2.6 billion, net bookings reached 48.3 GW, and year-end net cash was $2.4 billion; 2023 guidance called for $3.4-$3.6 billion of sales, $7-$8 EPS, and $660-$710 million of Section 45X credits. [Company release, published 2023-02-28](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2023/02/28/2617641/0/en/first-solar-inc-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2022-financial-results-and-2023-guidance.html).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior - present.** Before the policy agreement, First Solar had record bookings but volatile project-sale earnings, legacy impairments, large planned capex, and uncertainty over U.S. manufacturing economics.
- **Catalyst ladder - present.** Legislative agreement, Q2 backlog, enactment, a 4.4-GW U.S. expansion, Q3 booking growth, and explicit 45X guidance accumulated over seven months.
- **Relative breakaway - present.** FSLR exceeded TAN by 57.90 percentage points at 42 sessions and 63.01 points at 63 sessions as the solar cohort declined.
- **Liquidity confirmation - present.** The policy gap held at 3/5/10 sessions, and three of the five largest dollar-volume days clustered near Q2, expansion, or Q3 disclosures.
- **Options convexity - unknown.** A later public report showed call volume seven times the intraday norm, but complete trade sign, position history, and dealer state are unavailable.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Q2 showed a balance sheet capable of funding expansion and a backlog already larger than near-term production, but not clean recurring earnings. Net sales were $621 million, gross loss was $23 million, and operating income was $145 million because a $245 million gain on the Japan project-development-platform sale more than offset core costs and a Chile impairment. Cash, restricted cash, and marketable securities were $1.9 billion; year-to-date bookings were 27.1 GW and backlog exceeded 44 GW. [Company release, published 2022-07-28](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/07/28/2488302/0/en/first-solar-inc-announces-second-quarter-2022-financial-results.html).

Q3 demonstrated commercial strength alongside execution friction. Net sales were $629 million, operating loss was $68 million, loss per share was $0.46, and cash plus securities remained $1.9 billion. Year-to-date bookings rose to 43.7 GW and backlog exceeded 58 GW, but unforeseen logistics costs reduced full-year guidance. [Company release, published 2022-10-27](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/10/27/2543346/0/en/First-Solar-Inc-Announces-Third-Quarter-2022-Financial-Results.html). The policy rerating therefore did not erase manufacturing and logistics risk.

FY2022 closed with $2.6 billion of sales, a $0.41 loss per share, $2.4 billion of year-end net cash, and 48.3 GW of net bookings. The February 2023 outlook then provided the direct earnings bridge: $3.4-$3.6 billion of 2023 sales, $745-$870 million of operating income, $7-$8 EPS, and $660-$710 million of Section 45X credits, alongside $1.9-$2.1 billion of capex. [Company release, published 2023-02-28](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2023/02/28/2617641/0/en/first-solar-inc-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2022-financial-results-and-2023-guidance.html). The durable mechanism was backlog plus policy-supported domestic capacity; the main offset was the operational burden of building and ramping that capacity.

[Schaeffer's, published 2022-09-08](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2022/09/08/analyst-first-solar-stock-could-rally-30-percent). ## 7. Research echo

Research estimates reset in waves as analysts translated policy text into company economics. KeyBanc upgraded FSLR and established a $145 target on 2022-08-11, arguing that First Solar would benefit more than most renewable names. [Investing.com, published 2022-08-11](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/first-solar-to-benefit-more-than-most-from-ira--keybanc-432SI-2870520). BofA then moved its target to $141 from $104.50 on 2022-08-26 after explicitly revisiting the magnitude of credits accruing to First Solar. [Investing.com, published 2022-08-27](https://au.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/bofa-confident-of-significant-valuation-uplift-for-first-solar-432SI-2648742). On 2022-09-08, Goldman Sachs double-upgraded to Buy from Sell and moved to $172 from $60; FSLR was reported at $138.93 while the 15-firm consensus target was $125.90, already about 9% below spot. [Schaeffer's, published 2022-09-08](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/news/2022/09/08/analyst-first-solar-stock-could-rally-30-percent). Price escaped the published average because policy created an earnings stream absent from many prior models.
