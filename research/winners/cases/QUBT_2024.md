# QUBT 2024 - failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

QUBT's 2024 alpha was not a classic earnings re-underwriting; it was a thin, sub-$1-to-$25 quantum-infrastructure narrative repricing that arrived after a Nasdaq compliance overhang, a wave of quantum-computing sector speculation, the company's Arizona thin-film lithium niobate foundry progress, and a December capital raise that converted a balance-sheet survival question into a runway question. The mosaic was powerful enough to pull forward years of optionality, but the move failed to sustain because the public evidence still pointed to tiny revenue, heavy losses, prior control/filing issues, and commercialization milestones that were not yet large revenue contracts. The tape behaved like a vertical repricing of financing probability and quantum theme scarcity, not a durable fundamental compounding setup.

This is a **failed breakaway** case. The run did not sustain: after the 2024-12-18 closing peak of $25.68, QUBT fell to $4.52 by 2025-03-10, an -82.4% drawdown from the peak, before a later rebound.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2024-05-13 - regulatory/listing:** QUBT disclosed Nasdaq non-compliance because stockholders' equity was below the $2.5M continued-listing minimum ([SEC 8-K, filed 2024-05-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758009/000121390024044724/ea0206425-8k_quantum.htm)). This was not a positive catalyst, but it framed the later balance-sheet relief.
- **2024-06-24 - regulatory/listing:** QUBT disclosed a Nasdaq bid-price deficiency notice after the stock stayed below $1.00 for 30 business days ([SEC 8-K, filed 2024-06-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758009/000121390024056631/ea0208200-8k_quantum.htm)). The later move began from a delisting-risk base.
- **2024-08-12 - financial reporting/control:** QUBT filed an 8-K stating prior 2023 financial statements should no longer be relied upon because of errors in warrant accounting ([SEC 8-K, filed 2024-08-12](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758009/000121390024067742/ea0211344-8k_quantum.htm)). This matters to the failure case because the equity was repricing despite recent reporting-quality risk.
- **2024-08-19 - regulatory/listing:** QUBT disclosed a Nasdaq delinquency notice tied to late Form 10-K/A and Form 10-Q filings ([SEC 8-K, filed 2024-08-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758009/000121390024071905/ea0212363-8k_quantum.htm)).
- **2024-11-13 - product/foundry:** QUBT announced that its Tempe, Arizona thin-film lithium niobate photonic chip foundry was moving toward completion and initial production in Q1 2025 ([company press release, published 2024-11-13](https://quantumcomputinginc.com/news/press-releases/2024/quantum-computing-inc.-announces-progress-towards-completion-of-its-arizona-based-quantum-photonic-chip-foundry)). The stock closed +92.8% that day.
- **2024-11-14 - earnings:** QUBT reported Q3 2024 revenue of $101k, gross margin of 72%, operating expenses of $5.6M, net loss of $5.7M, and cash of $3.1M at 2024-09-30 ([company press release, published 2024-11-14](https://quantumcomputinginc.com/news/press-releases/2024/quantum-computing-inc.-reports-third-quarter-2024-financial-results)). The result did not support the move through current revenue; it highlighted the runway problem.
- **2024-12-10 - capital raise:** QUBT announced concurrent common-stock offerings for aggregate gross proceeds of $50M ([company press release, published 2024-12-10](https://quantumcomputinginc.com/news/press-releases/2024/quantum-computing-inc.-announces-concurrent-offerings-of-common-stock-for-aggregate-proceeds-of-50-million)). - **2024-12-16 - contract/application:** QUBT announced a contract with NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center to use its Dirac-3 entropy quantum optimization machine for phase unwrapping ([company press release, published 2024-12-16](https://quantumcomputinginc.com/news/press-releases/2024/quantum-computing-inc.-announces-contract-with-nasa-to-apply-dirac-3-quantum-optimization-technology-to-imaging-and-data-processing)). This was a credible logo but not disclosed as a large revenue contract.
- **2024-12-17 - product launch:** QUBT announced commercial availability of the Dirac-3 quantum optimization machine by subscription or dedicated cloud access ([company press release, published 2024-12-17](https://quantumcomputinginc.com/news/press-releases/2024/quantum-computing-inc.-announces-the-release-and-commercial-availability-of-its-dirac-3-quantum-optimization-machine)). This was the blow-off peak date.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior - present.** QUBT had traded below $1 earlier in 2024 and carried Nasdaq equity, bid-price, and filing delinquency notices before the run. The setup was compressed and distressed, not a quiet institutional base.
- **Catalyst ladder - present.** The ladder was real but fragile: foundry progress, Q3 reporting, a $50M financing, a NASA application contract, and Dirac-3 commercial availability each improved the narrative, but none converted the story into material trailing revenue.
- **Relative breakaway - present then failed.** Mechanical context shows +631.42pp trailing 21d excess versus SPY at t0, and calendar 2024 YTD excess versus SPY was +1687.82pp. Forward from t0, the stock was -23.91pp versus SPY and -57.26pp versus the quantum cohort by day 63.
- **Liquidity confirmation - present.** Liquidity arrived after the detector date: 2024-12-17 and 2024-12-18 printed $3.257B and $5.933B of dollar volume, respectively.
- **Options convexity - unknown.** Current options snapshots exist, but the repo has no per-ticker options history for 2024 and no trade-level NBBO to sign flow direction.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze - likely.** The move had public catalysts, but the magnitude, low starting price, quantum peer melt-up, and post-peak collapse indicate narrative/squeeze mechanics were a large part of the alpha.
- **one_day_binary - possible.** The run was not one single day: several November and December gaps held briefly. Still, the 2024-12-18 peak came only two sessions after the NASA and Dirac-3 headlines, and the 2024-12-23 follow-on gap failed by 5/10 sessions.
- **sector_beta - possible.** QUBT crushed SPY, but RGTI and QBTS also surged; from t0 through day 63, the constructed quantum cohort outperformed QUBT by +57.26pp. Sector/thematic beta was not enough to explain calendar-year alpha, but it was enough to weaken the durable-winner read.
- **options_mirage - possible.** Current options context shows high IV and short gamma, but there is no 2024 options history or trade-level NBBO. Options may have amplified the move, but the case does not rest on signed options-flow evidence.

Current hazards: extension after vertical move, catalyst exhaustion, small revenue base, equity issuance into strength, Nasdaq/compliance history, reporting-quality history, quantum theme crowding, current call-wall/short-gamma risk from 2026 snapshots, and lack of episode-period options history.
