# HIMS 2024 — failed breakaway autopsy with later resurrection

This is a FAILED breakaway case (case_type: failed_breakaway).

## 1. Bottom line

Hims & Hers' June 2024 signal is a path-dependent failure, not a claim that the company failed for the year. The first GLP-1 shock occurred on 2024-05-20, when the company announced compounded injections and the stock jumped sharply; the mechanical t0 did not arrive until 2024-06-04, without a fresh same-day catalyst ([company release, published 2024-05-20](https://investors.hims.com/news/news-details/2024/Hims--Hers-Announces-Access-to-GLP-1-Injections-Passing-Cost-Savings-Onto-Customers/)). That lag matters. The initial breakaway lacked expanding liquidity, became an expectation-heavy bet on a shortage-dependent product, and lost 29.86% by 63 sessions while XLV rose. Yet the underlying platform continued to execute: Q2 revenue grew 52%, and Q3 revenue grew 77% with two million subscribers and higher guidance ([Q2 release, published 2024-08-05](https://investors.hims.com/news/news-details/2024/Hims--Hers-Health-Inc.-Reports-Second-Quarter-2024-Financial-Results/default.aspx); [Q3 release, published 2024-11-04](https://investors.hims.com/news/news-details/2024/Hims--Hers-Health-Inc.-Reports-Third-Quarter-2024-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). Those later facts launched a new leg, making the 126-session return positive. For detector training, the June signal still died on the intended hold horizon; the November resurrection must not rewrite that earlier outcome.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — false at t0.** HIMS had already risen sharply after Q1 and the May 20 GLP-1 launch; June 4 was a delayed extension signal rather than a compressed setup (`data/yahoo/HIMS.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — false for the initial leg.** Q1 and GLP-1 preceded t0, no new event occurred on June 4, and Q2 did not restore the hold. Q3 later began a different leg.
- **Relative breakaway — failed, then resurrected.** HIMS lagged XLV by -38.21pp at 63 sessions, then led by +52.69pp at 126 after Q3.
- **Liquidity confirmation — false for June.** T0 printed only $0.165B; all five largest window sessions occurred in October-November.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** Public reporting mentioned options positions before t0, but trade-level evidence is insufficient to establish signed flow or durable OI migration.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Before t0, Q1 revenue was $278.2M, gross profit $229.1M in the local filed panel, net income $11.1M, adjusted EBITDA $32.3M and operating cash flow $25.8M; the filing date was 2024-05-06 (`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`; [company release, published 2024-05-06](https://investors.hims.com/news/news-details/2024/Hims--Hers-Health-Inc.-Reports-First-Quarter-2024-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). Cash was $105.2M locally, and the business had already crossed into GAAP profit.

During the failed leg, Q2 revenue was $315.6M, gross margin 81%, net income $13.3M, adjusted EBITDA $39.3M, free cash flow $47.6M and subscribers 1.9M ([company release, published 2024-08-05](https://investors.hims.com/news/news-details/2024/Hims--Hers-Health-Inc.-Reports-Second-Quarter-2024-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). The fundamentals did not collapse; the entry path and expectations did. Q3 then accelerated revenue to $401.6M and raised the annual envelope, creating the later resurrection ([company release, published 2024-11-04](https://investors.hims.com/news/news-details/2024/Hims--Hers-Health-Inc.-Reports-Third-Quarter-2024-Financial-Results/default.aspx)). Coverage gap: product-level GLP-1 contribution and gross margin were not separately disclosed in the main releases.
