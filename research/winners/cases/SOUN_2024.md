# SOUN 2024 — failed breakaway autopsy

## Bottom line

SOUN's February move was a reflexive re-rating of a small, heavily shorted voice-AI equity after Nvidia's 2024-02-14 13F disclosed a modest 1.73 million-share position, amplified by a 252-day breakout and extraordinary liquidity, then extended by a genuinely strong 2023 revenue/backlog print. It was not yet a durable earnings-power re-underwrite: at the 2024-02-26 onset, the quoted episode statistics were already +205.46 percentage points over 21 days and +170.95 points over 42 days versus SPY, while the next reported quarter still had a $33.0 million GAAP loss on only $11.6 million of revenue and a 10.8-point year-over-year gross-margin contraction. The market price rapidly outran published analyst models, short interest rose with the price, a critical report and downgrade supplied a counter-narrative, and the 2024-02-27 gap failed by session five. SOUN later produced a separate, acquisition-and-guidance-driven re-rating late in 2024, but that does not convert the February episode into a sustained breakaway.

**Format contract: this is a failed breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`); the run did not sustain, and the purpose is to identify what distinguished it from durable winners.**
