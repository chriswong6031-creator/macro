# AFRM 2022 — failed breakaway autopsy

This is a FAILED breakaway case (case_type: failed_breakaway).

## 1. Bottom line

Affirm's August 2022 breakaway failed because a bear-market bounce was mistaken for a durable operating inflection. The company still had powerful network growth—merchant count, active consumers and GMV were expanding—but rising funding costs, normalizing credit provisions, mix shift toward lower-take-rate enterprise programs and a large expense base prevented volume from converting into profit. The August 3 census t0 had no same-day company catalyst located in this study, an important absence. On 2022-08-25, FYQ4 showed 77% GMV growth and 39% revenue growth but a $277.2M operating loss, negative adjusted operating margin and a forward revenue outlook below Street expectations ([company release, published 2022-08-25](https://investors.affirm.com/news-releases/news-release-details/affirm-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2022-results/); [Reuters report, published 2022-08-26](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/bnpl-lender-affirms-shares-tumble-after-gloomy-fullyear-revenue-forecast-2880814)). The highest dollar-volume day was the post-earnings selloff, the August 10 gap failed by day ten, and AFRM lagged both fintech and SPY at every horizon after day five. Death came from weak conversion of growth into economics, not lack of customer adoption.

- **2022-08-25 — funding disclosure:** The FYQ4 release disclosed that Affirm had completed a $371.5M securitization on June 22; the ladder uses the later publication date and does not backdate the information ([company release, published 2022-08-25](https://investors.affirm.com/news-releases/news-release-details/affirm-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2022-results/)).
- **2022-08-25 — earnings/death confirmation:** FYQ4 revenue grew 39% and GMV 77%, but operating loss reached $277.2M, adjusted operating margin was -8.0%, and credit provision rose to $72.7M ([company release, published 2022-08-25](https://investors.affirm.com/news-releases/news-release-details/affirm-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2022-results/)).
- **2022-09-22 — partnership:** Affirm announced expansion of its Amazon relationship into Canada; the positive partnership rung did not repair the failed economics ([company release, published 2022-09-22](https://investors.affirm.com/news-releases/news-release-details/affirm-and-amazon-introduce-pay-over-time-option-customers)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** AFRM had fallen sharply from its 2021 peak and was deeply negative YTD before the August rebound (`data/yahoo/AFRM.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — false.** The census breakout lacked a same-day company event; the first major subsequent earnings publication contradicted durability, and Amazon Canada was insufficient.
- **Relative breakaway — failed.** +11.19pp versus ARKF at day five became -13.26pp at day 21 and -36.95pp at day 126.
- **Liquidity confirmation — failed.** The largest session was the post-earnings selloff, and the first major positive gap lost its hold line by day ten.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No episode-timely options trades or reliable historical OI surface is available.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Before t0, fiscal Q3 revenue was $354.8M, GMV $3.9B, revenue less transaction costs $182.4M, operating loss $226.6M and adjusted operating income $4.0M. Active consumers reached 12.7M and funding access remained adequate ([company release, published 2022-05-12](https://investors.affirm.com/news-releases/news-release-details/affirm-reports-fyq3-2022-results/)). This was genuine network scale, but GAAP loss and credit normalization were already visible.

During the break, fiscal Q4 revenue reached $364.1M and GMV $4.4B, while revenue less transaction costs was $184.3M, operating loss $277.2M, adjusted operating loss $29.3M and net loss $186.4M. Provision for credit losses was $72.7M versus $25.5M a year earlier; total funding capacity was $10.6B ([company release, published 2022-08-25](https://investors.affirm.com/news-releases/news-release-details/affirm-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2022-results/)). Cash runway was not the immediate death mechanism. The problem was that slower commerce, tighter underwriting, funding sensitivity and lower-take-rate mix impaired the path from GMV to profit. Normalized local SEC quarterly coverage is absent for AFRM.

## 7. Research echo

Bank of America reportedly reduced its target from $45 to $38 on 2022-08-30, when AFRM closed at $23.54 ([Motley Fool, published 2022-09-01](https://www.fool.com/investing/2022/09/01/why-affirm-stock-was-dropping-today/)). Full PIT consensus is unavailable; targets remained above spot while estimates moved down, a lagging failure echo.

- **one_day_binary — possible:** the breakout lacked a strong company catalyst and the FYQ4 release abruptly killed it.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out:** AFRM lagged ARKF by -36.95pp at 126 sessions and SPY by -34.27pp.
- **options_mirage — possible:** no episode options evidence exists to separate derivative amplification.
- **Current hazards:** `no_catalyst_at_t0`, `funding_cost_sensitivity`, `credit_normalization`, `negative_operating_margin`, `lower_take_rate_mix`, `growth_multiple_reset`.
