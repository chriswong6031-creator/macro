# MRNA 2026 — winner autopsy (seed case)

Distilled from `research/MODERNA_STYLE_SPONSORSHIP_BREAKAWAY_RESEARCH.md` (Codex,
2026-07-07). This is the seed case that chartered the Winner Autopsy Lab. All numbers are

## 1. Bottom line

Moderna's 2026 run was a **platform re-rating**, not a single-catalyst pop. A stale,
compressed prior (post-COVID revenue decay, losses, consensus rating still Hold with an
average target far below spot) met a *ladder* of public catalysts — oncology durability
data, a unanimous FDA advisory-committee flu vote, and a Science Day that reframed the
pipeline as a repeatable mRNA platform — that forced large investors to re-underwrite the
company faster than the biotech sector. The tape confirmed it: repeated
institutional-capacity dollar volume and a new multi-window closing high.
