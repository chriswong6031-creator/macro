# ENPH 2020 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Enphase's late-2020 alpha was a layered re-underwriting: Q2 marked a pandemic demand trough, July production shipments turned Encharge from an anticipated battery product into a revenue-bearing solar-plus-storage platform, Q3 showed core microinverter sell-through rebounding with record non-GAAP margin and cash generation, and the U.S. election added a clean-energy policy multiple on top. The move was therefore more than XLK beta, but it also became reflexive: ENPH had already risen 360.24% YTD by the November 5 onset, research targets chased the tape, S&P 500 inclusion added mechanical demand, and the shares reached $213.76 on January 7 before supply constraints and a modest margin step-down exposed how much execution was embedded in the price. The business kept growing; what failed was the clean-hold geometry and near-term relative return, not the product thesis.

**Format-contract classification: this is a failed breakaway (`case_type: failed_breakaway`); the run did not sustain.** At 126 trading days after `t0`, ENPH was down 1.78% while XLK was up 12.36%, and the January peak had suffered a 46.38% drawdown by 2021-05-13.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** The June 17 accusation generated $2.154 billion of turnover, while Q2 revenue fell 38.9% sequentially; the first 63/126/252-day closing high after that break did not arrive until the August 4 results (`data/yahoo/ENPH.parquet`; `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Encharge shipments, Q2 trough guidance, European distribution, and Q3 recovery progressively widened the model from microinverters to solar-plus-storage; election and index news later added non-operating demand.
- **Relative breakaway — present, but late.** At `t0`, 42-day excess was +77.21pp versus XLK and the stock was at a 63-day high, but 126-day trailing return was already +109.95%; the detector caught acceleration, not an early base exit.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** `t0` dollar volume was $592.77 million (z21 1.21; z60 1.69), yet the January 6 climax and April 28 decline were much larger. Liquidity confirmed tradability, not durable capital intent.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The latest annual observation known by `t0` is FY2019 in `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, PIT-gated by `asof_date: 2020-04-29`: revenue $624.3 million, gross profit $221.2 million (35.4% margin), operating income $102.7 million, net income $161.1 million, CFO $139.1 million, assets $713.2 million, and long-term debt $102.7 million. This was already a high-margin turnaround from FY2018 revenue of $316.2 million and operating income of only $1.6 million.

Quarterly data in `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, gated by `filed`, show the pandemic V and the reason capital re-underwrote it. Q1 2020 (filed 2020-05-05) produced revenue $205.5 million, gross profit $80.7 million (39.2%), operating income $44.7 million, and CFO $39.2 million. Q2 (filed 2020-08-04) fell to $125.5 million revenue, $48.4 million gross profit (38.5%), and $10.9 million operating income, with a $47.3 million GAAP net loss. Q3 (filed 2020-10-27) rebounded to $178.5 million revenue, $95.0 million GAAP gross profit (53.2%, including the tariff-refund effect), $51.8 million operating income, $39.4 million net income, and $661.8 million cash. The company separately reported a 41.0% non-GAAP margin and $67.5 million CFO on 2020-10-27 ([company release](https://investor.enphase.com/news-releases/news-release-details/enphase-energy-reports-financial-results-third-quarter-2020/)).

Runway was not the failure point: the March convertible raised roughly $312.5 million net before any over-allotment ([company release, 2020-03-05](https://investor.enphase.com/news-releases/news-release-details/enphase-energy-announces-pricing-320-million-025-convertible/)), and Q3 ended with net cash in the local quarterly statement. The later FY2020 annual row (not knowable at `t0`; `asof_date: 2021-04-30`) shows revenue $774.4 million, gross profit $346.0 million (44.7%), operating income $186.4 million, CFO $216.3 million, and cash $679.4 million. Q1 2021, filed 2021-04-27, reached $301.8 million revenue but gross margin normalized to 40.7% and semiconductor availability limited conversion of demand into shipments (`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`; [NBC/CNBC, published 2021-04-28](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/business/money-report/enphase-energy-posts-record-quarter-but-stock-slides-as-management-warns-of-chip-shortage-pains/2366390/)).

## 7. Research echo

Published research lagged the vertical tape. On 2020-12-28, with ENPH at a $181.39 reference price, Roth's $220 target implied about 21% upside, while the reported 15-analyst average target was only $132.69—roughly 27% below spot. Publication: 2020-12-28. [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/2376330). This is the cleanest “price escaped the published model” snapshot: the high target chased momentum, but the average model had already been left behind.

The echo then cut both ways. Q1 2021 produced strong operating results, yet contemporaneous coverage emphasized valuation and semiconductor constraints as the stock fell; one report cited 31× sales versus a five-year average of 6×. Publication: 2021-04-28. [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/investing/enphase-energy-guidance-cut-price-targets-reduced).
