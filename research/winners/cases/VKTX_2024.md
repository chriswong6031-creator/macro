# VKTX 2024 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Viking Therapeutics' 2024 run was an obesity-asset re-underwriting. The market moved from treating VKTX as another small clinical biotech to treating VK2735 as a credible GLP-1/GIP obesity asset with possible best-in-class or strategic scarcity value. The breakaway had the Moderna-style structure: compressed prior, a public data shock, immediate capital raise, very large dollar-volume confirmation, and sector-relative returns that dwarfed XBI.
