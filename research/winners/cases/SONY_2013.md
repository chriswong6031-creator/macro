# SONY 2013 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

SONY's 2013 alpha was a layered re-underwriting of a distressed conglomerate, not a single product headline: a deeply compressed ADR first broke away when the January 17 headquarters sale made balance-sheet repair tangible, while a weaker yen and Japanese-equity reflation improved the translation and risk backdrop; February earnings showed a return to quarterly operating profit, the PS4 reveal supplied a credible product-cycle runway, April's forecast increase and May's first annual profit in five years reduced survival risk, and Third Point's May 14 proposal put a public value-realization frame around entertainment assets that had been obscured by loss-making electronics. The qualifications matter. FY2012's reported operating profit depended heavily on asset-sale and remeasurement gains, constant-currency sales were weak, and the May activist burst became a liquidity climax followed by a second mechanical episode on May 21 that ultimately failed. Thus the durable alpha from the January onset came from serial reductions in distress and conglomerate-discount assumptions; the later vertical extension was not sustained.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2013-01-17 — balance-sheet action.** Sony announced the $1.1 billion sale-and-leaseback of its U.S. headquarters, expected to generate about $770 million of net cash and a $685 million gain. This was the closest public catalyst to the January 18/24 breakaway and turned restructuring from intent into cash ([Sony release, published 2013-01-17](https://sony.mediaroom.com/2013-01-17-Sony-Corporation-of-America-Announces-Sale-of-550-Madison-Avenue-Building)).
- **2013-02-07 — quarterly return to operating profit, but below consensus.** Sony reported a ¥46.4 billion December-quarter operating profit versus a ¥91.7 billion loss a year earlier; the reported result was below the ¥72.1 billion analyst average, making this a mixed rather than clean beat ([Reuters syndicated by Business Standard, published 2013-02-07](https://www.business-standard.com/article/reuters/sony-returns-to-black-in-q3-on-asset-product-sales-113020700501_1.html)).
- **2013-02-20 — PS4 reveal.** Sony introduced PlayStation 4 for a holiday-2013 launch, giving the re-rating a future product platform rather than only asset liquidation ([Sony Computer Entertainment release, published 2013-02-20](https://sony.mediaroom.com/2013-02-20-Sony-Computer-Entertainment-Inc.-Introduces-PlayStation-4-PS4)).
- **2013-02-28 — further asset monetization.** Sony announced the Sony City Osaki office sale; the later FY2012 release quantified a ¥42.3 billion gain, adding to financial repair ([Sony 2013 IR-news index, publication date 2013-02-28](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/IR/news/2013.html)).
- **2013-04-25 — forecast raised.** Sony increased FY2012 sales guidance from ¥6.6 trillion to ¥6.8 trillion, operating-income guidance from ¥130 billion to ¥230 billion, and net-income guidance from ¥20 billion to ¥40 billion ([Sony forecast revision, published 2013-04-25](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/IR/library/presen/er/12revision_sony.pdf)).
- **2013-05-09 — annual profit return and next-year runway.** Sony reported FY2012 sales of ¥6.801 trillion, operating income of ¥230.1 billion, and net income of ¥43.0 billion versus a ¥456.7 billion prior-year loss; it forecast FY2013 sales of ¥7.5 trillion, operating income of ¥230 billion, and net income of ¥50 billion ([Sony results, published 2013-05-09](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/IR/library/presen/er/12q4_sony.pdf)).
- **2013-05-14 — activist value-realization proposal.** Third Point publicly urged a partial IPO of Sony's entertainment business, reframing the conglomerate discount and producing the episode's largest dollar-volume session ([Los Angeles Times, published 2013-05-14](https://www.latimes.com/business/la-fi-ct-sony-stock-rises-third-point-entertainment-public-20130514-story.html)).
- **2013-05-22 — management strategy response.** Sony set FY2014 targets including a 4% mobile operating margin and reiterated profitable entertainment/financial-services roles; the event kept business-mix and margin repair in public focus ([Sony corporate-strategy release, published 2013-05-22](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/News/Press/201305/13-065E/)).
- **2013-06-11 — PS4 price and commercial architecture.** Sony unveiled a $399 U.S. price and detailed the system at E3, sharpening the console-cycle proposition after the February concept reveal ([Sony release, published 2013-06-11](https://www.sony.net/SonyInfo/IR/news/20130611_01E.pdf)).
- **2013-08-01 — post-run operating confirmation was mixed.** Fiscal Q1 sales rose 13.0% in yen and operating income rose to ¥36.4 billion, but constant-currency sales fell 3%; Game lost ¥14.8 billion while mobile and TVs improved. This publication came after the July 23 price peak and is not used to explain the January onset ([Sony results, published 2013-08-01](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/IR/library/presen/er/13q1_sony.pdf)).

`data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` and `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` have zero SONY rows, so they provide no filing anchors. This is a coverage gap, not evidence of no events; Sony's U.S. reporting route was foreign-issuer forms rather than domestic 8-Ks.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** The ADR bottomed at an adjusted $1.77 on 2012-12-04 after Fitch's November 22 speculative-grade downgrade; the 126-day excess at t0 was only +5.01pp versus SPY, showing repair rather than a long-established annual leader ([Engadget, published 2012-11-22](https://www.engadget.com/2012-11-22-sony-credit-rating-downgraded-to-junk-by-fitch.html); local price source above).
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Headquarters cash, a quarterly return to operating profit, PS4, a forecast raise, annual profitability, and activist pressure accumulated over five months.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 SONY was +19.06pp/+26.22pp versus SPY over 21/42 sessions and had fresh 63- and 126-day closing highs.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, delayed.** T0's z21 was only +0.36, though the five-day/60-day ratio was 2.59; May 14 then printed $680.69M and z60 7.33. This confirms capacity and disagreement, not participant identity.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2013 options history exists in the repository, and trade direction cannot be inferred without trade-level NBBO.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Repository coverage is **null**: `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` contain no SONY rows. Consequently there is no repo PIT panel observation to join at t0, and the following arc relies on dated Sony publications rather than silently backfilling those tables.

Before the run, the December quarter supplied the first visible earnings turn: ¥46.4 billion of operating profit versus a ¥91.7 billion loss, but below consensus ([Reuters/Business Standard, 2013-02-07](https://www.business-standard.com/article/reuters/sony-returns-to-black-in-q3-on-asset-product-sales-113020700501_1.html)). During the run, FY2012 sales increased 4.7% to ¥6.801 trillion and reported operating margin moved from **-1.04%** (a ¥67.3 billion loss) to **3.38%** (¥230.1 billion profit). Yet quality was mixed: constant-currency growth was 2%, pro-forma sales would have fallen about 2% with Sony Mobile consolidated in both periods, and large gains included ¥122.2 billion from M3, ¥65.5 billion from the U.S. headquarters, ¥42.3 billion from Osaki, and ¥9.1 billion from chemicals. Core weak spots remained: Game sales fell 12.2% and operating income fell to ¥1.7 billion; Home Entertainment & Sound still lost ¥84.3 billion, though that loss improved from ¥203.2 billion. Offset came from Devices turning from a ¥22.1 billion loss to ¥43.9 billion profit, Pictures operating profit rising 40.1% to ¥47.8 billion, and Financial Services operating profit rising 10.9% to ¥145.8 billion ([Sony results, 2013-05-09](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/IR/library/presen/er/12q4_sony.pdf)).

Runway was therefore credible but not clean: management forecast FY2013 revenue +10.3% to ¥7.5 trillion and net income +16.2% to ¥50 billion, while operating income stayed flat at ¥230 billion, implying a lower forecast margin of about **3.07%** ([Sony results, 2013-05-09](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/IR/library/presen/er/12q4_sony.pdf)). The PS4 cycle, smartphone growth, lower TV losses, and a weaker-yen translation tailwind could improve mix; declining cameras/legacy hardware, electronics losses, restructuring charges, and asset-gain dependence limited fundamental certainty.

## 7. Research echo

The contemporaneous echo lagged the tape at first. On February 7, the ¥46.4 billion quarterly operating profit was below the ¥72.1 billion Reuters/I/B/E/S average, yet the stock's 21-day return was already 23.7% by January 24 ([Reuters/Business Standard, published 2013-02-07](https://www.business-standard.com/article/reuters/sony-returns-to-black-in-q3-on-asset-product-sales-113020700501_1.html)). By May 9, annual net income of ¥43.0 billion exceeded Sony's freshly raised ¥40 billion outlook and the roughly ¥33 billion FactSet analyst consensus ([CBS/AP, published 2013-05-09](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/sony-returns-to-profit/)). This is the defensible “price escaped the published model” read: price led the earnings-consensus repair, then the model caught up as asset realizations and yen translation became measurable.

Notable broker target revisions are **null**. The repository has no analyst-target data, and no sufficiently attributable, publication-dated 2013 target-change series was found in public sources. It would be false precision to manufacture a target-versus-spot table. The observable substitute is a shift from a below-consensus February quarter to an above-consensus May annual result, followed by Third Point's sum-of-parts challenge. The absence of target history weakens, rather than disproves, the research-echo stage.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** The move predates modern meme dynamics and accumulated through distinct corporate, earnings, product, currency, and activist publications. Short-interest history is null, so this is not a claim about short participation.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The January episode had multiple rungs through June and +50.82pp forward excess at 126 sessions; however, the May 14 activist jump was locally binary-like and contributed to the later failed episode.
- **sector_beta — possible.** SONY had exposure to weaker-yen/Japan reflation and the repo lacks an assigned sector cohort. Still, +19.06pp/+26.22pp versus SPY and +17.16pp/+25.20pp versus XLY at 21/42 days show more than broad U.S. beta. EWJ comparison is contextual only: SONY exceeded EWJ by 23.08pp/26.22pp at those windows (`data/yahoo/EWJ.parquet`).
- **options_mirage — ruled_out.** No historical options evidence is being used; options history is structurally unavailable.
- **Current extension hazard — low on the latest local bar, episode-specific hazard historical.** As of 2026-07-17 SONY was 11.08% below its 200-day average and 30.20% below its trailing 252-day high (`data/yahoo/SONY.parquet`), so the local snapshot is not vertically extended. In May 2013, extension was real: the May 21 detector episode later became a blow-off/failed case.
- **Current call-wall hazard — unknown.** No SONY GEX or options-state snapshot exists.
- **Current catalyst-exhaustion hazard — unknown.** A 2013 catalyst ladder cannot establish the current event queue; within the historical run, exhaustion became possible after Third Point/PS4 expectations were broadly public and the July price peak passed.
