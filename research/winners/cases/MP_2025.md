# MP 2025 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

MP Materials' 2025 alpha was a policy-backed platform rerating with a later geopolitical overshoot, not simply a higher rare-earth price: January-to-May operating milestones showed that Mountain Pass feedstock was becoming separated NdPr and then U.S.-made magnetic precursor revenue ([product release, published 2025-01-22](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2025/MP-Materials-Restores-U-S--Rare-Earth-Magnet-Production/default.aspx); [Q1 release, published 2025-05-08](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2025/MP-Materials-Reports-First-Quarter-2025-Results/default.aspx)); on June 12 a late-session report that Washington was considering Defense Production Act funding made the policy put more concrete ([Bloomberg, published 2025-06-12 at 19:35 UTC](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-12/us-eyes-plan-to-use-cold-war-era-powers-to-develop-rare-earths)); and July converted three formerly open-ended valuation risks into contracts—DoD supplied a 10-year $110/kg NdPr floor, 10-year 10X offtake support, $400 million of preferred equity, a $150 million loan and a path to $1 billion of bank financing, while Apple supplied commercial demand and recycling legitimacy ([MP/DoD release, published 2025-07-10](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2025/MP-Materials-Announces-Transformational-Public-Private-Partnership-with-the-Department-of-Defense-to-Accelerate-U-S--Rare-Earth-Magnet-Independence/default.aspx); [MP/Apple release, published 2025-07-15](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2025/MP-Materials-and-Apple-Announce-500-Million-Partnership-to-Produce-Recycled-Rare-Earth-Magnets-in-the-United-States/default.aspx)). The cash tape treated those as changes to MP's future unit economics and financing probability, not as ordinary Materials beta: 21-session forward excess from the mechanical t0 was +98.04pp versus XLB. The part that failed to persist was the October scarcity premium—MP peaked after China widened export controls, then lost 48.8% by year-end as the U.S.–China deal publicly suspended the new controls and Q3 still showed a consolidated loss despite a profitable Magnetics segment ([China controls, published 2025-10-09](https://english.scio.gov.cn/m/pressroom/2025-10/09/content_118114428.html); [White House deal fact sheet, published 2025-11-01](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/11/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-strikes-deal-on-economic-and-trade-relations-with-china/); [Q3 release, published 2025-11-06](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2025/MP-Materials-Reports-Third-Quarter-2025-Results/)). The core rerating nevertheless survived: MP finished 2025 up 223.85%, 213.90pp ahead of XLB.
