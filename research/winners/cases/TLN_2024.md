# TLN 2024 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

TLN's 2024 alpha was a post-reorganization scarcity-and-capital-allocation rerating, not simply “AI electricity demand.” The March 4 AWS transaction simultaneously monetized the Cumulus campus, attached a long-duration customer to scarce 24/7 nuclear output, and supplied cash that—along with the ERCOT divestiture—could be recycled into a rapidly shrinking share count. March guidance, low leverage and hedged cash flows made that story financeable; later guidance increases, the $269.92/MW-day PJM capacity clearing price, Nasdaq access, index additions and repeated repurchase authorizations made it progressively easier for new capital to underwrite. The November FERC rejection impaired the most aggressive co-location path but did not erase the merchant-power scarcity, cash generation or per-share accretion. That mosaic explains both the breakaway and its durability: the March 27 episode went on to produce +71.05 percentage points against XLU over 126 trading days and +93.83 points over 252, although a separate July episode briefly met the repo's blow-off definition.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present, but unconventional.** The compression was informational and structural more than a textbook low-volatility base: TLN had only resumed public trading in June 2023 after reorganization, was still OTC, and local history begins 2023-06-02. A lightly followed, fresh-start-accounting equity with asset-sale optionality preceded the March repricing; the immediate 21-session tape was already expanding by t0.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** AWS monetization/contracting, raised guidance, ERCOT cash realization, debt repricing, escalating repurchases, PJM scarcity, Nasdaq access and index additions supplied distinct underwriting rungs; FERC supplied a negative stress test.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 TLN was +19.16pp/21d and +30.56pp/42d versus XLU and at a 63d high. The engine's OR threshold was met through the 42d leg, then excess widened to +71.05pp over the next 126 sessions.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** The onset-day z21 was only +0.45, but the 5d/60d dollar-volume ratio was 2.51; AWS day volume was a +12.77 prior-21d z shock, followed by repeated $300m-$1.2bn sessions later in the year.
- **Options convexity — unknown for the episode.** No 2024 per-ticker options history exists. Current 2026 positioning cannot be back-projected.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** No local evidence identifies social-driven price formation; the move persisted across earnings, market-structure and capital-return rungs. Low float after repurchases could still amplify moves, but amplification is not the primary mechanism.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** March 4 mattered, but the March 27 onset followed multiple events and 126d excess reached +71.05pp; later PJM, guidance and access catalysts extended the case.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** TLN exceeded XLU by +30.56pp over the trailing 42d at t0 and +71.05pp over the following 126d. Sector scarcity helped the fundamental mechanism but does not explain the magnitude of relative alpha.
- **options_mirage — possible.** 2024 options history is absent, so options cannot explain the episode and cannot be ruled out as an amplifier. Any stronger claim would be invented.

Current hazards are distinct from the 2024 autopsy: `extended_after_vertical_move` (current spot remains far above 2024 levels), `call_wall_overhead` (current $390 wall versus $368.29 spot), `catalyst_exhausted` (possible if future data-center contracts or capacity repricing fail to add new cash-flow rungs), `regulatory_co_location` (the 2024 FERC rejection established real path risk), and `low_float_after_repurchases` (large share-count reduction can magnify both directions).
