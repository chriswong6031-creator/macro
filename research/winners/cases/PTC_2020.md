# PTC 2020 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

PTC's late-2020 alpha was a two-step platform-and-quality rerating, not one headline and not generic technology beta. First, pandemic-era remote-work and factory-connectivity demand made the company's industrial IoT and cloud collaboration position more legible; then the October report put the subscription transition “in the rear-view mirror” while showing 14% reported ARR growth, 16% FY2020 revenue growth and a nine-point expansion in full-year GAAP operating margin ([PTC Q4 FY2020 release, published 2020-10-28](https://www.ptc.com/en/news/2020/ptc-announces-q4-fy20-results)). Arena then extended the model from recurring on-premise software toward a cloud-native CAD-plus-PLM platform ([PTC Arena announcement, published 2020-12-14](https://www.ptc.com/en/news/2020/ptc-to-acquire-saas-plm-leader-arena-solutions)), and the January quarter supplied the proof point: 12% constant-currency ARR growth, 20% revenue growth, $111 million of free cash flow and a 12-point GAAP operating-margin expansion ([PTC Q1 FY2021 release, published 2021-01-27](https://www.ptc.com/en/news/2021/ptc-q1-2021-results)). The resulting January 28 gap held through 3/5/10 sessions. The move was real but not durable for a full year: PTC beat XLK by 19.82 percentage points over the next 42 sessions and 7.62 points over 126 sessions from mechanical `t0`, then trailed it by 31.43 points over 252 sessions. Price had escaped the published target set, and the index addition announced on April 15 produced a giant but nondirectional rebalance print ([S&P DJI announcement, published 2021-04-15](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/indexnews/announcements/20210415-1358567/1358567_5var-6egov-pr.pdf)); both the April and July earnings responses then opened downside gaps that held. The autopsy is therefore a genuine 2020 winner with a finite rerating runway: recurring-revenue visibility, operating leverage and cloud-platform optionality created the alpha; slower incremental cash-flow/margin evidence and expectation saturation prevented it from sustaining that alpha.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** From 2020-07-31 through 2020-10-28, PTC's 63-session adjusted high-low range was 21.57%, annualized close-to-close volatility was 35.5%, and the stock ended 15.5% below the period high. That was a volatile reset after the July jump, not a tight compression.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** The twelve publication/filing-dated rungs above progress from partner-backed industrial IoT productization to subscription completion, cloud-native M&A, cash conversion, index demand and finally negative earnings reactions.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** Mechanical `t0` had +17.04pp/21d and +26.33pp/42d trailing excess versus XLK; forward excess reached +19.82pp/42d and remained +7.62pp at 126d before reversing by 252d.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, with contamination.** The October reversal printed `z21=3.10`; the January earnings gap printed `z21=4.13`/`z60=6.65` and held through ten sessions. The much larger April print was index-rebalance-adjacent and negative, so liquidity is not treated as direction.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** Per-ticker options history starts in 2026-06. Episode-time GEX, IV, open-interest surfaces and trade-level NBBO are null, so options cannot be credited with causing or sustaining the run.
