# PTC 2020 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

PTC's 2020 alpha was a platform and cash-flow re-rating rather than a pandemic-software headline or a single earnings gap: the completed subscription transition made a cyclical industrial-software vendor look increasingly recurring, COVID accelerated customer demand for remote product development and service, Onshape supplied a credible cloud-native CAD wedge, and resilient Q3 results followed by 14% reported ARR growth, sharply wider margins, strong Q4 bookings, and a large FY2021 free-cash-flow step-up let capital raise both the growth and quality of the model. The expanded Rockwell channel and December Arena deal then made the cloud story broader—CAD plus PLM—while reaffirming the cash target. The sequence explains why PTC outran both XLK and SPY; the tape was gradual and repeatedly made long-window highs, although onset-day turnover confirmation was only modest and the raw files cannot establish historical options convexity or gap holds.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** PTC closed at $82.72 on 2020-09-30, 15.3% below its 2020-09-02 close, and spent October largely between $81 and $89. The compression was a digestion after an earlier 252-day high, not a deep multi-year washout (`data/yahoo/PTC.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Onshape adoption, resilient Q3, the Q4 ARR/margin/cash-flow reset, Rockwell extension, Arena, and investor day progressively converted a cloud narrative into operating evidence.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At 2020-12-09 PTC exceeded XLK by +17.04 pp/21d and +26.33 pp/42d and was making 63-/126-/252-day closing highs (`data/yahoo/{PTC,XLK}.parquet`).
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, modest.** T0 z21 was +1.10 (supplied episode statistic +1.14), but the 5d/60d ratio was only 0.83. The clearer confirmation was repeated $100 million-plus sessions around Q4 results and in mid-December, not persistent volume expansion.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** Per-ticker options history starts in 2026-06. No 2020 chain, OI, IV or trade-level NBBO exists locally, so options cannot be assigned a causal role.

## 7. Research echo

The published model initially tracked spot, then fell behind it. On 2020-06-10, PTC was quoted at $83.42 while the reported consensus target was $86.60 (10 analysts); Rosenblatt's target was $95. Published 2020-06-10: Investing.com/Rosenblatt report, article 2198303. By 2020-12-13, spot was quoted at $112.98 while the reported eight-analyst average target was $103.38; Barclays maintained $120. Published 2020-12-13: Investing.com/Barclays report, article 2367798. After Arena and investor day, Needham reiterated its rating and set $125, while the reported average had moved only to $106. Published 2020-12-16: Investing.com/Needham report, article 2369770.

The read is therefore “price escaped the published model”: the average target moved from roughly in line with spot in June to about 8.5% below spot by mid-December, while individual targets began catching up after the SaaS/FCF frame strengthened. These syndicated reports are historical snapshots, not an in-repo target database, and their consensus samples changed from ten analysts to eight; they should not be treated as a clean time series.
