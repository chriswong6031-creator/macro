# PINS 2024 — failed breakaway autopsy

This is a FAILED breakaway case (case_type: failed_breakaway).

## 1. Bottom line

Pinterest's May 2024 breakaway failed because one excellent quarter reset expectations faster than the advertising model could keep accelerating. The 2024-04-30 Q1 release delivered 23% revenue growth, 518M monthly active users and a large adjusted-EBITDA improvement, producing a 21% next-session move ([company release, published 2024-04-30](https://investor.pinterestinc.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2024/Pinterest-Announces-First-Quarter-2024-Results-Reports-23-Revenue-Growth-and-More-Than-Half-A-Billion-Monthly-Active-Users/default.aspx)). The census t0 on May 6 came after that gap and had no new same-day catalyst. Q2 still showed 21% revenue growth and record users, but forward revenue growth was guided to 16%-18% while non-GAAP operating-expense growth was 17%-20%; that narrowing growth/expense spread punctured the reacceleration thesis ([company release, published 2024-07-30](https://investor.pinterestinc.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2024/Pinterest-Announces-Second-Quarter-2024-Results-Drives-Robust-Revenue-and-User-Growth-Momentum/default.aspx)). The Q1 gap held for ten sessions, but the largest later liquidity cluster was the Q2 rejection, and PINS lagged XLC by 32.99pp at 63 sessions. Death came from expectation deceleration, not user collapse.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2024-04-30 — earnings/guidance:** Q1 revenue grew 23% to $740M, MAUs rose 12% to 518M, adjusted EBITDA reached $113M and Q2 revenue guidance implied 18%-20% growth ([company release, published 2024-04-30](https://investor.pinterestinc.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2024/Pinterest-Announces-First-Quarter-2024-Results-Reports-23-Revenue-Growth-and-More-Than-Half-A-Billion-Monthly-Active-Users/default.aspx)).
- **2024-07-30 — earnings/death confirmation:** Q2 revenue grew 21% to $854M and MAUs rose 12% to 522M, but Q3 revenue guidance slowed to 16%-18% against 17%-20% guided non-GAAP operating-expense growth ([company release, published 2024-07-30](https://investor.pinterestinc.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2024/Pinterest-Announces-Second-Quarter-2024-Results-Drives-Robust-Revenue-and-User-Growth-Momentum/default.aspx)).
- **2024-11-07 — earnings:** Q3 revenue grew 18% to $898M, MAUs reached 537M and adjusted EBITDA was $242M, but the original May breakaway remained below t0 ([company release, published 2024-11-07](https://investor.pinterestinc.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2024/Pinterest-Announces-Third-Quarter-2024-Results-Delivering-Sustained-Revenue-and-User-Growth/default.aspx?Tag=story)).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — false at t0.** The Q1 gap had already repriced PINS by 21% before the May 6 census signal; t0 was post-catalyst extension (`data/yahoo/PINS.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — false.** No fresh t0 event appeared, and Q2 reduced the expected growth slope instead of adding a stronger rung.
- **Relative breakaway — failed.** Small +0.72pp five-session excess versus XLC became -32.99pp at 63 and -35.91pp at 126.
- **Liquidity confirmation — failed.** Q1 volume confirmed the original gap, but t0 volume was only $0.579B and the next major cluster was sustained downside after Q2.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No episode-timely trade-level options or historical open-interest surface is available.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Before t0, Q1 revenue was $740.0M, gross profit $558.9M in the local filed panel, GAAP net loss $24.8M, adjusted EBITDA $112.9M and operating cash flow $356.1M; the filing/publication date was 2024-04-30 (`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`; [company release, published 2024-04-30](https://investor.pinterestinc.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2024/Pinterest-Announces-First-Quarter-2024-Results-Reports-23-Revenue-Growth-and-More-Than-Half-A-Billion-Monthly-Active-Users/default.aspx)). Cash was $1.632B locally, so runway was not the issue.

During the failed leg, Q2 revenue rose to $853.7M, gross profit to $668.8M, GAAP net income to $8.9M and adjusted EBITDA to $179.9M; MAUs reached 522M. U.S./Canada MAUs grew only 3% versus 17% in rest-of-world, while regional monetization remained very uneven ([company release, published 2024-07-30](https://investor.pinterestinc.com/news-and-events/press-releases/press-releases-details/2024/Pinterest-Announces-Second-Quarter-2024-Results-Drives-Robust-Revenue-and-User-Growth-Momentum/default.aspx)). The failure was a slope and expectation problem: revenue remained healthy, but guidance no longer supported an accelerating platform rerating.

Piper Sandler cut its target from $52 to $45 on 2024-07-31 while retaining its stance ([Investing.com, published 2024-07-31](https://uk.investing.com/news/company-news/piper-sandler-cuts-pinterest-stock-target-by-10-keeps-overweight-93CH-3622624)). PINS closed at $31.95 that day (`data/yahoo/PINS.parquet`), already 29% below the new target. Full PIT consensus is unavailable. The gap between spot and the still-high revised target shows a model lag: the tape immediately priced a more severe growth-slope reset than the published target.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out:** a dated earnings shock and advertising fundamentals explain the move without a meme mechanism.
- **one_day_binary — likely:** the Q1 gap supplied the setup before t0, and the Q2 report supplied the decisive rejection.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out:** PINS lagged XLC by -32.99pp at 63 sessions and -35.91pp at 126.
- **options_mirage — possible:** historical options evidence is absent, but cash volume independently shows both legs.
- **Current hazards:** `late_entry_after_earnings_gap`, `no_fresh_t0_catalyst`, `revenue_growth_deceleration`, `expense_growth_catches_revenue`, `ad_cycle_competition`, `liquidity_distribution`.
