# ALNY 2024 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Alnylam's 2024 breakaway was a late-stage RNAi franchise re-underwriting. The market had already worried about HELIOS-B design changes and ATTR-CM competition, then the June 24 Phase 3 readout converted vutrisiran from uncertain expansion optionality into a credible cardiomyopathy label-extension path. Unlike the smaller binary biotechs, this was a large-cap platform name with immediate dollar-volume confirmation and clean relative performance versus XBI.
