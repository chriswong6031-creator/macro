# ILMN 2007 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Illumina's 2007 alpha was a platform re-rating built from several mutually reinforcing facts, not one binary announcement: the January Solexa acquisition gave Illumina a next-generation sequencing engine beside its already fast-growing array franchise; April results showed that the legacy business could fund the transition; June peer-reviewed applications reduced technical-risk while the public count of more than 75 Genome Analyzer orders converted a promising instrument into visible commercial adoption; and July and October results then lifted the revenue model while shipments passed 100. At the 2007-06-14 breakaway, ILMN was still down on a 126-session relative basis and only +2.71% YTD, so much of the move represented fresh re-underwriting rather than mature momentum. The run persisted because subsequent revenue, order, and shipment evidence confirmed a widening installed-base opportunity, although gross margin compressed as the instrument-heavy mix scaled. That combination—new platform, rapid adoption, recurring consumables optionality, and serial guidance increases—best explains why ILMN separated from both XLV and SPY.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2007-01-26 — M&A activity.** Illumina announced completion of the Solexa stock-for-stock acquisition, combining genotyping, gene-expression, and next-generation sequencing capabilities. Publication date: 2007-01-26. [Illumina release](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2007/Illumina-Announces-the-Completion-of-Its-Acquisition-of-Solexa/default.aspx).
- **2007-02-12 — product/technical evidence.** Illumina publicly described Genome Analyzer advances including more than one billion high-quality bases per flow cell and more than 40 orders. Publication date: 2007-02-12. [Illumina release](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2007/Illumina-Announces-Technical-Breakthroughs-at-the-2007-Advances-inGenome-Biology-and-Technology-Conference/default.aspx).
- **2007-04-24 — earnings and guidance.** Q1 revenue was $72.2m, +148% year over year; adjusted net income was $12.7m; management raised the low end of 2007 revenue guidance to $305m–$315m and disclosed $326.8m of cash and short-term investments. Publication and 8-K filing date: 2007-04-24. [Illumina release](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2007/Illumina-Reports-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-2007/default.aspx); local filing anchor: `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`.
- **2007-06-08 — clinical/scientific adoption evidence.** Illumina announced the first peer-reviewed ChIP-Seq studies using Solexa technology, reducing the risk that the Genome Analyzer was merely an instrument-order story without productive applications. Publication date: 2007-06-08. [Illumina release](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2007/Illumina-Announces-Publication-of-the-Industrys-First-Peer-ReviewedChIP-Seq-Studies-Using-SolexaR-Technology/default.aspx).
- **2007-06-13 — commercial adoption milestone.** Illumina announced more than 75 Genome Analyzer orders, including 16 already installed at the Broad Institute, and said demand spanned genome centers, core labs, industry, and academia. This is the closest public catalyst to the 2007-06-14 mechanical t0. Publication date: 2007-06-13. [Illumina release](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2007/Illumina-Receives-Order-for-75th-Genome-Analyzer/default.aspx).
- **2007-07-24 — earnings and guidance.** Q2 revenue reached $84.5m, +103% year over year; operating cash flow was $24.5m; management disclosed a subsequent single-customer order for 20 Genome Analyzers and raised 2007 revenue guidance from $305m–$315m to $335m–$345m. Publication and 8-K filing date: 2007-07-24. ## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present.** At t0, ILMN was -4.29% over 126 sessions and trailed XLV by 13.12pp and SPY by 13.10pp; YTD relative performance was also negative. The prior was not a quiet low-volatility base, however: February and March contained very large turnover and drawdowns.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** Solexa close → technical throughput → Q1 execution → peer-reviewed use cases → 75 orders → two later earnings/guidance increases. Each rung made either platform viability or commercial adoption more measurable.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0, +22.63pp/21d and +21.22pp/42d versus XLV, plus +20.62pp and +17.64pp versus SPY; first 63-session high had already occurred on June 4.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 dollar-volume z-scores were +1.60/+2.14 versus 21/60 sessions, and five-day turnover was approximately 1.95× its preceding 60-session baseline. Later earnings sessions produced year-leading turnover.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2007 options history exists in the repository; per-ticker history begins only in 2026-06, and no current ILMN snapshot is present.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The pre-run business already had exceptional growth. Illumina's 2006 10-K, filed 2007-02-28, reported revenue of $184.6m versus $73.5m in 2005 (+151%), a 67.1% product gross margin, and a 20% operating margin; consumables were 66% of product revenue. Filing date: 2007-02-28. [SEC 2006 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110803/000093639207000158/a27675e10vk.htm).

During the re-rating, quarterly revenue advanced from $72.2m in Q1 to $84.5m in Q2 and $97.5m in Q3. Year-over-year growth was +148%, +103%, and +82%, respectively. GAAP combined product/service gross margin stepped down from 65.1% to 64.3% to 61.9%, consistent with the releases' product-mix explanation: this was rapid instrument-platform scaling, not contemporaneous margin expansion. Adjusted earnings remained positive ($12.7m, $16.8m, and $19.9m), while GAAP Q1 contained a $303.4m non-cash acquired in-process R&D charge. Cash and short-term investments were $326.8m after Q1, $343.3m after Q2, and $352.9m after Q3; operating cash flow was positive in each reported quarter ($14.6m, $24.5m, and $5.3m). Publication dates: 2007-04-24, 2007-07-24, and 2007-10-23. [Q1 release](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2007/Illumina-Reports-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-2007/default.aspx), [Q2 release](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2007/Illumina-Reports-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-2007/default.aspx), [Q3 release](https://investor.illumina.com/news/press-release-details/2007/Illumina-Reports-Financial-Results-for-Third-Quarter-2007/default.aspx).
