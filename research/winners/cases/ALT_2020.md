# ALT 2020 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

ALT's 2020 alpha was a probability re-rating of a scarce intranasal-vaccine platform, not an improvement in recurring economics: a roughly $2 microcap entered the pandemic with prior human experience in nasal influenza and anthrax candidates, secured the COVID-19 field under its Janssen vector license, moved AdCOVID from design to a UAB mouse study, added a separate FDA-cleared intranasal therapeutic and a $4.7 million Defense Department award, then reported serum neutralizing activity plus respiratory mucosal IgA just as a $1.6 billion award to Novavax made government-funded vaccine optionality especially salient ([Altimmune, published 2020-04-08](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326190/000119312520100902/d915549d8k.htm); [Altimmune, published 2020-06-29](https://ir.altimmune.com/news-releases/news-release-details/altimmune-receives-award-us-department-defense-fund-phase-12); [Altimmune/UAB, published 2020-07-13](https://ir.altimmune.com/news-releases/news-release-details/altimmune-and-university-alabama-birmingham-uab-announce); [Novavax, published 2020-07-07](https://ir.novavax.com/press-releases/2020-07-07-Novavax-Announces-1-6-Billion-Funding-from-Operation-Warp-Speed)). The move failed because price capitalized the differentiated product profile before evidence quality caught up: at the 2020-07-14 detector onset ALT was +1,435.98% YTD, AdCOVID was still preclinical, the July financing priced new shares at $23 versus a $29.03 t0 close, and the company had no AdCOVID procurement award or human immunogenicity. Forward excess was already negative by 21 sessions; Pfizer/BioNTech's >90% Phase 3 efficacy then made ALT's late position explicit, an FDA clinical hold confirmed execution risk, and the eventual Phase 1 readout showed inadequate immune responses and ended AdCOVID ([offering pricing, published 2020-07-13](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/07/14/2061562/0/en/Altimmune-Announces-Pricing-of-a-Public-Offering-of-115-Million-of-Common-Stock-and-Pre-Funded-Warrants.html); [Pfizer/BioNTech, published 2020-11-09](https://www.pfizer.com/news/press-release/press-release-detail/pfizer-and-biontech-announce-vaccine-candidate-against); [Altimmune, published 2020-12-23](https://ir.altimmune.com/news-releases/news-release-details/altimmune-provides-update-its-investigational-new-drug); [Altimmune, published 2021-06-29](https://ir.altimmune.com/news-releases/news-release-details/altimmune-announces-update-adcovidtm-phase-1-clinical-trial)).

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`): the run did not sustain.**

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent at t0.** ALT did have an origin base: from 2020-01-02 through 2020-02-27 closes ranged only $1.69-$2.025 and median dollar volume was $0.19 million. But by the detector onset it was +1,435.98% YTD and +285.01% over 21 sessions. The scanner found a climax, not an early escape (`data/baskets/ohlcv/ALT.parquet`; `data/yahoo/ALT.parquet`).
- **Catalyst ladder — present, but low evidence quality.** License access, UAB, an FDA-cleared sister program, a $4.7 million therapeutic award, DynPort, and mouse data created repeated probability updates; none was human AdCOVID efficacy, direct procurement, or even an open AdCOVID IND at t0. The later high-quality rungs reversed the thesis.
- **Relative breakaway — present, then failed quickly.** Trailing excess was +270.46pp/21d versus XBI and +279.72pp versus SPY, but forward excess was -17.80pp and -28.03pp respectively by 21 sessions and deteriorated thereafter (`data/yahoo/{ALT,XBI,SPY}.parquet`).

Yet five of the six largest 2020 turnover days clustered in the final vertical week, the t0 gap failed by day 10, and later catalysts attracted far less depth (`data/baskets/ohlcv/ALT.parquet`).
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2020 trade-level options or open-interest history exists locally. Current options files begin in June 2026 and cannot be projected backward.
