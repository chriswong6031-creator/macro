# RDW 2025 — failed-breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

RDW's April-to-June move was a second attempt to capitalize a much larger defense-and-space platform, not an earnings-led compounder break: the [Edge Autonomy announcement (2025-01-20)](https://ir.rdw.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/153/redwire-announces-acquisition-of-edge-autonomy) supplied a dramatic pro-forma revenue/Adjusted-EBITDA reframe, [Golden Dome's architecture announcement (2025-05-20)](https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/article/4193417/secretary-of-defense-pete-hegseth-statement-on-golden-dome-for-america/) made space-based defense newly legible, and a rapid sequence of mostly amount-undisclosed contracts and technical milestones kept optionality fresh. The tape was real—RDW rose 125.2% from the April 7 reset low to mechanical t0, exceeded SPY by 48.82 points over 21 sessions and traded with expanding dollar volume—but the underwriting substrate was fragile. At t0, [Q1 results published 2025-05-12](https://ir.rdw.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/172/redwire-corporation-reports-first-quarter-2025-financial) showed legacy revenue down 30.1%, negative Adjusted EBITDA and negative $49.1 million free cash flow; the stock had cleared only 63-day overhead and remained 31.1% below its 126/252-day high; and the repo records 11 soft but zero hard 8-K events in the preceding 126 days. When the [transaction closed on 2025-06-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819810/000181981025000163/rdw-20250613.htm), it brought approximately 49.8 million consideration shares, a $90 million term loan and, four days later, a [$260 million common-stock offering published 2025-06-17](https://ir.rdw.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/181/redwire-announces-pricing-of-its-upsized-offering-of-common). [Q2 results published 2025-08-06](https://ir.rdw.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/191/redwire-corporation-reports-second-quarter-2025-financial) then exposed a negative gross margin, $93.5 million quarterly free-cash-flow use, a lower combined-company revenue range and withdrawn Adjusted-EBITDA guidance. The move failed because narrative breadth and tradable liquidity arrived before operating proof, while financing supply and execution costs arrived immediately after the peak.

**FAILED BREAKAWAY:** this case is `case_type: failed_breakaway`; the run did not sustain, and the failure—not the temporary alpha—is the object of the study.
