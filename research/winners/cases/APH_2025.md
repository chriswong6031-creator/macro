# APH 2025 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

APH's 2025 alpha was a compounding earnings-and-duration re-rating, not a one-headline AI trade: the market repeatedly learned that AI/data-center interconnect demand was converting into exceptional IT-datacom organic growth, that Amphenol could absorb acquired businesses while expanding margins, and that the acquisition machine had a much larger fiber runway. The January and April results moved the prior from “high-quality connector compounder” toward “AI infrastructure plus serial M&A platform”; July's 41% organic growth and 25.6% adjusted margin and October's 41% organic growth and 27.5% margin kept estimates chasing; the $10.5 billion CCS agreement added a large, accretive fiber asset. That mosaic explains why APH returned 96.08% in 2025 versus 24.61% for XLK and 17.72% for SPY. The move did sustain at the case-library horizon—forward 126-session excess was +32.85 percentage points with `clean_hold=true`—but 252-session excess later fell to -6.27 points, so this was a powerful 2025 winner whose relative advantage eventually attenuated rather than a permanently vertical repricing.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — present, mild.** This was not a distressed base: FY2023 revenue had slipped 0.54%, but FY2024 then grew 21.25%. The April tariff drawdown reset the tape before the May breakaway; the stronger compression was in expectations about the durability and margins of AI/datacom growth, not in the absolute business.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** January and April supplied pre-t0 earnings proof; July and October renewed organic-growth and margin evidence; Andrew and CCS expanded acquired runway.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At t0 APH led XLK by 25.28 points over 42 sessions and SPY by 31.75 points, while setting a new 63-day high. It finished 2025 71.48 points ahead of XLK.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present.** T0 turnover was $1.930 billion at 4.05/4.84 standard deviations versus trailing 21/60 sessions; January and April earnings also printed repeated billion-dollar sessions. Volume establishes repricing capacity, not participant identity or direction.
- **Options convexity — unknown for 2025.** Per-ticker history starts in 2026-06. No historical chain, IV, wall, or dealer-position evidence exists for the episode.
