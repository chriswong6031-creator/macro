# IREN 2023 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

IREN's 2023 alpha came from a three-part re-underwriting rather than one headline: Bitcoin-miner beta around the spot-ETF cycle, repeated miner purchases that advanced the stated capacity path from 5.6 EH/s toward 10 EH/s, and AI/HPC option value from H100 GPUs, WEKA and a 1.4 GW Texas site. The exact 2023-12-21 mechanical onset was the capstone—management said 10 EH/s was fully funded and ahead of schedule, but also said AI customers were still only in conversation and that the equity-raising program was complete ([company release, published 2023-12-21](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2023/12/21/2799869/0/en/10-EH-s-Equity-Raising-Program-Complete.html)). By then IREN had risen 167.60% in 21 sessions and 514.40% YTD, so the 4.03-z dollar-volume shock was confirmation of attention at an extended point, not proof of durable economics. The failure had both cohort and company components: after U.S. spot-Bitcoin ETP approval ([SEC statement, published 2024-01-10](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/gensler-statement-spot-bitcoin-011023)), the anticipated-event unwind took IREN down 47.92% over 21 sessions versus 34.08% for miner ETF WGMI; January then showed lower revenue, fewer coins and higher electricity cost per coin ([company update, published 2024-02-07](https://irisenergy.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/iris-energy-announces-monthly-investor-update-january-2024)). A later 126-session recovery followed new contracted AI and GPU catalysts, but it did not rescue the original breakaway's clean-hold test.

This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`).
