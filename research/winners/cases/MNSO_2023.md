# MNSO 2023 — failed breakaway autopsy

## Bottom line

MNSO's 2023 alpha was not simply China-reopening or consumer-discretionary beta: by the
March 2 onset it had beaten SPY by 23.75 percentage points over 21 sessions and 65.14
points over 42, while XLY and XRT were weak-to-modestly positive. Capital was
re-underwriting a real change in earnings power assembled across several disclosures:
overseas sales and higher-margin direct operations recovered, brand-upgrade products
lifted China unit economics, product-cost savings held, and the end of China's mobility
restrictions supplied a domestic traffic call option. A January 17 Goldman conviction
upgrade gave that mosaic a public valuation frame, and February 28 results then printed
40.0% gross margin despite a 10.0% revenue decline. The failure was in the setup and the
price path, not in the existence of the operating inflection. MNSO was already up 185.65%
over 126 sessions at t0, spot had slightly escaped Goldman's $17.80 model, the enormous
liquidity event occurred two sessions before t0 and did not persist, and the next
21/42/63 sessions lagged SPY. Stronger May and August results eventually drove a second
leg to a September peak, but that peak then fell 31.17% by year-end and the 252-session
return from t0 was only +0.26% versus SPY's +30.74%. This was a correct earnings
re-underwriting entered after a vertical move, not a durable breakaway from a compressed
base.

**This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`); the run did not
sustain.**

## Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** The stock was already +68.89% over 42 sessions and
  +185.65% over 126 at t0; all three trailing high windows first broke on January 6.
  This was a vertical reopening rerating, not release from a quiet base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** November margin proof, Goldman's January upgrade, and
  February margin resilience formed a coherent ladder; May and August results later
  strengthened it. The weakness was that the freshest hard catalyst preceded t0 by two
  sessions and much of the reopening thesis was already capitalized.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** MNSO beat XRT, XLY, and SPY by 59.35, 58.29, and
  65.14 points over 42 sessions. Forward SPY excess then became negative over
  21/42/63 sessions and over 252 sessions.
- **Liquidity confirmation — partial.** February 28 dollar volume was $221.14m at
  +4.30 z21 and +7.20 z60, but t0 itself was -0.14 z21. The five-day burst confirmed
  attention; persistence at the mechanical break did not.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** Per-ticker history starts in 2026-06 and no current
  MNSO GEX or entry-state record exists. Historical options causality is null.

## Research echo

The available publication-dated record shows research following the operating arc.
Goldman added MNSO to its conviction list with a **$17.80** target on 2023-01-17,
explicitly citing the reopening setup, 35–40% overseas sales exposure, and expected
mainland improvement from March ([Investing.com, published
2023-01-17](https://ng.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/todays-most-important-upgrades-432SI-781124)).
MNSO's unadjusted close was $18.51 at t0 (`data/yahoo/MNSO.parquet`), 4.0% above that
target: price had modestly escaped the published model before the mechanical onset.

The model later caught up. Goldman raised its target from $24.30 to **$27.90** on
2023-08-23 after the June-quarter print ([Benzinga analyst history, dated
2023-08-23](https://www.benzinga.com/quote/MNSO/analyst-ratings)).
On 2023-08-31, a compilation put the average one-year target at **$24.97**, up 16.84%
from $21.37 on August 1, with spot at $24.81—only 0.65% below the new mean
([Nasdaq/Fintel, published
2023-08-31](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/miniso-group-holding-ltd-adr-mnso-price-target-increased-by-16.84-to-24.97)).

published upside as estimates were revised. A reliable full 2023 firm-by-firm consensus

## Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** The move unfolded over more than 126 sessions and
  tracked repeated margin/profit disclosures. Historical short-interest data are null,
  but neither a one-day social burst nor options evidence is needed to explain it.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** February 28 was the largest 2023 volume day, yet the
  ladder began in November and continued through May and August. The failure followed a
  multi-stage rerating, not one binary outcome.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** At t0 MNSO led XRT by 59.35 points, XLY by 58.29, and SPY
  by 65.14 over 42 sessions. China reopening was a shared macro input, but sector and
  index returns cannot account for the magnitude.
- **options_mirage — ruled_out.** No historical options evidence is used as
  confirmation. The verdict rules out the explanatory claim, not the possibility that
  options traded.

`published_model_exhausted`. Current extension is **ruled out** relative to the 2023
peak because the latest local close is well below it; current call walls are **null**
