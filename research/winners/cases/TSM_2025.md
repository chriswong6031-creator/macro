# TSM 2025 — winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

TSM's 2025 index alpha came from a genuine earnings-duration reset wrapped in a large semiconductor-cycle move: 2024 had already established a 30% U.S.-dollar revenue rebound, but the 2025 ladder repeatedly reduced the risk that AI demand was only a short burst—Q1–Q3 revenue grew 42%, 39% and 30% in Taiwan dollars, gross margin stayed near 59% despite foreign-exchange and overseas-fab costs, full-year U.S.-dollar growth guidance moved from roughly the mid-20s to roughly 30% and then the mid-30s, and N2/A14 plus advanced-packaging plans extended the runway ([Q4 release, published 2025-01-16](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3201); [Q1 release, published 2025-04-17](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3222); [Q2 release, published 2025-07-17](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3249); [Q3 release, published 2025-10-16](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3264); [A14 release, published 2025-04-24](https://pr.tsmc.com/english/news/3228)). The extra $100 billion U.S. commitment, taking planned U.S. investment to $165 billion, also became a policy hedge when the administration later described exemptions for firms building domestically ([SEC 6-K, filed 2025-03-03](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000104617925000024/0001046179-25-000024-index.htm); [presidential remarks, published 2025-08-06](https://rollcall.com/factbase/trump/transcript/donald-trump-remarks-apple-investment-announcement-white-house-august-6-2025)). Yet the mechanical 2025-06-24 t0 was not a clean company-specific breakaway: TSM had +26.31pp of trailing 42-session excess versus SPY but only +3.84pp versus SOXX, then matched SOXX over the next 42 sessions and led it by only +4.34pp over 126. The mosaic is therefore real operating re-underwriting plus strong sector beta—not a squeeze or a solitary TSM revelation—with the durable residue visible in full-year gains of 55.95%, 15.21pp ahead of SOXX and 38.23pp ahead of SPY.
