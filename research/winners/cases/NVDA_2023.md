# NVDA 2023 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

NVIDIA's 2023 breakaway was a data-center AI demand re-underwriting that changed the market's earnings denominator in one night. The stock was already a semiconductor leader, but the 2023-05-24 FY2024 Q1 release and guide made the AI accelerator cycle look immediate, supply-constrained, and large enough to reset forward revenue expectations. The local tape shows the distinction between a strong chip cycle and a true rerating: by 2023-06-30, NVDA had outperformed SMH by +24.10pp over 42 trading days, +147.05pp over 126 trading days, and +144.30pp YTD.

- one_day_binary: ruled_out. The 2023-05-25 gap was the starting shock, but Q2 and Q3 revenue confirmations extended the ladder.
- sector_beta: ruled_out. SMH was strong, but NVDA still outperformed by +147.05pp over 126 trading days.
