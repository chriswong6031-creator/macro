## Batch decision

This batch adds **25 non-overlapping episodes: 20 winners and 5 failed breakaways**. It
therefore meets the masterplan's minimum one-failure-per-four-winners sampling rule
exactly. The slate excludes all eleven cases that were already present when the batch was
commissioned: NVDA 2023, CVNA 2023, VRT 2023, PLTR 2024, APP 2024, SMCI 2024, VKTX 2024,
SMMT 2024, ALNY 2024, ASTS 2024, and MRNA 2026.

Selection began with the mechanical rows in
`data/research/winner_episodes.parquet`, then favored episodes that diversify era, sector,
mechanism, and failure anatomy. The local forward-excess numbers below are selection
anchors, not scores or investment conclusions. Each linked case independently documents
its public catalyst dates, price/volume path, fundamentals, research echo, ownership
context, hazards, and evidence gaps.

## Sampling and interpretation fences

- Case selection does not change Detector D, outcome labels, board ordering, sizing, or
  any other money path.
- The files retain failed episodes even when a later, separate episode succeeds. HIMS is
  intentionally included because an honest 63-day failure label and a later recovery can
  both be true.
- Ownership and insider material is descriptive context only. It is not used as positive
  evidence in case selection or in any machine block.
- Historical options coverage is printed as unknown where episode-timely public evidence
  is absent; no signed flow direction is inferred without trade-level evidence.
- The user-supplied `winner_case.v1` contract is the governing output format. Every case
  ends in one machine-readable YAML block and prints missing evidence rather than filling
  gaps with inference.
