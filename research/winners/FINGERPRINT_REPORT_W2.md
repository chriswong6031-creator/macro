# Winner Autopsy Lab — First Cross-Case Fingerprint Report (W2)

**Status: DESCRIPTIVE ONLY.** This report registers no statistical hypotheses, issues no verdicts, and feeds no money path (per rulings WA-R1 / WA-R5 / WA-R8). Its sole job is to convert "here is what these 11 winners share" into "here are candidate features worth a pre-registered test against the FULL mechanical census." Nothing below is a signal, a score, or an escalation.

---

## 1. Bottom line

Across 11 hand-selected durable winners (ALNY, APP, ASTS, CVNA, MRNA, NVDA, PLTR, SMCI, SMMT, VKTX, VRT), a recurring anatomy shows up with striking regularity: a **compressed prior** (the market was actively doubting something), resolved by a **catalyst that broke a relative-strength high**, arriving as a **multi-rung ladder rather than a single pop**, with the **initial trigger gap holding over 3/5/10 sessions**, and producing **large idiosyncratic excess return** (+6pp to +262pp at 21d vs the relevant sector ETF — sector beta judged not to explain the move in every case). Ten of eleven were re-rated through the same channel we labelled `platform_rerating`; the one exception is **CVNA**, labelled `turnaround`.

**The loud caveat, stated up front and repeated throughout: this is selection on the dependent variable.** These 11 cases were chosen *because they worked*. Any feature they share is, at best, **necessary-looking** — it can never be shown **sufficient** from this set alone. The full mechanical census tells the opposite-tail story: `blow_off` onsets outnumber `durable_winner` onsets roughly **6:1**. That census — which contains the failures, the fake breakouts, and the reversals — is the *only* place where "does this feature discriminate winners from blow-offs?" can be answered. Everything in Section 4 is framed as a question *for the census*, not an answer *from the winners*.

A second structural warning: all 11 names produced **multiple onsets between 2021–2026 (72 total)**. Winner names break away *repeatedly*; only *some* of those breakaways were durable. So even *within* a winner ticker, "it broke away" is not the discriminating fact — the base rate of durable-vs-transient breakaways lives in the census, not here.

## 2. The common anatomy — the 5-stage skeleton

The recurring shape has five stages. Below, each stage is scored for how consistently it appears across the 11, with per-case evidence.

### Stage 1 — Compressed prior (the market was doubting something)
**Appears: 11/11.** Every case had an identifiable pre-catalyst doubt the catalyst then resolved.
- **ALNY** — pre-readout doubt on HELIOS-B design/timing + ATTR-CM competitive risk.
- **APP** — valuation-model debate: does adtech deserve a software-like multiple?
- **ASTS** — pre-commercial financing/launch/carrier-adoption doubts.
- **CVNA** — existential-risk pricing: debt load + high short interest priced survival near zero.
- **MRNA** — COVID-revenue decay kept the marginal model stale; consensus Hold, target 45.85 vs spot 81.80.
- **NVDA** — gaming-digestion uncertainty; buy-side estimates behind the AI data-center demand curve.
- **PLTR** — contested multiple: lumpy government contractor vs real commercial AI platform.
- **SMCI** — public estimates stale relative to the fiscal-Q2 revenue step-up.
- **SMMT** — small-cap single-licensed-asset oncology with a China-data / clinical-geography discount.
- **VKTX** — obesity upside not fully capitalized before the data shock.
- **VRT** — post-SPAC industrial name with underappreciated AI data-center exposure.

### Stage 2 — Catalyst that resolves the prior
**Appears: 11/11**, but the *type* splits cleanly (see Section 3): earnings/guidance operating-leverage prints (APP, NVDA, PLTR, SMCI, VRT), clinical/regulatory data (ALNY, MRNA, SMMT, VKTX), a distressed-balance-sheet turnaround (CVNA), and partnership-credibility milestones (ASTS).

### Stage 3 — Catalyst *ladder* (multiple rungs, not one pop)
**Appears: 11/11, rung count ranges 2–5.**
- 2 rungs: **ALNY** (topline → detailed ESC data), **VKTX** (clinical data → financing).
- 3 rungs: **MRNA, NVDA, SMCI, SMMT, VRT**.
- 4 rungs: **ASTS** (3 partnerships + product launch), **CVNA** (earnings/guidance/M&A/earnings).
- 5 rungs: **PLTR** (earnings ×2, index inclusion, market recognition, guidance).

This is the single most consistent structural feature after "sector beta ruled out." Note the honest read: rung count ≥2 is *definitionally* satisfiable post-hoc for a winner (a durable winner tends to accrete confirming events); its discriminating power is entirely a census question.

### Stage 4 — Trigger gap holds (3/5/10 sessions)
**Appears: 11/11 report `trigger_gap_held: yes`.** Where documented, the initial gap held across 3, 5 and 10 sessions (SMCI: all three gaps +11.60%/+17.82%/+14.75% held; ASTS: both +31.38%/+15.76% gaps held; PLTR: all three gap events held). **SMMT gives the one internal contrast:** its May qualitative top-line pop *failed* the 3/5/10 hold, while the September WCLC quantitative detail *held* — a within-episode false-positive vs true-positive contrast that is exactly the kind of thing the census must adjudicate at scale.

### Stage 5 — Large idiosyncratic excess (sector beta ruled out)
**Appears: 11/11.** 21d excess vs the relevant sector ETF ranges from **+6.32pp (NVDA vs SMH)** to **+261.78pp (VKTX vs XBI)**. Longer-horizon excess persists in the fundamental names (NVDA +147.05pp at 126d vs SMH; VRT +235.77pp at 126d vs XLI; PLTR +288.65pp at 126d vs IGV), and the catalyst day typically coincided with a fresh 252-day closing high (ALNY exact-day, NVDA same gap day, CVNA same session as the debt-exchange). Two informative exceptions on the high-timing: **VKTX** and **SMMT** set their first 252d closing high *before / on early legs*, hinting at pre-catalyst accumulation.

**Skeleton scorecard:** all five stages appear in all 11 cases. That uniformity is *expected* for winners-only and is precisely why it proves nothing on its own.

## 3. Sub-species — mechanism families

The 11 split into **four primary families plus one overlapping amplifier label (PLTR)**. The load-bearing distinction is **fundamentals present vs pre-revenue**, with **index inclusion** recurring as an *amplifier* rather than a primary mechanism.

### A. Fundamental operating-leverage rerating (5): APP, NVDA, PLTR, SMCI, VRT
Fundamentals **present**, and the shared signature is **profit stepping up faster than revenue**:
- **APP** — rev +11% QoQ but net income +40% ($310M→$434M), gross margin 73.8%→77.4%.
- **NVDA** — op income $601M→$2.14B while revenue $5.93B→$7.19B; forward guide to ~$11B was the jump condition.
- **PLTR** — op income $40M→$113M vs revenue $558M→$726M; fifth consecutive GAAP-profit quarter.
- **SMCI** — fiscal-Q2 guide raised $2.7–2.9B → $3.6–3.65B, confirmed $3.66B.
- **VRT** — rev +14% but op income +58% Q1→Q2; margin expansion both quarters.

Index inclusion recurs here as a *second leg*, not the mechanism: **APP** (Nasdaq-100), **PLTR** (S&P 500), **SMCI** (S&P 500). PLTR is distinct enough that it carries its own `index_flow_amplified` subspecies label — the forced passive inflow is treated as an amplifier stacked on the operating-leverage core.

### B. Clinical / milestone rerating (4): ALNY, MRNA, SMMT, VKTX
Fundamentals **absent / pre-revenue** (zero EDGAR rows locally; the re-rating is asset-value re-underwriting, not an earnings surprise). The driver is a clinical/regulatory readout:
- **ALNY** — HELIOS-B (2-rung: topline → ESC detail).
- **MRNA** — oncology data → regulatory panel → Science Day (also carries a Merck/Keytruda partnership dimension).
- **SMMT** — ivonescimab HARMONi-2 PFS head-to-head vs pembrolizumab.
- **VKTX** — VK2735 obesity Phase-2 shock + immediate $550M raise.

These are the cases where the **single-binary-event weakest link** is most acute — a negative readout is the mirror-image disaster, and the winners-only frame captures only the positive tail of that binary.

### C. Turnaround / distressed rerating (1): CVNA
Fundamentals **present but distressed** — survival math (GPU expansion, expense compression, narrowing loss) plus a July debt-exchange that removed 83% of 2025/2027 unsecured maturities and eliminated the binary default scenario. Meme/short-squeeze amplification rated **likely** here; mechanism and squeeze are not separable from this one case.

### D. Partnership-credibility rerating (1): ASTS
Fundamentals **absent / pre-revenue**; re-rating rode entirely on partnership credibility (Jan strategic investment, then AT&T + Verizon in the same quarter) and a satellite-launch milestone — the four rungs of Stage 3. Meme amplification flagged **possible, not ruled out**.

### E. Index-flow-amplified (1, overlapping A): PLTR
Broken out because the S&P 500 forced-inclusion rung is doing distinct mechanical work (passive inflow) on top of an operating-leverage core. Retail/meme contamination is explicitly acknowledged as inseparable from the fundamental signal in this name.

**What differs across families:** presence of fundamentals (A/C have them, B/D don't); whether the move is one-binary-event (B) or ladder-accretive (A); and whether index inclusion supplies a mechanical amplifier (APP, PLTR, SMCI). What is *shared* across all five: compressed prior, sector beta ruled out, gap-hold, `platform_rerating` mechanism (10/11).

## 4. Candidate fingerprints worth testing (NOT registered)

Each candidate below is **computable** and stated with the **base-rate question it must survive against the full census** (which is `blow_off`-dominated ~6:1). None is registered; registration is a W3 decision requiring an explicit ruling (WA-R8, Section 6). No composite is proposed — these are separable features precisely so the census can reject them individually. None is proposed as a filter, gate, screen, or entry condition; they are census questions, not operating rules (WA-R1/R5).

**F1 — Catalyst-ladder rung count ≥2 (within a bounded window).**
*Computable:* count distinct catalyst-tagged onsets/events within N trading days.
*Base-rate question:* Among *all* census onsets, do blow-offs *also* routinely show ≥2 rungs? If most blow-offs are single-pop, rung-count discriminates; if blow-offs accrete confirming events just as often (plausible — hype begets follow-on headlines), it does not. This is the feature most at risk of being post-hoc-inflated by winner selection.

**F2 — Trigger gap holds 3/5/10 sessions.**
*Computable:* gap % on onset day, close vs prior close at t+3/+5/+10.
*Base-rate question:* What fraction of *blow_off* onsets *also* hold their initial gap 3/5/10 sessions before failing later? SMMT's own May-fail / Sept-hold contrast warns that gap-hold is not binary at the episode level. The census must report the blow-off gap-hold rate; a high blow-off hold rate guts this feature.

**F3 — Profit step-up faster than revenue (fundamental subspecies only).**
*Computable:* sign of Δ(operating or net income %) − Δ(revenue %) QoQ, at the print nearest onset.
*Base-rate question:* Among census names *with fundamentals* that blew off, how common is margin-outpacing-revenue? If AI-cycle blow-offs show the same margin optics, this is a coincident-of-the-era artifact, not a discriminator. **Gated by the A2 firewall (WA-R7):** 2024+ fundamentals aggregation is restricted, and several winners (SMCI, ALNY) have **zero local fundamentals rows**, so this feature is only testable where in-repo panel coverage exists — a hard constraint on the census subset.

**F4 — New 63d (or 252d) high co-timed with excess_21d ≥ 20pp.**
*Computable:* first-new-high flag on/near onset AND 21d excess vs sector ETF ≥ 20pp.
*Base-rate question:* Blow-offs *by construction* also print new highs with large short-horizon excess — that's what a blow-off *is* at t0. So the real test is not the feature at onset but its **durability**: conditional on {new high + excess ≥20pp at 21d}, what is the census split of durable-winner vs blow-off at 63d/126d? This feature likely does *not* discriminate at t0 (to be measured) and is probably interesting only as a durability conditioner.

**F5 — Sector beta ruled out (idiosyncratic excess, not sector lift).**
*Computable:* excess vs sector ETF at 21d/63d/126d, sector ETF return over same window.
*Base-rate question:* Do blow-offs *also* clear their sector by large margins on the way up (very likely yes)? If so, "sector beta ruled out" is necessary but non-discriminating. Useful mainly to *exclude* sympathy-only moves, not to select winners.

**F6 — Compressed-prior presence (a pre-onset doubt the catalyst resolves).**
*Computable — hardest:* requires a machinable proxy (e.g., short interest percentile, consensus-target-vs-spot gap as in MRNA's 45.85 vs 81.80, or an analyst-dispersion measure) rather than a narrative.
*Base-rate question:* Is elevated pre-onset doubt *more* common before durable winners than before blow-offs? Note CVNA's distressed prior and the meme-adjacent names show doubt and squeeze fuel co-occur — so a naive "high short interest" proxy may load on blow-offs too.

**Explicit non-features:** we do **not** propose "meme/squeeze ruled out" as a positive fingerprint — it is unquantifiable pre-2026 (no per-ticker options/short history in-repo), and in CVNA/ASTS/PLTR/SMMT/VKTX it is flagged *possible/likely*, not excluded.

## 5. What the reconciliation taught us

Comparing the mechanical Detector D onset dates against the human-picked t0 over the 11 case tickers:

- **6/11 matched within ≤21 trading days of the human date:** ALNY (exact day), PLTR (next day), NVDA, SMCI (10td early), VRT, ASTS. The detector independently lands on the famous catalyst in a majority of cases.
- **3/11 the engine flagged EARLIER than the famous catalyst — the tape leads the headline:** CVNA (engine flags May vs the July debt deal), APP (September vs November earnings), MRNA (Dec-2025 vs Jan-2026 oncology data). The relative-strength breakaway *preceded* the event the human narrative anchors on. This is a caution against dating episodes by their most-quoted catalyst.
- **VKTX — cooldown suppression:** the engine dates onset to Dec-2023 oral data; a 63-trading-day cooldown then *suppressed* the famous Feb-2024 event as a separate onset. The mechanical onset and the human's "the" catalyst are simply different dates.
- **SMMT — data gap, not detector failure:** a 237-day store-coverage gap (SMMT store coverage begins ~2025-01) means its 2024 run is **unobservable in-store**. This is a coverage miss, and any census work must carry an explicit observability flag for such names.
- **Winners break away repeatedly:** all 11 names produced **72 onsets total across 2021–2026**. Being a winner ticker does *not* mean a clean single breakaway — it means *many* breakaways, only some durable. Base rates therefore cannot be read off the winner names; they live in the full census.
- **Onset-day dollar-volume z is NOT uniformly high:** on the *mechanical onset* day, `dollar_vol_z` was ALNY 4.3, **NVDA −0.3, PLTR 2.1, VRT 1.3** — even though the same cases report z≈4 on the *catalyst* day. The relative-strength breakaway often **precedes** the volume spike. Any fingerprint keyed to onset-day volume-z would miss the low-z onsets; volume confirmation is a *catalyst-day* phenomenon, not an *onset-day* one.

## 6. Honest limits & next step

**Selection on the dependent variable.** The single dominating limitation. These 11 are winners *by construction*; shared features are necessary-looking, never sufficient. Everything above is a hypothesis generator, not evidence.

**Small n = 11.** Five subspecies over eleven names means some families are n=1 (turnaround: CVNA; partnership: ASTS; index-flow: PLTR). No within-family generalization is defensible.

**Survivorship, concentrated.** Several cases are *the* standout of their cycle (NVDA the AI-capex beneficiary, APP the 2024 adtech winner, SMCI the 2024 AI-server move, PLTR among the most-followed retail names). These are once-per-cycle confluences, not repeatable setup frequencies.

**A2 firewall on 2024+ fundamentals (WA-R7).** Fundamentals aggregation for 2024+ is restricted, and local panel coverage is *absent* for several winners (SMCI and ALNY: zero rows; ASTS/SMMT/VKTX pre-revenue). Feature F3 is only testable on the census subset with in-repo panel coverage.

**Options / revisions unavailable pre-2026.** No per-ticker options history exists in-repo before ~2026-06, so the options-convexity / gamma-squeeze leg is **structurally unverifiable** for every 2023–2024 episode. "Meme/squeeze ruled out" is therefore a *narrative* judgment, not a measured one — and is explicitly *not* ruled out in CVNA, ASTS, PLTR, SMMT, VKTX.

**Meme/squeeze confound.** Where flagged likely/possible (CVNA especially), mechanism and squeeze magnitude cannot be separated from a single case.

**Next step — W3 pre-registered pairlet.** The disciplined move is a pre-registered test of **≤5 features** (draw from F1–F6), against the **full mechanical census** (blow_off-dominated ~6:1), with **one ruler** (single declared horizon), **month-clustered** standard errors (to defeat the time-confound where effective N is months, not fires), and an **era-split** (to check the features aren't just 2023–2024 AI-cycle artifacts). That test is what would tell us whether any of these features *discriminate* durable winners from blow-offs. **It is not registered here.** Registration requires an explicit Fable/operator ruling per **WA-R8** — this report only nominates the candidates and states the base-rate questions each must survive.

*No composite score is proposed. No feature is described as "validated." All claims above are display-only until gauntleted against the full census.*
