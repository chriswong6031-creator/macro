The inherited race ruler may scan up to **250 sessions**. Episode sealing can require the registered post-peak window. Do not truncate the outcome clock merely because only several weeks of refreshed data exist.

A future OOT prereg may tighten a floor before results if justified, but it may not lower the old protection after seeing results merely to produce a verdict.

### 5.4 Maturity state

Before final inference, publish/read a deterministic maturity receipt only:

- OOT start/end observed;

If floors are unmet the exact scientific state is:

`OOT_ACCRUING_NO_VERDICT`

No model may upgrade that state.

### 5.5 OOT final adjudication

When all frozen gates naturally clear, report separately by tier:

No pooled cross-tier scalar or “overall Top Anatomy score.”

## 11. COO / watcher operating model

Difficulty is high: statistical design, current R2/data plumbing, product integration, multiple existing owner fences and a natural-evidence wait. Preferred operator is **Fable principal builder using the strongest available Claude/Opus-class reasoning seat**, not a mechanical worker.

The sustained COO may retain program context across R0-R6, but each bounded child wave is a distinct operation with its own stable operation key and Slack thread.

For every child wave:

1. Initial Slack envelope contains the pre-work ACK requirement.
2. Receiver replies `ACK <operation_key>`, then reads the entire thread before work.
3. If gated, receiver posts a separate `START <operation_key>` only after the gate clears and after current pickup/collision recheck.
4. Sol and receiver both arm an exact-thread temporary watcher/wait bridge while canonical Turn-Watcher is not production-proven.
5. `BLOCKED`, `DECISION_REQUEST`, `PROGRESS`, `RESULT`, Sol `RULING`/`CONTINUE`/`STOP` all remain in that exact thread.
6. After each nonterminal turn both sides re-arm their wait/watch.
7. On Sol terminal acceptance/`STOP`, both watchers are terminated and the worker ends that session. The next independent child wave gets a new operation key/thread, even if the same Fable COO is assigned again.

Watcher behavior is attention/transport only; it never owns Job/Attempt/Worker state, retry, provider selection, authority or completion.
