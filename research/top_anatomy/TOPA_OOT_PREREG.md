## §4 Floors and maturity (tighten-only law)

Inherited floors per registered cell (masterplan §5.3 — tightened here where noted,
never loosened): **≥12 distinct episode-peak months** · **≥100 matched topped episodes**
· match rate printed, <50% adds MATCH-STARVED · AM-v2 signed validity rule governs each
(construction × panel).

**Sealing/maturity law (inherited timing, made explicit for OOT):**
- a case episode enters the candidate set only when §4.4 SEALS it (TOPPED via the −20%
  print with no intervening higher close, or SURVIVED via the full [peak, peak+126]
  window observed). Unsealed = IMMATURE, counted, never graded;
- a drawn control day's §4.3 race must have resolved, else it is CENSORED, counted,
  never imputed; races may take up to 250 sessions — the ruler is never shortened.

**Truncation/speed-selection bias (declared pre-results, with a tighten-only floor):**
on a tape truncated at read time, sealed cases are enriched in *fast* toppers and
resolved controls in *fast* continuers, concentrated in the most recent peak months.
Declared direction per registered leg: for B2 and B3 (+; heat legs) and for F1 (−; young
age) this selection plausibly biases the case−control delta TOWARD the registered
direction — i.e. **anti-conservative for all three registered cells** — so completeness
is load-bearing, not cosmetic. New tighten-only floor: within EVERY era block of a graded
cell, ≥80% of candidate case episodes must be sealed AND ≥80% of drawn control days'
races resolved; a cell failing this in any block is `OOT-UNDERPOWERED`. The §5 receipt
prints the per-era sealing/resolution census.

**Era blocks (MANDATORY, no activation condition):** era blocks always run on any cell
clearing the floors above, per the phase-1 §5 recipe — with ≥12 distinct peak months a
three-block split always exists. A cell below the floors is `OOT-UNDERPOWERED` and
prints no era cells. The `-ERA-CAVEAT` modifier can therefore never be silently deleted.

**Earliest-possible timing, stated so nobody cycles the gate early:** sealing arithmetic
alone permits the first SURVIVED seals around 2027-01 (126 sessions from 2026-07-03),
but the **binding** constraint on any graded cell is the ≥12 distinct-peak-month floor —
peaks spanning 2026-07 through at least 2027-06, plus their sealing windows — so no
registered cell can clear before roughly **2027-07**. A receipt printing
`final_verdict_eligible: false` throughout 2026 is the designed behavior.
