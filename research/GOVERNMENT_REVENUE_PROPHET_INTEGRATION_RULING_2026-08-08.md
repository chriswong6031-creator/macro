# Government Revenue × Prophet — integration ruling (2026-08-08)

**Operator question.** Should the govrev lobe assess price/technical confluence itself and score which names are asymmetric buys — or does Prophet onboard these names anyway? Both directions felt messy: a lobe that mints Prophet picks breaks separation; a lobe that only flags, with no score and no why, is useless.

**Ruling.** Neither. The messiness dissolves once "scoring" splits into three jobs that never share a hat:

1. **The lobe owns the WHY, never the verdict.** Every candidate carries named, separately-inspectable evidence legs — procurement event (family, receipts, magnitude vs baseline), issuer attribution (reviewed graph path), and market-context legs (technical trend / relative strength; volatility, liquidity, regime fit; run-up and extension percentile at candidate known_at; Prophet's own published technical-confluence state for the name where it exists, read read-only). No fused super-score anywhere: a fused number hides which leg did the work, and constitution A7 forbids the lobe originating a signal. "Flagging with no why" is thereby dead — the why IS the artifact.
2. **Prophet keeps sole pick authority.** Wave 9F delivers govrev to Prophet as post-selection annotation with a byte-identical on/off proof. Prophet cannot call govrev to source a candidate; govrev cannot push one in. Govrev context appears ON Prophet picks and on the govrev radar — never AS a Prophet pick.
3. **The grader is the only promotion path.** Wave 9G grades one preregistered family prospectively. If the family shows real lift, Wave 12's gauntlet proposes narrow authority: that specific event family, a bounded issuer universe, explicit abstention, rollback. That is the sanctioned form of "the lobe recommends to Prophet": an admitted confluence input to Prophet's anticipation funnel — Prophet still mints, sizes, and gates every pick.

**Why the lobe must not assess asymmetry directly.** "Assessed asymmetric by the lobe" is an origination event, and it is ungradeable: when the same layer selects and explains, the grader can no longer separate identity coverage, event coverage, and market outcome. The three-way split keeps every leg falsifiable on its own clock.

**Time-consciousness law (binding on all market legs).** Every market-context leg is computed strictly point-in-time at candidate known_at (latest bar with timestamp ≤ known_at); each leg carries its own source/observed/known-at clocks, freshness, and status (present | stale | missing | contradictory); run-up/extension are percentiles against the name's own history, never absolutes; any materiality ratio requires an exact issuer-attributed denominator with its own receipt clock — otherwise the leg reports abstained rather than guessing.
