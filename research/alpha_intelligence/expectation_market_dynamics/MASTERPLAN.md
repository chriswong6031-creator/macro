## 1. Outcome

Build an institutional-grade, point-in-time primitive that can answer, without
pretending certainty:

1. what an identified expectation-bearing source said, for which issuer,
   fiscal period, metric, horizon and population;
2. what changed in those expectations, when the change became observable and
   whether it was later corrected;
3. what the security, peers, factors, market, options and attention planes did
   before and after that change;
4. which parts of the response appear unincorporated, assimilating, contested,
   exhausted or simply unestimable; and
5. how those descriptive states behave prospectively under a preregistered,
   owner-preserving evaluation protocol.

This is not a five-state heuristic. The internal state is a versioned,
multi-axis observation and inference structure. A short label, if a product
eventually needs one, is only a projection with the complete decomposition and
degradation receipt still visible.

the engine is a composition of owner-native observations, deterministic views
and research artifacts, never a universal belief database.

## 4. Non-negotiable architecture

```text
owner-native sources
  -> PIT observation adapters
  -> expectation surface (typed, multi-horizon, population-receipted)
  -> owner-native market response adapters
  -> temporal alignment and factor/peer residual references
  -> multi-axis incorporation dynamics
  -> descriptive projection with UNESTIMABLE legs
  -> preregistered evaluation and prospective accrual
  -> optional Market OS reference projection after owner acceptance
```

Every edge preserves:

- source owner and source rights;
- issuer/security identity reference and share-class treatment;
- metric, unit, fiscal period, horizon and analyst/sample population;
- source observation time, system observation time, market session and
  correction/supersession lineage;
- coverage, freshness, denominator, degradation and uncertainty;
- whether a field is observed, deterministically derived, model inferred or
  unavailable; and
- permitted consumers and authority.

The system must never create a duplicate store, ranker, identity plane,
residual engine, event system, lifecycle, evaluation ledger or publication
plane. DRL, residual-alpha, stock identity, Earnings/event, options, Eval OS,
Prophet fusion and Market OS remain the owners they already are.

- `may_size: false`;

- `may_train_prophet: false`; and
- `proposal_weight: 0`.

No field named `gap`, `belief`, `phase`, `assimilation`, `surprise`, `expected`
or `incorporation` silently expands this authority. Prophet consumption needs
its existing conditional-fusion and Eval OS promotion gates. Market OS may
project accepted references but does not originate the underlying truth.

`UNESTIMABLE` is a successful first-class result whenever clocks, coverage,
rights, identity, baseline, price response or model support is insufficient.
Partial computation cannot look complete.

## 7. Bounded vertical sequence

The executable dependency graph is frozen in `BUILD_SEQUENCE.md`.

Immediately after K3E-0 acceptance, three independent lanes may run in
parallel, each as one independently useful PR and each stopping with an evidence
return:

1. `SRC-A1` — raw prospective multi-horizon EPS/revenue expectation accrual in
   the existing revisions owner lane;
2. `VEND-0` — institutional estimates vendor bake-off, with no recommendation
   from marketing material alone; and
3. `EVAL-0` — preregister baselines, targets, splits, missingness and promotion
   law before advanced model tuning.

No EXP/MKT/CPL runtime begins until all three returns are adjudicated. Later
waves remain bounded and owner-routed: expectation baselines, market response,
coupling/dynamics, historical casebook, prospective evaluation, Market OS
projection, and only then any promotion proposal.
