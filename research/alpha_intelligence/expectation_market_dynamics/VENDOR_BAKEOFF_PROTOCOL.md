# VEND-0 Vendor Bake-Off Protocol

## Goal

Determine whether a paid institutional estimates vendor materially changes the
lawful K3E capability relative to current free / existing estate.

## Decision question

Does a candidate vendor unlock enough of the following to justify later adapter
cost and rights complexity?

- analyst- or provider-level history
- broader horizon coverage
- richer KPI / estimate families
- fresher or cleaner revision chronology
- stronger correction lineage
- materially deeper point-in-time history

## Evaluation dimensions

1. granularity: consensus-only vs analyst/provider-native
2. horizon breadth: quarter / year / rolling windows
3. metric breadth: EPS, revenue, margin, KPI, guidance-like fields
4. PIT integrity: issued / available / known / superseded clocks
5. corrections and revision lineage
6. history depth by region and metric
7. rights / redistribution constraints
8. integration burden
9. failure modes and dark spots

## Deliverables

- one comparison table against current estate;
- one recommendation: `NO_VENDOR_YET`, `PROBE_FURTHER`, or `PURSUE_VENDOR_X`;
- sample-record receipts, not just marketing claims;
- a rights and cost memo.

## Explicit stop

No adapter, no procurement commitment, no runtime code.
