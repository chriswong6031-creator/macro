# EVAL-0 Preregistered Evaluation Protocol

## Why now

K3E must not graduate from descriptive architecture into predictive claims by
winner-picking after the fact.

## Freeze before advanced tuning

### Targets

- next revision direction
- next revision arrival
- next revision-cluster onset
- next consensus change
- next dispersion direction
- next phase transition

### Universe and eras

- exact issuer universe definition
- minimum observation thresholds
- development / validation / modern holdout era split
- explicit exclusions

### Baselines

- no-change
- latest consensus
- simple 30d / 90d revision summaries
- fresh median
- deterministic wave baseline

### Metrics

- calibration
- ranking quality where lawful
- event-time lead / lag accuracy
- abstention quality
- subgroup robustness

### Statistical hygiene

- hyperparameter-search budget
- multiple-testing / FDR procedure
- family and coverage subgroups
- promotion thresholds
- exact null / abstention accounting

## Promotion law

No advanced model receives production or scoring authority from EVAL-0. EVAL-0
only fixes the competition rules.
