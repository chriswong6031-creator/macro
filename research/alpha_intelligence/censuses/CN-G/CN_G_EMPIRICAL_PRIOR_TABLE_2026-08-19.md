## 0. How to use this pack

This pack is a **source-cited prior table**, not a result. A paper that finds alpha on 2009–2013 SZSE visits does not license a visit score on the 2026 Prophet shelf.

Standing constraints (inherited, not re-derived):

- Epistemics: display/research freely; gauntlet only at promotion. A factor that is null as a *standalone* signal may still be a confluence input. "Not found yet" ≠ "does not exist."
- Instrument verdicts are not market verdicts.
- Adjudication coverage: a rule that refuses the motivating exemplars is not the answer.
- `DNR:KILL-OWNERSHIP-BREAKAWAY` / `DNR:KILL-SPONSORSHIP-SCORE`: ownership/sponsorship as a *positive fused score* is already killed on the US side. China families must not re-mint that construction under a new name.
- `DNR:KILL-LLM-ORIGINATION`: LLMs may label Q&A/visit text; they may not originate a score.
- #5822 §18: primary scientific question is **incremental value conditional on Prophet eligibility**, fill-realistic T+1, H10/H21 primary. Sparse events use matched controls. Persistent-state features cannot use long-horizon feature-shift placebos (`DSC:CN-PERSISTENT-STATE-DEFEATS-PLACEBO-SHIFT`).
- Non-goal of this pack: **no weights. No "paper says alpha therefore wire it."**

Every Phase-0 test below is a **proposed measurement**, not a run. None of them is a promotion packet.
