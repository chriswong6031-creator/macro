# 0. Executive verdict

The existing Government Revenue implementation should not be discarded. It contains a meaningful evidence substrate:

- USAspending collection and receipts;
- award and transaction histories;
- normalized award-change events;
- SAM opportunity foundations;
- IDV, subaward, and SBIR foundations;

- prospective grading foundations;
- display-only federation into Neural Web and Prophet-adjacent context.

But the current product boundary is far too small, the live experience has not met the user's practical acceptance test, the identity graph remains the dominant bottleneck, and the current system does not yet answer the investor question:

> **Which public companies are gaining or losing economically meaningful government-program exposure, when will that exposure reach revenue and cash flow, what did the market already anticipate, what remains mispriced, what would falsify the thesis, and how much independent information should this contribute to Neural Web and Prophet?**

The V2 program therefore makes a larger strategic change:

> **Preserve and harden the existing evidence spine. Rebuild the product as a modular Defense Procurement Intelligence OS. Add a temporal procurement neural graph, a company/program/vehicle/supply-chain intelligence estate, a timing and seasonality engine, an economic-transmission engine, a market-incorporation and asymmetry engine, a forward-measurement factory, and governed Neural Web and Prophet adapters.**

The destination is not a GovCon CRM, not a contract-news feed, not a GovTribe clone, and not one opaque “defense score.”

The destination is:

> **The most advanced point-in-time public-market intelligence system for understanding the defense-procurement economy—from requirement and budget formation through forecasts, solicitations, vehicles, awards, obligations, execution, options, recompetes, protests, and program outcomes—and translating those changes into auditable issuer-level economic, timing, market-dislocation, and asymmetric-return hypotheses.**

This plan deliberately dreams at full scale and ships in small, independently useful vertical slices.

# 1. What changed in this hardening pass

The earlier procurement plan was directionally correct but still too close to a recovery roadmap. This V2 hardening pass incorporates the strongest lessons from the BioCatalyst recovery, the Seasonality program, the current Earnings Intelligence OS architecture, current Prophet family fusion, Neural Web, and a fresh public GovTribe product/docs scan.

## 1.1 BioCatalyst lessons carried forward

BioCatalyst demonstrated that a serious temporal evidence substrate can coexist with a weak, blank, or unusable product. The procurement program therefore adopts these binding rules:

1. **Preserve the evidence spine, but do not confuse it with the product.**
2. **One domain graph, many read models.** Do not create page-specific truth stores.
3. **Modular product estate.** A single three-column workbench is not the final information architecture.
4. **Typed failure states.** Authentication, entitlement, source, transport, projection, schema, empty, stale, and unavailable are distinct.
5. **Browser proof is completion.** A collector, schema, route, fixture, or `200` shell is not a shipped user capability.
6. **Evidence eligibility precedes modeling.**
7. **Opportunity and confidence remain separate.**
8. **Scenario distributions precede a display score.**
9. **Operator review belongs in an operator console, not the investor surface.**
10. **Prophet receives bounded evidence-family members only after shadow evidence.**

## 1.2 Seasonality lessons carried forward

The Seasonality program contributes several load-bearing ideas:

1. **Temporal uncertainty must be structural.** A quarter, month, range, or “expected in FY27” may not be silently collapsed to a fabricated exact timestamp.
2. **Use bounded temporal contracts:** `precision`, `lower_bound`, `upper_bound`, and `bound_rule`; exact values exist only when the source supports exactness.
3. **Built-but-inert engines are not capabilities.** Every event study, model, calibration, Neural Web, or Prophet module must have a real runner, DAG placement, publication artifact, and consumer.
4. **Evidence, not code, is the promotion bottleneck.**
5. **Unknown, unavailable, unresolved, quarantined, and abstained are first-class states.**
6. **Independent receipts matter.** A test that repeats a hard-coded claim from the same variable cannot falsify the claim.
7. **Separate source-content freshness from transport/publication freshness.**
8. **Conditional timing beats naive calendar averages.** Fiscal-year seasonality must be conditioned on agency, office, program, vehicle, event class, funding regime, and source coverage.
9. **Mutation-test critical guards.** A guard that has never been observed to fail is a hypothesis.

## 1.3 Sol executive doctrine carried forward

The V2 program follows these execution rules:

- define the outcome before code;
- design the complete system, then ship one useful vertical capability per PR;
- reuse canonical owners instead of creating duplicate planes;
- preserve exact evidence beside all derived analysis;
- use competitor research for clean-room workflow parity, not code or UI copying;
- green CI is necessary but not user acceptance;
- do not grant signal authority merely because infrastructure exists;
- write durable decisions, discoveries, and handoffs into the shared repository knowledge plane;
- require every major wave to produce a falsifiable evidence bundle.

# 4. Product north star

The OS should answer eight questions for every meaningful procurement change:

1. **What changed?**
2. **When did it become knowable and tradable?**
3. **Which public issuers are economically exposed, through which exact legal and program paths?**
4. **How material is it relative to the correct issuer, segment, program, contract, and market denominators?**
5. **When and how could it reach backlog, revenue, margin, cash flow, guidance, and valuation?**
6. **What did price, options, flow, analysts, management, and public attention already absorb?**
7. **What remains asymmetric, what is the uncertainty, and what would falsify the case?**
8. **What should Neural Web and Prophet know, and what authority—if any—has been earned?**

## 4.1 Proprietary moat stack

The moat is not one exclusive source. It is the compounding set of reviewed joins, temporal history, economic translation, and prospective evidence.

1. **First-Seen Temporal Ledger**  
   Exact source versions, first-known clocks, corrections, and as-known replay across the complete procurement lifecycle.

2. **Defense Identity Atlas**  
   Effective-dated issuer, subsidiary, JV, consortium, recipient, facility, program, and security relationships.

3. **Requirement-to-Revenue Lifecycle Graph**  
   The connected thread from strategic requirement and budget formation through forecast, solicitation, vehicle, award, obligation, execution, option, and recompete.

4. **Contract Quality and Revenue-Realization Engine**  
   Funding certainty, contract type, incentive structure, option quality, margin/cash-conversion risk, recognition timing, and downside conditions—not headline value.

5. **Defense Industrial-Base Capacity Graph**  
   Facilities, production lines, bottleneck suppliers, workforce, capex, lead times, materials, and the ability to convert public funding into actual deliveries and earnings.

6. **Program and Supply-Chain Read-Through**  
   Direct beneficiaries, suppliers, JV partners, incumbents, challengers, displaced competitors, and adjacent platforms.

7. **Conditional Timing and Procurement Seasonality**  
   Agency-, office-, program-, vehicle-, event-, and funding-regime-specific hazard rather than naive fiscal-year averages.

8. **Expectations and Market-Incorporation Engine**  
   Official facts versus prior disclosure, estimates, media attention, price, options, flow, and theme movement.

9. **Prospective Measurement Factory**  
   Frozen families, forward ledgers, fundamental outcomes, market outcomes, calibration, ablations, and a model kill registry.

10. **Neural Web and Prophet-Native Publication**  
    Compact causal graph deltas and independent, abstaining evidence members with explicit overlap, lineage, and authority.

Each moat improves with every reviewed mapping, source revision, event outcome, model forecast, and analyst adjudication. That compounding labeled history is more defensible than a one-time feature checklist.

## 4.2 The final user jobs

### Daily investor

- open a daily briefing and see the most economically important procurement changes;
- know which changes are genuinely new versus late backfills;
- filter by ticker, program, mission, agency, event, horizon, confidence, and market state;
- understand why an issuer is exposed;
- see the next confirmation or falsification clocks;
- add a company, program, contract, or theme to a watch.

### Deep analyst

- replay the procurement state as known at a historical time;
- inspect source records, files, diffs, ownership paths, and corrections;
- navigate from budget line to program to vehicle to award to supplier to issuer;
- compare programs, vehicles, agencies, incumbents, challengers, and peers;
- run scenario and event-study analyses;
- test whether a signal is already priced.

### Neural Web and Mastermind

- consume compact, versioned, evidence-backed graph deltas;
- receive causal mechanisms, affected entities, horizons, confidence, contradictions, and falsifiers;
- reason across procurement, earnings, options, macro, geopolitical, and supply-chain lobes;
- never parse raw stores or infer authority from prose.

### Prophet

- receive null-safe, point-in-time, independent feature-family contributions;
- know lineage and overlap with existing evidence;
- abstain on stale, ambiguous, late, or uncalibrated records;
- measure incremental rank lift in shadow before any live order effect.

# 7. Temporal and epistemic contract

Procurement intelligence is unusable for investment research unless it distinguishes occurrence from knowledge.

## 7.1 Required clocks

Every fact, edge, event, and derived observation should support the applicable subset of:

- `effective_lower_bound`;
- `effective_upper_bound`;
- `effective_precision`;
- `effective_bound_rule`;
- `source_published_at`;
- `source_updated_at`;
- `first_observed_at`;
- `retrieved_at`;
- `normalized_at`;
- `system_known_at`;
- `first_tradable_at`;
- `corrected_at`;
- `superseded_at`;
- `valid_from`;
- `valid_to`;
- `as_of`;
- `generated_at`.

## 7.2 No fabricated exactness

Examples:

- “Q2 FY27” is a bounded period, not midnight on April 1.
- “Expected award in summer” is a range with low precision.
- “Option year begins in September” is not an exact exercise date.
- A budget justification released as a PDF has a source-publication clock distinct from the fiscal period it describes.
- An award action effective in May but first observed in August is a late discovery, not a fresh May catalyst.
- An acquisition announced today but legally closed later changes issuer ownership on the effective interval, not retroactively.

Contracts should make invalid precision difficult or impossible to represent.

## 7.3 Required epistemic states

- `official_exact`;
- `official_bounded`;
- `derived_reviewed`;
- `model_estimate`;
- `unresolved`;
- `contradicted`;
- `corrected`;
- `superseded`;

# 13. Economic exposure and transmission engine

A procurement fact becomes investor-useful only when translated through the correct economic path.

## 13.1 Required semantic distinctions

- ceiling;
- current value;

- funded obligation;
- cumulative obligation;
- incremental action amount;
- deobligation;
- outlay;
- backlog;
- funded backlog;
- revenue;
- segment revenue;
- cash flow;
- budget request;
- appropriation;
- estimated opportunity value;
- probability-adjusted value.

Never add incompatible classes.

## 13.2 Event-specific exposure models

### Funded award/action

```text
attributable_funded_value
  = net_incremental_obligation
  × legal_entity_attribution_share
  × issuer_economic_share
```

```text
expected_revenue_t
  = attributable_funded_value
  × recognition_weight_t
  × execution_probability
```

### Ceiling or IDIQ

```text
expected_funded_value
  = incremental_ceiling
  × calibrated_drawdown_fraction
  × issuer_capture_share
  × execution_probability
```

### Opportunity

```text
probability_adjusted_exposure
  = estimated_funded_value
  × P(win | incumbent, vehicle, agency, competition, history)
  × issuer_share
  × execution_probability
```

### Recompete

```text
expected_delta
  = P(retain) × retained_economics
  + P(expand) × expansion_economics
  - P(loss) × lost_economics
```

### Budget/program

```text
expected_program_impulse
  = budget_delta
  × P(authorization)
  × P(appropriation)
  × P(procurement_conversion)
  × issuer_program_share
  × timing_discount
```

## 13.3 Denominator architecture

Every numerator is compared to appropriate point-in-time denominators:

### Procurement

- issuer-attributed TTM obligations;
- program obligations;
- agency/mission exposure;
- award/vehicle totals;
- historical option exercise;
- contract/recompete wall.

### Corporate

- TTM revenue;
- defense/segment revenue;
- backlog/funded backlog;
- operating income;
- FCF;
- market cap;
- enterprise value;
- shares and dilution;
- guidance range.

### Market

- ADV and liquidity;
- options-implied move;
- realized volatility;
- factor/theme expected response;
- analyst estimate dispersion where licensed.

No denominator means abstain or show a descriptive amount—not a materiality score.

- falsifier.

- backlog and funded backlog;
- program quantities;
- production cadence;
- guidance;
- segment targets;
- contract/program mentions;
- disclosed wins and losses;
- management timing claims.

Later official and financial evidence should confirm, revise, or falsify those commitments.

## 13.6 Contract Quality Engine

Headline amount is only one axis of contract value. The engine should assess:

- contract type: firm-fixed-price, cost-plus, incentive, T&M, hybrid;
- pricing and escalation clauses;
- fee/incentive structure;
- funded versus unfunded share;
- option quality and exercise history;
- period of performance and delivery cadence;
- customer/office payment behavior where supportable;
- competition and protest risk;
- termination rights;
- supplier pass-through;
- working-capital requirements;
- capital intensity;
- inflation and commodity exposure;
- schedule and technical risk;
- historical EAC charges or loss-contract behavior;
- cash-conversion profile.

Outputs:

- revenue certainty;
- margin confidence;
- cash-conversion confidence;
- downside-risk flags;
- contract-quality class;
- assumption and evidence trace.

A large fixed-price development award with capacity constraints can be economically worse than a smaller, funded, repeat production order. The system must be able to say so.

## 13.7 Industrial-Base Capacity and Monetization

Funding cannot become revenue without capacity.

Build a reviewed capacity graph for strategically important cases:

- factories and shipyards;
- production lines;
- depots and test facilities;
- specialized suppliers;
- energetic materials and critical components;
- workforce and skilled-labor constraints;
- tooling and qualification;
- lead times;
- capex and government-funded expansion;
- Defense Production Act/industrial-base awards;
- production-rate commitments;
- quality and schedule incidents;
- geographic concentration;
- single-source dependencies.

Estimate:

```text
economic realization
  = funded demand
  × executable capacity
  × supplier availability
  × schedule probability
  × contract economics
```

Capacity evidence belongs in the transmission and downside model, not as narrative decoration.

## 13.8 Government Truth versus Company Truth

Create a divergence detector between:

- official award/obligation/program evidence;
- company press releases;
- reported backlog;
- earnings-call commentary;
- guidance;
- segment results;
- analyst expectations.

Potential states:

- official evidence leads company disclosure;
- company disclosed before official record;
- official funding confirms management;
- official record contradicts or weakens narrative;
- company overstates ceiling as economic value;
- backlog conversion lags official funding;
- official activity is material but omitted from discussion;
- disclosure is present but market response is limited.

This divergence can be a high-value information-gap feature, but every side of the comparison must be point-in-time.

- redundancy controlled;
- cap and rollback;
- only already-admitted candidates;
- null/stale/ambiguous abstention;
- contribution trace;
- no selection/sizing/entry authority.

# 34. Final operating maxims

> **Evidence first. Exact identity second. Lifecycle third. Economic transmission fourth. Timing fifth. Market incorporation sixth. Forward measurement seventh. Neural synthesis eighth. Prophet authority last.**

> **Facts before joins. Joins before models. Models before shadows. Shadows before authority.**

> **One graph, many products. One owner per fact. One lineage per causal event.**

> **Unknown is not zero. Late is not fresh. Ceiling is not revenue. Attention is not incorporation. Call buying is not bullish proof.**

> **A builder is not a capability until it runs. An endpoint is not a product until the user journey works. A score is not evidence until it survives forward outcomes.**
