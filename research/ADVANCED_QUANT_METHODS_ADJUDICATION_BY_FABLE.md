## §1 What the intake is, and whether its evidence is real

The proposal is an honest, competent research synthesis — **not** a fabricated-citation document. Verification (all 20 citations fetched):

- **All arXiv/NBER IDs exist and are fairly represented**, including the 2026-dated ones (AlphaCFG 2601.22119; decision-focused-inflation 2605.01176; NBER w34861 "Machine Learning Meets Markowitz", Harvey et al., Feb 2026).
- **One overstatement found:** the path-signatures primer (1603.03788) contains **no finance examples** — its one worked example is digit classification. Cite it for signature theory only.
- **Recency risk is concentrated exactly where the proposal is most ambitious:** NewsNet-SDF's Sharpe 2.80 / "471% over CAPM" (2505.06864) is a ~3-month-old unreplicated preprint; AlphaCFG is ~6 months old, zero independent citations; the two decision-focused portfolio papers are 3–10 months old.
- **The deep-SDF headline deflates on inspection:** Chen-Pelger-Zhu (1904.00745, Management Science 2024) reports monthly Sharpe 2.68 train → 1.43 valid → **0.75 test**; no independent post-2020 re-run exists anywhere (open question, not a confirmed pass).
- **The strongest independently-evidenced family is distributional forecasting:** Baruník-Hronec-Tobek (2408.07497) is conditionally accepted at the *International Journal of Forecasting* with OOS portfolio value across global markets — genuinely independent of every other cited group.
- **Adversarial sweeps:** formulaic alpha mining decays/overfits by default (the field's own toolmakers concede it; no named system has survived an independent cost-realistic re-test). Stock-GNN gains have no cost-realistic independent replication; benchmark studies (LOBCAST 2024) show large performance drops on new data. Online expert aggregation verifiably beats the *best single expert* (Remlinger et al., journal-published) but "beats equal-weight" is **not** independently confirmed. Synthetic-data consensus: stress-testing yes, validation never. The foundation-model claim traces to a real paper (2511.18578) fairly paraphrased, in a still-settling literature.

Verdict on the document itself: use it as a map of the search space. Its own "what I would not prioritize" list (no off-the-shelf foundation models, no direct LLM predictions, no unconstrained RL, quantile-before-diffusion, no autonomous factor deployment) **already agrees with standing house law** — the disagreements are narrower than they look and are itemized below.

## §2 The seven walls every method was tested against

1. **Data entitlement.** `massive_stock_day` is the only wide US panel: 20,677 names, 2021-07→2026-07, daily OHLCV+`transactions`, **unadjusted**; per-trade tape and NBBO both 403 (`collectors/massive_flatfiles.py`). FINRA ATS has a ~2.4y local hole; deep short-interest history is in-flight (#4627), today 2 weeks deep. EDGAR fundamentals cover ~1,550 of 20,677 names (~7.5%) — the measured Quant Lab blocker.
2. **Compute.** Nightly render budget ~67 min, 4-core (HOUSE-U6: heavy compute off the render path, artifacts to R2). ML precedent is sklearn-only, ops-lane-only (`scripts/ops_train_flow_score.py` — "NEVER wired into daily.yml"). No torch/lightgbm anywhere, by design.
3. **Fusion law.** Fused composite scores in any scored path/ranking/sizing/escalation are FORBIDDEN (DNR row 45, MSP-R2, Signal-Commons positioning-fusion ILLEGAL; sole carve-out = PSI §3.1.2 display-tier equal-weight composite, operator-overridden 2026-08-03). The proposal's "final decision score" (R_online × S_causal × (E[r]−cost) − λ…) is exactly this forbidden shape.
4. **Origination law.** A7: LLMs never originate signals/scores/escalations (`constitution.py` refuses A7 unconditionally; `signal_foundry_blocklist.yml` BL-001 regex-enforces it at intake).
5. **Sizing law.** NWP-U18: no portfolio construction/sizing in this repo — no optimizer, no target weights, no hedge suggestions. Sizing belongs to the operator desk in the Mastermind repo (two-organisms law, NWC-U4).
6. **Adaptation law.** Online weight mutation is rejected doctrine: "at n = hundreds of episodes and dozens of signals, continuous weight updates are overfitting with a heartbeat" (`research/CENTRAL_BRAIN_BRAINSTORM_BY_FABLE.md`); PSI §11.4 "thresholds/weights NEVER mutate online." Lawful adaptation = quarterly, versioned, pre-registered reweighting + per-leg GO/NEUTRAL validity gates (`data/regime/anticipation_gate.json` pattern).
7. **Environment estimability.** The open lane #4621 measured that 5 of 6 regime axes cannot support environment-conditioned claims today (single-state or one-month coverage); the one estimable axis (`regime_at_entry`, 3-state, 64y) shows a **null** regime×family interaction (main effect 3.8× larger). Its frozen entry gates (MIN_COVERAGE 0.20, MIN_STATES 2, MIN_MONTHS_STATE 12) are the standing test any environment-partitioned method must pass first. (#4621 is unmerged — cited as an in-flight measurement, not re-litigated here.)

### Detail where the verdict has teeth

**#1 world model.** What the proposal actually requires first — "immutable feature snapshots, event timestamps, universe membership, model lineage" — is the part we already built: `data/us_prophet_rank/candidates/` (150 cols × full universe, monthly parts, stamp_date family key) and the CN 88-col sibling; S&P 1500 PIT membership to 2012; insider PIT panel 2006→. What it requires second (a learned multimodal encoder) fails three walls at once: sklearn-only/no-GPU compute posture, 7.5% fundamentals coverage on the wide panel, and Oracle Tier-C doctrine ("with n in the hundreds, a deep net is overfitting bait"). The buildable sliver is **historical-analog similarity on the transparent context vectors** (nearest-neighbor in named-feature space, display-tier, "resembles 27 prior episodes" with the episodes listed) — chartered wave-2c below. No embeddings required; embeddings are re-openable only with a concrete N argument per the meta-labeling precedent (`engine/meta_label.py` — the house template for admitting any ML technique: N-sufficiency, purged CV, default-off).

**#2 graph transformer.** The census found the honest gap: today's confluence graph is 98.6% static `feeds` edges; per-edge weights/timestamps/confidence don't exist; and **no ledger anywhere grades "edge fired → target moved X with lag L"** — which is precisely the training signal any learned propagation model would need, and also precisely the evidence CHF-R15 demands before the 2027 reconsideration. Wave-2b charters that ledger (deterministic, display-tier, single-writer JSONL folded nightly, AUTHORITY_BLOCK stamped). Event-conditioned *rule* activation extensions route through the existing `mechanism_pathways.py` idiom. Anything matching `_CHF_DENY_TOKENS` stays dead until the clock and preconditions say otherwise.

**#3 distributions.** Three sub-verdicts. (a) **Asset-level cone upgrade** (GARCH-skewed-t, Kupiec/Christoffersen coverage gates): already specced as `research/HEDGEYE_UPGRADE_MASTER_PLAN.md` §C.4 (P1, unbuilt) — that plan keeps ownership; do not re-charter. (b) **Cross-sectional pick-conditional cones: wave-1, built in this PR** — empirical analog-cohort quantiles (zero fitted weights, `forward_dist` kernel), five pre-registered conditioning schemes (trend / extension / vol / coil / trend×vol), H=21 primary verdict horizon, gates = calibration ∈ [0.72, 0.88] on the 80% band + pinball skill vs pooled-unconditional AND vs own-name-marginal baselines + cell-floor honesty. The Calibration Lab already grades cone coverage for cycles (0.80 nominal + recalibration multiplier) — per-pick cones join a measurement idiom that exists, and the surface language is already law ("windows, not certainties"). (c) **Joint conditional diffusion: rejected for now** — the proposal itself says quantile-before-diffusion; the single supporting paper is one-market/unreplicated; scenario generation has no lawful consumer here (walls 5, 6) and the validation use is barred by the synthetic-data consensus.

**#5 counterfactual events (wave-2a charter).** Build `engine/synthetic_control.py` as a *comparison-estimator* upgrade to the existing event-study harness (`scripts/backtest_event_priors.py`), not a new program: donor-pool weighted counterfactual per treated name (match on pre-event path/vol/beta/sector/size/liquidity), against the incumbent benchmark-adjusted CAR. **Positive control is pre-registered:** the S&P pure-add announce effect (+1.6% gross, t=4.6, already graded in `scripts/validate_index_reconstitution.py`) must remain visible under SC; **falsifier:** on the ClinicalTrials Phase-3 null family, SC must not manufacture an effect the random-date placebo kills. Event families with honest PIT stamps only (index adds; insider clusters 2006→; dilution filing-dates; earnings once #4624's Item-2.02 acceptance timestamps land). Heterogeneous-effects (causal forests / DML) are a later rung and route through CHF's instrument discipline; DML's foundations are the one part of this intake with Nobel-grade pedigree (w23564), but moderator-coverage is the binding constraint, not method quality.

**#6 decision layer.** NWP-U18 is dispositive for optimizers/weights/sizing. What survives: a **display-tier categorical overlap read on board picks** — "these 3 live picks share the same basket/factor cluster driver" — computed from the existing 9-factor exposure store (`engine/factor_exposure.py`, ~1,529 names) + basket taxonomy, folded by agreement (never a weighted sum), caution/de-escalation semantics only (RO-3). That is the lawful core of the proposal's "portfolio intelligence" scene, minus the sizing. Chartered wave-2d; PSI keeps ownership of everything touching the user's own book.

**#8 SDF.** If/when wanted, it enters as a **Quant Lab recreation** (the #4619 lab is the designated venue for external-model recreations with comparable rank-IC/HAC/FDR grading) — after fundamentals W2-A widens coverage, and with the in-sample-vs-test Sharpe distinction quoted honestly. Until then, the "is this alpha or risk-compensation" decomposition continues via incremental IC neutralized against owned style factors.

## §4 Wave plan

**Wave 1 (this PR, diagnostic tier):** this adjudication + pick-conditional forward-distribution Phase-0 (module, study on the real panel, tests, committed results). Ship gate: §0.

*Wave-1 result (2026-08-06, `research/PICK_FORWARD_DIST_PHASE0.md`):* on 6,617 names / 3.31M name-days / 856 OOS dates (2023-01→2026-06, quarterly refits), **S3 vol-state and S5 trend×vol pass all four gates (GO)** — calibrated 80%-band coverage (~0.79) *and* pinball skill vs both baselines (S3: +1.09% vs pooled, CI [+0.5%, +1.7%] of baseline units at H=21, 73% of dates positive; +1.60% vs own-name marginal). S1 trend / S2 extension / S4 coil are CALIBRATED_ONLY — honest width, no measured edge. The pattern replicates the house's asset-level finding cross-sectionally: **vol-state conditioning (width) carries the distributional skill; direction-flavored conditioning adds none.** Five pre-compute amendments declared (AM-1..5); single-era caveat printed; a scratchpad negative control (state-independent noise partition) scored exactly zero, so the harness manufactures no edge from partitioning alone; two independent runs were byte-identical. **Open question that gates the next rung:** outcomes are standardized by trailing 20d vol — a biased forward-vol forecast — so part of S3/S5's gain may be correction of known vol mean-reversion rather than state information. The discriminating experiment is a re-run with the shipped HAR forecaster (`engine/vol_forecast.py`) as the standardizer: edge survives → the conditioning carries information; edge collapses → the finding is "use a better vol forecaster," which is itself shippable width intelligence. Effect sizes are statistically clean but economically small (~1% of baseline pinball) — both readings ship honest width, and that discriminator runs before any emitter PR. Next rung (separate PR, operator ratification): HAR-standardizer discriminator, then a per-pick cone emitter conditioned on vol state, Calibration-Lab coverage grading, "windows, not certainties" surface copy.
