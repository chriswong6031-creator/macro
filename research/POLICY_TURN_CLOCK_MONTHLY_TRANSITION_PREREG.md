## Purpose and boundary

R0 is an outcome-blind feasibility audit. It records whether canonical source owners can lawfully supply point-in-time metadata for the ten frozen monthly-transition hypotheses. It does not read, join, derive, print, or retain any outcome, price-path, return, volatility, drawdown, directional, fit, statistical-significance, probability, score, rank, gate, size, or trade value.

The sole unit is a **standard-monthly OPEX cycle episode**, identified as `YYYY-MM`. Daily rows, roots, contracts, and revisions are source observations; none is an additional independent episode. Quarterly roll and catalyst facts are attributes of that same episode. The audit reports counts and availability only.

The R0 implementation may create only the five paths named in the controlling ruling. Raw, licensed, large, or credential-bearing source rows remain outside Git. It may neither modify nor consume W1, a product, engine, collector, workflow, CI manifest, data store, Prophet, portfolio, scheduler, lifecycle, or production path.

## Time, identity, correction, and missingness law

For each owner, the audit distinguishes `observed_at` (underlying occurrence), `available_at` (an explicit timezone-aware decision clock), correction identity/time, canonical root/entity identity, rights class, and availability state. File mtime, a generated timestamp, and a current snapshot are invalid `available_at` substitutes. Corrections remain linked, rather than silently deduplicated. Missing, stale, unknown, or unavailable are visible states, not zero, quiet, absent support, or evidence against a hypothesis.

A present-day OI, VX curve, broad-flow, event label, or current artifact cannot be retrofitted as history. Historical and forward-accrual cohorts stay distinct in every output.

## Eras, headline rule, and feasibility floors

The audit reports the complete lawful intersection and, without pooling, these eras:

| Era | Dates | Minimum independent episodes for a historical headline cell |
|---|---|---:|
| `pre_weeklies` | through 2004 | 24 |
| `weeklies` | 2005-2016 | 24 |
| `modern_pre_0DTE` | 2017-2022 | 24 |
| `0DTE_current` | 2023 onward | 24 |

Each H1-H9 has one predeclared headline cell: its specified mechanism plus its required canonical intersection, measured at the cycle level. H10's headline is the overlap of all required component mechanisms and has a floor of **48** independent cycles. A cell below its floor is `BELOW_FLOOR`, not a null efficacy result. A historically unavailable required owner makes that headline `UNAVAILABLE`; it may not borrow count from a broader calendar or source.

Any later outcome study uses episode-block resampling, non-pooled eras, split-half stability, the frozen BH-FDR headline family (`q <= 0.10`), and the listed placebos. Later estimators are registered here only as future intent; R0 cannot execute them.

## Later outcome contract (registered but held)

If and only if a separately authorized later wave opens, its outcomes may include predeclared path/volatility objects, event-window state transitions, and decision-usefulness receipts. It must keep directional return secondary, use the frozen nested H10 baselines, and test the following placebos: same weekday one/two weeks earlier; adjacent non-OPEX week; months without measured inventory; long-gamma high-charm non-OPEX observations; weekday-preserving shifted month boundaries; quarterly dates without observed migration; and synthetic VX rank rolls with no same-contract change.

No R0 output can promote a mechanism. Any later descriptive survivor, shadow-hazard candidate, or capital effect requires its own authorization and the original W1/prospective gates.

## Explicit refusals

R0 refuses to infer dealer inventory or a missing canonical field; backfill broad ETF flow, Rebalance Pulse, M1-M6 curve, W1 receipt, or migration evidence; pool eras or inflate cycles from daily/root/contract records; use a longest source history in place of an exact required intersection; copy proprietary ThetaData or raw source records into Git; or turn a coverage count, availability classification, or sample-floor result into an outcome, efficacy, product, or authority claim.
