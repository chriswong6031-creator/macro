## 1. The keystone — no outcome-feedback loop, so these desks can never learn what matters

The single deepest US problem is the masterplan keystone (§1): **the desks are architecturally incapable of learning "what matters" from results.** Every symptom below cascades from that one absence, and the US cross-desk pattern is unambiguous — nearly every desk ships a falsifiable-ledger *skeleton* whose `scored_total` is `0`.

Trace the intended pathway and watch it break at the same accountability point on every desk:

1. **The raw material is headline/artifact-only.** No US desk holds the underlying fact (masterplan §2). The news core has no article body anywhere; Special Situations' arb terms are a single free-LLM extraction; Alt-Data's affiliation graph is a 13-day-stale hand seed; Policy Watch reasons over one manually-curated JSON. Every "importance"/"conviction" score operates on ~10-15 words or a bag of hand-authored channels.

2. **"Importance"/"conviction" is asserted, never measured.** The core "signal vs noise" decision is a static additive scorer in every desk — Alt-Data's `CHANNEL_WEIGHTS` sum, Congress's `0.40·cluster + 0.35·tech + 0.25·zone`, Policy's ±5% falsifier, White House's `min_importance:60`. All literals are hand-authored 2024 opinions frozen in code.

3. **The desks that DO grade are graded on their PICKS, never on their importance ranking.** Alt-Data theses and Policy leans get a `check_by` and a proxy-vs-SPY falsifier — but they grade *which name was picked*, never *whether the importance/relevance judgment was right* (masterplan §1.3). So the system can never learn that (say) a distinct-member congress cluster predicts better than a top-ranked Alt-Data convergence count.

4. **The scoreboard is empty by construction.** Alt-Data's 132 theses cannot mature before 2026-09-18; Policy's `scored.jsonl` holds exactly 2 rows, both `outcome:"unscored"` (dated 2026-06-18); White House has produced zero activated alerts in all git history; Congress has no grader at all. Every "calibrated by the ledger" claim on a US page is a promise the system cannot yet keep.

5. **There is no leg-level attribution even in principle.** Even where a number matures (Smart-Money's insider `bps` inside the Top-Pick composite), the composite is graded — never the insider leg's marginal contribution over value/quality/momentum/residual-alpha. You could never learn whether the qualitative leg ADDS edge.

The result is definitive for the US suite: **these desks can DECIDE what to show and can LOG a promise to grade it, but not one has ever closed the loop from a qualitative call to a graded outcome.** The user's whole thesis — filter noise, use what's useful, translate qual→quant — is foreclosed at the foundation.

→ **Light solution directions for Fable:** make outcome-labeling a first-class stage, not a per-desk afterthought (masterplan §9 stage 7); grade *relevance judgments* (did high-importance events actually move?) alongside picks; where a ledger is cold-start (all of them), decide per desk whether a point-in-time historical backfill can produce `n>0` NOW versus waiting to September.

## 2. Alt-Data / Signal Intelligence — a dark brain behind an "AI conviction" header, leaking into three scored engines

**Severity: CRITICAL.**

The desk is a ~21-feed Quiver/Finnhub/Polygon aggregator that inverts each free/retail feed into per-ticker "channels," sums hand-authored `CHANNEL_WEIGHTS` into a convergence score, and emits a 0-100 "actionable" `signal_score` plus an Opus "brain." - **The Opus brain — the desk's entire named capability — ships dark while emit still claims conviction (AD-1, CONFIRMED).** `site/altdata/brain.json:8-10` `"regime_read": null … "theses": []`, `:72` `"degraded_reason": "llm_error"`, `:2-3` `"is_context_only": false, "actionable": true`; every one of 30 `mastermind.json` signals is `source:"deterministic"`, `action:"WATCH"`, `brain_present:false`. - **The uncalibrated `signal_score` reaches THREE scored engines, not two (AD-2 CONFIRMED + verifier missed-problem).** `altdata.py:18-20` says "Nothing here writes into a scored axis, allocation, or regime," yet `radar_ticker.py:133` `act = (float(score) - 50.0) / 25.0` (feeds `edge_score`), `radar_plus.py:89` `lean = 1 if avg >= 60` (scales divergence edge to 1.0×), and — the consumer the first audit undercounted — `intelligence.py:99-106` `if a_score >= 65 and action != AVOID: alt_dir = 1 … elif a_score < 35: alt_dir = -1`, which mints the shipped `early_edge`/`confirmed`/`fading` cross-facet labels. All while `calibration.n_scored=0`.
- **The #1 name is a maximally-extended chase because the guard lives only in the dark brain (AD-3, CONFIRMED).** `mastermind.json` INTC ships `score 94, rs_vs_spy_60d=137.0, weighted 1.85, extended:false`. `altdata_emit.py:50` adds a capped `+10` for rs (137/3 clamped), while the `_EXTENDED_PP=35` clamp lives only in `altdata_brain.py:51` — and `altdata_emit.py:85` sets `extended=bool(th.get('extended'))` from the brain thesis, so with the brain dark `extended` is ALWAYS false. - **The deterministic fallback is monotone-bullish (verifier-confirmed missed problem, MEDIUM).** `_signal_score` docks −30 for `action=AVOID` and −15 for `extended`, but both depend on brain-supplied fields; with the brain dark, all 30 actions are `WATCH` and `extended` is false, so **both downside guards are structurally dead** — every name can only be scored up (weighted + conviction + rs + n_actors), never down.

NVDA `convergence_score=8` mixes `retail_buzz/cnbc_pick/github_momentum/hf_model_momentum` as "independent" channels.
- **The affiliation graph is a 13-day-stale hand seed, double-counted (AD-6 CONFIRMED + verifier missed problem, MEDIUM).** `influence.json` is `as_of='2026-06-19'`, `n_candidates:0` — the gated LLM extractor added zero edges; 31 of 57 watch actor-edges are `qualitative:true`. The affiliation channel enters convergence at a flat `0.50` (`altdata_models.py:97`) with no provenance discount, AND the SAME actor ties are counted again in `_signal_score` via `altdata_emit.py:53` `base += min(10.0, n_actors*2.0)` — NVDA's 6 actors earn +10 on top of the channel credit, biasing the ranking toward marquee names.

- **Contract copy over-sells (AD-7, PARTIAL — verifier correction).** `altdata_emit.py:118-124` describes the axis as "weighted convergence + brain conviction … Brain calls are graded vs SPY," but the brain is dark and nothing is graded. ## 9. How the US desks plug into the ONE centralized engine

**Severity: framing — this is the net-new build.**

Today's six desks are forked silos (masterplan §4): `event_id`/`norm_title`/`source_tier` exist in `news_common.py` but `build_entity_map` has exactly one downstream consumer (`financial_news`), while `smart_money`, `special_situations`, `whitehouse`, and `congress` each roll their own ticker extraction — so "NVDA appeared in a 13F AND a policy tailwind AND three wires today" is structurally invisible. Each desk writes its own ledger (`data/ai_desk`, `data/altdata`, `data/policy_intent`, `data/radar`, `data/master_brain`, `data/thematic_desk`, `data/narrative_brain`) and none join. The fix is the masterplan's hub-and-spoke engine (§9), NOT a monolith (which would couple failure domains and fight the already-spoke-shaped architecture). The US desks map onto its seven stages as follows:

1. **INGEST** — thin spokes adapt each US source into a common raw ITEM `{source, source_tier, url, lang, seendate, raw_text, vintage}`: Alt-Data's Quiver/Finnhub feeds, Special Situations' EDGAR daily-index, Congress's Quiver Trader parquet, Smart-Money's 13F/Form-4 ZIPs, White House's WordPress RSS, Policy Watch's curated `intel.json`. Each stays independently failable (preserving today's `_safe`/fail-open discipline — but with "broken" distinguished from "quiet").
2. **NORMALIZE** — one text pipeline folds today's per-desk `is_low_value`/`clickbait_penalty` and Special Situations' `_NOISE_RE`/SPAC regex into a single pass.
3. **ENTITY/EVENT RESOLUTION** — ONE resolver promoted from `build_entity_map`, extended to people/agencies/policies so a White House EO and a Policy lean about the same action collapse into one `event_key`, and a 13F holding + a congress cluster + an Alt-Data affiliation on one ticker become a group-by. **This is the longest pole** — the current `$TICKER` regex + stopword list per desk won't cut it.
4. **DEDUP + NOVELTY** — a global `event_id` dedup across all six desks (today the same Reuters wire is counted in `macro_news` and again in `financial_news`), then a novelty score — the cheapest highest-value missing feature.
5. **IMPORTANCE/RELEVANCE SCORING** — the single importance model replaces the six hand-formulas (Alt-Data `CHANNEL_WEIGHTS`, Congress `0.40/0.35/0.25`, Policy ±5%, White House `min_importance:60`) with `source_tier`, novelty, entity-centrality, corroboration-count as FEATURES. Cold-start = today's constants; **learning is DEFERRED / shadow-only** until label volume clears a Wilson-CI gate — the near-term win is unification and a common yardstick, not learning.
6. **PER-ASSET + MACRO EMIT** — one contract emits per-entity signal (generalizing `altdata_emit`'s `signal_score` from the shared store) AND a macro/regime aggregate ("many industrials lit by congress + policy + 13F today" → sector pressure), which nothing produces today. Corroboration raises conviction automatically because signals are the cross-desk JOIN on an entity.
7. **ACCOUNTABILITY / FORWARD-GRADING** — ONE outcomes ledger keyed `(entity, date, desk, signal_id)`, generalizing the `ai_desk_scorer` + `scored.jsonl` proxy-vs-SPY + `check_by` chassis already reused by `altdata_ledger`, `policy_intent_desk`, and `foresight_grader` — unifying the 7+ scattered US dirs into one table. This is the stage every US desk is missing today.

**The keystone feedback loop for the US desks** turns frozen importance into learned importance by grading RELEVANCE, not just picks: snapshot PIT entry levels at detection, derive falsifier + `check_by` via `ai_desk._derive_check`, grade the resolved entity's forward return vs benchmark on window elapse — and crucially log a label even for high-ranked events that moved NOTHING (false-positive importance, e.g. an Alt-Data INTC=94 that goes nowhere) and sample LOW-ranked events (misses), or the model only sees its own confirmations. Then hit-rate per feature-slice answers the question no US desk can answer today: does a distinct-member congress cluster beat a top Alt-Data convergence count? Does high novelty beat echo?

The three near-term "stop the bleeding" fixes that are pure US-desk hygiene (masterplan §10): the leaking firewalls (Alt-Data/Policy un-graded outputs re-ranking traded pages — hard `n>0` gate or confidence down-weight, the value is already discarded at the boundary); the dark-brain contract copy that over-sells ("AI conviction, graded vs SPY" while the brain is dark); the polluted arb book sorting impossible returns to the top; and the monotone-bullish fallbacks + fail-open wrappers (`altdata.py:973`, `build_congress.py:434`) that make "broken" indistinguishable from "quiet."

## The Fable payload — the hard open questions

These are the genuinely unresolved US-specific tensions; the findings above exist to ground them. They feed the shared masterplan payload (§payload Q1-Q8).

1. **Should an ungraded desk's output move ANY ranking before its ledger matures (n>0)?** Alt-Data (`signal_score` → three engines) and Policy Watch (lean → `intel_hub` `gap_mult` + flags + stage) both silently give zero-skill outputs real weight on pages the user trades. Should this be a code-enforced precondition across the suite, and how do you represent "promise-to-grade" vs "actually-graded" so the UI never launders one as the other?

2. **Can these desks be graded NOW instead of waiting months?** Every US ledger is cold-start (Alt-Data earliest `check_by` 2026-09-18; Policy 2 unscored rows; White House never fired; Congress no grader). Is a point-in-time historical backfill feasible — Alt-Data convergence detections from parquet vintages, Policy predicates over history, White House events pooled by action-type (tariff vs sanction vs EO given sparsity) — or is the feed history too short / survivorship-prone to reconstruct clean entry levels? What is the minimal viable outcome-labeled dataset at solo-shop label velocity?

3. **Where free-tier data imposes a hard ceiling, is the capability achievable at all, or should it be relabeled?** The Special Situations arb book needs institutional deal-terms (free-LLM extraction admits floating-value stock deals as fixed-price — even a tight plausibility band cannot rescue a stock deal treated as fixed-price); the insider size-normalization needs mcap coverage beyond `factors.json`; White House / Policy need automated primary-doc ingest, not one hand-curated JSON. Decide per desk: buy the feed, or honestly demote to a non-scored event-calendar / reading-room. And for diffuse events (a defense-budget policy thesis with no clean single-ticker proxy), does forcing everything through proxy-vs-SPY quietly discard exactly the events with the most institutional value?

4. **What licenses moving a US leg from display to a bounded scored weight?** The one place a US hand-formula was actually falsified (the news uncertainty index, `gate_status:"pinned_off"`) is the template: it failed on forward vol incremental over VIX and was correctly demoted. What is the minimum protocol (regime not level, incremental over price+VIX, block-bootstrap, Wilson-CI gate) that would license a bounded scored weight for, say, an insider leg or a policy lean — and note the destination Top-Pick composite the insider leg feeds is ITSELF DSR-failing and cites a phantom report (`top_picks.py:9,40`), so this must be settled at the composite level, not per leg.

5. **Is there ANY defensible "narrative signal" here, and does a learned importance model even beat a well-calibrated frozen prior at US solo-shop label velocity?** Or is the correct near-term ceiling unification + a common yardstick, with learning deferred? What does a controlled, forward-graded experiment that lets ONE US qualitative feature (a congress cluster, an insider cluster, a policy phrase) bind a small capped weight look like, so the qual→quant claim is finally tested rather than perpetually deferred?

6. **The MNPI/mosaic boundary as US corroboration deepens.** As the entity resolver joins 13F + Form-4 + congress + policy + transcripts on one entity, where is the line between "aggregating public info into novel signal" (fine) and assembling a mosaic that behaves like MNPI — and does centralizing everything into one entity-resolved store *raise* that risk versus keeping desks siloed? The expert-network exclusion is already a documented compliance no-go; does the US corroboration engine need the same explicit boundary written into the methodology layer, not left as an oversight?
