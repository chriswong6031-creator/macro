### U4 [major] §1.1 / §5.1 (time-to-cushion) — Time-to-cushion is statistically ill-defined (censoring/conditioning unspecified), mislabeled (reaching +5% ≠ breakeven stop survives), and collinear with stop-out and the clean8 variant
*Lens: stats-overfit*

The definition 'days until the position is ≥ +5%' has no stated treatment of fires that never reach +5% in the grading window. If reported as median-of-reachers it is a selection-biased conditional statistic: a species with a lower reach rate excludes exactly its worst fires, so cross-species cushion comparisons are incoherent (a species can 'improve' cushion speed by stopping out its slow fires faster). It is also mechanically entangled with stop-out on the SAME ±5% barriers (stop-out = −5% before +5%; cushion is defined on the complement), and the rotational gate stacks it beside '+8% before −5%' clean-liftoff — three gate axes that are substantially one correlated barrier event, so a species can look 'multi-axis strong' from a single phenomenon. The parenthetical 'a breakeven stop survives' overclaims: hitting +5% does not imply price never returns through entry afterward — the metric as named asserts a safety property it does not measure. Finally, on close-only data (grading.forward_metrics uses closes; full-universe high/low history is a 30-day window outside ~224 deep names, per the plan's own §2) barrier ordering on gap bars is unresolvable, and this convention is unstated.

**Evidence:** Masterplan line 87 (definition, 'new — exists nowhere' — confirmed absent from repo), lines 93-95 (rotational gate stacks cushion + clean8 + dead-money); engine/grading.py:117-173 (close-based forward windows, no barrier-race machinery); masterplan §2 data-plane paragraph (high/low history gap)

**Fix applied:** In §1.1 redefine time-to-cushion as a cumulative-incidence statistic with stop-out as a competing risk: report "% of ALL fires cushioned by day k" at k ∈ {5,10,21} (matching the horizon set), and ban the wave1.py:1050 dropna-median pattern by name in §1.2 — never a median over reachers; if a central tendency is wanted, report it only alongside the reach rate and n. Replace the three stacked rotational axes with the mutually-exclusive per-fire terminal-state partition {stopped / dead-money / cushioned-no-liftoff / clean-liftoff}, designating that partition (or stop-out alone) as the primary rotational gate axis and demoting the overlapping rates to context. Delete the "(a breakeven stop survives)" parenthetical or operationalize it as a separate tracked rate (post-cushion breakeven-breach within the horizon). In §1.2, state once that all barrier races inherit wave1.py's close-basis sequential convention, and pre-register the high/low tie rule (conservative: stop wins on a straddle bar) now, since it becomes binding the day Polygon high/low history (plan lines 236-237) is wired into the spine.

### U5 [major] §3.4 / §5.1 / §5.4 (regime trust) — Learned regime-trust cells have no numeric honest-n floor, and the cell combinatorics vastly exceed the number of distinct regime episodes available
*Lens: stats-overfit*

The plan aggregates 8 regime axes and rolls every ledger into per-(species × archetype × regime-cell × horizon-class) scoreboards, then 'learns' trust multipliers 'when cells reach honest n (episode-clustered)'. No minimum episode count is ever stated — the exact discretionary gate the constitution elsewhere forbids. The binding constraint is structural: regimes persist for months, so per-cell effective n is the number of distinct regime EPISODES, not fires; even a 14-year panel yields maybe 10–30 episodes per axis, and stamping only starts 2026-07 ('stamped on every NEW ledger row from day one'), so live-accrued cells at the W4 'regime-trust learned-cell review #1' will hold single-digit episode counts. With dozens of cells per species and a scoreboard that surfaces the best cells, max-cell selection guarantees spurious 'trusted regimes' (e.g., 3-of-4-episodes cells reported as 75% edges). The vol_regime additive-value-gate pattern is cited but it does not substitute for a minimum-episode floor or a null calibration.

**Evidence:** Masterplan lines 321-347 (§3.4, 8 axes, day-one stamping, 'NO ledger stamps regime today'), 483-484 (§5.1 species-cell rollup), 528-534 (§5.4, 'learned multipliers only when cells reach honest n (episode-clustered, not row counts)' — no number), 597-598 (W4 review timing); episode-clustering precedent: WAVE2_REPORT.md §1 (180-day purge after fold boundaries)

**Fix applied:** Amend §5.4 with pre-committed numeric criteria: (1) define "honest n" = distinct regime EPISODES (contiguous same-cell runs, 180d purge-separated per the WAVE2 precedent); a trust multiplier is estimable only for cells with ≥20 episodes AND ≥5 episodes in each time-half (two-halves sign stability is meaningless below that).
