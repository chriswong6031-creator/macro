## 1. The telecom template (1996–2002) — the prior cycle of record

The canonical credit-funded infrastructure overbuild. US IG telecom capex $43.9B (1996)
→ $89.9B (2001); >$500B of US telecom bonds issued 1996–2001; FCC's July-2002 estimate:
$1T of global industry debt "much of which will never be repaid" [v]. Default rates hit
9.8% (2001) then a record 12.8% (2002); telecoms were ~52% of all defaulted dollars both
years; telecom bond recoveries averaged ~16¢ [v].

**The mechanism map, telecom → AI analog:**

| # | Telecom mechanism (evidence) | AI-cycle analog | Earliest observable |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Vendor financing as hidden credit subsidy** — 9 equipment vendors extended $25.6B to customers by end-2000, booked as revenue at inception; Lucent $8.1B commitments (~24% of FY2000 revenue), $3.5B bad-debt provisions 2001-02 [v] | NVDA's $40B+ 2026 equity deployment into GPU customers (OpenAI $30B, CRWV ~$4.4B total, Nebius $2B, xAI $2B, IREN/Corning warrants) + $6.3B CRWV capacity-purchase backstop through 2032 [v] — equity into customers who buy your chips | NVDA investment-portfolio write-downs; receivables/DSO growth vs revenue; SEC scrutiny of vendor-finance accounting |
| T2 | **Cohort sequencing: HY upstarts first** — CLEC HY spreads widened ~300bp in early 2000 BEFORE the Mar-2000 equity peak; then mid-tier carriers; equipment vendors (Lucent A2→near-junk in ~2 months, Dec-2000); IG fallen angels last (WorldCom/Qwest May-2002) [v] | Neocloud HY (CRWV notes) → AI-adjacent HY → equipment/hyperscaler IG — the S1/S3 hypothesis; the CCW theme ladder is *ordered to observe* this sequence | CRWV note spreads / neocloud theme g-spread vs HY index (CCW theme differential) |
| T3 | **Market-access cliff** — debt markets went from open to shut for telecom in ~4 months (Jan→Apr 2001); refinancing-dependent issuers died regardless of asset quality [v] | Neoclouds with negative FCF and current maturities (CRWV: $7.5B current debt vs −$4.7B/qtr FCF, Q1-2026 [v]) | Failed/pulled taps; new-deal cover ratios; the CCW maturity-wall view |
| T4 | **Round-trip revenue** — Global Crossing booked 32% of Q2-2001 "cash revenue" from capacity swaps (EBITDA +$472M would have been −$43M without); Qwest $3.8B fraudulent swap revenue [v] | Round-trip compute agreements; hyperscaler "AI revenue" partly funded by their own investments in the buyers | Counterparty cross-referencing of simultaneous revenue+capex growth between the same two names |
| T5 | **Demand forecast tethered to a myth** — "traffic doubles every 90 days" (actual: ~annual); DWDM multiplied existing-fiber capacity 10x/yr; only 2.7–5% of fiber lit by 2002 [v] | Inference-demand projections vs GPU efficiency gains (Blackwell ~90-100x Hopper inference efficiency [1]); H100 spot rentals already $8/hr → $2/hr [1] | Cohort GPU utilization; spot rental-rate trajectory; price-per-token trends |
| T6 | **Fallen-angel index distortion** — WorldCom ($29.3B bonds) instantly became the largest HY issuer at downgrade, forcing IG-mandate selling and technical widening unrelated to other issuers' fundamentals [v] | ORCL ($108B debt) at BBB−/negative is the analog watch; a cut makes it among the largest HY issuers ever | The CCW fallen-angel watch (membership transitions) + the fallen-angel-pipeline archetype (§4) |
| T7 | **Equipment-vendor contamination** — Lucent's IG credit was destroyed by CLEC receivables its bondholders hadn't mapped [v] | NVDA balance-sheet co-dependence on customer solvency (T1); DELL/SMCI server receivables | Vendor DSO/receivable disclosures; NVDA stake write-downs |

**What telecom credit investors caught early:** HY-vs-IG telecom divergence from late
1999; cash-burn vs maturity walls (computable from filings). **What they missed:** the
vendor-finance revenue inflation, swap circularity, the SPEED of market-access closure,
IG contamination via off-balance-sheet exposure, and that the demand forecast was
vendor-supplied and unchecked. The CCW miss-taxonomy inherits all five.

## 2. The hyperscaler issuance wave (2025–2026) — where we are now

The largest sustained IG supply event on record; H1-2026 AI-sector IG issuance $244B
(vs $136B full-year 2025); the five hyperscalers now rival the big-six banks as the
largest IG-index issuers; tech ~10% of the $7.6T Bloomberg IG index and the hyperscaler
subset doubled to ~4.1% in the year to Apr-2026 [v].

The cover-ratio slide 4x → 1.6x inside nine months is the cleanest observable of supply
saturation (the AMZN book internals are single-source — re-verify before any study
leans on the 1.6x numerically). Goldman's internal read: the IG absorption threshold has fallen from ~$75B
to ~$25B ("carnage" framing) [1]; Fidelity publicly avoiding hyperscaler paper [1].
IG *tech* BBB has underperformed IG industrials BBB by ~20bp since Nov-2025 [v] — the
sector-vs-index differential is already moving (see the §4 energy archetype).

**Free cash flow after capex (the fundamental clock; company guidance/actuals unless marked):**

2027; GOOGL 2026 capex $180-190B, FCF → ~zero 2027 [1]; MSFT the anchor — Aaa/AAA,
still FCF-positive ($15.8B Q3-FY2026, compressing).

**Oracle is the wave's leading indicator.** Baa2/negative (Moody's, OpenAI
counterparty-concentration named: $300B contract, 4.5GW), BBB−/negative (S&P since
07-09), 5y CDS 43bp (Jan-2025) → ~198bp record [v]. One more notch = the largest

**Phase-transition milestones (benign → credit-negative), each observable:** (1) ≥2 of

## 4. What widens first — sequencing empirics (the ruler-derivation base)

The pre-registered W6 studies (S1/S1b/S2/S3) take their constructions from this section.

- **The energy archetype (sector-vs-index) — the 2015-16 episode.** Energy HY decoupled
  from the broad index from H2-2013, accelerated H2-2014, peaked ~1,500bp vs the index's
  887bp (Feb-2016) — a 600-700bp differential and **12-24 months of sector-level warning**
  before the default wave (energy = 67% of 2016 HY defaults) [v/1]. This is the direct
  empirical license for CCW's theme-vs-index g-spread differentials.
- **Quality-ladder sequence.** CCC widens before BB before IG in fundamental cycles
  (2000-02: Caa +753bp during 2000 while IG lagged quarters behind; 2022 hiking cycle
  same order) [v]. **CCC−BB differential as cycle-phase meter**: long-run median ~632bp;
  compressions to 147bp (Jun-2007), 228bp (Jun-2014), 203bp (Dec-2021) marked risk-
  appetite extremes; decompression from the extreme is the trigger [1]. CCW computes
  this from the W1 ladder (accruing; ~3y visible history — S1b is backfill-gated).
- **The 2020 exception (liquidity inversion).** In the fastest widening on record (23
  business days), IG widened BEFORE CCC — forced selling for cash, not fundamentals;
  it retraced fast [v]. Law for readers: distinguish liquidity-vs-fundamental widening
  by retracement speed; never read an IG-first spike as the telecom-style sequence.
- **Dispersion is regime-dependent.** HY cross-sectional dispersion predicted widening
  in the dot-com era (+0.380, p<0.001 [1]) but mean-reverts in non-fragile regimes —
  supporting evidence for CCW-R11's "hypothesis, not claim" stance on S2.
- **Credit-leads-equity.** ABX led the S&P top by ~4 months in 2007; CDS leads cash
  bonds in price discovery (Blanco et al 2005), strongest for low-grade names [v].
  The CCW credit-vs-equity divergence quadrant reads this cross-asset lead per theme.
- **Fallen-angel mechanics (quantified).** ~245bp average deterioration in the 3 months
  BEFORE downgrade; index exclusion at downgrade-month-end (Bloomberg/ICE rule); fallen
  angels enter HY ~150bp cheap to BB peers and outperform broad HY by 200-400bp over
  12-18 months (15 of 22 years since 2004) [v/1]. 2020 wave: ~$197-240B (Ford $78B) [v].
  **The pipeline archetype:** the 2020 wave was visible in 2019 as $375B of
  BBB-negative-outlook paper [1] — the pipeline is trackable BEFORE the event. (An
  archetype label, not a chartered study — the program's studies remain S1/S1b/S2/S3.)
- **EBP is the strongest macro-credit predictor in the literature** (already computed in `engine/bonds.py`):
  +50bp ⇒ ~+15pp 12-month recession probability; the predictive content of credit
  spreads is entirely the risk-appetite residual, not default risk (Gilchrist-Zakrajšek
  2012) [v].
- **Do not read absolute spread levels.** HY OAS ~280bp (Sep-2025) is a 25-year 5th
  percentile, but index quality composition has shifted — tier-relative ratios and
  differentials only (this restates VSB's "calm is not reassurance" + CCW-R11) [v].
