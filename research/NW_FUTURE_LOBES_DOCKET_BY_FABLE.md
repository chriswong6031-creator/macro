## 1. What qualifies as a lobe (the bar), and the three-way taxonomy

A **lobe** owns: its own objective function, its own FDR family, its own falsifiers, and plugs into the synapse/spine/kernel rails. Scope border (NW masterplan §9): Neural Web owns rails/memory/governance/synthesis; lobes own their signals.

The red-team surfaced a taxonomy this house has been missing, and it directly explains the recurring "is this a lobe?" confusion:

- **LOBE** — own objective/FDR/falsifiers (Oracle, entry stack, long-hold).
- **RAIL** — serves every lobe, has no objective function of its own (spine, kernel, replay). Rails live INSIDE Neural Web core.
- **WAVE** — an increment to an existing lobe wearing a new name (event playbooks = a Signal Commons wave, not a lobe).

Misfiling waves as lobes is how program sprawl happens. Every future research doc gets sorted against this taxonomy first.

## 2. Core rail extensions (build before any new lobe; they multiply everything)

### R1 — Arbitrary-rule replay ("imagination") — THE top build
**Killer question:** "What would have happened under rule X?" — for any X — answered by computation instead of debate.
**What actually exists:** `scripts/replay_standout_pipeline.py` (2012→present production-gate replay, PIT, grades MAE/MFE at 5/10/21/63/126d) and `engine/provisional_replay.py` (repaint/flicker measurement). **Both replay only the production gate.** The genuinely missing capability is parametrizing the rule (exit/hold/sizing variant) as a free variable over the existing fire tape.
**Mandatory governor (house-law critical):** an unrestricted replay engine is a multiple-comparisons hazard — thousands of queries against the same tapes launder exploration into apparent edge. Every replay registers in a **rule-experiment registry** (declare the cut BEFORE running), counts against a trial budget, carries `fdr_family='replay'`, survivorship stamps, and pre-registered n-floors. Nulls printed. Replay outputs are display-only until independently gauntleted.
**Known n-reality:** e.g. "hold all compounder-labeled fires 12mo" runs on n=196 survivorship-caveated names — the registry must force that number to be stated up front.

**Synergy:** long-hold W2's thesis_clock gives exit a second evidence stream (entry-reason expiry vs hold-reason breakage) — the two programs must share the falsifier-tripwire substrate, not fork it.

### L3 — Correlation-Regime / Dispersion lens (small charter)
