## Re-admission (churn) pocket

Second episodes after a prior losing episode: **win 33% (n=15, median −5.7)**
vs 86% after a winning episode (n=50, +6.4). All 15 after-loss re-admissions
happened before the prior episode's H=10 verdict had resolved — the graded-loss
cooling-off trigger is HINDSIGHT here (US finding replicates in CN). Any
cooling-off prereg must use a price-relative trigger (re-admitted while below
prior episode fill), not the unresolved verdict.
