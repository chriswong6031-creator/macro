## §2 Hypotheses — frozen verbatim from the packet

Do not paraphrase these into a new taxonomy. Operationalizations below are
measurement rules, not restated claims.

### GD-H1 — duration × broken leadership

When leadership is already broken/dislocated, accelerating long yields increase
the hazard of a ≥3%/≥5% residual drawdown in high-duration equities over 1 and
3 sessions.

### GD-H2 — volatility acceleration conditional on fragility

Volatility/realized-variance acceleration adds information about near-term
transmission when leadership is already fragile, even if VIX level itself is
not historically extreme.

### GD-H3 — Treasury auction tail as conditional trigger receipt

Auction concession/tail has incremental information only when duration
fragility exists and long-yield/rate-vol confirmation follows. Standalone
“bad auction predicts equities” remains disallowed.

### GD-H4 — defensive rotation as transmission confirmation

High-beta breadth damage plus positive defensive residual strength is a
same-session confirmation of broadening transmission, not necessarily a
leading predictor.

### GD-H5 — China Prophet board-health confirmation

Cross-sectional collapse of the canonical China board adds incremental
real-time evidence of market-wide transmission beyond the index alone.

### GD-H6 — PBOC zero-operation sign is conditional

Zero 7-day reverse repo has no fixed sign. Adverse interpretation requires
net drain and funding/FX/equity transmission stress.

### GD-H7 — repair impulse versus durable repair

Issuer/policy support without cross-asset repair has elevated probability of
failed repair/retest versus a repair that broadens across trigger,
correlation, breadth and exposed-cohort state.

### GD-H8 — contagion sequence

Clock-ordered residual damage across regions adds descriptive and potentially
predictive information beyond one global-index return, but any lead claim
requires a mature PIT sample.

### 3.9 Repair (GD-H7)

Separate, in this order:

1. issuer-only (SK Hynix / named support name)
2. memory/semiconductor cohort
3. regional index (KOSPI, then Nikkei / TW / HSI / CSI300 as available)
4. US futures / next US cash session
5. rates / volatility
6. breadth / correlation

exposed cohort after the impulse, before confirmation.

A broad index bounce carried by one supported mega-cap with fragile
breadth is **issuer-only**, not confirmed repair. Report both.

Descriptive sequence is always reportable. A **lead** claim requires a
mature PIT sample (design-era event-level N declared in §7) and is

## §4 Primary outcomes — exact labels

Do not move these after the incident is inspected.

### 4.1 Broad market

For each of `{SPY, QQQ, IWM, RSP, EWY, EWJ, EWT, EWH, FXI}` and each
horizon `h ∈ {1,3,5,10,21}` sessions in that venue:

- `dd_h` = `min(P_{t+h}/P_t − 1, 0)` from the observation price
- `mae_h` = minimum close-to-close path return over `1..h`

Intraday MAE is used only if a genuine PIT intraday history exists;
otherwise the close path is labeled `mae_h_close` and intraday MAE is
`UNAVAILABLE`.

### 4.2 High-duration / high-beta residual

- `resid_dd_h` = `min(resid_i,h, 0)` vs the §3.3 benchmark
- `cohort_median_resid_h`
- `breadth_lt_m2` / `breadth_lt_m3` / `breadth_lt_m5` = share of the
  leadership-crack cohort with same-session residual ≤ −2/−3/−5%

Hazard labels for GD-H1 (binary, session-level):

- `y_resid_dd_ge3_1s` = 1 iff cohort-median residual drawdown ≤ −3% over 1 session
- `y_resid_dd_ge5_1s` = 1 iff ≤ −5% over 1 session
- `y_resid_dd_ge3_3s`, `y_resid_dd_ge5_3s` analogously

### 4.3 Crowded-winner liquidation

Prior leaders = names in the leadership-crack cohort that, at `t−21`, were
in the top tercile of 63d residual vs SPY (design-era definition, not
re-ranked on the incident).

- cohort median loss over each `h`
- downside correlation = average pairwise correlation of negative residuals
  over the 10 sessions ending `t+h`
- fraction of prior leaders with residual ≤ −5% over `h`
- failed-rebound frequency = share that are below their `t` price at `t+5`
  after a same-session bounce ≥ +1%

### 4.4 China board damage

The six metrics in §3.7, EOD and (if retained) lawful intraday snapshots.

### 4.5 Contagion

- `n_regions_confirm` by the next lawful clock of each region
- `dt_first`, `dt_second`, `dt_third` = time from origin observation to
  the 1st/2nd/3rd region confirmation

### 4.6 Repair

- `retest_new_low` after impulse (bool + timestamp)
- `breadth_resid_stabilized` (bool)
- `trigger_decelerated` (bool)

## §7 Missing-data, clocks, and effective-N

### 7.1 Clock schema

Every load-bearing row carries the packet §11 columns:

`source_id, source_family, market, field_or_artifact, source_owner,
source_url_or_repo_path, event_time, measurement_end, available_at,
observed_at_mastermind, produced_at_mastermind, effective_session,
settled_at, revision_basis, rights_basis, historical_coverage_start,
intraday_available, quality_state, notes`

A source with unknown `available_at` **may not** support an anticipatory
claim. It may appear as a descriptive coincidence.

TDS coalesce: `known_at := coalesce(published_at, ingested_at)`;
`INTELLIGENCE` coalesces to `served_at`. `effective_at` is never a
knowability clock.

### 7.2 Missing-data behavior

- Missing required clock → row `quality_state = CLOCK_UNKNOWN`; the claim
  that needed it is `BLOCKED`, not guessed.
- Missing vintage for a revisable series → that series is dropped from the
  scored frame (fail closed). It is not filled from latest-revised.
- Missing organ artifact at `t` → replay from truncated inputs at the SHA
  in force; if definition/input vintage is unavailable and no lawful
  substitute exists → that organ is `BLOCKED` for that `t`.
- Missing rights → `RIGHTS_BLOCKED` and a named minimum lawful substitute.
- Partial cohort membership → report coverage; do not default missing
  names to zero residual.

### 7.3 Effective-N

Promotion-bearing (here: **research-adjudication**, never live authority)
reads use **event-level / episode-level N**, not fire-dates.

- A US session-day is one episode for US-only hypotheses.
- A multi-region contagion path is one episode (GD-H8).
- Clustered CIs: block by episode, not overlapping 21-day rows.
- Honest-N is printed next to every cell. A cell with `< 30` episodes or
  `< 10` distinct calendar months is `UNDERPOWERED — DISCLOSED`, not a
  pass or fail.

## §9 Statistical treatment

- Block/bootstrap or episode-clustered CIs, B = 2000, 95%.
- Multiple-testing control: Benjamini–Hochberg inside each hypothesis
  family `{GD-H1, GD-H2, GD-H3}` (predictive family) and separately
  inside `{GD-H4, GD-H5, GD-H6, GD-H7, GD-H8}` (mostly confirmatory /
  descriptive). Cross-family pooling is not a gate.
- Calibration metrics (Brier, reliability slope) only if a probability
  output is produced; this prereg does **not** introduce one. Existing
  organ probabilities, if any, are evaluated as-is.
- Precision–recall for the rare hazard labels in §4.2, vs each baseline
  in §5, design era only.
- Capital-utility for Prophet sidecars: losses avoided, winners withheld,
  MAE avoided, upside forgone, board expected-shortfall change, time in
  protection, action churn (packet §15).
- Report nulls and sign inversions. Descriptive coincidence is labeled
  `COINCIDENCE`. Predictive lead is labeled `LEAD` only after the mature
  PIT sample and the baseline gate.

## §11 Temporal reconstruction protocol

For each replay timestamp `t`:

1. construct the source availability set as known at `t`;
2. refuse revisions/publications unavailable at `t`;
3. select the code definition actually in force, or replay the current
   definition as a **separate** counterfactual;
4. compute each existing organ from truncated inputs where reproducible;
5. compare with the committed historical artifact if one exists;
6. explain any divergence by source vintage, definition version, build
   ordering, stale carry, or unavailable history;
7. record authority separately from observation.

A historical artifact created later is **recomputed history**, not proof
the system emitted it at `t`.

## §12 Prophet counterfactual protocol

For each raw US/CN board session:

1. preserve exact board definition, raw population, rank, and lane;
2. never rebuild the board with Grey Deer inputs;
3. attach hypothetical vulnerability/policy dispositions as a **sidecar**;
4. measure outcomes for **all** candidates, withheld or not;

absence of technology from US actionable recommendations was (a) a

entry-status/availability gate, (c) ranking/order, (d) data absence, or
(e) chance composition. Return the exact rejection/disposition
distribution for technology versus defensive sectors. Do not infer
“Prophet knew the crash” from a defensive shelf.
