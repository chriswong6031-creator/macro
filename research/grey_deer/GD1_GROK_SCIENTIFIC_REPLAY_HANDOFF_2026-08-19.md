# 7. GD-H1 through GD-H8 — freeze before outcomes

Do not change these after opening the result columns. Refinements require a new prereg version and Fable approval.

### GD-H1 — duration × broken leadership

When leadership is already broken/dislocated, accelerating long yields increase the hazard of a ≥3%/≥5% residual drawdown in high-duration equities over 1 and 3 sessions.

### GD-H2 — volatility acceleration conditional on fragility

Volatility/realized-variance acceleration adds information about near-term transmission when leadership is already fragile, even if VIX level itself is not historically extreme.

### GD-H3 — Treasury auction tail as conditional trigger receipt

Auction concession/tail has incremental information only when duration fragility exists and long-yield/rate-vol confirmation follows. Standalone “bad auction predicts equities” remains disallowed.

### GD-H4 — defensive rotation as transmission confirmation

High-beta breadth damage plus positive defensive residual strength is a same-session confirmation of broadening transmission, not necessarily a leading predictor.

### GD-H5 — China Prophet board-health confirmation

Cross-sectional collapse of the canonical China board adds incremental real-time evidence of market-wide transmission beyond the index alone.

### GD-H6 — PBOC zero-operation sign is conditional

Zero 7-day reverse repo has no fixed sign. Adverse interpretation requires net drain and funding/FX/equity transmission stress.

### GD-H7 — repair impulse versus durable repair

Issuer/policy support without cross-asset repair has elevated probability of failed repair/retest versus a repair that broadens across trigger, correlation, breadth and exposed-cohort state.

### GD-H8 — contagion sequence

Clock-ordered residual damage across regions adds descriptive and potentially predictive information beyond one global-index return, but any lead claim requires a mature PIT sample.

# 10. Temporal reconstruction protocol

For each replay timestamp `t`:

1. construct the source availability set as known at `t`;
2. refuse revisions/publications unavailable at `t`;
3. select the code definition actually in force or explicitly replay the current definition as a separate counterfactual—never mix the two silently;
4. compute each existing organ from truncated inputs where reproducible;
5. compare with committed historical artifact if one exists;
6. explain any divergence by source vintage, definition version, build ordering, stale carry or unavailable history;
7. record authority separately from observation.

A historical artifact created later is **recomputed history**, not proof that the system emitted it at `t`.
