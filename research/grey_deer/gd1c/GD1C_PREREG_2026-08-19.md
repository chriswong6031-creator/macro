## §1 Mission, sample, and lanes

### 1.1 Design and coverage windows

- **Design era:** every eligible NYSE session from `2016-01-04` through
  `2026-07-31`, inclusive.
- **Out-of-design incident coverage:** `2026-08-01` onward, reported separately
  if lawful inputs exist. It never enters a percentile, fit, calibrator,
  endpoint choice, or verdict gate.
- All outcomes use future sessions strictly after the observation close. A
  same-session drawdown cannot earn anticipatory lead.

### 1.2 Reconstruction lanes

Two lanes are declared and may never be pooled:

1. **`pit_membership` — PRIMARY.** Run the current
   `leadership_crack.v1` definition on inputs truncated at observation session
   `t`, using only cohort membership whose identity and effective interval can
   be established point-in-time. A git blob or current membership file is not
   by itself proof that a constituent belonged to the cohort historically.
   Each member requires an effective start/end or an equivalent first-known
   membership receipt. If the full primary design-era sample cannot be
   reconstructed without guessing identity, the affected hypothesis verdict is
   `BLOCKED`.
2. **`def_current_cf` — SECONDARY COUNTERFACTUAL.** Run the same current
   definition on truncated price/rate/volatility inputs using the live/current
   cohort membership frozen at the reconstruction input vintage. Every row is
   labeled `def_current_cf`. This lane can produce useful secondary evidence,
   but it cannot rescue, replace, or be mixed into a blocked primary lane.

`def_at_t` is outside GD-1C. No output may claim that the current definition was
historically emitted.

### 1.3 Required row identity

Every reconstruction or episode row carries at least:

`lane, membership_basis, definition_schema, code_commit_sha, code_blob_sha,
observation_session, effective_session, available_at, source_ids,
input_vintages_json, input_sha256_json, member_count, member_coverage,
required_source_coverage, quality_state, notes`.

Unknown `available_at`, identity, or vintage is explicit and removes that row
from promotion-bearing evaluation.

## §3 Frozen measurement rules

### 3.1 leadership_crack.v1

Use the current source exactly:

- membership keys: `ai_semiconductors`, `ai_infra`, `memory_storage`,
  `semicap_equipment`;
- member prices forward-filled at most 3 days; members more than 3 sessions
  stale at the panel end are excluded for that truncated run;
- velocity: causal z-score of 5-session equal-weight cohort excess return over
  SPY, trailing 120 sessions, minimum 60, with normalization inputs shifted one
  session;
- carnage: member drawdowns from 63-session rolling highs; share at or below
  −20% smoothed by a 3-session EMA;
- `CRACKING`: `z_vel <= -1.5` or (`med_dd <= -0.12` and carnage EMA ≥ 0.40);
- `BROKEN`: `med_dd <= -0.20` and carnage EMA ≥ 0.60, with the frozen
  three-session heal hysteresis;
- `dislocation`: state in `{CRACKING, BROKEN}` while SPY is within 3% of its
  63-session high.

The fragile conditioning indicator is
`fragile_t = 1[state_t in {CRACKING, BROKEN}]`. `BROKEN` and `CRACKING` are
also reported separately. State at `t` is computed from information through
the close of `t`; targets start at `t+1`.

### 3.2 Long-yield acceleration for GD-H1

Required nominal features:

- `d_dgs10_1d`, `d_dgs30_1d`: one-session changes;
- `d_dgs10_3d`, `d_dgs30_3d`: three-session changes;
- `rate_accel_raw = max(d_dgs10_3d, d_dgs30_3d)` after preserving each
  series' units;
- `accel_long_nom = 1` when either three-session change exceeds its own
  training-design 80th percentile.

The real-yield leg `d_dfii10_1d` is reported only if a lawful first-available
vintage exists; otherwise it is `UNAVAILABLE` and does not block the nominal
test. Latest-revised FRED values may not enter a promotion-bearing historical
frame. Fold thresholds use training-era rows only; the final forward threshold
is then frozen once on the complete ≤2026-07-31 design era.

The deterministic challenger score before calibration is the maximum of the
training-empirical percentile ranks of `d_dgs10_3d` and `d_dgs30_3d`, multiplied
by `fragile_t`. This fixes the interaction without selecting a rate series after
seeing outcomes.

### 3.3 Volatility acceleration for GD-H2

- `rv_spy_5d`: five-session realized variance of SPY close-to-close returns;
- `drv_spy_5d`: one-session change in `rv_spy_5d`;
- `vol_accel = 1` when `drv_spy_5d` exceeds its training-design 80th
  percentile;
- `vix_level`: VIX close, baseline only;
- optional VIX9D/VVIX/term fields are `UNAVAILABLE` unless depth and rights
  are established before use.

The deterministic challenger score before calibration is the
training-empirical percentile rank of `drv_spy_5d`, multiplied by `fragile_t`.
No absolute VIX threshold is a challenger (`DNR:KILL-ABSOLUTE-VIX-THRESHOLDS`).

### 3.4 Outcome cohort and residuals

The outcome cohort is the same row-specific membership used to reconstruct
Leadership Crack in that lane. Partial membership is not filled as zero.

For member `i` and horizon `h ∈ {1,3}`:

`resid_i,h = (P_i,t+h / P_i,t − 1) − (SPY_t+h / SPY_t − 1)`.

The four co-primary binary endpoints, frozen from GD-1, are:

- `y_resid_dd_ge3_1s = 1` iff cohort-median residual ≤ −3% at `t+1`;
- `y_resid_dd_ge5_1s = 1` iff cohort-median residual ≤ −5% at `t+1`;
- `y_resid_dd_ge3_3s = 1` iff cohort-median residual ≤ −3% at `t+3`;
- `y_resid_dd_ge5_3s = 1` iff cohort-median residual ≤ −5% at `t+3`.

Both hypotheses are evaluated on all four predeclared endpoints. No composite
endpoint is introduced. Endpoint-specific results remain separate.

## §4 Independent episodes and honest effective N

Daily fires and overlapping labels are not independent observations.

For each hypothesis separately, an eligible fire at `t` is:

- GD-H1: `fragile_t == 1 and accel_long_nom_t == 1`;
- GD-H2: `fragile_t == 1 and vol_accel_t == 1`.

Eligible fire sessions separated by no more than three NYSE sessions belong to
one candidate episode (the maximum declared outcome horizon is three sessions).
The first eligible fire is the immutable episode anchor; later fires inside the
cluster cannot move the anchor or improve its outcome. A new episode requires a
subsequent eligible fire after at least three complete non-fire sessions.

For each hypothesis/endpoint/lane:

- `effective_N` is the number of anchored candidate episodes with lawful
  features and matured outcomes;
- `adverse_N` is the number whose frozen endpoint equals 1;
- a candidate episode is a non-event false critical episode for that endpoint
  when it fires and its frozen outcome is 0;
- calendar-quarter false-alarm counts use episode anchors, not fire dates;
- uncertainty resamples whole anchored episodes in chronological blocks; no
  daily row may be resampled independently.

The episode ledger also prints raw eligible fires, deduplicated episodes,
distinct calendar months, era counts, adverse episodes, and coverage. The
August incident is flagged `out_of_design_sample=true` and excluded from all
design-era counts.

## §5 Baselines and probability construction

### 5.1 Baselines

Each challenger is compared on identical eligible episodes with:

- constant training prevalence;
- `b_leadership_crack` severity (`CRACKING < BROKEN`);
- `b_vix_level`: training-empirical VIX percentile;
- `b_rv_level`: training-empirical `rv_spy_5d` percentile;
- `b_rv_target`: `rv_spy_5d` above its trailing 252-session median;
- `b_ma_break`: SPY below its 50-session moving average.

Unavailable baseline inputs are named; common-row comparison is required. A
challenger matched by a simpler volatility baseline at lower false-alarm cost is
`REDUNDANT_WITH_BASELINE`, not a GD-5 candidate.

### 5.2 Fixed probability mapping

Each hypothesis/endpoint uses its single deterministic score from §3. A
one-feature, intercept-plus-score logistic calibration is fit on training
episodes only, with fixed L2 regularization (`C=1.0`) and no feature selection.
Training fold percentile transforms are also fit on training rows only.

If a training fold has only one outcome class or too few lawful episodes to fit
the frozen calibrator, that fold is not imputed. The relevant gate is
`UNDERPOWERED` unless a temporal/source condition makes it `BLOCKED`.

## §6 Splits, leakage control, and stability

### 6.1 Era folds

Three predeclared eras:

1. `E1_2016_2019`: 2016-01-04..2019-12-31;
2. `E2_2020_2022`: 2020-01-01..2022-12-30;
3. `E3_2023_2026H1`: 2023-01-03..2026-07-31.

OOS predictions use leave-one-era-out cross-fitting: train the percentile
transforms and fixed calibrator on the other two eras; evaluate only the held

The frozen split halves for Brier/sign reporting are:

- `H1`: 2016-01-04..2020-12-31;

## §7 Statistical treatment and multiple testing

- OOS discrimination: average precision (AP) on cross-fitted episode
  probabilities, event prevalence, and `AP / prevalence`.
- Uncertainty: episode-block bootstrap, `B=2000`; report ordinary 95% intervals
  and the promotion gate's one-sided 90% lower bound for AP lift.
- Brier skill: `1 - Brier_challenger / Brier_constant_prevalence`, printed for
  both frozen calendar halves and overall OOS.
- Calibration: intercept and slope from endpoint outcome on the logit of OOS
  probabilities, with probabilities clipped only at `1e-6` and `1-1e-6`.
- Sign/order: the calibrated score coefficient and event rate by score quintile,
  reported overall, both halves, every era, and every leave-one-crisis-out run.
- Lead time: for true-positive candidate episodes, number of full sessions from
  anchor close to the first future session on which the endpoint threshold is
  crossed; print N, median, p25, p75, min, and max.
- Multiple testing: the declared GD-1C predictive family is all eight
  `(GD-H1, GD-H2) × four co-primary endpoints` tests. Benjamini–Hochberg at
  `q=0.10` applies to the one-sided episode-bootstrap AP-lift p-values. No
  endpoint or hypothesis is removed after outcomes are seen.
- Nulls, inversions, non-estimable calibration, and baseline wins are printed.

## §8 Frozen promotion gates and verdicts

For a named hypothesis/endpoint to PASS, **all** must hold in the PRIMARY
`pit_membership` lane:

1. 100% PIT/source-availability compliance on evaluated episodes;
2. at least 30 independent episodes and 12 adverse outcomes;
3. at least three eras, including a real post-2020 cell;
4. OOS AP ≥ 1.25× prevalence and the 90% block-bootstrap lower bound above
   1.0× prevalence;
5. positive Brier skill in both frozen calendar halves;
6. calibration slope 0.70–1.30 with no material probability-bin inversion;
7. expected economic sign/order in both halves and no leave-one-crisis-out
   reversal;
8. for an `ANTICIPATORY` label, median lead ≥1 full session and p25 lead >0;
   otherwise the result is explicitly `COINCIDENT` and fails the anticipatory
   gate;
9. no more than two non-event critical episodes in any held-out calendar
   quarter;
10. at least 80% declared required-source coverage at firing time;
11. the endpoint's AP-lift test survives family BH control;
12. no simpler baseline matches or beats protection at lower false-alarm cost.

Verdict precedence per hypothesis:

- **BLOCKED:** primary PIT membership, clocks, rights, or required input
  vintages cannot be lawfully reconstructed. A secondary `def_current_cf`
  result never changes this verdict.
- **UNDERPOWERED:** the primary lane is lawful but misses the sample/adverse/
  era requirement or cannot estimate a frozen fold/calibration metric.
- **PASS:** at least one predeclared endpoint clears every gate after BH. The
  PASS scope is limited to the exact cleared endpoint(s); it reopens only the
  applicable hypothesis's GD-5 shadow build and grants no live authority.
- **FAIL:** the primary lane is lawful and adequately powered, but no endpoint
  clears every gate.

`REDUNDANT_WITH_BASELINE` is reported alongside FAIL and cannot reopen GD-5.
If endpoints differ, the hypothesis verdict follows the precedence above and
the endpoint-level table remains authoritative.

  all price inputs are truncated correctly.

  lack history/rights.
