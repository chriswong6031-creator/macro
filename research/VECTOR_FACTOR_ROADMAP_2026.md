# Bitcoin Vector — Factor & Stability Roadmap (2026-06)

Research output (14-agent workflow, web + codebase-grounded). Tiered by value × orthogonality × free-data-feasibility × low-overfit, under the house rules: both-halves calibration, ~3-cycle anti-overfit, free data, no server, ML-meta-labeling skepticism.

## Executive summary

One roadmap to raise the Bitcoin Vector's signal STRENGTH, STABILITY, and ACCURACY, tiered by (value x orthogonality x free-data-feasibility x low-overfit) and respecting the house rules (both-halves gate, ~3-cycle anti-overfit, free-data, no-server, ML/heavy-meta-labeling skepticism). The dominant finding, confirmed against the code: the highest-leverage wins are NOT new data — they are four compute-only methodology gates the harness is missing (purged/embargoed CV to replace the single leaky split_date at calibrate_vector.py:318; an expanding-window/PIT fit for the regression-based valuation bands; probability calibration of the conviction layer that build_vector.py:293 asserts with no reliability curve or Brier; and porting the existing orthogonalize() at forex_signals.py:70 + a VIF screen into BTC's heuristic composite_state). These cost ~0 data, can only REDUCE overfit, and are the precondition that lets every thin post-2024 factor (ETF flow, stablecoin peg, gamma-flip) ship honestly instead of being silently overfit. On the DATA side, the genuinely orthogonal, both-halves-survivable, near-zero-pipe wins cluster tightly: realized-vol cones + vol-of-vol (Coinbase OHLC 2014->, on disk), taker CVD aggressor-flow (entirely new microstructure axis), the dealer gamma-flip / zero-gamma boundary (pure local recompute of the already-fetched Deribit gamma surface), the LTH-vs-STH SPREAD (STH legs already wired; only the LTH legs + differencing are net-new), the stablecoin supply-growth TIDE (3119-row parquet already on disk and wired), Wikipedia pageviews attention (keyless, 2015-> full split-half coverage, the one attention source that survives both halves), and the stablecoin PEG-deviation veto (a true new modality — collateral solvency, event-driven so it dodges the ~3-cycle trap, and the missing BTC-side Gate-1 the dislocation engine lacks). The miner hashprice/difficulty-shock factor is the one deep-history NEW miner anchor (2010-> on disk). Everything post-2024 (ETF flow, IBIT options, corporate treasuries, narrative-regime) is admitted EXTREMES-only / context under the formal <2yr protocol, never as calibrated alpha. Block-bootstrap + Deflated-Sharpe on the allocation backtest is a near-free stability port from the sibling dislocation harness. REJECTED-by-tiering: stacking a 6th valuation oscillator (RHODL/AVIV/Metcalfe/NVTS) onto the already-saturated cost-basis cluster — the multicollinearity the upgrade doc forbids; these are demoted to discipline cross-checks or single-representative-per-axis only.
