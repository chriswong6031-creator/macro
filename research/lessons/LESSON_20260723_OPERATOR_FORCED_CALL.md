**What happened.** 07-11: operator ordered the site to show Mag-7 leadership ("MAG 7 is on
the run … our dashboard does not reflect any of this") while the action board read mag7
*deteriorating → avoid*, Ignition Radar read *OFF K=0/8*, and the masterplan itself called
the move "a recovery inside a larger consolidation." Evidence for the call: multi-name
3-day bullish-crossover confluence — taken against Nasdaq weekly/bi-weekly/monthly topping
structure. 07-23: false breakout resolved as the worst 1-day Mag-7 basket decline in ~5y
(GS); TSLA −15%, GOOGL −7% intraday.

**Why (mechanism).** Conviction peaks exactly when the gauntlet matters most. A pinned
board/alert is authority-tier (it tells readers to act), so pinning an un-gauntleted call
is a promotion-gate bypass — regardless of who originates it. Topic importance (it's
Mag-7!) masqueraded as evidence; lower-timeframe signal was promoted over a standing
higher-timeframe veto; three fresh postmortems on the same signal family (07-14/16/22)
went un-re-read.

**The rule.** Operator ideas enter as display-tier watch items or pre-registrations —
never as pinned boards/alerts. The engines' skeptical read is overridden only by a
gauntlet pass, not by rank. Corollary (candidate, market-class — needs its own prereg):
leadership/breakout calls stay non-actionable while the parent index's weekly+monthly
structure reads topping.
