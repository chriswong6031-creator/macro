**What happened.** On the 07-23 crash tape, us_stocks' Ignition Radar strip read "Warming
up" with 1 of 8 broad signals lit. The headline state was carried by a narrow-theme
streak: #1 XLE (day 8 — an Iran-war energy bid, not risk appetite), #2 Crypto, #3 AI
Semiconductors (a falling knife). The surface also shipped ahead of its own printed gate
("display-only until ≥30 grades + operator ruling" — never met). Same channel had scored
"Magnificent Seven 0.70 igniting" on 07-11, feeding the forced call.

**Why (mechanism).** A top-N board renders a podium even when no race is on — ranking by
checklist-fraction is honest at Tier-2, but the glance tier reads *a podium as a starting
gun*. Event-driven single-sector moves satisfy persistence/breadth checklists without
being the thing the board claims to measure (risk appetite turning on). And a display gate
that doesn't bind is decoration: the card's own self-audit line admitted it wasn't ready.

**The rule.** Any always-on ranking surface must: (a) have an honest-null glance state
("nothing igniting — stand by") instead of a forced top-3; (b) exclude event-driven
single-sector moves from ignition-class narratives; (c) never let a narrow streak escalate
the board's headline state while broad confirmation is dark; (d) respect its own
pre-registered display gate — self-audit lines are contracts, not captions. Until (a)-(d)
plus ≥30 forward grades + operator ruling, the machinery runs background-only.
