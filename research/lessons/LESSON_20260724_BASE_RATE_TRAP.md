**What happened.** The washout census read 10/13 "good entries, all failures in 2022" —
which *feels* like reliability. The §4 adjudication measured it: in 2015→2026, a RANDOM
day on the Mag-7 EW basket was a "good entry" 69% of the time (median fwd63 +9.5%).
Against spacing-matched random placements, 10/13 is p=0.367; the median edge survives at
p=0.048 raw but 0.238 after the ×5 selection haircut; the same construction on the other
2W anchor phase shows nothing (p=0.314).

**Why (mechanism).** A decade-long secular uptrend IS the signal in any long-entry
backtest on that tape — the tool inherits the drift. Hit-rates without a base-rate
comparison are marketing, not evidence. Selection compounds it: the "best tool" was
chosen from a zoo after seeing the bottoms, and the zoo's size must haircut the p.
"Ex-2022" is hindsight conditioning until an ex-ante rule exists that excludes 2022
without excluding 2019/2020/2023.

**The rule.** No reliability claim without: (a) the base rate on the same tape and
ruler printed next to it, (b) a selection haircut for the construction zoo it came
from, (c) robustness across trivial variants (anchor phase!), and (d) any exclusion
year backed by a pre-registered conditioner. Stable-but-insignificant ≠ dead — it means
the forward record decides, which is what shadow books are for.
