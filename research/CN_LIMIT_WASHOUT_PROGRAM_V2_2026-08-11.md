Reverse-engineer the **light footprints that precede a first limit-up**, and feed them to
Prophet / Golden Influence as a scoring layer — so that among already-admitted, washed-out
picks, the ones with blast-off propensity rank first. Prophet is the quality floor and the
universe; this program is the winner-surfacing ranker inside it. Genesis case: Prophet
ranked 300363.SZ #1 the day before its 20% board.

The operator's named footprint families:

- **Washout / basing**: deep, long drawdowns; under-200DMA; basing duration.
- **Terminal confluence**: MACD-RSI + Stoch-RSI confluence on the 2D/3D clocks, as
  transcribed from the Terminal's own indicator math.
- **Sector-wide washout**: the reversion mechanism — a beaten-down name inside a
  beaten-down sector.
- **Accumulation footprints**: institutions hammering price below intrinsic value to
  accumulate, then releasing news — chip-distribution (筹码) concentration shifts, quiet
  volume patterns, insider-accumulation tells.

Out of scope by standing kills that this ruling did not disturb: **continuation riding**
(entry-family constructions on post-ignition cohorts were killed convergently by two
independent stacks; those kills stand and are not re-litigated here). The thesis is
pre-ignition selection, where the holder already owns the name when it boards and the
