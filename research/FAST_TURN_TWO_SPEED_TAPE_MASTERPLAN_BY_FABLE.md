## §0 Executive ruling

The recon falsifies the operating theory ("the spike was too fast for the system to see"). The nightly at 02:00 UTC captured the 2026-07-08 closes the same evening; the semicap basket page is stamped `as_of: 2026-07-08`. The system **saw** the event. Four separate failures made it invisible:

1. **Construction latency (dominant).** Every surface the operator checked is built to be slow, by design:
   - The allocation "Prevailing narrative" is a 63/126/252d ensemble momentum rank with a **hard 21-trading-day skip** (`engine/narrative_rotation.py:47-48`, `SKIP_D=21`) — a 12-1-style strategic allocator. A regime break at day 0 cannot enter *any* momentum input until day 22; headline flip estimate 22–40+ sessions. Nothing on the page says this. It is labeled "current best place to allocate."
   - The basket neural score (semicap 51) weights trend 0.26 (5/20/60d relative returns), breadth 0.18 (% above 50d MA), and its macro leg is absent for semicap (no `_MACRO_PRIOR`/`_SECTOR_PROXY` entry) — the score renormalises over available legs (`engine/theme_scoring.py:38-39`), so it is macro-**blind** for this basket, not macro-dragged. The fast legs did fire on 07-08 (impulse +0.555, MTF +0.4) but carry 0.07/0.16 weight, and the crowding penalty (rs_pctile 0.857 ≥ 0.85) blocks ACCUMULATE outright. Flip estimate 5–15 sessions.
   - Oracle episode onset needs `accel_z_5d > 1.0` (5-day-smoothed) + 5 consecutive sessions to confirm: ≥6 sessions minimum. Cycle engines: 14% ZigZag, EMA(10) smoothing, weekly MACD: 5–10 sessions; `cycle_blocked` suppressed the only two `buy_soon` members (KLAC, LRCX).
2. **Disconnected shock plumbing.** `repricing_coherence` printed **SHOCK 75 on 2026-07-07 — the eve of the reversal** — and the shipped shock de-escalation reflex (#2015) posts its "ranks computed on pre-shock data, discount" banner on sector.html, subsector_rotation.html and the dashboard action board… but **not** on baskets.html, allocation.html, or any basket detail page — precisely the surfaces the operator reads for rotation. The flip-confirmation lens (26 flips, 58% fade at T+1) and the policy-lever card land on the dashboard only. `sector_pulse` (1d/5d rank/score deltas, heating/hot tiers — an already-shipped fast-turn read) is not wired to the baskets page at all.
3. **Staleness defect.** `data/allocation/latest_us.json` froze at `as_of: 2026-07-06`; the 07-08/07-09 nightlies advanced baskets but not allocation, while a manual run today produces changed bytes and `as_of: 2026-07-08`. The nightly `git add data/` pathspec is broad (daily.yml:207) and the file *did* commit on 07-07 — so the fault is **upstream in content**: the sub-build is invoked from `build_baskets.py:229` under an exit-0-always wrapper with no freshness alarm, and it has been failing (or computing on a stale input) silently since. Same silent-freeze class as the Oracle Time-Machine outage (#2002). W0 root-causes the actual exception from nightly logs; the git-add theory is REJECTED on evidence.
4. **Taxonomy blindness.** The operator's evidence ticker (AVGO) lives in `ai_semiconductors`; the checked page was `semicap_equipment` (16 small/mid WFE names; AMAT was **-5.4%** over the same 5 days). No surface aggregates the AI-capex complex into one tape view. An in-house complex grouping already exists (`engine/demand_capex.py:45-52`, `AI_CAPEX_THEMES` with directness tags); the TIL crosswalk (merged 07-09) is theme-level only — complex-level grouping is TIL-owned taxonomy work (§5 W5).

**And one thing the house must say honestly:** the empirical case law is that chasing day-2 violent flips is the minority trade — the flip lens base rate is **58% fade at T+1 (15/26)**, confirmed-tier rotation continuation is NULL both directions (Oracle constitution §IV), defensive-rotation lead FAILED OOS, sector-level standalone washout→turn triggers printed NULL (Oracle P8 P-W1/S-W3; DO_NOT_REBUILD §2 "Washout × turn"), and the house's own tactical report *The Relapse* (published 07-08) explicitly read the semis bounce as short-covering to sell. "Front-run the rotation" as an auto-escalating signal is both forbidden (TI-R5, PS-R1) and empirically unsupported at current n. What is legal, buildable, and was plainly missing is: **same-day/intraday/premarket visibility of the turn, honest staleness marking, shock-conditioned de-escalation on the pages that matter, complex-level aggregation, push notification of computed states, and forward-graded fast-turn detectors run as expected-NULL meters that could earn authority later** (PS-R8 path, earliest 2027). Detection speed with printed error rates IS the product (Oracle constitution). This program ships that.
