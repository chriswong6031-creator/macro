## Core outcome

BioCatalyst must answer:

> What matters next in biotechnology, for which stock, why does it matter, how uncertain is it, what does history suggest, what appears incorporated, and what should I research now?

The existing Trial Intelligence Workspace is preserved as source-truth and evidence drill-down. It no longer defines product completion.

## Delegated operating boundary

Fable owns end-to-end in-scope progression: architecture completion, bounded child commissioning, review/repair/acceptance, merge/release/deploy, production proof, durable closeout and planned principal succession. Fable does not wait for routine Sol/CEO continuations.

The delegation does not grant trade origination, deterministic Availability, position sizing, portfolio authority, new external licenses/credentials, destructive non-rollback acts, or permission to duplicate canonical owner planes. Those remain explicit escalation boundaries.

Every child remains independently bounded with a fresh operation/carrier/pickup/START/proof/STOP. The parent Fable program source remains active across child boundaries.
