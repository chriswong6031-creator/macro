## 0. Executive ruling

BioCatalyst is not to be judged by whether it can render truthful ClinicalTrials.gov records. It is to be judged by whether a serious investor can open the product and answer:

> **What matters next in biotechnology, for which stock, why does it matter, how uncertain is it, what does history suggest, what appears incorporated, and what should I research now?**

The current Trial Intelligence Workspace is retained as an evidence and investigation surface. It is no longer the primary product or the completion standard.

The immediate company objective is a **What Matters Next** decision-intelligence board that converts lawful event, identity, corporate-disclosure, historical-response, fundamental and market-context inputs into a transparent research workflow. The product may publish calibrated intelligence and research priority where evidence is adequate. It must abstain loudly where it is not.

The opposite extremes are rejected:

- **Facts-only forever** is rejected because it cannot satisfy the user job.
- **One opaque Catalyst Score** is rejected because it collapses distinct claims and creates false authority.

The product architecture separates facts, estimands, context, ranking and trade authority rather than banning intelligence or hiding it in one number.

## 1. Why the prior program failed despite large effort

### 1.1 The code did not merely malfunction; the roadmap optimized the wrong completion law

The current product is a coherent, source-grounded registry workspace over a four-NCT canary. Its narrow capability is real. Its investment utility is not.

The system repeatedly proved:

- authenticated and signed-out behavior;
- pointer-bound generation reads;
- exact source attribution;
- typed locked, empty, outage and integrity states;
- registry revision lineage;
- browser geometry in EN and ZH;
- CI, contracts, deployment and production availability.

But the Chairman still cannot discover an investable catalyst universe, compare stocks, understand event probability or materiality, inspect historical outcomes, or receive a prioritized research queue.

The failure pattern was:

```text
source / schema / store / validator / provenance / browser proof
    treated as product completion
without
broad discovery / identity / expectation / materiality / historical response /
calibrated uncertainty / incorporation / prioritization / learning
```

### 1.2 Foundation became the roadmap

The prior work generated valuable substrate, but every substrate item was allowed to create another substrate prerequisite. The program advanced by technical dependency rather than by independently useful user capability.

### 1.3 The current authority rule made the desired product impossible

`WS:BIOCATALYST-CORE-PRODUCT` currently says no score, probability, materiality, rank or composite may appear anywhere. That protected the Trial Milestones facts lane from pretending to be a market signal. It was defensible for that payload. It became destructive when generalized across the entire product.

The repair is not to let models rewrite facts. It is to create separate governed intelligence objects with explicit clocks, methods, uncertainty and abstention.

### 1.4 Breadth never graduated

The production-proven Trial Milestones product is cohort-limited. The frozen source-soak window ended 2026-08-26, but the current committed launch manifest remains `soak_scheduled` with no completed source result. The product therefore never received a clean successor source/coverage transition.

### 1.5 Identity remained too weak for stock discovery

Trial identity is not stock identity. Sponsor strings, subsidiaries, license relationships, co-development, royalty economics, asset ownership and security listings must be handled explicitly. Historical Event History PR #6389 reports 4,067 identity-resolved versus 12,317 unresolved projected rows. That corpus may still be useful, but it cannot by itself support a reliable stock-level opportunity board.

### 1.6 Intelligence components were built but left inert

The Seasonality estate has event-study, response-model, calibration and Prophet-shaped code. Its own canonical handoff says the event-study engine never executes, the model never produces a forecast and the Prophet bridge has no caller because the production builders were never written.

This is the clearest symbol of the program failure: tested modules were counted as progress even though they produced no nightly evidence and served no decision workflow.

### 1.7 Draft integration architectures never became one accepted product program

Biopharma Cycle Intelligence and Catalyst Federation contain useful ideas—market episodes, expectations, surprise, materiality, historical response and incorporation—but remain draft or research-only. They must be reconciled into the current owner estate, not treated as shipped and not copied into another program.

### 1.8 Operational churn displaced product progress

#6389 has consumed extensive reconciliation, CI and RuntimeBinding attention while remaining an open DRAFT historical-data carrier. That work may be worth finishing, but it is not allowed to block the first useful V3 product. One stale carrier cannot become the critical path for the company outcome.

## 2. Product thesis and value model

### 2.1 Primary persona

A serious public-markets biotechnology investor or analyst who needs to discover, triage and investigate company-moving clinical, regulatory and corporate catalysts without manually assembling a calendar from scattered issuer releases, SEC filings, trial registries and historical market data.

### 2.2 Primary user job

In under five seconds:

1. See which biotechnology stocks have important upcoming events.
2. Understand what the event is and when it may happen.
3. Distinguish source-confirmed timing from inferred timing.
4. Understand why the event may matter to the issuer.
5. See what is known, unknown, revised or disputed.
6. Decide which opportunities deserve deeper research.

In under five minutes:

1. Inspect exact source evidence and correction lineage.
2. Understand asset/company ownership and economic exposure.
3. Compare historical analogues and counterexamples.
4. See calibrated outcome/timing estimates where estimable.
5. See historical response distributions and incorporation context.
6. Export the research question into the broader Mastermind workflow.

### 2.3 Machine/intelligence job

Continuously transform owner-produced source facts into a point-in-time, correction-safe, rights-safe catalyst investigation graph, then produce separate calibrated intelligence objects and prospective evaluation records without contaminating source truth or granting premature trade authority.

### 2.4 Commercial value

A useful BioCatalyst creates a premium daily workflow rather than an occasional reference page. It supports retention through:

- broad upcoming-event discovery;
- personalized watchlists and revision alerts;
- differentiated historical analogue and response intelligence;
- transparent probability/materiality assessment;
- company/asset dossiers;
- integration with Terminal, Stock Dossiers, Portfolio and Prophet after lawful promotion.

### 2.5 Data and learning moat

The defensible moat is not a copied catalyst calendar. It is the cumulative point-in-time system of:

- exact event facts and revisions;
- source/evidence clocks;
- sponsor/issuer/asset/security relationship history;
- expectation baselines known before events;
- family-specific outcomes and censoring;
- historical market response paths;
- model versions and prospective predictions;
- calibration, ranking and lead-time evidence;
- user investigation and alert interaction outcomes.

## 3. The 10/10 experience architecture

### 3.1 Primary page: What Matters Next

The first BioCatalyst screen is a ticker-level catalyst opportunity board, not the raw registry browser.

Each row or card eventually contains:

| Field | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| Ticker / company | canonical point-in-time issuer/security identity or explicit unresolved state |
| Asset / program | canonical or provisional asset identity with ownership/economic relationship |
| Catalyst | family-specific event definition; not a generic label |
| Timing | exact date, bounded window or declared period with native precision |
| Timing source class | source-confirmed, company-guided, rule-derived, model-inferred, unresolved |
| Timing confidence | separate from outcome probability; method and evidence disclosed |
| Outcome probability | family-specific calibrated estimate or `NOT_ESTIMABLE` |
| Issuer materiality | decomposed economic importance, not a hidden score |
| Historical response | distribution, N, effective N, analogue concentration and counterexamples |
| Incorporation evidence | price/IV/positioning/context with source clocks and missingness |
| Research priority | transparent triage ordering, initially research-only |
| Why now | one concise explanation of the current clock/change/edge |
| Evidence | one-click source, revision, identity and methodology drill-down |

### 3.2 Default ordering

The default board is not ordered solely by date and not by an opaque model score. R0 must freeze an explainable research-priority contract. The minimum candidate ordering inputs are:

- event-window imminence;
- timing-source quality;
- issuer materiality estimability and magnitude;
- novelty or revision recency;
- unresolved evidence burden;
- historical response dispersion;
- market-incorporation uncertainty;
- user watchlist/portfolio relationship, when lawfully private and user-specific.

The board must disclose why an item is high priority. Missing fields cannot be silently treated as zero or low risk.

### 3.3 Progressive disclosure

**Glance tier:** ticker, asset, catalyst, timing window, source class, materiality band/unknown, probability/NOT_ESTIMABLE, research-priority explanation.

**Investigation tier:** exact source evidence, revisions, trial/regulatory state, company guidance, identity/ownership graph, key assumptions, probability/materiality methodology and counterevidence.

**Deep research tier:** historical analogues/counterexamples, response paths, expectation history, incorporation analysis, capital/financing context, options geometry, related companies and unresolved questions.

### 3.4 Existing Trial Intelligence Workspace

The current screen remains useful under the deep-research tier for:

- ClinicalTrials.gov current state;
- primary/study completion schedule facts;
- date and status revisions;
- evidence provenance;
- trial dossier and peer investigation;
- source and integrity states.

It is not renamed into a prediction system. Registry completion dates remain registry schedule facts and never become readout dates or success probabilities through wording.

### 3.5 Required product states

Every board and dossier must deliberately render:

- populated;
- valid empty, with reason;
- partial coverage with denominator;
- source delayed/stale;
- source outage;
- evidence-integrity block;
- entitlement locked;
- unresolved sponsor/issuer/security;
- unresolved asset or ownership relationship;
- date precision unavailable;
- event family unsupported;
- timing not estimable;
- outcome not estimable;
- materiality not estimable;
- historical sample insufficient;
- analogue no-match / out-of-distribution;
- incorporation data uncovered;
- correction pending;
- conflicting source claims;
- model version unavailable or calibration failed;
- current-only relationship that cannot be backdated;
- superseded/cancelled/withdrawn/terminated/suspended using source-native semantics rather than invented generic cancellation.

## 6. Intelligence object architecture

The V3 product is a composition of distinct objects. R0 must map each to an existing contract or propose the smallest owner-compatible extension.

### 6.1 EventFact

Represents what a source or lawful owner asserts happened or is scheduled.

Minimum semantics:

- stable source-native event identity;
- event family and subtype;
- subject asset/program/trial/application;
- issuer/security/economic relationship with PIT validity;
- native effective date/window and precision;
- first-known/observed/publication clocks;
- source URL/evidence pointer;
- revision/supersession/correction lineage;
- source-native status;
- coverage and rights class;
- exact unresolved states.

No model-generated probability belongs inside EventFact.

### 6.2 TimingAssessment

Answers: **when is the catalyst likely to occur or become knowable?**

Timing confidence is not outcome probability. Sources may include:

- exact official date;
- issuer-guided month/quarter/half/year;
- protocol schedule facts;
- statutory/regulatory clocks;
- historical sponsor behavior;
- rule-derived trial completion-to-readout windows;
- model inference where separately authorized.

It must retain:

- source class;
- lower/upper bound and precision;
- inference method/version;
- assumptions;
- calibration evidence;
- abstention reason;
- correction behavior when guidance changes.

### 6.3 ExpectationBaseline

Answers: **what was believed or expected before the event?**

Candidate hierarchy:

- E0: explicit contemporaneous issuer/regulator/consensus facts;
- E1: deterministic owner-derived expectation;
- E2: fold-frozen statistical expectation;
- E3: model-generated narrative context with zero standalone authority.

Expectation must be frozen at a declared `as_known_at`. Post-event evidence may not leak backward.

### 6.4 OutcomeProbabilityAssessment

Answers a family-specific binary, multinomial or time-to-event question with a defined target.

It requires:

- event-family target definition;
- eligible denominator and censoring rules;
- training cutoff and as-of contract;
- feature availability at prediction time;
- model/version and calibration regime;
- raw N, issuer concentration and effective N;
- reliability/Brier/log-loss or family-appropriate diagnostics;
- uncertainty interval;
- out-of-distribution/no-match state;
- `NOT_ESTIMABLE` when minimum evidence is not met.

A generic clinical-success percentage across incompatible phases, indications, endpoints and sponsor types is prohibited.

### 6.5 IssuerMaterialityAssessment

Answers: **how economically consequential could the event be for this issuer?**

Keep separate dimensions rather than hiding them in one score:

- program value/exposure denominator;
- ownership and economics (wholly owned, partnered, royalty, option, regional rights);
- revenue/market opportunity range and duration;
- pipeline concentration;
- balance-sheet and financing runway dependency;
- competitive/substitution context;
- strategic optionality;
- downside asymmetry and sunk/continuing cost;
- evidence quality/estimability.

BioCatalyst consumes financial/capital facts and projections. It does not calculate a parallel financial truth plane.

### 6.6 HistoricalResponseDistribution

Answers: **how did comparable securities behave around comparable events?**

Required outputs:

- event-window definitions;
- benchmark/sector adjustment;
- pre-event and post-event paths;
- median/quantiles/tails, not only average;
- upside/downside asymmetry;
- raw N and effective N;
- issuer/date/family concentration;
- analogue similarity inputs available at t0;
- counterexamples;
- missing price/identity/corporate-action handling;
- no-match/out-of-distribution state;
- reproducible as-of replay.

The finite BPC snapshot may seed event labels but cannot donate export-time price, IV, OI, market cap or current identity as historical pre-event features.

### 6.7 IncorporationEvidence

Answers: **what evidence suggests the market has or has not incorporated the event/expectation?**

Separate observations may include:

- price trend and event-relative move;
- relative performance/residual response;
- options implied move/term structure/skew where covered;
- volume/liquidity and crowding context;
- short interest/ownership/insider/attention sources with native clocks;
- expectation revisions and evidence-price gap;
- dispersion and uncertainty.

No single observation proves bullish/bearish positioning or mispricing. Missing/uncovered never equals neutral or safe.

### 6.8 ResearchPriority

ResearchPriority is a transparent triage object, not trade authority.

The R1 version may be deterministic and rules-based using source quality, imminence, materiality estimability, revisions, evidence gaps and user relevance. Statistical ranking may be added only after R0 freezes the target and R6 accrues prospective evidence.

Every priority output must state:

- components used;
- components unavailable;
- reason for current placement;
- model/rule version;
- authority `research_priority_only`;
- explicit prohibition on entry/size/trade origination.

## 8. Time, correction and point-in-time law

Every object must preserve at least the clocks it genuinely owns rather than normalizing everything to one ambiguous timestamp.

Candidate clocks:

- source effective/event time;
- source scheduled window;
- source publication/filing time;
- first Mastermind observation time;
- evidence retrieval time;
- generation/publication time;
- correction/supersession time;
- prediction freeze/as-known-at time;
- market observation session/time;
- outcome resolution time.

Hard laws:

1. `effective_at <= known_at <= observed_at` where those clocks semantically apply; exceptions must be typed, never silently reordered.
2. A later correction appends a revision and closes prior transaction validity; it does not rewrite the historical belief state.
3. Current sponsor/issuer/asset/security relationships cannot be backdated into historical events without PIT evidence.
4. Current-vintage adjusted price data cannot be claimed as a historical vendor vintage.
5. An approximate quarter or month remains a bounded interval, never a fabricated date.
6. A registry primary-completion date is not a topline announcement date.
7. A source date revision changes Timing/EventFact lineage; it does not imply the clinical outcome changed.
8. An outcome label added after the event cannot leak into pre-event analogue retrieval or model construction.
9. Every forecast or priority evaluation must freeze features and model version before the outcome.
10. Missing evidence is a typed state, not zero.

## 10. Statistical and model architecture

### 10.1 Deterministic first

R1 must become useful before sophisticated prediction is ready. Deterministic source quality, timing class, revision recency, horizon, identity coverage, issuer concentration and evidence gaps can produce a valuable research board without fake outcome probabilities.

### 10.2 Family-specific estimation

Outcome and timing models are trained and calibrated per defensible family or hierarchical family structure. Pooling decisions must be evidence-driven and fold-frozen. Small families should display descriptive history or abstain instead of emitting unstable percentages.

### 10.3 Baselines before complexity

Every model family requires boring baselines:

- unconditional/family base rate;
- phase/indication/sponsor-size stratified baseline where powered;
- simple regularized model;
- calibrated tree/boosted or survival model where justified;
- hierarchical/shrinkage model for sparse families;
- model-generated narrative only as context, never as the calibration target.

A complex model must beat the relevant baseline out of sample and prospectively before gaining authority.

### 10.4 Leakage and selection controls

Mandatory controls include:

- issuer/date/grouped cross-validation;
- leave-one-issuer and time-forward evaluation;
- feature availability audits at `as_known_at`;
- outcome attachment only after retrieval/fingerprint freeze;
- current-universe survivorship audits;
- missingness and coverage-selection diagnostics;
- vendor-vintage and corporate-action handling;
- model/version immutability for prospective predictions;
- effective N and concentration reporting;
- placebo/negative-control families where possible.

### 10.5 Calibration and promotion

A probability number is not accepted because retrospective AUC looks good. Required evidence includes:

- reliability/calibration curves;
- Brier/log-loss and baseline comparison;
- family/issuer/time-slice stability;
- coverage and abstention behavior;
- decision-utility metric tied to the research job;
- prospective forward ledger;
- minimum effective N and elapsed evidence windows frozen before evaluation;
- independent review and Sol acceptance.

Trade/rank authority remains under existing Eval/Fusion/Prophet law.
