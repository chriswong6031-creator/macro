## 0. The one-sentence diagnosis

**All four China desks run the same architecture and share the same failure: they ingest a wholly free-tier, 98%-retail-scrape Chinese-text stack, reduce it to a hand-authored score or z-band rendered with quantitative authority, and grade it against forward outcomes nowhere — while the two things that make Chinese qualitative signal genuinely different (state media as intentional signaling, and censorship survivorship) are unmodeled in every desk, so what ships is a decorative attention-router wearing a validated-signal costume, and one of its two live engines is a dead render silently freezing the data.**

Concrete shipped state, quantified:

- **The state-media "signal" is a symmetric bag-of-words that confuses control with crisis.** CCTV 新闻联播 tone is `(pos−neg)/items` over 35 hand-picked `_POS` and 32 `_NEG` words (`collectors/china_news.py:47-58`, self-labeled "deliberately CRUDE" at `:26`). A pro-Party crackdown headline ("坚决打击违规") scores negative because 打击/整治/违规/遏制 are all in `_NEG` — verifier-confirmed.
- **The one path from qual to a displayed number rests on an unproven sign.** China Intel fuses news sentiment into a 0-100 `context_conviction` using a hand-set contrarian sign (`sign_expected=-1`, `china_validation.py:68`), but the scorecard reads `status:"accruing", proven:false, n_obs:0, reason:"signal series missing"` (`data/china_validation/scorecard.json`).
- **That sign is structurally unfalsifiable on current data.** The blended tone series is ~19 days (`cctv_tone.parquet` 19 rows 2026-06-13→07-01, `wire_tone.parquet` 11 rows); validation needs `_MIN_PROVEN_N_TS=25` pooled obs (`china_validation.py:62`), and `_news_sentiment_series` returns None below 60 days (`china_validation.py:234`) — so `n_obs=0` persists indefinitely unless a multi-year backfill is built (verifier-confirmed missed problem).
- **A live render is dead, freezing the machine feeds for 10 days.** `build_china_news.py:73` calls `render(news=ni.panel())` but `templates/china_news.html.j2` reads `CN = china_news`, raising `UndefinedError 'dict object' has no attribute 'theme_label'` (reproduced live), swallowed at `build_china.py:912`. The JSON writes at `build_china_news.py:86-90` are never reached, so `site/chinanews/{sentiment,feed,by_ticker}.json` are frozen at asof `2026-06-22` while `events.parquet` is fresh to `2026-07-01`.
- **The displayed z is stale AND statistically void.** Shipped `sentiment.json` ships `z:-2.06, n_days:10, band:"cautious"` — a 90-day-window z (`sentiment_window:90`, `config.yml:2561`) computed over ~19 points. Recompute on fresh data gives `z:-0.79` (verifier: band stayed "cautious", did NOT flip to "steady" — the audit's regime-flip claim is corrected).
- **The alt-data desk's own survivorship fix is dead code.** `china_altdata.py:40-43` sign-flips 龙虎榜/block to `lhb:-0.10, block:-0.05`, but the shipped weights are the un-flipped positive priors `{value:0.24, margin:0.2, flow:0.18, comment:0.15, lhb:0.1, block:0.05, analyst:0.08}` — 92% of convergence weight sits on untested/wrong-sign/ungradeable legs (verifier-confirmed).
- **The source floor is hard.** `events.parquet` is 848/866 (97.9%) tier-2 retail scrapes (em 501, futu 175, ths 172), 18/866 (2.1%) tier-1, 0 tier-3, so the `TIER_W` model is inert. A word-boundary grep across `collectors/`+`engine/` for Wind/Choice/iFinD/CSMAR/CNInfo full-text/expert networks returns zero integrations.

## 1. The keystone — no outcome-feedback loop, so the China desks can never learn what matters (and China has an extra trap the US desks lack)

The single deepest problem is the same one the masterplan names for the whole suite: **the China qualitative layer is architecturally incapable of learning "what matters" from results.** Every China symptom cascades from this absence. But China carries a second, compounding trap: the data itself is *intentionally shaped and censored at source*, so even if the loop existed, a naive model would learn the shape of the propaganda, not the market.

Trace the intended pathway and watch it break at the same accountability point in every China desk:

1. **The raw material is capped at headlines and a bag-of-words tone.** Every desk ingests only what free retail wires and English state RSS *publish*; there is no article body, no full-text filing, no expert color. The "media-sentiment index" is `pos−neg` keyword counts over a 35/32-word lexicon (`collectors/china_news.py:73-75`), and the theme/importance/ticker layers all operate on ~10-15 words of headline.

2. **"Importance" and "conviction" are asserted, never measured.** `china_news_intel.importance_score` (`:258-267`) is a pure additive formula (`TIER_W` + `0.30` scheduled + `0.40` surprise + `0.50` high-impact-kw + theme weight + basket count, 48h half-life), and China Intel's `_edge_remaining` (`china_intel_analysis.py:247-271`) uses fixed weights `1.0/0.9/0.8/0.7` and fixed scores `0.7/0.45/0.2`. No literal has ever been regressed against a forward move; a grep for a grader/IC/backtest across the China desks returns nothing but the accruing-forever `china_validation` timer.

3. **The one desk that DOES have a validation hook is dark by starvation, not design.** `china_validation._news_sentiment_series` requires ≥60 blended-tone days before it will z-score and validate the news leg (`china_validation.py:234`); the live series is ~19 days, so it returns None and the grader silently never runs — with no telemetry that it is dark (verifier-confirmed missed problem). The accountability scaffolding is *present-but-starved*, which is a different and more fixable problem than "no grader exists."

4. **The scored sign is applied as if measured.** `china_intel_analysis.py:158-164` hard-codes the comment "news is CONTRARIAN in China (validation sign_expected = -1)" and inverts the z-sign into every conviction row — dressing an unproven prior in the language of the validation machine. `china_signal_lab.leg_weights_for` keeps the leg at its `0.20` prior floor because the family is not `proven+sign_ok`.

5. **The China trap on top of the loop absence: the substrate is intentionally shaped.** State media is deliberate signaling (§7), and censorship removes negative signal at source (§8), so a rising tone can be *suppression, not easing*. Even with a working loop, a naive learner would fit the shape of what the state chose to publish — the highest-conviction novel edge (the removed-negative signal) is exactly the thing a survived-corpus model cannot see.

The result is definitive: **the China suite can DECIDE what to show and can promise to grade it, but it has never once closed the loop from a qualitative call to a graded outcome — and even if it did, it would be learning from a corpus the state curates.** Because nothing is graded, the hand-authored weights cannot improve, the contrarian sign cannot be earned, and the survivorship trap remains invisible.

→ **Light solution directions for Fable:** make outcome-labeling a first-class stage (grade whether "High"-importance items' tagged tickers actually move vs CSI-300 over 1-5d), and grade *relevance judgments* not just picks; before any of that, decide whether Chinese state-media tone can EVER be a real signal on a few-readouts-a-year cadence or should be replaced by a canonical-phrase-diff detector; and treat censorship survivorship as a first-class leg so the loop does not learn the propaganda's shape.

## 7. The defining China gap #1 — state media is intentional signaling, modeled as a bag-of-words

**Severity: CRITICAL (unmodeled across all four desks).**

This is the whole point of the China audit, and it is the strongest, most-verified finding: **in China the news IS the driver, not commentary about it — and every desk treats it like a Western headline feed.** The signal in 新闻联播 is lead-item ORDERING, which Politburo member appears, whether a phrase is ELEVATED to a slogan (新质生产力, 稳增长, 反内卷), a byline tier (评论员/社论 vs routine copy), and verbatim formula shifts (稳中求进→稳增长, the reappearance of 适度宽松, the dropping of 房住不炒). None of that is modeled anywhere.

- The CCTV tone is `(pos−neg)/items` over 35 `_POS` and 32 `_NEG` hand-picked words (`collectors/china_news.py:47-58`, self-labeled "deliberately CRUDE" at `:26`), and `_tone_features` (`:61`) concatenates all broadcast columns into one blob and counts tokens — order and structure discarded (CN-QUAL-6, CI-5, CN-NEWS-04, all CONFIRMED).
- The lexicon confuses control-signaling with crisis: 打击/整治/违规/遏制 are all in `_NEG`, so a pro-Party crackdown headline ("坚决打击违规") scores negative (verifier-confirmed). It cannot tell "we are cracking down (control = confidence)" from "there is a crisis."
- The desk's own code acknowledges the gap and ignores it: `collectors/china_news.py:4` explicitly notes "lead-item ordering and word choice" matter, yet the feature set uses neither (verifier note on CN-NEWS-04).
- A phrasing shift like 稳中求进→稳增长 or a dropped 房住不炒 — the actual policy signal — registers as ~zero tone change because both terms are 增长-family `_POS` tokens (CN-QUAL-6, CONFIRMED).
- China Policy Watch detects 政治局/发改委/Politburo as keywords but only to ROUTE them into a link list; the readout's stance/tone is never scored (CPW-1/CPW-4, CONFIRMED). A 100-bp-easing Politburo tone shift with unchanged rates yields "neutral."

→ **Light solution directions for Fable:** replace the bag-of-words tone with a canonical-phrase-diff engine over free official corpora (新闻联播 lead items, Politburo/CEWC readouts, State Council/PBOC/NDRC/CSRC, People's Daily front page) treating added/removed/reordered stock phrases as the primary signal; separate "control language" from "risk language" in the lexicon; add a lead-item-ordering / slogan-first-appearance feature. See §9 for how this becomes the primary China policy-signal engine.

## 8. The defining China gap #2 — censorship survivorship, the highest-conviction novel edge every desk ignores

**Severity: CRITICAL (unmodeled across all four desks).**

The second China-defining gap: **negative signal is removed at source by design, so absence-of-negative reads as neutral or positive — and a rising tone can be suppression, not easing.** Every China pipeline is keep-first, append-only, and reads a quiet tape as "quiet," never as "suppressed." This is the edge nobody else in the suite has, and it is entirely unbuilt.

- No deletion/retraction/URL-liveness tracking anywhere; `accrue` is append-only keep-FIRST (`china_news_intel.py:369`), and a grep for censor/deletion/survivorship in the China qual engines returns nothing (CN-QUAL-5, CI-5, CONFIRMED).
- The trap compounds the contrarian sign: a regime tightening its news control mechanically raises tone, which the contrarian leg (CI-1) then reads as a fade signal at exactly the moment risk is highest — potentially inverting the intended read (CI-5, CONFIRMED).
- The alt-data desk *found* survivorship (LHB up-day inflation, `china_altdata.py:33` "the apparent positive is all in survivorship-inflated up-day flags") and then dead-coded the corrective sign-flip (F1/F6, CONFIRMED). The one place survivorship was identified was discovered and silently discarded.
- The attention leg (千股千评) is survivorship-inflated toward officially-tolerated names, because negative ST/delisting discourse is scrubbed — so ranking names by "high attention + inst participation" is systematically biased toward the names the state wants visible, the opposite of an edge (F6, CONFIRMED).
- Baidu Index / social attention feeds carry the same structural bias: a scrubbed/blocked term silently drops out, so a collapsing term could be demand collapse, topic suppression, or seasonality (strategy `cn_land`, Baidu source note).

→ **Light solution directions for Fable:** a first-class censorship/survivorship guard — track headline-URL liveness across runs as a deletion-rate metric, or build an onshore-CCTV vs offshore-English-GDELT tone DIVERGENCE index where a widening gap = suppression = risk-off; treat abnormal negative-word suppression as a risk flag rather than a positive; flag attention/search series as "possibly suppressed" rather than "demand fell" when the index collapses abnormally. This is the highest-conviction novel edge the suite currently ignores.

## 9. China institutional-grade acquisition — the honest thesis (the edge is cheap, not bought)

## 10. How the four China desks share ONE centralized engine while respecting different information content

**Severity: framing — this is the net-new build, and it aligns to masterplan §9.**

Today the China desks are forked silos exactly like the US ones: `china_news_intel.py` re-implements its own `event_id`, `_norm_title`, `source_tier`, entity map (`CN_BASKETS`), and `importance_score` from scratch (masterplan §4), and each writes its own ledger. The fix is the same hub-and-spoke engine the masterplan specifies (INGEST → NORMALIZE → ENTITY/EVENT RESOLUTION → DEDUP+NOVELTY → IMPORTANCE/RELEVANCE → PER-ASSET+MACRO EMIT → ACCOUNTABILITY) — with China desks as spokes on the shared kernel — **but with two China-specific constraints baked into the shared stages:**

1. **Different information content, same schema.** US filings/wires are informative and legally clean; China state-media tone, THS scrapes, and policy signaling are noisier and *partly intentional signaling with censorship survivorship*. The China spokes must feed the same entity model, importance model, and outcomes ledger, but their IMPORTANCE features differ: for China, the load-bearing features are canonical-phrase presence/absence/reorder and a censorship/deletion-rate flag, not Western sentiment polarity.
2. **The bilingual entity resolver is the longest pole.** Masterplan §9 flags entity/event resolution as the highest-risk item; for China it is harder still — bilingual resolution (中国平安↔601318↔Ping An) and generic NER over Chinese policy text is a research problem, and the current CJK substring match (CN-NEWS-09, the 2-char-name false-tag) is exactly the failure mode a shared resolver must fix. Promote a bilingual resolver over the unified entity graph and delete the China duplicate `event_id`/`norm_title`/`source_tier`.

The open design tension (see payload): whether importance is ONE model with region features or two models sharing a schema — and how to stop a China-specific frozen prior (the state-media z-score, the contrarian sign) being *learned as spurious edge* on a small, regime-dominated n (~19-day tone series, `n_obs=0`). Per masterplan §9, **learning is DEFERRED / shadow-only** for China too: the near-term win is unification + a common yardstick, and given China lacks clean point-in-time history to backtest phrase signals leak-free, China policy-language signals should stay display-only until an out-of-sample forward log accrues.

→ **Light solution directions for Fable:** extract the shared kernel first and migrate the four China desks onto one outcomes ledger keyed `(entity, date, desk, signal_id)` before any learning; make the censorship/deletion-rate flag and the phrase-diff a first-class shared feature so every China desk inherits the survivorship guard; emit a China macro/regime aggregate ("many CN names in one theme lit today"), which nothing produces; keep the state-media leg magnitude-only (no learned sign) until a multi-year backfill can falsify it.

## The Fable payload — the hard open questions

These are the genuinely unresolved China tensions. The findings above exist to ground them.

1. **Can Chinese state-media tone EVER become a real signal, or only a formula-diff detector?** The bag-of-words misses the actual signal (lead-item ordering, slogan appearance/disappearance, formula shifts). Given only a few Politburo/CEWC readouts per year, is a canonical-phrase-diff engine the right replacement — and is the low-n curse escapable, or is this structurally a context overlay, not standalone alpha?

2. **Is the contrarian-news sign structurally unfalsifiable on our data?** The blended tone series is ~19 days; validation needs ≥25 pooled obs and `_news_sentiment_series` needs ≥60 days, so `n_obs=0` persists indefinitely unless a multi-year tone backfill is built (verifier-confirmed). Where does the akshare `ak.news_cctv` history actually break, and can a deeper 新闻联播 transcript archive be seeded like other intl deep-history seeds? Should the news leg be demoted to weight-0 decoration rather than awaiting proof that cannot arrive on current data?

3. **How to model censorship survivorship as a first-class leg, and separate suppression from genuine decay?** Options: deletion-rate / URL-liveness tracking, or an onshore-CCTV vs offshore-English-GDELT tone DIVERGENCE index (widening gap = suppression = risk-off). Operationally, a collapsing Baidu term could be demand collapse, topic suppression, or seasonality — what cross-checks (parallel un-censored proxies, cross-platform divergence, timing vs known policy events) separate these, and what confidence do you attach when you can't? This is the highest-conviction novel edge the suite ignores.

4. **What is the right formal model for the policy-phrase signal without leak-free history?** Is it a supervised map (phrase-cluster → forward sector return, needing clean point-in-time labels we mostly lack) or an unsupervised novelty/surprise score (this readout diverges from the running language distribution)? The survivorship problem may make supervised labeling unsafe — how do you validate without the CSMAR/Wind time-stamped corpora institutions use?

5. **One importance model or two, and the China spurious-edge trap?** Should importance be ONE model with region features or two sharing a schema? How do you stop a China-specific frozen prior (state-media z-score, contrarian sign) being learned as spurious edge on a small, regime-dominated n — and does the bilingual entity resolver (the longest pole) make a shared kernel worth the coupling, or should China stay a schema-compatible but separately-modeled spoke?

6. **The minimum forward-grading protocol that licenses moving a China leg from display to weight.** The accountability chassis is total-vacuum (radar `n_resolved:0/9`, news `n_obs:0`, no policy-ledger grader). Given clean point-in-time history is the exact thing China lacks, should policy-language signals be display-only until an out-of-sample forward log accrues — or is China structurally a context/overlay layer, not a standalone alpha source? How do you represent "promise-to-grade" vs "actually-graded" so the UI (the "cautious" red pill, the 0-100 conviction) never launders one as the other?

7. **Where is the true China edge boundary?** Is the small-shop advantage in SPEED/DISCIPLINE of reading free official text (a process edge institutions are too slow to exploit at the margin), or is it structurally impossible to beat desks that have BOTH the clean corpora AND the compliance apparatus to run legal primary intelligence — meaning China should be treated as a context/overlay layer rather than a standalone alpha source? Given the user risks real capital on low-n mechanism theses, what is the minimum-viable validation bar before a China policy-language signal is allowed to size a position?
