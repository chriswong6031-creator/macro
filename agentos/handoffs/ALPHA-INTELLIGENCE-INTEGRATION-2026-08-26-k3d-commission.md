## Observable mission

Ship one independently useful **display/research-tier Economic Propagation hypothesis
contract/compiler** that can deterministically answer:

> Given one source event and one exact target identity, what owner-backed economic,
> fundamental/narrative and residual-market paths support a plausible **operating**
> read-through; which generator admitted the target; what alternatives/falsifiers/
> expiry apply; and what is unknown or unavailable?

The implementation must compose existing owner evidence into one typed hypothesis
record while preserving Graph 1 / Graph 2 / Graph 3 separation. It must prove both:

1. a lawful composed record when owner evidence actually supports the required legs;
2. a real-data typed abstention when Graph-1 economic relationship, exact identity,
   rights, clocks or coverage are insufficient.

A correct abstention is a successful product behavior. K3-D may not manufacture an
economic cause merely because narrative similarity or market sympathy exists.

## Why it matters

Mastermind already has many relationship-like facts but lacks one governed object
that tells a researcher **why a read-through is plausible, what kind of evidence
supports it, and what would falsify it** without turning correlation into causality.
K3-D converts that fragmented substrate into useful machine/research context while
protecting the moat: exact provenance, explicit alternatives, correction-safe clocks,
and honest missingness rather than another opaque alpha score.

## Three-graph law

### Graph 1 — Economic relationship / why transfer could occur

Customer, supplier, partner, competitor, distributor/license, common customer or
supplier, product/end-market/geography/regulation exposure, program/facility/
bottleneck and ownership only when it changes cash-flow rights. Source must be
owner-backed and role-specific enough to support the claim.

### Graph 2 — Fundamental / narrative similarity

Comparable products/KPIs/Q&A/guidance/segments, narrative/topic/theme vocabulary,
curated peers and protocol comparability. Graph 2 explains comparability, **not**
a customer/supplier relationship.

### Graph 3 — Residual market / how market is treating names

Residual co-movement, group pulse, participation/breadth, earnings sympathy,
incorporation, attention/options/volume and contagion/leadership state. Graph 3
can exist without Graph 1 and cannot be inverted into economic cause.

Every relationship/generator leg is tagged with its graph and owner. No opaque
`RELATED` edge may erase that distinction.

## Data / contract / time / null / correction law

- Exact identity is Data OS / Stock Identity-owned. Alias/ticker/theme text cannot
  substitute for a resolved identity.
- Every evidence leg carries its native owner ref plus event/as-of/known-at state.
  Later filings/edges/corrections cannot rewrite a prior as-of hypothesis.
- Owner correction/supersession semantics are inherited, not translated into a new
  K3-D correction store. Recomposition at a later cutoff may yield a new version or
  changed state while preserving the earlier receipt.
- Graph-1 absence is `unknown/unavailable`, never false relationship and never zero.
- Graph-2/3 presence cannot fill a missing Graph-1 role.
- Rights blocked, unresolved identity, stale/unsupported owner object, missing clock,
  ambiguous role or correction not yet knowable all remain typed non-usable states.
- `economic_share` stays null/unavailable until its canonical owner mints a formula;
  K3-D may not invent one.
- Coverage/availability must be derived from the actual owner objects, not field names
  or nominal source meaning.
