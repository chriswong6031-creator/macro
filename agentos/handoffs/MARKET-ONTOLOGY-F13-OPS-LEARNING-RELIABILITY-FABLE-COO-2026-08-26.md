## Data/time/null/correction law

Last attempted, last successful, data-as-of, render/release and evaluation resolution clocks are distinct. Source/capability failure cannot remain green/current. Missing telemetry is unknown, not zero adoption. Methodology/version changes retain revision history. Claim evaluation binds preregistered claim type, horizon/resolution/source and appropriate metric; later evidence cannot rewrite the original claim.

## Method / failure law

Operational health and usage aggregation are deterministic over existing receipts/events. Statistical evaluation routes through Eval OS. LLM may summarize support/methodology/release state but cannot author health or grade. Fail on stale success masking a later failure, missing version, telemetry privacy breach, capability identity mismatch, evaluation leakage, wrong scoring rule, unresolved claim, source-rights redaction or duplicate observability/evaluation owner.
