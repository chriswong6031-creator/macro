Routine implementation uncertainty is not an executive gate.

## 7. Acceptance / proof law

Every claimed capability must name what newly works for a user or machine. Green CI, merged schemas and attractive UI are insufficient.

A product family is `PROVEN_LIVE` only when its real promised path has been exercised with real production inputs/consumers and the required failure/degraded states are also observable. Lower states remain honest: `BUILT_NOT_PROVEN`, `PARTIAL`, `DARK_OR_DISCONNECTED`, `SPEC_ONLY`, `NOT_BUILT`.

Context/research features do not need to prove alpha to count as product capability. They do need to prove truthful data, useful consumer behavior, correct missingness/rights/clock semantics and non-authoritative boundaries.
