## Observable mission

Own the cross-repository closure program until every material Macro / Terminal / Portfolio crossing
has an explicit canonical producer/owner, versioned contract, source/import clocks, null/correction/
auth/privacy/authority/fallback semantics, and genuine proof through its existing transport and
actual consumer — or an explicit evidence-backed `REJECTED_BY_DESIGN` ruling.

The machine capability unlocked at completion is cold-start-safe composability: a fresh Sol/Fable
or product consumer can identify exactly which bytes/meaning crossed repositories, what revision/
generation they belong to, what authority they carry, and whether the real production consumer
actually used them.

## Why it matters

Current cross-repo product behavior already exists, but several boundaries are only implicit in
implementation. That creates three dangerous failure families:

1. **authority drift** — consumer defaults exceed producer law;
2. **identity/freshness drift** — floating/last-good data remains readable without proving current
   producer/import identity;
3. **ownership drift** — one repository mutates another repository's normal source checkout.

A governance runtime would add another failure plane. The goal is to make the existing organism
self-describing and testable instead.
