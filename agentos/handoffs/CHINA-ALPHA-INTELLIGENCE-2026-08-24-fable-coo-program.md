1. **Reality** — what changed in the actual economic/business world?

source dependence, learn which information channels add incremental value, and
improve ordering of lawful China Prophet candidates without contaminating

Truth + Intelligence + Product + Learning system and, only if earned, a
prospectively proven China Rank vNext challenger that can be presented to
Sol/Chairman for live promotion.
