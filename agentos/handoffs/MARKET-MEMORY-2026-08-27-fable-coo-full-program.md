## 5. Data, contract, time, null and correction behavior

- Event/measurement/available/observed/as-known-at clocks are distinct and must remain so.
- `available_at <= as_known_at` is required for operational-PIT evidence. Later outcomes or
  revisions cannot enter a packet retroactively.
- Exact PIT lookup never falls back to nearest date/current state/recomputation.
- Missing/uncovered/late data is typed missingness or abstention, never zero/neutral.
- Reconstruction is allowed only under its explicit weaker basis and never upgrades to
  operational PIT.
- Source and owner bytes remain source/owner truth; Market Memory stores/returns receipts,
  references and validated context, not rewritten owner history.
- Corrections append a new revision/version lineage. Earlier known-at observations remain
  immutable. Owner v1 semantic conflict refusal is not permission to overwrite; use a
  separately versioned owner contract when correction semantics require it.
- Retrieval candidate population and coordinates must be frozen before outcomes are joined.
- Purge/embargo/de-overlap remains deterministic; selected count must not masquerade as
  effective independent N.

## 6. Deterministic vs statistical vs model-generated method

Deterministic:

- clock and packet validation;
- content identity, ancestry and generation pinning;
- exact known-at lookup;
- source/correction/refusal state;
- candidate eligibility filters, purge/embargo and de-overlap;
- evidence/citation/missingness structural validation;
- authority gates.

Statistical (only when evidence-ready):

- coordinate normalization/fits;
- retrieval-quality evaluation;
- dependence/effective-N estimation;
- any forecast/value/skill estimate.

Model-generated:

- may summarize/cross-examine already-grounded evidence inside a read-only Cortex/research
  consumer, but may not determine clocks, provenance, candidate completeness, rank, gate,
  size, trade or promotion state.
