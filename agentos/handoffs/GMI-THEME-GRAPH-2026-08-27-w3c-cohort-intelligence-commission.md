# W3C — Selection Cohort Intelligence · held principal-builder commission

**Operation key:** `gmi-theme-cohort-w3c-20260827-sol-001`  
**State at mint:** `DEPENDENCY_HELD`  
**Depends on:** accepted W3B

## Observable mission

Given an already-finalized U.S. or China selection set, return a deterministic cohort-intelligence packet that preserves every selected instrument and the source ordering, resolves exact identity, joins PIT-valid local-theme memberships, attaches current/as-of ThemeState and legitimate canonical relationships, and explains meaningful overlapping semantic neighborhoods or an honest no-overlap state.

## Why it matters

This is the user-visible payoff that motivated the W3 re-charter: five independently selected lithium names should be recognizable as an overlapping battery/lithium semantic cohort without pretending GMI selected them because of that theme. The same behavior must work for U.S. local subthemes.

## Authority / precedence

Chairman completion freeze; W3B accepted contract; CEO W3 continuation directive §§39–46; current U.S./China selection producer contracts; Data OS identity; GMI PIT membership; zero-authority law.

## Exact scope / non-goals

Build one read-only cohort contract/reader/projection and real consumers for the incumbent U.S. Prophet/actionable board and China Prophet/What-to-Act-On-Now surfaces that are current at claim time. Preserve existing selection artifacts; do not fork their lifecycle or publication truth. No ranking, no candidate generation, no board order changes, no neighbor insertion, no trade recommendation, no W4 relationship origination.

## Complete user / machine journey

1. Ingest source artifact ID/schema/market/source-as-of/selection timestamp/order and selected exact instrument IDs/reasons where the owner exposes them.
2. Preserve the source selection list byte/identity-equivalently; GMI adds context only.
3. Resolve exact identity through canonical owner seams.
4. Join each security to memberships valid and knowable at the selection/as-of cutoff.
5. Attach local ThemeState and canonical state/relationships only for matching lawful cutoffs.
6. Compute descriptive overlap: selected count per local theme, multi-theme intersections, concentration/participation descriptors already lawful under W3B, and neighborhood membership.
7. Preserve overlapping subthemes; do not collapse `EV/Batteries`, `Renewable/Materials`, `Metals/Battery` into one invented “Lithium” edge.
8. Distinguish selected vs non-selected verified neighbors for research context only; never insert neighbors upstream.
9. Emit typed `no_meaningful_overlap`, missing-identity, missing-state and stale-context states where appropriate.
10. Render/serve the packet through the existing board/product consumer with evidence/source drill-down.

## Data / time / null / correction law

Selection timestamp and source as-of are immutable inputs. Membership/state reads use decision-time PIT cutoffs, not latest current rows. Missing identity/state/membership is explicit. Later mapping/state corrections do not rewrite the historical cohort episode; a later recomputation is a new belief/version. Source ordering is preserved and separate from any descriptive cohort ordering.

## Method

Identity/PIT joins and overlap computations are deterministic. Existing W3B statistical legs may be displayed with native semantics. A model may summarize a fully grounded cohort packet for display but may not choose membership, alter counts, infer missing relationships, rank stocks or recommend trades.

## Failure states

Refuse/degrade on missing source timestamp, unresolved identity, no as-of membership reader, stale/missing ThemeState, selection list mismatch, post-cut membership leakage, canonical mapping not adjudicated, source order mutation, neighbor inserted into selection, or any GMI output consumed as upstream selection authority.
