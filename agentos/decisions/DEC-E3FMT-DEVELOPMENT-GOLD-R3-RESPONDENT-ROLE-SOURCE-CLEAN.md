## The discriminator that decides whether the law was implemented

`SCCO/2026Q2` and `COF/2026Q2` also publish blank segment roles yet **are** source-clean, because
those revisions carry replayable same-revision roster/title declarations. `ARQQ` and `FANG` do not.
A method that refuses every blank segment role is exactly as wrong as one that accepts every blank
segment role. Separating these two pairs on positive same-revision evidence — not on blankness — is
the test of whether the corrected law was actually implemented.
