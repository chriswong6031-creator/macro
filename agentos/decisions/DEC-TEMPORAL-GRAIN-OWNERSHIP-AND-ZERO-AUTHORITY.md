# Ownership and authority consequence

The W0 records release creates organizational and scientific boundaries only. It performs no
market-data collection, backfill, indicator calculation, signal mutation, runtime admission,
worker START, Slack delivery, Linear projection, product change or production proof.

The first empirical child may return only `ARTIFACT`, `FILTER_MEMORY`, `SESSION_GRAMMAR`, `MIXED`
or `UNRESOLVED_DATA` from the frozen Gate-1/2 interventions. `STRUCTURE_SCALE`,
`INFORMATION_TIME` and `REGIME_MULTISCALE` require later separately preregistered waves. A null or
kill is an acceptable completion.
