# Binding rider

At program close, `88/88` means **baseline authenticated coverage**, not sufficient
proof of current competitor coverage by itself.

F00 must preserve two ledgers/views:

1. **Authenticated baseline** — the immutable 88-row paid census and its final
   Mastermind dispositions.
2. **Current-public delta** — every materially named capability/workflow observed
   after the baseline research date, with source URL + observed date + reconciliation
   result + canonical owner/disposition.

The current-public delta does not require purchasing another subscription unless a
specific unresolved capability cannot be classified without paid-state evidence and
that evidence is material enough to justify a new bounded commercial/research gate.
Publicly observable names can first be reconciled against the retained authenticated
archive and current Mastermind estate.

## Seed delta candidates from 2026-08-26/27 public check

These are **candidates**, not automatically new engines:

- `Market Feed` — reconcile to event/change-feed family.
- `Research Screener` — reconcile to security/discovery/screening family.
- `Portfolio Constructor` — reconcile to Portfolio Superintelligence + research
  expression/sizing boundaries; do not grant trade authority by name.
- `Theme Map` — reconcile to GMI/theme/economic relationship owners; no new graph by
  default.
- `Decision Zones` — determine whether this is a projection of existing decision /
  price-zone / dislocation objects or a genuinely missing workflow.
- `Professional` full-portfolio event-resolution tier — reconcile as a coverage/SLO /
  entitlement state rather than assume a new intelligence engine.

F00 may add further delta candidates as the competitor evolves. Every candidate must
end with an explicit alias/new/rejected disposition; `UNRECONCILED` is nonterminal.

This rider narrows one phrase in the earlier complete-parity DEC: the final closure
set is **the authenticated 88-row baseline plus all validated current-public deltas**,
not the 88 rows alone. All other laws in the earlier DEC remain controlling.
