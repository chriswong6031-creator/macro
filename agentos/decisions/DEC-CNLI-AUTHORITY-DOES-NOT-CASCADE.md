key: CNLI-AUTHORITY-DOES-NOT-CASCADE
question: >
  Does success at one CN-Limit authority level (research anatomy, prediction,
  ordering shadow) automatically grant the next level (live ordering, score,
  gate, size, public probability)?
answer: >
  No. Research accrual, prediction/grading, counterfactual ordering, live
  ordering input, score authority, gate authority, position size, and public
  probability display are separate authority grants, each requiring its own
  explicit Sol/Chairman decision. R6 itself grants no runtime authority of any
  kind, and the highest R4 verdict
  (ELIGIBLE_FOR_SHADOW_IMPLEMENTATION_COMMISSION) is not live authority.
rationale: >
  Success at one level does not establish safety or value at the next: a
  calibrated hazard can still destroy access when acted on; a winning
  ordering shadow can still be unsafe as a live input under drift; a lawful
  internal probability can still be an unlawful or unsuitable public claim.
  Collapsing the ladder is how research artifacts silently become product
  authority — the exact failure mode the withdrawn W1-W3 picks surface
  demonstrated. Keeping grants separate also keeps rollback surgical.
alternatives:
  - option: Automatic promotion on gauntlet pass
    why_not: >
      Converts a measurement verdict into a product decision nobody took;
      leaves no seam for access, suitability, drift, or rollback review; A5
      exists precisely so live ordering is decided, not inherited.
