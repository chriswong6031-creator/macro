## Scope

This decision changes architecture ownership only. It creates no new runtime artifact,
strategy state, market support, entry, hold, plan, position, outcome, rank, size, leverage,
trade or publication authority.

## Operational interpretation

A future Cycle Capture experience should expose separate, source-bound projections:

```text
strategy eligibility / context
new-entry or add Availability
hold-thesis / falsifier state
portfolio action and risk state
```

The projections may share a strategy and canonical candidate/plan identity. They may
disagree. They may not be collapsed into one opaque score or lifecycle.

`CORE_HOLD` is legal only as a Long-Hold-compatible read projection. It is not a new
append-only state machine. `WAIT_PULLBACK` or `RAN_DONT_CHASE` may block a new add while
the core thesis remains valid. A tactical signal cannot terminate the core by itself;
a structural invalidation or downstream risk constraint can.

## Cross-market stop law

CW0 must name an accepted identity and candidate/event owner for the first proving market.
When none exists, the lawful result is a typed HOLD/REROUTE with
`reason=IDENTITY_OR_CANDIDATE_OWNER_GAP`, not a new generic candidate database.

A future, ETF, producer, miner and royalty company may share an economic exposure while
remaining distinct instruments with different carry, roll, dilution, balance-sheet,
jurisdiction and operating-leverage risk.

## Narrow supersession

This decision supersedes only any reading of
`DEC:PROPHET-ONE-PLATFORM-MULTI-STRATEGY-AND-CYCLE-CAPTURE` that:

- treats B1 as globally proven beyond current U.S. scope;
- creates a new Cycle Capture hold/falsifier/case/outcome owner;
- promotes Thesis Funnel or current hold_thesis artifacts into entry authority;
- lets consumer code relabel horizon role.

The one-platform/multiple-sleeve ruling, DNRs, D5 sequence, B2/B3/B4 separation, V3
control, owner boundaries and downstream Portfolio/Risk authority otherwise remain
controlling.
