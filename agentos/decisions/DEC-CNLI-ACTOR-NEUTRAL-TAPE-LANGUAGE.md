key: CNLI-ACTOR-NEUTRAL-TAPE-LANGUAGE
question: >
  May tape-derived features (OHLCV/volume anatomy) be described with actor
  labels such as hidden accumulation, insider activity, smart money, or
  manipulation?
answer: >
  No. Tape features describe downside ineffectiveness, bad-day resilience,
  reclaim/hold quality, and constructive-volume asymmetry — actor-neutral
  language only. Actor claims require independent lawful public evidence, and
  UNKNOWN never becomes institution, retail, informed, insider, or smart money.
rationale: >
  Intent is latent and cannot be inferred from OHLCV; an actor label minted
  from price alone is an unfalsifiable accusation dressed as a feature. It also
  creates a compliance and product-language hazard: no user-facing or internal
  surface may imply insiders, manipulation, or orchestration from chart
  anatomy. The actor-state law (professional discovery, public dissemination,
  retail recognition, speculative crowding as distinct observed states with
  potentially opposite signs) only works if actor states come from evidence
  planes with their own clocks, never from the tape.
alternatives:
  - option: Hidden-accumulation / smart-money labels on tape features
    why_not: >
      Unfalsifiable from price data; poisons the mechanism taxonomy by mixing
      observed evidence with inferred intent; forbidden in every R6 experience
      state and review question.
