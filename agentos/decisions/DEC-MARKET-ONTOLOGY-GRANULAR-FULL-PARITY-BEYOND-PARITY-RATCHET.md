# Binding granular parity and beyond-parity law

This decision narrows the meaning of **complete parity** without changing canonical system ownership.
It supersedes only older wording that could be read to permit cherry-picking, a family-level-only
closure test, or omission of low-priority captured capabilities. Existing no-third-workstream,
canonical-owner, rights, authority, PIT/null/correction, one-carrier and production-proof laws remain.

## 1. Unit of obligation

The closure unit is the **captured granular capability/job row**, not only a paid marketing anchor,
page, family, or engine. Rows may be compressed into one canonical Mastermind system, but the
compression must be many-to-one with explicit traceability rather than destructive deduplication.

Every admitted source row must retain at least:

- immutable source/evidence identity;
- evidence set and observation date;
- normalized capability/job identity;
- canonical Mastermind owner/workstream/module;
- current house capability state;
- disposition;
- stronger-equivalent pointer when applicable;
- user/machine journey that satisfies the row;
- data/source/rights dependency;
- authority ceiling;
- implementation/proof carrier;
- proof identity or explicit missing proof;
- beyond-parity hypothesis/review state.

## 2. Evidence union

F00 coverage is the union of:

1. exact retained public P1 corpus: **1,556 capability rows + 460 quality findings**;
2. authenticated paid baseline: **88/88** advertised paid targets;
3. current-public delta ledger: existing **42** post-baseline candidates plus subsequent additions;
4. new dated competitor censuses, including the Chairman's active 2026-08-28 Terra website sweep;
5. future lawful first-party/public observations discovered before final closeout.

A newer census never deletes a historical capability automatically. A removed competitor feature may
still remain valuable product research and keeps its row until Mastermind explicitly disposes it.

## 3. Allowed dispositions

The existing disposition vocabulary remains, interpreted as follows:

- `PROVEN_EXISTING`: the complete row-level job is already PROVEN_LIVE in Mastermind.
- `UPGRADE_EXISTING`: an existing canonical owner needs a bounded upgrade to satisfy the row.
- `BUILD_NEW`: the job is not currently implemented and needs a new bounded capability under an existing canonical owner.
- `PROJECTION_OVER_EXISTING`: the underlying truth/intelligence exists and only a lawful consumer/projection/workflow is missing.
- `RESEARCH_CONTEXT_ONLY`: the capability is intentionally descriptive/research-only; this is still a product capability and still owes a real consumer if the row is user-facing.
- `REJECTED_BY_DESIGN`: the competitor mechanism itself is rejected. The row must still name either a stronger Mastermind equivalent satisfying the lawful underlying job or the exact reason the underlying job itself cannot lawfully be provided.

`boring`, `low priority`, `not alpha`, `already similar`, `probably unnecessary`, and `commercially minor`
are not valid exclusion reasons.

## 4. Four F00 control stages

### F00A — exact corpus admission

Recover and byte-verify the retained P1 artifacts. Archive Mastermind's own black-box research without
reconstructing rows from model memory. Admission verifies exact size/hash receipts and preserves
source provenance. Corpus unavailability keeps the coverage gate open; inconvenience is not a parity
exception.

### F00B — coarse current-owner map

The already-running `marketontology-f00b-current-owner-crosswalk-20260828-sol-001` remains bounded to
88 authenticated baseline + current deltas. Its output is the coarse owner/collision map and must not
be presented as granular full-parity closure. Do not restart or widen that live child.

### F00C — granular reconciliation

After F00A admission and F00B return, reconcile every admitted granular row into current canonical
Mastermind owners. Acceptance requires zero `UNKNOWN`, zero `UNOWNED`, zero `UNASSESSED`, and no
row lost merely because multiple rows compress into one family.

### F00D — beyond-parity synthesis

For every parity-satisfied capability/family, perform a separate product-intelligence review asking:
what Mastermind-native systems can strengthen it, what new combined workflow becomes possible, what
learning loop can validate it, and whether the result creates a new moat or product surface. High-value
expansions become bounded existing-owner work; low-priority expansions remain durable backlog rather
than disappearing.

## 5. Lane execution

F01-F13 remain domain/lane identities, not permanent Fable seats. Each bounded child wave must name
the granular row IDs/family IDs it closes. One PR may close many rows when one independently useful
Mastermind capability genuinely satisfies them; one row may compose multiple owners only when the
user job actually requires composition. A merged PR does not close rows until the required real
consumer/production proof exists at the claimed tier.

## 6. Three closure milestones

### COVERAGE_COMPLETE

- all admitted evidence sets are deduplicated without row loss;
- exact retained P1 corpus is admitted;
- current Terra/public delta census is reconciled;
- zero unassessed/unowned/unknown granular rows.

### PARITY_COMPLETE

- every bona fide product/user/machine capability row has a PROVEN_LIVE Mastermind equivalent or stronger equivalent;
- non-user-facing method/security/data rows have corresponding canonical implementation/owner coverage;
- every product claim has its promised real consumer and production proof;
- rights and degraded/null/correction states are truthful;
- no duplicate truth/control plane was created.

### BEYOND_PARITY_RATCHET_ACTIVE

- every parity capability/family has a recorded beyond-parity review;
- high-value expansion hypotheses have durable bounded owners/next actions;
- Evaluation OS/learning instrumentation can distinguish product usefulness from alpha/trading authority.

`PARITY_COMPLETE` is required before Mastermind may claim complete Market Ontology functional absorption.
`BEYOND_PARITY_RATCHET_ACTIVE` is the continuing competitive program that keeps Mastermind ahead.

## 7. Authority boundary

Parity never grants rank, gate, size, origination, `ENTRY_OPEN`, Prophet or trade authority. Competitor
confidence/direction/pricing-gap/composite-opportunity semantics may be reproduced as transparent
research experience only through original Mastermind decomposition and remain authority=false until
Mastermind's prospective evidence/promotion law independently earns authority.
