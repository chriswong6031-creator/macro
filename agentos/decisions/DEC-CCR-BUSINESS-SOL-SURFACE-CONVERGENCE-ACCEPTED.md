## Operating consequence

Business Surface Convergence is an organizational continuation of
`WS:CHAIRMAN-CONTROL-ROOM`, not a replacement for its accepted P0A/H0/X1/P0B or ASD waves.
The existing workstream remains the parent; this decision does not rewrite its current
`next_action`, start a new implementation carrier, or make Business capability live.

The program may proceed through separately gated BSC-F0, BSC-P1, BSC-A1, Steward,
Executive, enrollment, canary, RuntimeBinding, dual-run, cutover and subtraction waves.
Each wave keeps its own exact Git carrier and current-source gate. Presence, plugin
installation, OAuth, Slack identity, app access or ChatGPT confirmation never manufactures
organizational or child-turn action authority.
