## Operational consequence

The future semantic program key is `organizational-learning`; its durable workstream key is
`WS:OUTCOME-LEARNING-POLICY-CALIBRATION`. Neither is created by this records-only decision because
the current semantic registry has an exact-count contract and PR #6615 already owns the next
stacked program registration. The registration wave must re-read current `main`, current registry
count and current owner collisions immediately before it starts. Attaching this work to
`project-active-build-control`, `shared-ai-provider-control`, Eval OS, Portfolio or Agent OS itself
would be a false parent.

Routine evidence stays in its owner. A material organizational decision may later carry a sealed
machine-readable ex-ante block in its Agent OS DEC. A routine operational exposure may carry the
same semantics in an immutable Executive event payload linked to its Job/Attempt/placement. The
original expectation is never overwritten after the outcome becomes knowable.

The Outcome Compiler may validate contracts, join typed references, compute declared metrics,
missingness, censoring, overlap, estimators, uncertainty and sensitivity, and emit an immutable
`mastermind.outcome_study.v1`. It does not choose workers, mutate source records, create jobs,
change policy or raise its own causal grade. Model prose is downstream explanation only.

## Causal admission law

Every study states the causal question, estimand, treatment, comparison, cohort, source cutoff,
metric definitions, missingness, censoring, overlap, estimator, uncertainty, sensitivity and
known unmeasured confounders. Evidence grades are:

- `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY`
- `ADJUSTED_ASSOCIATION`
- `PARTIALLY_IDENTIFIED`
- `OPE_SUPPORTED`
- `RANDOMIZED_CANARY`
- `NOT_IDENTIFIED`

Missing propensities, unsupported alternatives, post-outcome feature leakage, ill-defined
interventions, uncontrolled censoring or unresolved source identity force the study to a lower
honest grade. A doubly robust estimator does not manufacture positivity or eliminate unobserved
confounding.

## First consumer ruling

Do not begin with worker/model routing. Start with a compliant handoff-quality intervention whose
assignment can be prospective and whose effects are comparatively low-risk:

- baseline: current law-compliant operator handoff;
- candidate: the same required handoff plus a deterministic preflight validator and concise
  mission checksum naming mission, non-goals, proof target, stop condition and return packet;
- eligible cohort: bounded, reversible, routine/elevated, frozen-scope, capacity-selectable work;
- design: pre-registered blocked randomization where lawful, intention-to-treat analysis, terminal
  and delayed follow-up, explicit guardrails and rollback;
- primary outcome: first substantive return accepted without a handoff-attributable repair;
- guardrails: escaped defects, downstream rework, independent-review severity, effect-unknown,
  authority/duplicate-carrier defects, abandonment and cost/quota class.

No promotion exists until an independent reviewer accepts the study, a load-bearing consequence is
recorded as DSC, a separate DEC authorizes or rejects the policy delta, and the owning runtime proves
the canary/rollback path.

## No-rebuild boundary

Do not create an organizational outcome DB, universal experiment registry, worker leaderboard,
parallel identity/event/time/correction store, new lifecycle, queue, watcher, retry plane, routing
authority, hidden utility score or automatic policy editor. Do not ingest private chain of thought
or publish punitive individual rankings. Do not call a schema, dashboard, merged PR or descriptive
study an institutional learning loop.
