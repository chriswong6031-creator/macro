# Grounds and scope

This decision changes **execution routing**, not the frozen CN-Limit product,
scientific or authority architecture.

The controlling product law remains:

> CN-Limit estimates and explains rerating hazard. China Alpha Intelligence owns
> upstream professional/corporate evidence. Prophet remains the sole timing,
> admission, execution and current live-ranking authority.

The controlling system objective remains:

> Structural carrier × lawful mechanism evidence × transition/release ×
> sector/catalyst authorization × Prophet actionability × executable access.

A successful program may end in either:

- a prospectively certified challenger worthy of an A5 Sol/Chairman live-ordering
  decision; or
- a recorded kill/null showing that the tested constructions do not add enough
  value beyond Prophet under the frozen evidence bar.

Both are completion. Outcome-shopping is not.

## Course-change law

The R6 program is a dependency graph, not a rigid waterfall. At a wave boundary,
Fable may choose a different feasible next wave if it records:

```text
PLANNED FRONTIER:
NEW FACT:
WHY IT MATTERS:
DEPENDENCIES STILL TRUE:
FROZEN BOUNDARIES UNCHANGED:
NEW COURSE:
FALSIFIER / ACCEPTANCE BAR:
PRODUCTION PROOF OWED:
EXACT NEXT ACTION:
```

Examples of allowed COO course changes:

- an exact backfill is I/O-bound -> continue another truly disjoint owner-native
  research/product task while the campaign resumes in bounded windows;
- T2/T3/T4 share a candidate-writer collision -> serialize them instead of
  forcing parallel branches;
- one primary transition feature fails -> record the kill and continue the other
  preregistered primary features rather than changing the threshold;
- G6 is still accruing -> maintain the frozen challenger and work disjoint
  descriptive/product/coverage improvements without peeking or retuning;
- China Alpha exposes a stronger canonical owner output -> consume it through the
  owner seam and shrink CN-Limit code rather than duplicating the producer;
- a product explanation is useful while alpha is null -> keep it descriptive,
  zero-authority and clearly labeled; do not smuggle it into ordering.

A course change that crosses a mandatory return gate is not COO execution and
must come back to Sol.

## Milestone review cadence

No per-turn or per-PR Chairman report is required. Durable state is the report.
Fable returns to Sol at hard gates and at these milestone bundles:

1. **Exact Truth Release** — #6207 integrated, technical canary accepted, bulk
   readiness lawfully promoted, exact range campaign complete, sanitized
   completeness manifest proves DEP-EXACT.
2. **PIT Risk-Set Release** — DEP-ID-ELIG production-proven: survivorship-honest
   security-session eligibility plus time-valid membership/sector edges.
3. **Transition Anatomy Release** — primary I1A transition family projected
   prospectively through the canonical candidate seam with audit consumers and
   zero incumbent-order change.
4. **Measurement Shadow Release** — I1B sidecar + exact target/access/grade path
   and one frozen R4 battery produce advance/kill/accrue verdicts without a
   parallel grader/candidate population.
5. **Prospective Certification Release** — I1C-G6 reaches the frozen future-data
   floors under one keep-first challenger era and returns a certification or
   kill/accrual verdict.
6. **Ordering Shadow / A5 Decision** — only after G6 qualification, I2 fixed-set
   counterfactual ordering evidence is complete. Sol/Chairman then decide whether
   any bounded live ordering commission exists. Fable does not self-promote.

A milestone bundle must include all PR/merge/proof receipts since the prior
bundle, capability-state changes, killed/deferred hypotheses, exact definition
hashes/eras, production evidence, user/machine-consumer proof, unresolved risks,
and the next intended frontier.
