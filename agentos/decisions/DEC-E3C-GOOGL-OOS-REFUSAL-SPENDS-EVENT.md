# Continuation law

## E3-FMT — Transcript Format Generalization

E3-FMT is a development wave, not an OOS pass. Before the first compiler behavior change it must freeze a bounded development corpus and method contract. The corpus must exclude CAT, BAC and SNOW and may not use GOOGL as a success criterion. Prefer multiple already-held non-golden transcript formats plus minimal synthetic hostile fixtures only for discriminating grammar/identity properties; synthetic bodies never become production-source evidence.

The wave may generalize only the existing deterministic Q&A compiler/adapter and, if proven necessary, the existing transcript normalization owner. It must preserve source-span byte replay, event/revision binding, source-supported identity, explicit unresolved/null states, unknown transcript availability clocks, zero model authority, AAPL 7/26/68 regression, and no new store/registry/publisher/model router. A roleless-management rule must derive identity from independently source-supported transcript evidence rather than a name allowlist or issuer constant. Vendor-neutral boundary logic must be structural and fail closed; it may not encode GOOGL text, ticker, segment indexes or answer identities.

Completion of E3-FMT means the generic method is refrozen with RED-first format hostiles, at least two pre-declared real non-golden development transcripts exercising materially different role/boundary shapes where available, exact replay/identity/cross-event safety, full regression, exact-head CI and independent adversarial review. It does not publish GOOGL and does not complete E3-C.

## E3-OOS2 — Fresh Second-Issuer Retest

Only after E3-FMT is accepted and frozen may E3-OOS2 begin. Its source-selection operation must inspect CAT, then BAC, then SNOW and stop at the first current package meeting the original §11 completeness bar. The receipt and pass rule must land before extraction or model output is inspected. No issuer may be skipped because its source format looks difficult.

E3-OOS2 passes only with non-empty accepted qa_exchange.v1 through the refrozen generic method, canonical event_workspace.v1 publication, a real product consumer, accepted-unsupported=0, cross-event contamination=0, 100% accepted-span replay, correct identity collapse, correction/revision refusal, and truthful clock/rights behavior. Honest failure remains a receipt and keeps parent E3-C open. E3-P remains locked until parent E3-C closes.
