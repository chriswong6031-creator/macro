key: VALUE-AND-PERSONAL-ACT-PRECEDE-REGISTRATION
question: >
  What is the default ordering of the commercial front door — when may the product ask a
  visitor to create an account?
answer: >
  A genuine current intelligence experience and a personal act precede the registration
  request in the default journey. The initial preferred personal act is an anonymous
  Watchlist save; the third distinct symbol is the strongest initial prompt moment. The
  third-symbol trigger is a preregistered product hypothesis to be judged by the first
  30-day cohort, not a proven law. A visitor may always volunteer into signup or Pricing
  earlier; the product never forces it earlier.
rationale: >
  Registration before value measures willingness to fill a form, not product pull. A
  personal act creates something concretely worth preserving, which turns the free
  account into a continuity mechanism ("Save My Market") rather than a tollbooth. This
  also gives activation measurement an honest denominator: intelligence.viewed →
  personal.act → watchlist.saved → account.created is a joinable sequence, while
  banner-driven signups are not attributable to any experienced value.
alternatives:
  - option: Prompt for signup on first page load (status quo growth-banner pattern)
    why_not: >
      Maximizes account count while destroying the meaning of the activation metric and
      contradicting the frozen five-law product architecture (law 1 and 2).
  - option: Gate all intelligence behind registration
    why_not: >
      Kills the deep-linked public-intelligence acquisition unit; the public surfaces
      are PROVEN_LIVE and are the distribution asset.
  - option: Prompt at the first symbol add instead of the third
    why_not: >
      One symbol is curiosity, not a set worth preserving; the third distinct symbol is
      the hypothesized intent threshold — kept falsifiable via the preregistered cohort
      review rather than promoted to law now.
