A UI may be called **Market Belief**. That name never grants it storage or signal authority.

The composition must preserve owner, actor/channel, method, clock, scope, units/distribution,
coverage, rights, correction state and authority for every included observation. Historical belief
must remain as-known-at; later corrections are additive/superseding, not hindsight rewrites.
