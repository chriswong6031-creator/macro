## Promotion stages

- S0 deterministic/adversarial corpus.
- S1 frozen real-source snapshot.
- S2 point-in-time historical replay where source history is reconstructable.
- S3 prospective report-only multi-program shadow — strongest calibration evidence.
- S4 Control Room default compact composition after accepted S3.
- S5 any proactive delivery/routing change is out of scope and requires separate Wake-owned architecture/release.

## Hard non-negotiable gates

Promotion may not trade away priority-to-authority separation, complete independent interrupt
visibility, exact-only suppression, valid-wait fidelity, point-in-time source integrity or the
report-only no-mutation boundary. A real baseline is required before percentage targets, but those
zero-violation laws do not wait for calibration.
