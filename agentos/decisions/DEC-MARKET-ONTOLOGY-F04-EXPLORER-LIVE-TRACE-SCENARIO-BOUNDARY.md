## Observed-versus-assumed boundary

### LIVE_TRACE

Only current or historical owner facts satisfying their clocks, rights, correction and identity contracts may appear as observations.

### SCENARIO

A scenario is an explicit assumption with node, definition, magnitude, unit, horizon, path shape, origin class, effective as-of and knowledge cutoff. It is evaluated without mutating TXI episodes/state, GMI beliefs, Portfolio/Watchlist, owner calibration, alerts, Prophet or trades.

A scenario may yield compatible owner-model estimate, same-window empirical sensitivity, historical transition/outcome context, threshold state, hypothesis mechanism or typed unavailability. It may not invent a point estimate because the UI expects one.

## Path arithmetic

A path-level combined numerical effect requires one accepted owner explicitly modeling the full joint path/dependence. F04 may not multiply arbitrary hop coefficients, mix horizons or sum unlike units. Without a joint owner, show hop evidence and `no_combined_estimate_reason`.

## WTI reference law

The first vertical uses the current WTI chain exactly as emitted. At the archaeology base the root oil threshold and breakeven hop are false while downstream real-yield and QQQ-relative conditions are true. The path remains dormant. Downstream observations do not prove the upstream oil mechanism.

Historical hop frequencies display rising-edge episode numerator/N/span/regime under
`HISTORICAL_TRANSITION_RATE`, never confidence. When no valid null comparator exists, show
`baseline_comparison: unavailable`. The terminal cohort event study remains separate: its
current N is overlapping per-bar cohort N, not independent episode N; effective N, overlap
treatment, interval, span and concentration remain typed unavailable until the owner emits
them. An unresolved current regime is `CURRENT_REGIME_UNAVAILABLE`, never an inferred cell.
