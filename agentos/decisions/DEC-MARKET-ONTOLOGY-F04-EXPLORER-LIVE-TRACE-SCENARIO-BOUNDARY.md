# Binding F04 Explorer ruling

## One parent, one product composition owner

The operation `marketontology-f04-ontology-transmission-20260826-fable-001` remains the parent. No new Market Ontology program, F04 workstream or rival graph is created.

The explorer may advance independently only when its exact child scope is genuinely disjoint from held owner paths. Every child performs a fresh current-main/open-PR/active-runtime collision census before writing.

## Product route

- `/ontology.html` is the multi-owner interactive workspace.
- `/transmission.html` remains the rates/inflation/TXI owner product.
- Owner surfaces may deep-link into the explorer and the explorer links back to exact source desks.
- The HTML shell follows existing public-shell policy; current analytical payload follows existing paid `site_full` enforcement.
- No new plan tier or entitlement key is introduced.

## Observed-versus-assumed boundary

### LIVE_TRACE

Only current or historical owner facts that satisfy their own clocks, rights, correction and identity contracts may appear as observations.

### SCENARIO

A scenario is an explicit assumption with node, definition, magnitude, unit, horizon, path shape, origin class, effective as-of and knowledge cutoff. It is evaluated without mutating:

- TXI episodes or chain state;
- GMI beliefs;
- Portfolio or Watchlist;
- owner calibration ledgers;
- alerts, Prophet candidates or trades.

A scenario may yield:

- a compatible owner-model estimate;
- a same-window empirical sensitivity;
- a historical transition rate or outcome distribution;
- a threshold satisfied/failed/unknown state;
- hypothesis mechanism context;
- typed unavailability.

It may not yield an invented point estimate merely because the UI expects one.

## Path arithmetic

A path-level combined numerical effect requires one accepted owner that explicitly models the full joint path and its dependence. F04 may not multiply arbitrary hop coefficients, mix horizons or sum unlike units. When no joint owner exists, show hop-level evidence and `no_combined_estimate_reason`.

## WTI reference law

The first vertical uses the current WTI chain exactly as emitted. At the archaeology base the root oil threshold and breakeven hop are false while downstream real-yield and QQQ-relative conditions are true. The path therefore remains dormant. F04 must expose that downstream observations do not prove the upstream oil mechanism.

Historical hop frequencies may be shown with N/span/regime under `HISTORICAL_TRANSITION_RATE`. They are not named `confidence`. The terminal cohort event study remains separate and may contradict the mechanism without being hidden.

May proceed before D2C/K3-D acceptance:

- TXI/rates-only Live Trace;
- WTI current/historical context;
- explicit scenario-assumption contract;
- existing rate-sensitivity scenario;

- response models whose source/method owner does not yet exist.
