# AI Hub H1A preserves dated facts, not independent action permission

This records the rationale of Sol's
[immutable-head ruling 5124093204](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/Mastermind/pull/424#pullrequestreview-5124093204).
It is organizational memory, not an execution gate, runtime lease, dispatch,
source-writer assignment or permission grant. Current protected procedure and
the exact live carrier still govern later action.

## Product and owner boundaries

AI Hub extends the existing Chairman Control Room and Project Workroom architecture.
Its durable owner remains
[WS:CHAIRMAN-CONTROL-ROOM](../workstreams/WS-CHAIRMAN-CONTROL-ROOM.md).
Do not create a parallel AI Hub workstream, compositor, cache, lifecycle or
permission system.

At this decision's source observation, H1A is **NOT_BUILT / HELD**. It is scoped
to provide one useful operational orientation, with an eventual acceptance
ceiling of PARTIAL, not the complete flagship Workroom. H1B is the separately held
outcome/capability/accepted-proof slice; H2 actions remain held. This preserves
the full product outcome while keeping the first implementation bounded. See the
[source-completeness ruling](https://github.com/mastermindx-market-intelligence/Mastermind/pull/424#pullrequestreview-5107389361).

## Exact compatibility choice

Preserve H1A's closed top-level keys, operations allowlist, one canonical source
snapshot, and exactly three strategic truth gaps: objective, capability_state
and latest_accepted_proof, with SOURCE_FIELD_NOT_PROJECTED.
CURRENT_PERMISSION_NOT_PROJECTED belongs in the existing sorted degraded list;
it is not a fourth strategic truth-gap row.

Source-reported freshness, actionability, decision requirement, worker, owed turn,
dispatch, blocker and next action remain attributed to canonical document time.
The UI must identify them as observation facts, not freshly evaluated permission.
Their values must not independently produce LIVE, YOUR CALL, an actionable CTA
or an enabled owed-open control.

The new source-only validity object is not exported as an arbitrary mapping.
Its presence also must not null an otherwise valid operational card. Valid,
absent, malformed, zero, expired and wrong-proof/publication validity cases must
preserve useful allowed facts without adding current-action authority.

Do not copy B5's clock, proof map or browser state, merge H1A's observation with
the base module's independently fetched state, widen the nine H1A source paths,
or alter the shared Control Room JavaScript owner. H1A's bounded GET follow-up
timer observes acquisition only; it is not a permission clock. A future
current-permission consumer needs an explicitly reviewed existing-owner interface,
not a private reconstruction.
