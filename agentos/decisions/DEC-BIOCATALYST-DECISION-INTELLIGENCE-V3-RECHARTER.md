## Supersession boundary

This decision supersedes the **top-level product interpretation** that the phrase
"no score, probability, materiality, rank, or composite anywhere in any BioCatalyst
payload or UI" permanently forbids all separately governed intelligence outputs.
It does not retroactively change the accepted P1-1 Trial Milestones payload, which
remains deterministic facts-and-context only. It does not authorize a score inside
that source-fact contract. It also does not supersede:

- `DNR:KILL-PHASE3-START-WEIGHT` on the tested construction;
- the rule that registry completion dates are not readout announcements;
- point-in-time, source-clock, correction and anti-backdating laws;
- canonical owner boundaries for SEC/IR disclosures, company/security identity,
  financial facts, capital structure, market data, options, alerts, Eval/Fusion or
  Prophet;
- rights, entitlement, evidence-projection and private-location protections;
- the requirement for prospective evidence before any rank/gate/size/trade authority.

R0 must translate this ruling into exact contracts, owner ports, product states,
statistical methods, failure states and bounded implementation handoffs. Until R0 is
Sol-accepted, this decision grants **no runtime implementation, merge, deployment,
model promotion or trade authority**.

## Fable principal boundary

Fable's takeover means the Chairman should no longer have to babysit the program,
reconstruct its state or shuttle routine messages. It does not mean one immortal
provider session or one unbounded modifying operation. Fable maintains the durable
program thread, creates bounded child commissions, reviews their returns against the
frozen architecture, updates Agent OS and returns decisive architecture/authority or
acceptance gates to Sol. Every child still requires its own operation identity,
carrier, pickup, start, proof and terminal STOP.
