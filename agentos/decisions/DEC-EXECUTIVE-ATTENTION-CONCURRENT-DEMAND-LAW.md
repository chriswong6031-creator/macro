## Operational consequence

EAF's compact service selection applies only after exact fan-in and may compact ordinary FOCUS/BATCH
work. It never drops an independent interrupt. Control Room and Chat-native projections must show the
complete interrupt-root set plus a concurrent-demand summary.

A source-backed `PROVEN_WINDOW_COLLISION` is a truth statement about current facts, not permission to
reassign authority, dispatch workers, wake more sessions or mutate lifecycle. When feasibility facts
are absent, the correct result is `FEASIBILITY_UNKNOWN`.
