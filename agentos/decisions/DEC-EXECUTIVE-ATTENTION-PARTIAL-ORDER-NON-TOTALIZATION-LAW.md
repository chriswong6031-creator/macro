## Service consequence

The compact surface may show one unique focus only when grounded relations justify one. If multiple
items are genuinely non-dominated/incomparable, show the smallest truthful tradeoff set. Context
batching may service equivalent ordinary candidates, but every deferred candidate receives an exact
receipt and no independent interrupt can be hidden.
