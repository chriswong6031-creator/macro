## Scope

This decision freezes product and authority architecture only. It does not freeze a
strategy wire schema, washout formula, asset-class threshold, leverage rule, first proving
universe, parameter, model, product layout, or promotion verdict. Those require the
bounded current-owner research and implementation waves in
`research/prophet_v4/PROPHET_STRATEGY_PLATFORM_AND_CYCLE_CAPTURE_ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE_2026-08-30.md`.

## Narrow supersession

This decision narrows any reading of the current V4 program that treats tactical early
rotation as Prophet's sole future strategy. It does not supersede the V4 planes, D5
contract, B1 candidate identity, B2/B3/B4 sequence, TOI two-queue law, Radar ownership,
GMI ownership, Fusion promotion law, Evaluation OS, or the frozen V3 same-tape control.
