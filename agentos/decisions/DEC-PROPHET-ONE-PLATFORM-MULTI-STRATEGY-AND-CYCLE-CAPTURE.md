## Scope

This decision freezes product and authority architecture only. It does not freeze a
strategy wire schema, formula, market, threshold, model, hold period, exit rule,
leverage rule, product layout or promotion verdict. Those require the bounded current-owner
research and implementation waves in
`research/prophet_v4/PROPHET_STRATEGY_PLATFORM_AND_CYCLE_CAPTURE_ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE_2026-08-30.md`.

## Narrow supersession

This decision narrows any interpretation of the V4 program that makes tactical early
rotation Prophet's sole future strategy. It does not supersede the V4 six-plane law,
B1 candidate identity, D5 contract, B2/B3/B4 sequence, TOI two-queue law, Radar/GMI/
Earnings/Stock Identity/Evaluation ownership, Conditional Fusion promotion law or the
frozen V3 same-tape control.

## Binding negative evidence

The broader founding strategy is not authority to retry killed technical constructions.
In particular:

- `DNR:KILL-WASHOUT-TURN` remains terminal for the exact two-week Washout x Turn seed;
- `DNR:KILL-ROTATION-CYCLE-CONFLUENCE` keeps Early Rotation and Cycle Capture separate;
- `DNR:KILL-REGIME-SCORECARD` prevents a fused market-regime scorecard/router;
- `DNR:KILL-FUSED-COMPOSITE` prevents one opaque opportunity number;
- `DNR:KILL-PROPHET-POP-MERGE` prevents trigger-lane contamination of the graded board;
- `DNR:KILL-LLM-ORIGINATION` prevents model-originated signals, ranks, sizes or escalations;
- `DNR:KILL-OFFHORIZON-VERDICTS` requires all outcome claims to use registered horizons.

A future species may proceed only if it is genuinely different, preregistered, owner-compatible,
and evaluated on lawful untouched or prospective evidence.
