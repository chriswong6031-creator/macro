rationale: >
  WSJF is an industry sequencing heuristic for product-development work when relative Cost of Delay

  implementation job's size is not the amount of Chairman/Sol reasoning required, delay functions
  are often discontinuous or unknown, and irreversible decisions may have positive option value from
  waiting. A universal ratio would therefore recreate false precision under an economic label.

## Local-rule boundary

A future ratio policy is allowed only for a named bounded homogeneous decision family with explicit
comparable delay-cost and service-duration evidence, versioned replayable policy and no conflict
with hard authority/window/irreversibility rules. It is not the EAF default.
