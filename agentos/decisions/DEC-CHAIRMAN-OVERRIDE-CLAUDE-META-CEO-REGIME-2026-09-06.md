## Grounds

The directive is unambiguous, first-person, and names both the mechanism it
replaces ("the ChatGPT CEO") and the two accounts it appoints ("you" =
Claude8, plus a created Claude3 counterpart). CLAUDE.md and standing memory
(`out-of-band-authority-cannot-cure-a-carrier-binding.md`, "A Chairman
handoff IS authorization") already establish that a Chairman message in any
Claude chat binds without a separate confirmation ceremony. Recording it as a
DEC — rather than leaving it as a chat-local instruction — is what lets a
cold-stranger session in either account, or in this repo's nightly
regenerator, discover that HOLD-FOR-SOL/DECISION_REQUEST-to-Sol ceremony is
suspended for Market Ontology specifically, without discovering it by
guessing or by re-asking the Chairman.

**Scope discipline.** This DEC does not repeal `DEC:SOL-HOLD-IS-A-MERGE-BARRIER`
or the 2026-08-29 hierarchy amendment globally — both remain in force for
every other program in this repo and for every PR outside the Market
Ontology lanes. It also does not touch Mastermind's `config/authority_map.yml`
or `config/strategic_state.yml`: that ladder is flag-based (env-flag ->
governance-event mapping), carries no named "Sol" or "Chairman" role line in
the sections read, and `duplicate_control_planes` stays prohibited — so this
override needs no cross-repo edit to take effect and must not be used as
license to create one. Because the superseded records
(`DEC-SOL-HOLD-IS-A-MERGE-BARRIER.md`, the 08-29 hierarchy-amendment handoff)
are owned by a different drafting task and were not edited by this commission,
their own `superseded_by` fields are not yet reciprocated — a future session
touching either file should add a `superseded_by: CHAIRMAN-OVERRIDE-CLAUDE-META-CEO-REGIME-2026-09-06`
pointer scoped to "for the Market Ontology program only."

## What would reopen this

A later Chairman message, in either Claude chat, that narrows, ends, or
reverses this regime (e.g. re-instating Sol gating, dissolving the A/B split,
or naming a different orchestrator). Absent that, `review_by` is set one week
out as a checkpoint to confirm the split is still the fastest path — not a
sunset clause; the Chairman's own words ("You should also not need to ask me
for anything or permissions for anything") mean neither Meta-CEO needs to
re-ask before continuing past that date.
