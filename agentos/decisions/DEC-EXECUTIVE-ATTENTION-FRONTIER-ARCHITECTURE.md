## Frozen mechanism

The controlling pipeline is:

```text
explicit valid attention demand
-> canonical authority requirement
-> source-backed attention pressure
-> independent action serviceability
-> exact canonical-root / projection relation
-> source-backed factor vectors
-> genuine partial-order frontier
-> deterministic local context/fairness service
-> read-only Control Room / Chat-native CEO projection
```

Closed V1 **attention classes** are:

- `INTERRUPT_NOW`
- `FOCUS_NOW`
- `BATCH_NEXT`
- `AUTONOMOUS_CONTINUE`
- `VALID_WAIT`
- `NON_ACTIONABLE`

Independent **serviceability** states are `READY`, `BLOCKED`, `UNKNOWN` and `NOT_APPLICABLE`, with
typed source-backed reasons. `RECONCILE_FIRST` may survive as human copy but is not an exclusive
pressure class; reconciliation/target/effect defects live on the serviceability axis so an urgent-
but-blocked demand remains visible. `COVERED_BY_BUNDLE`, `DOMINATED_BY`, equivalent-context deferral
and ordinary-service deferral are projection relations, not pressure classes.

These are derived read projections only. They grant no authority and never become Executive OS or
Agent OS lifecycle states.

## Authority and source law

Authority is resolved before any pressure factor. A high-pressure Sol item remains Sol. A Chairman-
only item remains Chairman-only even when it is a valid wait. Unknown/conflicted authority does not
escalate upward. `needs_ceo`, urgency text, age, dependency fan-out, resource burn, economic delay and
model output may never confer Chairman authority.

EAF admits only explicit valid attention demand from accepted source-owner contracts. It may display
prose as context after admission but may not parse Markdown/next_action text to manufacture demand,
authority, deadlines, impact, waits or urgency.

The protected Executive Steward is the canonical pure federated read seam. EAF consumes caller-
supplied source-attributed results and owns no gatherer, identity resolver, persistence or source
correction plane.

## Service law

Every independent `INTERRUPT_NOW` root remains visible after exact fan-in; no quota, top-N, model,
context preference or interruption-reduction target may hide one. Multiple independent interrupts
prove congestion, not overload, unless source-backed windows and service/capacity facts prove a
collision.

Among ordinary backlog, context/exact-root batching can reduce switching cost. At least one oldest
source-backed ready eligible item remains visible as a fairness sentinel per active executive
authority partition when backlog exists. Valid future waits do not age into false urgency. If ready
age is not source-backed, fairness age is unknown rather than stored in a new allocator ledger.

## Promotion law

Initial implementation is deterministic and report-only. It may not mutate Wake delivery, Executive
lifecycle, placement, action targets or notification behavior. Promotion requires adversarial
semantics review plus point-in-time real multi-program evidence showing no missed accepted interrupts,
no priority-driven authority escalation, truthful degraded states, preserved valid waits, exact
omission receipts and materially reduced executive scanning/interruption.
