## Concurrency rules

- F01-F13 keep their existing unique operation keys and durable handoffs. They are
  organizational lane/work identities, not a seat/account registry.
- A lane may progress without unrelated lanes or a Sol round-trip only when an actual
  governed receiver/execution path exists and its owner/dependency/collision census is
  clean. Slack delivery, membership or provider login is not execution proof.
- Multiple modifications may execute simultaneously across disjoint canonical owners,
  but each modifying child keeps one stable operation/carrier until reconciliation.
- Under production-proven Executive routing, subordinate concurrency uses ordinary
  accepted Jobs/Attempts/workers beneath the logical F00/root responsibility.
- Ordinary unbound new bounded work is CAPACITY_SELECTABLE and WAITING_CAPACITY /
  needs_placement. PREFERRED_AVENUE is guidance only. Do not emit broad OPEN_PICKUP or
  request routine Chairman account selection merely because automatic placement is not live.
- A current live explicit manual directive/delegation may establish a concrete receiver or
  CHAIRMAN_SELECTS exception. Before START, PRESTART_REBIND preserves the same operation,
  carrier, scope and logical responsibility. After START, receiver binding is sticky;
  EFFECT_UNKNOWN must reconcile before any retry/failover.
- Bounded work uses the least-scarce capable avenue. Record WHY FABLE when principal-level
  ambiguity/continuity/integration genuinely requires Fable; otherwise preserve WHY NOT FABLE
  and use a lower-scarcity capable avenue for architecture-frozen work.
- Reciprocal continuation watchers are temporary attention/transport only. A watcher owns
  no Job/Attempt/Worker/Event state, execution, retry, completion or authority. When a
  watcher detects an already-authorized return, current protected action-loop law requires
  Sol to re-pin, adjudicate and post the lawful same-carrier edge before reporting.
- F00 continuously reconciles complete-parity coverage and prevents omissions, duplicate
  owners and cross-lane collisions; it does not rewrite specialist truth or become a
  serial approval queue.
- Complete-parity closure remains granular: retained P1 1,556 capability rows + 460 quality
  findings + authenticated 88/88 paid targets + living public deltas + accepted Terra
  preservation recensus + later validated deltas. Priority controls sequencing, never
  inclusion; compression may not lose source-row traceability; REJECTED_BY_DESIGN must
  preserve the lawful underlying job through a stronger equivalent or adjudicated impossibility.
- Sol review remains mandatory at the executive gates frozen by the program and at
  milestone/final acceptance, not after every reversible implementation step.
- K2-C and K3-D retain their separate operation/carrier law and are not F04/F10 substitutes.
  K5 remains separately held until their separate acceptance gates clear.
- No parity, topology or routing record grants rank/gate/size/origination/ENTRY_OPEN/Prophet/trade authority.

## Capacity fallback

If governed subordinate capacity is lower than the number of runnable lanes, do not invent
more Fable seats, pin lanes to accounts, create broad OPEN_PICKUP, or collapse the frozen lane
identities. Keep eligible unbound work explicitly WAITING_CAPACITY / needs_placement, prioritize
highest-value collision-free bounded work, select the least-scarce capable PREFERRED_AVENUE,
and consume a concrete manual receiver only when current live explicit authority actually
binds one. Provider/account/session rollover never renames the durable Fxx lane.
