## Representative real cases

### Breathing Platform

`owner: ceo-sol` coexists with an explicit placement debt described as `WAITING_CAPACITY /
needs_placement`. The durable accountable owner is not the same thing as the current cognition-turn
owner. EAF must consume current placement/turn facts and never turn that debt into routine Chairman
account scheduling.

### Account Identity Hardening

A blocked user-facing workstream says `Decide the CI gate fix` in `next_action` while its owner is
`terminal-platform`. The verb `Decide` and `blast_radius: user_facing` do not establish executive
authority or current user harm.

### Advanced Data Options

The workstream prose says `Sol commissioning of AD-1T2`, while its old options-source `needs_ceo`
gate is explicitly retired and no replacement structured `needs_ceo` block is present in the
observed YAML. EAF must not resurrect a retired gate by semantic text matching.

## Implementation law

The safe V1 may output many `UNKNOWN` factor values. It may not guess them. Missing optional data
makes items less comparable; missing load-bearing identity/authority/effect truth requires
reconciliation. Real shadow evaluation must separately report source coverage so product calmness
cannot be achieved by silently failing to admit valid attention demand.
