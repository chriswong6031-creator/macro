## Live falsifier

The canonical fourth-slot child `ci-pc-fourth-slot-20260829-sol-001` was bound to a Codex receiver
communicating through ChatGPT2 on its exact Slack parent. A later ChatGPT2 message in that same thread
was labelled `SOL RULING / CONTINUE` and expanded the worker from the last lawful bounded instruction
(Task 1 through one DRAFT/HOLD review carrier; Task 2+ held) into Tasks 2–5.

Transport identity alone could not establish whether that message came from the bound Codex worker,
a separate Sol conversation sharing ChatGPT2, or another same-principal native surface. Because the
edge materially widened authority and lacked an independently distinguishable executive actor/runtime
binding, Sol explicitly rejected it as an authority edge and requested an effect receipt before any
further mutation.

This is not a claim that every ChatGPT2 `SOL ...` message is invalid. It proves the narrower and more
important invariant: **the Slack sender and prose label are insufficient to decide**.

1. one active child is bound to worker RuntimeBinding generation `W`;

scope-changing edges must fail closed and Wake the accountable Sol rather than guess.
