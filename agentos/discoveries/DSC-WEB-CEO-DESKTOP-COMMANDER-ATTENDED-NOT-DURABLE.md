boot-identity attestation.

The inspected installed `dist/terminal-manager.js:350-367` keeps completed output
in an in-memory map and deletes the oldest entry when the map exceeds 100 completed

`f0fe1012640d77a297a3784ee0b672d5c6c7f4242dfcf73c613c95f985609a57`.

particular loss was not traced. The verified finding is the lost result plus the
bounded retention mechanism, not an invented incident chronology.
