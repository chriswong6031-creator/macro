BASES PLAYBOOK — sideways is where the next move gets built.
Definitions (all verifiable in the digest): a base = a sideways shelf after a real move — swings compress (each smaller than the last), a floor forms (a support level gaining touches), volatility drifts toward "quiet". Describe shapes by their skeleton, not just a nickname: flat shelf (level floor, level ceiling) · cup (falling swings that flatten, then climb back to the old ceiling) · double bottom (two lows on the same shelf with a bounce between) · handle (a small, shallow drift in the top third after the right side forms).
Validity conditions — it is only a base when: it follows a real prior move; it has lasted weeks, not days (count bars in the digest's trend segments); the floor is defended (multiple touches, side support); and the swings CONTRACT. Widening swings = a battleground, not a base.
Procedure:
1) chart_digest → identify the ceiling (the resistance capping the range) and the floor. The ceiling is the breakout line — draw.hline or draw.zone, one plain caption.
2) Grade the base: touches on the floor, contraction in swing sizes, volatility context ("quiet" = coiled). Position in the larger trend matters most: a shelf sitting ON TOP of a prior advance, above old resistance, outranks a base carved at the lows.
3) The trigger is a decisive CLOSE through the ceiling. The retest that holds the ceiling-turned-floor is the second — often better — entry. A close back INSIDE the base after a breakout = failed breakout; flip the read, the trapped buyers are fuel.
4) Cross-check the stage tools: a base resolving upward from Stage 1 into Stage 2 (get_stage_peers will show it) is the textbook version. A "base" under a falling weekly average is usually just a pause in a downtrend — say so.
Invalidation: the base thesis dies on a close below the floor; the breakout thesis dies on a close back inside the range.
Honesty: never quote pattern odds. If asked where it could go, project the range height as "a typical objective, not a promise".
Worked shape: eight weeks sideways between 47 and 52 after a run from 30; floor touched four times; volatility quiet. "A coiled shelf on top of a big run — constructive. 52 is the line: a real close through it starts the next leg. 47 is where the idea is simply wrong. Until 52 breaks, there is nothing to chase."
