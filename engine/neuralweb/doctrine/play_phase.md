PHASE PLAYBOOK — who is filling, who is unloading. Read it from behavior at levels, in our own terms.
Premise: size cannot buy or sell all at once, so it leaves footprints inside ranges: repeated defenses of a floor with shrinking downside progress (absorption — likely accumulation), or repeated failures at a ceiling with heavy trade and no upside progress (likely distribution). These are INFERENCES — grade them "likely", never certainties.
Tell-tale sequences (all checkable against the digest's swings and levels):
- Shakeout: the range floor gets pierced, then price closes back inside quickly — the same or next bar — and rallies away. The failed break flushes weak holders and traps shorts — one of the strongest single tells that a range resolves UP. The mirror image at a ceiling — a poke above that fails — marks distribution.
- Higher lows pressing INTO a flat ceiling = pressure building upward; buyers keep stepping in earlier. Lower highs sagging onto a flat floor = the opposite.
- After the range resolves: the trend and structure lenses take over.
Procedure:
1) chart_digest → find the range (flat ceiling and floor, multiple touches each). Walk the touch sequence in time order; look for the failed-break bar, and whether swing lows inside the range are rising or falling.
2) Confirm with the desk's tools rather than lore: get_stage_peers — an accumulation story is credible when the engine has the name basing (Stage 1) or early in an advance; a distribution story matches its topping read (Stage 3). If the engine disagrees with your phase story, lead with the engine.
3) Volume character grades the key bars: absorption at the floor supports accumulation; heavy churn at the ceiling supports distribution. Volume alone proves neither.
4) Mark only the range boundaries and the failed-break bar (draw.zone plus draw.marker). The story goes in one caption, not ten objects.
Invalidation: an accumulation read dies on a decisive close below the range floor — the shakeout that keeps falling was just a breakdown. Distribution dies on a real close above the ceiling.
Worked shape: floor at 88 pierced once to 86.40 with a recovery back above 88 the same week, then higher lows into 94. "Someone bought that flush — the break under 88 lasted a day and reversed, and every dip since has bottomed higher. That's absorption, not weakness. Above 94 it's confirmed; a real close under 88 kills the story."
