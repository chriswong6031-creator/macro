CROSS-ASSET LENS — a story that only explains one asset class is a caption, not an explanation.
Law: before you commit to a cause, walk the five: equities, rates, the dollar, commodities, vol/credit. The packet TAPE block carries the walk; a real driver leaves a consistent signature across most of it.
Signatures worth knowing cold:
- Oil up + long yields up + breakevens up + energy the only green sector → supply-side oil shock. The same oil rally WITH yields calm reads as demand strength instead — direction alone never identifies the shock.
- Dollar up + gold up together → genuine fear (both safety bids); dollar up + gold down → a rates/carry story, not panic.
- VIX up modestly on a −2% day → orderly repricing; VIX exploding + credit spreads gapping → forced-flow territory, treat every price as suspect.
- Crypto tracking the Nasdaq → it's trading as risk, not as a haven; say which mode it's in today, not which mode its fans claim.
- Credit calm while equities fall → valuation/discount-rate repricing, usually multiple compression from the rates side; credit widening WITH equities → the market questions solvency, a heavier regime.
Procedure:
1) Run the five-asset walk and score your candidate story: which legs does it explain, which does it leave over?
2) Name the leftovers explicitly. One clean contradiction ("gold should be up in that story — it's down") demotes a story from diagnosis to hypothesis; say what you'd need to see to restore it.
3) A second, independent driver is common — a macro shock plus one sector's own story (an earnings bomb, a capex scare). Don't force one cause to carry two moves; two half-stories that each fit their half beat one grand story that fits neither.
Invalidation of this lens itself: on a one-asset day (a single sector or name moving on its own news, tape quiet elsewhere) skip the walk — say it's idiosyncratic and analyze the name.
Worked shape: "The oil-shock story explains stocks down, long yields up, energy green, staples flat. It does NOT explain semis down 4% — twice the market. That's a second story running: the AI-capex repricing on the wire, riding the same red tape."
