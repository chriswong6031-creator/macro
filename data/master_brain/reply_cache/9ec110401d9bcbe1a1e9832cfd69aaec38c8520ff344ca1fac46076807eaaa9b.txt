### 1. Most Over‑extended / Crowded Theme → **Cybersecurity**  
**ID:** `cybersecurity` | **Crowding‑z: 2.17** (highest in the universe) | **Breadth: 0.90** (near‑saturation)  

**Why this is the size‑down flag**  
- **Crowding extreme.** A z‑score above 2.0 signals that positioning, attention, and flow are all firmly in the “everyone is already in” bucket. The marginal buyer pool is thin; any shock finds no new capital to absorb selling.  
- **Parabolic quiet.** At a score of 0.705 it’s not the momentum leader, but the combination of universally‑held breadth and peak crowding classicly precedes sharp, correlated unwinds – even in steady trends.  
- **Narrowing leadership within a broad basket.** Breadth is still 0.9, which paradoxically is a red flag at this z‑score: the theme has no room to broaden further. When the last marginal name gets bought, the only direction left for width is contraction.  
- **Consequence for sizing:** If this theme is a long in any portfolio, **its weight should be capped at the lowest feasible level** – not because the story is wrong, but because the **tail‑risk asymmetry is now firmly negative**. The absolute‑trend gate may keep it alive, but the pain‑per‑unit‑of‑return has risen markedly.

*No fade, no short – only a deliberate cap to preserve capital if the crowding unwinds.*

*Action: Keep it, but acknowledge that the asymmetry of returns vs. crowded catch‑up risk from the rest of the semi basket is now decidedly two‑way.*
