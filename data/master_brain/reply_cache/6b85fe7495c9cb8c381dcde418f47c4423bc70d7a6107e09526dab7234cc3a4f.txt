- **Absorption is low** — The market is not efficiently digesting flows into a dominant narrative; leadership can rotate quickly. This reduces conviction in any single theme’s staying power.

Why not outright defensive:
- **Gate_helps is true** — The absolute-trend discipline has a validated edge in controlling left-tail risk on the TSX. That means trend-following, vol-aware exposure to the leading theme(s) is a risk-managed way to participate.

- **Rotation is broad, not degenerate** — A broad eligible set (0.62) suggests the market is not broken; it’s rotating healthily across sectors. This is a typical “stock picker’s” backdrop, not a universal sell-everything signal.

**Net call:** The regime permits **small, strictly gated risk-on exposure** to the leadership theme(s) — but **position size must be below baseline**, and cash must be materially higher than in a high-absorption, one-narrative regime. Use the absolute-trend gate to enforce drawdown limits; do not let any exposure run un-gated.
