The sheer extreme of the Z‑score (north of 1.7) is itself a caution flag: positioning is one‑sided; any hiccup in the rate, credit, or regulatory backdrop that has driven the move can trigger a rapid unwind. For a trend‑following allocation, this is the strongest‑possible signal to down‑size — not because the trend must break, but because the payoff‑to‑risk is skewed against adding here.

**Sizing caution, not a fade**
The discipline’s sole validated edge is drawdown control via the absolute‑trend gate, and the gate‑helps flag is true for this market — it does cut risk. So the correct response to crowding is always a size‑down, never a reversal bet. Fading the leader is the documented failure mode; reflexivity means the most crowded theme can continue melting up beyond any rational analyst’s scare‑lines. Undersize, don’t fight.
