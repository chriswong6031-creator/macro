2. **Geopolitical supply‑chain concentration** – HBM production is dominated by a few Korean fabs. Escalating US‑China tech sanctions or a Korean peninsula shock would strangle the supply narrative overnight.

**Sizing implication:** The theme is leader, not crowded, so a modest overweight is tenable. But **size it below the point where a 15–20% drawdown would violate risk limits**, and honor the absolute‑trend gate without hesitation.

**Falsifiable conditional:** If the absolute‑trend signal flips to “off” or durability drops below 0.55, the long is no longer sustainable regardless of score—exit.
