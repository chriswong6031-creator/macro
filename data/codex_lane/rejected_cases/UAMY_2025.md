# UAMY 2025 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

UAMY's 2025 alpha came from a rare three-part re-underwriting: realized antimony pricing lifted a tiny operating base (first-half revenue +160% year over year and gross margin to 30%), China's export controls made North American refining capacity strategically scarce, and the September 23 publication of a five-year, sole-source DLA contract with a **maximum** value of $245 million made that scarcity legible as a government-backed order pipeline ([SEC filing, 2025-09-23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101538/000165495425011011/uamy_8k.htm); [Reuters, 2025-09-23](https://www.reuters.com/markets/commodities/us-antimony-corp-wins-245-million-pentagon-contract-build-defense-stockpile-2025-09-23/)). The move failed to sustain because price capitalized the contract ceiling and future mine/smelter scale much faster than reported earnings could: the September 30 first delivery order was only about $10 million, third-quarter revenue was $8.70 million with a $4.78 million net loss, and a rapid series of ATM/direct offerings expanded supply just as the stock went vertical. This was therefore not merely sector beta, but neither was it a durable earnings-led rerating: it was a policy-scarcity and contract-ceiling rerating whose time horizon escaped both the near-term financial model and the share-supply constraint.

**Format-contract status: this is a failed breakaway case; the run did not sustain.**

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — possible.** The policy narrative, small starting base, 36.9% single-day jump, and climactic turnover are compatible with speculative reflexivity, but the DLA contract and reported revenue growth were substantive public facts.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The rerating accumulated through Q1, Q2, DLA award, first delivery order, and financing events; it was not one isolated session.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** At t0 UAMY exceeded XME by 30.26 pp over 21d and 66.60 pp over 42d. By year-end UAMY's 63d return was -33.51% while XME's was +8.84%.
- **options_mirage — possible.** There is no 2025 options history; current data show a thin, high-IV chain, so options cannot be credited as a durable mechanism.
- **Current extension hazard — possible.** The 2025 vertical was decisively reset, but the current $5.46 snapshot still carries 112.2% IV30 and a 7.1% modeled daily move.
- **Current call-wall hazard — unknown.** The latest surface identifies no call wall; absence in a thin chain is not evidence of clear overhead.
- **Catalyst exhaustion — likely for the failed 2025 leg.** The maximum contract headline, initial order, guidance, and successive financings were all public by mid-October; subsequent financials exposed the longer conversion timeline.
