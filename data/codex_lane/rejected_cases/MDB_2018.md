# MDB 2018 — failed-breakaway winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

MDB's March 2018 alpha was not one headline or simple software beta: a newly public, thinly seasoned stock was being re-underwritten from “open-source database vendor with losses” into a cloud platform with two reinforcing engines—Atlas had reached 11% of quarterly revenue while growing more than 500% year over year, and the promised MongoDB 4.0 multi-document ACID capability weakened a central objection to using MongoDB for mission-critical workloads. The March 13 results supplied revenue growth, customer evidence, product proof and forward guidance at once; the tape then detached from both SPY and the IGV software proxy on sharply expanded dollar volume. But the March 26 onset came after a vertical move, without a compressed base, and two weeks after the hard information arrived. Liquidity confirmed attention, not durable price acceptance: the close fell 22.1% in 21 sessions and underperformed SPY by 21.54 percentage points. Later earnings, the 4.0 launch and faster Atlas mix growth rebuilt the thesis and produced separate 2018 breakaways, but they do not retroactively make the March episode clean.

**Case state: failed breakaway. The run did not sustain; the initial March 26 breakaway is the object of this study, not MDB's later recovery.**

## 3. Catalyst ladder

Dates below are publication or filing dates, never later effective-date reconstructions.

1. **2018-02-16 — MongoDB 4.0 transaction capability became public.** TechTarget reported that version 4.0 would add multi-document ACID transactions, directly addressing a relational-database objection. Published 2018-02-16: https://www.techtarget.com/searchdatamanagement/news/252435244/MongoDB-40-takes-ACID-transactions-to-multi-document-level
2. **2018-03-13 — fiscal Q4 2018 results and product/cloud re-underwriting.** Revenue was $45.0m, +50% year over year; Atlas was 11% of revenue, grew more than 500% year over year and more than 40% sequentially, and had over 3,400 customers. Management guided FY2019 revenue to $211m–$215m. Published and filed 2018-03-13: https://investors.mongodb.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mongodb-inc-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-fiscal-2018 and SEC Form 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1441816/000144181618000019/mdb-013118x8kxearningsrele.htm
3. **2018-06-06 — fiscal Q1 2019 beat and Atlas mix step-up.** Revenue was $48.2m, +49% year over year; Atlas reached 14% of revenue, grew more than 400%, and added roughly 1,000 customers. Published/filed 2018-06-06: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1441816/000144181618000050/mdb-043018xex991xrelease.htm
4. **2018-06-27 — MongoDB 4.0 and Stitch general availability.** MongoDB publicly released multi-document ACID transactions and made its Stitch serverless platform generally available, broadening the credible workload set. Published 2018-06-27: https://www.mongodb.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/mongodb-expands-its-leadership-with-a-number-of-new-product-announcements
5. **2018-09-05 — fiscal Q2 2019 acceleration.** Revenue grew 61%, subscription revenue grew 63%, Atlas reached 18% of revenue and grew 400%; FY2019 revenue guidance rose to $228m–$230m from March's $211m–$215m. 6. **2018-10-10 — mLab acquisition filing.** MongoDB filed its agreement to acquire the cloud database service for $68m, expanding self-service expertise and the Atlas funnel. Filed 2018-10-10 (agreement dated 2018-10-09): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1441816/000144181618000112/mdb8-k100918.htm
7. **2018-10-16 — SSPL licensing change.** MongoDB introduced the Server Side Public License for Community Server releases from this date, attempting to protect its code from cloud providers offering it as a service without opening their surrounding service code. FY2019 revenue guidance increased to $243.7m–$244.7m, including about $5m expected from mLab. ## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior — absent.** MDB rose 41.68% in the 21 sessions and 59.03% in 42 sessions before `t0`; this was a vertical price-discovery phase in a five-month-old listing, not a compressed base.
- **Catalyst ladder — present.** The February ACID disclosure and March 13 earnings/Atlas evidence changed both product applicability and the revenue model. Later results and launches formed a new ladder, but arrived after the initial break failed.
- **Relative breakaway — present.** At `t0`, excess was +44.79 pp/21d and +65.04 pp/42d versus SPY, with a 63-day closing high. Versus IGV, independently recomputed excess was +40.04 pp/+55.02 pp.
- **Liquidity confirmation — present, but not durable acceptance.** Dollar-volume z-scores and the 2.60× 5d/60d ratio confirmed attention. The 21-session reversal shows that volume alone could not distinguish climax liquidity from accumulation.
- **Options convexity — unknown.** No 2018 options history exists in the repository; per-ticker history starts in 2026-06.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The repository fundamentals surfaces have a complete ticker-level gap for MDB: `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` return zero MDB rows, so no local PIT-gated ratios can be printed.

Public disclosures available by each date show the arc. Before `t0`, fiscal Q3 2018 revenue was $41.5m, +58%, with a 73% GAAP gross margin and a $24.1m operating loss (published 2017-12-12: https://investors.mongodb.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mongodb-inc-announces-third-quarter-fiscal-2018-financial). Fiscal Q4 then delivered $45.0m revenue, +50%, 74% GAAP gross margin, but a $26.4m operating loss; full-year revenue was $154.5m, +52%, while operating loss was $84.9m (published 2018-03-13: https://investors.mongodb.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mongodb-inc-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-fiscal-2018). Thus revenue and gross economics were strong, but self-funding was absent.

During the broader 2018 run, Q2 FY2019 revenue reached $57.5m (+61%) with a 71% GAAP gross margin and a $30.6m operating loss; cash, equivalents, investments and restricted cash were $523.2m after $254m net convertible-note proceeds, while quarterly free cash flow was -$18.0m (published 2018-09-05: https://investors.mongodb.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mongodb-inc-announces-second-quarter-fiscal-2019-financial). Q3 revenue reached $65.0m (+57%), gross margin recovered to 73%, operating loss was $29.1m, and free cash flow was -$9.7m (published 2018-12-04: https://investors.mongodb.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mongodb-inc-announces-third-quarter-fiscal-2019-financial). The re-underwriting was therefore about a fast-growing, high-gross-margin platform with ample financed runway—not contemporaneous profitability or organic self-funding.

## 7. Research echo

The external historical target record is incomplete, and the repo has no analyst-target data. What can be established is revealing: a February upgrade helped lift MDB 18.8% that month, but by the article's 2018-03-08 publication the shares already traded above that analyst's $34 target—“price escaped the published model” before the March earnings catalyst itself (published 2018-03-08: https://www.fool.com/investing/2018/03/08/heres-how-mongodb-inc-stock-rose-188-last-month.aspx). The local close reached $37.86 on 2018-03-13 and $44.80 at `t0`, 31.8% above $34.

The later echo caught up only after repeated operating proof. Needham's target was lifted to $97 from $91 on 2018-12-14, after MDB had closed $90.74 on 2018-12-06 (published 2018-12-14: https://www.streetinsider.com/Analyst+PT+Change/MongoDB+%28MDB%29+PT+Raised+to+%2497+at+Needham+%26+Company/14923835.html). The consensus-versus-spot series and a complete 2018 revision census remain null; any stronger consensus claim would exceed source coverage.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze — ruled_out.** The public evidence is a repeated product/revenue/customer ladder, not a social-coordination catalyst. Short-interest coverage is absent, so “ruled out” applies to meme mechanism, not to all possible short covering.
- **one_day_binary — ruled_out.** The onset followed February product disclosure and March earnings, and later 2018 events repeatedly extended the fundamental thesis. The initial break still behaved like a short-lived climax.
- **sector_beta — ruled_out.** MDB beat SPY by 44.79 pp and IGV by 40.04 pp over 21 sessions; broad software beta cannot explain that magnitude.
- **options_mirage — possible.** There is no 2018 options history, so an options mechanism cannot be tested. It must not be inferred from the 2026 snapshot.
- **Current hazard: extension after a vertical move — likely.** The stock was already +41.68%/21d and +59.03%/42d at onset; absence of compression distinguished this failure from a cleaner launch.
- **Current hazard: catalyst exhaustion — likely for the March episode.** `t0` lagged the hard March 13 release by nine trading sessions; no new hard event accompanied the March 26 leap.
- **Current hazard: call wall overhead — unknown historically; possible currently.** No 2018 data exist. The 2026-07-26 snapshot places a hard call wall at $330, about 10.7% above the site snapshot spot.
- **Current hazard: financing/runway dependence — possible.** High gross margins coexisted with operating and free-cash-flow losses; the later convertible issue expanded runway but underscored that growth was not self-funded.
