# LAC 2025 failed breakaway

## 1. Bottom line

LAC's 2025 alpha was not a normal lithium-cycle move; it was a policy-financing re-underwriting of Thacker Pass after media reports, company confirmation, DOE confirmation, and final loan amendments reframed the project from a pre-revenue developer with financing conditions into a U.S. critical-minerals beneficiary with DOE/GM alignment and a visible first draw. The move failed because the same facts that reduced project-financing risk also made the equity math worse: DOE penny warrants, a new reserve-account burden, at-the-market issuance into the spike, Orion note conversions, no revenue base, and analyst targets that remained below the tape after the vertical move. The tape confirms that distinction: the 21-trading-day impulse into the mechanical 2025-10-03 t0 was +224.0% for LAC versus +3.4% for SPY, but the next 21 trading days were -43.7% for LAC versus +2.1% for SPY.

This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`): the run did not sustain, and the useful lesson is that project-financing de-risking plus policy attention can create huge temporary alpha while still failing the durable-winner test when dilution, no current earnings power, and model escape dominate the follow-through.
