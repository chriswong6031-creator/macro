# CLSK 2024 Failed Breakaway

## 1. Bottom line

CLSK's 2024 alpha was front-loaded, not durable: capital first re-underwrote the company as the relatively clean, rapidly scaling U.S. bitcoin miner into the spot-bitcoin-ETF launch, the pre-halving bitcoin rally, and a visible hashrate step-up from Georgia and Mississippi assets, but the tape peaked exactly as the company expanded its ATM equity program and then had to keep proving that mined coin, power access, and treasury growth could outrun dilution, halving economics, network difficulty, fleet replacement, and bitcoin-miner beta. The mosaic was not one headline; it was a January-March stack of BTC beta, hashrate acceleration, balance-sheet liquidity, and post-halving scarcity narrative that could not sustain once the market saw the funding requirement and the operating P&L remained volatile.

This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`): the 2024-03-25 breakaway did not hold, and the study is about why the early alpha failed to become a durable winner.

## 4. Stage anatomy

Compressed prior: present. CLSK entered 2024 after a volatile bitcoin-miner recovery and then compressed less than durable bases normally do; by 2024-02-09 it was already printing fresh 63d/126d/252d highs from `data/yahoo/CLSK.parquet`, so the March breakaway was late-stage rather than early.

Catalyst ladder: present. The ladder was dense and public: spot bitcoin ETP approval on 2024-01-10, CleanSpark hashrate updates and acquisitions in February-March, and earnings beats in February/May; all were then offset by the 2024-03-28 ATM expansion and post-halving economics.

Relative breakaway: present but failed. Operator context marks t0 2024-03-25 with `new_high_63d: true`; Yahoo confirms 63d/126d/252d closing highs on the same date, but 5d, 21d, 42d, 63d, and 126d forward relative returns versus XLK and SPY were all negative.

Liquidity confirmation: present. t0 dollar volume was about `$1.13B`; z21 was about `+1.17` from Yahoo and `+1.06` from the massive-store episode context. Liquidity was real, but it appeared at the top and around financing events rather than during orderly digestion.

Options convexity: unknown historically. The repo's per-ticker options history starts in 2026-06, so there is no 2024 options-history basis. Current 2026 snapshots show high IV and nearby walls, but they cannot be back-projected into the 2024 tape.
