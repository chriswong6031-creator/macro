# BILI 2020 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Bilibili's May 2020 breakaway was a China-online-entertainment platform rerating, but the cleanest alpha was versus SPY, not versus the closest China internet cohort. The mosaic was not one headline: Sony's April 9 strategic investment reframed content and game optionality, Q1 results published May 18 showed 69% revenue growth, 70% MAU growth, 134% paying-user growth, and a fourth straight quarter of gross-margin expansion, and the May/June convertible raise supplied roughly $800 million for content and R&D while stay-home demand was pulling video, live, games, and advertising into one platform story. The tape confirmed a new-high breakaway on 2020-05-13 and strong 2020 YTD leadership, but BILI did not sustain large alpha versus KWEB at 63/126 trading days because the China internet cohort also rerated hard; the more idiosyncratic second leg came later, after Q2/Q3 execution, Riot Games esports rights, Huanxi content investment, and another large liquidity expansion in November 2020.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- **2020-04-09 - partnership / financing:** Bilibili announced that Sony Corporation of America would invest approximately $400 million for newly issued Class Z ordinary shares, equal to roughly 4.98% ownership, and that the companies would pursue collaboration in China's entertainment field including animation and mobile games. [Bilibili SEC exhibit, filed/public 2020-04-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1723690/000119312520102159/d915379dex991.htm); [Sony release, published 2020-04-09](https://sony.mediaroom.com/2020-04-09-Sony-Announces-Investment-in-the-Chinese-Entertainment-Platform-Bilibili-Inc).
- **2020-05-18 - earnings:** Q1 revenue reached RMB2.3155 billion, up 69%; MAUs reached 172.4 million, up 70%; DAUs reached 50.8 million, up 69%; monthly paying users reached 13.4 million, up 134%; gross margin reached 23%, the fourth consecutive quarterly expansion. [Company release, published 2020-05-18](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/05/18/2035302/0/en/Bilibili-Inc-Announces-First-Quarter-2020-Financial-Results.html).
- **2020-05-27 - financing:** Bilibili announced a proposed $650 million convertible senior note offering due 2027, with proceeds intended for content, R&D, and general corporate purposes. [Company release, published 2020-05-27](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/05/27/2039739/0/en/bilibili-inc-announces-proposed-offering-of-us-650-million-convertible-senior-notes.html).
- **2020-06-02 - financing completion:** Bilibili's IR event list records completion of the 2027 convertible notes on 2020-06-02; Q2 later disclosed aggregate principal amount of $800 million and RMB15.6 billion of cash, time deposits, and short-term investments at 2020-06-30. [Bilibili IR event list, publication date 2020-06-02](https://ir.bilibili.com/en/news-and-events/?keyword=&page=1&searchAction=true&yearTab=results); [Q2 release, published 2020-08-26](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/08/26/2084432/0/en/Bilibili-Inc-Announces-Second-Quarter-2020-Financial-Results.html).
- **2020-08-03 - partnership:** Bilibili and Riot Games' LoL Esports announced a three-year strategic partnership giving Bilibili China broadcasting rights for global League of Legends esports events from Worlds 2020 through the 2023 Mid-Season Invitational. [Inven Global press-release republication, published 2020-08-03](https://www.invenglobal.com/lol/articles/11845/bilibili-and-lol-esports-reach-strategic-partnership-in-china).
- **2020-08-26 - earnings:** Q2 revenue reached RMB2.6176 billion, up 70%; MAUs reached 171.6 million, up 55%; monthly paying users reached 12.9 million, up 105%; gross margin rose to 23.1%; management guided Q3 revenue to RMB3.05-3.10 billion. [SEC 6-K, filed 2020-08-31](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1723690/000119312520235346/d67615d6k.htm); [TechNode, published 2020-08-31](https://technode.com/2020/08/31/bilibili-invests-66-million-for-stake-in-production-firm/).

## 4. Stage anatomy

- **Compressed prior - weak / partly absent.** BILI was not deeply washed out before `t0`; 21 trading days earlier it was only 4.22% below its trailing 252-day closing high. The compression was more narrative than price-based: the market was still deciding whether BILI was a niche ACG/game community or a mass-market video platform.
- **Catalyst ladder - present.** Sony investment, Q1 user/revenue proof, note financing, Riot esports rights, Q2/Q3 execution, and Huanxi content investment created repeated public chances to re-underwrite the addressable market.
- **Relative breakaway - present versus SPY, mixed versus KWEB.** The episode row records +12.42pp/+26.10pp trailing excess at onset and +13.09pp/+15.65pp forward excess at 63/126 sessions versus SPY; versus KWEB, forward excess was only +10.11pp at 42 sessions and then -1.49pp at both 63 and 126 sessions.
- **Liquidity confirmation - present but late.** `t0` dollar volume was elevated but not explosive; the decisive liquidity expansion came on 2020-11-19 with $1.420B close x volume and z21 12.81 after Q3 results.
- **Options convexity - unknown.** There is no BILI options history in the repo for 2020, no `site/gex/BILI.json`, no `data/polygon_gex/summary_BILI.parquet`, and no trade-level NBBO evidence.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Repo fundamentals coverage is absent: `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements.parquet`, `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`, and `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` contain zero BILI rows, so there is no PIT-gated local fundamentals panel for this ticker. The following public-company financial arc is therefore sourced externally with publication/filing dates.

Before the May breakaway, the 2019 annual report filed 2020-03-27 showed a still-loss-making growth platform: 2019 net revenues were RMB6.7779 billion versus RMB4.1289 billion in 2018, while cost of revenues was 82.4% of revenue, implying gross margin of roughly 17.6%. [SEC Form 20-F for FY2019, filed 2020-03-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1723690/000119312520088657/d63580d20f.htm). The investment case entering 2020 was therefore growth plus monetization leverage, not current profitability.

During the run, the fundamental arc improved on revenue scale and gross margin while losses widened as management spent for content, user growth, and platform breadth. Q1 2020 revenue rose 69% to RMB2.3155 billion, MAUs rose 70%, paying users rose 134%, and gross margin reached 23.0%. Q2 revenue rose 70% to RMB2.6176 billion, gross margin reached 23.1%, and cash/time deposits/short-term investments reached RMB15.6 billion after the convertible note raise, but operating loss widened to RMB610.1 million. Q3 revenue rose 74% to RMB3.2257 billion, with VAS up 116% and advertising up 126%, showing monetization beyond games; losses were still part of the model. [Q1 release, published 2020-05-18](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/05/18/2035302/0/en/Bilibili-Inc-Announces-First-Quarter-2020-Financial-Results.html); [Q2 release, published 2020-08-26](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/08/26/2084432/0/en/Bilibili-Inc-Announces-Second-Quarter-2020-Financial-Results.html); [Q3 release, published 2020-11-18](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/11/18/2129689/0/en/Bilibili-Inc-Announces-Third-Quarter-2020-Financial-Results.html).

Runway improved materially: Sony added $400 million in April, and the 2027 convertible note financing added $800 million principal by June. That mattered because BILI's strategic playbook required spending into content, games, brand, and R&D before mature profitability. [Sony/Bilibili release, published 2020-04-09](https://sony.mediaroom.com/2020-04-09-Sony-Announces-Investment-in-the-Chinese-Entertainment-Platform-Bilibili-Inc); [Q2 release, published 2020-08-26](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/08/26/2084432/0/en/Bilibili-Inc-Announces-Second-Quarter-2020-Financial-Results.html).

## 7. Research echo

The repo has no analyst-target data, so this section uses external sources only. Public research appeared to chase the tape: on 2020-07-29, Schaeffer's reported BILI was already up 115% YTD and that the six analysts in coverage all had positive ratings, but the consensus target of $43.23 was only about 3% above the then-current price. [Schaeffer's, published 2020-07-29](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/options/2020/07/29/china-stock-could-be-pushed-higher-by-bull-signal). After Q2, The Motley Fool reported on 2020-08-27 that Nomura moved from a more constructive stance to neutral while increasing its target from $39 to $50, and Morgan Stanley reiterated an overweight stance while lifting its valuation estimate from $46 to $53. [The Motley Fool, published 2020-08-27](https://www.fool.com/investing/2020/08/27/why-bilibili-stock-plunged-today/). On 2020-11-02, Investing.com reported Nomura's $50 target, a $44.67 spot reference, and a seven-analyst average target of $56 with a $50-$60 range. [Investing.com, published 2020-11-02](https://www.investing.com/news/nomura-stick-to-their-hold-rating-for-bilibili-inc-2339174).

The read: published models kept raising the destination as revenue, users, and content breadth improved, but price repeatedly met or exceeded the then-published target set. By year-end 2020, the stock's +309% YTD move had escaped the midyear consensus framing; analyst targets described catch-up to a platform rerating more than they led it.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- **meme_squeeze: possible.** BILI had a real fundamental/catalyst ladder, but Schaeffer's reported on 2020-07-29 that short interest had risen and represented about 10% of float; reflexive participation could have amplified the run without being the core mechanism.
- **one_day_binary: ruled_out.** The episode was not one binary print: Sony, Q1, financing, Riot rights, Q2, Huanxi, and Q3 unfolded across April-November 2020.
- **sector_beta: possible.** Versus SPY the alpha was durable; versus KWEB, BILI failed to sustain excess return by 63/126 sessions, so part of the May-November move was China internet beta.
- **options_mirage: possible.** Local options history and current BILI GEX files are unavailable, and no signed-flow inference is allowed without trade-level NBBO.
- **Current hazards:** `extended_after_vertical_move`, `catalyst_exhausted`, `sector_beta_mirage`, `content_cost_inflation`, `regulatory_vie_china_adr`, `negative_operating_income`, and `competition_for_user_attention`.
