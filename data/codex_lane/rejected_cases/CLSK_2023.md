# CLSK 2023 winner autopsy

## Bottom line

CLSK's 2023 alpha was not a single earnings headline; it was a crypto-miner re-underwriting loop. Bitcoin moved from recovery to ETF/halving anticipation while CleanSpark showed that it had converted the bear market into cheap machine procurement, higher hashrate, better fleet efficiency, and a larger BTC/cash buffer. The stock was already up sharply before the 2023-11-24 mechanical onset, but the late-year tape had the ingredients capital could chase: a 63-day closing high, dollar-volume expansion, public proof of 10 EH/s scale, FY2023 liquidity of about $168 million as of 2023-11-30, and monthly production updates that made the operating leverage visible. The move did sustain beyond the first impulse, though not linearly: the 42-trading-day mark digested, then the February 2024 bitcoin/miner leg made the 63d and 126d relative returns extreme.

## Hazards & false-positive checks

- meme_squeeze: possible. The stock is a high-beta crypto miner and the December bitcoin tape had momentum/short-covering characteristics, but the catalyst ladder was not message-board-only; operating capacity, production, and liquidity improved publicly.
- one_day_binary: ruled_out. The move was not one trial/approval-style binary; it had repeated events from April procurement through January production updates.
- sector_beta: likely. Bitcoin and crypto-miner beta were central. The reason CLSK produced large alpha versus XLK/SPY was that the relevant economic cohort was bitcoin miners, not broad Information Technology.
- options_mirage: possible. Current options data show strong walls and high IV, but 2023 per-ticker history is absent; any 2023 options explanation would be unsupported.

Current hazards: extension after a vertical move, sensitivity to BTC drawdowns and mining difficulty, post-halving margin pressure, dilution/capex funding risk, catalyst exhaustion after capacity milestones become expected, and current call-wall/vol-hole context near 14.00 in `site/gex/CLSK.json`.
