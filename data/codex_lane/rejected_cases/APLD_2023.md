# APLD 2023 - winner autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

Applied Digital's 2023 alpha came from a violent re-underwriting attempt: the market moved it from small bitcoin-hosting infrastructure toward AI/HPC cloud capacity after a May-June ladder of public contract and partner announcements, but the move did not sustain because the tape had already pulled forward a very large amount of future execution while the available local fundamentals were thin, the announced AI-cloud economics were mostly forward-looking, and July 2023 short reports attacked the core premise and governance credibility. The failed-breakaway read is not "AI headline worked"; it is that AI cloud contract optionality, H100/HPE/NVIDIA association, and fiscal 2024 guidance created enough narrative surface for a squeeze-like repricing, while evidence of durable funded execution was still too immature to hold the breakaway once credibility risk became the marginal variable.

This is a FAILED breakaway case (`case_type: failed_breakaway`). The run did NOT sustain; the purpose of the study is why it failed and what distinguished it from durable winners.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- Compressed prior - present: before the May AI-cloud launch, APLD was still mostly understood through bitcoin/data-center hosting and had just reported FY2Q/FY3Q operating scale far below the later announced AI-cloud contract headline values. Local YTD to 2023-07-03 was already +438.59%, so by detector t0 it was no longer compressed in price.
- Catalyst ladder - present: May 15 launch, May 16 $180 million contract, June 23 $460 million second customer, June 30 HPE/H100 collaboration, July 14 Character.AI onboarding, July 24 FY2024 guidance, and Aug. 22 NVIDIA partner status all gave capital repeated reasons to revisit the model.
- Relative breakaway - present but unstable: supplied episode context says `new_high_63d=true`, `excess_21d_pp=2.00`, `excess_42d_pp=204.20`; local checkout bars show the post-t0 close failed almost immediately versus SPY. The disagreement is a coverage gap, but both views agree the stock had already produced huge 2023 alpha before the failure window.
- Liquidity confirmation - mixed: May 16 and June 23 dollar-volume spikes were institutional-capacity, but t0 dollar-volume z-score was only -0.21 on 21d and +0.03 on 60d; the July 24/25 turnover burst did not prevent a 10-session gap failure.
- Options convexity - unknown for 2023: per-ticker options history starts 2026-06 in this repo. Current snapshots show high-volatility, short-gamma descriptive structure, but that cannot be backcast into 2023.
