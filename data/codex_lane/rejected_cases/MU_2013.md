# MU 2013 Winner Autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

MU's 2013 alpha was not a one-headline reaction; it was the market re-underwriting a cyclically depressed memory producer into a scarcer, more consolidated DRAM franchise while its reported tape began to prove the turn. The stock had already made a 63-day and 126-day closing high on the mechanical t0 of 2013-06-05, then the June earnings filing confirmed positive net income, DRAM ASP recovery, margin expansion, and cash generation; the July Elpida close added scale, mobile DRAM exposure, and Rexchip control; the September SK Hynix Wuxi fire tightened the already-improving DRAM setup; and the October fiscal Q4 print showed the combined effect in revenue, margins, and balance-sheet liquidity. The move was therefore sustained by a mosaic of supply discipline, acquisition completion, margin inflection, and repeated liquidity confirmation rather than a single binary event.

## 3. Catalyst ladder

- 2013-02-19, Micron/Elpida antitrust clearance. Micron announced China's Ministry of Commerce clearance, the last antitrust pre-merger clearance required for the Elpida transaction, while noting remaining court/reorganization conditions and a target close in first-half calendar 2013. Source: Micron press release, 2013-02-19, https://investors.micron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-and-elpida-announce-antitrust-clearance-transaction.
- 2013-03-21, fiscal Q2 2013 earnings. Micron still reported a net loss, but gross margin improved to 18% from 12% in fiscal Q1 and DRAM revenue rose 24% sequentially on 38% volume growth, giving capital an early operating-turn clue. Source: Micron press release, 2013-03-21, https://investors.micron.com/node/27956.
- 2013-06-17, research/spot-price echo before Q3 print. Bloomberg/Newsmax reported MU at a six-year high after Jefferies and Credit Suisse target increases tied to steadier memory pricing and production discipline; MU had already advanced 109% YTD versus 23% for the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index. Source: Newsmax/Bloomberg, 2013-06-17, https://www.newsmax.com/finance/investinganalysis/micron-shares-chip-prices/2013/06/17/id/510370/.
- 2013-06-19, fiscal Q3 2013 earnings 8-K anchor. Local anchor: `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` has MU filing date 2013-06-19, acceptance 2013-06-19T20:01:09Z, items 2.02 and 9.01. Public release showed net income of $43 million, consolidated gross margin 24% versus 18% in Q2, DRAM revenue +23% sequentially due to +16% ASP and +6% volume, and $624 million quarterly operating cash flow. Source: Micron press release, 2013-06-19, https://investors.micron.com/node/28346.
- 2013-07-31, Elpida/Rexchip close. Micron announced the closing of 100% of Elpida equity and an additional 24% of Rexchip, giving Micron about 89% of Rexchip and 100% of Rexchip product supply; Micron said the acquired assets could produce more than 185,000 300mm wafers per month, about a 45% increase in current manufacturing capacity. Source: Micron press release, 2013-07-31, https://investors.micron.com/node/30531. SEC 8-K source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312513000133/form8k-elpidaclosingpr.htm.
- 2013-09-04, SK Hynix Wuxi fire. Reuters reported a fire at SK Hynix's Wuxi plant and noted the facility produced around 12%-15% of global computer memory chips; Micron shares initially rallied on concern over already-tight DRAM supply. Source: Reuters/Yahoo, 2013-09-04, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/sk-hynix-suspends-china-memory-151129056.html.
- 2013-09-17, spot DRAM follow-through. DigiTimes reported spot DRAM prices up more than 30% in less than two weeks after the SK Hynix Wuxi fire. Source: DigiTimes, 2013-09-17, https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20130917PD209.html.
- 2013-09-27 and 2013-10-02, DRAM contract-price confirmation. TrendForce/DRAMeXchange reported spot PC DRAM 2Gb prices up 36% since the fire and later said September contract price growth approached 15%, with graphics/server DRAM also likely affected. Sources: TrendForce, 2013-09-27, https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20130927-7809.html; TrendForce, 2013-10-02, https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20131002-7819.
- 2013-10-10, fiscal Q4 2013 earnings 8-K anchor. Local anchor: `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet` has MU filing date 2013-10-10, acceptance 2013-10-10T20:00:43Z, items 2.02 and 9.01. Public release showed fiscal Q4 net income of $1.71 billion, net sales of $2.8 billion, fiscal-year net income of $1.19 billion versus a $1.03 billion fiscal 2012 loss, Elpida-related purchase accounting gains, DRAM revenue +50% sequentially, and cash/investments of $4.2 billion. Source: Micron press release, 2013-10-10, https://investors.micron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-technology-inc-reports-results-fourth-quarter-fiscal-2013.

`data/edgar/material_8k_events.parquet` has no MU rows for 2012-2014 in this checkout. Earnings anchors above come from `data/edgar/earnings_8k_dates.parquet`.

## 4. Stage anatomy

- Compressed prior: present. Before 2013, the local quarterly panel shows fiscal 2012 and early fiscal 2013 losses, weak gross margins, and a stock that had been left for cyclical-decline treatment; by t0 it was breaking through 63/126/252-day closing highs.
- Catalyst ladder: present. The ladder included China antitrust clearance, Q2/Q3 earnings improvement, Elpida close, SK Hynix fire-driven DRAM tightening, and Q4 earnings confirmation.
- Relative breakaway: present. From 2013-06-05 to 2013-12-03, MU returned 81.61% versus 11.58% for XLK and 12.59% for SPY; full-year 2013 MU returned 243.06% versus 26.24% for XLK and 32.31% for SPY.
- Liquidity confirmation: present. Dollar-volume z-scores expanded from modestly positive at t0 to repeated multi-sigma volume days in September, October, November, and December, including $1.69 billion on 2013-09-04 and $2.53 billion on 2013-10-11.
- Options convexity: unknown. Current options snapshots exist, but per-ticker options history starts in 2026-06 in repo doctrine and cannot support a 2013 options-convexity claim.

Before the run, fiscal 2012 quarters showed depressed profitability: fiscal Q1 2012 revenue $2.09 billion, gross margin 14.59%, operating margin -3.92%, and net loss $0.19 billion; fiscal Q2 2012 revenue $2.01 billion, gross margin 10.45%, operating margin -10.20%, and net loss $0.28 billion; fiscal Q3 2012 revenue $2.17 billion, gross margin 10.77%, operating margin -8.79%, and net loss $0.32 billion. Early fiscal 2013 remained loss-making but improving: fiscal Q1 2013 revenue $1.83 billion, gross margin 11.83%, operating margin -8.56%, net loss $0.28 billion; fiscal Q2 2013 revenue $2.08 billion, gross margin 17.61%, operating margin -1.11%, net loss $0.29 billion.

During the run, fiscal Q3 2013 crossed into reported profitability: local quarterly data shows revenue $2.32 billion, gross margin 23.99%, operating margin 6.43%, and net income $0.04 billion, with EPS `eps_quarterly` +$0.04 as of 2013-07-29. Fiscal Q4 2013 is partly outside the local quarterly statements table because that table labels the next available period as fiscal Q1 2014 ending 2013-11-28, but Micron's 2013-10-10 release supplies the publication-date evidence: fiscal Q4 net sales $2.843 billion, gross margin $708 million, fiscal-year net income $1.190 billion, and cash flow from operations $1.8 billion for fiscal 2013. The subsequent local fiscal Q1 2014 row shows the combined-company step-up: revenue $4.04 billion, gross margin 31.69%, operating margin 13.63%, net income $0.36 billion, cash $3.65 billion, and inventory $2.46 billion.

Runway improved from "memory downturn with transaction uncertainty" to "profitable, cash-generating consolidated DRAM platform." Local balance-sheet data shows cash around $2.44 billion at fiscal Q3 2013 and $3.65 billion by fiscal Q1 2014; Micron's own Q4 release says cash and investments reached $4.2 billion, including restricted cash for the first Elpida creditor installment.

## 7. Research echo

The repo has no analyst-target history, so this section uses external dated sources. By 2013-06-17, after the stock had already reached a six-year high, Bloomberg/Newsmax reported Jefferies lifting its target to $18 from $15 and Credit Suisse to $20 from $14, with the rationale tied to memory-price predictability, production discipline, and mobile-chip shortage. Source: Newsmax/Bloomberg, 2013-06-17, https://www.newsmax.com/finance/investinganalysis/micron-shares-chip-prices/2013/06/17/id/510370/. By 2013-10-14, TheStreet reported Bernstein moving its target to $24 from $4 and Jefferies to $30 from $25 after the Q4 print, while Wells Fargo was still cautious even as it raised its range to $14-$17. Source: TheStreet, 2013-10-14, https://www.thestreet.com/markets/analysts-reiterate-positive-outlook-for-micron-technology-mu-12068414. Fintel's historical feed shows the dispersion around this period: supportive reiterations on 2013-06-20, a Susquehanna upgrade on 2013-09-09, later Wells Fargo/BofA downgrades on 2013-10-11 and 2013-12-23, and a Nomura upgrade on 2013-12-12. Source: Fintel, accessed 2026-07-17, https://fintel.io/sfo/us/mu.

Read: the price escaped the published model first, then research targets chased the new margin/industry structure. June target increases still clustered near the spot price after the stock had doubled YTD; by October/December, target dispersion widened because analysts were debating whether DRAM consolidation was structurally more durable or still just a cyclical spike.

## 9. Hazards & false-positive checks

- meme_squeeze: ruled_out. The move mapped to DRAM pricing, Elpida completion, reported margin/cash inflection, and repeated post-earnings repricing rather than social attention or isolated short-interest mechanics.
- one_day_binary: ruled_out. There were multiple public catalysts across February, March, June, July, September, and October; the 126-day relative return persisted well beyond one event day.
- sector_beta: ruled_out. XLK and SPY rose in 2013, but MU beat XLK by 216.82 pp for the full year and by 70.03 pp over 126 trading days from t0.
- options_mirage: possible. Current options files exist only from 2026; 2013 options convexity is unobservable here, so options cannot explain the 2013 tape from repo data.
- Current hazards: extended_after_vertical_move, call_wall_overhead, catalyst_exhausted. Current snapshot files show short gamma, high IV30, 0DTE share 45.7%, and nearby wall structure, but these are 2026 context only and not part of the 2013 cause.
