# IONQ 2025 — failed breakaway autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

IONQ's May 2025 move was a mosaic of a risk-on rebound in an already reflexive quantum-computing cohort, a genuinely stronger commercialization story (the $22 million EPB system/hub deal), a newly acquisition-heavy platform narrative (ID Quantique, Capella, Lightsynq), and an application story investors could repeat (the May 20 Einride logistics partnership), all amplified by an attention/liquidity shock. It was not a clean fundamental inflection: Q1 revenue was flat year over year, operating loss widened, and the cash increase came mainly from a $372.6 million at-the-market equity raise. Nor was it broad cohort alpha: IONQ trailed an equal-weight RGTI/QBTS/QUBT basket over most lookbacks into May 22. The breakaway failed because price and liquidity ran far ahead of reported economics and published valuation models, the May 22 impulse did not hold, and the next major strategic announcement—Oxford Ionics—introduced explicit stock dilution rather than near-term earnings power.

**Format-contract classification: this is a failed breakaway (`case_type: failed_breakaway`); the run did not sustain.**

## 5. Fundamentals arc

The requested repo panels have a complete IONQ coverage gap: zero rows in [`data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet), [`data/edgar/statements.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/statements.parquet), [`data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet), and [`data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet`](../../../data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet). Before the run, IonQ had 2024 revenue of $43.1 million, up 95%, but a $331.6 million net loss and $107.2 million adjusted EBITDA loss; cash, cash equivalents and investments were $363.8 million at year-end. [IonQ FY2024 release, published 2025-02-26](https://investors.ionq.com/news/news-details/2025/IonQ-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2024-Financial-Results/).

During the run, Q1 revenue was $7.566 million versus $7.582 million a year earlier (flat), while cost of revenue rose to $4.315 million from $3.414 million. The implied gross margin before depreciation/amortization fell to about 43.0% from 55.0%. Operating loss widened to $75.7 million from $52.9 million; operating cash use rose to $33.0 million from $20.5 million. Cash/investments rose to $697.1 million chiefly after $372.6 million of ATM proceeds, so runway improved through dilution rather than self-funding. Management said liquidity was sufficient for at least the following 12 months but expected significant losses for the foreseeable future. [IonQ 10-Q, filed/published 2025-05-07](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1824920/000095017025065699/ionq-20250331.htm); [IonQ Q1 release, published 2025-05-07](https://investors.ionq.com/news/news-details/2025/IonQ-Announces-First-Quarter-Financial-Results/default.aspx).

The arc therefore improved in bookings narrative, strategic reach and financing runway, not in contemporaneous organic revenue or operating leverage. That mismatch helps explain why a large price impulse could not hold.
