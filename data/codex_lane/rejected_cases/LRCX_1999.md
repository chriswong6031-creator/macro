# LRCX 1999 Winner Autopsy

## 1. Bottom line

LRCX's 1999 alpha was a cycle-turn plus company-specific operating leverage case: after a severe 1998 wafer-fab-equipment downturn and restructuring, Lam began printing sequential order recovery, higher book-to-bill, rising gross margin, lower operating expense intensity, and positive cash flow, while its product story shifted from "survivor" to "0.18 micron and copper-capable share gainer." The tape did not need a single binary event. It re-underwrote the name through a ladder of evidence: March-quarter orders above 1.4x book-to-bill, Exelan dielectric etch launch, CMP-clean installed-base proof, AMD and STMicro customer wins, a July return to profitability, then October confirmation from TSMC copper CMP qualification and another quarter of revenue, margin, and EPS acceleration. The move was sustained into year-end because fundamentals kept broadening while the sector was also recovering, but LRCX's incremental orders, margins, and customer proof made it much more than sector beta.

## 4. Stage anatomy

Compressed prior: present but imperfect. LRCX had already more than doubled YTD by t0 and was at 252d highs, so this was not a quiet base; the compression was in fundamentals and expectations after the 1998 equipment downturn, not in a low-volatility price range.

Catalyst ladder: present. The ladder moved from Q3 order recovery and 1.4x book-to-bill, to Exelan launch, CMP-clean installed-base proof, AMD/ST customer wins, return to profitability, TSMC copper CMP qualification, and Q1 FY2000 acceleration.

Relative breakaway: present. From t0 to 126 trading days, LRCX was +117.59% versus XLK +30.56% and SPY +6.11% in local Yahoo data; the episode context also flags +24.12 pp and +33.70 pp excess versus XLK at 21d/42d.

## 5. Fundamentals arc

Repo PIT coverage: `data/edgar/fundamentals_panel.parquet` has LRCX annual rows from 2010 onward, not 1999. `data/edgar/statements.parquet` has LRCX rows from 2020 onward. `data/edgar/statements_quarterly.parquet` has LRCX rows from fiscal 2020 onward. `data/edgar/eps_quarterly.parquet` has LRCX rows from period_end 2019-12-29 onward. Therefore, the repo's normalized PIT panels cannot support a 1999 fundamentals reconstruction; the 1999 arc below uses dated company releases and filing-directory anchors.

Before the run, the fundamental arc was still ugly in annual terms: fiscal 1999 revenue was $648m versus $1.05b in fiscal 1998, and fiscal 1999 net loss was $54.5m excluding non-recurring charges. But the March 1999 quarter showed the turn: revenue grew 8% sequentially to $153.0m, book-to-bill exceeded 1.4x, gross margin improved to 35.5%, operating expenses fell, and cash flow from operations turned positive.

During the run, operating leverage became visible. June-quarter revenue rose 38% sequentially to $210.9m, gross margin rose to 38.8%, and EPS turned positive at $0.28. September-quarter revenue then rose to $241.6m, up 15% sequentially and 70% y/y, EPS rose to $0.58, gross margin rose to 41.7%, and operating cash flow was positive for the third consecutive quarter. Runway evidence came from book-to-bill staying above 1.15x, Asian demand, Japan strength in September, and customer/product proof in Exelan, Teres, AMD, STMicroelectronics, and TSMC.
