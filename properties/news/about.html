About Mastermind News

Mastermind News is a markets wire. The Brief is its desk, and it files short notes on what moved in a session and what appears to be behind it — the kind of thing you read standing up, between a screen and a meeting, then close.

Every note carries the date and the minute it cleared the desk, and the front page runs newest first, so the top of the page is always the most recent thing we know. Copy here is machine-assisted and checked against the desk's own sources before it goes out. Treat that check as a floor rather than a promise: when a piece is wrong, the correction runs on the piece itself, dated and in plain sight, instead of a quiet edit.

The wire is part of Mastermind, our markets research desk. Everything published here is educational. It is not investment advice, none of it is tailored to your situation, and markets involve risk.
