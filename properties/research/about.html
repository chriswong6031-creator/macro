About Mastermind Research

Mastermind Research reads what institutions publish and writes up what the document actually says. The Research Desk works from the papers themselves — central bank notes, working papers, official statistics releases, the reports firms put out under their own name.

A note here sets out what a document claims, what that claim rests on, and where a careful reader should stay skeptical. It is not a summary service: the reading is the point, not the recap. Every note carries the date it was filed and the desk that wrote it. Copy is machine-assisted and checked against the source document before it publishes, and when a note turns out to be wrong the correction appears on the note itself, dated, rather than as a quiet revision.

This journal is part of Mastermind, our markets research desk. Everything published here is educational. It is not investment advice, none of it is tailored to your circumstances, and markets involve risk.
