Cycle & regime research

We map continuously where each market sits in its cycle, and read the regime forward — base-effect-aware, so a read anticipates the transition rather than describing the one that has already passed.

Inflection is the object of the work. When conditions are quietly turning, a cross-country cycle clock shows it first — often before price makes the change visible to the herd.

  • Forward, base-effect-aware regime reads
  • Inflection detection
  • Cross-country cycle clocks

Rotation & thematic allocation

Capital rotates across sectors, styles, countries and themes in patterns that rhyme. We trace those flows directly to establish what is working, when, and why — and where the transition points sit.
