The firm

Built for markets that move in transitions.

Markets do not move in straight lines. They rotate between sectors, styles, countries, and regimes — in cycles that rhyme with history. Grey Deer Capital is built to read those transitions.

Signal-based AI quantitative research senses where capital is shifting. An AI-assisted qualitative confirmation layer tests whether the story holds. And disciplined portfolio construction positions capital where it is about to flow — not where it has already been.

Capital flows

Capital moves like water.
We map the current.

Beneath a calm surface, capital is always moving — draining from one theme, pooling into another, converging where conviction builds and diverging where it breaks. Most of that movement is invisible until price confirms it.

We trace the current directly: the flows between sectors, styles, asset classes, countries, and macro regimes. Where those flows converge is where the next opportunity forms — and where a regime is quietly about to turn.

The process

A disciplined loop, not a straight line.

Regime analysis never finishes. Our process runs as a cycle — each turn sharpening the next — mirroring the markets it is built to read.

RegimeAware
  1. 01
    Observe
    Watch markets, flows, and macro across regions and asset classes — continuously.
  2. 02
    Model
    Frame the data with multi-factor quantitative models and regime classifiers.
  3. 03
    Detect
    Surface the signal — where a regime, flow, or rotation is beginning to shift.
  4. 04

    Test the signal against fundamentals, narrative and context with an AI research layer.
  5. 05

  6. 06

    Re-read the cycle as it turns, and begin again. The loop never closes.
