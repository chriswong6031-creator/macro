The firm

A boutique with an institutional mind.

Grey Deer Capital is a modern active investment firm. We pair signal-based AI quantitative research with an AI-assisted qualitative confirmation layer, then position capital where the two agree.

We serve private and institutional capital. The firm is deliberately small: disciplined in construction, curious by temperament, and unafraid of complexity where complexity is where the edge lives. We would rather be early and prepared than loud and late.

A deer is alert and still. It reads the smallest change in its environment — a shift in the wind, a break in the pattern — before that change is visible to anything else. It moves first, and it moves with intent.

A grey deer at the edge of the treeline reads the turn before the herd does. That is the posture we take toward markets: patient, watchful, and positioned before the regime confirms what the signals already sensed.
