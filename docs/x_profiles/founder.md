## Notes for the operator

- The link is intentionally absent from this account's publisher policy
  (`publish.links_allowed` has no `founder` key, so it is false). Links from a
  freshly wired personal account are a named X spam trigger; the flagship ramp is
  the precedent for when to flip it.
- **AM-R1 applies to the human too.** The engine cannot post "I bought X" from
  this account — the dial's AM-R1 detectors now reject it in copy. When the
  founder posts manually he is free to say what he likes, but the moment a
  position claim appears on the account the graded-record promise has to cover
  it, and it does not. Keep positions off the account entirely.
- The bio should not carry a follower count, a launch date, or a "1/" thread hook.
