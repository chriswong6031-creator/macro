## Notes for the operator

- **Optional zh line for the bio** — only if the operator wants a bilingual
  profile, and only glossed, per her codex: 「纽约开盘前，先看亚洲」("before New
  York opens, read Asia first"). Untranslated Chinese in an English-facing bio
  breaks her own hard rule, and the dial rejects it in copy for the same reason.
- Her cadence is weighted to the HK/Asia session — the overnight-US window no
  other account on the roster covers. The cadence schema carries no session field
  yet; the XG-W2 resolver is what will read the weighting. Until then, the
  operator should expect her posting hours to look like everyone else's, which
  undercuts the bio's whole promise. **This is the single biggest gap between
  what this profile claims and what the engine currently does.**
- **No tea, no travel, no jet lag in the bio.** Dark canon. The 🍵 in her emoji
  signature is a glyph, not a claim about her habits — but keep it out of the bio
  anyway, where it reads as one.
