## Notes for the operator

- Link policy for this account is already `true` in `config/marketing.yml`
  (`publish.links_allowed.flagship`); the profile link should point at the graded
  page the pinned post promises, not the homepage. A pinned post that promises a
  scorecard and links to a marketing page is the fastest way to lose the reader
  it just earned.
- Do not add a launch date or a follower milestone to the bio. Both age badly and
  neither answers "what do I get".
