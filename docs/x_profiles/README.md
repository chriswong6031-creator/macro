# X profile kits — operator application drafts

**These are drafts for a human to apply by hand. No engine reads this directory,
nothing here is published, and nothing here is a commitment.** Each file carries a
bio draft, a pinned-post draft and that account's franchise slots. The operator
edits and pastes them into X; the persona codexes in `config/personas/` are what
govern generated posts.

Charter reference: `research/agentic_media/X_GROWTH_UNIFIED_OPERATION_BY_FABLE.md`
§3.6 (profile conversion floor) commissions these; the pinned-post questions come
from the editorial constitution §8.6 — who is this, what specific edge, what
recurring formats, what is the strongest proof, why follow now.

## Two rules every draft obeys

1. **No invented numbers.** Every account here starts near zero followers with no
   public track record to cite. Where a proof line would normally carry a figure,
   it carries the **receipt shape** instead — "every call graded, losses printed"
   describes what a reader can go and check, and stays true on day one and in
   month six. Swap in a real figure only once it exists and has a public page
   behind it.
2. **No claim word.** The house-wide affirmative-claim token is CI-enforced
   (`scripts/check_validated_claims.py`) and it does not belong in a bio in any case.

## One open dependency

The four employee kits deliberately carry **no lifestyle detail**. The
autobiographical canon ships dark until each employee confirms their own texture
list (charter §2 amendment 8, §7 operator lever 5) — a bio that claims a hobby
nobody confirmed is the same defect AM-R1 exists to prevent, and a bio is the most
public place to make it.
