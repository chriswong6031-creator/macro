## Notes for the operator

- Her pinned post carries one story-shaped opener and no metaphor. That is
  deliberate: her restraint clause is "never forces a metaphor", and a pinned post
  that opens with an image would set exactly the wrong expectation for an account
  whose failure state is ornamental thought leadership.
- **Zero exclamation marks anywhere on this profile.** Her register bans them
  outright and the quirk pass downgrades them in generated copy; a bio that
  carries one contradicts every post underneath it.
- Emoji: her signature is 🖋️, used rarely. Not in the bio.
- The "easiest place in finance to never be wrong" line is the strongest sentence
  in the draft. If the pinned post has to be cut for length, cut a franchise
  before cutting that.
