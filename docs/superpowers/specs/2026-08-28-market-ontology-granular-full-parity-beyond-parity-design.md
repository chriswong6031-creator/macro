# Market Ontology Granular Full-Parity + Beyond-Parity Design

## Goal

Make the existing Market Ontology productization program provably exhaustive at the granular capability/job level, while preserving Mastermind's canonical owners and lawful independent implementation, then convert parity into a continuing beyond-parity product/intelligence ratchet.

## Governing outcome

The primary user job is not "copy a competitor." It is to ensure Mastermind does not discard a useful product idea before it has been experienced inside the Mastermind estate and evaluated for synergy with native intelligence. Every captured Market Ontology capability therefore remains an inclusion obligation even when its current priority or alpha value is low.

The machine/intelligence job is to preserve row-level provenance and traceability while compressing many competitor states/interactions into the smallest coherent set of canonical Mastermind systems. Provenance must survive compression; duplicate architecture must not.

The completion claim is stronger than research coverage and stronger than a merged implementation: every bona fide user/product capability must have a real production-proven Mastermind equivalent or stronger equivalent before `PARITY_COMPLETE`.

## Existing architecture preserved

No third MarketOntology workstream is created.

- `WS:MARKET-OS` owns product composition and user-facing research/decision experience.
- `WS:ALPHA-INTELLIGENCE-INTEGRATION` owns shared semantic intelligence contracts.
- Existing specialist owners retain event, identity, graph, valuation, portfolio, options, earnings, policy/geopolitics, technical, capital-markets, human-research, tenant/API, evaluation and learning truth.
- Executive OS remains Job/Attempt/Worker/Event lifecycle authority.
- Agent OS remains durable organizational workstream/decision/discovery/handoff authority.
- GitHub remains implementation/evidence truth.
- Linear remains selective portfolio projection.
- Slack/watchers remain transport/attention only.

The existing parent program remains `marketontology-complete-parity-fanout-20260826-sol-001`; F00 remains coverage/program control; F01-F13 remain durable domain identities rather than permanent provider/account seats.

## Evidence model

The canonical coverage input is a union, not a replacement chain.

### E1 — retained public P1 corpus

Expected source truth: 1,556 granular capability rows plus 460 structured quality findings from Mastermind's lawful black-box research. Exact retained bytes must be admitted through F00A with size/hash verification. The archive is evidence, not implementation authority.

### E2 — authenticated paid baseline

88/88 advertised paid capability/submodule targets. These provide strong route/state anchors and populated-state evidence.

### E3 — living public delta ledger

Existing 42 post-baseline candidates plus later validated additions.

### E4 — Terra 2026-08-28 recensus

The Chairman's active Terra website sweep enters as a dated delta source. It may add, deepen, alias or retire observed competitor behavior, but it never deletes historical opportunity rows automatically.

### E5 — future delta observations

Until final closeout, bounded current-public checks continue. A newer competitor release may add closure obligations; a removed competitor feature remains historical product research unless explicitly disposed.

## Source identity and deduplication

Every admitted observation gets an immutable `source_row_id` scoped to its evidence set. A separate normalized `capability_family_id` may group rows that share one Mastermind implementation.

Required relationship:

`source_row_id -> capability_family_id -> canonical_owner -> disposition -> implementation/proof carrier`

Deduplication may be many-to-one. It is forbidden to delete a source row merely because another row is similar.

A row may map to multiple owner components only when the user/machine journey genuinely composes them. The ledger must identify one coordinating owner for closure accounting and list dependent owners explicitly.

House capability state vocabulary remains `PROVEN_LIVE`, `BUILT_NOT_PROVEN`, `PARTIAL`, `DARK_OR_DISCONNECTED`, `BROKEN`, `SPEC_ONLY`, `NOT_BUILT`, `REJECTED_BY_DESIGN`.

Disposition vocabulary remains `PROVEN_EXISTING`, `UPGRADE_EXISTING`, `BUILD_NEW`, `PROJECTION_OVER_EXISTING`, `RESEARCH_CONTEXT_ONLY`, `REJECTED_BY_DESIGN`.

`parity_status` is one of `UNASSESSED`, `MAPPED`, `IMPLEMENTATION_OWED`, `PROOF_OWED`, `PARITY_SATISFIED`, `JOB_LAWFULLY_IMPOSSIBLE`.

`JOB_LAWFULLY_IMPOSSIBLE` requires an exact rights/legal/safety reason and Sol/Chairman adjudication; inconvenience or low expected value never qualifies.

## Interpretation of REJECTED_BY_DESIGN

`REJECTED_BY_DESIGN` rejects a mechanism, not automatically the job.

Examples:

- opaque competitor composite score -> transparent Mastermind decomposition with authority=false;
- duplicate competitor portfolio store -> projection over Mastermind Portfolio canonical owner;
- vendor-only proprietary feed -> lawful alternative source or explicit rights blocker;
- unsafe authority semantics -> descriptive/research projection with no promotion.

If the lawful underlying job remains useful, `stronger_equivalent_ref` or a bounded implementation carrier is mandatory.

## F00 stage architecture

### F00A — exact corpus admission

Purpose: make the retained P1 evidence byte-verifiable and current-session recoverable.

Expected archive root:

`research/market_intelligence_productization/market_ontology_p1_archive/`

F00A copies only Mastermind-owned research artifacts, preserves filenames/bytes, and creates `IMPORT_MANIFEST.json` containing filename, byte size, SHA-256, source receipt and import timestamp. It does not rewrite historical owner recommendations.

Acceptance: exact source bytes available; recorded historical size/hash matches or discrepancy is explicitly reconciled; archive commit remains research-only; zero competitor proprietary data/code/assets are added.

If exact source bytes are unavailable, F00A returns `BLOCKED SOURCE_BYTES_UNAVAILABLE`; coverage continues elsewhere but `COVERAGE_COMPLETE` cannot close.

### F00B — coarse owner crosswalk

Existing live operation: `marketontology-f00b-current-owner-crosswalk-20260828-sol-001`.

It remains bounded to the 88 paid baseline + current deltas. Its result provides current owner/collision/disposition scaffolding and must not claim granular parity closure. It is not restarted or widened.

### F00C — granular parity reconciliation

Inputs: accepted F00A corpus admission, accepted F00B coarse map, current Terra/public delta result, current canonical repository/Agent OS state.

F00C maps every admitted source row to exact current owner/disposition/proof status. It creates the overlay granular ledger and family map. It may fan out read-only row batches to bounded research workers, but one F00C coordinator owns deterministic merge/dedup/zero-unassessed validation.

Acceptance:

- zero `UNASSESSED` rows;
- zero missing `source_row_id`;
- zero missing canonical owner except rows adjudicated `JOB_LAWFULLY_IMPOSSIBLE`;
- every many-to-one family retains all source row IDs;
- every non-PROVEN row has an implementation/proof owner and operation class;
- each F01-F13 family receives at least one independent spot audit;
- current Terra delta is incorporated or explicitly classified as duplicate/depth/new/removal.

### F00D — beyond-parity synthesis

Inputs: F00C ledger plus parity implementation/proof returns.

For every `PARITY_SATISFIED` capability family, F00D asks:

1. Which Mastermind-native data/intelligence systems can deepen this capability?
2. Which neighboring product workflows can combine with it?
3. Can it become predictive, agentic, collaborative, portfolio-aware, personalized or self-correcting without violating authority law?
4. What user/research/distribution/commercial/data-moat value could emerge?
5. What evidence would prove the expansion is useful?

Output rows include `beyond_parity_hypothesis`, `native_synergies`, `value_model`, `proposed_owner`, `priority`, `evidence_gate`, `disposition`.

High-value expansions receive bounded existing-owner handoffs. Low-priority expansions remain durable hypotheses; they are not silently discarded.

## F01-F13 implementation rules

Each bounded implementation child must declare:

- exact `source_row_id` and `capability_family_id` set it intends to close;
- coordinating canonical owner and dependent owners;
- complete user/machine journey;
- deterministic/statistical/model-generated boundary;
- time/PIT/null/correction behavior;
- rights/source dependencies;
- authority ceiling;
- failure/degraded states;
- production proof required for `PARITY_SATISFIED`.

A child PR should still deliver one independently useful Mastermind capability. It may close many rows when one real capability satisfies them.

No child may create a second identity, event, graph, financial, portfolio, thesis, tenant, API, evaluation, learning, queue, watcher or lifecycle plane to make parity easier.

## Experience parity versus visual copying

Parity concerns user jobs and interaction/workflow capability, not competitor branding or copyrighted expression. Mastermind may use different information architecture, terminology and visual design when it satisfies or improves the same job.

A feature whose value depends on a specific interaction pattern should preserve the interaction's functional semantics: state transitions, inputs, outputs, persistence, notifications, reopen behavior, collaboration state and failure behavior. It need not reproduce proprietary pixels or copy.

## Authority separation

Competitor fields such as direction, confidence, impact, already-priced, pricing-gap, opportunity score or portfolio action are evidence of a user job, not authority.

Mastermind may reproduce the research experience through transparent components such as observed facts, inference, incorporation/dislocation, transmission, valuation, scenario, path/timing, uncertainty and failed gates. These remain descriptive/research unless separately promoted through Mastermind's prospective evaluation law.

Parity can therefore be achieved with `authority_ceiling=RESEARCH_CONTEXT` while rank/gate/size/trade remains false.

## Time, null and correction law

Every row inherits the canonical owner's clock and correction semantics. F00 must never invent a generic competitor clock.

Where the competitor product does not reveal a clock/null/correction contract, the parity ledger records `UNVERIFIED_COMPETITOR_SEMANTICS`; the Mastermind implementation still follows its own owner law truthfully.

Missing data is not zero. Unknown source/rights state blocks a claim that depends on it. Historical competitor observations remain historical and must not be presented as current product truth.

## Failure states

Program-level blockers include:

- retained P1 exact bytes unavailable;
- source row lost during deduplication;
- unknown/unowned/unassessed row;
- current Terra/public delta not reconciled;
- duplicate canonical plane created;
- `REJECTED_BY_DESIGN` used as a low-priority kill switch;
- implementation merged without real consumer/proof;
- rights dependency hand-waved;
- context feature silently gains trade authority;
- provider/session timeout mistaken for organizational workstream death;
- active child rerouted without effect reconciliation;
- row marked parity-satisfied by docs/spec only.

## Closure metrics

`coverage_total_rows` = count of all admitted granular source rows after evidence-union admission, before family compression.

`coverage_assessed_rows` must equal `coverage_total_rows` for `COVERAGE_COMPLETE`.

`parity_satisfied_product_rows` must equal all lawful bona fide product/user/machine capability rows for `PARITY_COMPLETE`.

Method/security/data rows must separately show canonical implementation/owner coverage; they are not allowed to hide in a product-row denominator.

`beyond_parity_reviewed_families` must equal total parity families before `BEYOND_PARITY_RATCHET_ACTIVE` can be claimed.

## Proof law

Research/records work may be accepted as records-only. It does not make product parity live.

Implementation parity requires the real producer -> canonical owner/state -> real consumer/projection -> user/machine proof path appropriate to the capability. User-facing features require browser proof in relevant real-data and degraded states. Machine-facing capabilities require real consumer proof. CI alone never closes parity.

## Continuity model

Provider accounts and sessions are replaceable execution surfaces; they do not own durable MarketOntology responsibility. F00/F01-F13 identities survive context limits through Agent OS/GitHub handoffs. Every independent child retains one operation key/one carrier and explicit watcher continuation/STOP semantics under the current protected Skillpack.

Current preferred manual routing is avenue-first: Sol recommends `Terra`, `CTO Sol`, `Grok`, `Opus` or `Fable`; the Chairman selects the concrete quota-bearing account/session unless explicitly delegated.

## Completion

The program may say **Market Ontology functional capability space is absorbed** only after `PARITY_COMPLETE`.

The program should not be considered strategically finished at parity. `BEYOND_PARITY_RATCHET_ACTIVE` establishes the continuous improvement layer that turns competitor-inspired capability coverage into Mastermind-native product and intelligence advantage.
