## 1. Outcome

Mastermind needs an institutional learning loop that can answer, for an operating decision or policy exposure:

1. What information, alternatives, expectation, uncertainty and guardrails existed **before** the result was known?
2. What happened afterward, over which observation horizon, according to which canonical evidence owners?
3. Is the result merely observed, comparably associated, partially identified, supported for off-policy evaluation, or randomized?
4. How much of the result is plausibly attributable to the policy rather than selection, external delay, review topology, luck or missing follow-up?
5. What remains uncertain or right-censored?
6. What explicit policy delta, if any, is justified—and who has authority to approve, canary, promote, roll back or reject it?

The end product is not a model leaderboard. It is a scientific operating discipline for Mastermind itself.

### 1.1 Chairman job

The Chairman should be able to ask whether a route, handoff pattern, review topology, proof discipline, watcher policy or decomposition method actually improved the company without manually reconstructing dozens of sessions, Slack threads, PRs and delayed consequences.

### 1.2 Machine/intelligence job

The machine must seal decision-time expectations, join owner-native evidence point-in-time, calculate transparent metrics, refuse unsupported causal claims, preserve corrections and delayed outcomes, and produce a reviewed learning artifact that can inform—but never enact—policy.

### 1.3 Moat

Mastermind compounds not only market knowledge but operating knowledge: which procedures work for which contexts, how confident we were beforehand, what downstream costs appeared later, and where evidence is too weak to learn. The moat is a correction-safe history of calibrated operating beliefs plus lawful prospective experimentation, not accumulated prose or raw activity counts.

### 1.4 10/10 end state

A material operating-policy choice has a sealed ex-ante expectation. Its actual outcomes are compiled from existing owners at immediate, terminal and delayed horizons. The resulting study states its estimand, cohort, missingness, overlap, estimator, assumptions, uncertainty and causal grade. A load-bearing conclusion becomes an Agent OS discovery. Any policy change is a separate Agent OS decision reviewed by Sol and an independent auditor, exercised first in shadow or a bounded canary, and reversible through the existing owner. At least one real policy improvement is production-proven with measured benefit and no hidden quality regression.

## 4. Research rulings

### 4.1 Preserve the information set before the outcome

Baron and Hershey demonstrated that evaluators rate the same decision differently after learning whether its outcome was favorable. Mastermind must therefore seal the decision-time information set, expectations and uncertainty before measuring consequences. A later outcome cannot be allowed to rewrite the original rationale or forecast. See [Baron & Hershey (1988)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3367280/) and the preregistered replication [Outcomes Affect Evaluations of Decision Quality](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40951810/).

Probabilistic expectations should be scored with proper scoring rules where the event and probability are well-defined. The original probability-score reference is [Brier (1950)](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0493%281950%29078%3C0001%3AVOFEIT%3E2.0.CO%3B2). Calibration, discrimination and decision usefulness must remain separate; a single Brier score is not a universal operating score.

### 4.2 Historical route outcomes are selected, not randomized

A route is chosen because of task kind, ambiguity, risk, capability, quota, availability and prior judgment. Only the chosen action's outcome is observed. Raw acceptance or speed by worker/model therefore mixes worker effect with case mix and assignment policy.

Doubly robust evaluation combines a reward/outcome model with a logging-policy model, but it does not create support where an alternative was never assigned and it does not eliminate unobserved confounding. See [Dudík, Langford & Li (2011)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.4601), [Swaminathan & Joachims (2015)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/swaminathan15.html), and the real-world OPE benchmark design in [Saito et al. (2021)](https://datasets-benchmarks-proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/33e75ff09dd601bbe69f351039152189-Abstract-round2.html).

The governing causal discipline is to state the causal question and assumptions before selecting an estimator. Observational estimates require well-defined interventions, temporal ordering, exchangeability or a defended adjustment strategy, positivity/overlap, and correct treatment of missingness and censoring. See [Hernán & Robins, *Causal Inference: What If*](https://miguelhernan.org/whatifbook). Where unobserved confounding remains plausible, report sensitivity or partial-identification bounds rather than causal certainty; see [Ishikawa & He (2023)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v206/ishikawa23a.html).

### 4.3 Productivity is multidimensional

The SPACE framework explicitly rejects individual activity or any single metric as developer productivity. DevEx similarly emphasizes feedback loops, cognitive load and flow. Mastermind should report a vector of quality, flow, coordination, governance and resource outcomes—not collapse them into a worker score. See [Forsgren et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3454122.3454124) and [Noda et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3610285).

### 4.4 Learning must be blameless, reviewed and acted upon

Psychological safety is associated with learning behavior in teams; a surveillance or blame-oriented leaderboard would suppress the very defect reporting the system needs. See [Edmondson (1999)](https://doi.org/10.2307/2666999). Structured debriefs can improve performance when they support reflection and goal setting; see [Tannenbaum & Cerasoli (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1177/0018720812448394). Google's SRE practice likewise treats postmortems as reviewed, blameless system learning rather than individual punishment: [Postmortem Culture](https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/).

### 4.5 Metrics become dangerous when optimized as targets

Goodhart failures have multiple mechanisms, including regressional, extremal, causal and adversarial forms. Outcome metrics must therefore carry known gaming modes, guardrails, audit sampling and limits on use. See [Manheim & Garrabrant (2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.04585).

## 5. Alternatives considered

### Alternative A — a new mutable Outcome DB

**Rejected.** A store that copies Executive events, GitHub state, Slack turns and Agent OS records would become a competing lifecycle/correction plane. Derived snapshots and studies may be materialized as immutable evidence, but current truth remains with its owner.

### Alternative B — generalize Portfolio's experiment registry

**Rejected.** The live registry is designed for market/portfolio forward validation, calibration accrual, PIT data and signal-promotion constraints. Organizational policy has different identities, interventions, privacy risks and authority. Reusing it would blur domain law and create a second company experiment authority.

### Alternative C — add a worker/model leaderboard

**Rejected.** A leaderboard compresses context, route selection, review quality, downstream rework, external gates and luck into a false scalar. It invites gaming, punitive use and route feedback loops.

### Alternative D — add a high-volume `LRN` Agent OS record for every episode

**Rejected for v1.** Agent OS is durable organizational memory, not an event warehouse. Routine exposure and outcome facts belong in owner-native receipts and rebuildable studies. Existing DSC records can preserve load-bearing learned consequences, and DEC records can preserve policy changes. A new record type may be reconsidered only if repeated production evidence shows DSC/DEC cannot represent durable consequences without semantic distortion; convenience is not enough.

### Alternative E — thin owner-joined compiler plus prospective policy canaries

**Selected.** Seal expectations in existing owners, read outcomes from existing owners, compile deterministic studies, persist only significant learning in DSC, decide policy through DEC, and obtain causal leverage prospectively through bounded randomized or otherwise identified canaries.

## 8. Outcome Study

Recommended schema:

```text
mastermind.outcome_study.v1
```

A study is a rebuildable, immutable result bound to exact source cutoffs and hashes.

### 8.1 Required semantics

- `study_id`, schema version, compiler version and generation time;
- exact decision/exposure refs and policy versions;
- explicit causal question and estimand;
- cohort inclusion/exclusion rules decided before results are inspected;
- source-owner snapshot refs, observed-at times and semantic hashes;
- treatment/action definition and comparison policy;
- outcome metric definitions, horizons and denominators;
- missingness, right-censoring, correction generation and late-arriving evidence;
- balance, overlap/positivity and effective-sample diagnostics;
- estimator and all tuning/clipping/weighting choices;
- uncertainty intervals and, where relevant, multiple-look correction;
- sensitivity analyses and known unmeasured confounders;
- evidence grade plus machine-readable reasons;
- result vector and guardrail vector;
- policy recommendation class: `NO_CHANGE`, `MORE_EVIDENCE`, `PROPOSE_DEC`, or `ROLLBACK_REVIEW`;
- zero authority to enact the recommendation.

### 8.2 Evidence grades

These are epistemic labels, not workflow states or authority:

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY` | Reports observed facts; no comparison claim |
| `ADJUSTED_ASSOCIATION` | Comparable-cohort association after declared adjustment; residual confounding remains |
| `PARTIALLY_IDENTIFIED` | Reports a defensible bound/sensitivity region rather than a point causal claim |
| `OPE_SUPPORTED` | Logging propensities, overlap and estimator diagnostics support the stated off-policy estimand under declared assumptions |
| `RANDOMIZED_CANARY` | Prospective randomized assignment supports the stated intention-to-treat estimate, subject to missingness/noncompliance limits |
| `NOT_IDENTIFIED` | The causal question cannot be answered from the available evidence |

The compiler fails closed to `NOT_IDENTIFIED` when treatment is ill-defined, time order is contaminated, alternatives lack support, propensities are missing for an OPE claim, censoring is uncontrolled, or required evidence is stale/unknown. A statistical model cannot raise the grade.

## 9. Decision quality versus outcome luck

Every study must keep three judgments separate:

1. **Decision-process quality:** Was the question well-formed? Were alternatives, owner evidence, uncertainty, authority and guardrails correct at decision time?
2. **Forecast calibration:** Across comparable decisions, did stated probabilities match frequencies and remain appropriately sharp?
3. **Realized consequence:** What happened on this episode, including delayed rework and external conditions?

The report may use explanatory labels such as `sound_process / adverse_realization` or `weak_process / favorable_realization`, but only when each component is separately evidenced. It must never infer that a process was good solely because the outcome was favorable.

Expected utility, regret or cost-benefit calculations require an explicit, reviewed utility model. The compiler may not invent hidden weights that collapse quality, speed, cost and risk into one score.

## 10. Metric ruler

Metrics are vectors with definitions and guardrails, not a universal score. Every metric definition must state owner, event window, numerator, denominator, null/censoring behavior, disaggregation, correction policy and likely gaming mode.

### 10.1 Quality

- first substantive return accepted without a handoff-attributable repair;
- independent-review defect yield by severity;
- downstream rework/rollback within a declared horizon;
- production-proof integrity and escaped-defect incidence.

### 10.2 Flow

- elapsed time with external-gate time separated;
- blocked time by reason code;
- repair/clarification loops;
- time to first meaningful return and time to production proof.

### 10.3 Coordination

- communication/return contract defects;
- stale-session recovery and unresolved continuation;
- missing pickup/START/RESULT/CONTINUE/STOP edges;
- handoff-caused decision requests.

### 10.4 Governance and safety

- Sol or Chairman interventions, classified by cause;
- effect-unknown incidence;
- unauthorized widening, duplicate carrier or owner-boundary violation;
- rollback and stop-condition behavior.

### 10.5 Resource

- quota/cost class and provider scarcity consumed;
- review burden;
- compute/tool calls where owner evidence exists.

No metric may treat missing as zero. External/admin/CI waiting must not be charged to a worker or route unless the causal question explicitly studies that policy. Per-worker drilldowns remain access-controlled diagnostics and are never published as a ranked table.

## 11. First prospective consumer: compliant handoff-quality policy

### 11.1 Ruling

The first causal consumer should **not** be worker/model routing. Current routing is deterministic, selection-biased, not production-armed, and lacks full action-set propensities. Begin with a lower-risk process intervention whose assignment can be prospective and whose outcomes already have owner evidence.

### 11.2 Treatment

Both arms remain fully compliant with current Commission Wave, worker-routing, pickup/START and dialogue-close law.

- **Baseline:** the current law-compliant operator handoff.
- **Candidate:** the same required handoff plus a deterministic preflight validator and a concise mission checksum containing the observable mission, non-goals, evidence/proof target, stop condition and return packet. The candidate may reject a malformed handoff before dispatch; it may not waive required content or choose the worker.

### 11.3 Eligibility

Include bounded, routine or elevated, reversible implementation/research/review jobs with a frozen mission and capacity-selectable route. Exclude emergency recovery, exact-session work, critical/destructive operations, unresolved ownership, principal-level ambiguity, security-sensitive jobs, tiny mechanical tasks where the treatment has no plausible effect, and any job whose current carrier already fixes the handoff variant.

### 11.4 Design

- pre-register the cohort, estimand, metric definitions, expected direction and guardrails;
- perform 1:1 blocked randomization within task-kind/risk/ambiguity/repository strata where sample permits;
- record the complete eligible action set and assignment probability before delivery;
- analyze intention-to-treat first;
- treat pilot observations used to stabilize instrumentation as `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY`, not promotion evidence;
- perform power/sequential-boundary design from observed baseline rates before a promotion canary;
- stop or roll back on any material quality, safety or effect-unknown regression.

### 11.5 Primary outcome and guardrails

Primary outcome:

```text
first_substantive_return_accepted_without_handoff_attributable_repair
```

Secondary outcomes: handoff-caused clarification/repair loops, attributable blocked time, terminal elapsed time, Sol/Chairman intervention and production-proof rate.

Guardrails: escaped defects, downstream rework, independent-review severity, effect-unknown incidence, duplicate/authority defects, quota/cost class and abandonment.

Observation horizons:

- **first return** — immediate communication quality;
- **terminal** — accepted operation result;
- **14-day delayed** — downstream rework, rollback or proof correction;
- longer windows only where the program declares a justified maturation period.

### 11.6 Promotion standard

A candidate policy may be proposed for promotion only when:

- the randomized or otherwise identified study meets its pre-registered evidence threshold;
- no quality/safety guardrail shows a material adverse movement;
- independent review confirms metric and causal integrity;
- an Agent OS DSC records the learned consequence;
- a separate Agent OS DEC authorizes the policy delta, canary scope and rollback;
- the owning runtime applies the change through its normal reviewed path.

## 12. Later routing consumer

Routing may become a consumer only after the runtime logs, before assignment:

- the complete eligible action set after hard authority/capability exclusions;
- chosen action and exact policy version;
- assignment method and probability for every supported alternative;
- context features available at assignment time, not post-outcome leakage;
- capacity snapshot/version and any exclusions;
- expected outcome vector and guardrails.

Exploration is limited to near-equivalent eligible routes on bounded, reversible, noncritical work. Deterministic/high-risk routes remain nonexploratory. No route is made eligible merely to obtain statistical support. Historical data without support remains descriptive even after a sophisticated model is fitted.

Required OPE diagnostics include overlap by action/context, effective sample size, weight tails/clipping, estimator agreement, confidence intervals, nonstationarity checks and unobserved-confounding sensitivity. `Doubly robust` is an estimator property, not a permission slip.

## 13. Identity, time and correction semantics

### 13.1 Identity

Do not mint a new universal person/session/job identity. Preserve typed references:

```text
executive_job:<id>
executive_attempt:<id>
executive_worker:<id>
agentos_dec:<key>
agentos_dsc:<key>
github_pr:<owner/repo>#<number>
slack_message:<channel>/<ts>
linear_issue:<identifier>
```

A study carries crosswalks among these refs but does not become their owner. Provider/model aliases are versioned policy variables, not human identity.

### 13.2 Time

Every observation distinguishes:

- `event_time` — when the underlying event occurred;
- `available_at` — when the evidence became observable to the decision system;
- `observed_at` — when this study read it;
- `matures_at` — when an outcome horizon is expected to close;
- `corrected_at` — when an owner correction was published.

Only evidence available before the decision may enter ex-ante context. Late outcomes are right-censored until mature; they are not treated as failures or zeros.

### 13.3 Corrections

Owner corrections remain with the owner. The compiler emits a new study revision with `supersedes`, preserves the prior source cutoff/hash, and explains the delta. A corrected result cannot silently rewrite the sealed expectation or an earlier published study.

## 14. Privacy, rights and organizational safety

- Evaluate policies and processes, not personal traits.
- Never ingest or retain private chain of thought.
- Prefer owner references, reason codes, timestamps, hashes and bounded metadata over raw Slack messages, prompts or transcripts.
- Preserve repository/channel/app access boundaries; a compiler read does not grant wider disclosure rights.
- Do not retain secrets, credential files, provider tokens or raw account identifiers.
- Aggregate small cohorts where disclosure or punitive inference is plausible.
- Label model-generated classifications and require deterministic or human-reviewed reason codes before they affect a study.
- Never use the study as compensation, punishment, public ranking or autonomous hiring/firing evidence.
- Blamelessness does not suppress accountability: authority violations, fabricated receipts or intentional bypasses remain explicit governance findings.

## 15. Experience architecture

The first user-facing study view must show, without a hidden composite score:

1. decision and policy identity;
2. sealed ex-ante expectation and information cutoff;
3. actual outcome vector by horizon;
4. decision-process assessment separate from realization;
5. cohort comparability, overlap and missingness;
6. causal grade and exact reasons it could not be higher;
7. uncertainty/sensitivity;
8. policy recommendation class;
9. linked DSC/DEC, canary and rollback evidence.

Required failure states include `EXPECTATION_MISSING`, `OUTCOME_IMMATURE`, `SOURCE_STALE`, `IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED`, `TREATMENT_ILL_DEFINED`, `NO_COUNTERFACTUAL_SUPPORT`, `PROPENSITY_MISSING`, `UNMEASURED_CONFOUNDING`, `CENSORING_UNCONTROLLED`, `METRIC_DEFINITION_DRIFT`, `CORRECTION_PENDING`, `NOT_IDENTIFIED` and `POLICY_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED`.

A dashboard that merely shows throughput or success rates fails this architecture.

## 17. Acceptance and stop condition

The program is not complete when schemas or a dashboard merge. It stops only when all of the following are true:

- **Truth:** decision-time expectations and owner evidence are point-in-time, correction-safe and rights-safe;
- **Intelligence:** the compiler produces a transparent study with missingness, comparability, causal grade and uncertainty;
- **Product:** Sol/Chairman can inspect expectation versus actual and the reason a policy is or is not justified;
- **Learning:** at least one real operating-policy canary shows measured benefit with no hidden quality/safety regression;
- **Governance:** a DSC records the learned consequence, a DEC records the accepted/rejected policy delta, and rollback/proof remain recoverable to a fresh session.

---

## 18. Frozen no-rebuild boundaries

Do not create:

- an outcome, experiment, worker-performance or routing database parallel to existing owners;
- a universal identity/session registry;
- a worker/model leaderboard or hidden composite productivity score;
- another queue, lifecycle, watcher, retry, policy promotion or routing authority;
- a generalized copy of Portfolio experiments or Eval OS;
- automatic source-law/route/policy edits;
- causal claims from raw historical success rates;
- raw transcript or chain-of-thought surveillance;
- a UI that calls descriptive telemetry learned policy.

The architecture may be refined only through an explicit superseding DEC that preserves these owner and authority boundaries.
