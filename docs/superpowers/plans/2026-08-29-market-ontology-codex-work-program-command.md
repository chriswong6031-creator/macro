## 1. The 10/10 end state

The target is not a collection of lookalike pages and not a literal clone.

The target is a lawful, original, integrated Mastermind intelligence operating system that satisfies
every useful Market Ontology job and combines those jobs with Mastermind's native strengths:

```text
truth
-> evidence and identity
-> event/development state
-> economic relationships and transmission
-> company/security consequence
-> market incorporation and opportunity context
-> options/valuation/portfolio expression
-> human research and thesis continuity
-> monitoring/revision/alerts/distribution
-> prospective learning and calibration
```

### Retail end state

A self-directed investor opens a development, security or portfolio and quickly understands:

- what changed;
- why it matters;
- what assets and companies are affected;
- what the market appears to have incorporated;
- what opportunity context remains;
- what invalidates the read;
- what comes next;
- what it means for owned or watched positions;
- which sources, clocks, assumptions and degraded states support the answer.

### Desk end state

A PM, analyst or internal research agent can inspect the same canonical intelligence at full depth:

- source lineage and correction history;
- exact economic episode and event identity;
- transmission mechanisms, contradictions and alternatives;
- company drivers, assumptions, valuation and capital structure;
- options and scenario expression;
- portfolio intersections and risk;
- historical analogs and evaluation receipts;
- saved research, thesis revisions, monitors and team workflow;
- API/webhook/export projections over the same objects.

### Machine end state

Agents and deterministic consumers receive typed, point-in-time, correction-safe, missing-aware objects
rather than copied prose or a second truth store. Every generated conclusion declares method,
provenance, authority ceiling and failure state.

### Competitive end state

Mastermind reaches three distinct milestones:

1. **`COVERAGE_COMPLETE`** — every admitted granular capability/job row is accounted for with no
   `UNKNOWN`, `UNOWNED` or `UNASSESSED` row and no destructive family compression.
2. **`PARITY_COMPLETE`** — every lawful product/user/machine job has a production-proven Mastermind
   equivalent or stronger equivalent, with real consumers and truthful failure states.
3. **`BEYOND_PARITY_RATCHET_ACTIVE`** — every parity family has been reviewed for Mastermind-native
   combination, hardening, expansion, instrumentation and moat creation.

Only Sol/Chairman may declare these milestones after adversarial proof review.

## 3. Why the program drifted off track

The program's current problem is not lack of intelligence or ambition. It is command fragmentation.

### 3.1 Architecture became stronger than execution

The parity law, owner map and no-rebuild boundaries are sophisticated, but too much energy has gone
into re-reconciling records carriers while visible product breadth remained small.

### 3.2 Moving-main churn became a substitute for landing

Records PRs such as F00B repeatedly rebased/reconciled against nightly data movement. Exact-current
proof matters, but a carrier that never lands does not unlock the next dependency. The command loop
must distinguish material source-law collision from unrelated data/render movement and complete the
bounded landing safely.

### 3.3 Lane topology described capacity that did not exist

F01-F13 are useful domain identities, but treating them as thirteen durable provider/Fable seats left
work commissioned but unclaimed. Provider sessions are execution surfaces, not organizational roles.

### 3.4 Blocked dependencies stalled unrelated work

F00A is genuinely blocked on original raw files. K5 is genuinely blocked on accepted K2-C and K3-D.
Those blockers must remain honest, but they do not justify pausing independent F01-F13 product work.

### 3.5 Too many transitions required a Sol/Chairman relay

The system correctly protected architecture and authority, but ordinary reversible implementation
became overly dependent on manual routing and review ceremony. Codex Work now owns the continuous
translation from plan to child waves and from child returns to bounded next actions.

### 3.6 Feature families risked becoming checklists instead of journeys

Parity rows are necessary for coverage, but the product moat comes from coherent continuation:
`event -> research -> implication -> expression -> monitor -> revision`, not isolated screens.

## 12. Engineering and product quality law

### 12.1 Original implementation only

Reproduce useful jobs and interaction laws with original Mastermind code/design and lawful
first-party/public/licensed data. Never copy proprietary code, private corpora, copyrighted prose,
assets, branding, credentials or hidden/private interfaces.

### 12.2 Canonical owner reuse

Before `BUILD_NEW`, prove that the job is not already owned by an existing engine, object or active
carrier. A new projection may be correct; a second truth system is not.

### 12.3 Failure states are first-class

Every vertical must design and test relevant states such as:

- no coverage;
- source unavailable or rights blocked;
- stale or corrected evidence;
- unresolved or superseded identity;
- partial denominator;
- conflicting owners/models;
- uncalibrated inference;
- dependency held;
- unauthorized action;
- transport/runtime unavailable;
- consumer disconnected.

Missing is never silently neutral.

### 12.4 Models do not inherit authority

Model-generated summaries, causal hypotheses, sentiment or confidence cannot rank, size, gate,
originate or open trades without point-in-time replay, forward validation and explicit promotion.

### 12.5 Production is the acceptance test

Green CI proves implementation integrity, not product completion. User-facing work requires real
browser proof at relevant breakpoints. Machine-facing work requires a real producer and consumer.
Data/learning work requires natural-time or accepted production receipts at the claimed tier.

### 12.6 Builder/reviewer separation

Consequential children use an independent reviewer where practical. Prefer cross-model or
cross-provider review for architecture-sensitive, rights-sensitive or data-sensitive waves.
