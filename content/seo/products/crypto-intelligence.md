<p>The Crypto Intelligence Cockpit reads digital assets as a market class rather than reducing the category to one Bitcoin chart. It combines the total-market trend with a ranked asset board, capital-flow receipts, leverage, volatility, allocation and listed crypto equities.</p>

<h2>Start with the class, then inspect the assets</h2>
<p>The opening state derives total crypto market capitalization from Bitcoin market cap and dominance, then checks that read against daily market snapshots. The ranked board adds current price, daily change, available history and a restrained state label for the largest assets.</p>

<h2>Follow capital before narrative</h2>
<p>Spot-Bitcoin ETF flows, stablecoin supply and spot-volume participation show whether capital is entering, leaving or simply rotating. Each tile names its source or proxy so a narrow Bitcoin series is never presented as complete class volume.</p>

<h2>Read leverage and heat together</h2>
<p>Funding, open interest relative to Bitcoin market value, implied volatility and the altseason meter expose where positioning can amplify the next move. These are descriptive conditions, not a new composite trading score.</p>

<h2>Keep allocation tied to governed strategy</h2>
<p>Bitcoin Vector continues to own total crypto exposure. The cockpit splits that governed budget across Bitcoin, Ethereum, altcoins and cash using the existing cycle and alt-season overlay, so the two pages cannot recommend conflicting total exposure.</p>

<h2>Move into the evidence</h2>
<p>Bitcoin links into the full Vector and its strategy track record. Ethereum remains an explicitly labeled relative-trend preview until its governed state machine has enough defined evidence. Listed-equity lanes connect into the existing stock dossiers.</p>

<h2>Coverage and limitations</h2>
<div class="callout warn"><span class="co-h">New universe history begins now</span><p>The ranked crypto universe accrues one daily snapshot per asset. Short histories are labeled as building rather than backfilled or simulated. Market data can be delayed, unavailable or sourced from a disclosed fallback when the primary endpoint is unreachable.</p></div>
<p>The cockpit is research context, not investment advice. Check timestamps, source notes and the Bitcoin Vector strategy receipts before acting on any state.</p>
