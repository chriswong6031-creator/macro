<p>Mastermind AI is a market research assistant that explains dashboards, symbols and available market context with read-only tools connected to the MastermindX workspace.</p>

<h2>Ask from the page you are reading</h2>
<p>Open the assistant from a dashboard or symbol view and the relevant page, panel or ticker can accompany the question. That context makes it possible to ask what a field is showing without restating every value on screen.</p>

<h2>Use the right depth for the question</h2>
<p>Short questions can use the faster response lane, while deeper research work can produce a more structured, multi-section answer. Access and allowances vary by plan, so the pricing page remains the source for current entitlements.</p>

<h2>See what the assistant checked</h2>
<p>When sources are available, the response can show the research surfaces consulted and attach citations. Those links are the useful part: they let a reader inspect the underlying dashboard, dossier or report instead of accepting a summary on trust.</p>

<h2>Work with symbols, charts and images</h2>
<p>The assistant can use supported symbol, page, chart or screenshot context. In compatible Terminal sessions it may return visual annotations or chart commands, which are aids for investigation rather than original trading signals.</p>

<h2>Limitations</h2>
<div class="callout warn"><span class="co-h">A cited answer can still be wrong</span><p>Mastermind AI can be incomplete, misread a source or lag the latest market move. It does not place trades or provide personalized investment advice. Verify important claims against the linked evidence and its timestamp.</p></div>
<p>Signals and scores come from the platform's deterministic engines; the assistant explains available outputs and may de-escalate uncertainty, but it does not originate those signals.</p>
