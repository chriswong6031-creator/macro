<p>MastermindX market dashboards turn nightly engine output into focused views of regional markets, macro conditions, cycles, sectors, themes and cross-asset relationships.</p>

<h2>Read regional markets on their own terms</h2>
<p>Coverage spans the US, China, Hong Kong, Canada and an international comparison set. Each region has dedicated macro and equity views, while region-specific surfaces add the flows, policy, currency or commodity context that matters there.</p>

<h2>Start with market state and health</h2>
<p>Macro views organize available growth, inflation, liquidity, rates, credit and regime context. Market-health views add breadth, volatility, index condition and scheduled catalysts where those inputs exist. They describe state at their stated cadence rather than pretending every field is a real-time feed.</p>

<h2>Move from markets to sectors and themes</h2>
<p>Sector and thematic views organize heat, cycle position, breadth and rotation for scanning. Links into baskets and stock dossiers make a broad observation investigable at the constituent level.</p>

<h2>Add cross-asset context</h2>
<p>Dedicated surfaces cover commodities, foreign exchange, bonds, Bitcoin and cross-asset relationships. They help reveal whether rates, the dollar, credit or commodity moves support or conflict with the equity picture.</p>

<h2>Connect the reading to AI and research</h2>
<p>Mastermind AI can explain a page or panel using the current context, while linked stock dossiers and institutional research pages provide ticker- and report-level detail. The dashboard is a starting point for investigation, not the final word.</p>

<h2>Coverage, cadence and access limits</h2>
<div class="callout warn"><span class="co-h">Not every surface updates together</span><p>Most engine-generated dashboards rebuild nightly; selected quote or intraday surfaces may update more frequently. Regions do not expose identical fields, missing data can produce unavailable readings, and deeper dashboards may require registration or a paid plan.</p></div>
<p>Dashboard outputs are research context, not investment advice. Read the timestamp and limitation labels attached to each surface before comparing two states.</p>
