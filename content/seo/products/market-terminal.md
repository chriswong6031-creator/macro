<p>The Market Terminal is a browser-based workspace for moving from a live chart to the company and research context behind it, without installing desktop software.</p>

<h2>Chart without a desktop install</h2>
<p>Use candlestick charts, multiple timeframes and indicators directly in the browser. Current quote context stays near the chart so price structure and the broader workspace do not become separate research tasks.</p>

<h2>Read a complete technical workflow</h2>
<p>Five complementary indicator systems organize 31 advanced modules that users can combine around structure, momentum rotation, trend confirmation, invalidation and staged profit objectives. Focus, Workflow and Research profiles let a chart begin clean and add depth only when the thesis requires it. Insider unlocks a curated selection across the five systems; Pro opens the complete library. The pricing page carries the current plan-by-plan access details.</p>

<h2>Keep the evidence beside the chart</h2>
<p>Watchlists, comparison controls, key statistics and earnings context sit inside the same working view. When MastermindX has a current engine reading for a symbol, the Terminal can connect that reading to the chart without presenting the two as if they share one timestamp.</p>

<h2>Move from chart to dossier</h2>
<p>Open a public stock dossier when the chart is only the beginning. Dossiers organize available technical, financial, valuation, options, ownership and company context by ticker, with their own freshness labels where available.</p>

<h2>Bring Mastermind AI into the symbol</h2>
<p>Supported sessions can pass the active symbol or page context into Mastermind AI. The assistant may explain a field or map chart structure visually, but its answer remains a research aid that should be checked against the linked source.</p>

<h2>Access and limitations</h2>
<div class="callout warn"><span class="co-h">Research workspace, not execution venue</span><p>The Terminal does not place trades. Market data, research panels and engine outputs can update on different schedules, and some live options or intraday features require an eligible plan or trial. The pricing page owns the current access boundary.</p></div>
<p>Always check the timestamp attached to the surface you are reading. A chart label, engine state or AI annotation is not a recommendation and cannot guarantee an outcome.</p>
